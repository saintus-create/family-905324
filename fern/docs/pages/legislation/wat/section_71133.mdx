# WAT § 71133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 71133

---

## Text

If the county elections official’s certificate shows that the formation petition is sufficient, the county elections official shall present the petition to the board of supervisors without delay.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 201. Effective January 1, 2003.)
