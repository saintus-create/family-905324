# WAT § 44632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 2. Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 44632

## Text

The notice shall be published once a week for two successive weeks in some newspaper published in the office county.
