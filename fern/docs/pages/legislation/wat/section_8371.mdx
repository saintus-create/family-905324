# WAT § 8371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Maintenance and Operation at Sacramento River Flood Control Project > Article: 3. Duties and Liabilities of Districts and Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8371

---

## Text

The department shall, in connection with the flood control works of the Sacramento River Flood Control Project, make inspections and prepare reports which are required because of assurances given to the Secretary of the Army pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 8615) of Chapter 3 of Part 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 185.)
