# WAT § 72708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 72708

---

## Text

From and after the date of the adoption of the resolution approving the annexation, the area described therein is added to and forms a part of the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
