# WAT § 22752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 5. Settlement of Damages to Water Rights of State Land

**Citation:** WAT § 22752

---

## Text

The money collected in the settlement pursuant to this article shall be paid into the treasury of the district for district purposes and be apportioned to any funds the board deems advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
