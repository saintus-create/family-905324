# WAT § 78672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 6. Water Supply Reliability > Article: 3. Water Conservation and Groundwater Recharge

**Citation:** WAT § 78672

---

## Text

Any loan contract entered into pursuant to this article may include provisions determined to be necessary by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
