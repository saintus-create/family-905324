# WAT § 21608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Division and Precinct Boundaries

**Citation:** WAT § 21608

---

## Text

When land is included within a district after its formation and less than 120 days before an election, the voters in the area included shall not be entitled to vote at the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1045, Sec. 12.)
