# WAT § 8671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 4. Lease and Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 8671

---

## Text

The board may, at such session, if it deems the action to be for the best public interest, reject any and all bids either written or oral, and withdraw the property from lease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
