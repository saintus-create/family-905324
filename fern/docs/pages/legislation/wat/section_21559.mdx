# WAT § 21559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Divisions and Methods of Electing Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 21559

---

## Text

If an order made pursuant to this article changes the number of divisions, the board shall forthwith redivide the district into the number of divisions specified in the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
