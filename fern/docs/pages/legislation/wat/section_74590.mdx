# WAT § 74590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts With Other Agencies > Article: 2. Contracts for Supply of Water and Construction of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 74590

---

## Text

A district may contract with any city, city and county, county, or district organized under the laws of this state for a water supply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
