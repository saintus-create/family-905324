# WAT § 52175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52175

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall add the principal of the bonds so called for redemption specified in the order, and any premium provided by those bonds to be paid upon such call, to the amount which he is required to collect for payment of the next maturing principal and interest of the outstanding bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
