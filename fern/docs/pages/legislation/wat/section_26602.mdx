# WAT § 26602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 26602

---

## Text

Certified copies of such resolution shall be served on the clerk of the board of supervisors and on the auditor, assessor and collector of the county or counties in which the lands within the district are located and shall be filed with the State Board of Equalization.
