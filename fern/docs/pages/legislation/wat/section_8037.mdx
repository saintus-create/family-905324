# WAT § 8037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8037

## Text

The interest coupons shall be numbered consecutively and signed by the treasurer.
