# WAT § 6112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 3. Emergency Work

**Citation:** WAT § 6112

---

## Text

The department shall continue in full charge and control of such dam or reservoir, or both, and its appurtenances until they are rendered safe or the emergency occasioning the action has ceased.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
