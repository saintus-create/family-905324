# WAT § 11772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 7. Payment of Principal and Interest

**Citation:** WAT § 11772

---

## Text

All funds transferred for the payment of principal or interest on bonds shall be segregated and applied solely for the payment of the principal and interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
