# WAT § 9554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 2. Purchase and Sale of Land by the Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 9554

---

## Text

The purchase price received in cash shall be paid by the board to the State Treasurer and any bonds or coupons received in payment by the board shall be canceled by the board and delivered to the State Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
