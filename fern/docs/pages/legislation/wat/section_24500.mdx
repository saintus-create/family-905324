# WAT § 24500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Payment and Purchase of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24500

---

## Text

Upon presentation of any matured bond of the district, the treasurer shall pay it from the bond principal fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
