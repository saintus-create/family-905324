# WAT § 34802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34802

---

## Text

The board shall hold regular meetings at such time and place as may be agreed upon by resolution.
