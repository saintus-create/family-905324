# WAT § 26629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 2. Assessment and Equalization

**Citation:** WAT § 26629

---

## Text

Within 15 days after receipt of the statement of values and duplicate rolls, the district’s board of directors shall levy an annual assessment upon the land within the district in the same manner as provided in Chapters 2 and 3 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1938.)
