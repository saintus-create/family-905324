# WAT § 52584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 4. Collection and Disposition of Supplemental Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 52584

---

## Text

Any surplus in the supplemental assessment fund after the principal and interest on the bonds next maturing are paid in full shall be refunded by the county treasurer to the persons who have paid the supplemental assessment, in proportion to the amounts they paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
