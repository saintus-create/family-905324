# WAT § 51332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51332

---

## Text

At the hearing, the board of supervisors shall hear any evidence offered in support of the written objections and may modify or amend the assessment valuations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
