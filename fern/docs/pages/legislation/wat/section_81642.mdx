# WAT § 81642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Government of Authority > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 81642

---

## Text

The board may require, and establish the amount of, official bonds of officers and employees that are necessary for the protection of the funds and property of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
