# WAT § 55333.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 55333.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 42. Effective January 1, 2006.)
