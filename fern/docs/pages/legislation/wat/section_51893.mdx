# WAT § 51893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 5. Reapportioning Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51893

---

## Text

The board shall require, as a condition to the making of any reapportionment pursuant to this article, that each parcel of the divided or subdivided parcel be given suitable means of ingress and egress.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 104, Sec. 6.)
