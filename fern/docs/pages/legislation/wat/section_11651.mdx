# WAT § 11651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 2. Contracts with State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 11651

---

## Text

The governing body charged with the levying of taxes or assessments in any state agency which contracts to purchase from the department any water, use of water, water storage, electric power, or other service shall provide for the punctual payment to the department of all amounts which become due under the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
