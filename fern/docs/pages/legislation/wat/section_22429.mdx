# WAT § 22429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22429

---

## Text

The right is hereby granted to locate, construct, and maintain any of the works of a district on any land which is now or hereafter owned by the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
