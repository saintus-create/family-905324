# WAT § 6077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 1. Powers in General

**Citation:** WAT § 6077

---

## Text

It is unlawful to construct, enlarge, repair, alter, remove, maintain, or operate any dam or reservoir except upon approval of the department as provided in this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
