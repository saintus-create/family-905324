# WAT § 26106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 2. Publication of List of Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26106

---

## Text

If any land assessed to the same person lies in more than one county, publication may be made in any county in which any portion of the land lies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
