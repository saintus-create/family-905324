# WAT § 25113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 4. Unauthorized Refunding Modification

**Citation:** WAT § 25113

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be given and it shall be held in substantially the same manner as a bond election, except that a majority vote only is required for approval of the modification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
