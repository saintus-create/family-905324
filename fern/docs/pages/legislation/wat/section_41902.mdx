# WAT § 41902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Contest of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41902

---

## Text

The court shall determine upon the hearing whether the election was fairly conducted and in substantial compliance with the requirements of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
