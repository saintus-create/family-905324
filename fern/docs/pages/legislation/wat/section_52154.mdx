# WAT § 52154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52154

---

## Text

The county treasurer may, and upon written request of a majority of the board, shall reject any and all bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
