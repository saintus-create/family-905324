# WAT § 21578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Abolition and Reestablishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 21578

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors finds as to all of such matters in the affirmative, it may order that there shall be no divisions in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
