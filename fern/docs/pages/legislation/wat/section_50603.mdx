# WAT § 50603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Selection, Tenure and Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 50603

---

## Text

After the first election of the board, the district is organized and has power to sue and be sued.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 681.)
