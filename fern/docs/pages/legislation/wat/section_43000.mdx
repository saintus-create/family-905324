# WAT § 43000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Water and Electricity > Article: 1. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 43000

---

## Text

The board may acquire, improve, and operate the necessary works for the storage and distribution of water, and any drainage or reclamation works connected therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
