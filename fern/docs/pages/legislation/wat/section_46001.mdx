# WAT § 46001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46001

---

## Text

At its first regular meeting, or within 90 days thereafter, the board shall levy a preliminary assessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
