# WAT § 37824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Merger

**Citation:** WAT § 37824

---

## Text

Upon the merger of the two districts, the water storage district for all purposes ceases to exist, and the offices of its directors and officers are terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
