# WAT § 72151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 72151

---

## Text

The territory proposed to be excluded may consist of one or more parcels which need not be contiguous to each other and may consist of parcels situated in more than one county if the district contains such parcels.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
