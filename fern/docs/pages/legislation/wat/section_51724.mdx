# WAT § 51724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51724

---

## Text

The board shall apportion the costs of making the reapportionment to the several parts into which the parcel is subdivided and between the several assessments upon the parcel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
