# WAT § 25240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 2. Payment Solely from Revenue

**Citation:** WAT § 25240

---

## Text

As used in this article and Article 3 of this chapter “revenue” means all or any part of any source or sources of payment excluding assessments but including the proceeds of any existing or proposed contract or contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
