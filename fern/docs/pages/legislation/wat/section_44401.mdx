# WAT § 44401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Limitations on Liabilities

**Citation:** WAT § 44401

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this article shall be construed to limit the right of the board to enter into any contract or lease for property, as in this division provided, and to bind the district for the payment of the rental or consideration specified in the lease or contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
