# WAT § 8555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8555

---

## Text

The board may elect one of its members other than the president as vice president.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
