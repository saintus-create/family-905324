# WAT § 53046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 3. Time Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53046

---

## Text

Time warrants shall be paid upon their due date upon presentation and surrender to the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
