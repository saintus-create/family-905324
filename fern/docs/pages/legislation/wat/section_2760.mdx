# WAT § 2760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2760

---

## Text

The order of determination, the statements or claims of claimants, and the exceptions made to the order of determination constitute the pleadings.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
