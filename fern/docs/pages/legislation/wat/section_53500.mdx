# WAT § 53500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Consolidation

**Citation:** WAT § 53500

---

## Text

A copy of any proposal for consolidation shall be forwarded to the State Lands Commission prior to the circulation of any petition or the adoption of any resolution making such proposal.
