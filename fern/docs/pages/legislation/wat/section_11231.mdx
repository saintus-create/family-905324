# WAT § 11231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 6. Madera Canal

**Citation:** WAT § 11231

---

## Text

The Madera Canal shall be of such capacity as the department determines to be necessary to furnish an adequate supply of water for beneficial purposes in the area to be served by it.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
