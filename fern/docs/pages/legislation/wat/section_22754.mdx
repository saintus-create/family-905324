# WAT § 22754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 5. Settlement of Damages to Water Rights of State Land

**Citation:** WAT § 22754

---

## Text

The tax title of the State to the land involved remains wholly unimpaired by proceedings had pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
