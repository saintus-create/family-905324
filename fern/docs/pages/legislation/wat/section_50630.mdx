# WAT § 50630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Officers

**Citation:** WAT § 50630

---

## Text

The board shall elect one of its members as its president, and one of its members, or any other person, as its secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
