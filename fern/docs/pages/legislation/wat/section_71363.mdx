# WAT § 71363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71363

---

## Text

The general manager shall perform such duties as may be imposed on him by the board. He shall report to the board in accordance with such rules and regulations as it may adopt.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1151.)
