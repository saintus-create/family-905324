# WAT § 46281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 6. Lien of Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 46281

---

## Text

Where bonds of a district have been issued upon any assessment, no act or conduct on the part of the board or any officer shall invalidate the assessment after it has become a lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
