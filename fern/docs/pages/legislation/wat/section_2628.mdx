# WAT § 2628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 6. Objections to the Report

**Citation:** WAT § 2628

---

## Text

The objections shall be in writing and shall state with reasonable certainty the grounds of the objection.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
