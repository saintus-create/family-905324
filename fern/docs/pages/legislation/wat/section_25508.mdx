# WAT § 25508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25508

---

## Text

On or before the first Monday in August in each year the assessor shall complete his assessment book and deliver it to the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
