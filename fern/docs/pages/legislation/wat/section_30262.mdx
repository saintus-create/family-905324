# WAT § 30262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30262

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may adjourn the hearing from time to time, not exceeding four weeks in all.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
