# WAT § 60449

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60449

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that no district shall be disincorporated until all bonded indebtedness shall have been fully paid, and by the word “indebtedness” as used herein is meant all indebtedness other than said bonded indebtedness unless the latter is expressly used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
