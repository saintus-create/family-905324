# WAT § 31142.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9.3. Alameda County Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31142.39

---

## Text

All actions authorized by this article shall be undertaken in a manner that is consistent with the federal Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. Sec. 1341 et seq.) and Division 7 (commencing with Section 13000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 563, Sec. 1. (SB 133) Effective January 1, 2010.)
