# WAT § 74253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** WAT § 74253

---

## Text

The bond of the secretary shall be recorded in the office of the recorder of the principal county and then filed with the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
