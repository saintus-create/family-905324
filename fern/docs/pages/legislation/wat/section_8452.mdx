# WAT § 8452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Maintenance Costs of Vegetation on Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8452

---

## Text

Wherever the words “department or the Reclamation Board” are used together in this chapter, they shall mean the Reclamation Board as to any flood control project levees under its jurisdiction and the Department of Water Resources as to all other flood control project levees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 995.)
