# WAT § 55521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 55521

---

## Text

The board by an order entered upon its minutes, shall, subject to the provisions of this division, prescribe the form of bonds issued and of the interest coupons attached thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
