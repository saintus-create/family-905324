# WAT § 22121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22121

---

## Text

Subject to the conditions in this article a district may in the manner required by law make special appropriations of water for power purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
