# WAT § 72281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Automatic Exclusion of Cities

**Citation:** WAT § 72281

---

## Text

The board or the secretary of the municipal water district shall file a certified copy of the ordinance adopted pursuant to Section 72280 with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
