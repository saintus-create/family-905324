# WAT § 25203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25203

---

## Text

Each issue of bonds shall be numbered consecutively as authorized, and the bonds of each issue shall be numbered consecutively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
