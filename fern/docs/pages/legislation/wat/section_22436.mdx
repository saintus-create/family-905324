# WAT § 22436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22436

---

## Text

A district may in its name take conveyances, leases, contracts, or other assurances for all property acquired by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
