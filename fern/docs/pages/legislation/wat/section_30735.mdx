# WAT § 30735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 1. Creation of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 30735

---

## Text

Directors elected by division shall be voters of the divisions by which they are elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
