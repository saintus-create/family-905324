# WAT § 42227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 2. Units of Construction

**Citation:** WAT § 42227

---

## Text

Upon resolving to construct a unit, the board may amend the plans, specifications, and estimates of costs of the unit by making changes, modifications, and additions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 1993.)
