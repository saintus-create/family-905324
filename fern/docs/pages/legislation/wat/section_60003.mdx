# WAT § 60003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60003

---

## Text

“District” means a water replenishment district formed under the provisions of the Water Replenishment District Act, or this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
