# WAT § 36199

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36199

---

## Text

The interest on general obligation bonds shall be payable at such place or places as may be prescribed by the board upon the presentation and surrender of the proper coupons.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
