# WAT § 21558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Divisions and Methods of Electing Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 21558

---

## Text

If an order made pursuant to this article changes the method of electing directors, all directors thereafter elected in the district shall be elected as provided in the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
