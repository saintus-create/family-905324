# WAT § 12920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.5. Protection of Ground Water Basins > Article: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 12920

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known as, and may be cited as, the Porter-Dolwig Ground Water Basin Protection Law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1620.)
