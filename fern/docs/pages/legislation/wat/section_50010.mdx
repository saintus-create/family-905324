# WAT § 50010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50010

---

## Text

“County treasurer” means the county treasurer of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
