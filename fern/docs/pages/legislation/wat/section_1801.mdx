# WAT § 1801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 3. Enlargement of Existing Works of Another

**Citation:** WAT § 1801

---

## Text

The repairing, improving, adding to, supplementing, or enlarging of works under this article, shall not materially interfere with the proper use thereof by the owner of the works nor materially injure the works.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
