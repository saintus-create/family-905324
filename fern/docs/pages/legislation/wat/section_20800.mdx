# WAT § 20800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 1. Preliminary Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20800

## Text

The formation petition shall be presented to the board of supervisors of the principal county.
