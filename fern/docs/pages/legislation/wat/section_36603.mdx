# WAT § 36603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 4. Hearing on Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 36603

---

## Text

From the time of calling the hearing and until the hearing is held, the assessment book shall be open to public inspection at the office of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
