# WAT § 22077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 22077

---

## Text

A district may deliver water for fire protection purposes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
