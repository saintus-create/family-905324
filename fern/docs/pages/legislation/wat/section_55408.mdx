# WAT § 55408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Use of the Improvement Act of 1911 > Article: 2. Terms and Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 55408

---

## Text

All references to municipal officers and matters mean and include the corresponding officers of county waterworks districts and matters under this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 452.)
