# WAT § 50740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Elections > Article: 1.5. Appointments in Lieu of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 50740

---

## Text

If by 5 p.m. on the 54th day prior to a general district election the number of nominees does not exceed the number of trustees to be elected and if a petition signed by 5 percent of the voters requesting that the election be held has not been presented to the board, an election shall not be held.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 218.)
