# WAT § 13166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 3. State Water Quality Control > Article: 4. Other Powers and Duties of the State Board

**Citation:** WAT § 13166

---

## Text

The state board, with the assistance of the regional boards, shall prepare and implement a statewide water quality information storage and retrieval program. Such program shall be coordinated and integrated to the maximum extent practicable with data storage and retrieval programs of other agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
