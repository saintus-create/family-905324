# WAT § 50750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Elections > Article: 2. Conducting

**Citation:** WAT § 50750

---

## Text

Each member of the election board, upon entering upon his duties, shall be sworn to a faithful performance thereof by some officer authorized to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
