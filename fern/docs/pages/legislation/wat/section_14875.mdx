# WAT § 14875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 22. Graywater Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 14875

---

## Text

This chapter applies to the construction, installation, or alteration of graywater systems for subsurface irrigation and other safe uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 961, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
