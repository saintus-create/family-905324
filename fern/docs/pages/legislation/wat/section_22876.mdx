# WAT § 22876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 3. Flood Protection

**Citation:** WAT § 22876

---

## Text

The district may contribute funds for the purpose stated in Section 22875 to be expended by or jointly with the United States, other governments, or persons benefited by the same protective works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
