# WAT § 39933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39933

---

## Text

If upon the canvass it appears that a majority of all the votes cast are “Water storage district—No,” the result shall be declared and recorded in the records kept by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
