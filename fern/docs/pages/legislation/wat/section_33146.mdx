# WAT § 33146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 33146

---

## Text

Land may be added to the surviving district or excluded therefrom in any manner and with the same effect as provided in this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
