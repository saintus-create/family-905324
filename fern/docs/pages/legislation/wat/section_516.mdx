# WAT § 516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 516

---

## Text

“Water meter” includes any suitable water measuring device or facility which measures or determines the volumetric flow of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 407, Sec. 2.)
