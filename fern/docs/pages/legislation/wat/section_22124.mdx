# WAT § 22124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22124

---

## Text

Every district furnishing electrical power shall comply with Section 8029.5 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 549, Sec. 5.)
