# WAT § 48028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 2. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 48028

---

## Text

The expenses of giving the notice provided for in Article 1 of this chapter and of the hearing on the exclusion petition shall be paid by the persons filing the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
