# WAT § 42725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Project Uncompleted > Article: 2. Elections and Subsequent Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 42725

---

## Text

If the vote at either of the elections prescribed in this part is adverse to the report, the proposed project is abandoned. Thereafter the district shall continue, and the original project shall be and remain in the same full force and effect, as though no proceedings had been taken under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 74. Effective January 1, 1993.)
