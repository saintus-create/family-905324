# WAT § 35602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 35602

---

## Text

There is given, dedicated, and set apart for the uses and purposes of each district all water and water rights belonging to the State within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
