# WAT § 34181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 34181

---

## Text

The first publication of the notice shall be at least 21 days before the day set for the hearing. If at the hearing it appears that the notice is defective in any material respect, the board shall set another time for the hearing and cause notice again to be published.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
