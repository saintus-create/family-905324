# WAT § 10750.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10750.10

---

## Text

This part is in addition to, and not a limitation on, the authority granted to a local agency pursuant to other provisions of law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 947, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
