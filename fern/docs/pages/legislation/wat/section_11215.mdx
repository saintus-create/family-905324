# WAT § 11215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 3. Contra Costa Conduit

**Citation:** WAT § 11215

---

## Text

The unit designated as the Contra Costa Conduit consists of a conduit or canal equipped with all necessary pumping plants and other works to convey water and extending westerly from a diversion point near Knightsen southerly of Suisun Bay to the vicinity of Martinez.
