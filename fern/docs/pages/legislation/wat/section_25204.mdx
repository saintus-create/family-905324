# WAT § 25204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25204

---

## Text

The board shall fix the date of each issue of bonds and may divide any issue into two or more divisions and fix different dates for the bonds of each respective division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
