# WAT § 75175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Benefit Assessment Improvement Districts > Article: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 75175

---

## Text

The formation petition, plans, specifications, estimate, and statement and assessment shall be filed in the office of the district and shall be subject to the inspection of all parties interested.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
