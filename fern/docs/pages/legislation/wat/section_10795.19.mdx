# WAT § 10795.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.78. LOCAL GROUNDWATER MANAGEMENT ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2000 > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10795.19

---

## Text

A local public agency receiving a grant under this part shall submit to the department copies of all data collected pursuant to the grant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 708, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
