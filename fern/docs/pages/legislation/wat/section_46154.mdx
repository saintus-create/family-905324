# WAT § 46154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 1. Commissioners

**Citation:** WAT § 46154

---

## Text

Compensation to the commissioners shall be paid out of the funds of the district, which may be included in any bond issue authorized for the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 106. Effective January 1, 1993.)
