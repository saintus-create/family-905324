# WAT § 37202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 7.5. ALTERNATIVE PROVISIONS FOR LEVY, COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF DISTRICT ASSESSMENTS BY THE COUNTY > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37202

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms used in this part shall mean:

(a) “Board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of each county within which a district is located.

(b) “Bond” or “bonds” mean a general obligation bond or bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1301.)
