# WAT § 14916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 23. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act > Article: 2. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14916

---

## Text

Purchase agreements entered into pursuant to this chapter may provide for the return of the property, that is the subject of the purchase, to irrigated agricultural use if affordable technological solutions to the drainage and environmental problems are identified and implemented.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 959, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993. Section operative July 1, 1993, pursuant to Section 14920.)
