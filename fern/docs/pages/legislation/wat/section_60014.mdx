# WAT § 60014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60014

---

## Text

“Fiscal year” means the period commencing on July 1st of one calendar year and ending on June 30th of the calendar year immediately following.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
