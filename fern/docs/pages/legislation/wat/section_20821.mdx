# WAT § 20821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 2. Reference to Department

**Citation:** WAT § 20821

---

## Text

Upon the adoption of the preliminary formation resolution the board of supervisors shall send a copy of the resolution to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
