# WAT § 20937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20937

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken by any party to an election contest within 30 days from entry of judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
