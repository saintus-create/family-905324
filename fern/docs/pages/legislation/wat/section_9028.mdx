# WAT § 9028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 3. Exchange of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9028

---

## Text

When any such exchange has been effected, the board of trustees of the reclamation district shall notify the county treasurer of the county in which the land, or the largest portion thereof, is situated, of the exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
