# WAT § 25354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 7. Bond Cancellation

**Citation:** WAT § 25354

---

## Text

Any bonds and coupons so canceled shall be destroyed under the direction of the board.
