# WAT § 55524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 55524

## Text

The bonds shall be issued in such denomination or denominations as the board may determine.
