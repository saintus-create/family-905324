# WAT § 26082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 1. Date and Penalties for Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 26082

---

## Text

The provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to pleadings, proofs, and trials are applicable to proceedings to enforce collection of assessments.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
