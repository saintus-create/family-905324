# WAT § 51040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 2. Districts Outside the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51040

---

## Text

If no part of a district is within the boundaries of the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District, the board shall report each plan for reclamation of district lands to the board of supervisors by filing with the county clerk two copies thereof with an estimate of the cost of the contemplated works, including incidental expenses, maintenance and repairs, necessary for the reclamation of district lands pursuant to the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
