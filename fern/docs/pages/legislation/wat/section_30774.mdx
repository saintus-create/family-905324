# WAT § 30774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 3. Preparation for Election

**Citation:** WAT § 30774

---

## Text

All ballots printed shall be precisely on the same size, quality, tint of paper, kind of type, and color of ink so that without the number it would be impossible to distinguish one ballot from another.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
