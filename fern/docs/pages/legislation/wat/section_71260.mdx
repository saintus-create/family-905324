# WAT § 71260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1.5. Three Valleys Municipal Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 71260

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to the Three Valleys Municipal Water District. Except as provided in this chapter, this division applies to the Three Valleys Municipal Water District.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 410, Sec. 1.)
