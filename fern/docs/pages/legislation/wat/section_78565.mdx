# WAT § 78565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 6. Delta Recreation Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78565

## Text

Any acquisition pursuant to this article shall be from willing sellers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
