# WAT § 11567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Construction of Project by the Department of Water Resources > Article: 3. Sequence of Units

**Citation:** WAT § 11567

---

## Text

The department shall ascertain and determine when the project or any unit thereof is completed and thereupon the project or unit shall be deemed completed for all purposes of this part.
