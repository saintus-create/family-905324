# WAT § 55533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 55533

---

## Text

Any bonds issued by the district may be subject to call and redemption prior to maturity at such times and prices and upon such other terms as the board may specify.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1015.)
