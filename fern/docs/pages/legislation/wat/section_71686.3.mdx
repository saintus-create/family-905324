# WAT § 71686.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 4. Water Replenishment Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 71686.3

---

## Text

The board may appoint a qualified registered engineer familiar with water problems as a hearing officer to conduct the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1725.)
