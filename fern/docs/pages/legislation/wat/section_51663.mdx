# WAT § 51663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 1. Sale After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51663

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall convey parcels purchased by him at a sale for the delinquency of a bonded assessment, free of encumbrance except the unpaid balance of the assessment and any other district assessment thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
