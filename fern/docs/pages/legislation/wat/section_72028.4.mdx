# WAT § 72028.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Apportionment of Extraordinary Costs of Water Delivery

**Citation:** WAT § 72028.4

---

## Text

When additional debt is imposed on improvement districts pursuant to this chapter, the board shall take action at the time and in the manner required by law to require the board of supervisors to levy, in addition to any other tax it levies, a tax upon the property within the affected improvement districts at a rate or rates fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 830.)
