# WAT § 31503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 1. Authorization of Improvements

**Citation:** WAT § 31503

---

## Text

The Improvement Bond Act of 1915 is also so applicable for use in conjunction with the Improvement Act of 1911 or with other improvement acts.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
