# WAT § 24766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 2. Modification of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24766

---

## Text

If a majority of the votes cast for and against the proposal is in favor of modifying the bonds or warrants, the proposal is approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
