# WAT § 9037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9037

---

## Text

If the amount is less than the amount to which the tract or owner would have been entitled, the difference shall be paid to the owner of such tract by the reclamation district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
