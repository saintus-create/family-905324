# WAT § 23977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 23977

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants shall bear interest at the rate fixed at the time of the levy of the improvement district assessment, and the interest may be made payable semiannually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
