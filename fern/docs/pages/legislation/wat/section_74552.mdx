# WAT § 74552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 74552

---

## Text

A district may take conveyances or other assurances for all property acquired by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
