# WAT § 1731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 1. Temporary Changes

**Citation:** WAT § 1731

---

## Text

Following the expiration of the temporary change period, all rights shall automatically revert to the original holder of the right without any action by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1145, Sec. 3.)
