# WAT § 74228.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 74228.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 44. Effective January 1, 2006.)
