# WAT § 22700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 3. Limitations

**Citation:** WAT § 22700

---

## Text

No contest of any thing or matter herein provided shall be made other than in the time and manner herein specified.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
