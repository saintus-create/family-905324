# WAT § 6393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 5. Complaints as to Unsafe Conditions

**Citation:** WAT § 6393

---

## Text

If, after an inspection is made on account of a complaint, the complaint is found by the department to have been without merit, any money deposited therefor shall be payable into the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
