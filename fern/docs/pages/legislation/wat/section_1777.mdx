# WAT § 1777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 2. Joint Occupancy and Use

**Citation:** WAT § 1777

---

## Text

The applicant shall be required to pay to the person owning the works a pro rata portion of the total cost of the old and the new works.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
