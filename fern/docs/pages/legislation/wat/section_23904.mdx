# WAT § 23904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 23904

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publishing a copy of the resolution of intention in a newspaper of general circulation published in each affected county, pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code, the first publication to be at least fourteen (14) days prior to the time fixed for the hearing. The notice shall also be given by posting a copy of the resolution in three public places within the proposed improvement district for at least fourteen (14) days prior to the time fixed for the hearing. No notice other than that required by this section need be given.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 580.)
