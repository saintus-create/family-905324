# WAT § 56051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 5. Preliminary Report and Survey

**Citation:** WAT § 56051

---

## Text

The engineer or engineers may, subject to the direction of the district board, employ such surveyors and others as may be necessary to prepare the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
