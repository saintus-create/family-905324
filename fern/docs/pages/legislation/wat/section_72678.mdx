# WAT § 72678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 72678

---

## Text

If the secretary certifies in his certificate that the petition for annexation is insufficient, the petition may be amended by filing a supplemental petition or petitions within 10 days of the date of such certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
