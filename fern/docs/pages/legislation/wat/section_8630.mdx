# WAT § 8630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 3. Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8630

---

## Text

As used in this article the term “levee” means any levee within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
