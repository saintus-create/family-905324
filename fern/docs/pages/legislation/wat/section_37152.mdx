# WAT § 37152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and its Termination

**Citation:** WAT § 37152

---

## Text

One of the duplicate certificates of redemption shall be given to the redemptioner.
