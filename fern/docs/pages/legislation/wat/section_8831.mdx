# WAT § 8831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8831

---

## Text

Any person interested in any land upon which any charge has been assessed, who is aggrieved by the decision of the board approving the assessment, may commence an action against the district in the superior court of the county in which the land or the greater part thereof is situated, to have the assessment upon such land modified or annulled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
