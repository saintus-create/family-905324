# WAT § 24020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 4. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 24020

---

## Text

All acts, proceedings, conclusions, and findings of fact, including the levy of an assessment, by a board of a district concerning an improvement district therein shall be conclusive except in an action or proceeding instituted within six months after the acts, proceedings, conclusions, or findings were had or made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
