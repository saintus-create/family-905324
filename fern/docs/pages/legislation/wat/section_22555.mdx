# WAT § 22555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 6. Water Leases for Mechanical Purposes

**Citation:** WAT § 22555

---

## Text

At the time and place appointed the board shall open in public the proposals to lease the use of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
