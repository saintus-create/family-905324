# WAT § 75168.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 7. Transfer of District Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 75168.3

---

## Text

To the right of or below the question on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 72. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
