# WAT § 43501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 43501

---

## Text

The board may pay for property and rights with bonds of the district upon such terms and conditions as it deems best.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
