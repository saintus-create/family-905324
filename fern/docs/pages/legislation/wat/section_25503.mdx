# WAT § 25503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25503

---

## Text

All of the land shall be assessed in accordance with Section 401 of the Revenue and Taxation Code as of 12:01 a.m. on the first day of March of that year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 818.)
