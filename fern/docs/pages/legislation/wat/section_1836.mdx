# WAT § 1836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 12. Enforcement of Water Rights > Article: 2. Cease and Desist Orders

**Citation:** WAT § 1836

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall preclude the board from issuing any order or taking any other action authorized pursuant to Sections 275 and 1052.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 933, Sec. 13.)
