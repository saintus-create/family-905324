# WAT § 51462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 9. Warrant Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51462

---

## Text

The board shall call and collect the assessment in the manner provided in this part in the amount necessary to pay the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
