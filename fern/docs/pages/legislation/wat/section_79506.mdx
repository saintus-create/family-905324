# WAT § 79506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79506

---

## Text

Every proposed activity to be financed pursuant to this division shall be in compliance with the California Environmental Quality Act (Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000)) of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
