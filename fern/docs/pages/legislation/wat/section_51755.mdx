# WAT § 51755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 4. Possession

**Citation:** WAT § 51755

---

## Text

The bonds or coupons purchased by the board shall be forthwith delivered to the county treasurer and canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
