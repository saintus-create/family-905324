# WAT § 44625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 2. Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 44625

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are applicable only to warrants payable on demand.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
