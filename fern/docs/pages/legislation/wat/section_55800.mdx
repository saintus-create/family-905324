# WAT § 55800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 55800

---

## Text

Any portion or portions of a county containing unincorporated territory, or containing the whole or any portion of one or more incorporated cities, and not included in a district, may be added to any district as prescribed in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
