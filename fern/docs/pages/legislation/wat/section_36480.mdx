# WAT § 36480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 36480

---

## Text

After the filing of the formation petition, the plans and specifications, the estimate of cost and statement of the proposed assessment, the board shall give notice of a hearing upon the petition, which said notice shall also state that the hearing is called to determine whether or not the special assessment should be levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
