# WAT § 22875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 3. Flood Protection

**Citation:** WAT § 22875

---

## Text

Any district may expend sums that seem necessary to its board for the protection of its canal system or of land from damage by flood and the overflow of rivers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
