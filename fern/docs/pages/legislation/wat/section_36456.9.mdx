# WAT § 36456.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 36456.9

---

## Text

All words relating to municipal officers and matters refer to the corresponding officers of water districts and matters under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 268.)
