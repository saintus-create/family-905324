# WAT § 55031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Applicability of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 55031

## Text

The provisions of this division shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes thereof.
