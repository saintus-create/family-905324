# WAT § 10604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.5. SOUTH FORK AMERICAN RIVER > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10604

---

## Text

The department shall undertake cooperative studies pursuant to this part for the following:

(a) The Georgetown Divide Public Utility District and the South Fork of the American River between Chili Bar Reservoir and Salmon Falls Bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1472, Sec. 1.)
