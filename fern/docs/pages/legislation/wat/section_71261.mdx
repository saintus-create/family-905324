# WAT § 71261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1.5. Three Valleys Municipal Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 71261

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, the Board of Directors of the Three Valleys Municipal Water District shall consist of seven directors. Each director shall be a resident of the division from which he or she is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 410, Sec. 1.)
