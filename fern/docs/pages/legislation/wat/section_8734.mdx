# WAT § 8734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8734

---

## Text

The board may require the applicant to pay all or part of the expenses of any outside hearing, if held at the request of the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
