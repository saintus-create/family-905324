# WAT § 50780.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Reclamation District No. 1000

**Citation:** WAT § 50780.2

---

## Text

“District,” for purposes of this chapter, means the Reclamation District No. 1000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
