# WAT § 51637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51637

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall not accept any bid for any parcel less than the aggregate sum then due on the installment thereon, with penalty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
