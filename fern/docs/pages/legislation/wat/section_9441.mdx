# WAT § 9441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 4. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 9441

---

## Text

The bonds shall not be sold for less than 95 per cent of their face value and the accrued interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
