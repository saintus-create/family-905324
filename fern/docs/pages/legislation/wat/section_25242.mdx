# WAT § 25242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 2. Payment Solely from Revenue

**Citation:** WAT § 25242

---

## Text

If any or all of the principal of any bonds is made payable only from revenue, the board shall cause a brief statement of the limitations upon the payment of principal or portion thereof to be set forth in the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
