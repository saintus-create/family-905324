# WAT § 22172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6. Sewage Disposal

**Citation:** WAT § 22172

## Text

The ballots shall include instructions to voters that are consistent with the requirements of Section 13204 of the Elections Code.
