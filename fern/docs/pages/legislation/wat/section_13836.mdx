# WAT § 13836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.2. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1984

**Citation:** WAT § 13836

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services, after public notice and hearing and with the advice of the department, shall, from time to time, establish a priority list of suppliers to be considered for financing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 378, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 28 at the November 6, 1984, election.)
