# WAT § 79511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 2. The Water Security, Clean Drinking Water, Coastal and Beach Protection Fund of 2002

**Citation:** WAT § 79511

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund shall be used only for the purposes and in the amounts set forth in this division and for no other purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
