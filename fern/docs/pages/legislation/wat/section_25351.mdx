# WAT § 25351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 7. Bond Cancellation

**Citation:** WAT § 25351

---

## Text

Whenever a resolution to cancel bonds is offered, its consideration shall be postponed to a date fixed by the board which is subsequent to final publication of the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
