# WAT § 22088.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22088.5

---

## Text

The possession or use of water when it has been so denied by the watermaster is prima facie evidence of the guilt of the person using it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
