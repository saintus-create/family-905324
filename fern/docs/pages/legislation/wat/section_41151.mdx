# WAT § 41151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redivision and Establishing of Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41151

---

## Text

The board may at any time, but not less than 60 days before an election to be held in the district, change the boundaries of any election precinct. The changes shall be entered upon the minutes of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
