# WAT § 32603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 8.2. Coachella Valley Water District > Chapter: 3. Inclusion in Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 32603

---

## Text

(a) The use of nonpotable water, including recycled water, in accordance with this part is subject to all applicable state regulation.

(b) This part only applies to a use of water within the district’s service area that is not the subject of a determination pursuant to Article 7 (commencing with Section 13550) of Chapter 7 of Division 7.

(c) This part is in addition to, and not a limitation upon, any powers of a public agency or a court to prevent the waste or unreasonable use of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 671, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
