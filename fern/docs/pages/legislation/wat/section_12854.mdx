# WAT § 12854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Watershed Protection Flood Prevention Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 12854

## Text

“Local organizations” means any county, city, state agency or public district.
