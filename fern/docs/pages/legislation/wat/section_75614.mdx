# WAT § 75614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 4. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 75614

---

## Text

Whenever any water-producing facility in an affected zone is permanently abandoned, the operator thereof shall give written notice of such abandonment to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
