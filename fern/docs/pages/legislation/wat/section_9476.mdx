# WAT § 9476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9476

---

## Text

The State Treasurer shall pay the warrants or make delivery of the bonds as provided in this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
