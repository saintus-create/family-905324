# WAT § 25035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. General Refunding Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 25035

---

## Text

Any district may issue refunding bonds for the purpose of refunding any or all of the outstanding bonds and warrants of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1943, Ch. 684.)
