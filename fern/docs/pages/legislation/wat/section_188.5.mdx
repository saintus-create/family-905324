# WAT § 188.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 3. State Water Resources Control Board

**Citation:** WAT § 188.5

---

## Text

The board shall publish biennial progress reports relating to the activities of the board and California regional water quality control boards.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
