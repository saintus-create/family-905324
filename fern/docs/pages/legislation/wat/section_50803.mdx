# WAT § 50803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Special Elections > Article: 1. Calling

**Citation:** WAT § 50803

---

## Text

When a special election is called to determine whether bonds of the district shall be issued, the notice shall specify the time and place of holding the election, the aggregate face value of bonds proposed to be issued and the names of three landowners or legal representatives to act as an election board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1763.)
