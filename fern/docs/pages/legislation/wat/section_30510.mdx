# WAT § 30510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30510

---

## Text

The oath of office may be taken before the secretary, any member of the board of directors, or any officer authorized by law to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
