# WAT § 12875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Watershed Protection Flood Prevention Projects > Article: 5. Procedure for Allocation

**Citation:** WAT § 12875

---

## Text

The department may refuse to make any order for the reallocation of any of the funds if the provisions of this chapter are violated.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 230.8. Effective October 12, 2001.)
