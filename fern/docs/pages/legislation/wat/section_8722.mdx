# WAT § 8722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8722

---

## Text

The board may at any time change or alter the plans or specifications for work undertaken upon its own initiative.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
