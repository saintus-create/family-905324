# WAT § 71127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 71127

---

## Text

When the county elections official has completed the examination of the formation petition, he or she shall attach to it the certificate, properly dated, showing the result of the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 196. Effective January 1, 2003.)
