# WAT § 76030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Dissolution > Article: 1. Petition and Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 76030

---

## Text

A district may be dissolved by the board of supervisors of the principal county in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
