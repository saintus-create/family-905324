# WAT § 23501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23501

---

## Text

As used in this part, “federal reclamation law” shall mean the Federal Reclamation Act of June 17, 1902 (32 Stats. 388), and all acts amendatory thereof or supplemental thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
