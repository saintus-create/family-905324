# WAT § 41362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 3. Conduct at Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41362

---

## Text

Any member of the election board may administer and certify oaths required to be administered during the progress of an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
