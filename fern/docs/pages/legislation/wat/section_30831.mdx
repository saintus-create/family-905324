# WAT § 30831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Initiative; Referendum; Recall

**Citation:** WAT § 30831

---

## Text

Ordinances may be subject to referendum in accordance with Article 2 (commencing with Section 9140) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 240. Effective January 1, 1995.)
