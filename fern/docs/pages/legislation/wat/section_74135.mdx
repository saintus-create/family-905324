# WAT § 74135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74135

---

## Text

Either party to the record may appeal to the Supreme Court of this state within 30 days from entry of judgment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
