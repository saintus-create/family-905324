# WAT § 2010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 2. Report of Referee

**Citation:** WAT § 2010

---

## Text

The board may base its report solely upon its own investigations or in addition thereto may hold hearings and take testimony.
