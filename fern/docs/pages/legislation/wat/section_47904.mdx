# WAT § 47904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 13. Alternate Provisions for Collection and Enforcement of District Assessments by the County > Article: 1. Collection of Assessments by the County

**Citation:** WAT § 47904

---

## Text

On or before the 15th day of July, the board shall designate and determine by resolution the percentage of any assessment theretofore levied that shall be called and collected pursuant to this chapter.
