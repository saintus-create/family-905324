# WAT § 79504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79504

---

## Text

It is the intent of the people that investment of public funds pursuant to this division should result in public benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
