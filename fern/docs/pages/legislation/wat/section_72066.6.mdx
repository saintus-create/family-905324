# WAT § 72066.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 72066.6

---

## Text

“Service supplier” shall mean any person required to collect a tax under the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
