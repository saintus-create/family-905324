# WAT § 24658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 3. Registration of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24658

---

## Text

All warrants designated in the notice shall cease to draw interest at the time of the first publication or posting of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
