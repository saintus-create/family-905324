# WAT § 51631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51631

---

## Text

If no newspaper of general circulation is published in the county where the delinquent land is situated, publication shall be made in a newspaper published in an adjoining county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
