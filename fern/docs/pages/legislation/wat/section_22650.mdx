# WAT § 22650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 22650

---

## Text

A district may commence and maintain any actions and proceedings to carry out its purposes or protect its interests and may defend in any action or proceeding brought against it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
