# WAT § 20722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 20722

---

## Text

The petition may consist of any number of separate instruments, which shall be duplicates except as to signatures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
