# WAT § 39030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39030

---

## Text

“Acknowledged” means acknowledged and certified in the same form and manner as is required for recording of conveyances of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
