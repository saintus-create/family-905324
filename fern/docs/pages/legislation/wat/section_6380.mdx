# WAT § 6380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 4. Dams Completed Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6380

---

## Text

The department shall make inspections at State expense of all dams in the State completed prior to August 14, 1929.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
