# WAT § 8840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8840

---

## Text

The map when approved by the board, shall be certified by the secretary of the board as having been so approved.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
