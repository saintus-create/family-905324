# WAT § 51544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51544

---

## Text

The principal and interest of any operation and maintenance assessment levy may be paid in lawful money of the United States or approved warrants of the district payable from such assessment levy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
