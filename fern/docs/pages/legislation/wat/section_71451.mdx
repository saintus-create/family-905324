# WAT § 71451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 71451

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this division, the provisions of the Elections Code, so far as they may be applicable, shall govern all district elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
