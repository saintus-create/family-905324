# WAT § 25038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. General Refunding Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 25038

## Text

The board shall call an election for the purpose of authorizing the issuance of the refunding bonds.
