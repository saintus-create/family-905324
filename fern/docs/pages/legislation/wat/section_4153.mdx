# WAT § 4153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Distribution of Water in Service Areas > Article: 1. Procedure of Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 4153

---

## Text

The notice is legal notice to all persons interested in the diversion and distribution of the water of the conduit or reservoir.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
