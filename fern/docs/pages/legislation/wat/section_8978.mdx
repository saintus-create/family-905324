# WAT § 8978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8978

---

## Text

All such exchanges or sales of warrants shall be effected without loss to the State.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
