# WAT § 35045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 3. Alternative Method of Conducting Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 35045

---

## Text

Each petition shall be signed and dated by registered voters who reside within the district and shall show their residence address.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 643.)
