# WAT § 72000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 72000

---

## Text

Whenever the board deems it necessary to form an improvement district of a portion of the district for a purpose other than the incurring of bonded indebtedness, the board shall by resolution declare that it intends to form an improvement district of a portion of the district which in its opinion will be benefited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
