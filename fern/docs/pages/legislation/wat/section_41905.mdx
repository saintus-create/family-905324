# WAT § 41905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Contest of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41905

---

## Text

The appeal shall be heard and determined by the Supreme Court within 60 days from the time of the filing of the notice of appeal.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
