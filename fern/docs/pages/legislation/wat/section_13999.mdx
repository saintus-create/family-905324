# WAT § 13999

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 15. Clean Water Bond Law of 1984

**Citation:** WAT § 13999

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Clean Water Bond Law of 1984.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 377, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 25 at the November 6, 1984, election.)
