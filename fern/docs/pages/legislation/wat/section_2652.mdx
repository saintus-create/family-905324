# WAT § 2652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 7. Hearing of Objections

**Citation:** WAT § 2652

---

## Text

The costs of taking testimony at a hearing on an objection shall be borne by the parties thereto as follows: each party shall pay for the direct examination of his own witness and the cross-examination of opponent’s witness, and the parties shall share equally for that part of the examination directed by the representative of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
