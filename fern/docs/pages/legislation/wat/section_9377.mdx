# WAT § 9377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 3. Voting Lists and Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 9377

---

## Text

The ballot cast at the election shall contain the words “Bonds—Yes,” or the words “Bonds—No,” and also the signature of the person casting the ballot, with the number of votes cast by the voter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
