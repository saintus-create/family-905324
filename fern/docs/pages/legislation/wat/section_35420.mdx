# WAT § 35420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 35420

---

## Text

All water distributed for irrigation purposes, except as otherwise provided in this article, shall be apportioned ratably to each holder of title to land upon the basis of the ratio which the last assessment against his land for district purposes bears to the whole sum assessed in the district for district purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
