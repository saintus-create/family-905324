# WAT § 30776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 3. Preparation for Election

**Citation:** WAT § 30776

---

## Text

A column may be provided on the right-hand side for questions permitted by this division to be voted upon at water district elections.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
