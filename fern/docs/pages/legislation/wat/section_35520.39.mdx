# WAT § 35520.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.2. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Lost Hills Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35520.39

---

## Text

Whenever any tolls or charges for the use of drainage facilities or for other services rendered by the district provided for in this chapter have been fixed by the board, they may be made payable in advance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 57, Sec. 1. Effective May 31, 1983.)
