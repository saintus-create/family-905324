# WAT § 30542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 3. Other Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 30542

---

## Text

The general manager, secretary, treasurer, and auditor shall each receive the compensation determined by the board and shall serve at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 454, Sec. 5.)
