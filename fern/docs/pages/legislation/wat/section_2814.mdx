# WAT § 2814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2814

---

## Text

The clerk shall post notice of the time for hearing the proof at the courthouse where the hearing is to be held and at least 10 days prior to the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
