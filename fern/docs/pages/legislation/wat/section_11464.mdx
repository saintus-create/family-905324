# WAT § 11464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 3. Limitation of Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 11464

---

## Text

No water right, reservoir, conduit, or facility for the generation, production, transmission, or distribution of electric power, acquired by the department shall ever be sold, granted, or conveyed by the department so that the department thereby is divested of the title to and ownership of it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
