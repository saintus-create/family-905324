# WAT § 36212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 4. Destruction and Redemption of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36212

---

## Text

If at the time the vote cast at the election is canvassed a two-thirds majority of the votes cast upon the proposition favors the destruction of the unsold general obligation bonds, the president, in the presence of a majority of the members of the board, shall destroy the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
