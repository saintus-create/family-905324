# WAT § 24479

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24479

---

## Text

Surplus funds on hand and available for payment into a refunding bond sinking fund may on order of the board be paid into it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
