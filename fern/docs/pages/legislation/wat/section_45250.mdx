# WAT § 45250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 1. Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 45250

---

## Text

The notice of a special bond election shall state the aggregate face value of bonds proposed to be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
