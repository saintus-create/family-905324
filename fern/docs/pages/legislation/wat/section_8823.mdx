# WAT § 8823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8823

---

## Text

The notice of hearing in each county shall be filed with the county treasurer and published once a week for four weeks in some newspaper published in the affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
