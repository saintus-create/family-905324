# WAT § 50007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50007

---

## Text

“Parcel” means a tract of land within a district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
