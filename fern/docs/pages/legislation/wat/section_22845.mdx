# WAT § 22845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 2. Internal Organization

**Citation:** WAT § 22845

---

## Text

The treasurer, assessor, and collector shall each qualify by taking and filing with the secretary the oath of office and shall give a bond to the district as required by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 33.)
