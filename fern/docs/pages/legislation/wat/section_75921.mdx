# WAT § 75921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 5. Recordation of Order and Petition and Filing of Certificate

**Citation:** WAT § 75921

---

## Text

Upon the filing of the copies of the order pursuant to Section 75920, the secretary shall record the petition for inclusion of lands in the minutes of the board; and such minutes, or a certified copy thereof, shall be admissible in evidence with the same effect as the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
