# WAT § 22086.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22086.5

---

## Text

The notice is legal notice to all persons interested in the diversion and distribution of the water of the conduit or reservoir.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
