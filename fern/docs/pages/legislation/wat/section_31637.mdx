# WAT § 31637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Coachella Valley Water District Water Replenishment Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 31637

---

## Text

Should any producer, subject to a replenishment assessment, knowingly fail to file the water-production statement, such producer shall, in addition to interest as provided in Section 31636.5, become liable to the district for a penalty of one hundred fifty dollars ($150).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 498.)
