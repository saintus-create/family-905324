# WAT § 30526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30526

---

## Text

Except where action is taken by the unanimous vote of all directors present and voting, the ayes and noes shall be taken upon the passage of all ordinances, resolutions, or motions and entered upon the minutes of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
