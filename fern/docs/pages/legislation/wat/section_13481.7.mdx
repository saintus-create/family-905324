# WAT § 13481.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.5. State Water Pollution Control Revolving Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 13481.7

---

## Text

Subject to all applicable constitutional restrictions, a municipality may borrow money and incur indebtedness pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 513, Sec. 8. (SB 244) Effective January 1, 2012.)
