# WAT § 30018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 30018

---

## Text

“Assessee” means the person to whom property is assessed.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
