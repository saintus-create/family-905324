# WAT § 46329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 8. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46329

---

## Text

After the supplementary assessment rolls have been filed the assessment is an assessment upon each of the smaller parcels in accordance with the reapportionment and not an assessment upon the tract as a whole.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
