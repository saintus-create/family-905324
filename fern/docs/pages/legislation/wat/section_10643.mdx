# WAT § 10643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Urban Water Management Plans > Article: 3. Adoption and Implementation of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 10643

---

## Text

An urban water supplier shall implement its plan adopted pursuant to this chapter in accordance with the schedule set forth in its plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1.)
