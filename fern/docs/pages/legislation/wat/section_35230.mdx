# WAT § 35230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Method of Election for the Rancho California Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35230

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to the manner in which elections are conducted in the Rancho California Water District. For the purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Rancho California Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 228, Sec. 1.)
