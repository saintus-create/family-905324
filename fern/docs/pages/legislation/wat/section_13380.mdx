# WAT § 13380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5.5. Compliance With the Provisions of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act as Amended in 1972

**Citation:** WAT § 13380

---

## Text

Any waste discharge requirements or dredged or fill material permits adopted under this chapter shall be reviewed at least every five years and, if appropriate, revised.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 746.)
