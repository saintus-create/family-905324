# WAT § 8973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8973

---

## Text

The sum of the substituted warrants shall equal the principal amount of the original warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
