# WAT § 12000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 4. CO-OPERATION AND CONTRACTS BETWEEN PRIVATE ENTITIES AND THE UNITED STATES > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** WAT § 12000

---

## Text

As used in this part, “person” means any person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, or company, but not including any public corporation or other public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1697.)
