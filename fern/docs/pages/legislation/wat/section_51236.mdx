# WAT § 51236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51236

---

## Text

The assessment commissioners shall assess the estimated cost of the reclamation plan upon the district land in the manner provided in Section 51231.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
