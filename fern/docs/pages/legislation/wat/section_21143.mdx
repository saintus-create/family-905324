# WAT § 21143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 3. Official Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 21143

---

## Text

All bonds shall be in the form prescribed for the official bonds of county officers, and the premiums may be paid by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
