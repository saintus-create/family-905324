# WAT § 22076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 22076

---

## Text

A district may do any act in order to put to any beneficial use any water under its control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
