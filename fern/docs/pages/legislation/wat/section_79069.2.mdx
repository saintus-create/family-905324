# WAT § 79069.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 5. Flood Protection Program > Article: 9. Arroyo Pasajero Watershed Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79069.2

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, the definitions set forth in this section govern construction of this article.

(a) “Subaccount” means the Arroyo Pasajero Watershed Subaccount created pursuant to Section 79069.4.

(b) “Watershed” means the Arroyo Pasajero Watershed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
