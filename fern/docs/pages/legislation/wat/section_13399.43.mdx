# WAT § 13399.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5.9. The Storm Water Enforcement Act of 1998

**Citation:** WAT § 13399.43

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “NPDES permit” means a permit issued under the national pollutant discharge elimination system program in accordance with the Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C.A. Sec. 1251 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 998, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
