# WAT § 52173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52173

---

## Text

The order calling bonds shall specify the bonds to be called by number, maturity or such other designation as may be provided by the board.
