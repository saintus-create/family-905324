# WAT § 55407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Use of the Improvement Act of 1911 > Article: 2. Terms and Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 55407

---

## Text

“Right of way” means and includes any parcel of land through which a right of way has been granted to the county waterworks district for any purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 452.)
