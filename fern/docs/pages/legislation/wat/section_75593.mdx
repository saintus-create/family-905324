# WAT § 75593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 3. Levy

**Citation:** WAT § 75593

---

## Text

Different rates may be established in different zones; provided, however, that in each zone the rate for agricultural water shall be fixed and uniform and the rate for water other than agricultural water shall be fixed and uniform.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
