# WAT § 24604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 1. Payment of Claims

**Citation:** WAT § 24604

---

## Text

The verification shall state in substance all of the following:

(a) The money claimed was actually expended or the mileage stated was actually traveled for the district in pursuance of the duties of the officer or employee presenting the claim.

(b) The claim is presented in good faith and has not been previously paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
