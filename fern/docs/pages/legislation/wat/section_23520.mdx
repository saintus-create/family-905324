# WAT § 23520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23520

---

## Text

After the filing of the formation petition and the estimate of cost and the statement of the estimated rate of ad valorem tax, the board shall give notice of a hearing on the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
