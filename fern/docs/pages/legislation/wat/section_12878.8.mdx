# WAT § 12878.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 1. Formation of Maintenance Areas

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.8

---

## Text

Any maintenance area may be divided into zones when it is determined by the investigation that the benefit to the land in the area is not uniform throughout the area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
