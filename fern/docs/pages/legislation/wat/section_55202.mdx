# WAT § 55202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Formation of Zones With Formation of District

**Citation:** WAT § 55202

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may direct that notice of hearing, hearing, notice of election, or election upon the formation and zone petitions shall be consolidated and combined into one notice of hearing, one hearing, one notice of election, or one election; except that the requirements of this division with respect to the proceedings upon each of the petitions shall be complied with.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
