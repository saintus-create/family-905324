# WAT § 14073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 17. Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14073

---

## Text

The bonds may be refunded in accordance with Article 6 (commencing with Section 16780) of the State General Obligation Bond Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 47, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 83 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
