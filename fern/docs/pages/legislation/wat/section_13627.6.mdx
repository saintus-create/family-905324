# WAT § 13627.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 9. Waste Water Treatment Plant Classification and Operator Certification

**Citation:** WAT § 13627.6

---

## Text

The state board, by regulation, shall prescribe the procedures and requirements for designation of a person as the chief plant operator and the duties that a chief plant operator is required to perform.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 305, Sec. 26. (AB 2890) Effective January 1, 2017.)
