# WAT § 26672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Collection, Payment in Lieu of Assessment and Distribution of Water by Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26672

---

## Text

The water made available by the district to the public agency may be used upon the lands covered by such contract for such irrigation, municipal, domestic or other uses as the district and the public agency may agree upon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 603.)
