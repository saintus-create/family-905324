# WAT § 36828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 36828

---

## Text

The tax collector shall mark the date of payment in the assessment book opposite the name of the assessee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
