# WAT § 37922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Groundwater Management Authority

**Citation:** WAT § 37922

---

## Text

Any person who intentionally violates any provision of this act or any district ordinance shall be guilty of an infraction and may be required to pay a fine to the district not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 350, Sec. 1. (AB 2453) Effective January 1, 2015.)
