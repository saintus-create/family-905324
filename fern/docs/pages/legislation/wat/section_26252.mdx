# WAT § 26252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 2. Partial Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26252

---

## Text

If the portion to be separately redeemed does not have a separate valuation shown on the assessment book, the collector shall submit the description of the portion to the assessor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
