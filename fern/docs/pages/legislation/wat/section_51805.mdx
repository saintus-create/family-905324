# WAT § 51805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51805

---

## Text

At the hearing the board of supervisors shall hear any evidence offered in support of the written objections and may approve, reject, modify or amend the request in any particular.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
