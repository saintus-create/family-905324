# WAT § 78530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 2. Central Valley Project Improvement Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78530

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created in the account the Central Valley Project Improvement Subaccount.

(b) For the purposes of this article, “subaccount” means the Central Valley Project Improvement Subaccount created by subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
