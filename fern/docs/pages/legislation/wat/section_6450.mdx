# WAT § 6450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 9. Dams Under Construction Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6450

---

## Text

Any dam which the department finds was not 90 percent constructed on August 14, 1929 shall be subject to the same provisions as a dam commenced after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
