# WAT § 8982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8982

---

## Text

Upon payment of the warrants the proceeds thereof shall be returned into the fund out of which the purchase or investment was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
