# WAT § 48350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2.6. Consolidation

**Citation:** WAT § 48350

---

## Text

A district may be consolidated in the same manner as is provided for the consolidation of county water districts in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 32650) of Part 8 of Division 12.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 48300 (as added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 163) by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2132.)
