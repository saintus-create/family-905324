# WAT § 13522.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Water Reclamation > Article: 4. Regulation of Reclamation

**Citation:** WAT § 13522.6

---

## Text

Any person failing to furnish a report under Section 13522.5 when so requested by a regional board is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 918.)
