# WAT § 8776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 2. Assessors and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 8776

---

## Text

The assessors shall be disinterested persons, and shall have no interest in any real estate within the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
