# WAT § 10750.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10750.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this part requires a local agency overlying a groundwater basin to adopt or implement a groundwater management plan or groundwater management program pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 947, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
