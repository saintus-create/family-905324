# WAT § 51728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51728

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall execute a deed to the purchaser upon completion of the sale conveying the subdivided parcel free of encumbrances, except the unpaid balance of the reapportioned assessment and any other assessments of the district thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
