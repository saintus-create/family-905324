# WAT § 22475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 3. Power Lines on Public Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22475

---

## Text

There is granted to every district the right to construct and operate in a manner affording security for life and property electric light and power lines along, over, under, or upon any road or across any railway or conduit which the route of the lines intersects or runs along.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
