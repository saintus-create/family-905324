# WAT § 72013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearing and Adoption of Resolution of Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 72013

---

## Text

After the formation of the improvement district pursuant to this part, all taxes levied for the carrying out of its purpose shall be levied exclusively upon the taxable property in the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
