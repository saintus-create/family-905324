# WAT § 85202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 1. Delta Stewardship Council

**Citation:** WAT § 85202

---

## Text

Council members shall possess diverse expertise and reflect a statewide perspective. It is the intent of the Legislature that the council appointees have knowledge about the Delta.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 324, Sec. 1. (SB 650) Effective January 1, 2026.)
