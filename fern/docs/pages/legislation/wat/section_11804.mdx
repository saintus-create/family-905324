# WAT § 11804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 1. General Financial Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11804

---

## Text

The depositaries and security provided for or agreed upon shall be qualified and eligible in accordance with the requirements of law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
