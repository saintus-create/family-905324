# WAT § 72735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Dissolution of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 72735

---

## Text

When the ordinance declaring an improvement district dissolved becomes effective, the dissolution of such improvement district is complete.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
