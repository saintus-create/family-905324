# WAT § 8838

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8838

---

## Text

Each of the maps shall be inscribed and designated as “reclamation board assessment map No.____,” giving each map a distinctive number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
