# WAT § 21121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Consolidation and Segregation

**Citation:** WAT § 21121

---

## Text

The order of consolidation shall be made at least 120 days prior to a general election and shall take effect at said election.
