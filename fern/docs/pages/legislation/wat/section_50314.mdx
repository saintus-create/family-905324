# WAT § 50314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 2. Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 50314

---

## Text

If the notice is given by personal service, the service shall be made at least three days prior to the date fixed for the further hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
