# WAT § 52176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52176

---

## Text

The county treasurer, in his notice of call of assessment, shall specify the amount which is included for payment of principal of and interest on the bonds to be called and premium thereon.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
