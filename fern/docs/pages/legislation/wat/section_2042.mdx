# WAT § 2042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 3. Expenses of Reference

**Citation:** WAT § 2042

---

## Text

During the pendency of any reference, upon motion of the board and upon a hearing by the court after at least 10 days’ notice to the parties, the court may order such interim or partial payments of the expense to be made by the parties as it deems proper and equitable under the circumstances.
