# WAT § 25952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 2. Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 25952

---

## Text

The resolution shall be adopted at or prior to the time of the levy of any annual assessment it is to affect.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
