# WAT § 60163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Board Action

**Citation:** WAT § 60163

---

## Text

No ordinance, motion or resolution shall be passed or become effective without the affirmative vote of a majority of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
