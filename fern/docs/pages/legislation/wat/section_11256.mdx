# WAT § 11256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 9.2. Los Banos Grandes Reservoir

**Citation:** WAT § 11256

---

## Text

In conducting the feasibility investigations for the Los Banos Grandes Reservoir, the department shall consider the impact of the project on drainage and groundwater lands in the adjacent farm areas, including making a study of the occurrence and distribution of selenium in the drain waters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1656, Sec. 1.)
