# WAT § 23204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 2. Contracts With United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23204

---

## Text

Any property owned or acquired by a district may be conveyed by it to the United States in so far as the property may be needed for the construction or operation of works by the United States for the benefit of the district pursuant to the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
