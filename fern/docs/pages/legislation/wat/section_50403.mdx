# WAT § 50403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Districts Formed to Operate Without a Board

**Citation:** WAT § 50403

---

## Text

The presentation of a petition for formation of a district to operate with a board is an abandonment of the rights, immunities, and privileges conferred upon the owners or their predecessors in interest in the land by the creation of the original district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
