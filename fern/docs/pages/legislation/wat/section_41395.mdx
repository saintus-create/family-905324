# WAT § 41395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 4. Returns of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 41395

---

## Text

No list, tally paper, or certificate returned from any election shall be set aside or rejected for want of form if it can be satisfactorily understood.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
