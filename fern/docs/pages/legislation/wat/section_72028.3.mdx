# WAT § 72028.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Apportionment of Extraordinary Costs of Water Delivery

**Citation:** WAT § 72028.3

---

## Text

If the majority of the registered voters in all of the combined improvement districts in which the election is held voting on the proposition vote to impose the additional debt on the improvement districts, it shall be imposed; otherwise it shall not.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 830.)
