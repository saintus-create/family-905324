# WAT § 1352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Proceedings on Application > Article: 2. Action on Application

**Citation:** WAT § 1352

---

## Text

Upon failure of any party in interest to appear at a hearing or show good cause within five days thereafter for his failure, final action may be taken without further hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
