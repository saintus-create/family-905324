# WAT § 9471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9471

---

## Text

The bond fund of each assessment shall be held by the State Treasurer and shall be applied by him toward the payment of the bonds and coupons thereon based upon the assessment, as the bonds and coupons fall due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
