# WAT § 45804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 45804

---

## Text

If on the date fixed for redemption the district has provided funds available for the payment of the principal and interest of the bonds called, interest on them ceases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1137.)
