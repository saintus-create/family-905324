# WAT § 25091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Authorized Refunding Modification

**Citation:** WAT § 25091

---

## Text

The board shall call an election for the purpose of authorizing the modification of a refunding plan.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1226, Sec. 54.)
