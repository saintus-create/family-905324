# WAT § 21400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. District Office

**Citation:** WAT § 21400

---

## Text

The office of each district shall be established and kept at a fixed place, determined by its board, which place need not be within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
