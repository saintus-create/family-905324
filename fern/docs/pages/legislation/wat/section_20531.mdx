# WAT § 20531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20531

---

## Text

“Conduits” include canals, laterals, ditches, flumes, pipes, and their appurtenances.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
