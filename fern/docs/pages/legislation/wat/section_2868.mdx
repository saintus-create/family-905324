# WAT § 2868

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2868

---

## Text

If a party fails to pay the expenses apportioned to that party when due, the board may refer the matter for collection of the unpaid expenses pursuant to Section 1536.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 89. Effective January 1, 2004.)
