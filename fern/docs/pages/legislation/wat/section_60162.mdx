# WAT § 60162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Board Action

**Citation:** WAT § 60162

---

## Text

Resolutions and motions may be adopted by voice vote, but on demand of any member the roll shall be called.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
