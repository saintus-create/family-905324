# WAT § 11813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 2. Construction Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11813

---

## Text

All costs and expenses of the department, including interest due and payable, incurred in performing the duties prescribed in this part prior to and during the construction of the project and for a period of one year after completion thereof, shall be paid out of the construction fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
