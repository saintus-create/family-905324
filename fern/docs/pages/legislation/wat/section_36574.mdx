# WAT § 36574

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 2. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 36574

---

## Text

If the district is contained in more than one county the assessment book shall be prepared with a separate part in a separate volume for the land in each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
