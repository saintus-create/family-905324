# WAT § 35055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 3. Alternative Method of Conducting Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 35055

---

## Text

All directors thereafter elected shall be residents of the district, and shall be registered voters at the time of their election and during their term of office. Such directors need not be landowners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 643.)
