# WAT § 71070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion of Cities

**Citation:** WAT § 71070

---

## Text

As used in this article, “territory of a city” means the corporate area of the city and all territory as to which proceedings for annexation to the city have been commenced by a valid petition filed with the governing body of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
