# WAT § 463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 6. Water Reuse > Article: 3. Action by the Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 463

---

## Text

The department shall study and investigate the technology of the reuse of reclaimed water and further the development of the technology of the reclamation of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 724, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
