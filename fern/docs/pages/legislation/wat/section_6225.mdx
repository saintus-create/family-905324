# WAT § 6225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 2. Repairs, Alterations or Removals

**Citation:** WAT § 6225

---

## Text

Before commencing the repair, alteration, or removal of a dam or reservoir, including the alteration or removal of a dam or reservoir so that it no longer constitutes a dam or reservoir as defined in this part, the owner shall secure the written approval of the department, except as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
