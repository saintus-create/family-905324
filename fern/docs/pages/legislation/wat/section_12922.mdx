# WAT § 12922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.5. Protection of Ground Water Basins > Article: 3. Declaration of Policy

**Citation:** WAT § 12922

---

## Text

It is hereby declared that the people of the State have a primary interest in the correction and prevention of irreparable damage to, or impaired use of, the ground water basins of this State caused by critical conditions of overdraft, depletion, sea water intrusion or degraded water quality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1620.)
