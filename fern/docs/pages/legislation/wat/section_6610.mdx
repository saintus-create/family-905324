# WAT § 6610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Part: 3. Citizens Advisory Commission for Oroville Dam > Chapter: 10. Dams and Reservoirs Under State Supervision Through 1965 Revisions of Part 1 > Article: 3. Fees for Dams or Reservoirs Under Construction Before 1965 Revisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6610

---

## Text

The commission shall have the power to visit the dam, its site, and site personnel in furtherance of the goals of this part. The power of visitation specified in this section shall not extend to a situation where institutional security would be jeopardized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 509, Sec. 2. (SB 955) Effective January 1, 2019.)
