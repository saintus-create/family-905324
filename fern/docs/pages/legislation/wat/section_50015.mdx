# WAT § 50015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50015

---

## Text

“Legal representative” means an officer or other person or persons appointed to serve in that capacity by a landowner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 38, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
