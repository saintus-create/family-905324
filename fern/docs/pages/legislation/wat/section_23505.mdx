# WAT § 23505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23505

---

## Text

The petition may consist of any number of separate instruments, which shall be duplicates except as to signatures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
