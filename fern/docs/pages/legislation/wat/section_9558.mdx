# WAT § 9558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 2. Purchase and Sale of Land by the Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 9558

---

## Text

The board shall deposit the proceeds of the sale with the State Treasurer to the credit of the bond fund of the assessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
