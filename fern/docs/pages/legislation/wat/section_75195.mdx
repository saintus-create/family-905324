# WAT § 75195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Benefit Assessment Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75195

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing, if the board determines that the improvement district should be formed and assessments levied to pay the costs of the proposed improvement, it shall make a final order approving the formation petition, the improvement district assessment, and the apportionment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
