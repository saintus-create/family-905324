# WAT § 44904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 44904

---

## Text

Warrants payable at a future time or times may also be issued to obtain funds or property for any lawful purpose of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 92. Effective January 1, 1993.)
