# WAT § 12540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 12540

---

## Text

From a list of not less than two persons submitted to it by the commissioner, the board shall appoint an executive director, whose duties shall be as required by the commissioner and by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 232, Sec. 1.)
