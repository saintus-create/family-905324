# WAT § 51697

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 2. Sale After One Year After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51697

---

## Text

The proceeds of sale of parcels acquired by the county treasurer at delinquency sale for bonded assessments as trustee for the district, shall be deposited in the bond fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 804.)
