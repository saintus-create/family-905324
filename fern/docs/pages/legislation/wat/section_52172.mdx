# WAT § 52172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52172

---

## Text

Less than all of the bonds of any maturity may be called in inverse numerical order or by lot and, if called by lot, the board shall ascertain by a drawing the bonds to be called in such maturity by lot.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
