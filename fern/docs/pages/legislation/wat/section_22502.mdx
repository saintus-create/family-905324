# WAT § 22502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 4. Disposition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22502

---

## Text

All conveyances and leases of district property shall be executed by the secretary and president on behalf of the district in accordance with a resolution of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
