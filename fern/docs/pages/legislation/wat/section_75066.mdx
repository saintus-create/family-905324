# WAT § 75066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75066

---

## Text

If the bond election has been fairly held and conducted, no informality or omission to perform a prescribed duty shall be held to invalidate or affect the legality of any bonded debt authorized to be incurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
