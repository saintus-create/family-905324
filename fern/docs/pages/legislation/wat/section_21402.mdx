# WAT § 21402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. District Office

**Citation:** WAT § 21402

---

## Text

All records of the district shall be open to public inspection during the hours when the office of the district is open to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
