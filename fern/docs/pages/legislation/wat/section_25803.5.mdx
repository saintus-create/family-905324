# WAT § 25803.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 1. Regular Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25803.5

---

## Text

Annual ad valorem assessments of a distribution district shall be entered in the assessment book by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
