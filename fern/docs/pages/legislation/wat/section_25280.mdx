# WAT § 25280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 4. Allocation of Revenue to Reserve Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 25280

---

## Text

Any sources of revenue of any district may, by order of its board, be irrevocably allocated to a reserve fund established to pay the interest or principal of any bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1226, Sec. 59.)
