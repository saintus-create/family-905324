# WAT § 72683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 72683

---

## Text

The petition and notice shall also be posted in three public places in the improvement district and three public places in the area proposed to be annexed at least two weeks prior to the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
