# WAT § 8036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8036

---

## Text

The bonds shall be executed on the part of the city by the mayor or other executive, and the treasurer, and countersigned by the clerk of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
