# WAT § 79035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 5. Flood Protection Program > Article: 2.5. Flood Protection Corridor Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79035

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created in the account the Flood Protection Corridor Subaccount.

(b) For the purposes of this article, “subaccount” means the Flood Protection Corridor Subaccount created by subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
