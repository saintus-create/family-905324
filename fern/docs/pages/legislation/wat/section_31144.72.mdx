# WAT § 31144.72

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9.7. Malaga County Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31144.72

---

## Text

In addition to the other powers provided by law, the Malaga County Water District may levy and collect fees or charges in the manner provided for in this division for activities undertaken pursuant to Section 31144.71.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 580, Sec. 1.)
