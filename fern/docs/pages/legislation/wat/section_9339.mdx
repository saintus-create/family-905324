# WAT § 9339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9339

---

## Text

If the right of any person to vote as the owner of any such tract of land is disputed or challenged, the question of his right to vote shall be determined by the board of election after examining him under oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
