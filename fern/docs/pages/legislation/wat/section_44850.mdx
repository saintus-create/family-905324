# WAT § 44850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 4. Alternative Provisions for the Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 44850

---

## Text

This article provides an alternative procedure for the deposit of all, or any part of, proceeds of bond sales, proceeds of assessments and proceeds of sale of land and of all, or any part of, other funds of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 695.)
