# WAT § 78648.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 5. Clean Water and Water Recycling Program > Article: 6. Seawater Intrusion Control

**Citation:** WAT § 78648.16

---

## Text

Not more than 3 percent of the total amount deposited in the subaccount may be used to pay for both of the following:

(a) To pay the costs incurred in connection with the administration of this article.

(b) For the purposes of Section 78648.14.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
