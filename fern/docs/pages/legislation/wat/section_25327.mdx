# WAT § 25327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 6. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 25327

---

## Text

The board shall give notice of the sale by publication for at least three weeks in some newspaper published in the office county and in any other newspaper at its discretion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
