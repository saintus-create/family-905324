# WAT § 26278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 3. Deed on Termination of Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26278

---

## Text

If a collector’s deed is demanded by an assignee of a certificate of sale, the form of deed shall be amended by striking out the words “the purchaser” and inserting in lieu thereof substantially the following: “____ to whom the certificate of sale has been assigned.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
