# WAT § 75165.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 6. Alternative Provisions for Government of Special Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 75165.6

---

## Text

The special election shall be held and conducted, candidates nominated, the results declared and the returns canvassed in the same manner as the regular biennial election of officers of the water conservation district, insofar as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1651.)
