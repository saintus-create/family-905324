# WAT § 8565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8565

## Text

The board shall have a seal of such device as the board may adopt.
