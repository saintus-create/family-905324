# WAT § 74134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74134

---

## Text

The election contest shall be brought within 20 days after the canvass of the votes and declaration of the result by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
