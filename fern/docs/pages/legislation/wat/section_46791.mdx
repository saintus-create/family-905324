# WAT § 46791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 6. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 46791

---

## Text

The deed executed by the district shall convey the property free and clear of all liens and encumbrances, except as provided in this article for a deed by the county treasurer to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
