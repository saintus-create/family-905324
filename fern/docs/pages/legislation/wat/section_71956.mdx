# WAT § 71956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Sale of Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 71956

---

## Text

The principal and interest on the bonds shall be payable in lawful money of the United States at the office of the treasurer of the district or such other place or places as may be designated, or at either place or places at the option of the holder of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
