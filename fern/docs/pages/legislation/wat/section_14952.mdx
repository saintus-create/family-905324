# WAT § 14952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 24. Shellfish Protection Act of 1993

**Citation:** WAT § 14952

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, a commercial shellfish growing area is an area certified pursuant to Section 112170 of the Health and Safety Code in which shellfish are grown and harvested.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 452. Effective September 29, 1996.)
