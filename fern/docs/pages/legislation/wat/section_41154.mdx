# WAT § 41154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redivision and Establishing of Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41154

---

## Text

The board shall at its next regular meeting establish a convenient number of election precincts in each of the new divisions. No precinct shall include any land situate in more than one of the divisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
