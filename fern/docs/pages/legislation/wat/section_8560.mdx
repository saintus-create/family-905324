# WAT § 8560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8560

---

## Text

No action of the board shall be effective unless a quorum is present and the action is concurred in by a majority of all of the voting members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 275, Sec. 10. (AB 1165) Effective January 1, 2010.)
