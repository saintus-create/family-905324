# WAT § 21100.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualification and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 21100.7

---

## Text

The landownership requirements set forth in subdivision (a) of Section 21100 do not apply to the South Bay Irrigation District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 99, Sec. 1. (AB 348) Effective January 1, 2010.)
