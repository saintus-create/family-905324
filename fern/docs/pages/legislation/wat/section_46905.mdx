# WAT § 46905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Supplementary Assessments for Completion of Project > Article: 1. Levy

**Citation:** WAT § 46905

---

## Text

Upon completion of the supplementary assessment roll, the board shall file with the county treasurer of each affected county a copy of so much of the assessment roll as pertains to land within that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
