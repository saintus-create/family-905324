# WAT § 2817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2817

---

## Text

Appeals from any action of the court under this article may be taken by the board or any party in interest in the same manner and with the same effect as in the civil cases.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
