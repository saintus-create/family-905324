# WAT § 8656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 4. Lease and Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 8656

---

## Text

The Department of General Services upon request of the board may sell the excess land at a purchase price determined upon by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
