# WAT § 56052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 5. Preliminary Report and Survey

**Citation:** WAT § 56052

---

## Text

When the engineer’s report is filed, the district board shall examine it and may thereupon:

(a) Reject it and direct that a new report be prepared;

(b) Direct that changes be made in it; or

(c) If it complies with the provisions of this chapter and is satisfactory to the district board, it shall fix a time and place for hearing objections to the report and to doing all or any of the work referred to in the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
