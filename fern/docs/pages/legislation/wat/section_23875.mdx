# WAT § 23875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Further Assessments and Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 23875

---

## Text

If at any time it is desired to include additional land in an improvement district, a petition for inclusion signed by the holders of title to the land to be included may be filed with the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 536.)
