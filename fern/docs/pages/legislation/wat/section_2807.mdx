# WAT § 2807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2807

---

## Text

Time for completion may be extended from time to time by the court upon motion made prior to the expiration of the limit of time fixed therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
