# WAT § 25677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25677

---

## Text

If a majority of the votes cast at the election are “Assessment—Yes,” the district by its board shall levy an assessment in the amount stated in the order of submission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
