# WAT § 72703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 72703

---

## Text

A copy of the resolution proposing annexation shall also be posted in three public places within the improvement district and three public places in the area proposed to be annexed at least two weeks prior to the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
