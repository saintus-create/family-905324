# WAT § 25950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 2. Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 25950

---

## Text

The board may when it so desires and shall, when a written petition signed by a majority of the assessment payers in the district is filed requesting it, pass a resolution providing that thereafter annual assessments, including improvement district assessments or installments thereof but excepting completion assessments, shall be payable in two installments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
