# WAT § 71360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71360

---

## Text

The president and secretary, in addition to the duties imposed on them by law, shall perform such duties as may be imposed on them by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
