# WAT § 11750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 5. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 11750

---

## Text

Bonds may be issued and sold from time to time and in such amounts as may be deemed necessary in the judgment of the department to provide sufficient funds for the purposes authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 4. Effective June 6, 1991.)
