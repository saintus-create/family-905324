# WAT § 374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 3.4. Allocation-Based Conservation Water Pricing

**Citation:** WAT § 374

---

## Text

(a) Allocation-based conservation water pricing under this chapter may be used on an ongoing basis and shall not require any finding of emergency or other water shortage conditions.

(b) The authority granted in this chapter is in addition to any other authority that a public entity has to use rate structure design to foster the conservation of water.

(c) The imposition and revision of rates and charges by a public entity under this chapter shall be subject to the procedures otherwise required by law for the public entity’s water rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 610, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
