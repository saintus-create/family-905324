# WAT § 10750.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10750.6

---

## Text

Nothing in this part affects the authority of a local agency or a watermaster to manage groundwater pursuant to other provisions of law or a court order, judgment, or decree.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 947, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
