# WAT § 45803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 45803

---

## Text

The first publication of the redemption notice shall be not less than 30 days nor more than 90 days prior to the date fixed for redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1137.)
