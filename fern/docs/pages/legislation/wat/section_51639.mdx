# WAT § 51639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51639

---

## Text

Bonds or interest coupons tendered in payment for a parcel purchased at a delinquency sale, or the redemption of such a parcel, pursuant to this article shall be accepted at face value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
