# WAT § 12929.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.2

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Water Resources.

(b) “Board” means the State Water Resources Control Board.

(c) “Mono Lake Basin” means Mono Lake and its watershed.

(d) “Environmental Water Program” means the Environmental Water Program created pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 12929.20).

(e) “Mono Lake Committee” means the California nonprofit citizens organization formed to save Mono Lake.

(f) “Water Quality Program” means the Water Quality program created pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with Section 12929.30).
