# WAT § 39402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 1. Petitioners and Land

**Citation:** WAT § 39402

---

## Text

The land proposed to be formed into a district need not consist of contiguous parcels.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
