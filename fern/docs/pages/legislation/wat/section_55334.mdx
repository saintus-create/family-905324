# WAT § 55334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 55334

---

## Text

Any violation of a regulation or ordinance of a district is a misdemeanor punishable by fine not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000), or imprisonment not to exceed six months, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 415. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
