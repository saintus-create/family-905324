# WAT § 51727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51727

---

## Text

The balance of the purchase price may be paid in cash or, to the extent that a bonded assessment constitutes a part of the purchase price in cash or in any matured bonds and coupons issued on the reapportioned assessment taken at their face value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
