# WAT § 8718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8718

---

## Text

No levee, embankment or other structure within any by-pass or overflow channel adopted by the board shall be raised, widened, strengthened or altered without permission of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
