# WAT § 6265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 4. Approval of Applications

**Citation:** WAT § 6265

---

## Text

Actual construction shall be commenced within one year after date of approval, otherwise the approval becomes void.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
