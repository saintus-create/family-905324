# WAT § 35539.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.7. Provisions Pertaining only to Irvine Ranch Water District and Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35539.14

---

## Text

In order to carry out the powers and purposes granted under this chapter, the districts may exercise any of the powers otherwise granted to a district by this division to the extent those powers may be made applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 209, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
