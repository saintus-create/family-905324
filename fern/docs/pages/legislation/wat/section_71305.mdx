# WAT § 71305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71305

---

## Text

The board may create any necessary offices and establish and re-establish the powers, duties, and compensation of all officers and employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
