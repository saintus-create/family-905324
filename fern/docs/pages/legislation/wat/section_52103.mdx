# WAT § 52103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52103

---

## Text

Bonds shall be numbered consecutively in the order of their maturity.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
