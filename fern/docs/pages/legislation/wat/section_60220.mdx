# WAT § 60220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Purposes and Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 60220

---

## Text

A district may do any act necessary to replenish the ground water of said district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
