# WAT § 22335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 5. Supervision by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 22335

---

## Text

During the construction of any works of a district to be paid for out of the proceeds of any bond issue, the department shall have access to all plans, specifications, and records of the construction and shall from time to time make any investigations and reports to the board the department deems to be in the interest of the public or of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1993.)
