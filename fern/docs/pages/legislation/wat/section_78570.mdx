# WAT § 78570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 7. CALFED Bay-Delta Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78570

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created in the account the CALFED Subaccount.

(b) For the purposes of this article, “subaccount” means the CALFED Subaccount created by subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
