# WAT § 71120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 71120

---

## Text

A petition for the formation of a district, which may consist of any number of separate instruments, shall be filed with the county elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 193. Effective January 1, 2003.)
