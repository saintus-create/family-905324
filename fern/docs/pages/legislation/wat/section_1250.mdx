# WAT § 1250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1250

---

## Text

The board shall consider and act upon all applications for permits to appropriate water and shall do all things required or proper relating to such applications.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
