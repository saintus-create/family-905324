# WAT § 60278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 60278

---

## Text

The secretary of the board, as soon as the result is declared, shall enter in the records of such board a statement of such results.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
