# WAT § 9264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 9264

---

## Text

No officer shall charge or receive any fee for any services required to be performed by him under the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
