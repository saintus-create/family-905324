# WAT § 22162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 5. Flood Control

**Citation:** WAT § 22162

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 22160 apply only to districts containing 200,000 acres or more.
