# WAT § 9600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 9600

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Central Valley Flood Protection Act of 2008.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 364, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2008.)
