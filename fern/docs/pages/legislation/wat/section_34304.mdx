# WAT § 34304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 34304

---

## Text

The board shall exclude any land which it finds will not be benefited by the water service of the proposed district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 899.)
