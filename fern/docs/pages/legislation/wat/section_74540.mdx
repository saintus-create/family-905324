# WAT § 74540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 74540

---

## Text

A district may acquire, construct, maintain, and operate recreational facilities in connection with any dams, reservoirs, or other works owned or controlled by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
