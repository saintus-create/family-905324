# WAT § 8805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8805

---

## Text

The expenses of publication and such other expenses as the board may order shall be borne and paid by the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
