# WAT § 23953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 1. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** WAT § 23953

---

## Text

The work of improvement provided for in this part and the purchase of all supplies, material, and equipment therefor shall be performed by the district, or in the discretion of the board contracts may be made for the work and material after notice calling for bids, as prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
