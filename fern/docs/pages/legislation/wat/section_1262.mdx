# WAT § 1262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 2. Contents of Applications

**Citation:** WAT § 1262

---

## Text

If for agricultural purposes the application shall give the legal subdivisions of the land and the acreage to be irrigated, as near as may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
