# WAT § 6251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 3. Dams Constructed Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6251

---

## Text

A separate application shall be made for each dam and shall be filed with the department upon forms to be supplied by it and shall supply such appropriate information concerning the dam as the department requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
