# WAT § 31180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation > Article: 3. Inclusion in Municipal Utility District

**Citation:** WAT § 31180

---

## Text

A county water district may be annexed to or included within the territory of any municipal utility district organized under the Municipal Utility District Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
