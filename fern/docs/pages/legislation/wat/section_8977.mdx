# WAT § 8977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8977

---

## Text

The Department of Finance may exchange any warrants purchased pursuant to the next preceding section for other warrants drawn against the same assessment, and sell all or any part of the warrants so purchased or taken in exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
