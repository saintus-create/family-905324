# WAT § 30322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 30322

---

## Text

The district shall have been duly incorporated upon the filing of the certificate or a copy of the order declaring the district formed with the county assessor and the State Board of Equalization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 829, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 1999.)
