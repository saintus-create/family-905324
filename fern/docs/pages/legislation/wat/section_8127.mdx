# WAT § 8127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Flood Control in Counties > Article: 4. Improvement of Non-Navigable Streams

**Citation:** WAT § 8127

---

## Text

The board may make regulations for the use of the streams and the repair and control of the works.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
