# WAT § 34031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34031

---

## Text

“Property” embraces all real and personal property, including water, water rights, works, easements, and rights of way.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
