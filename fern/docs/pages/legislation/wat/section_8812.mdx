# WAT § 8812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8812

---

## Text

The list shall also contain the name of the owner, if known, or if unknown, that fact, and the amount of the charge assessed against each tract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
