# WAT § 75904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75904

---

## Text

The notice of election shall describe the proposed change of the boundaries in such manner and terms that it can readily be traced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
