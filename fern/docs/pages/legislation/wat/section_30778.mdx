# WAT § 30778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 3. Preparation for Election

**Citation:** WAT § 30778

---

## Text

The county elections official shall cause the ballots to be printed, bound, and numbered as provided in the Elections Code, except as otherwise required in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 159. Effective January 1, 2003.)
