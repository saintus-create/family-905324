# WAT § 9550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 2. Purchase and Sale of Land by the Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 9550

---

## Text

If no bid is made for any parcel at the delinquent sale equal to the amount of installment or installments delinquent thereon, including the penalty, the county treasurer shall bid in and sell the parcel to the drainage district for the amount of the installment or installments and penalty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
