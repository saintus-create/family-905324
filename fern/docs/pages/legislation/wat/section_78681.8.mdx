# WAT § 78681.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 6. Water Supply Reliability > Article: 5. Sacramento Valley Water Management and Habitat Protection Measures

**Citation:** WAT § 78681.8

---

## Text

The board shall provide adequate public review for proposed programs or projects and shall determine that those programs or projects are consistent with the requirements of Section 78681.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
