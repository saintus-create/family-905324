# WAT § 4304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 5. Payment by and Collection From State Agencies and Public Entities

**Citation:** WAT § 4304

---

## Text

If the owner of the right is a public utility as defined in the Public Utilities Code, the amount apportioned, together with the penalty in case of delinquency, is a lien against any money of the public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 360.)
