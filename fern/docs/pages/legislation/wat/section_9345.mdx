# WAT § 9345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9345

---

## Text

All subsequent proceedings in regard to the assessment shall be conducted as provided in Part 4 of this division and without any further reference to the provisions of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
