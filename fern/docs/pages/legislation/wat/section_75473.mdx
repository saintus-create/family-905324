# WAT § 75473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. (Multiple Versions)

**Citation:** WAT § 75473

---

## Text

Any charge erroneously made by reason of inadvertence or clerical mistake may be refunded upon order of the board at any time after payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 444, Sec. 1.)
