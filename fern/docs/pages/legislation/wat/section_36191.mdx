# WAT § 36191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36191

---

## Text

General obligation bonds shall be payable in lawful money of the United States.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
