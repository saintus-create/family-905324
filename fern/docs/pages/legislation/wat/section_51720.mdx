# WAT § 51720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51720

---

## Text

At any time after the expiration of the redemption period, the county treasurer may, after approval of the board, sell and convey, pursuant to this article, a portion of any parcel purchased at delinquency sale by a county treasurer as trustee for the district at public or private sale and with or without notice.
