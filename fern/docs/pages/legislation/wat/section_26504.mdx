# WAT § 26504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 1. Assessment and Levy by County

**Citation:** WAT § 26504

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in levying the assessment shall be borne by the district concerned. Unless the expenses are paid within 60 days from the time when a demand for them is made, they shall be collected by an action commenced by the district attorney of the county whose board of supervisors prepared the assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
