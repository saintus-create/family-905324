# WAT § 5207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Part: 5.2. Groundwater Extraction Reporting for Probationary Basins and Basins Without a Groundwater Sustainability Agency > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 5207

---

## Text

A right to extract groundwater that may otherwise occur shall not arise or accrue to, and a statute of limitations shall not operate in favor of, a person required to file a report pursuant to this part until the person files the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 347, Sec. 10. (AB 1739) Effective January 1, 2015.)
