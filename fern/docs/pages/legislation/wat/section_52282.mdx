# WAT § 52282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 5. Cancellation

**Citation:** WAT § 52282

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the order, the county treasurer to whom the unsold bonds have been delivered shall cancel the unsold bonds and all coupons attached thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
