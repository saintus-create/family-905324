# WAT § 60154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 60154

---

## Text

At its first meeting in the month of January of each odd-numbered year, the board shall choose one of its members president.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
