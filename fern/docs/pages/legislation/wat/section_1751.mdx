# WAT § 1751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1751

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “power” includes electricity, electrical power, and other power.
