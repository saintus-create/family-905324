# WAT § 6371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 3. Removal of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6371

---

## Text

This evidence shall show that a sufficient portion of the dam has been removed to permit the safe passage of floods down the watercourse across which the dam was located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
