# WAT § 35455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 3. Applications for Water

**Citation:** WAT § 35455

---

## Text

Nothing in this article restricts or limits existing powers of a district to control and provide for distribution of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
