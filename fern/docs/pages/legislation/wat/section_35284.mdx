# WAT § 35284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Method of Elections for Ramirez Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35284

---

## Text

This chapter does not in any way invalidate any act of the district, its directors, or its officers and employees made or performed before the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 699, Sec. 1. Effective October 10, 1995.)
