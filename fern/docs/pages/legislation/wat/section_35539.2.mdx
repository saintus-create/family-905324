# WAT § 35539.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Kern Delta, Cawelo, Rag Gulch, and Kern-Tulare Water Districts > Article: 3. Special Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 35539.2

---

## Text

No levy of assessment shall be made pursuant to this article unless the resolution pursuant to the hearing is properly made and entered in the records of the district at least 31 days before the time for levying such assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 35.)
