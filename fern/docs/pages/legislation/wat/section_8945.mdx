# WAT § 8945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 3. Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Refunding Act

**Citation:** WAT § 8945

---

## Text

Partial payments on any tract in excess of any regular installment amount shall be credited to the payment of the principal amount of the assessment upon that tract thereby reducing the interest that would thereafter accrue upon that tract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
