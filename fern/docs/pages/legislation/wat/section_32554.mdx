# WAT § 32554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Inclusion in Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 32554

---

## Text

Territory annexed to an improvement district shall be subject to existing bond issues and indebtedness of the improvement district from and after the filing with the county assessor specified in Section 32553.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 41.)
