# WAT § 75595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 3. Levy

**Citation:** WAT § 75595

---

## Text

In any county which has a population of 503,000 or more and less than 600,000, any ground water charge in any year shall be established at a fixed and uniform rate for each acre-foot for water other than agricultural water in such proportion to the fixed and uniform rate established for agricultural water as the board shall determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
