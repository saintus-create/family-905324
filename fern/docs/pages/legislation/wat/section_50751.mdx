# WAT § 50751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Elections > Article: 2. Conducting

**Citation:** WAT § 50751

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors fails to appoint an election board, the voters present at the opening of the polls may appoint the election board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
