# WAT § 23221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 3. Approval of Federal Contracts

**Citation:** WAT § 23221

## Text

Proceedings at the election shall be had in so far as applicable in the manner provided in the case of the ordinary issuance of district bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
