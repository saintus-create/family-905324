# WAT § 8837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8837

---

## Text

Each of the separate tracts shall be designated on the map by a distinctive number.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
