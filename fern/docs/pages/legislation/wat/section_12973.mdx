# WAT § 12973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 8. WATER AGENCY RECREATIONAL FACILITIES > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12973

---

## Text

The governing body of a water agency may adopt regulations binding upon all persons covering the use of such recreational facilities. Any violation of such regulations is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 214.)
