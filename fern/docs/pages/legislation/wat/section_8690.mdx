# WAT § 8690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8690

---

## Text

Any cash, bond, check or other security forfeited by any bidder or contractor for failure to enter into or to perform any contract for doing work under this article shall be forfeited to and recovered by the board for the use of the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
