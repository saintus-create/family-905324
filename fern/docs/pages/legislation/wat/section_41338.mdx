# WAT § 41338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41338

---

## Text

No director shall be elected by the district at large, unless a court of competent jurisdiction has determined or hereinafter so determines.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1593, Sec. 12. Effective September 30, 1990.)
