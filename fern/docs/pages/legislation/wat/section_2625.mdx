# WAT § 2625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 6. Objections to the Report

**Citation:** WAT § 2625

---

## Text

The notice shall also set a time and place reasonably convenient to the claimants when and where the proofs of claim and measurements and other data collected by the board during the proceedings will be open and available for inspection by all interested persons. The period of inspection shall be fixed by the board but shall be not less than five days.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
