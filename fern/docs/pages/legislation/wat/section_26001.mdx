# WAT § 26001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 4. Modification and Refund of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 26001

---

## Text

On order of the board any assessments, penalties or costs thereon, or portions thereof, shall be refunded by the treasurer if they were either:

(a) Paid more than once.

(b) Erroneously or illegally collected.

(c) Paid with respect to property not in the district and which has never been in the district.
