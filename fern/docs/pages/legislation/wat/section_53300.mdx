# WAT § 53300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. Interest Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 53300

---

## Text

The board may provide, by resolution entered in its minutes, such rate of interest upon warrants as it deems reasonable, not to exceed 12 percent per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1109, Sec. 7. Effective September 28, 1983.)
