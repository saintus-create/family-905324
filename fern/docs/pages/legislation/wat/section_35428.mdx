# WAT § 35428

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 35428

---

## Text

No right in any water or water right owned by the district shall be acquired by use permitted under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
