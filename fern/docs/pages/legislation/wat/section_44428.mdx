# WAT § 44428

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Statements and Reports

**Citation:** WAT § 44428

---

## Text

The board, following each fiscal year, shall render and promptly thereafter cause to be published in the county at least once a week for two successive weeks a verified statement of the financial condition of the district, showing particularly the receipts and disbursements of the last preceding fiscal year, together with the source of the receipts and purpose of the disbursements.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 85. Effective January 1, 1993.)
