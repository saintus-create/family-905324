# WAT § 75972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Annexation of Districts > Article: 3. Procedure in Annexing District

**Citation:** WAT § 75972

---

## Text

If the question of annexation is submitted to the electors of the annexing district, the election shall be held in the same manner as provided in this chapter for the election in the petitioning district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
