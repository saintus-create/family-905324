# WAT § 40102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 40102

---

## Text

From the filing for record of the order declaring the district formed the formation of the district is complete.
