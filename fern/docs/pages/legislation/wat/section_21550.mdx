# WAT § 21550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Divisions and Methods of Electing Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 21550

---

## Text

Each district shall contain five divisions unless otherwise provided by the more recent of:

(a) The order on final hearing on the formation petition.

(b) An order made pursuant to this article.

(c) An order made pursuant to Article 2 of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
