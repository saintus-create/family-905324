# WAT § 6052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 3. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6052

---

## Text

The department shall employ such clerical, engineering, and other assistants as are necessary for carrying on the work of dam and reservoir supervision in accordance with this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
