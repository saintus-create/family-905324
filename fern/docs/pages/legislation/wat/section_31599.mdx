# WAT § 31599

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Formation and Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 31599

---

## Text

At the time and place fixed for the hearing, or at any time and place to which the hearing is adjourned, the board shall proceed with the hearing and shall consider all written and oral objections to any matters set forth in the resolution. Any person may appear at the hearing and present any matters material to the subject thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
