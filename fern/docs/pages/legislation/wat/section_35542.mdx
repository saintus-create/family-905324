# WAT § 35542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.75. Provisions Pertaining Only to Lost Hills Water District > Article: 1. Plan of Water Allocation

**Citation:** WAT § 35542

---

## Text

A plan adopted or approved pursuant to the provisions of this chapter may be changed, supplemented, amended, or rescinded in the manner herein provided for the adoption and approval of an original plan.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 63.)
