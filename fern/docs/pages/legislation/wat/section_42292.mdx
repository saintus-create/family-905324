# WAT § 42292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 5. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 42292

---

## Text

At the time and place fixed for the hearing, or at any time and place to which the hearing is adjourned, the board shall proceed with the hearing. Any person interested, including any person owning land within the district, may appear at the hearing and present any matters material to the questions which are the subject of the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 755.)
