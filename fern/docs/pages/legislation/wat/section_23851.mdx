# WAT § 23851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Further Assessments and Inclusion > Article: 1. Supplemental Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 23851

---

## Text

The procedure followed in making the supplemental levy shall be substantially the same as for making the original levy, except that no petition is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
