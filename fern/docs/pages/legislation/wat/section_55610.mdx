# WAT § 55610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 4. Adoption of the Revenue Bond Law of 1941

**Citation:** WAT § 55610

---

## Text

The Revenue Bond Law of 1941 is applicable to districts for the purpose of financing the production, transmission and distribution of water for public or private purposes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 53.)
