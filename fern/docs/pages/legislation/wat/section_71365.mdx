# WAT § 71365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71365

---

## Text

The general manager, secretary, and treasurer, and other employees or assistants of the district designated by the board, shall give such bonds to the district conditioned for the faithful performance of their duties as the board from time to time may provide. The premiums on the bonds shall be paid by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
