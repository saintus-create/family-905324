# WAT § 39429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 39429

## Text

The department may require the furnishing of any additional undertaking or payment of money it deems necessary.
