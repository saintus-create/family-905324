# WAT § 46670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 46670

---

## Text

At the end of 30 days, the county treasurer shall make return to the board of all assessments paid.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
