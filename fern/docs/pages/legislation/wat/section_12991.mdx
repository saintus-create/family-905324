# WAT § 12991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 9. DELTA LEVEE MAINTENANCE > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12991

---

## Text

The board is authorized to make, from time to time, such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out, and as are consistent with, this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 717.)
