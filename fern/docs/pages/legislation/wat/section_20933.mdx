# WAT § 20933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20933

---

## Text

The election contest shall be brought in the superior court of the county where the formation proceedings were had.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
