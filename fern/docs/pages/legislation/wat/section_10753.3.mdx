# WAT § 10753.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Groundwater Management Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 10753.3

---

## Text

(a) After the conclusion of the hearing, and if the local agency adopts a resolution of intention, the local agency shall publish the resolution of intention in the same manner that notice for the hearing held under Section 10753.2 was published.

(b) Upon written request, the local agency shall provide any interested person with a copy of the resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 947, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
