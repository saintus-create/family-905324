# WAT § 1776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 2. Joint Occupancy and Use

**Citation:** WAT § 1776

---

## Text

The board shall take into consideration the reasonable cost of the original and new work, the good faith of the applicant, the market for water or power to be supplied by the original and the new work, and the income or use that may be required to provide fair and reasonable returns upon such cost.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
