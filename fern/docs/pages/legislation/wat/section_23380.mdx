# WAT § 23380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Cooperation With Other Districts > Article: 1. Cooperation With Districts in Other States

**Citation:** WAT § 23380

---

## Text

Any rights or disputes arising out of or from the agreements may be tried before and enforced by any court of competent jurisdiction in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
