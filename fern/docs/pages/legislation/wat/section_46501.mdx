# WAT § 46501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46501

---

## Text

During the time the assessment lists are open any person may pay to the county treasurer in lawful money of the United States or in warrants of the district all or any part of the charge assessed against any tract of land.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 821.)
