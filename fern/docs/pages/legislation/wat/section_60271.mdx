# WAT § 60271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 60271

---

## Text

The board shall fix a date upon which an election shall be held for the purpose of authorizing said bonded indebtedness to be incurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
