# WAT § 71996

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Formation of Improvement Districts for Issuance of Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Advance of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 71996

---

## Text

The board may repay the district for any advance of funds from the proceeds of the sale of revenue bonds authorized for the purposes of the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 229.)
