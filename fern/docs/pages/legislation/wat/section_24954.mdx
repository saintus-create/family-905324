# WAT § 24954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Construction Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24954

---

## Text

The surveys, examinations, drawings, and estimate may provide that the works necessary for a completed project shall be constructed progressively during a period of years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
