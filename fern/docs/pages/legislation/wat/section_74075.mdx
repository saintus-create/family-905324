# WAT § 74075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 74075

---

## Text

The determination of the board of supervisors shall be entered upon the minutes of the board.
