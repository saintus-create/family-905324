# WAT § 22117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22117

---

## Text

The officers, agents, and employees of districts have the same powers, duties, and liabilities respecting electric power and the acquisition, operation, and control relating to it as they have respecting irrigation or districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
