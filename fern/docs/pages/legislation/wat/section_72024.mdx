# WAT § 72024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 72024

---

## Text

The secretary, as soon as the result of the special election is declared, shall enter in the records of the board a statement of such results.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
