# WAT § 43700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 43700

---

## Text

A district may commence and maintain any actions and proceedings to carry out its purposes or protect its interests and may defend in any action or proceeding brought against it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
