# WAT § 71689.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 4. Water Replenishment Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 71689.9

---

## Text

To the extent that ground water supplies are replenished under this article no person shall acquire any property or other right in the waters provided by the district for replenishment purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1725.)
