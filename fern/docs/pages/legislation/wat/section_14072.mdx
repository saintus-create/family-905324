# WAT § 14072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 17. Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14072

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund which is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 47, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 83 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
