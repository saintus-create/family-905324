# WAT § 75560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 1. Investigation and Report

**Citation:** WAT § 75560

---

## Text

The district shall annually cause to be made an engineering investigation and report upon ground water conditions of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
