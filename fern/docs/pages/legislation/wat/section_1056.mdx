# WAT § 1056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 1056

---

## Text

The board and the department may fix reasonable charges for publications issued under their respective authorities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
