# WAT § 22119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22119

---

## Text

A district may distribute power without regard to any assessments levied by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
