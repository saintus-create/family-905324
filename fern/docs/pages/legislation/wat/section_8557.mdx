# WAT § 8557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8557

---

## Text

The board shall have its office in the County of Sacramento. The office is the principal place of business and legal residence of the board and of the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 497, Sec. 4. Effective September 14, 2004.)
