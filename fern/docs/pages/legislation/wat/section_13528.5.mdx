# WAT § 13528.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Water Reclamation > Article: 4. Regulation of Reclamation

**Citation:** WAT § 13528.5

---

## Text

(a) The state board may carry out the duties and authority granted to a regional board pursuant to this chapter.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 191. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, by its own provisions.)
