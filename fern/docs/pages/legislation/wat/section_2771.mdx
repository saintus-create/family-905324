# WAT § 2771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2771

---

## Text

Appeals from the decree may be taken by the board or any party in interest, in the same manner and with the same effect as in civil cases.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
