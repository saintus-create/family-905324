# WAT § 36434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36434

---

## Text

The board shall repay the district for any advance of funds with any money received which is authorized by law to be used for such a purpose including the proceeds of the sale of bonds and warrants authorized for the purposes of the improvement district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1085.)
