# WAT § 72066.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 72066.4

---

## Text

“Person” shall mean any domestic or foreign corporation, association, syndicate, joint stock company, partnership of any kind, joint venture, club, Massachusetts business or common law trust, society, individual, municipal corporation, and the state and any political subdivision thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
