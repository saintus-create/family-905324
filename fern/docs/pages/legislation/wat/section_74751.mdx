# WAT § 74751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 74751

---

## Text

No claim shall be paid by the treasurer until allowed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
