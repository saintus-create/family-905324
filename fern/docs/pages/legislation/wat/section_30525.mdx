# WAT § 30525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30525

---

## Text

No ordinance, resolution, or motion shall be passed or become effective without the affirmative votes of at least a majority of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
