# WAT § 22078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 22078

---

## Text

A district may control, distribute, store, spread, sink, treat, purify, recapture and salvage any water including but not limited to sewage waters for the beneficial use or uses of the district or its inhabitants or the owners of rights to waters therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 719.)
