# WAT § 12929.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 2. Environmental Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.10

---

## Text

The Environmental Water Fund is hereby created in the State Treasury. There shall be deposited in the fund all moneys transferred or appropriated to the fund and all income derived from investment of moneys that are in the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 716, Sec. 4.)
