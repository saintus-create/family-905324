# WAT § 22970.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 7. Runoff Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 22970.10

---

## Text

The governing body shall certify the two candidates who receive the highest number of votes for the elective office to the county elections official as the candidates in the runoff election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 2003.)
