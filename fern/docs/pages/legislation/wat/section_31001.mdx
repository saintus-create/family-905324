# WAT § 31001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31001

---

## Text

Each district has the power generally to perform all acts necessary to carry out fully the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
