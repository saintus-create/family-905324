# WAT § 10609.63

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Small Water Suppliers and Nontransient Noncommunity Water Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 10609.63

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to small water suppliers, or small water suppliers integrated into larger water systems, that voluntarily choose to instead comply with Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 10620) of Part 2.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 245, Sec. 1. (SB 552) Effective January 1, 2022.)
