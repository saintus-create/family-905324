# WAT § 178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 3. State Water Resources Control Board

**Citation:** WAT § 178

---

## Text

The members of the board may be removed from office by the Legislature, by concurrent resolution adopted by a majority vote of all members elected to each house, for dereliction of duty or corruption or incompetency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 188 by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
