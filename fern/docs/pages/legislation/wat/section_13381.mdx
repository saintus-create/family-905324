# WAT § 13381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5.5. Compliance With the Provisions of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act as Amended in 1972

**Citation:** WAT § 13381

---

## Text

Waste discharge requirements or dredged or fill material permits may be terminated or modified for cause, including, but not limited to, all of the following:

(a) Violation of any condition contained in the requirements or permits.

(b) Obtaining the requirements by misrepresentation, or failure to disclose fully all relevant facts.

(c) A change in any condition that requires either a temporary or permanent reduction or elimination of the permitted discharge.
