# WAT § 24653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 3. Registration of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24653

---

## Text

A warrant so indorsed is a registered warrant.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
