# WAT § 45932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 45932

---

## Text

No irregularity or omission not affecting the substantial rights of the landowners within a district shall affect the validity of any act done or proceeding taken in relation to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
