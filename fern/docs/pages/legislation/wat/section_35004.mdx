# WAT § 35004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 35004

---

## Text

Every voter, or his legal representative, may vote at any district election either in person or by a person duly appointed as his proxy.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
