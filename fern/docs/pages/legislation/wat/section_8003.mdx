# WAT § 8003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 8003

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “city council” includes the legislative body of any city by whatever name it may be designated.
