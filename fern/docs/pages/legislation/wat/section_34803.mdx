# WAT § 34803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34803

---

## Text

Special meetings of the board shall be held pursuant to the Ralph M. Brown Act (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 9 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 1993.)
