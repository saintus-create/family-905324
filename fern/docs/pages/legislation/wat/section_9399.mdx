# WAT § 9399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9399

---

## Text

Coupons for each installment of interest shall be attached to the bonds and shall bear the facsimile signature of the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
