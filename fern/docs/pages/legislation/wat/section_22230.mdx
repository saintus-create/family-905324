# WAT § 22230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 22230

---

## Text

A district may make and perform any necessary contracts to carry out the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
