# WAT § 52553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 52553

---

## Text

The refunding bonds shall be sold by the county treasurer in the manner provided in Article 3, Chapter 1 of this part for the sale of bonds and refunding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
