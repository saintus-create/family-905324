# WAT § 31051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Property Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31051

---

## Text

A district may acquire by any means stock of any mutual water company or corporation for the purpose of furnishing a supply of water for public, municipal or governmental purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1652.)
