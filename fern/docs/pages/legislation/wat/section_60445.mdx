# WAT § 60445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60445

---

## Text

All warrants for said indebtedness shall be drawn on order of the board of supervisors of the principal county in which said district is situated, on the fund hereinabove provided for in the county treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
