# WAT § 8942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 3. Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Refunding Act

**Citation:** WAT § 8942

---

## Text

Any officer mentioned in the next preceding section shall accept such warrants so presented or tendered in payment of the assessment, or any part thereof, at the face value of the warrant or warrants so presented, plus accrued interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
