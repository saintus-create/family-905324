# WAT § 81400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 3. Government of Agency > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 81400

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business. The board shall act only by ordinance, resolution, or motion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
