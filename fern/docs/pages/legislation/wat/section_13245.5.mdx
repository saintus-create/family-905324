# WAT § 13245.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 3. Regional Water Quality Control Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 13245.5

---

## Text

Guidelines adopted by a regional board shall not become effective unless and until approved by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 758, Sec. 3.)
