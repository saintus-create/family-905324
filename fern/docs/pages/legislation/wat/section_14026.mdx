# WAT § 14026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 16. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 2. California Safe Drinking Water Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14026

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services shall notify suppliers that may be eligible for loans pursuant to this chapter of (a) the purposes of this chapter and (b) the rules and regulations adopted by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 45, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 81 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
