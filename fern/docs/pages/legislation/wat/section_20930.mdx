# WAT § 20930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20930

---

## Text

The formation election may be contested by any person owning property liable to assessment within the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
