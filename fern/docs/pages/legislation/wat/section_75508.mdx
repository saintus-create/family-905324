# WAT § 75508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 75508

---

## Text

“Agricultural water” means water first used on lands in the production of plant crops or livestock for market.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
