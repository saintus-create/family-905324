# WAT § 71278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71278

---

## Text

Resolutions and motions may be adopted by voice vote, but on demand of any member of the board the roll shall be called.
