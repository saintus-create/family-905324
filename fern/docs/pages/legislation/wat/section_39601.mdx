# WAT § 39601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Investigation by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 39601

---

## Text

The department, for the purposes of the determination, may employ all necessary assistants, or acquire and use any existing estimates, surveys, and reports.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
