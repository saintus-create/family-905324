# WAT § 8523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 8523

---

## Text

“State Plan of Flood Control” has the same meaning as that set forth in subdivision (j) of Section 5096.805 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 366) by Stats. 2009, Ch. 275, Sec. 8. (AB 1165) Effective January 1, 2010.)
