# WAT § 20201.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. FINANCIAL SUPERVISION OF DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Compensation of Water District Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 20201.5

---

## Text

Reimbursement for expenses of members of a governing board of a water district is subject to Sections 53232.2 and 53232.3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 700, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2006.)
