# WAT § 31409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 31409

---

## Text

The notice shall be published for two weeks in at least one newspaper, designated by the board, printed and published in the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
