# WAT § 23955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 1. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** WAT § 23955

---

## Text

The board of the district may, provide for the maintenance and operation of the works of an improvement district from the funds of the irrigation district, in lieu of levying further improvement district assessments for such purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 301.)
