# WAT § 24370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Deposits > Article: 2. Deposits in County Treasury

**Citation:** WAT § 24370

---

## Text

The board may draw from time to time from the construction fund and deposit in the county treasury of the office county any money in the construction fund in excess of twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
