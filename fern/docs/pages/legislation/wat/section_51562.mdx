# WAT § 51562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 4. Installments Called to Pay Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 51562

---

## Text

Any bond or coupon received in payment for such an installment shall be canceled forthwith by the county treasurer and filed in his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
