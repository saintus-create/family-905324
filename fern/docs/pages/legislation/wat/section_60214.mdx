# WAT § 60214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Conduct of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 60214

---

## Text

The board shall call and canvass all elections involving matters of initiative and referendum and shall call all other elections which it is authorized to canvass.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
