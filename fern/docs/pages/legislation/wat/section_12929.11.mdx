# WAT § 12929.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 2. Environmental Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.11

---

## Text

The department shall expend the money appropriated to it from the Environmental Water Fund for projects or programs concerning the water resources of the state that will contribute significant environmental benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
