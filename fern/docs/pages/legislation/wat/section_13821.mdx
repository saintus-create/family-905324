# WAT § 13821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.2. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1984

**Citation:** WAT § 13821

---

## Text

Applications for grants under this chapter shall be made to the department in the form and with the supporting material as prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 378, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 28 at the November 6, 1984, election.)
