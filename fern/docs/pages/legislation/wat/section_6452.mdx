# WAT § 6452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 9. Dams Under Construction Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6452

---

## Text

Dams found to be 90 per cent or more constructed on August 14, 1929 shall be subject to the same supervision as dams which were completed prior to that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
