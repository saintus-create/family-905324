# WAT § 71615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water > Article: 1. Development and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 71615

---

## Text

Any special water rate fixed in accordance with the terms and conditions of an annexation shall be deemed to be a rate for a different class or condition of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
