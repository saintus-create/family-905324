# WAT § 8303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Powers of Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8303

---

## Text

The department may purchase, construct and operate one or more dredges or any other needed appliances to promote or properly carry out the work of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
