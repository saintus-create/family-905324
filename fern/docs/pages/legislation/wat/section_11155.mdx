# WAT § 11155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 5. Record of Proceedings and Report to Governor

**Citation:** WAT § 11155

---

## Text

The State Controller, the State Treasurer, and the department shall keep full and particular account and record of all their proceedings under this part.
