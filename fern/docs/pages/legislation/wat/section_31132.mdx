# WAT § 31132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 8. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 31132

---

## Text

The exercise of the power of eminent domain to acquire any land or interest therein for recreational purposes is limited to land within the district or land contiguous to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 214.)
