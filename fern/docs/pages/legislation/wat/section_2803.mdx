# WAT § 2803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2803

---

## Text

The order of determination shall provide that within the time prescribed for completion of the appropriation, or such further time as the court may allow, the claimant shall submit to the court proof of completion and the amount of water actually applied to beneficial use.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 53.)
