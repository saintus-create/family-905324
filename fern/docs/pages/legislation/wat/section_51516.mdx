# WAT § 51516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 2. Original and Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51516

---

## Text

The board shall require that an agent give a bond in the amount it considers sufficient for the faithful performance of his duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
