# WAT § 26003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 4. Modification and Refund of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 26003

---

## Text

The board may order the collector to cancel the uncollected assessment on any property which because of transfer to the State or another public agency is determined to be uncollectible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 475.)
