# WAT § 41904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Contest of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41904

---

## Text

Either party of record has the right to appeal within 30 days from entry of judgment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
