# WAT § 39928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39928

---

## Text

At the formation election there shall be elected a board of directors corresponding in number to the number of divisions in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
