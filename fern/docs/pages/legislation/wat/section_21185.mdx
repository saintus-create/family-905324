# WAT § 21185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 5. Deputies and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 21185

---

## Text

The board shall:

(a) Employ agents, officers, and employees as required.

(b) Prescribe their duties and fix their salaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
