# WAT § 9359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9359

---

## Text

The board shall in the order providing the election, appoint a board of election for each polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
