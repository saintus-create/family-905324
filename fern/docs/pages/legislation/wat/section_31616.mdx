# WAT § 31616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 31616

---

## Text

The procedure for estimating, levying, and collecting taxes in any improvement district shall be the same as is provided in Part 7 (commencing at Section 31650) of this division for taxes on property in improvement districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
