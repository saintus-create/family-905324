# WAT § 71364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71364

---

## Text

The attorney shall be the legal adviser of the district and shall perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
