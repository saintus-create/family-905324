# WAT § 22825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 1. Introduction

**Citation:** WAT § 22825

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply only to districts containing 500,000 acres or more.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
