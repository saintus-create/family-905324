# WAT § 36543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Dissolution of Distribution Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 36543

---

## Text

The order of dissolution shall be recorded in the same manner as the order forming the distribution district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
