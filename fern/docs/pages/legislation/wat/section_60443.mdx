# WAT § 60443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60443

---

## Text

Upon the disincorporation of said district every public officer of said district shall immediately turn over to the board of supervisors of the principal county in which said district is situated, all public property of every nature and description in their possession, and including all public records and data of every nature and description.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
