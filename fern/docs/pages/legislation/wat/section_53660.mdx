# WAT § 53660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Change of Boundaries > Article: 3. Inclusion

**Citation:** WAT § 53660

---

## Text

Land in compact form, capable of being embraced in a district, and not a part of another district, may be annexed to a district.
