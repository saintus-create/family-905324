# WAT § 40301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualification and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 40301

---

## Text

Within 10 days after receiving his certificate of election or being appointed, each director shall take and subscribe the official oath, and file it in the office of the district and execute the bond required of him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
