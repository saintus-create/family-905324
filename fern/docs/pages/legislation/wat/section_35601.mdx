# WAT § 35601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 35601

---

## Text

The right is hereby granted to locate, construct, and maintain works of a district on any land owned by the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
