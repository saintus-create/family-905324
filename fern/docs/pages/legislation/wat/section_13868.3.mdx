# WAT § 13868.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.5. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1976

**Citation:** WAT § 13868.3

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services, after public notice and hearing and with the advice of the department, shall from time to time establish a priority list of suppliers to be considered for financing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1008, and approved in Prop. 3 on June 8, 1976.)
