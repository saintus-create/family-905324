# WAT § 35286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Method of Elections for Ramirez Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35286

---

## Text

The board of directors may at any time alter, repeal, or amend the district’s bylaws by a majority vote. Any alteration, repeal, or amendment to the bylaws shall be recorded with the county recorder of each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 699, Sec. 1. Effective October 10, 1995.)
