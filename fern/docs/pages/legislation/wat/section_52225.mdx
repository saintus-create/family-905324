# WAT § 52225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 2. Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 52225

---

## Text

The proceeds from the sale of the bonds shall be placed in the county treasury to the credit of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
