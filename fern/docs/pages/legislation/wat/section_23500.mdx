# WAT § 23500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23500

---

## Text

Contiguous land may be formed into a distribution district for the purpose of contracting with the United States, pursuant to the federal reclamation laws, for the construction of a distribution system separate from or supplemental to the works of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
