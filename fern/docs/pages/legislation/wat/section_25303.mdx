# WAT § 25303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 5. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 25303

---

## Text

The first publication of the redemption notice shall be not less than 30 days nor more than 90 days prior to the date fixed for redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
