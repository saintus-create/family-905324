# WAT § 50656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 50656

---

## Text

All documents requiring approval by the board shall bear the signature of either a trustee or the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 40. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
