# WAT § 4400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 4400

---

## Text

The foregoing provisions of this part shall be applicable to this chapter, so far as adaptable thereto or consistent therewith, and whenever rights to water have been ascertained and determined as specified in Section 4027 the department may elect to proceed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 657.)
