# WAT § 36363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 4. Action to Secure Payment of Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36363

---

## Text

Within 30 days after publication of the summons has been completed and proof filed with the court any person may appear and answer the complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
