# WAT § 45907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 1. Project and Government Contract Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 45907

---

## Text

Any surplus funds and any money held by a district in a sinking or depreciation fund may in the discretion of its board be invested in direct assessment warrants of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
