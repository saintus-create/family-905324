# WAT § 24371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Deposits > Article: 2. Deposits in County Treasury

**Citation:** WAT § 24371

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall receive and receipt for district money deposited with him and place it to the credit of the district, and he shall be responsible upon his official bond for the safe-keeping and disbursing of the money.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
