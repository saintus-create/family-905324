# WAT § 22251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 22251

---

## Text

Any landowner may assign for use within the district his right to the whole or any portion of the water apportioned to him pursuant to Section 22250.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
