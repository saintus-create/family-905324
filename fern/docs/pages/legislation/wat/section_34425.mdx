# WAT § 34425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34425

---

## Text

The returns of the election shall be forwarded to the board which shall meet on the second Monday following the election and canvass the returns and declare the results.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
