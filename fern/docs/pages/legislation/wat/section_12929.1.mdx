# WAT § 12929.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.1

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature, in enacting this chapter, to prescribe the purposes of the Environmental Water Fund, and to create, and prescribe the purposes of, the Environmental Water Program and the Water Quality Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
