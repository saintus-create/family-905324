# WAT § 75590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 3. Levy

**Citation:** WAT § 75590

---

## Text

Prior to the end of the water year in which the hearing is held, and based upon the findings and determinations from the hearing, the board shall determine whether or not a zone or zones should be established and a ground water charge levied therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
