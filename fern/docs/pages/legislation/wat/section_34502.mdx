# WAT § 34502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 34502

---

## Text

With the filing for record of the order declaring the district formed the formation of the district is complete.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
