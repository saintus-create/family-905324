# WAT § 36431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36431

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing on the resolution of intention and of the hearing, if any, upon proposed modifications the board may by resolution order the territory annexed to said improvement district upon the terms and conditions set forth in said resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 357.)
