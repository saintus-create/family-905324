# WAT § 26605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 26605

---

## Text

Upon termination of the contract, the board of directors of the district shall appoint an assessor, or both an assessor and collector, who shall serve until the election of their successor at the next general district election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1938.)
