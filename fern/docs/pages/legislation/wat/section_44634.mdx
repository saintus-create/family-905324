# WAT § 44634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 2. Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 44634

---

## Text

Upon the presentation of any warrant entitled to payment under the terms of the notice, the treasurer shall pay it together with interest due on it from the date of its original presentation for payment to the date of the first publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
