# WAT § 35539.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.7. Provisions Pertaining only to Irvine Ranch Water District and Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35539.10

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to the Irvine Ranch Water District and Santa Margarita Water District. The powers granted in this chapter supplement the existing powers of the districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 209, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
