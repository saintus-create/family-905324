# WAT § 8014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 2. Preliminary Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 8014

---

## Text

After the filing of the general plans and estimates, and by resolution or ordinance of intention passed at a regular meeting by a vote of two-thirds of all its members and approved by the executive of the city, the city council shall determine, if so advised, that the public good demands the construction, acquisition, and completion, or either, of the works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
