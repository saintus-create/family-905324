# WAT § 36350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36350

---

## Text

Revenue bonds shall be payable at such time and place as may be designated by the board, but in no case shall the maturity of any bond be more than 40 years from its date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
