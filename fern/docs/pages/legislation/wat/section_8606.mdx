# WAT § 8606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 8606

---

## Text

The board may insert a stipulation providing for such inspection and supervision in any order, contract or other instrument, relating to the work authorized in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
