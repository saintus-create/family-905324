# WAT § 24480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24480

---

## Text

Any money in a refunding bond sinking fund may be invested in bonds of the United States or of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
