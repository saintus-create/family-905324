# WAT § 51685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 2. Sale After One Year After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51685

---

## Text

Prior to the sale of any parcel, the county treasurer shall cause its fair market value to be ascertained by an appraisal made by a board of appraisement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
