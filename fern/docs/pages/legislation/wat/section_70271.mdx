# WAT § 70271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Reorganization

**Citation:** WAT § 70271

---

## Text

The original district directors shall be deemed the successors and legal representatives of the district so reorganized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
