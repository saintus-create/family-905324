# WAT § 31635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Coachella Valley Water District Water Replenishment Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 31635

---

## Text

Any replenishment assessment levied pursuant to this chapter shall be due and payable to the district at the time or times determined by the district and stated in the notice of levy, and shall accompany the statement required from each producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 498.)
