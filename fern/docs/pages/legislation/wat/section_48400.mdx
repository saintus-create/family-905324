# WAT § 48400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Dissolution

**Citation:** WAT § 48400

---

## Text

Any district organized pursuant to this division may be dissolved as provided by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 129. Effective January 1, 1993.)
