# WAT § 50930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 50930

---

## Text

A district may acquire, by purchase, condemnation, gift, lease or other legal means, such real or personal property as is necessary for accomplishing the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
