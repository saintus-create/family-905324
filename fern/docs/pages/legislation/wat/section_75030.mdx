# WAT § 75030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 3. Calling of Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75030

---

## Text

If the board makes the final order approving the formation petition, it may, by resolution adopted by a majority of its members, call an election for the purpose of authorizing the incurring of a bonded indebtedness by the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
