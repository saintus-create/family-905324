# WAT § 75016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75016

---

## Text

The order shall be entered in the minutes of the board and a copy thereof shall be recorded in the office of the county recorder of each county in which any of the lands of the improvement district are located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
