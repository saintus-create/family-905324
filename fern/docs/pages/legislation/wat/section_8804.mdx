# WAT § 8804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8804

---

## Text

The filing of the lists shall be conclusive evidence that the requisite notice has been given, and that all acts required to be done before the filing of the lists have been duly done and performed, and the decision of the board is final and conclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
