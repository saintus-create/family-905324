# WAT § 23179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

The board shall perform all acts necessary to carry out the enlarged powers enumerated in this chapter.
