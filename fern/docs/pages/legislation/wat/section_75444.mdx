# WAT § 75444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75444

---

## Text

The ballots at the election shall contain a general statement of the amount and purpose of the assessment to be levied. To the right of or below the statement of the proposition to be voted on, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 75. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
