# WAT § 44626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 2. Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 44626

---

## Text

Whenever any warrant of the district is presented for payment when funds are not available for payment, it shall thereafter draw interest at a rate determined by resolution of the board, not to exceed 7 percent per year, until public notice is given that such funds are available.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
