# WAT § 23670.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23670.1

---

## Text

Upon approval by two-thirds of the board, the assessment may be made payable in not more than 20 annual installments.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1226, Sec. 30.)
