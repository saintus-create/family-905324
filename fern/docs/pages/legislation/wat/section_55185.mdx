# WAT § 55185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 55185

---

## Text

In all particulars not recited in the ordinance or resolution, the election shall be held as provided by law for holding general elections in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
