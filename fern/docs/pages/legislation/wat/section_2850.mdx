# WAT § 2850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2850

---

## Text

At the time of the submission of proofs, the board shall collect from each claimant the fee established pursuant to Section 1528.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 2004.)
