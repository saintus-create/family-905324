# WAT § 30731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 1. Creation of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 30731

---

## Text

If the question is submitted to the voters at a general district election, the notices required by Section 12112 of the Elections Code shall contain a statement of the question to appear on the ballot. If the question is submitted to the voters at any district election, other than a general district election, the notice of election and ballot shall contain a statement of the question.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 588, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007.)
