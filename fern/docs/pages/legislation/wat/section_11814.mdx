# WAT § 11814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 2. Construction Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11814

---

## Text

All the costs and expenses mentioned in the next preceding section shall be charged as a part of the cost of construction of the project and the money in the construction fund is hereby appropriated for such purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
