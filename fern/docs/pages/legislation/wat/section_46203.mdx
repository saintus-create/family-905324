# WAT § 46203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 3. Assessment Rolls

**Citation:** WAT § 46203

---

## Text

In the report of the commissioners, land embraced within a comprehensive area or a political subdivision of the State may be referred to generally as land lying within the area or subdivision without further description.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
