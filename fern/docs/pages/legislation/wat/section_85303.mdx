# WAT § 85303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 1. The Delta Plan

**Citation:** WAT § 85303

---

## Text

The Delta Plan shall promote statewide water conservation, water use efficiency, and sustainable use of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
