# WAT § 23176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 23176

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply only to proceedings taken under it and do not apply to district operations unrelated to cooperation with the United States.

Except as required expressly or by necessary implication by this chapter all other provisions of this division are unaffected by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
