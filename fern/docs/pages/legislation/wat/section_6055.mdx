# WAT § 6055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 3. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6055

---

## Text

The cost and expense of a consulting board if appointed on the request of an owner shall be paid by the owner.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
