# WAT § 24735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 1. Surrender of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24735

---

## Text

Any owner of any bonds or warrants of a district may surrender them to the district by giving the bonds or warrants to the secretary for cancellation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
