# WAT § 73500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.5. WHOLESALE REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM SECURITY AND RELIABILITY ACT

**Citation:** WAT § 73500

---

## Text

This division shall be known as and may be cited as the Wholesale Regional Water System Security and Reliability Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 841, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003. Repealed as of January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 73514.)
