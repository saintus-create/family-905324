# WAT § 71300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71300

---

## Text

All powers, privileges, and duties of a district shall be exercised and performed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
