# WAT § 13224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 2. General Provisions Relating to Powers and Duties of Regional Boards

**Citation:** WAT § 13224

---

## Text

Each regional board may issue policy statements relating to any water quality matter within its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
