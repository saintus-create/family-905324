# WAT § 36456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 36456

---

## Text

As used in the laws referred to in this chapter the terms therefrom set forth in this article refer for the purposes of this chapter to that which this article states each respectively refers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 268.)
