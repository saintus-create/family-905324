# WAT § 13465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.1. Water Conservation and Water Quality Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13465

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 13458 and 13459, the committee may prescribe further terms and conditions for loan contracts to authorize a deferment on payment of all or part of the principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 678. Effective January 1, 2007. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 6, and approved in Prop. 44 on June 3, 1986.)
