# WAT § 39902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39902

---

## Text

The notice of the formation election shall be published once a week for at least three weeks prior to the election in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
