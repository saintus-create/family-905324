# WAT § 46152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 1. Commissioners

**Citation:** WAT § 46152

---

## Text

Each commissioner before entering upon his duties shall take and subscribe an oath that he is not in any manner interested directly or indirectly in any land in the district and that he will perform the duties of commissioner to the best of his ability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
