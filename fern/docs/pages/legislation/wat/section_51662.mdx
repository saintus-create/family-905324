# WAT § 51662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 1. Sale After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51662

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall convey parcels purchased by him at a sale for the delinquency of an unbonded assessment, free of all liens and encumbrances except any prior or subsequent district assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
