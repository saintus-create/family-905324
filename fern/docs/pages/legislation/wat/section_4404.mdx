# WAT § 4404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 4404

---

## Text

Obligations to pay for the respective allocations of cost set forth in the order or decree may be enforced by the department against the respective owners or their successors by execution or as may be otherwise provided in such order or decree.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 657.)
