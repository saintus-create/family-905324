# WAT § 44080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States, the State and Other Districts > Article: 4. Distribution of Water

**Citation:** WAT § 44080

---

## Text

All water which the district has the right to use under any contract with the United States may be distributed and apportioned by the district in accordance with acts of Congress applicable thereto and any rules or regulations thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
