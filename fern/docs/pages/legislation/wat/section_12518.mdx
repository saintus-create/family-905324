# WAT § 12518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 12518

---

## Text

The board shall make such reports and recommendations to the Governor as he requires or as the board deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
