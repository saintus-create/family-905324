# WAT § 51021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 1. Districts Within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51021

---

## Text

Within five days after the copies of the plan and estimate are filed with the county clerk he shall certify two of the copies and transmit them to the Secretary of the Reclamation Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
