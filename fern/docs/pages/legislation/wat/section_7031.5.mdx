# WAT § 7031.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 3. Bridges and Conduits on or Near Highways

**Citation:** WAT § 7031.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “bridge” means a structure constructed to allow the conducting of water underneath by canal, ditch, flume or other uncovered appliance for conducting water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 998.)
