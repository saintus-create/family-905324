# WAT § 20980.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Change of Name

**Citation:** WAT § 20980.6

---

## Text

The Helix Irrigation District may change its name pursuant to this chapter to the Helix Water District.

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 330.)
