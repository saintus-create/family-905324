# WAT § 10550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10550

---

## Text

The plan or project shall not be funded pursuant to this part if it would fund activities inconsistent with applicable state and federal water quality laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 5. Effective March 1, 2009.)
