# WAT § 8012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 2. Preliminary Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 8012

---

## Text

The city council shall have some competent person make general plans and estimates of the cost of the contemplated works.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
