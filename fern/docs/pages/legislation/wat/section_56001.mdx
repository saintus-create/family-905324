# WAT § 56001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 56001

---

## Text

“District” as used in this part means any county drainage district formed pursuant to this part or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
