# WAT § 46756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 5. Sale for Nonpayment

**Citation:** WAT § 46756

---

## Text

At the time stated in the notice, or such other time to which the sale may have been postponed, the county treasurer shall sell the property to the highest bidder for lawful money of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
