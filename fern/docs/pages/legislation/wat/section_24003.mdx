# WAT § 24003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 24003

---

## Text

Land upon which the assessment for improvement district warrants, including any interest, or for any other obligation payable out of the assessment, has been paid pursuant to Section 24002 shall not be subject to further assessments levied for the purposes of the improvement, but it shall remain liable for any assessments levied for operation and for any supplemental or additional improvement district assessments levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 505.)
