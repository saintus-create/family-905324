# WAT § 2012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 2. Report of Referee

**Citation:** WAT § 2012

---

## Text

The report shall set forth such findings of fact and conclusions of law as may be required by the court’s order of reference.
