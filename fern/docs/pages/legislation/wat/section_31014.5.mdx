# WAT § 31014.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31014.5

---

## Text

The term “irrigation district” as used in Chapter 1046 of the Statutes of 1969 includes a county water district and the Orange County Water District.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31014 by Stats. 1977, Ch. 579.)
