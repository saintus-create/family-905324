# WAT § 9468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9468

---

## Text

Any bonds purchased pursuant to the provisions of this article, and held in the bond fund may from time to time be sold and the proceeds temporarily reinvested in bonds as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
