# WAT § 31337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 3. Investments

**Citation:** WAT § 31337

---

## Text

The investment may be made by purchase of the bonds or registered warrants or part thereof at the original sale or by purchase after they have been thus issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
