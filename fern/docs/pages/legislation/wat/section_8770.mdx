# WAT § 8770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 8770

---

## Text

The board has no power or authority to levy any assessment for any purpose upon property outside of the boundaries of the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
