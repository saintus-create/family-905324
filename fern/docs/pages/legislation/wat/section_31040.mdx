# WAT § 31040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Property Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31040

---

## Text

A district may take any property necessary to carry out the business of the district by grant, purchase, gift, devise, condemnation, or lease with or without the privilege of purchase.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 582.)
