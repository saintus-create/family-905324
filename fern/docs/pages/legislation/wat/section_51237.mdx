# WAT § 51237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51237

---

## Text

The sums assessed shall be collected in the manner provided in Chapter 3 of this part and paid into the county treasury to the credit of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
