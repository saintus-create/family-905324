# WAT § 35155.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Bond Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 35155.1

---

## Text

The board may divide the principal amount of any issue into two or more series and fix different dates for the bonds of each series.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
