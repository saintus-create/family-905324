# WAT § 36258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternate Provisions for the Issuance of General Obligation Bonds > Article: 2. Authorization of Bonds and Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 36258

---

## Text

The last equalized assessment roll shall be used as a basis for determining whether or not a majority written protest has been made. The board shall be under no duty to obtain or consider any other evidence as to ownership of property and its determination of ownership shall be final and conclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1458.)
