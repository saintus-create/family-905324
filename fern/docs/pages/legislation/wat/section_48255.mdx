# WAT § 48255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 48255

---

## Text

To the right of or below the question on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 60. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
