# WAT § 20843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 3. Final Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20843

---

## Text

Failure to give notice of the final hearing shall not affect the validity of subsequent proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
