# WAT § 26501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 1. Assessment and Levy by County

**Citation:** WAT § 26501

---

## Text

The applicable part of the equalized county assessment rolls of the affected counties shall be the basis of assessment for the district when its assessments are levied pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
