# WAT § 37910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 37910

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law or the bylaws of the district, all elections for the board of directors of the district shall be conducted in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 350, Sec. 1. (AB 2453) Effective January 1, 2015.)
