# WAT § 25304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 5. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 25304

---

## Text

If on the date fixed for redemption the district has provided funds available for the payment of the principal and interest of the bonds called, interest on them ceases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
