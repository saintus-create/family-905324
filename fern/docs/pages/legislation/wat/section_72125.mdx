# WAT § 72125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payments by Public Agencies in Lieu of Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 72125

---

## Text

“Public agency,” as used in this chapter, means any city, county, district, other local authority or public body of, or within, this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 550.)
