# WAT § 34182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 34182

---

## Text

No clerical error in the published text of the petition and no error or omission in the publication of the names attached to the petition invalidates any action on the petition or deprives the board of jurisdiction to proceed in the matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
