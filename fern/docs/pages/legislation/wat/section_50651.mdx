# WAT § 50651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 50651

---

## Text

The powers of the district enumerated in this division shall, except as therein otherwise provided, be exercised by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
