# WAT § 76060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Dissolution > Article: 4. Vesting of Property Upon Dissolution

**Citation:** WAT § 76060

---

## Text

Upon the dissolution of a district, any and all real property belonging to the district shall become and be the property of the county in which the real property is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
