# WAT § 60026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60026

---

## Text

“Affected county” means each county in which some land of the district or proposed district is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
