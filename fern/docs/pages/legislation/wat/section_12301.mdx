# WAT § 12301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Delta Flood Protection Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12301

---

## Text

The Delta Flood Protection Fund is hereby abolished on July 1, 2010, and all unencumbered moneys in the fund are transferred to the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 548, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 2006.)
