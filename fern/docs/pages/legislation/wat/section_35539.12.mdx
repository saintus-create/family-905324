# WAT § 35539.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.7. Provisions Pertaining only to Irvine Ranch Water District and Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35539.12

---

## Text

The districts may acquire, construct, operate, maintain, and furnish facilities for the diversion of urban runoff from drainage courses within the districts, the treatment of the urban runoff, the return of the water to the drainage courses, or the beneficial use of the water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 209, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
