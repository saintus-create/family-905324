# WAT § 46150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 1. Commissioners

**Citation:** WAT § 46150

---

## Text

If a district project is adopted as provided in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 42500) of Part 5 of this division, the board shall appoint three commissioners, one of whom shall be a civil engineer and one shall have a practical knowledge of irrigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 102. Effective January 1, 1993.)
