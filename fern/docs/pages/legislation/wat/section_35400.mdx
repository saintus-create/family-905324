# WAT § 35400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 35400

---

## Text

Each district has the power generally to perform all acts necessary or proper to carry out fully the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
