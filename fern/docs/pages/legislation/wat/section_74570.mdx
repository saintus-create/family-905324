# WAT § 74570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts With Other Agencies > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 74570

---

## Text

A district may cooperate and contract with one or more other districts or public corporations or agencies, whenever, in the opinion of the board, such cooperation or contract is desirable or advantageous to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
