# WAT § 13399.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5.9. The Storm Water Enforcement Act of 1998

**Citation:** WAT § 13399.25

---

## Text

This chapter supplements, and does not supplant, other laws relating to the discharge of storm water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 998, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
