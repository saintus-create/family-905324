# WAT § 11802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 1. General Financial Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11802

---

## Text

The proceedings authorizing the issuance of bonds may contain appropriate provisions governing the use and application of bond proceeds and revenues for the purposes specified in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
