# WAT § 1265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 2. Contents of Applications

**Citation:** WAT § 1265

---

## Text

If for mining purposes the application shall state the nature and location of the mines to be served and the methods of supplying and utilizing the water.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
