# WAT § 531.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 4.3. Agricultural and Urban Water Use Reporting

**Citation:** WAT § 531.20

---

## Text

To the extent that the provisions of this article conflict with the requirements of Chapter 4800 of the State Administrative Manual, the requirements of that chapter shall control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 675, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
