# WAT § 35210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Method of Elections for the El Toro Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35210

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply only to the El Toro Water District. For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the El Toro Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 234, Sec. 2. Effective July 20, 1981.)
