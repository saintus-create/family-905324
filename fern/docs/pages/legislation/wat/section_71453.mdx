# WAT § 71453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 71453

---

## Text

No person shall vote at any district election unless he is a voter residing in the division of the district in which he casts his vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
