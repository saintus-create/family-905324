# WAT § 48256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 48256

---

## Text

If at the election a majority of all the votes cast are against the change of the boundaries of the district, the petition is denied.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
