# WAT § 8034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8034

---

## Text

The interest rate shall not exceed 8 percent per annum, and shall be fixed by the city council.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
