# WAT § 1705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10. Change to an Application, Permit, or License

**Citation:** WAT § 1705

---

## Text

After the hearing, the board shall grant or refuse, as the facts warrant, permission to change the point of diversion, place of use, purpose of use, or any other provision or condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 255, Sec. 4. (SB 779) Effective January 1, 2020.)
