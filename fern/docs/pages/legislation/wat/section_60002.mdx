# WAT § 60002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60002

---

## Text

“Include” except when used in relation to the inclusion of land into a district does not necessarily exclude matters not enumerated.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
