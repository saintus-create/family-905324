# WAT § 39014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39014

---

## Text

“Land,” except in Chapter 2 of Part 10, means land in the district or proposed district involved.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
