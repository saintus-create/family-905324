# WAT § 71017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 71017

## Text

“Voter” means a voter as defined in Section 359 of the Elections Code.
