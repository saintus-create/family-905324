# WAT § 36321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 2. Lien of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36321

---

## Text

No holder of any revenue bond may ever compel any exercise of the taxing power to pay the bond, interest thereon, or any part of either.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
