# WAT § 12890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 10. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12890

## Text

The department is authorized to make from time to time such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out, and as are consistent with, this chapter.
