# WAT § 44910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 44910

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be given and the election shall be held and the result determined as nearly as practicable in the manner provided for bond elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1367.)
