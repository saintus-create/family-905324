# WAT § 26673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Collection, Payment in Lieu of Assessment and Distribution of Water by Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26673

---

## Text

Upon the execution of such contract and so long as the contract remains in force and effect, the district shall be relieved of any further obligation to furnish water for use on the lands covered by the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 603.)
