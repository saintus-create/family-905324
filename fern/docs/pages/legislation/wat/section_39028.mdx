# WAT § 39028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39028

---

## Text

“Special election” means any election other than a general election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
