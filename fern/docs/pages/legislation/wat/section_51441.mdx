# WAT § 51441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 8. Assessments for Water Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 51441

---

## Text

Water charges, included as a part of an operation and maintenance assessment, when collected shall be deposited in the irrigation fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1229.)
