# WAT § 74202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 74202

---

## Text

The directors of any district who are elected shall hold office pursuant to Section 10505 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 267. Effective January 1, 1995.)
