# WAT § 21576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Abolition and Reestablishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 21576

---

## Text

The board of the district may petition the board of supervisors of the principal county for an order by the board of supervisors abolishing the divisions in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
