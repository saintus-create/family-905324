# WAT § 37204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 7.5. ALTERNATIVE PROVISIONS FOR LEVY, COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF DISTRICT ASSESSMENTS BY THE COUNTY > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37204

---

## Text

The board may from time to time by resolution provide for a district fiscal year, to commence and end at the dates specified in said resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36504 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 614.)
