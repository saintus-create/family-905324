# WAT § 8940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 3. Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Refunding Act

**Citation:** WAT § 8940

---

## Text

The board may, by order entered in its minutes, postpone or discontinue from time to time the calling of the semiannual installments or any of them for such periods of time as it deems advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
