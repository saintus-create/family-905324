# WAT § 47184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 47184

---

## Text

The board may, at any time after any toll or charge provided for in this division has become delinquent, direct that proceedings be not taken to enforce the lien therefor, and in place of such proceedings bring suit in the name of the district against the delinquent to enforce collection of the delinquent toll or charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
