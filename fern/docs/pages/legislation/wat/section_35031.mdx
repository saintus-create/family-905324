# WAT § 35031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 2. Establishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 35031

---

## Text

Divisions once established may be modified by the same procedure as herein set forth for establishing divisions.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 207.)
