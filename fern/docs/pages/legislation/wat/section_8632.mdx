# WAT § 8632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 3. Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8632

---

## Text

Whenever any levee is in the opinion of the board insufficient or necessary for flood control, the board shall publish notice to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
