# WAT § 30501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30501

---

## Text

The directors first elected upon the formation of the district shall hold office pursuant to Section 10505 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 235. Effective January 1, 1995.)
