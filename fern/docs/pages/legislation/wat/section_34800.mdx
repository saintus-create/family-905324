# WAT § 34800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34800

---

## Text

The board shall elect a president from its members and appoint the secretary.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
