# WAT § 23773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Alternative Procedure for Formation > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23773

---

## Text

Proceedings at the hearing shall be the same as provided for at a hearing upon the formation petition pursuant to Chapter 1 of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
