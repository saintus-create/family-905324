# WAT § 20962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 20962

---

## Text

From the filing for record of the order declaring the district formed the formation of the district is complete.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
