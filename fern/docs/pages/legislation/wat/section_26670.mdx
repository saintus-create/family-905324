# WAT § 26670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Collection, Payment in Lieu of Assessment and Distribution of Water by Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26670

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public agency” means any city or district, other than an irrigation district, authorized to distribute water, which is in whole or in part included within the exterior boundaries of an irrigation district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 603.)
