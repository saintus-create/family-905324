# WAT § 2756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2756

---

## Text

Service by registered mail and by publication shall be deemed full and sufficient notice to all parties in interest of the date and purpose of the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
