# WAT § 8872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8872

---

## Text

No license shall be required of the county treasurer for conducting the sale.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
