# WAT § 23504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23504

---

## Text

The petition, all proceedings in reference to it, the distribution district, and the land in it shall be designated by a number.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
