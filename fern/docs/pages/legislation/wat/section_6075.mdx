# WAT § 6075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 1. Powers in General

**Citation:** WAT § 6075

---

## Text

The department, under the police power of the state, shall supervise the construction, enlargement, alteration, repair, maintenance, operation, and removal of dams and reservoirs for the protection of life and property as provided in this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
