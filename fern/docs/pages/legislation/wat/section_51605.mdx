# WAT § 51605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 1. Delinquency Sales on Unbonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51605

---

## Text

At the time of the sale, the county treasurer shall sell the parcel to himself or herself and his or her successors in office, as trustee for the district, for the amount due on the installment, accrued interest, penalty, and costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 322, Sec. 2. Effective August 22, 1994.)
