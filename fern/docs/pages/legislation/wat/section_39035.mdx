# WAT § 39035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39035

---

## Text

“Board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 779, Sec. 9. Effective October 10, 1999.)
