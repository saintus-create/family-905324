# WAT § 52712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52712

---

## Text

An issue of refunding bonds issued pursuant to this chapter shall be based upon and payable out of the assessment from which the bonds to be refunded were payable, and shall mature at one time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
