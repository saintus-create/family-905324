# WAT § 12891.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 10. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12891.4

---

## Text

All loans or grants made pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to the provisions of this chapter and subject to the approval of the California Water Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 359.)
