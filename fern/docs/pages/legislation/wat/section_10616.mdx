# WAT § 10616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10616

---

## Text

“Public agency” means any board, commission, county, city and county, city, regional agency, district, or other public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1.)
