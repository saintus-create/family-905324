# WAT § 52703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52703

---

## Text

The board shall call and conduct an election of the landowners in the manner provided in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 50800) of Part 4 of this division for the purpose of authorizing the issuance of the refunding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1993.)
