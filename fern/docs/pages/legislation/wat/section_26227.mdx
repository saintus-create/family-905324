# WAT § 26227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 26227

---

## Text

One of the duplicate certificates of redemption shall be given to the redemptioner.
