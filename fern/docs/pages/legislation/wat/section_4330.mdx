# WAT § 4330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 6. Alternative Method of Payment by Owners

**Citation:** WAT § 4330

## Text

The department shall deposit the money in the Water Resources Revolving Fund to the credit of the amount of the service area affected.
