# WAT § 51002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Adoption

**Citation:** WAT § 51002

---

## Text

The plan and estimate may include any irrigation works which the district is empowered to acquire.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
