# WAT § 1320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 3. Posting and Mailing Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1320

---

## Text

Notice of an application for three cubic feet or less per second or for 200 acre-feet or less per annum of storage shall be given by posting and mailing in accordance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
