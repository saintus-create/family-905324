# WAT § 35405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 35405

---

## Text

A district may take conveyances, contracts, leases, or other assurances for property acquired by the district pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
