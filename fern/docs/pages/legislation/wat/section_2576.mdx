# WAT § 2576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 4. Proof of Claim

**Citation:** WAT § 2576

---

## Text

Each claimant shall certify to the proof of claim statements under oath or penalty of perjury.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
