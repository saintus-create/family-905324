# WAT § 9043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 5. Land Subject to Lien of Assessment No. 6

**Citation:** WAT § 9043

---

## Text

It is discretionary with the board of trustees of any such reclamation district, levee district or drainage district to apply such warrants in payment of the assessment, or to apply the same as provided by Article 2 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
