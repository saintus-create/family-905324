# WAT § 71959

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Sale of Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 71959

---

## Text

Any bonds issued by a district have the same force, value, and use as bonds issued by a city and are exempt from all taxation within the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
