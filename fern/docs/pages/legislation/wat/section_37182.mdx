# WAT § 37182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37182

---

## Text

If all payments are not made on or before the dates prescribed, a collector’s deed of the property may be delivered and the right of redemption may be terminated in the same manner as if no election to pay delinquent assessments in installments had been made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 50.)
