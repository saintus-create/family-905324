# WAT § 11822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 3. Revenue Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11822

---

## Text

The money remaining in the revenue fund after providing the amount required for interest and redemption of bonds shall be devoted to the payment of the costs of operation and maintenance of the project including necessary replacements thereto to the extent necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
