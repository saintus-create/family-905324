# WAT § 42201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 1. Plans, Specifications, and Estimate of Costs

## Text

The board may use and adopt all previous estimates, surveys, reports, and other data it may have or acquire.
