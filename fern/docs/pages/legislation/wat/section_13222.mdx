# WAT § 13222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 2. General Provisions Relating to Powers and Duties of Regional Boards

**Citation:** WAT § 13222

---

## Text

Pursuant to such guidelines as the state board may establish, each regional board shall adopt regulations to carry out its powers and duties under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
