# WAT § 36573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 2. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 36573

---

## Text

The assessment book shall contain:

(a) The name of the holder of title to the land if known, and if unknown, that fact shall be stated.

(b) A description of the land sufficient to identify it.

(c) The cash value of the land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
