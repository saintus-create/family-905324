# WAT § 25325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 6. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 25325

---

## Text

A district may sell any bonds from time to time in such quantities as may be necessary and most advantageous to raise money for the purposes for which they were authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
