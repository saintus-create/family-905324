# WAT § 13541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Water Reclamation > Article: 6. Waste Well Regulation

**Citation:** WAT § 13541

---

## Text

As used in this article, “waste well” includes any hole dug or drilled into the ground, used or intended to be used for the disposal of waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
