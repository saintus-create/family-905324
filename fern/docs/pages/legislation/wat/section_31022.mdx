# WAT § 31022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 31022

---

## Text

A district may operate water rights, works, property, rights, and privileges useful or necessary to convey, supply, store, or make use of water for any purpose authorized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
