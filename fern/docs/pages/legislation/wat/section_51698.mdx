# WAT § 51698

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 2. Sale After One Year After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51698

---

## Text

The proceeds of the sale of parcels acquired by the county treasurer as trustee for the district at a delinquency sale for unbonded original and additional assessments shall be credited to the general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 804.)
