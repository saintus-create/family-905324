# WAT § 4101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 4. Construction and Maintenance of Works and Measuring Devices > Article: 1. By Owners of Conduits

**Citation:** WAT § 4101

---

## Text

The owner of a conduit shall construct and maintain to the satisfaction of the department a substantial and serviceable headgate in the conduit through which the water is diverted at or near the diversion dam or works.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
