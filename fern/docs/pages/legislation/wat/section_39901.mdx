# WAT § 39901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39901

---

## Text

The notice of the formation election shall contain:

(a) A description of the boundaries of the proposed district or the land included.

(b) A description of each division.

(c) The name of the proposed district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
