# WAT § 8038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8038

---

## Text

Any of the bonds may be issued and sold by the city council at not less than its face value.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
