# WAT § 9369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9369

---

## Text

Duplicate original affidavits of publication and posting of the notice, shall be filed in the office of the board.
