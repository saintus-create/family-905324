# WAT § 9311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9311

---

## Text

The report of payment of the assessment shall be made by the county treasurer at once to the secretary of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
