# WAT § 13490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.7. Cost Recovery, Enforcement, and Administration

**Citation:** WAT § 13490

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to consolidate administrative enforcement authority available to the state board to enforce the terms, conditions, and requirements of its financial assistance programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 187, Sec. 6. (SB 776) Effective January 1, 2022.)
