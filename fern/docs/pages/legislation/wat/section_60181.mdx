# WAT § 60181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 60181

---

## Text

Said board may consolidate the office of secretary and treasurer.
