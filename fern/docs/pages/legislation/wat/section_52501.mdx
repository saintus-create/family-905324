# WAT § 52501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52501

---

## Text

All proceedings for the issuance of refunding bonds commenced prior to August 14, 1929 shall be completed in accordance with the provisions of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
