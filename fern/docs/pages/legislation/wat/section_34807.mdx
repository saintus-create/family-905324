# WAT § 34807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34807

---

## Text

The board, except as otherwise specifically provided, shall manage and conduct the business and affairs of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
