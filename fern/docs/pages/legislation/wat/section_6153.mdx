# WAT § 6153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 5. Action and Procedure to Restrain Violations

**Citation:** WAT § 6153

---

## Text

Any action or proceeding under this article shall be brought by petition in the superior court, alleging the violation or threatened violation complained of, and praying for appropriate relief by way of mandamus or injunction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
