# WAT § 43703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 43703

---

## Text

The costs of any action, proceeding, or contest may be allowed and apportioned between the parties or taxed to the defeated party in the discretion of the court or other tribunal before which it is heard.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
