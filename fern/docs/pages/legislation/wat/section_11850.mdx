# WAT § 11850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 7. Insurance and Indemnity Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11850

---

## Text

When any of the works authorized by this part are being constructed by the department, the department may carry or cause to be carried such an amount of insurance or indemnity bonds as protection against loss or damage as the department deems proper.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
