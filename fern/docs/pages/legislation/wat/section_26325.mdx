# WAT § 26325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 5. Recordation of Deed to District

**Citation:** WAT § 26325

---

## Text

A certificate of the secretary on a collector’s deed stating that it has been accepted by the board entitles the deed to be recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
