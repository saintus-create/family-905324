# WAT § 79067.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 5. Flood Protection Program > Article: 7. San Lorenzo River Flood Control Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79067.2

---

## Text

The sum of two million dollars ($2,000,000) is hereby transferred from the account to the subaccount for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
