# WAT § 46003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46003

---

## Text

In the event the first preliminary assessment is not sufficient, the board shall from time to time levy further preliminary assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
