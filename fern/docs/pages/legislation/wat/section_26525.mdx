# WAT § 26525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 2. Collection by County

**Citation:** WAT § 26525

---

## Text

In case of the neglect or refusal of the collector of any district to perform the duties imposed upon him, the tax collector of the office county shall perform his duties and be accountable therefor upon his official bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
