# WAT § 60308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings

**Citation:** WAT § 60308

---

## Text

The board may appoint a qualified registered engineer familiar with water problems as a hearing officer to conduct said hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
