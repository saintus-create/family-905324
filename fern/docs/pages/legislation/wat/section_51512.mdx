# WAT § 51512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 2. Original and Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51512

---

## Text

If a district is situated partly in different counties the assessment shall be paid into the treasury of the county in which the particular parcel is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
