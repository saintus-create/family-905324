# WAT § 71689.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 5. Sanitation Service

**Citation:** WAT § 71689.23

---

## Text

The district may determine whether sanitary service is necessary or feasible for all residents or properties within the district and shall thereupon make a finding as to which type of customer or area shall be excluded from service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
