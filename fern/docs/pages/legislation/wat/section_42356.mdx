# WAT § 42356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Recommendation of Abandonment of Project > Article: 3. Proceedings After Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42356

---

## Text

If completion of the project is not approved at the election, the board may modify the project or propose a new project. If a revised report is not prepared within six months of the election, the board shall enter an order declaring the project abandoned.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 59.3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
