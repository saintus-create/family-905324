# WAT § 74644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Controversies

**Citation:** WAT § 74644

---

## Text

In all courts, actions, suits, or proceedings, the board may sue, appear, and defend in the name of the district in person or by attorneys.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
