# WAT § 36260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternate Provisions for the Issuance of General Obligation Bonds > Article: 2. Authorization of Bonds and Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 36260

---

## Text

Bonds issued hereunder and interest which accrues thereon shall be paid in a manner identical to bonds issued on behalf of a district or improvement district of a California water district as otherwise provided in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1458.)
