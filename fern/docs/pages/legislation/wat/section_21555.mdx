# WAT § 21555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Divisions and Methods of Electing Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 21555

---

## Text

With regard to submitting a proposal at the general district election, the notices required by Section 12112 of the Elections Code shall contain a statement of the change or changes proposed to appear on the ballot.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 588, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007.)
