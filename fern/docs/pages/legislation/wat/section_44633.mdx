# WAT § 44633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 2. Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 44633

---

## Text

All warrants designated in the notice shall cease to draw interest at the time of the first publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
