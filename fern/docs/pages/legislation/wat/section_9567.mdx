# WAT § 9567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 3. Redemption and Conveyancing of Lands

**Citation:** WAT § 9567

---

## Text

The deed shall convey to the grantee therein named the land free and clear of all encumbrances except State, county and municipal taxes, assessments levied or assessed by statutory authority, and the unpaid balance of any assessment made by the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
