# WAT § 1322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 3. Posting and Mailing Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1322

---

## Text

The applicant shall post the notice within 20 days of the date of issuance thereof in at least two conspicuous places in the locality to be affected by the proposed appropriation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 773.)
