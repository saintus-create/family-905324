# WAT § 53074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 4. Warrants Drawn on Maintenance Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53074

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 2 of this chapter and Article 2, Chapter 2 of this part relating to the payment or renewal of a demand warrant apply to a warrant issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
