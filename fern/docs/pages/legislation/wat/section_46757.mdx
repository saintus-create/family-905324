# WAT § 46757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 5. Sale for Nonpayment

**Citation:** WAT § 46757

---

## Text

Out of the proceeds of sale, the county treasurer shall deposit the amount due on the property as shown in the notice in the proper fund of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
