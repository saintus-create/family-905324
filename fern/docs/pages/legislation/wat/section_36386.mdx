# WAT § 36386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.3. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 36386

---

## Text

No revenue warrants shall be issued or sold until their issuance has been authorized by resolution of the board adopted by a four-fifths vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 1993.)
