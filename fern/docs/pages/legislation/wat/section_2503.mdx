# WAT § 2503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 2503

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “minor quantities of water” refers to the diversion or extraction by any person of not to exceed 10 acre-feet of water annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 794.)
