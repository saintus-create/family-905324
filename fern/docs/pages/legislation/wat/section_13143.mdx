# WAT § 13143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 3. State Water Quality Control > Article: 3. State Policy for Water Quality Control

**Citation:** WAT § 13143

---

## Text

State policy for water quality control shall be periodically reviewed and may be revised.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
