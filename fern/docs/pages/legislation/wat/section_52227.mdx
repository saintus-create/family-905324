# WAT § 52227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 2. Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 52227

---

## Text

The board may draw demand warrants upon the county treasurer against the funds provided by sale of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
