# WAT § 9469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9469

---

## Text

Sales of any bonds purchased and held in the bond fund shall from time to time be made so that the proceeds may be applied to the purpose for which the bond fund was created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
