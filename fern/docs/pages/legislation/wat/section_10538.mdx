# WAT § 10538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10538

---

## Text

“Regional reports or studies” means reports or studies relating to any of the matters described in subdivisions (a) to (f), inclusive, of Section 10537, that are identified in an integrated regional water management plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 5. Effective March 1, 2009.)
