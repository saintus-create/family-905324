# WAT § 22847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 2. Internal Organization

**Citation:** WAT § 22847

---

## Text

The board may:

(a) Appoint one person to hold the offices of assessor, treasurer, and collector.

(b) Segregate the offices and appoint a person to fill each office.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 33.)
