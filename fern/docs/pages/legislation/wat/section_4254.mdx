# WAT § 4254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 3. Apportionment of Expense

**Citation:** WAT § 4254

---

## Text

In making an apportionment to any owner of the right to store water, 350 acre-feet of storage capacity which the owner is entitled to use shall be considered the equivalent of the right to divert one cubic foot per second of the direct flow of a stream.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
