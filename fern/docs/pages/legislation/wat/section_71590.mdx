# WAT § 71590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 71590

---

## Text

A district may exercise the powers which are expressly granted by this division or are necessarily implied.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
