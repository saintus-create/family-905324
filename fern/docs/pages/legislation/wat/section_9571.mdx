# WAT § 9571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 3. Redemption and Conveyancing of Lands

**Citation:** WAT § 9571

---

## Text

All deeds required by this chapter to be executed by the board may be executed by the president and secretary thereof on behalf of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
