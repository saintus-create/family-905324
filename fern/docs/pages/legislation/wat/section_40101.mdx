# WAT § 40101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 40101

---

## Text

The department shall immediately file for record in the office of the county recorder of each affected county a certified copy of the order declaring the district formed.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
