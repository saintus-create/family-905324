# WAT § 24271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Settlements and Statements

**Citation:** WAT § 24271

---

## Text

The collector shall be credited in his settlement with both:

(a) The amount due to the district on property he sells to the district for delinquent assessments.

(b) The amount by which any assessment is canceled or modified by order of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
