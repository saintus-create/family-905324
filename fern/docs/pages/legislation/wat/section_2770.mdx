# WAT § 2770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2770

---

## Text

Upon the hearing the court may assess and adjudge against any party such costs as it may deem just.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
