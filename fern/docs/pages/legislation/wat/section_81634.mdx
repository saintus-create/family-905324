# WAT § 81634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Government of Authority > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 81634

---

## Text

The board shall hold its first meeting as soon as possible after the appointment of the initial directors. At its first meeting, and at its first meeting in January each year thereafter, the board shall elect a chairperson and a vice-chairperson from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
