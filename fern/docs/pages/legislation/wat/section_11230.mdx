# WAT § 11230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 6. Madera Canal

**Citation:** WAT § 11230

---

## Text

The unit designated as the Madera Canal consists of a canal or conduit leading from the reservoir of Friant Dam northerly to a point on the Chowchilla River.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
