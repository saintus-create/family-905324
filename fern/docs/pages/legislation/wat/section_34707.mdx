# WAT § 34707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualifications and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 34707

---

## Text

If any office becomes vacant from any cause it shall be filled by appointment by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
