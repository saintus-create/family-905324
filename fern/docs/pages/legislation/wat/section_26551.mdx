# WAT § 26551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 3. Supervision by District Attorney and Attorney General

**Citation:** WAT § 26551

---

## Text

Unless the board of supervisors or county official proceeds to perform the duties he has been notified to perform within 30 days after the receipt of notice, the district attorney shall take action in court to compel performance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
