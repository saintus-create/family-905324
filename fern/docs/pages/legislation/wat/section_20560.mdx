# WAT § 20560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 20560

---

## Text

Districts, regardless of the date of formation, are subject to the provisions of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
