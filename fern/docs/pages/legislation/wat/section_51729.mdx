# WAT § 51729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51729

---

## Text

The partial payment shall be forfeited if the purchaser fails to complete the purchase of the parcel within 20 days after tender of the deed thereto by the county treasurer, and a demand for the balance of the purchase price.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
