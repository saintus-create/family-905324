# WAT § 36433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36433

---

## Text

The board may advance general funds of the district to accomplish an approved plan of works of an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 104.)
