# WAT § 8827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8827

---

## Text

The board may modify or amend the assessment, and may reapportion all or any part of the entire assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
