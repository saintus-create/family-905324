# WAT § 23534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23534

---

## Text

The secretary shall cause a certified copy of the order creating the distribution district to be recorded in the office of the county recorder in each county in which any land of the distribution district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
