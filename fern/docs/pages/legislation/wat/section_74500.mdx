# WAT § 74500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 74500

---

## Text

A district may:

(a) Have perpetual succession.

(b) Adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
