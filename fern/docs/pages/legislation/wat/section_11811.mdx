# WAT § 11811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 2. Construction Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11811

---

## Text

The construction fund shall at all times be kept segregated and set apart from all other funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
