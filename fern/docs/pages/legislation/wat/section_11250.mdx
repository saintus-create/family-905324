# WAT § 11250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 9. Tehama-Butte Conduit

**Citation:** WAT § 11250

---

## Text

The unit designated as the Tehama-Butte Conduit consists of a conduit or canal, equipped with all necessary dams, pumping plants, and other appurtenant works, extending from the Sacramento River at or near Red Bluff southerly through Tehama, Butte, and Sutter counties.
