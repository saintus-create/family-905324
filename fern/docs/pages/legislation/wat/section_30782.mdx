# WAT § 30782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 3. Preparation for Election

**Citation:** WAT § 30782

---

## Text

Half-inch spaces shall be left below the printed names of candidates for each office, equal in number to the number of offices to be filled, wherein the voter may write the name of any person for whom he wishes to vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
