# WAT § 8571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8571

---

## Text

The board may adopt, amend or repeal rules to promote the convenient, orderly and just conduct of the business of the board and of the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
