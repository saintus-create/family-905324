# WAT § 50977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Co-operation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 50977

---

## Text

The board may execute the contract on behalf of the district if a majority of the votes cast at the election favor the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1111.)
