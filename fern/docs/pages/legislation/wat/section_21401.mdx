# WAT § 21401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. District Office

**Citation:** WAT § 21401

---

## Text

The board shall fix the time during which the office of the district shall be open to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
