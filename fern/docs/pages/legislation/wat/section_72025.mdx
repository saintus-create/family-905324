# WAT § 72025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 72025

---

## Text

If from the special election returns it appears that a majority of the votes cast at such election were in favor of the formation of the improvement district, the formation of the improvement district shall be complete.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
