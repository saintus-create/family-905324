# WAT § 26280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 3. Deed on Termination of Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26280

---

## Text

The collector shall receive from the purchaser for the use of the district two dollars ($2.00) for making the deed except when the deed is to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
