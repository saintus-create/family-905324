# WAT § 60008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60008

---

## Text

“General water replenishment district election” means the election required to be held in each district at the time of the general election in each even-numbered year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 7, Sec. 1. Effective February 8, 1984.)
