# WAT § 25507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25507

---

## Text

Land as to which a partial redemption has been effected shall thereafter be separately described on the assessment book.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
