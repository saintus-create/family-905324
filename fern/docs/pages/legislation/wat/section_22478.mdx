# WAT § 22478

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 3. Power Lines on Public Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22478

---

## Text

A district constructing lines pursuant to this article shall restore the road, railway, or conduit intersected to its former state of usefulness as near as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
