# WAT § 8601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 8601

---

## Text

The board may construct, purchase, rent, sell or exchange dredgers, machines, appliances, tools, apparatus and other property necessary or convenient for doing any construction work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
