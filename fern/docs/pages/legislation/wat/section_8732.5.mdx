# WAT § 8732.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8732.5

---

## Text

All hearings or partial hearings shall be open and public.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
