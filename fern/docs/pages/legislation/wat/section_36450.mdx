# WAT § 36450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 36450

---

## Text

Improvement districts may be formed in districts in the same manner as improvement districts are formed in irrigation districts. When formed, such improvement districts shall be governed in the same manner as improvement districts in irrigation districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 991.)
