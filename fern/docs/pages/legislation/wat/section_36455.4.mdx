# WAT § 36455.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 1. Authorization of Improvements

**Citation:** WAT § 36455.4

---

## Text

A district may enter into an agreement with any municipality within the district whereby any work or improvement constructed pursuant to the provisions of this chapter may be transferred to the municipality for operation and maintenance. The power to make such an agreement is conditioned upon a finding by the board that the residents of the district would be better served by such municipal operation and maintenance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 268.)
