# WAT § 45406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 45406

---

## Text

Sale and delivery of bonds by the treasurer is conclusive evidence that the sale was made upon due authority and notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
