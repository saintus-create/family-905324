# WAT § 31650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31650

---

## Text

A district may cause taxes to be levied for the purpose of paying any of its obligations and to accomplish the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
