# WAT § 55991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Part: 6. REPEALS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 55991

---

## Text

The repeal effected by this part shall not be construed to deprive any district or any person or other entity of any substantial right which would have existed or hereafter exists had such repeal not be effected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
