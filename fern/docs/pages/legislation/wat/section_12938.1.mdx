# WAT § 12938.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Water Resources Development Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 12938.1

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 13320) of Chapter 3, Part 3, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code are applicable to the department with respect to expenditures of money pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1955.)
