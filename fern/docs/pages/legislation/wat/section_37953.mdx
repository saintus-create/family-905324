# WAT § 37953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Groundwater management

**Citation:** WAT § 37953

---

## Text

The board may, by ordinance, establish reasonable methods to be used in computing the amount of water extracted by extraction facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 350, Sec. 1. (AB 2453) Effective January 1, 2015.)
