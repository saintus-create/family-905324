# WAT § 32550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Inclusion in Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 32550

---

## Text

Territory within a district may be annexed to an improvement district whether or not contiguous thereto, provided that such territory is not a part of an improvement district constituted for a similar purpose.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 425.)
