# WAT § 31021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 31021

---

## Text

A district may store water for the benefit of the district, conserve water for future use, and appropriate, acquire, and conserve water and water rights for any useful purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
