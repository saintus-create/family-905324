# WAT § 71051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 71051

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, as used in this part, “board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
