# WAT § 79155.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 8. Water Conservation Program > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79155.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, regulations set forth in Chapter 2.3 (commencing with Section 450.1) of Division 2 of Title 23 of the California Code of Regulations that are in effect on March 8, 2000, may be used to carry out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
