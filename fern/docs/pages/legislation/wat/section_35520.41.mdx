# WAT § 35520.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.2. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Lost Hills Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35520.41

---

## Text

After any toll or charge becomes delinquent, the board may, by resolution, direct the assessor to add to the assessment of the parcel of land to which it relates all delinquent tolls and charges, penalties, and interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 57, Sec. 1. Effective May 31, 1983.)
