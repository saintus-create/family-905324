# WAT § 35206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Method of Elections for the Borrego Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35206

---

## Text

This chapter shall in no way invalidate any act of the district or of its directors, officers, and employees made or performed while the district was operating as a landowner voter district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 456, Sec. 1.)
