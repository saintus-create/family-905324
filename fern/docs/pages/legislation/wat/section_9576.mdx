# WAT § 9576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 4. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** WAT § 9576

---

## Text

The State Treasurer shall place the amount to the credit of the bond fund of the drainage district for the particular bond issue upon the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
