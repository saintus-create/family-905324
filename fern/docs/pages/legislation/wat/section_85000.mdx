# WAT § 85000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Legislative Findings

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta Reform Act of 2009.

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
