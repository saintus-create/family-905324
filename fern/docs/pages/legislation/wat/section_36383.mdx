# WAT § 36383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.3. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 36383

---

## Text

Revenue warrants shall mature at such time or times as the board may prescribe, but not more than 10 years from the date of issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1117.)
