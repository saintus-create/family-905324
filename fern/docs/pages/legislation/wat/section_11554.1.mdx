# WAT § 11554.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Construction of Project by the Department of Water Resources > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11554.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provisions of the State Contract Act, the department may award and enter into construction contracts which require expenditures in excess of funds presently available therefor, but the obligation of the department and the State in such contracts shall be expressly contingent upon sufficient funds being made available by future appropriations or by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2054.)
