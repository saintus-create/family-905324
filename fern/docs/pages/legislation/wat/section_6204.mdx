# WAT § 6204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 1. New Dams and Reservoirs or Enlargements of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6204

---

## Text

In instances wherein the physical conditions involved and the size of the dam or reservoir are such as to render the above requirements as to drainage areas, rainfall, streamflow, floodflow, and drilling or prospecting of site unnecessary, the department may waive the requirements.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
