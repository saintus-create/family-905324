# WAT § 34806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34806

---

## Text

The vote of a majority of the directors present at any meeting attended by a quorum is necessary to determine any proposition or resolution presented.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
