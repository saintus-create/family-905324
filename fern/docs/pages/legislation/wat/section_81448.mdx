# WAT § 81448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Water Supply

**Citation:** WAT § 81448

---

## Text

The agency may not sell water to any retail user within the boundaries of the agency. Sales to Stanford University shall not be deemed sales to a retail user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
