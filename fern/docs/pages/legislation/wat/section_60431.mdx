# WAT § 60431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60431

---

## Text

Upon the filing of a petition the county elections official shall examine the petition within 30 days and ascertain whether or not the petition is signed by the requisite number of voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 187. Effective January 1, 2003.)
