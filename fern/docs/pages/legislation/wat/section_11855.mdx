# WAT § 11855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 7. Insurance and Indemnity Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11855

---

## Text

The department may provide in the proceedings authorizing the issuance of bonds for the carrying of insurance as authorized by this part, and the purchase and carrying of such insurance shall thereupon be obligatory upon the department and be paid for out of the revenue fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
