# WAT § 8605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 8605

---

## Text

The board may inspect and supervise any work done or under construction pursuant to the provisions of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
