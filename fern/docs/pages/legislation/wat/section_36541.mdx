# WAT § 36541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Dissolution of Distribution Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 36541

---

## Text

A hearing on dissolution shall be had in the same manner and after the same notice as is required for the formation of a distribution district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
