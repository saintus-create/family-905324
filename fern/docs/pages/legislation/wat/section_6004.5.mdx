# WAT § 6004.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 6004.5

---

## Text

“Reservoir” means any reservoir which contains or will contain the water impounded by a dam.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
