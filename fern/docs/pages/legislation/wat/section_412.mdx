# WAT § 412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 4. Weather Resources Management > Article: 2. Notice of Intention

**Citation:** WAT § 412

---

## Text

Proof of publication shall be by copy of the notice as published attached to and made a part of the affidavit of the publisher or foreman of the newspaper publishing the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 950, Sec. 2.)
