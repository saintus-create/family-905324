# WAT § 39929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39929

---

## Text

No director shall be elected by the district at large, but one director shall be elected by each division to represent that division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
