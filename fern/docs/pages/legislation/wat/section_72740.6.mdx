# WAT § 72740.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Consolidation of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 72740.6

---

## Text

The determinations made in the resolution of consolidation shall be final and conclusive, and the resolution of consolidation shall be effective on the 91st day after completion of the publication and posting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 555, Sec. 4. Effective August 22, 1986.)
