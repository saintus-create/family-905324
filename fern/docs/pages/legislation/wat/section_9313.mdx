# WAT § 9313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9313

---

## Text

The State Treasurer shall credit the amount of assessments received by him to the construction fund of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
