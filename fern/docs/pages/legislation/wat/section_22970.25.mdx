# WAT § 22970.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 7. Runoff Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 22970.25

---

## Text

The county elections official shall commence the canvass of the returns on the first Wednesday after each runoff election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 156. Effective January 1, 2003.)
