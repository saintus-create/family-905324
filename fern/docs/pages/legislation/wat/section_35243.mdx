# WAT § 35243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Method of Election for the Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35243

---

## Text

All directors elected after January 1, 1994, shall be residents of the district and shall be registered at the time of their election and during their term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1047, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
