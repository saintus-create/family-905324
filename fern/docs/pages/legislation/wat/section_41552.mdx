# WAT § 41552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Special Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41552

---

## Text

The notice shall specify the time, place, and purpose of the election.
