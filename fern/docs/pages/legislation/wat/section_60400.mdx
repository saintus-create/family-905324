# WAT § 60400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Exclusion

**Citation:** WAT § 60400

---

## Text

Territory included within any district may be excluded from such district and the parcels so to be excluded need not be contiguous one with the other.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
