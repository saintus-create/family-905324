# WAT § 7030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 3. Bridges and Conduits on or Near Highways

**Citation:** WAT § 7030

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “conduit” includes canal, ditch, culvert, pipeline, flume, or other appliance for conducting water.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1786.)
