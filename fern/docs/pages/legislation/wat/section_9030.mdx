# WAT § 9030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 3. Exchange of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9030

---

## Text

Proper entries of the surrender payment shall be made upon the books of the reclamation district, and also of the county treasurer of the county having the custody of the funds of the reclamation district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
