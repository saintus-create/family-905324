# WAT § 51667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 1. Sale After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51667

---

## Text

The proceeds of a sale of parcels acquired by the county treasurer as trustee for the district at a delinquency sale for operation and maintenance assessments shall be credited to the maintenance fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 804.)
