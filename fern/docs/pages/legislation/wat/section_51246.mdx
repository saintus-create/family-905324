# WAT § 51246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51246

---

## Text

No objection shall be considered by the board of supervisors, or allowed in any other action or proceeding, unless it is made in writing to the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
