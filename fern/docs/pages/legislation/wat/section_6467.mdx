# WAT § 6467

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 10. Dams and Reservoirs Under State Supervision Through 1965 Revisions of Part 1 > Article: 2. Dams and Reservoirs Under Construction Before 1965 Revisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6467

---

## Text

Such dams or reservoirs as are 90 percent or more constructed on September 17, 1965, shall be subject to the same supervision as dams or reservoirs which were completed prior thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
