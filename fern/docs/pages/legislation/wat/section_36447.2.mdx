# WAT § 36447.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.95. Alternative Provisions for the Issuance of Bonds of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 36447.2

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapters 2 (commencing with Section 35950) and 3 (commencing with Section 36150) shall apply to the consolidated bonds to the extent they may be made applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 998, Sec. 3. Effective September 11, 1984.)
