# WAT § 51517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 2. Original and Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51517

---

## Text

All payments on unpaid original assessments shall be made in separate installments, of such amounts, and at such times as the board, by order entered in its minutes, may direct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
