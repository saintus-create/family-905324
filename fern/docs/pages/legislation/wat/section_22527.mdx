# WAT § 22527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 5. Lease of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 22527

---

## Text

When the board contemplates the leasing of the works of the district, it shall give notice thereof at least three weeks prior to the making of the lease, by publication at least once in some newspaper published in the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
