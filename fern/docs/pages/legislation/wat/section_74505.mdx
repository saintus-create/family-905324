# WAT § 74505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 74505

---

## Text

A district shall provide for the payment, from the proper fund, of all the debts and just claims against the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
