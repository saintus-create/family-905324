# WAT § 24477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24477

---

## Text

A district may establish any fund required to comply with the terms of any plan by which any bonds are to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
