# WAT § 31586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31586

---

## Text

Division 4 (commencing at Section 2800) of the Streets and Highways Code shall not be applicable to the formation of any improvement district pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
