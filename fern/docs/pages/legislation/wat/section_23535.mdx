# WAT § 23535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23535

---

## Text

All lands of the distribution district shall remain jointly liable for all obligations accruing to the United States under any contract between the distribution district and the United States pursuant to the federal reclamation laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
