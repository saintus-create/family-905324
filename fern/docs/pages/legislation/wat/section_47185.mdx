# WAT § 47185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 47185

---

## Text

In the suit the district may recover the amount of the toll or charge, together with penalties and interest, and costs of suit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
