# WAT § 78684.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 7. CALFED Bay-Delta Ecosystem Restoration Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78684.14

---

## Text

Not more than 3 percent of the total amount deposited in the account may be used to pay the costs incurred in connection with the administration of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
