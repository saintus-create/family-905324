# WAT § 8969

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8969

---

## Text

The Controller shall notify the State Treasurer of the cancellation and the State Treasurer shall then make the proper entries in his books.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
