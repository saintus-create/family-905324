# WAT § 6362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 2. Repaired or Altered Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6362

---

## Text

As soon as practicable the dam or reservoir as repaired or altered shall be inspected by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
