# WAT § 23510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 2. Plans and Estimate of Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23510

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a petition to form a distribution district the board shall cause a survey to be made of the proposed distribution system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
