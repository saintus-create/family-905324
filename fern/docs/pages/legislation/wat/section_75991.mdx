# WAT § 75991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Annexation of Districts > Article: 4. Conditions to Annexation

**Citation:** WAT § 75991

---

## Text

Bonds of the petitioning district may be voted upon and issued in the manner provided for the incurring of bonded indebtedness of the district and may thereafter be delivered at not less than par value to constitute payment under Section 75990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
