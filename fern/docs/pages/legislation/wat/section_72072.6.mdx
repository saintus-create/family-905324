# WAT § 72072.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 6. Powers—Utility Tax

**Citation:** WAT § 72072.6

---

## Text

Taxes collected from the service user which are not remitted to the board on or before the due dates provided in this article are delinquent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
