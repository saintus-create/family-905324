# WAT § 26674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Collection, Payment in Lieu of Assessment and Distribution of Water by Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26674

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter authorizes the sale or transfer of any water right nor shall the agreement authorized in Section 26671 be construed or deemed to constitute the sale or transfer of a water right.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 603.)
