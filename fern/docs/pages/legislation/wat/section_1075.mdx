# WAT § 1075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Witnesses and Production of Evidence > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 1075

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “proceeding” means any inquiry, investigation, hearing, ascertainment, or other proceeding ordered or undertaken by the board pursuant to this code.
