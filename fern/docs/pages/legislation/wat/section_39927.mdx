# WAT § 39927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39927

---

## Text

The formation election shall be conducted as nearly as practicable in accordance with the provisions of this division concerning general elections, but no particular form of ballot shall be required.

The qualifications of voters at a formation election shall be the same as the qualifications of voters for general elections provided for in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
