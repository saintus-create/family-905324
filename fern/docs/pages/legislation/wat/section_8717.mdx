# WAT § 8717

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8717

---

## Text

All emergency work shall be subject to the subsequent approval of the board, and the board may require its removal or alteration if not approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
