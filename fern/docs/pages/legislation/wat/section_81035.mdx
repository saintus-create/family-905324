# WAT § 81035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. CALCONSERVE WATER USE EFFICIENCY REVOLVING LOAN PROGRAM > Part: 2. CalConserve Water Use Efficiency Revolving Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 81035

---

## Text

Any repayment of fund moneys, including interest payments, and all interest earned on, or accruing to, any moneys in the fund, shall be deposited in the fund and shall be available for expenditure for the purposes and uses authorized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 825, Sec. 2. (AB 2636) Effective January 1, 2015.)
