# WAT § 12890.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 10. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12890.2

---

## Text

In making loans or grants pursuant to this chapter, the department shall impose terms and conditions that are designed to protect the state’s investment and that are necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
