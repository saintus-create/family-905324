# WAT § 31143.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9.5. San Lorenzo Valley Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31143.5

---

## Text

Any violation of a regulation of the district adopted pursuant to Section 31143 is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500), or imprisonment not to exceed 60 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment. Each day of such a violation shall constitute a separate offense. Any violation or threatened violation of a regulation of the district may also be enjoined by civil suit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1135, Sec. 1.)
