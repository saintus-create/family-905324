# WAT § 1226.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2.5. Stockponds

**Citation:** WAT § 1226.4

---

## Text

The board may, after notice and hearing, revoke any certificate of a water right granted pursuant to this article upon a finding that the water has ceased to be used for the purposes specified in Section 1226.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 667, Sec. 7. Effective September 20, 1996.)
