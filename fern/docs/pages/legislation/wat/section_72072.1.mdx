# WAT § 72072.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 6. Powers—Utility Tax

**Citation:** WAT § 72072.1

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as imposing a tax upon any person when the imposition of such tax upon that person would be in violation of the Constitution of the United States or the Constitution of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
