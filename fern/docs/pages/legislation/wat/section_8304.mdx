# WAT § 8304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Powers of Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8304

---

## Text

The department may obtain or condemn any right-of-way necessary for any construction under this chapter.
