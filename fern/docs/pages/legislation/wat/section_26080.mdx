# WAT § 26080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 1. Date and Penalties for Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 26080

---

## Text

The assessment book, a copy of any portion of it certified by the collector, or the published list of delinquencies, showing unpaid assessments against any property is prima facie evidence of the assessment, the property assessed, the delinquency, the amount of assessments due and unpaid, and compliance with all forms of law relating to the assessment, equalization, and levy of the assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
