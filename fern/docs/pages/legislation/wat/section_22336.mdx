# WAT § 22336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 5. Supervision by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 22336

---

## Text

During the construction of any work to be paid for out of the proceeds of the sale of any bonds of any district, the secretary shall within one week after each regular meeting of the board forward to the department copies of all reports made to the board as to the progress of the work and a statement of the amounts paid for any part of the work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
