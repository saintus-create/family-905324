# WAT § 31156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation > Article: 1. Cooperation Under Irrigation District Federal Cooperation Law

**Citation:** WAT § 31156

---

## Text

The contract shall not include any land which is not susceptible of service with water from the water works or system contemplated in the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
