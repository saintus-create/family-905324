# WAT § 10726.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Powers and Authorities

**Citation:** WAT § 10726.9

---

## Text

A groundwater sustainability plan shall take into account the most recent planning assumptions stated in local general plans of jurisdictions overlying the basin.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 347, Sec. 14. (AB 1739) Effective January 1, 2015.)
