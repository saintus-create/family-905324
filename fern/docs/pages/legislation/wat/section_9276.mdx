# WAT § 9276

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 9276

---

## Text

No objection to the assessment shall be considered by the court unless the objection has been made in writing to the board as provided in Part 4 of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1536.)
