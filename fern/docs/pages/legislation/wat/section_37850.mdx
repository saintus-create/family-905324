# WAT § 37850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 37850

---

## Text

Except as in this part expressly provided, the surviving district shall in all respects be operated, managed, and governed, its directors and officers shall be elected or appointed, and its revenues shall be raised and expended as provided by law for California water districts generally.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
