# WAT § 31046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Property Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31046

---

## Text

A district may sell or lease the mineral or hydrocarbon rights in any land belonging to it when deemed by the board for the best interest of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
