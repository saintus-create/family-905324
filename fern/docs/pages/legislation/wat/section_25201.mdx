# WAT § 25201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25201

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this article the board shall prescribe the form of the bonds issued by the district and of the attached interest coupons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
