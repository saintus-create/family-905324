# WAT § 74033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 74033

---

## Text

The territory to be included within a district need not be contiguous.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
