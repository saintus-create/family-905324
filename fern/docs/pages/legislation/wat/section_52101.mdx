# WAT § 52101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52101

---

## Text

Bonds shall be of the denomination of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) each.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
