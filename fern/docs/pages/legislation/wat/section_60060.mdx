# WAT § 60060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Information by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 60060

---

## Text

The department shall supply information so far as it may be practicable to persons contemplating the formation of districts.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
