# WAT § 51252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

## Text

The endorsement shall be signed by the chairman of the board of supervisors and attested by the clerk thereof.
