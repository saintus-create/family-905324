# WAT § 6309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 6. Fees

**Citation:** WAT § 6309

---

## Text

The fees provided for in this chapter shall be required of any “owner,” as defined in Section 6005.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 95. Effective January 1, 2004.)
