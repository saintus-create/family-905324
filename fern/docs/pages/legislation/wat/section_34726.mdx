# WAT § 34726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Consolidation and Segregation

**Citation:** WAT § 34726

---

## Text

The order of consolidation of offices shall be made at least 90 days prior to a general district election.
