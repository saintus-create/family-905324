# WAT § 7042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 4. Maintenance of Flow in Streams

**Citation:** WAT § 7042

---

## Text

No act authorized in this chapter when performed, shall prevent, retard or obstruct the building and maintenance thereafter of any reclamation, protection, or flood control levee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
