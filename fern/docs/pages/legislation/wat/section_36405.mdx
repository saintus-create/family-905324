# WAT § 36405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.5. Provisions Relating to Short Term Borrowings

**Citation:** WAT § 36405

---

## Text

Coupons payable to bearer shall be in a form and signed as prescribed by the board and may be attached to warrants issued under this chapter to evidence their interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1954, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 2.)
