# WAT § 13896.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.7. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13896.1

---

## Text

Applications for grants under this chapter shall be made to the department in the form and with the supporting material as prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 410, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 55 at the November 4, 1986, election.)
