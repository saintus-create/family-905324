# WAT § 44962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 44962

---

## Text

Revenue warrants shall not be issued under this chapter by any district in any one fiscal year in excess of one million dollars ($1,000,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 36.)
