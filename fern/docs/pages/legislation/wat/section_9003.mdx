# WAT § 9003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 1. Flood Control Works, Purchases and Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9003

---

## Text

In lieu of levying an assessment pursuant to Section 9002 the cost of the claim may be included as one of the items in any assessment that may be levied in the drainage district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
