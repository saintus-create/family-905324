# WAT § 41390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 4. Returns of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 41390

---

## Text

The ballots shall be strung upon a cord or thread by the inspector during the counting in the order in which they are entered upon the tally list by the judges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
