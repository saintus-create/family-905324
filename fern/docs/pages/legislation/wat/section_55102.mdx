# WAT § 55102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 55102

---

## Text

A petition for the formation of a district may be presented to the board of supervisors.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
