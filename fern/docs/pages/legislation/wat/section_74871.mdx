# WAT § 74871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 6. Sale of Bonds and Use of Proceeds

**Citation:** WAT § 74871

---

## Text

The proceeds from the sale of bonds shall be paid into the treasury of the district, placed to the credit of a special fund, and expended only for the purpose for which the indebtedness was created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
