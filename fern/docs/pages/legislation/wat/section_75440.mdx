# WAT § 75440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75440

---

## Text

If sufficient objections are not presented to the board at the hearing, it shall call an election to be held in the district for the purpose of authorizing the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
