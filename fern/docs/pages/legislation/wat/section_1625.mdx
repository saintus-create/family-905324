# WAT § 1625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Licenses > Article: 5. Contents and Conditions of License

**Citation:** WAT § 1625

---

## Text

Each license shall be in such form and contain such terms as may be prescribed by the board.
