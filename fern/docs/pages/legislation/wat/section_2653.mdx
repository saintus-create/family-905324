# WAT § 2653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 7. Hearing of Objections

**Citation:** WAT § 2653

---

## Text

One copy of the transcript of testimony taken at the hearing shall be furnished to the board at the cost of the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
