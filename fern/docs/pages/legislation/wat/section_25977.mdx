# WAT § 25977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 3. Segregation Before Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 25977

---

## Text

The application shall be signed, as evidence of their approval, by each of the persons assessed on the current assessment book with the parcel to be segregated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
