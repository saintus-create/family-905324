# WAT § 25984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 3. Segregation Before Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 25984

---

## Text

The aggregate of the valuations of the segregated portions shall be the same as the valuation of the parcel before segregation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
