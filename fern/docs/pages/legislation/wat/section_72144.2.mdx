# WAT § 72144.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Chino Basin Production Assessments > Article: 5. Advisory Committee

**Citation:** WAT § 72144.2

---

## Text

The advisory committee shall review all proposed studies, programs, and expenditures of proceeds of production assessments under this chapter and shall advise and consult with the board in the administration of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 165.)
