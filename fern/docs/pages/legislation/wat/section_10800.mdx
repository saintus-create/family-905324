# WAT § 10800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Declarations and Policy

**Citation:** WAT § 10800

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Agricultural Water Management Planning Act.
