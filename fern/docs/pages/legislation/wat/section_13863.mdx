# WAT § 13863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.5. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1976

**Citation:** WAT § 13863

---

## Text

As much of the moneys in the fund as may be necessary shall be used to reimburse the General Obligation Bond Expense Revolving Fund pursuant to Section 16724.5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1008. Approved in Proposition 3 at the June 8, 1976, election.)
