# WAT § 8128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Flood Control in Counties > Article: 4. Improvement of Non-Navigable Streams

**Citation:** WAT § 8128

---

## Text

No regulations of the board nor improvements directed by it to be made shall in any manner interfere with the private rights or privileges of riparian owners, miners, or others.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
