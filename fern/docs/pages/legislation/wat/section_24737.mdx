# WAT § 24737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 1. Surrender of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24737

---

## Text

Upon the making of the order, the bonds or warrants shall cease to be an obligation of the district as of the time of their presentation to the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
