# WAT § 9029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 3. Exchange of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9029

---

## Text

After the exchange has been effected, the warrants of the district shall be marked: “Surrendered for warrants of Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
