# WAT § 75885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 75885

---

## Text

If more than one petition for the inclusion of land has been presented, the board may in one order include within the district any number of separate tracts of land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
