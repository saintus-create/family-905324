# WAT § 25931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 1. Collection Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 25931

---

## Text

The collector shall mark the date of payment of any assessment in the assessment book opposite the name of the assessee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
