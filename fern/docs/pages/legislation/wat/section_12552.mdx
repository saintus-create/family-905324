# WAT § 12552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. The Colorado River Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12552

---

## Text

All contributions accepted by the board shall be deposited by it in the State Treasury to the credit of the fund.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
