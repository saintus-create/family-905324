# WAT § 12929.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 5. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.40

---

## Text

In developing its recommendations for the appropriation of funds pursuant to this chapter, the department shall consult with the Department of Fish and Game and other appropriate local, state, and federal agencies and interested private groups and members of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
