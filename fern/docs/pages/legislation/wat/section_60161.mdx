# WAT § 60161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Board Action

**Citation:** WAT § 60161

---

## Text

On all ordinances the roll shall be called and the ayes and noes recorded in the journal of the proceedings of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
