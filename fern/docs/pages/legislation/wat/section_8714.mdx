# WAT § 8714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8714

---

## Text

No claim shall ever be made against the board or the drainage district for compensation, through or by assessment or otherwise, for any part of the work under Section 8713 in excess of the requirements of the plan of flood control for that locality finally adopted and approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
