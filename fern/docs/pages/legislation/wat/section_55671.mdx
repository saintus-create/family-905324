# WAT § 55671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 6. Zones

**Citation:** WAT § 55671

---

## Text

The board shall appoint three qualified electors residing in the proposed zone to conduct the election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
