# WAT § 25557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 3. Equalization

**Citation:** WAT § 25557

---

## Text

The board acting as a board of equalization shall order any changes in the assessment that it deems just.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
