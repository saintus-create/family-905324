# WAT § 6253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 3. Dams Constructed Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6253

---

## Text

The notice provided for in this article may be given by registered mail and a return receipt signed by the owner shall constitute prima facie evidence of service.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
