# WAT § 35425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 35425

---

## Text

If its board deems it to be for the best interests of the district, a district may enter into a contract for the lease, sale, or use of any surplus water not then necessary for use within the district, for use either within or without the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
