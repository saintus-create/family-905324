# WAT § 23804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23804

---

## Text

Land, in the proposed improvement district, located outside the district shall be described in the petition by each owner, which shall be according to the next preceding equalized county assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 717.)
