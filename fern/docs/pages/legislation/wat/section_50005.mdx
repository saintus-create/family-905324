# WAT § 50005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50005

---

## Text

“Trustee” means a member of the board of trustees of a reclamation district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
