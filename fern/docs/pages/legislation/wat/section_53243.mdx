# WAT § 53243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 3. Lost, Destroyed, Mutilated or Defaced Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53243

---

## Text

The bond must be approved by the board and conditioned to indemnify and save harmless the district for any claim upon the lost or destroyed warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
