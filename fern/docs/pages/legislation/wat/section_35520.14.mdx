# WAT § 35520.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.2. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Lost Hills Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35520.14

---

## Text

After the hearing of objections, the board shall, by resolution, approve the assessment as finally fixed or modified. A certified copy of the assessment roll as finally fixed shall be filed in the district records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 57, Sec. 1. Effective May 31, 1983.)
