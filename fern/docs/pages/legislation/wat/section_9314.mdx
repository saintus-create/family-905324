# WAT § 9314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9314

---

## Text

All assessments not paid in full within the 30-day period bear interest thereafter at the rate of 7 per cent per annum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
