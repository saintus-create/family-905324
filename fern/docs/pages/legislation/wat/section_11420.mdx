# WAT § 11420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11420

---

## Text

All books and papers pertaining to matters provided for in this part shall at all reasonable times be open to the inspection of any party interested or any citizen of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
