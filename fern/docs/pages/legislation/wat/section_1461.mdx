# WAT § 1461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Priorities > Article: 3. Preferred Priorities of Municipalities

**Citation:** WAT § 1461

---

## Text

The application for, or the granting of, a permit to any municipality to appropriate water does not authorize the appropriation of any water for other than municipal purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
