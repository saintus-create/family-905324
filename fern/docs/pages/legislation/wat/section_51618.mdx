# WAT § 51618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 1. Delinquency Sales on Unbonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51618

---

## Text

If, prior to the redemption, the parcel has been sold at a delinquency sale, the money paid by the county treasurer at the sale shall be refunded to him or her upon redemption of the parcel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 322, Sec. 9. Effective August 22, 1994.)
