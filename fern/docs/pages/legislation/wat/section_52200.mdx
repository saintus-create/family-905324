# WAT § 52200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 52200

---

## Text

All provisions of Chapter 1 of this part are applicable to bonds issued pursuant to this article.
