# WAT § 35028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 2. Establishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 35028

---

## Text

After the entry of the order establishing divisions within a district, one director shall be elected from each division and shall be a holder of title to land therein.
