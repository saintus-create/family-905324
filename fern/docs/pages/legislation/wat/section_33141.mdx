# WAT § 33141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 33141

---

## Text

After the merger no action or proceeding shall be brought for or against the storm water district or its trustees or officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
