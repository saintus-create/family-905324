# WAT § 47104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 1. Report and Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 47104

---

## Text

At the hearing all protestants shall be permitted to appear in person or by attorney and present their objections to the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
