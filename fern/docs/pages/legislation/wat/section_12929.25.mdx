# WAT § 12929.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 3. Environmental Water Program

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.25

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect the rights or obligations of any party involved in litigation regarding the Mono Lake Basin.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
