# WAT § 53022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 2. Demand Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53022

---

## Text

Demand warrants not paid on presentation shall bear interest from date of registration at the rate adopted by the board, pursuant to Section 53300.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1109, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1983.)
