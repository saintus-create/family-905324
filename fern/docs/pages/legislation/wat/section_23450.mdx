# WAT § 23450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Cooperation With Other Districts > Article: 2. Cooperation with County Water Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23450

---

## Text

As used in this article “districts” means one or more irrigation districts together with one or more county water districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
