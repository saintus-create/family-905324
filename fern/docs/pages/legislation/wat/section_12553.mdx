# WAT § 12553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 12553

---

## Text

The State Controller shall draw warrants upon the fund from time to time upon demand of the commissioner, and the State Treasurer shall pay the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
