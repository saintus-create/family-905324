# WAT § 36830.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 36830.1

---

## Text

The tax collector may, in his discretion, accept negotiable paper in payment of any assessment or on a redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 438.)
