# WAT § 8102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Flood Control in Counties > Article: 2. Flood Control Work Outside of County

**Citation:** WAT § 8102

---

## Text

Work under this article may be done upon channels, streams or rivers which flow through or lie in more than one county, or where the work is reasonably necessary for the control of flood waters in the county upon a channel, river or stream which does not lie or flow in or through two or more counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
