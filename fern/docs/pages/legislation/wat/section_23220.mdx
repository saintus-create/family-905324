# WAT § 23220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 3. Approval of Federal Contracts

**Citation:** WAT § 23220

---

## Text

A proposal to enter into a contract with the United States shall be authorized at an election if the contract is for any or all of the following:

(a) Repayment of construction money.

(b) Repayment of the cost of acquiring any property.

(c) Issuance of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
