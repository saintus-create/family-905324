# WAT § 35029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 2. Establishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 35029

---

## Text

The entry of the order dividing the district into divisions shall not affect the term of any director until his term expires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 207.)
