# WAT § 60027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60027

---

## Text

“Replenishment assessment” means any assessment levied pursuant to Part 6 (commencing with Section 60300).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 389, Sec. 1.)
