# WAT § 79587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 11. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79587

---

## Text

All money derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
