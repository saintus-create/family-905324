# WAT § 81033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. CALCONSERVE WATER USE EFFICIENCY REVOLVING LOAN PROGRAM > Part: 2. CalConserve Water Use Efficiency Revolving Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 81033

---

## Text

Funds made available pursuant to this division to a local agency shall not be used for that local agency’s administrative costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 825, Sec. 2. (AB 2636) Effective January 1, 2015.)
