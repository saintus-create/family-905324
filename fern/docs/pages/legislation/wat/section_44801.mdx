# WAT § 44801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 3. Proceeds of Sale of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 44801

---

## Text

Payment may be made either in cash or in matured bonds and coupons issued on the assessment, taken at their face value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
