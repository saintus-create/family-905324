# WAT § 60433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60433

---

## Text

After an election for the disincorporation of a district hereunder the sufficiency of such petition in any respect shall not be subject to judicial review or be otherwise questioned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
