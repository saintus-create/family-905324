# WAT § 36829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 36829

---

## Text

The tax collector shall give a receipt to the person making payment specifying:

(a) The land assessed.

(b) The amount of the assessment.

(c) The amount paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
