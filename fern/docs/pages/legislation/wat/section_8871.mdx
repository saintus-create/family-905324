# WAT § 8871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8871

---

## Text

If not completed on the first day the sale may be continued from day to day and over Sundays and legal holidays until completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
