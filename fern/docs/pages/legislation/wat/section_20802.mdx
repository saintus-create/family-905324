# WAT § 20802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 1. Preliminary Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20802

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall hear all competent and relevant testimony offered in support of or in opposition to the sufficiency of the petition and the publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
