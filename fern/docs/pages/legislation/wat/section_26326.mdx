# WAT § 26326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 5. Recordation of Deed to District

**Citation:** WAT § 26326

---

## Text

A collector’s deed to a district shall be recorded in the usual manner of recording conveyances of real property.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 51.)
