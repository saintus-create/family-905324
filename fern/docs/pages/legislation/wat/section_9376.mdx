# WAT § 9376

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 3. Voting Lists and Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 9376

---

## Text

The voting list shall be used by the board of election in determining the right to vote and the number of votes to be cast by each voter, and shall be sufficient evidence of these matters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
