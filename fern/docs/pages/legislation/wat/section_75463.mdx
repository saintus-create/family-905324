# WAT § 75463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 75463

---

## Text

The proceeds arising from the sale of the warrants shall be paid into the treasury of the district and placed to the credit of a special project fund and expended only for the purpose for which the project assessment was authorized to be levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
