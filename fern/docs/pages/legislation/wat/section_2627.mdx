# WAT § 2627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 6. Objections to the Report

**Citation:** WAT § 2627

---

## Text

The period of inspection shall be concluded at least 30 days prior to the expiration of the time allowed claimants for filing objections to the report and preliminary order of determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
