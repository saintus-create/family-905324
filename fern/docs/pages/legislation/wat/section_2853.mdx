# WAT § 2853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2853

---

## Text

A statement setting forth the expense and the apportionments thereof against the respective parties shall be sent by registered mail by the board to each of the parties and filed with the court.
