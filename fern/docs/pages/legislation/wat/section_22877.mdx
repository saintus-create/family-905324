# WAT § 22877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 3. Flood Protection

**Citation:** WAT § 22877

---

## Text

A district may do all things necessary to insure its canal system and the land from any damage by flood or overflow without holding an election to authorize the expenditure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
