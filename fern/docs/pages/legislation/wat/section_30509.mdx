# WAT § 30509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30509

---

## Text

Before entering upon the duties of his office each director shall take and subscribe the official oath and file it with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
