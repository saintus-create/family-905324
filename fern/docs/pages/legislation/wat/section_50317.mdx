# WAT § 50317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 2. Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 50317

---

## Text

Upon the final hearing on the petition the board of supervisors shall make an order approving the petition, as originally presented, or in a modified form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
