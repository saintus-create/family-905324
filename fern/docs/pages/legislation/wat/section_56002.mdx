# WAT § 56002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 56002

---

## Text

“District board” as used in this part means the board of directors of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
