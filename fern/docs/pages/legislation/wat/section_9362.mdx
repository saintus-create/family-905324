# WAT § 9362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9362

---

## Text

The compensation of the election officers shall be paid by the board out of any funds of the drainage district or of the board applicable thereto.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
