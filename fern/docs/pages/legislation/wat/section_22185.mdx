# WAT § 22185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 7. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 22185

---

## Text

A district may construct, maintain, and operate recreational facilities in connection with any dams, reservoirs, or other works owned or controlled by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 533.)
