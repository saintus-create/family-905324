# WAT § 11254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 9.1. Hogan Dam

**Citation:** WAT § 11254

---

## Text

Contracts entered into between the department and the City of Stockton in connection with the transfer of “Old Hogan” to the State, including provisions for the delivery of water from the dam and reservoir, shall not be subject to the limitations contained in Section 11139.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1750.)
