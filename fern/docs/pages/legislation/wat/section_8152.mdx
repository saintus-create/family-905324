# WAT § 8152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Transfer of Storm Drain Improvements, Drainage Improvements, and Drainage Systems to Flood Control Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 8152

## Text

As used in this chapter “city” includes city and county, and “county” does not include city and county.
