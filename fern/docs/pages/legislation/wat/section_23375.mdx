# WAT § 23375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Cooperation With Other Districts > Article: 1. Cooperation With Districts in Other States

**Citation:** WAT § 23375

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter do not extend the right of eminent domain to any public use in behalf of which it may not otherwise be exercised.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
