# WAT § 79056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 5. Flood Protection Program > Article: 4. Flood Control Subventions Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79056

---

## Text

The sum of forty-five million dollars ($45,000,000) is hereby transferred from the fund to the subaccount.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
