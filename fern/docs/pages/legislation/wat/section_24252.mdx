# WAT § 24252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Creation of Liabilities

**Citation:** WAT § 24252

---

## Text

A district shall have the right to enter into any contract or lease for any property necessary in the judgment of its board for any of the uses or purposes of the district and by the lease or contract to bind the district for the payment of the consideration specified in the lease or contract as may be provided therein.
