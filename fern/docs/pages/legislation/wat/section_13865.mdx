# WAT § 13865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.5. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1976

**Citation:** WAT § 13865

---

## Text

First priority for loans shall be given to suppliers with the most critical public health problems. Priority for loans shall also be given to suppliers which have a lesser capability to reasonably finance system improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1008. Approved in Proposition 3 at the June 8, 1976, election.)
