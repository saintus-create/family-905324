# WAT § 74501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 74501

---

## Text

A district may make contracts and do all acts necessary for the full exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
