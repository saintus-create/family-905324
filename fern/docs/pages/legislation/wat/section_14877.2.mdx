# WAT § 14877.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 22. Graywater Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 14877.2

---

## Text

A graywater system may be installed if the city or county having jurisdiction over the installation determines that the system complies with standards adopted by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 961, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
