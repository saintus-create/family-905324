# WAT § 31101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6. Sewer Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 31101

---

## Text

The district may prescribe, revise and collect rates or other charges for the services and facilities furnished pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1114.)
