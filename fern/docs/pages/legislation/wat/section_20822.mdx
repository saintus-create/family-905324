# WAT § 20822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 2. Reference to Department

**Citation:** WAT § 20822

---

## Text

Upon receiving a copy of the resolution, the department shall make such preliminary investigation as may be practicable to determine the feasibility of the proposed project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
