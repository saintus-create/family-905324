# WAT § 22552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 6. Water Leases for Mechanical Purposes

**Citation:** WAT § 22552

---

## Text

Whenever the board desires to lease the use of water it shall pass a resolution of intention to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
