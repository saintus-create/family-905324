# WAT § 12948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12948

---

## Text

The department either independently or in cooperation with any county, state, federal, or public or private agency or corporation may conduct a program of investigation, study, and evaluation in the field of saline water conversion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 993.)
