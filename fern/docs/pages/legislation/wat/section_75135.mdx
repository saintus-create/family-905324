# WAT § 75135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75135

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing, after making all necessary and proper changes in the boundaries, the board shall, by resolution, form the special improvement district and fix and determine its boundaries.

The determination of the board as to the area benefited by the bond or other proceedings shall be final and conclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
