# WAT § 33121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Merger

**Citation:** WAT § 33121

---

## Text

Upon the merger the surviving district succeeded to all properties, rights and contracts of each of the two districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
