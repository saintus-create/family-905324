# WAT § 75062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75062

---

## Text

Only qualified electors of the improvement district may vote at the bond election.

All electors residing within the improvement district whose names appear on the register of voters of the last general election and at any time within 40 days preceding the bond election are qualified electors of the improvement district eligible to vote at the bond election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
