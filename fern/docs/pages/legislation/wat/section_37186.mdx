# WAT § 37186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37186

---

## Text

Excepting as provided in this chapter, the redemption shall be made in the manner provided by Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 37150) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 50.)
