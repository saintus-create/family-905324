# WAT § 39465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 4. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 39465

---

## Text

The department shall give personal notice by registered mail to all holders of title to land whose names and addresses appear on the list filed pursuant to Section 39427.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
