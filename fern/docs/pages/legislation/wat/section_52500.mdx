# WAT § 52500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52500

---

## Text

A district may refund the whole or any part of any installment of the principal of an outstanding bond issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
