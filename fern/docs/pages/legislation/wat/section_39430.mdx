# WAT § 39430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 39430

## Text

The formation petition and accompanying documents shall be presented to and filed with the department.
