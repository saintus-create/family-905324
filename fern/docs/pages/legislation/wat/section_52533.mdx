# WAT § 52533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 2. Exchange

**Citation:** WAT § 52533

---

## Text

The outstanding bonds may be refunded pursuant to this article with the consent of the holders thereof at any time before or after their final maturity date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
