# WAT § 79551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 7. CALFED Bay-Delta Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79551

---

## Text

All appropriations pursuant to this chapter shall include money for independent scientific review, monitoring, and assessment of the results or effectiveness of the project or program expenditure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
