# WAT § 74136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74136

---

## Text

The appeal shall be heard and determined by the Supreme Court within 60 days from the time of filing the notice of appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
