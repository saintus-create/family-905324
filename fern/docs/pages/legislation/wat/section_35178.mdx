# WAT § 35178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Method of Elections for the Irvine Ranch Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35178

---

## Text

All directors elected after the effective date of this chapter shall be residents of and registered voters in the district and shall be registered voters at the time of their election and during their term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 6, Sec. 1.4. Effective February 4, 1980.)
