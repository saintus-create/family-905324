# WAT § 70238

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 70238

---

## Text

The district taxes shall be collected at the same time and in the same manner as county taxes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 62.)
