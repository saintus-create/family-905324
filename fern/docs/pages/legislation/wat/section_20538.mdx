# WAT § 20538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20538

---

## Text

“Particular purpose assessment” means an assessment levied pursuant to Article 3 of Chapter 2 of Part 10.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
