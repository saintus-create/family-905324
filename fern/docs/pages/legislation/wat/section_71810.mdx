# WAT § 71810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Promissory Notes

**Citation:** WAT § 71810

---

## Text

A district may issue negotiable promissory notes pursuant to Sections 71811 and 71812. Such promissory notes shall bear interest at a rate not to exceed 12 percent per year. They shall be payable from revenues and taxes levied for purposes of the district other than the payment of principal and interest on any bonded debt of the district or of an improvement district therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 466, Sec. 1.)
