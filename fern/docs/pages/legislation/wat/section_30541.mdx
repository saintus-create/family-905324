# WAT § 30541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 3. Other Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 30541

---

## Text

A director shall not be the general manager, secretary, treasurer, or auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 454, Sec. 4.)
