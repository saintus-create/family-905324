# WAT § 31320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Settlements and Statements

**Citation:** WAT § 31320

---

## Text

On Monday in each week the collector shall do both of the following:

(a) Settle with the secretary for all money collected for taxes during the week next preceding.

(b) Pay the money to the depositary designated by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
