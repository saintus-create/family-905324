# WAT § 13466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.1. Water Conservation and Water Quality Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13466

---

## Text

For the 1987–88 fiscal year and each year thereafter, a loan may be made by the department only upon the specific approval of the Legislature, by an act enacted after the receipt of a report filed pursuant to Section 13467.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 321, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1993. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 6, and approved in Prop. 44 on June 3, 1986.)
