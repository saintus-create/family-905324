# WAT § 55014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 55014

---

## Text

“County” means the county within which the district or proposed district is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
