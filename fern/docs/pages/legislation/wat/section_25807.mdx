# WAT § 25807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 1. Regular Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25807

---

## Text

If the annual district assessment is payable in two installments the unpaid charges may be added to and become a part of the first installment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
