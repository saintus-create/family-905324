# WAT § 51644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51644

---

## Text

After crediting the amount due on the parcel, as shown in the notice of sale, to the bond fund, the county treasurer shall pay to the owner of the parcel any surplus remaining.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
