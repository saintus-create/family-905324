# WAT § 30543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 3. Other Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 30543

---

## Text

The same person may serve as general manager and secretary, secretary and auditor, or secretary and treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 454, Sec. 6.)
