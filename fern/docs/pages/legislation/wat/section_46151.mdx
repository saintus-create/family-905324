# WAT § 46151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 1. Commissioners

**Citation:** WAT § 46151

---

## Text

No commissioner shall have any interest in any land in the district either directly or indirectly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
