# WAT § 72095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Taxes Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 72095

---

## Text

The county auditor shall compute and enter in the county assessment roll the respective sums to be paid as a district tax on the property in the district, using the rate or rates of levy as fixed by the board and the assessed value as found on the assessment roll for the property subject to the particular tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 17.)
