# WAT § 74541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 74541

---

## Text

A district may fix and assess reasonable charges for the use of the recreational facilities by members of the public, and may deposit any funds collected thereby with the treasurer to the credit of the district as provided in Section 74750.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
