# WAT § 23264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 5. Construction of Works for United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23264

---

## Text

Any money received by a district from the United States under the contract shall first be applied to the retirement of notes, warrants, or other evidences of indebtedness issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
