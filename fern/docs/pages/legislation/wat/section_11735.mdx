# WAT § 11735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 4. Bond Form and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 11735

---

## Text

Bonds may be issued in coupon form with interest payable at such times as determined by the department and shall mature at such times and in such amounts as the department prescribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 404, Sec. 1. Effective July 27, 1983.)
