# WAT § 71277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71277

---

## Text

On all ordinances the roll shall be called and the ayes and noes recorded in the journal of the proceedings of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
