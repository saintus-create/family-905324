# WAT § 75782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 3. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 75782

---

## Text

The expenses of giving the notice of the filing of the petition for exclusion of lands and of the proceedings on the petition shall be paid by the person or persons filing the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
