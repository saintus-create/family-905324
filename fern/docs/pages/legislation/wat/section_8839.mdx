# WAT § 8839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8839

---

## Text

Any map may consist of any number of sheets attached together and designated as one map.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
