# WAT § 8951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 4. Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 8951

---

## Text

The money in the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Fund shall be paid out upon warrants of the Controller, and the Controller shall issue warrants upon the funds whenever drafts of the board are presented to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
