# WAT § 36202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36202

---

## Text

All the signatures on the bond or coupons may be printed, lithographed or otherwise mechanically reproduced, except that one of the signatures on the bond shall be manually affixed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
