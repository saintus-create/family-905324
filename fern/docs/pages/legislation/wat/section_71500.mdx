# WAT § 71500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election of Directors > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71500

---

## Text

The successors to the first directors shall be elected or chosen at the times and in the manner provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
