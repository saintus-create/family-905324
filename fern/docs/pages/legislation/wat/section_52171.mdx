# WAT § 52171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52171

---

## Text

At least 120 days prior to any interest payment date, the board may, by a two-thirds vote, adopt an order calling any amount of callable bonds for payment on the interest payment date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
