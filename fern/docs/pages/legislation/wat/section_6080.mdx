# WAT § 6080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 1. Powers in General

**Citation:** WAT § 6080

---

## Text

In making any investigations or inspections required or authorized by this part the department or its representatives may enter upon private property as may be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
