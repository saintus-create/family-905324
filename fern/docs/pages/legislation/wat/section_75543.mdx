# WAT § 75543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Establishment of Zones and Registration of Water-Measuring Devices

**Citation:** WAT § 75543

---

## Text

Any water-measuring device required by and satisfactory to any other public agency, county, or district, or zone thereof, the boundaries of which substantially contain the area of the district, may be declared by the board to be sufficient compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
