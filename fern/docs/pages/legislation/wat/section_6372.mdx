# WAT § 6372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 3. Removal of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6372

---

## Text

Before final approval of the removal of a dam or reservoir is issued, the department shall inspect the work and determine that all danger to life and property has been eliminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
