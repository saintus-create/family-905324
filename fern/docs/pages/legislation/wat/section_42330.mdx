# WAT § 42330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Recommendation of Abandonment of Project > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42330

---

## Text

The election shall be conducted as nearly as practicable in accordance with the provisions of this division relating to general elections, but no particular form of ballot shall be required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
