# WAT § 75481.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. North San Joaquin Water Conservation District

**Citation:** WAT § 75481.5

---

## Text

Any assessments erroneously made by reason of inadvertence or clerical mistake may be refunded upon order of the board at any time after payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 318, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
