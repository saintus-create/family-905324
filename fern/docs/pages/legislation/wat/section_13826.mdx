# WAT § 13826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.2. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1984

**Citation:** WAT § 13826

---

## Text

First priority for loans shall be given to suppliers with the most critical public health problems. Priority for loans shall also be given to suppliers which have a lesser capability to reasonably finance system improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 378, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 28 at the November 6, 1984, election.)
