# WAT § 12848

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3.5. Fish, Wildlife, and Recreation in Connection With Flood Control and Watershed Protection Projects > Article: 3. State Financial Assistance

**Citation:** WAT § 12848

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby declares that there shall be included in the budget of the department for flood control subventions or for direct acquisitions by the state for each fiscal year an appropriation of funds from the General Fund in an amount sufficient to pay all costs required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 537.)
