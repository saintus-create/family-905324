# WAT § 75032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 3. Calling of Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75032

---

## Text

The resolution shall further provide for submitting the question of the incurring of the indebtedness to the qualified electors of the improvement district at an election called for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
