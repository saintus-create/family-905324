# WAT § 20532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20532

---

## Text

“Acquire” includes construct, purchase, lease, exchange, condemn, jointly acquire when joint acquisition is permitted, and contract to acquire.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
