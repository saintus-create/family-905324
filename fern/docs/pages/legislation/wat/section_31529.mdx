# WAT § 31529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 31529

---

## Text

All words relating to municipal officers and matters refer to the corresponding officers of county water districts and matters under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
