# WAT § 16201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 28. California Wetlands Policy Act

**Citation:** WAT § 16201

---

## Text

It is the policy of the state to ensure no net loss and long-term gain in the quantity, quality, and permanence of wetlands acreage and values in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 579, Sec. 1. (AB 2875) Effective January 1, 2025.)
