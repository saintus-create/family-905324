# WAT § 52177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52177

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall publish, in a newspaper of general circulation printed and published in the principal county once a week for four weeks prior to the date on which bonds are called for payment, a notice of the call of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
