# WAT § 22826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 1. Introduction

**Citation:** WAT § 22826

---

## Text

Except as provided in this chapter every district containing 500,000 acres or more shall be governed by the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
