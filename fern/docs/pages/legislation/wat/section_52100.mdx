# WAT § 52100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52100

---

## Text

The term bonds, as used in this chapter, includes bonds and refunding bonds, unless otherwise provided.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
