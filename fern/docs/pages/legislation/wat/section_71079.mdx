# WAT § 71079

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion of Cities

**Citation:** WAT § 71079

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, if, prior to the filing of a formation petition, any portion of the corporate area of a city is included within an existing municipal water district, a district may be formed pursuant to this division to include the remainder of the corporate area of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
