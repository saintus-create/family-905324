# WAT § 25679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25679

---

## Text

No informalities in the conduct of the election shall invalidate the election if fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
