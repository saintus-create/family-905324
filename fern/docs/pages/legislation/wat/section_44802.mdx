# WAT § 44802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 3. Proceeds of Sale of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 44802

---

## Text

The treasurer shall execute a deed to the purchaser upon the sale, conveying the property free of encumbrances, except as provided for deeds where no redemption is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
