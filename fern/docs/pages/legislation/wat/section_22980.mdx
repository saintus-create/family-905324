# WAT § 22980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Merced Irrigation District and the East Contra Costa Irrigation District

**Citation:** WAT § 22980

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Byron-Bethany Irrigation District, the Merced Irrigation District, or the East Contra Costa Irrigation District.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1994.)
