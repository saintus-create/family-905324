# WAT § 71617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water > Article: 1. Development and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 71617

---

## Text

The rates for water in each improvement district may vary from the rates of the district and from other improvement districts therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
