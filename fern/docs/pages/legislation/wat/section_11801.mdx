# WAT § 11801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 1. General Financial Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11801

---

## Text

All interest received or earned on money deposited in a fund provided for in this part shall be credited to and become a part of the particular fund upon which the interest accrues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
