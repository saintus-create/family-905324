# WAT § 8887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8887

---

## Text

Payment for the land purchased pursuant to the provisions of this chapter shall be made in lawful money of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
