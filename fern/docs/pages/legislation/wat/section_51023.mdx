# WAT § 51023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 1. Districts Within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51023

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Reclamation Board shall publish a notice of the hearing once a week for two weeks in some newspaper of general circulation published within the district, or, if no newspaper is published within the district, then in the county seat of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
