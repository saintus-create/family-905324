# WAT § 36553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Annual Estimate

**Citation:** WAT § 36553

---

## Text

The annual estimate shall specify the portion thereof which will be required for the payment of bonds and of the interest on bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
