# WAT § 21584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Abolition and Reestablishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 21584

---

## Text

The board may independently investigate whether or not the facts are consistent with the showing made in the petition and shall hear any evidence produced to sustain or disprove the showing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
