# WAT § 26657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 26657

---

## Text

The county auditor shall apportion to the district the money paid for redemption in the manner provided by Division 1, Part 8, Chapter 1(c) of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1938.)
