# WAT § 24390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Deposits > Article: 3. Special Accounts

**Citation:** WAT § 24390

---

## Text

The board may by resolution establish special accounts from the general fund when they are convenient for the efficient and economical operation of the district and designate the person or officer to have custody of the several accounts so established.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
