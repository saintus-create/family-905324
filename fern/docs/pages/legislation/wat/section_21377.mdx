# WAT § 21377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 21377

---

## Text

The board shall hold a regular meeting on the first Tuesday of each month at the district office.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
