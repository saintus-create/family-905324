# WAT § 25928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 1. Collection Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 25928

---

## Text

Failure to publish the assessment notice in any county other than the office county shall not affect any assessment on land in a county in which notice was published.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
