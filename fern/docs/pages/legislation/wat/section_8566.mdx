# WAT § 8566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8566

---

## Text

The seal of the board is also the seal of the drainage district.
