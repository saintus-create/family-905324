# WAT § 6501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Part: 2. FISHWAYS OVER DAMS > Chapter: 10. Dams and Reservoirs Under State Supervision Through 1965 Revisions of Part 1 > Article: 3. Fees for Dams or Reservoirs Under Construction Before 1965 Revisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6501

---

## Text

The provisions for the installation of fishways over or around dams and for the protection and preservation of fish in streams obstructed by dams are contained in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 5900), Part 1, Division 6 of the Fish and Game Code.
