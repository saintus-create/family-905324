# WAT § 10755.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10755.3

---

## Text

Local agencies within the same groundwater basin that conduct groundwater management programs within that basin pursuant to this part, and cities and counties that either manage groundwater pursuant to this part or have ordinances relating to groundwater within that basin, shall, at least annually, meet to coordinate those programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 833, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
