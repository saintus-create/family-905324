# WAT § 11917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 10. Fish and Wildlife and Recreation in Connection With State Water Projects > Article: 5. Powers and Duties of the Department of Fish and Game and the Department of Natural Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 11917

---

## Text

The Department of Fish and Game shall manage fish and wildlife resources at state water projects, including any such additional resources as are created by such projects, in a manner compatible with the other uses of such projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 867.)
