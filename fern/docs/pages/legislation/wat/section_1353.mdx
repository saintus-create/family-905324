# WAT § 1353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Proceedings on Application > Article: 2. Action on Application

**Citation:** WAT § 1353

---

## Text

In the conduct of hearings technical rules of evidence need not be applied.
