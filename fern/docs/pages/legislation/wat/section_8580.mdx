# WAT § 8580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 2. Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 8580

---

## Text

(a) The board may appoint an executive officer.

(b) The board may appoint a chief engineer.

(c) The board may employ legal counsel and other necessary staff.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 366, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2008.)
