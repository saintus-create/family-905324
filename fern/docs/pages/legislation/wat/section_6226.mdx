# WAT § 6226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 2. Repairs, Alterations or Removals

**Citation:** WAT § 6226

---

## Text

The application shall give such pertinent information or data concerning the dam or reservoir, or both, as may be required by the department and such information as to other matters appropriate to a thorough consideration of the safety of such a change as may be required by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
