# WAT § 8670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 4. Lease and Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 8670

---

## Text

The board may make a final acceptance either at the same session or at any adjourned session of the same meeting held within the 10 days next following.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
