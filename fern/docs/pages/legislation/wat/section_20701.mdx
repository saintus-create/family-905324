# WAT § 20701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 1. Petitioners and Land

**Citation:** WAT § 20701

---

## Text

The land proposed to be formed into a district need not consist of contiguous parcels.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
