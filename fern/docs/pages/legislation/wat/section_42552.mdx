# WAT § 42552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Recommendation of Completion of Project > Article: 3. Proceedings After Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42552

---

## Text

If completion of the project is not approved at the election, the project is abandoned.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 1993.)
