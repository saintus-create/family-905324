# WAT § 40303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualification and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 40303

---

## Text

After such classification the board shall elect a president from their number and appoint a secretary and treasurer, each of whom shall hold office during the pleasure of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
