# WAT § 462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 6. Water Reuse > Article: 3. Action by the Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 462

---

## Text

The department shall conduct studies and investigations on the availability and quality of wastewater and the uses of reclaimed water for beneficial purposes, including, but not limited to, groundwater recharge, municipal and industrial use, irrigation use, and cooling for thermal electric powerplants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 724, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
