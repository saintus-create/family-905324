# WAT § 40657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Board Meetings and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 40657

---

## Text

All records of the board shall be open to public inspection during business hours.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
