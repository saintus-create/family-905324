# WAT § 8764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 8764

---

## Text

The resolution shall be annexed by the county treasurer to the assessment list of the assessment for his county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
