# WAT § 52710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52710

---

## Text

The outstanding bonds refunded or exchanged shall be immediately canceled by the county treasurer.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
