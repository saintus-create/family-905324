# WAT § 20820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 2. Reference to Department

**Citation:** WAT § 20820

---

## Text

On or before the day on which the petition is presented to the board of supervisors, a copy of the petition shall be filed in the office of the department.
