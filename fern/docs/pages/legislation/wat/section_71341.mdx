# WAT § 71341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71341

---

## Text

The board may employ such additional assistants and employees as it deems necessary to efficiently maintain and operate the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
