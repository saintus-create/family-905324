# WAT § 8943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 3. Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District Refunding Act

**Citation:** WAT § 8943

---

## Text

All warrants when received by any officer authorized by law to receive them shall be by him transmitted to the Controller who shall give the officer a receipt for the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
