# WAT § 30012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 30012

---

## Text

“Include” except when used in relation to the inclusion of land into a district does not necessarily exclude matters not enumerated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
