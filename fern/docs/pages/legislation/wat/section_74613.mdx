# WAT § 74613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts With Other Agencies > Article: 3. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 74613

---

## Text

As used in this article, “United States” includes the United States, and any board, bureau, agency, office or officers, department, or corporation of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
