# WAT § 75015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75015

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing, if the board determines that the improvement district should be formed, it shall make a final order approving the formation petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
