# WAT § 11664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 3. Additional Provisions Relating to Contracts with State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 11664

---

## Text

All funds accruing to a special account established pursuant to the next preceding section, or deposited therein, in compliance with the terms and provisions of any contract with the department constitute a trust fund for the purpose of making payments to the department as provided in the contract.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
