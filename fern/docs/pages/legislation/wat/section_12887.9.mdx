# WAT § 12887.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 8. Powers of Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 12887.9

---

## Text

Whenever a public agency is required to have legal counsel in connection with any of its activities pursuant to this chapter, it may use the services of the district attorney or county counsel of the county in which it is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
