# WAT § 35421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 35421

---

## Text

Water sold to holders of title to land pursuant to Section 35470 shall be apportioned ratably to each holder of title to land making application therefor under such rules and regulations as the board may from time to time establish.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
