# WAT § 51809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51809

---

## Text

The warrants shall be payable out of the fund of the district into which the proceeds of the assessments had been paid.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 805.)
