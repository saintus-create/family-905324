# WAT § 35203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Method of Elections for the Borrego Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35203

---

## Text

All directors elected after January 1, 1981, shall be qualified voters of the district at the time of their election and during their term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 456, Sec. 1.)
