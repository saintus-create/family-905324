# WAT § 22226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 22226

---

## Text

A district may construct the necessary works for the collection of water for the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
