# WAT § 71512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election of Directors > Article: 2. Appointment in Lieu of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71512

---

## Text

A person appointed to office of director shall qualify and take office and serve exactly as if elected at a general district election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
