# WAT § 1539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Right Fees > Article: 2. Collection and Enforcement

**Citation:** WAT § 1539

---

## Text

If a permit or license holder fails to pay an annual fee imposed pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 1525 for a period of five years, the board may revoke the permit or license in accordance with the procedures for revocation specified in Section 1241.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 85. Effective January 1, 2004.)
