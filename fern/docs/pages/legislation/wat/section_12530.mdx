# WAT § 12530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Commissioner

**Citation:** WAT § 12530

---

## Text

The commissioner shall investigate past, present, and potential uses of the water of the Colorado River System within and without the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
