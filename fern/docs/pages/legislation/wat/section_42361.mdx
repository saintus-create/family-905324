# WAT § 42361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Recommendation of Abandonment of Project > Article: 3. Proceedings After Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42361

---

## Text

All liens which have attached to land under any provisions of this division are discharged with the filing of the copy of the order, and any undertakings given are annulled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
