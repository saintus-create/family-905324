# WAT § 45551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 45551

## Text

The bonds shall be signed by the president and attested by the treasurer.
