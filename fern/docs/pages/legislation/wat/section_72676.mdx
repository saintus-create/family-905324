# WAT § 72676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 72676

---

## Text

When the secretary has completed his examination of the petition for annexation, he shall attach to it his certificate, properly dated, showing the result of such examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
