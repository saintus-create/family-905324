# WAT § 36602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 4. Hearing on Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 36602

---

## Text

The first publication of the notice shall be at least 15 days prior to the date fixed for the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
