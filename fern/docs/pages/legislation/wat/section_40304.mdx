# WAT § 40304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualification and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 40304

---

## Text

On the day of the regular meeting of the board in April next following each general election, the directors shall meet and organize as a board, elect a president and appoint a secretary and a treasurer, who shall each hold office during the pleasure of the board.
