# WAT § 6206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 1. New Dams and Reservoirs or Enlargements of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6206

---

## Text

The application shall be accompanied by maps and plans and specifications of such character and size and setting forth such pertinent details and dimensions as the department requires.

The maps and plans and specifications shall be a part of the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
