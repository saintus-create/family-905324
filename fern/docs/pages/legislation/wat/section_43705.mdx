# WAT § 43705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 43705

---

## Text

If two or more actions or contests are pending at the same time in the same court or before the same tribunal for the purpose of contesting or determining the validity of identical or similar acts or matters under the provisions of this division, the actions or contests shall be consolidated and tried together.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
