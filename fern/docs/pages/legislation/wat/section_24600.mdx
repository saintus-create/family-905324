# WAT § 24600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 1. Payment of Claims

**Citation:** WAT § 24600

---

## Text

No claim shall be paid by the treasurer until allowed by the board, and only upon a warrant signed by the president and countersigned by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
