# WAT § 31413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 31413

---

## Text

As soon as the result is declared, the secretary shall enter in the records of the board a statement of the result.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
