# WAT § 30068

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 30068

---

## Text

Districts governed by this division are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.
