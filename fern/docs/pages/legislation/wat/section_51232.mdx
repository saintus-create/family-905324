# WAT § 51232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51232

---

## Text

The assessment shall be collected and paid into the county treasury in the manner provided in Chapter 3 of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
