# WAT § 9122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 9. Reports > Article: 2. State Reports

**Citation:** WAT § 9122

---

## Text

The board shall determine the areas benefited by facilities of the State Plan of Flood Control based on information developed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 366, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2008. See identical section in the Chapter 9 added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 368.)
