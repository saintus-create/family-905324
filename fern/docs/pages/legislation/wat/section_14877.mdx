# WAT § 14877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 22. Graywater Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 14877

---

## Text

“Graywater system” means a system and devices, attached to the plumbing system for the sanitary distribution or use of graywater.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 226, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
