# WAT § 75080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 6. Powers of the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 75080

---

## Text

Any improvement district formed pursuant to this chapter shall be under the supervision, direction, and control of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
