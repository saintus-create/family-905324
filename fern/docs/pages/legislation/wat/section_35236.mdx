# WAT § 35236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Method of Election for the Rancho California Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35236

---

## Text

This chapter shall in no way invalidate any action of the district or of its directors, or officers, and employees made or performed while the district was operating as a landowner voting district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 228, Sec. 1.)
