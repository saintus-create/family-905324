# WAT § 36170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 2. Sale and Exchange of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36170

---

## Text

The board may order general obligation bonds sold at not less than 90 percent of par value when the board deems it for the best interest of the district to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
