# WAT § 60437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60437

---

## Text

Such election shall be held and conducted in the same manner as the election on the organization of said district, as nearly as practicable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
