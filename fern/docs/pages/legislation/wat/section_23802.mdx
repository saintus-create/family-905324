# WAT § 23802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23802

---

## Text

Land may be formed into an improvement district for one or more of the purposes provided for in Chapter 1 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 717.)
