# WAT § 22701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 3. Limitations

**Citation:** WAT § 22701

---

## Text

In any such action all findings of fact or conclusions of a board or board of supervisors upon all matters shall be conclusive unless the action was instituted within six months after the finding or conclusion was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
