# WAT § 37806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 37806

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall impair or adversely affect any right of any bondholder or creditor of the water storage district or the California water district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
