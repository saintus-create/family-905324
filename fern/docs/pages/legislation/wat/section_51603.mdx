# WAT § 51603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 1. Delinquency Sales on Unbonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51603

---

## Text

The county treasurer may postpone the sale for not less than 10 nor more than 30 days at any one time by a written notice posted at the place of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
