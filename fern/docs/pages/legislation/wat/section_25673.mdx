# WAT § 25673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25673

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be given by both:

(a) Posting notices in three public places in each election precinct in the district for at least 20 days.

(b) Publication in a newspaper published in the office county, once a week for at least three successive weeks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
