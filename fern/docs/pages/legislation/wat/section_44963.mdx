# WAT § 44963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 44963

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Sections 44953 and 44962, revenue warrants may be issued prior to August 1, 1978, pursuant to this chapter for a term not to exceed 10 years from the date of issuance and for an amount not in excess of four million dollars ($4,000,000) in any one fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 188.)
