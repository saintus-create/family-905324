# WAT § 9467

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9467

---

## Text

The investment may be made by the board by direct purchase of the bonds from time to time and in such manner as it determines and at such price or prices as it deems advisable but not to exceed par, plus the accrued interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
