# WAT § 8775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 2. Assessors and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 8775

---

## Text

When an assessment is to be levied, the board shall appoint three assessors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
