# WAT § 22120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22120

---

## Text

A district may sell, dispose of, and distribute electric power for use outside of its boundaries.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1943, Ch. 687.)
