# WAT § 26257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 2. Partial Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26257

---

## Text

Separate redemption, if authorized by the board, may be made in the manner provided for in Article 1 of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
