# WAT § 22843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 2. Internal Organization

**Citation:** WAT § 22843

---

## Text

All department heads, executives, and employees shall hold their employment at the will of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
