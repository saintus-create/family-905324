# WAT § 46328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 8. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46328

---

## Text

The board shall file with the treasurer of each county in which any portion of the tract so subdivided is situated, the supplementary assessment roll relating to the land in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 123.5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
