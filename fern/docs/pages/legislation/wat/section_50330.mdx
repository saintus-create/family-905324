# WAT § 50330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 3. Filing and Recording

**Citation:** WAT § 50330

---

## Text

The petition, with the order of the board of supervisors endorsed thereon or attached thereto, shall be recorded by the county recorder.
