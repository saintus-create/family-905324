# WAT § 51807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51807

---

## Text

When the order is attached to the assessment roll the secretary shall endorse upon the assessment roll after each parcel assessed the amount of the assessment as to each particular parcel which has been canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
