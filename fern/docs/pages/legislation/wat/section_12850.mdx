# WAT § 12850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Watershed Protection Flood Prevention Projects > Article: 1. Short Title

## Text

Chapter 4 of this part shall be known and may be cited as “The California Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Law.”
