# WAT § 31135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9. Sanitation Service

**Citation:** WAT § 31135

---

## Text

A district may acquire, construct, and operate facilities for, or may contract with others for, the collection and disposal of the garbage, waste, and trash of the district and its inhabitants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1981.)
