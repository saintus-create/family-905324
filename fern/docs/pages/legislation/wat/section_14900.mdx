# WAT § 14900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 23. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14900

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 959, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993. Section operative July 1, 1993, pursuant to Section 14920.)
