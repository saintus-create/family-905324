# WAT § 6250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 3. Dams Constructed Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6250

---

## Text

Unless application for approval of the dam has heretofore been made, every owner of a dam completed prior to August 14, 1929 shall, immediately after the effective date of this part, file an application for the approval of such dam.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
