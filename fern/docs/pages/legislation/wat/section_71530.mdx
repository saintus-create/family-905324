# WAT § 71530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Initiative, Referendum, and Recall

**Citation:** WAT § 71530

---

## Text

Ordinances may be passed by the voters of a district in accordance with the methods provided by the Elections Code for direct legislation in cities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
