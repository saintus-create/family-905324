# WAT § 8873

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8873

---

## Text

No bid for any parcel shall be accepted which is less than the aggregate sum then due for the installment thereon, together with accrued interest, penalty and cost of publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
