# WAT § 9415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 9415

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1537.)
