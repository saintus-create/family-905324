# WAT § 71689.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 5. Sanitation Service

**Citation:** WAT § 71689.21

---

## Text

The district may prescribe, revise, and collect rates or other charges for the services furnished pursuant to this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
