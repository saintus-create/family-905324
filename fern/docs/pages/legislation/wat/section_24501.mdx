# WAT § 24501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Payment and Purchase of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24501

---

## Text

Upon presentation of any matured interest coupon of any bond of the district, the treasurer shall pay it from the bond interest fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
