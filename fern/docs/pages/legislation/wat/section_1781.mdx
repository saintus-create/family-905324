# WAT § 1781

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 2. Joint Occupancy and Use

**Citation:** WAT § 1781

---

## Text

If any of the water is used or to be used for purposes other than those specified in this article, the applicant shall pay to the person owning the works such a pro rata portion of the total cost of the old and new works as appears to the board to be just and equitable.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
