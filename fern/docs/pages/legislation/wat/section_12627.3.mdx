# WAT § 12627.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Investigation of Projects > Article: 5. Investigation of Water Development Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 12627.3

---

## Text

It is declared to be the policy of the State that the costs of solution of seepage and erosion problems which arise or will arise by reason of construction and operation of water projects should be borne by the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2128.)
