# WAT § 23583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Dissolution of Distribution Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23583

---

## Text

The order of dissolution shall be recorded in the same manner as the order forming the distribution district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
