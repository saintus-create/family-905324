# WAT § 25353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 7. Bond Cancellation

**Citation:** WAT § 25353

---

## Text

After the adoption of a resolution to cancel bonds no other bonds shall be issued in pursuance of the proceedings taken in relation to the issuance of the bonds so canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
