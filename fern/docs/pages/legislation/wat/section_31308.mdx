# WAT § 31308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31308

---

## Text

The county auditor shall draw warrants to pay demands made against the district when the demands have been approved by at least three members of the board and the general manager.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
