# WAT § 41334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41334

---

## Text

The perforated tab shall be torn from the ballot by the inspector immediately before the voted ballot is placed in the ballot-box, and shall be preserved by him and sent with the ballots to the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
