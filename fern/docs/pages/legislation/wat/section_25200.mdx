# WAT § 25200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25200

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “bonds” includes both construction and refunding bonds except as otherwise expressly provided.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
