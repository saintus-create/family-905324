# WAT § 74832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74832

---

## Text

Only qualified electors of the district may vote at the bond election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
