# WAT § 37805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 37805

## Text

This part shall be given a liberal construction for the purpose of sustaining any and all proceedings taken hereunder.
