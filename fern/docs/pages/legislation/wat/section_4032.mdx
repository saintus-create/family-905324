# WAT § 4032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Creation of Watermaster Service Areas

**Citation:** WAT § 4032

---

## Text

Service areas may be enlarged, reduced, consolidated, or abolished from time to time as convenience of administration may require.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
