# WAT § 34805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34805

---

## Text

A majority of the membership of the board shall constitute a quorum of the board for the transaction of business.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1323.)
