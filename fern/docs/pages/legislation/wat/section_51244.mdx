# WAT § 51244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51244

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication once a week for two successive weeks in some newspaper of general circulation published in the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
