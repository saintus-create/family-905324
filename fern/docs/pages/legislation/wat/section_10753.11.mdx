# WAT § 10753.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Groundwater Management Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 10753.11

---

## Text

A plan shall not be considered invalid, and the local agency shall not be required to recirculate the plan for public comment or to delay implementation of the plan, if the local agency substantially complies with the public notice provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 572, Sec. 7. (AB 359) Effective January 1, 2012.)
