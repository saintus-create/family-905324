# WAT § 31618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 31618

---

## Text

For the purpose of any tax levied under this article, the properties within any improvement district shall be deemed to be equally benefited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
