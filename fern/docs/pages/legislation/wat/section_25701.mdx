# WAT § 25701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 3. Particular Purpose Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25701

---

## Text

The board may at any time call an election to submit to the voters a proposal to levy a particular purpose assessment to be applied to any of the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
