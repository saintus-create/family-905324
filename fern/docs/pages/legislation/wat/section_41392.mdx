# WAT § 41392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 4. Returns of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 41392

---

## Text

One of the certificates, with the poll list and the tally paper to which it is attached, shall be retained by the inspector and preserved by him at least six months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
