# WAT § 50756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Elections > Article: 2. Conducting

**Citation:** WAT § 50756

---

## Text

The candidates, of the number to be elected, receiving the highest number of votes shall be deemed elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 218.)
