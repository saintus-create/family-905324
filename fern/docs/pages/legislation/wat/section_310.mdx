# WAT § 310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Miscellaneous Powers of Department > Article: 4. Waste from Artesian Wells

**Citation:** WAT § 310

---

## Text

All prosecutions for the violation of any of the provisions of this article shall be instituted in the superior court of the county where the well is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 449, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2004.)
