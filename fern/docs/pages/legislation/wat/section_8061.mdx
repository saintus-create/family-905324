# WAT § 8061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 6. Taxation

**Citation:** WAT § 8061

---

## Text

The taxes required by this chapter to be levied and collected shall be in addition to all other taxes levied for municipal purposes, and shall be collected at the same time and in the same manner as other municipal taxes are collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
