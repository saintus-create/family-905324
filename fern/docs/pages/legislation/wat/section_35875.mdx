# WAT § 35875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 35875

---

## Text

Districts may cooperate and contract with the United States, under the Federal Reclamation Act of June 17, 1902, and all acts amendatory thereof or supplementary thereto or any act of Congress heretofore or hereafter enacted permitting cooperation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 991.)
