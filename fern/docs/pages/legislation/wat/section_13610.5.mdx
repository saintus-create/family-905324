# WAT § 13610.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 8.5. Perchlorate

**Citation:** WAT § 13610.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to the following:

(a) A facility that stores perchlorate for retail purposes or for law enforcement purposes.

(b) Drinking water storage reservoirs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 614, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
