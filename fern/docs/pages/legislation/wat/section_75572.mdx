# WAT § 75572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 75572

---

## Text

The notice, among other information which the district may provide therein, shall contain an invitation to all operators of water-producing facilities within the district to call at the office of the district to examine the engineering investigation and report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
