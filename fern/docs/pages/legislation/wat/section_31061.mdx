# WAT § 31061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 4. Construction on Rights of Way

**Citation:** WAT § 31061

---

## Text

Every company whose right of way is intersected or crossed by works of the district shall unite with the district in forming the intersections and crossings and grant the rights therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
