# WAT § 52241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Additional Series

**Citation:** WAT § 52241

---

## Text

The provisions of this part relative to the original issue of bonds shall apply to additional series of bonds so far as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
