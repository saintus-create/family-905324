# WAT § 31143.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9.5. San Lorenzo Valley Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31143.4

---

## Text

In order to avoid duplication, either the district or the County of Santa Cruz may contract with the other party for any services or activities authorized to be performed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1135, Sec. 1.)
