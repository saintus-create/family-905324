# WAT § 46153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 1. Commissioners

**Citation:** WAT § 46153

---

## Text

The commissioners shall be paid as compensation for the services rendered by them the sum, or sums, as the board fixes, which is a part of the cost of the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 105. Effective January 1, 1993.)
