# WAT § 8857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8857

---

## Text

The remaining portion not yet ordered paid by the board of the assessment upon any tract of land may be voluntarily paid in full, with the accrued interest thereon, at any time after the lien of the assessment has accrued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
