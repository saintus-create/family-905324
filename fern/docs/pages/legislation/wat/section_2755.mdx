# WAT § 2755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2755

---

## Text

The board shall file with the clerk of the court proof of service by registered mail and by publication.
