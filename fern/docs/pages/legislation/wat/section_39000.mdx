# WAT § 39000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 39000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Water Storage District Law.
