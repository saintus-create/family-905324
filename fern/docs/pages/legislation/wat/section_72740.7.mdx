# WAT § 72740.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Consolidation of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 72740.7

---

## Text

Upon completion of the consolidation, a statement of consolidation, certified by the secretary of the district, shall be filed with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 555, Sec. 4. Effective August 22, 1986.)
