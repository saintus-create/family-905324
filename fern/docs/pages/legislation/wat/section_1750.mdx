# WAT § 1750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1750

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “works” includes dams, tunnels, diversion works, ditches, and other works or constructions.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
