# WAT § 31674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Special Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 31674

---

## Text

After a copy of the ordinance or resolution is delivered to the county assessor, the officer charged with the duty of entering on the assessment roll the amount of district taxes on the land shall in making the entry observe and conform to the ordinance or resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 592. Note: The earlier version in Stats. 1949, Ch. 274, remains applicable as prescribed by Stats. 1967, Ch. 592, Sec. 8.)
