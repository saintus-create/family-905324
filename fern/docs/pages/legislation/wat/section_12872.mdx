# WAT § 12872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Watershed Protection Flood Prevention Projects > Article: 5. Procedure for Allocation

**Citation:** WAT § 12872

---

## Text

The findings of the department required by subsection (d) of Section 12867 or of subsection (f) of Section 12868 shall be based upon current cost estimates or actual costs incurred or expended.
