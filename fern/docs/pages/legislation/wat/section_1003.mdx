# WAT § 1003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Interpretation of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 1003

---

## Text

As used in this division, “Water Commission Act” means Chapter 586, Statutes of 1913, as amended.
