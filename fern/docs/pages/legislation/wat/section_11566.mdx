# WAT § 11566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Construction of Project by the Department of Water Resources > Article: 3. Sequence of Units

**Citation:** WAT § 11566

---

## Text

In case the construction of all or any part of the San Joaquin Pumping System is deferred, the construction of all other units of the project shall be commenced and carried to completion without reference to the construction so deferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
