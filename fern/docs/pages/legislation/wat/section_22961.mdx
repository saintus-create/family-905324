# WAT § 22961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 22961

---

## Text

A reserve fund from assessments or other revenues for emergencies, replacements or other lawful purpose of the district may be provided by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 686.)
