# WAT § 71166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71166

---

## Text

No person may vote at any formation election unless he is a voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
