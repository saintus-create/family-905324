# WAT § 74952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Negotiable Promissory Notes

**Citation:** WAT § 74952

---

## Text

If a district undertakes to issue promissory notes pursuant to the limitations set forth in Section 74950, the limitations provided in Sections 75357 and 75358 shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 35.)
