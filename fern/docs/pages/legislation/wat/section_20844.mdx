# WAT § 20844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 3. Final Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20844

---

## Text

On a final hearing the board of supervisors may adjourn from time to time, but at no time for a longer period than three days, until a determination of the matter is reached.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
