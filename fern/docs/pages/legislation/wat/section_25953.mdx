# WAT § 25953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 2. Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 25953

---

## Text

The resolution may be rescinded or modified only so as to affect any assessment levied subsequent to the rescission or modification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
