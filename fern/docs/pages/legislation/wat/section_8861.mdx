# WAT § 8861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8861

---

## Text

From date of delinquency until time of delinquent sale, the unpaid installment, together with added interest and penalty, bears interest at the rate of 7 per cent per annum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
