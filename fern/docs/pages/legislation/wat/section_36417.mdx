# WAT § 36417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36417

---

## Text

The board shall not order any modifications except after publication of the resolution proposing said modifications once in the same newspaper in which the resolution of intention was published, said publication to be at least fourteen (14) days prior to the date of hearing on said proposed modifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 71.)
