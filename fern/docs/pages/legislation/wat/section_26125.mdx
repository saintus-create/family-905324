# WAT § 26125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 3. Assessment Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 26125

---

## Text

The place of the delinquent sale shall be at a point, designated by the collector, within the district or at the district office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
