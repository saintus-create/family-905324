# WAT § 71690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 71690

---

## Text

A district may, within or without the district:

(a) Take real and personal property of every kind by grant, purchase, gift, device, or lease.

(b) Hold, use, enjoy, lease, or dispose of real and personal property of every kind.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
