# WAT § 40103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 40103

---

## Text

At the time the department declares the district formed it shall issue certificates of election to each person elected a director.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
