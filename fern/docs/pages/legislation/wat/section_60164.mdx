# WAT § 60164

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Board Action

**Citation:** WAT § 60164

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances passed by the board shall be: “Be it ordained by the Board of Directors of _______ Water Replenishment District as follows:”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
