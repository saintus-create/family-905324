# WAT § 9016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 2. Application of Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9016

---

## Text

When compensation is received by any levee district, or drainage district, it shall be paid or delivered by the governing body thereof to the officer having the custody of the funds who shall place the compensation into the proper funds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
