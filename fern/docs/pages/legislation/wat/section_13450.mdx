# WAT § 13450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.1. Water Conservation and Water Quality Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13450

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Water Conservation and Water Quality Bond Law of 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 44 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
