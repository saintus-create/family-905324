# WAT § 31548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 3. Engineer of Work

**Citation:** WAT § 31548

---

## Text

The contract shall be entered into by the engineer, and the assessment and warrant, when confirmed, shall be recorded in the office of the engineer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
