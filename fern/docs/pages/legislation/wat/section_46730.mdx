# WAT § 46730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 4. Notice of Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 46730

---

## Text

Immediately after the installment has become delinquent, the county treasurer shall prepare and publish once a week for two consecutive weeks in each affected county in one notice a list of all delinquencies in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
