# WAT § 25064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 2. Refunding Bond Sinking Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 25064

---

## Text

All proposals received in response to the notice shall be opened by the board in open meeting at the time specified in the notice or at a time to which the meeting is adjourned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
