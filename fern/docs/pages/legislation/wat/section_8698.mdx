# WAT § 8698

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8698

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the board shall cooperate with the department in all matters of mutual concern to the fullest extent practicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
