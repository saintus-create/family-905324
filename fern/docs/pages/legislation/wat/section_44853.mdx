# WAT § 44853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 4. Alternative Provisions for the Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 44853

---

## Text

Where the deposit of money is insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation no additional security need be required from the bank for the portion of the district’s deposits so insured.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 695.)
