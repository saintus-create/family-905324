# WAT § 74250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** WAT § 74250

---

## Text

At each of its organization meetings the board shall elect a president from their number, and shall appoint a secretary, who shall each hold office at the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
