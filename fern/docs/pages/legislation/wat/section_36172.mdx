# WAT § 36172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 2. Sale and Exchange of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36172

---

## Text

Bonds of the State that may be received for general obligation bonds shall be placed to the credit of the district to be sold as the board may direct, for not less than par value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
