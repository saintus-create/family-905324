# WAT § 81662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Authority > Article: 3. Controversies

**Citation:** WAT § 81662

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of any contract, bond, note, or other evidence of indebtedness may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
