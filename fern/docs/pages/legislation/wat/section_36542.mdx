# WAT § 36542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Dissolution of Distribution Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 36542

---

## Text

The board may, after the hearing, order the distribution district dissolved.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
