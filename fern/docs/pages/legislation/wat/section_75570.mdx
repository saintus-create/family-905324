# WAT § 75570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 75570

---

## Text

On or before the day of the regular meeting of the board in March of each year, the engineering investigation and report shall be delivered to the secretary in writing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 255, Sec. 1.)
