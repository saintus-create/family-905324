# WAT § 23542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion and Exclusion of Land > Article: 1. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 23542

---

## Text

The same proceedings shall be had on the distribution district inclusion petition as upon the original petition for the formation of a distribution district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
