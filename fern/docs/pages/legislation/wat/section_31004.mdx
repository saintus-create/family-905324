# WAT § 31004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31004

---

## Text

A district may make contracts and employ labor to carry out the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
