# WAT § 25264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 3. Allocation of Revenue to Bond Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 25264

---

## Text

Any allocation shall be irrevocable until all of the bonds for which the allocation was made and their appurtenant coupons have been paid or retired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
