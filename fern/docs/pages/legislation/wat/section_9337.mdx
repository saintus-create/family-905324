# WAT § 9337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9337

---

## Text

No person shall vote by proxy at the election unless authority to vote is evidenced by an instrument in writing duly executed, acknowledged and certified in the same manner as grants of real property and filed with the board of election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
