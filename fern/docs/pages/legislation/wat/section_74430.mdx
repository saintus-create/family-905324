# WAT § 74430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Relocation of Division Boundaries

**Citation:** WAT § 74430

---

## Text

The board of directors shall, by resolution, adjust the boundaries of any divisions pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 22000) of Division 21 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 435, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1999.)
