# WAT § 36402.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.5. Provisions Relating to Short Term Borrowings

**Citation:** WAT § 36402.2

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be given and the election shall be held and the result determined as nearly as practicable in the manner provided for bond elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 164.)
