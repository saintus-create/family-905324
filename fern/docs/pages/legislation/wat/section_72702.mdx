# WAT § 72702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 72702

---

## Text

The text of the resolution proposing annexation shall be published, pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code, prior to the time of hearing in at least one newspaper printed and published in the district, if there is a newspaper published and printed in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
