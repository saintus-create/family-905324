# WAT § 55534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 55534

---

## Text

When bonds are made callable, a statement to that effect shall be set forth on the face of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1015.)
