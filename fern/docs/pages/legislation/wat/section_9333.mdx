# WAT § 9333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9333

---

## Text

In case there is more than one owner of any tract of land separately assessed in and by the assessment, all of the owners shall unite in the ballot to be cast at the election for and on behalf of the tract, or shall authorize one or more of their number or some other person to cast the vote for them by proxy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
