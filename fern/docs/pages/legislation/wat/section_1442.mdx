# WAT § 1442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 6.6. Temporary Changes > Article: 1. Temporary Urgency Changes

**Citation:** WAT § 1442

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to any permittee or licensee petitioning for a temporary change pursuant to Chapter 10.5 (commencing with Section 1725).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 678, Sec. 14. (AB 658) Effective January 1, 2020.)
