# WAT § 35472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 35472

---

## Text

For the purpose of providing funds to pay revenue bonds and interest when due the board shall fix and collect charges for the sale of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
