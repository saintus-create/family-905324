# WAT § 42202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 1. Plans, Specifications, and Estimate of Costs

**Citation:** WAT § 42202

---

## Text

The cost of the examinations, surveys, plans, specifications, and estimates is a part of the expense of the project. The board may issue warrants therefor, which shall be payable out of the funds of the district and may be included in any bond issue authorized for the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
