# WAT § 25978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 3. Segregation Before Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 25978

---

## Text

Each application shall be accompanied by a fee of two dollars ($2) for each separate portion into which the applicant desires the parcel to be segregated.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 995.)
