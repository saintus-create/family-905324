# WAT § 72086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 8.5. EXCLUSION OF TERRITORY FROM IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 7. Determination of and Alternative Methods of Payment of Debt Service and Costs of Maintenance and Operation

**Citation:** WAT § 72086

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing, the board shall by resolution determine whether it is necessary or desirable to exclude the territory. If so, the resolution shall also state:

(a) The reasons why such exclusion is necessary or desirable.

(b) That the exterior boundaries of the improvement district following such exclusion are set forth on a map on file with the secretary, which map shall govern all details as to the extent of the then existing improvement district.
