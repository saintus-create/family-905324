# WAT § 75443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75443

---

## Text

Only qualified electors of the district may vote at the election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
