# WAT § 1745.08

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 4. Water Supplier Contracts

**Citation:** WAT § 1745.08

---

## Text

This article is in addition to, and not a limitation on, the authority of any public agency under any other provision of law, including, but not limited to, Article 1 (commencing with Section 1725).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 481, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
