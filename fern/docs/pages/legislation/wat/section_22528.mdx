# WAT § 22528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 5. Lease of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 22528

---

## Text

The lease shall be made to the highest bidder, but any and all bids may be rejected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
