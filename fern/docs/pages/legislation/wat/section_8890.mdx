# WAT § 8890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8890

---

## Text

The proceeds of the sale shall be deposited with the State Treasurer to the credit of the assessment under which the property became delinquent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
