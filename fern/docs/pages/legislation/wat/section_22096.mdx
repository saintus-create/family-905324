# WAT § 22096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Drainage

**Citation:** WAT § 22096

---

## Text

All laws respecting irrigation by districts or respecting districts shall also be so construed and enforced as to apply to drainage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
