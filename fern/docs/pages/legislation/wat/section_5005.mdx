# WAT § 5005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Part: 5. RECORDATION OF WATER EXTRACTIONS AND DIVERSIONS > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 5005

---

## Text

Except as specified in Section 5004, failure to file the notice or delay in filing the same shall not cause the loss of rights to ground water which existed on January 1, 1956.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1869.)
