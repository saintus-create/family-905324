# WAT § 39034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39034

---

## Text

“Principal county” means the county in which the greater portion of the land of a district is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 779, Sec. 8. Effective October 10, 1999.)
