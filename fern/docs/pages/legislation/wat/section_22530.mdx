# WAT § 22530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 5. Lease of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 22530

---

## Text

A good and sufficient bond shall be required by the board to secure the faithful performance of the lease by the lessees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
