# WAT § 2808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2808

---

## Text

Notice of motion for an extension of time for completion shall be filed not more than 90 days prior to the expiration of the time limit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
