# WAT § 78525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 1. The Delta Improvement Account

**Citation:** WAT § 78525

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, as used in this chapter, “account” means the Delta Improvement Account created by Section 78526.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
