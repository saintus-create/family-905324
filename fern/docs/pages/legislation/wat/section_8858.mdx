# WAT § 8858

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8858

---

## Text

If any installment is unpaid at the expiration of 30 days from the date of the board order calling the installment, the installment is delinquent, together with accrued interest to date of delinquency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
