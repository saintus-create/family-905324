# WAT § 39427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 39427

---

## Text

The petitioners shall file with the petition a list of the names and addresses of all persons holding title to land within the proposed district as shown by the last equalized assessment roll of each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
