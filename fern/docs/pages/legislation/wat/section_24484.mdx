# WAT § 24484

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24484

---

## Text

The proceeds of limited assessments shall be paid into the district treasury for the purpose for which the assessments were respectively authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
