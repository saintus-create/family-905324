# WAT § 35252.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.3. Method of Election for the Riverview Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35252.6

---

## Text

All directors elected after January 1, 1996, shall be residents of the district and shall be registered at the time of their election and during their term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 833, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
