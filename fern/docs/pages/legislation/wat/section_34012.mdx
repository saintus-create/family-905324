# WAT § 34012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34012

---

## Text

“Include” except when used in relation to the inclusion of land into a district does not necessarily exclude matters not enumerated.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
