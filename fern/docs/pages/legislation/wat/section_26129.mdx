# WAT § 26129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 3. Assessment Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 26129

---

## Text

Thereupon the collector shall as to each parcel sold make an entry “sold to the district” in the assessment book.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
