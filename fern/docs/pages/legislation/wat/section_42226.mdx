# WAT § 42226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 2. Units of Construction

**Citation:** WAT § 42226

---

## Text

In the event of the division of the project into units of construction, and the specification of one or more units for future construction, the board shall, when it determines upon the construction of any unit, pass a resolution to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1993.)
