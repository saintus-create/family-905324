# WAT § 53043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 3. Time Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53043

---

## Text

No time warrants shall be issued, sold, or disposed of for less than the face amount thereof and accrued interest thereon to date of delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
