# WAT § 71000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 71000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Municipal Water District Law of 1911.
