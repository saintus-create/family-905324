# WAT § 50972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Co-operation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 50972

---

## Text

A district may carry out and perform the terms of any contract so made.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1111.)
