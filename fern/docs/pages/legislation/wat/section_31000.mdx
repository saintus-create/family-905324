# WAT § 31000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31000

---

## Text

County water districts shall be managed under the provisions of this division and may exercise the powers therein expressly granted or necessarily implied therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
