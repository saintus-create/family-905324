# WAT § 71503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election of Directors > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71503

---

## Text

Each candidate for director who at the general district election receives the highest number of votes cast for the candidate for that office is elected as a director of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 7, Sec. 7. Effective February 8, 1984.)
