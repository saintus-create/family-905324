# WAT § 8971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8971

---

## Text

When the State Treasurer pays a warrant he shall, in addition to the amount for which the warrant was drawn, pay the interest accrued thereon as certified to by the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
