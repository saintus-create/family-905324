# WAT § 8554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8554

---

## Text

The Governor shall select one of the members of the board as president.
