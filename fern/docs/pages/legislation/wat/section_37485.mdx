# WAT § 37485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 1. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 37485

---

## Text

Land not a part of the district whether or not contiguous to it may be included within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
