# WAT § 11851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 7. Insurance and Indemnity Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11851

---

## Text

The department may carry insurance to cover any accident or destruction in part or in whole to any works until all bonds and interest thereon have been fully redeemed and paid.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
