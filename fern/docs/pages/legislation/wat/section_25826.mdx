# WAT § 25826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 2. Special Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25826

---

## Text

The petition shall request that the board determine what proportion of the regular rates of assessment in the district should be used in levying assessments on the land described in the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
