# WAT § 8720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8720

---

## Text

Any person who does any act contrary to or in violation of any of the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
