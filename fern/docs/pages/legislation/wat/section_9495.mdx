# WAT § 9495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 7. Annual Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 9495

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall make a report to the board as often as requested of all entries made by him on the collection list.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
