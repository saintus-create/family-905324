# WAT § 52261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 4. Exchange

**Citation:** WAT § 52261

---

## Text

Exchange bonds shall be executed by all persons required by law to execute the bonds for which the exchange is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
