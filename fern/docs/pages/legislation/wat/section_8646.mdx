# WAT § 8646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 3. Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8646

---

## Text

The board may do the necesssary work immediately for the protection or preservation of the levee, without giving the notice provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
