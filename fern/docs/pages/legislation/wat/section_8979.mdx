# WAT § 8979

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8979

---

## Text

All warrants purchased or acquired by exchange by the Department of Finance shall be registered by the State Treasurer, unless already registered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
