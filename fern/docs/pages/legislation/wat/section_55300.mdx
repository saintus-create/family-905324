# WAT § 55300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Governing Body

**Citation:** WAT § 55300

---

## Text

The powers of the district enumerated in this division shall, except as therein otherwise provided, be exercised by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
