# WAT § 6364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 2. Repaired or Altered Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6364

---

## Text

The certificate of approval shall supersede any previous certificate of approval issued for the dam or reservoir so repaired or altered.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
