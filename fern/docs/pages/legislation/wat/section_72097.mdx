# WAT § 72097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Taxes Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 72097

---

## Text

All county officers charged with the duty of collecting taxes shall collect district taxes at the same time and in the same form and manner as county taxes are collected, and shall pay the collected district taxes to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
