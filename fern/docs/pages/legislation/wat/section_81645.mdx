# WAT § 81645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Authority > Article: 1. Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 81645

---

## Text

The authority may exercise the powers that are expressly granted by this division, together with other powers that are reasonably implied from those expressed powers, and powers necessary and proper to carry out this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
