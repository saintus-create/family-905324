# WAT § 60232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 60232

---

## Text

Each district has the power generally to perform all acts necessary to carry out fully the provisions of this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
