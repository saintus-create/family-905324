# WAT § 75060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75060

---

## Text

The bond election shall be conducted in accordance with the general election laws of the state as far as applicable and except as otherwise provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
