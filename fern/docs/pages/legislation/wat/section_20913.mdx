# WAT § 20913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20913

---

## Text

At the formation election the elective officers of the district shall be elected, but only one person shall be elected to fill the offices consolidated, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
