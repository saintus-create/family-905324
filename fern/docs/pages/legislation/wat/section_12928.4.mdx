# WAT § 12928.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.6. Groundwater Recharge Facilities Financing > Article: 4. Loans

**Citation:** WAT § 12928.4

---

## Text

As a part of the contract for a loan, the agency shall agree to indemnify and hold and save the state, the department, and its employees free from any and all liability for damages that may arise out of the work performed on projects financed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1034, Sec. 1.)
