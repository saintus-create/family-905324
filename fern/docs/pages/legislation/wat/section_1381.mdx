# WAT § 1381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 6. Permits > Article: 2. Issuance of Permit

**Citation:** WAT § 1381

---

## Text

The issuance of a permit gives the right to take and use water only to the extent and for the purpose allowed in the permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
