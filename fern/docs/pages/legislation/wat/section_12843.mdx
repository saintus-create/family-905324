# WAT § 12843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3.5. Fish, Wildlife, and Recreation in Connection With Flood Control and Watershed Protection Projects > Article: 2. Responsibilities of State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 12843

---

## Text

In order to achieve the goals set forth in this chapter, all state agencies exercising resource management responsibilities shall review reconnaissance and feasibility reports submitted for state review and comment by federal agencies relating to any project or project feature affecting their responsibilities and shall submit their comments in accordance with the requirements of federal and state law and this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 537.)
