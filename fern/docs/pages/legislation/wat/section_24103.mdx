# WAT § 24103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Dissolution of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 24103

---

## Text

The order of dissolution shall be recorded in the same manner as the order forming the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
