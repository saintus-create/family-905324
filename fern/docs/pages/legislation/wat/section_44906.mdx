# WAT § 44906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 44906

---

## Text

Coupons payable to bearer in a form and signed as prescribed by the board may be attached to warrants payable at a future date to evidence their interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1367.)
