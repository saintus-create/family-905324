# WAT § 1323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 3. Posting and Mailing Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1323

---

## Text

Proof of posting shall be by affidavit of the applicant or the person posting notice on behalf of the applicant and shall be filed within 40 days from the date of issuance of notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
