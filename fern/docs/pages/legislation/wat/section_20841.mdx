# WAT § 20841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 3. Final Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20841

---

## Text

The time set for the final hearing shall not be less than one week from the meeting at which the time is set.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
