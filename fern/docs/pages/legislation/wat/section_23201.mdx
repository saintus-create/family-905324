# WAT § 23201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 2. Contracts With United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23201

---

## Text

A district may pursuant to the contract sell or lease water for private land, to entrymen, or to municipalities, in the neighborhood of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
