# WAT § 31426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 4. Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 31426

---

## Text

The district may dispose of the bonds so issued at the times or in the manner the board deems to be to the public interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
