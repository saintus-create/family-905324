# WAT § 75801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 4. Recordation of Change of Boundaries and Filing of Certificate

**Citation:** WAT § 75801

---

## Text

A certified copy of the entry in the minutes of the board excluding any land, certified by the president and secretary, shall be filed for record in the recorder’s office of each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
