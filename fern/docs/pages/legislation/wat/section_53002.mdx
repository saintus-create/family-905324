# WAT § 53002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 53002

---

## Text

Demand warrants are warrants, drawn for any purpose, which are payable upon demand.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
