# WAT § 13284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 5. Individual Disposal Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 13284

## Text

The state board may adopt guidelines, regulations, or policies necessary to implement the provisions of this article.
