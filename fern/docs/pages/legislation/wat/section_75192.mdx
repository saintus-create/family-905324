# WAT § 75192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Benefit Assessment Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75192

---

## Text

At the hearing the board shall hear such objections to the formation petition, to the formation of the improvement district and its boundaries, to the plans, specifications, and estimates, and to the proposed assessment and the apportionment thereof as may come before it, and shall make such changes in reference thereto as it may consider proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
