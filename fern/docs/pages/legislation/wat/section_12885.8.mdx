# WAT § 12885.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 6. Loans

**Citation:** WAT § 12885.8

---

## Text

The department shall require the payment of interest on each loan that is made pursuant to this chapter after the effective date of the 1967 act amending this section, at a rate of 212 percent per annum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 179.)
