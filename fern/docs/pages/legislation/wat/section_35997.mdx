# WAT § 35997

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Provisions Relating to All Bonds > Article: 3. Sale and Exchange of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 35997

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 36170, a district shall not sell or exchange any of its bonds for less than par value plus the accrued interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
