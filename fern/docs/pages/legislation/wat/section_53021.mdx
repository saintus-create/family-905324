# WAT § 53021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 2. Demand Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53021

---

## Text

Demand warrants shall be presented to the county treasurer, and, if not paid on presentation, shall be registered and an endorsement of nonpayment made thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
