# WAT § 20627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Information by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 20627

---

## Text

Pending the completion of the preliminary surveys and field investigations, the State Water Resources Control Board may withhold from appropriation any unappropriated water likely to be needed by the district.
