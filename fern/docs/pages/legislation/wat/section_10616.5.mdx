# WAT § 10616.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10616.5

---

## Text

‛Recycled water” means the reclamation and reuse of wastewater for beneficial use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 854, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
