# WAT § 8715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8715

---

## Text

Any existing levee may be protected or strengthened in case of emergency during the season of flood water, where it is in danger of injury or destruction from flood water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
