# WAT § 23287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 6. Borrowing from United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23287

## Text

A majority vote at an election shall be sufficient to authorize the execution of the contract.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
