# WAT § 300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Miscellaneous Powers of Department > Article: 4. Waste from Artesian Wells

**Citation:** WAT § 300

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, an artesian well is any artifical hole made in the ground through which water naturally flows from subterranean sources to the surface of the ground for any length of time.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
