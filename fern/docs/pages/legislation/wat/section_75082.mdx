# WAT § 75082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 6. Powers of the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 75082

---

## Text

All provisions of this division, insofar as they are applicable, including provisions for bonds and assessments, shall apply to and govern improvement districts formed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
