# WAT § 2601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 5. Report and Preliminary Order of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2601

---

## Text

The report shall note separately any material differences between the claims and the factual determinations made by the representatives of the board during the investigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
