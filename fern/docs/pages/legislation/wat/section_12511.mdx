# WAT § 12511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 12511

---

## Text

The board consists of eight members appointed by the Governor as provided in this chapter, two of whom shall be public members appointed by the Governor, and the Director of Water Resources and the Director of Fish and Game or their designees.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1027, Sec. 2.)
