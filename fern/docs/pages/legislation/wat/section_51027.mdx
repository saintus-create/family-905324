# WAT § 51027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 1. Districts Within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51027

---

## Text

The Reclamation Board may continue the hearing from time to time and may cause such investigation and report of the plan to be made by the engineers connected with the Reclamation Board or by such other competent authority as the board deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
