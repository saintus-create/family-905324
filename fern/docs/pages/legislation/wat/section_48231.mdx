# WAT § 48231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 2. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 48231

---

## Text

No inclusion of land into any district impairs its existence, its rights, including those in or to property, or its obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
