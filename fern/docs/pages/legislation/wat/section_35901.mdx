# WAT § 35901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 35901

---

## Text

All money belonging to the district, or to which the district is entitled, shall, when received, be paid by the person receiving it to the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
