# WAT § 36195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36195

---

## Text

General obligation bonds shall be payable at such time or times and place or places as may be designated by the board, but in no case shall the maturity of any bond be more than 40 years from its date.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
