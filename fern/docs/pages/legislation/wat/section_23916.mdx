# WAT § 23916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 23916

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the provisions of this division applicable to general obligation bonds of the district and assessments therefor shall, so far as consistent with this chapter, apply to general obligation bonds of the district for improvement districts therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 580.)
