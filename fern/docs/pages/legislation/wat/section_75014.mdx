# WAT § 75014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75014

---

## Text

If more than one-third of the holders of title or evidence of title of lands within the proposed improvement district object in writing at the hearing to the formation of the proposed improvement district, the board shall deny the formation petition and no further proceedings shall be had thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
