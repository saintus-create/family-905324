# WAT § 9036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9036

---

## Text

If it appears that the amount that would have been assessed against any tract of land is greater than the amount to which the tract or owner thereof would have been entitled if the distribution had been made, the excess shall be assessed to the tract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
