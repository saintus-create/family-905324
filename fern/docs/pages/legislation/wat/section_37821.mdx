# WAT § 37821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Merger

**Citation:** WAT § 37821

---

## Text

The surviving district comprises all land in the water district immediately following the merger plus inclusion, and less exclusions, of land thereafter made pursuant to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
