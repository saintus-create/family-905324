# WAT § 46207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 3. Assessment Rolls

**Citation:** WAT § 46207

---

## Text

The objections to any assesment shall be in writing, verified, and filed with the secretary of the board, and shall set forth the grounds of the objection.
