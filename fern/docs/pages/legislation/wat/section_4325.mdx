# WAT § 4325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 6. Alternative Method of Payment by Owners

**Citation:** WAT § 4325

---

## Text

The procedure set forth in this article for collecting and making available money for watermaster distribution is in lieu of that set forth in the other provisions of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
