# WAT § 44057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States, the State and Other Districts > Article: 3. Transfer or Deposit of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 44057

---

## Text

The contract with the United States may call for the payment of the amount, or any part thereof, to be paid by the district to the United States at specified times, in installments, and with interest not exceeding 6 percent per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
