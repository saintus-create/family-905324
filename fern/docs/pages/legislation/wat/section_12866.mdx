# WAT § 12866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Watershed Protection Flood Prevention Projects > Article: 4. Declaration of Intent

**Citation:** WAT § 12866

---

## Text

The costs of local cooperation which the State will assume pursuant to Section 12865 shall be limited to the costs of lands, easements and rights of way.
