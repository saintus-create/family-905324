# WAT § 42550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Recommendation of Completion of Project > Article: 3. Proceedings After Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42550

---

## Text

If a majority of all votes cast are “Completion of project—Yes” and also a majority of the qualified voters who voted at the election voted “Completion of project—Yes,” the report and recommendation is adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
