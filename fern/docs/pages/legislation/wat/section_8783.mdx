# WAT § 8783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 2. Assessors and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 8783

---

## Text

The assessors, may amend, modify or change the exterior boundaries of the land that, in their opinion, will be benefited by such expenditures.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
