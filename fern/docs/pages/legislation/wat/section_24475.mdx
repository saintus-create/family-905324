# WAT § 24475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24475

---

## Text

The following funds are created to which district money properly belonging shall be apportioned:

(a) Bond principal fund.

(b) Bond interest fund.

(c) Construction fund.

(d) General fund.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
