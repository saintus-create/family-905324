# WAT § 47379

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 8. Assessments Upon Which Bonds Have Been Issued > Article: 4. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 47379

---

## Text

Every deed by a county treasurer purporting to be executed under this chapter shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of the matters therein recited, and of ownership by the grantee of the land therein described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
