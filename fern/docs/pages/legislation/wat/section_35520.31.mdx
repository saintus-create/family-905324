# WAT § 35520.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.2. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Lost Hills Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35520.31

---

## Text

The secretary shall file certified copies of any amendatory or reapportioned assessment with the district assessor, the district tax collector, and the district treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 57, Sec. 1. Effective May 31, 1983.)
