# WAT § 31392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 31392

---

## Text

In determining the amount of debt to be incurred, the legislative body may include:

(a) All costs and estimated costs incidental to or connected with the acquisition, construction, improving or financing of the project.

(b) All engineering, inspection, legal and fiscal agent’s fees, costs of the bond election and of the issuance of said bonds, and bond interest estimated to accrue during the construction period and for a period of not to exceed 12 months after completion of construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 962.)
