# WAT § 24021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 4. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 24021

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of an assessment or of warrants may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
