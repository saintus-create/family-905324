# WAT § 13414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6. State Financial Assistance > Article: 2. Loans to Local Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 13414

---

## Text

All money received in repayment of loans under this chapter shall be paid to the State Treasurer and credited to the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
