# WAT § 20529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20529

---

## Text

“Property” except in Part 10 embraces all real and personal property, including water, water rights, works, franchises, concessions and rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
