# WAT § 46002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46002

---

## Text

The amounts required to be raised to pay warrants of the department and to pay expenses shall be separately stated in the resolution levying the preliminary assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 99. Effective January 1, 1993.)
