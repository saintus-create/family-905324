# WAT § 23178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 23178

---

## Text

Article 35 (commencing with Section 20560) of Chapter 1 of Part 3 of Division 2 of the Public Contract Code does not apply in the case of any contract between a district and the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 682. Effective January 1, 2007.)
