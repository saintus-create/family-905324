# WAT § 48086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 4. Liability of Excluded Land

**Citation:** WAT § 48086

---

## Text

The land excluded shall not be held answerable or chargeable for any obligation incurred after the exclusion of the land from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
