# WAT § 30230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Notice of Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30230

---

## Text

The county elections official of the county containing the proposed district shall publish a notice of a hearing on the formation petition once a week for at least two weeks before the time when the petition is to be presented and considered in at least one, but not to exceed three, newspapers printed and published in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 158. Effective January 1, 2003.)
