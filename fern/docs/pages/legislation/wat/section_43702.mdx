# WAT § 43702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 43702

---

## Text

Service of summons upon a district may be made by serving it upon the secretary or a majority of the directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
