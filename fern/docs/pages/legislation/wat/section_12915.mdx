# WAT § 12915

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. California Water Fund > Article: 2. The California Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12915

---

## Text

To the extent the provisions of this chapter are inconsistent or in conflict with any of those provisions of Part 3 (commencing at Section 11100) of Division 6, the provisions of this chapter shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 140.)
