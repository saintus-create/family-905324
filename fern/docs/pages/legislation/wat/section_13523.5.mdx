# WAT § 13523.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Water Reclamation > Article: 4. Regulation of Reclamation

**Citation:** WAT § 13523.5

---

## Text

A regional board may not deny issuance of water reclamation requirements to a project which violates only a salinity standard in the basin plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1541, Sec. 9.)
