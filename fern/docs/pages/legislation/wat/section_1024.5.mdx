# WAT § 1024.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1.5. Water Leases

**Citation:** WAT § 1024.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not limit any review of the lessee’s use of the leased water.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 847, Sec. 3.)
