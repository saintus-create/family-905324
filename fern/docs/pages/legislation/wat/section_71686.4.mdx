# WAT § 71686.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 4. Water Replenishment Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 71686.4

---

## Text

All evidence relevant to the engineering survey and report and the board’s determination that such a replenishment assessment shall be levied may be introduced.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1725.)
