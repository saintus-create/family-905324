# WAT § 36171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 2. Sale and Exchange of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36171

---

## Text

General obligation bonds may be exchanged dollar for dollar for bonds of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
