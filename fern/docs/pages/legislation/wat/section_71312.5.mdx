# WAT § 71312.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71312.5

---

## Text

A district may issue bonds, borrow money, and incur indebtedness as authorized by law or this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1151.)
