# WAT § 79716.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.7. WATER QUALITY, SUPPLY, AND INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2014 > Chapter: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79716.5

---

## Text

Each state agency that receives an appropriation of funding made available by this division shall do the following:

(a) Evaluate the outcomes of projects funded by this division.

(b) Include in the agency’s reporting pursuant to Section 79716 the evaluation described in subdivision (a).

(c) Hold a grantee of funds accountable for completing projects funded by this division on time and within scope.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 27, Sec. 20. (SB 88) Effective June 24, 2015.)
