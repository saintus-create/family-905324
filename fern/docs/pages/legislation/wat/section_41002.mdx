# WAT § 41002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Voters and Qualifications

**Citation:** WAT § 41002

---

## Text

Each male or female voter over the age of 18 years may vote in person or by proxy.
