# WAT § 45271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 45271

---

## Text

To the right of or below the question on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 57. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
