# WAT § 41554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Special Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41554

---

## Text

No informalities in conducting the special election shall invalidate it, if otherwise fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
