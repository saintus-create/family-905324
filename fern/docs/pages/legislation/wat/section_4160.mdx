# WAT § 4160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Distribution of Water in Service Areas > Article: 2. Remedies for Improper Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 4160

---

## Text

Any person who is injured by the action of any watermaster may apply to the superior court of the county in which the injury takes place, for an injunction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
