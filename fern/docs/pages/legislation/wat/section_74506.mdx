# WAT § 74506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 74506

---

## Text

A district may disseminate information concerning the rights, properties, activities, plans, and proposals of the district; provided, however, that expenditures during any fiscal year for such purposes shall not exceed three cents ($0.03) for each one hundred dollars ($100) of assessed valuation of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
