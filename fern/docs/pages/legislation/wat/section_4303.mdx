# WAT § 4303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 5. Payment by and Collection From State Agencies and Public Entities

**Citation:** WAT § 4303

---

## Text

If the owner of the right is a county, municipality, district, political subdivision, public corporation, or state agency, the amount apportioned, together with the penalty in case of delinquency, is a legal charge against the general fund thereof or such fund as may be provided for the maintenance and operation of its public works.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 360.)
