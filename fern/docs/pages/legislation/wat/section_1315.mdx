# WAT § 1315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 2. Publication of Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1315

---

## Text

Proof of publication shall be filed by the applicant within 60 days from the date of issuance of the notice.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
