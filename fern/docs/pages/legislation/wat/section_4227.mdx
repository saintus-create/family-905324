# WAT § 4227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 2. Statement and Budget

**Citation:** WAT § 4227

---

## Text

The statement shall also contain an apportionment of the amount of the budget among the owners of the various rights to store or divert within the service area.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 25. Effective August 16, 2004.)
