# WAT § 14914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 23. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act > Article: 2. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14914

---

## Text

The purchase price of any interest in land or water acquired pursuant to this chapter may reflect the benefit to the state of alleviating drainage problems in the San Joaquin Valley and the conversion of property to wildlife habitats.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 959, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993. Section operative July 1, 1993, pursuant to Section 14920.)
