# WAT § 41337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41337

---

## Text

The ballots in each division of the district shall contain names of persons to be voted for as director to represent that division only.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
