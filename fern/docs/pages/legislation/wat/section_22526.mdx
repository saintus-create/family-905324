# WAT § 22526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 5. Lease of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 22526

---

## Text

A district may lease part or all of its works whenever the leasing will benefit the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
