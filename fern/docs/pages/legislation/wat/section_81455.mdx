# WAT § 81455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 6. Changes in Organization

**Citation:** WAT § 81455

---

## Text

Any territory annexed to, or detached from, a member public entity, upon completion of the annexation or detachment, shall be deemed annexed to, or detached from, the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
