# WAT § 30503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30503

---

## Text

If a person elected fails to qualify, the office shall be filled as if there were a vacancy in the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
