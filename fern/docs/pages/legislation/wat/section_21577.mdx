# WAT § 21577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Abolition and Reestablishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 21577

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall thereupon find whether or not there are less than 2,500 acres in the district, less than 100 electors residing in it, and only three divisions in it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 454, Sec. 3.)
