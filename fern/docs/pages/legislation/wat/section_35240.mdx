# WAT § 35240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Method of Election for the Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35240

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to the Santa Margarita Water District. For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Santa Margarita Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1047, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
