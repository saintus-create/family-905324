# WAT § 35540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.75. Provisions Pertaining Only to Lost Hills Water District > Article: 1. Plan of Water Allocation

**Citation:** WAT § 35540

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply only to the Lost Hills Water District as a member unit of the Kern County Water Agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 63.)
