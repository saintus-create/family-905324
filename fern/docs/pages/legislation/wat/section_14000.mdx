# WAT § 14000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 16. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14000

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 45, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 81 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
