# WAT § 76020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Consolidation

**Citation:** WAT § 76020

---

## Text

A district may be consolidated in the same manner as is provided for the consolidation of county water districts in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 32650) of Part 8 of Division 12 of this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
