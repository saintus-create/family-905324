# WAT § 51894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 5. Reapportioning Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51894

---

## Text

When any assessment has been reapportioned on any parcel, the board shall cause the divisions or subdivisions and reapportionment to be properly entered or endorsed upon the assessment list, and shall file notice of all the changes with the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 104, Sec. 7.)
