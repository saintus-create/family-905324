# WAT § 50310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 2. Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 50310

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors finds, on the hearing of the petition, that the statements therein are correct, it shall make an order approving the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
