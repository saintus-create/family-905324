# WAT § 2043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 3. Expenses of Reference

**Citation:** WAT § 2043

---

## Text

The total expense shall be equitably apportioned by the board against the parties to the suit, and a statement thereof and of the apportionment shall be sent by registered mail by the board to the parties and filed with the court.
