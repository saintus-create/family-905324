# WAT § 46760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 5. Sale for Nonpayment

**Citation:** WAT § 46760

---

## Text

Upon filing of a resolution pursuant to Section 46759.5 or if no bid is made for the property equal to the amount due thereon, the property shall be sold to the district for the amount due.
