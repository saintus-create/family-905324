# WAT § 81404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 3. Government of Agency > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 81404

---

## Text

Before the vote on any motion, resolution, or ordinance is taken, any director may call for weighted voting. Upon such a call, Section 81405 shall apply as to that motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
