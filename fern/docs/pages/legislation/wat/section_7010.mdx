# WAT § 7010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 1. Joint Use of Wells, PumpingPlants and Conduits > Article: 4. Proceeding for Declaration of Rights in Jointly Used Wells, Pumping Plants or Conduits

**Citation:** WAT § 7010

---

## Text

The decree shall include a determination of the proportionate amount which all users shall contribute to the maintenance, repair, improvement and other expenses relating to the conduit, well, or pumping plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
