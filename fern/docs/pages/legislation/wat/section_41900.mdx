# WAT § 41900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Contest of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41900

---

## Text

Any election held under the provisions of this division may be contested by any holder of title to land liable to assessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
