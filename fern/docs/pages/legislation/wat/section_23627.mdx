# WAT § 23627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 2. Plans and Estimate of Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23627

---

## Text

If there are any, the plans and specifications, estimate of cost, and the statement of the proposed assessment shall be filed with the secretary in the office of the district and may be inspected by all persons interested.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
