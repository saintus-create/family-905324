# WAT § 45274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 45274

---

## Text

The board of election shall deliver a certificate showing the result and the number of votes cast for and against the issuing of the bonds to the county elections official of each county, and a duplicate to the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 2003.)
