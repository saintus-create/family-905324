# WAT § 41442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 6. Results of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 41442

---

## Text

The secretary shall immediately make out and deliver to the elected persons certificates of election signed by him and authenticated with the seal of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
