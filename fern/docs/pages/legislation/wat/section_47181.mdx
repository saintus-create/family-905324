# WAT § 47181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 47181

---

## Text

Whenever any tolls or charges for the use of the water or for other services rendered by the district provided for by this division have been fixed by the board, they may be made payable in advance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
