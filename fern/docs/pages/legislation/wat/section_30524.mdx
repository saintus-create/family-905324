# WAT § 30524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30524

---

## Text

A majority of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
