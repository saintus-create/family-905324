# WAT § 8799

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8799

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication for two weeks in some newspaper of general circulation published in the county in which the land or some portion thereof is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
