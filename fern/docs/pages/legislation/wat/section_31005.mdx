# WAT § 31005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31005

---

## Text

A district may have construction or other work performed by contract or may do the work under its own superintendence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
