# WAT § 31306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31306

---

## Text

If designated as depositary the county treasurer shall:

(a) Receive and receipt for all of the district’s money received by him and place it in the county treasury to the credit of the district.

(b) Be responsible upon his official bond for the safekeeping and disbursing of all district money so held by him.
