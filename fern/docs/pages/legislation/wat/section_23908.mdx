# WAT § 23908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 23908

---

## Text

The hearing on any proposed modifications, or any continuance thereof, may be held at the same time and place as any continued hearing on the resolution of intention and both hearings may be held and conducted concurrently.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 580.)
