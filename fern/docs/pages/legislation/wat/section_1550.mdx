# WAT § 1550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Right Fees > Article: 3. Water Rights Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 1550

---

## Text

There is in the State Treasury a Water Rights Fund, which is hereby established.
