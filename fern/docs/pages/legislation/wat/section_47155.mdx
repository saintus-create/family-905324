# WAT § 47155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 47155

---

## Text

The supplementary assessment shall be collected in the manner provided in this division for the collection of original assessments and the board may call the same as a whole or in installments from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
