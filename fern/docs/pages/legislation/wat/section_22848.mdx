# WAT § 22848

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 2. Internal Organization

**Citation:** WAT § 22848

---

## Text

The same person may hold the office of president, secretary, treasurer, assessor, and collector, or any of those offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 577, Sec. 2.)
