# WAT § 70204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 70204

---

## Text

Claims for salaries and services shall be sworn and approved by the board of directors before a warrant is drawn for the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
