# WAT § 6229

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 2. Repairs, Alterations or Removals

**Citation:** WAT § 6229

---

## Text

In case of an emergency where repairs are necessary to safeguard life and property repairs may be started immediately, but the department shall be notified at once of proposed repairs and of work under way.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
