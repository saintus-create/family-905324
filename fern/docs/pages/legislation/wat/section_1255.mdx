# WAT § 1255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1255

---

## Text

The board shall reject an application when in its judgment the proposed appropriation would not best conserve the public interest.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
