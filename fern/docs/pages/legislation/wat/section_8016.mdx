# WAT § 8016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 2. Preliminary Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 8016

## Text

The resolution or ordinance of intention, shall, after its passage and approval, be published.
