# WAT § 71932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation of Uninhabited Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71932

---

## Text

All ballots shall be returned to the secretary of the district at the office of the district on or before the last date established for the return of the ballots by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 385.)
