# WAT § 8031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8031

---

## Text

No bond shall be for less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor for more than one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
