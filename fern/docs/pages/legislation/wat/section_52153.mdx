# WAT § 52153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52153

---

## Text

The date fixed for the sale of refunding bonds shall be at least 95 days prior to the maturity date of the principal of the bonds to be refunded by the sale of the refunding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
