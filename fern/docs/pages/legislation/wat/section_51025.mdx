# WAT § 51025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 1. Districts Within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51025

---

## Text

At the hearing the Reclamation Board shall hear any evidence offered with respect to the plan, and thereafter shall approve, modify, amend or reject the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
