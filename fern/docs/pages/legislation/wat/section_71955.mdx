# WAT § 71955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Sale of Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 71955

---

## Text

The denomination of the bonds shall be stated in the resolution providing for their issuance but shall not be less than one hundred dollars ($100).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
