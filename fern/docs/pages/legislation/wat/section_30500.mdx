# WAT § 30500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30500

---

## Text

Each district shall have a board of five directors each of whom, whether elected or appointed, shall be a voter of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
