# WAT § 79547

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 6. Contaminant and Salt Removal Technologies

**Citation:** WAT § 79547

---

## Text

(a) Funds made available pursuant to Section 79545 shall be administered in accordance with this section.

(b) Grants shall be awarded in accordance with Section 79545 on a statewide competitive basis.

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 240, Sec. 33. Effective August 13, 2003.)
