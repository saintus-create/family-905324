# WAT § 46696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 2. Notice of Time for Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 46696

---

## Text

The notice shall be sent through the mail, addressed to each holder of title to land at the address shown on the county assessment roll, together with a statement showing the amount due for the applicable parcel or parcels. The notice shall also be published once a week for two consecutive weeks in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 793, Sec. 2. Effective September 18, 1987.)
