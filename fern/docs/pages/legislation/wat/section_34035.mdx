# WAT § 34035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34035

---

## Text

“Bonds” includes both general obligation bonds and revenue bonds except as otherwise expressly provided.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
