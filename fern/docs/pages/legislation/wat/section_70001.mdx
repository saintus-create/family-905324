# WAT § 70001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 70001

---

## Text

Nothing in this part affects the provisions of any statute in relation to levee districts now in force.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
