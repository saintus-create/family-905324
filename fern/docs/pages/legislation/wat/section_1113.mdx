# WAT § 1113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3.5. State Water Resources Control Board Administrative Hearings Office

**Citation:** WAT § 1113

---

## Text

In accordance with subdivision (c) of Section 1110, the Administrative Adjudication Code of Ethics (Article 16 (commencing with Section 11475) of Chapter 4.5 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code) shall apply to hearing officers of the Administrative Hearings Office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 668, Sec. 1. (AB 747) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative July 1, 2019, pursuant to Section 2 of Stats. 2018, Ch. 668.)
