# WAT § 75600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 3. Levy

**Citation:** WAT § 75600

---

## Text

Clerical errors occurring or appearing in the name of any person or in the description of the water-producing facility where the production of water therefrom is otherwise properly charged, or in the making or extension of any charge upon the records which do not affect the substantial rights of the assessee or assessees, shall not invalidate the ground water charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
