# WAT § 26083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 1. Date and Penalties for Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 26083

---

## Text

In a suit for assessments the district may recover the amount of the delinquent assessments, penalties, and costs of suit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
