# WAT § 11465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 3. Limitation of Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 11465

---

## Text

The department shall not make any change, alteration, or revision of any rates, prices, or charges established by any contract entered into pursuant to this part except as provided by the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
