# WAT § 11984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** WAT § 11984

## Text

The department is authorized to adopt those rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out, and as are consistent with, this chapter.
