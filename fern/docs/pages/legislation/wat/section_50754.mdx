# WAT § 50754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Elections > Article: 2. Conducting

**Citation:** WAT § 50754

---

## Text

The board of directors of irrigation districts holding title to lands obtained through collectors’ deeds may cast the votes belonging to such lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
