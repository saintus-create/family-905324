# WAT § 70075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. The Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 70075

---

## Text

The directors shall keep a minute book in which shall be entered the proceedings of all meetings and shall keep an office for the transaction of the district’s business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
