# WAT § 513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 513

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, association, partnership, corporation, or public entity of any kind.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 407, Sec. 2.)
