# WAT § 31142.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9.3. Alameda County Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31142.30

---

## Text

Any abandoned or unused well, exploratory hole, or other excavation that creates or threatens to create a water contamination hazard is a public nuisance. The board shall adopt, by ordinance, regulations to carry out this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 563, Sec. 1. (SB 133) Effective January 1, 2010.)
