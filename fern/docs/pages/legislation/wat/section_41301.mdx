# WAT § 41301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 1. Preliminary Procedure and Nominations

**Citation:** WAT § 41301

---

## Text

Prior to the election, the board shall appoint for each precinct, from the voters thereof, one inspector and two judges, who constitute an election board for the precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
