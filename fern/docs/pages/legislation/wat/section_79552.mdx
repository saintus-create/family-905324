# WAT § 79552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 7. CALFED Bay-Delta Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79552

---

## Text

All projects financed pursuant to this chapter shall be consistent with the CALFED Programmatic Record of Decision including its provisions regarding finance and balanced implementation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
