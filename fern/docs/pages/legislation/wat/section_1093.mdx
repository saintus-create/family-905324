# WAT § 1093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Witnesses and Production of Evidence > Article: 3. Compelling Attendance

**Citation:** WAT § 1093

---

## Text

The petition shall ask an order of court compelling the witness to attend, testify, and produce the evidence before the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
