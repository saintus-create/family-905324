# WAT § 71015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 71015

---

## Text

“Principal county” means the county in which the greater portion of the land of a district or a proposed district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
