# WAT § 78651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 6. Water Supply Reliability > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 78651

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, as used in this chapter, “account” means the Water Supply Reliability Account created by Section 78652.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
