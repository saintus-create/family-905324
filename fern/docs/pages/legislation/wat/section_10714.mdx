# WAT § 10714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.7. GROUNDWATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10714

---

## Text

This part neither preempts, negates, affects, nor infers the existence of any powers of a local agency in other groundwater basins of the state to establish programs for the management of groundwater resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 472, Sec. 1.)
