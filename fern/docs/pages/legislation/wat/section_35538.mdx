# WAT § 35538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Kern Delta, Cawelo, Rag Gulch, and Kern-Tulare Water Districts > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 35538

---

## Text

Proceeds from assessments levied pursuant to this chapter may be used to pay any lawful obligation of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1085.)
