# WAT § 8835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8835

---

## Text

As used in this article map means a map or maps prepared by the assessors appointed for any assessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
