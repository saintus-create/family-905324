# WAT § 12921.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.5. Protection of Ground Water Basins > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 12921.2

---

## Text

“Local agency” means any county, city, state agency or public district.
