# WAT § 8885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8885

---

## Text

Any parcel of land bid in and purchased by the drainage district at the delinquent sale shall be held in trust for the assessment under which the parcel of land was sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
