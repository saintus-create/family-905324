# WAT § 40658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Board Meetings and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 40658

## Text

The board shall manage and conduct the business of the district.
