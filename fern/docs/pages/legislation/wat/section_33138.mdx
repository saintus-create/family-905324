# WAT § 33138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 33138

---

## Text

In the case of conflict between the Storm Water District Act of 1909 and this division, this division prevails.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
