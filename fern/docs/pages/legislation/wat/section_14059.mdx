# WAT § 14059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 17. Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 2. Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14059

---

## Text

As approved by the Legislature annually in the Budget Act, the board may expend for the administration of this chapter not more than 5 percent of the amount of the bonds authorized to be issued under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 47, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 83 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
