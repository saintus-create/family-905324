# WAT § 75542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Establishment of Zones and Registration of Water-Measuring Devices

**Citation:** WAT § 75542

---

## Text

Any new water-producing facility constructed or reestablished after the period prescribed in Section 75541 shall be registered with the district and, if required by the board, measured with a water-measuring device satisfactory to the district within 30 days after the completion or reestablishment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
