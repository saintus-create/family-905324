# WAT § 75575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 75575

---

## Text

The findings and determinations by the board are conclusive and binding upon all persons and parties.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
