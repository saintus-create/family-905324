# WAT § 52507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52507

---

## Text

The principal and interest of refunding bonds shall be based on and payable out of the assessment upon which the bonds to be refunded are payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
