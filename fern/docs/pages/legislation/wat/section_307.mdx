# WAT § 307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Miscellaneous Powers of Department > Article: 4. Waste from Artesian Wells

**Citation:** WAT § 307

---

## Text

Any person owning, possessing, or occupying any land upon which is situated an artesian well, who causes, suffers, or permits water unnecessarily to flow from the well or to go to waste is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
