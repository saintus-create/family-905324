# WAT § 46950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Supplementary Assessments for Completion of Project > Article: 3. Additional Property or Work

**Citation:** WAT § 46950

---

## Text

The board may determine upon the acquisition of property or the construction of work not contemplated in the report and recommendation, with accompanying plans and specifications, originally adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
