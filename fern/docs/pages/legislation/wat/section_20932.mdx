# WAT § 20932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20932

---

## Text

The directors elected at the formation election shall be parties defendant in an election contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
