# WAT § 48205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 1. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 48205

---

## Text

The petitioners shall advance to the board sufficient money to pay the estimated costs of all proceedings upon the petition.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1607.)
