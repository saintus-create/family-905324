# WAT § 13171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 3. State Water Quality Control > Article: 4. Other Powers and Duties of the State Board

**Citation:** WAT § 13171

---

## Text

The state board may establish a Water Quality Coordinating Committee, consisting of at least one member of each of the nine regional boards, to assist the state board in carrying out its responsibilities in water quality control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 813.)
