# WAT § 25110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 4. Unauthorized Refunding Modification

**Citation:** WAT § 25110

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are applicable only to refunding plans which both:

(a) Were adopted prior to January 31, 1939.

(b) Then contained no provisions for modification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
