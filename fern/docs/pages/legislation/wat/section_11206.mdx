# WAT § 11206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 2. Shasta Dam

**Citation:** WAT § 11206

---

## Text

The transmission line shall be located in such manner and along such route as to enable the most convenient distribution of electric power to the load centers traversed thereby or capable of service therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
