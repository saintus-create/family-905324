# WAT § 44601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 1. Execution

**Citation:** WAT § 44601

---

## Text

Warrants drawn by the board shall be signed by its president or vice president and secretary or assistant secretary and countersigned by the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
