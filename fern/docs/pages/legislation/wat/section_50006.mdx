# WAT § 50006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50006

---

## Text

“Landowner” means the holder of title or evidence of title to land within a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
