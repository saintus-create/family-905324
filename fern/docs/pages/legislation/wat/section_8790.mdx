# WAT § 8790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 2. Assessors and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 8790

---

## Text

The amounts stated and placed opposite each assessment shall be no part of the assessment and shall in no way affect the assessment charged against each tract of land as the same may be fixed, but shall be subject to review and readjustment in the same manner as the assessment itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
