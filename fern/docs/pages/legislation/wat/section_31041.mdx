# WAT § 31041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Property Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31041

---

## Text

A district may hold, use, enjoy, lease, or dispose of property within or without the district necessary to the full exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
