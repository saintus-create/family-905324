# WAT § 41008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Voters and Qualifications

**Citation:** WAT § 41008

---

## Text

Each precinct list shall be delivered to the election board for the precinct prior to the election.
