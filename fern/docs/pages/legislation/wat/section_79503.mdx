# WAT § 79503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. WATER SECURITY, CLEAN DRINKING WATER, COASTAL AND BEACH PROTECTION ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 79503

---

## Text

It is the intent of the people that water facility projects financed pursuant to this division shall be designed and constructed so as to improve the security and safety of the state’s drinking water system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 50.)
