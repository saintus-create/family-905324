# WAT § 12533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Commissioner

**Citation:** WAT § 12533

---

## Text

The commissioner shall do and perform all other things deemed necessary or expedient to carry out the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
