# WAT § 22477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 3. Power Lines on Public Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22477

---

## Text

Permission pursuant to Section 22476 is not necessary where the road to be used is a convenient part of the route of the lines and at the time construction of the lines was commenced or the plans for them were adopted was located in territory not within an organized city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
