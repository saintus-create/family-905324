# WAT § 8033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 8033

---

## Text

Each bond shall be made payable in lawful money of the United States on a day and at a place designated in the bond, with interest at the rate specified in the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
