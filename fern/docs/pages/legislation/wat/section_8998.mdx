# WAT § 8998

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 1. Flood Control Works, Purchases and Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 8998

---

## Text

The title to any such right of way or other easement or levees, cuts, canals or gates shall be conveyed to the drainage district upon compensation being made for the cost thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
