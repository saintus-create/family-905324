# WAT § 30296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30296

---

## Text

The vote shall be canvassed by the board of supervisors at the next regular meeting which is five or more days after the election.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 747.)
