# WAT § 74450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Redivision of a District Within Ventura County > Article: 1. Introduction

**Citation:** WAT § 74450

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply only to districts within Ventura County.
