# WAT § 8572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8572

---

## Text

The rules may be suspended or compliance therewith may be waived by the board at any meeting to such extent as may be deemed proper.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
