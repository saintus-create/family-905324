# WAT § 36402.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.5. Provisions Relating to Short Term Borrowings

**Citation:** WAT § 36402.3

---

## Text

The proceeds of such warrants or negotiable notes may be used and applied to the purchase or redemption of any outstanding warrants or negotiable notes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1993.)
