# WAT § 10656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10656

---

## Text

An urban water supplier is not eligible for a water grant or loan awarded or administered by the state unless the urban water supplier complies with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 14, Sec. 46. (SB 606) Effective January 1, 2019.)
