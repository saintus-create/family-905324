# WAT § 72031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Advance of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 72031

---

## Text

The board may provide that the district shall be repaid for any advance of funds, with interest at a rate not to exceed the interest value of money to the district, from the taxes levied exclusively upon the taxable property in the improvement district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 708.)
