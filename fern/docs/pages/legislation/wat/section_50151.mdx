# WAT § 50151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 5. Actions Against Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 50151

---

## Text

An action against any person by reason of his official connection with a district, or against the board, or the district, shall be instituted and tried in the proper court in the principal county. No court in any other county shall have jurisdiction over such action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
