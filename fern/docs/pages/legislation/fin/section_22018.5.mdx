# FIN § 22018.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22018.5

---

## Text

“Property owner” means all property owners of record on the property subject to the PACE assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 16. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017.)
