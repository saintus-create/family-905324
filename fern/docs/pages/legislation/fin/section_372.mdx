# FIN § 372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 4. Administration of the Department

**Citation:** FIN § 372

---

## Text

The department may expend moneys in accordance with law for the necessary travel expenses of officers and employees of the department while traveling in the line of their duties either within or without the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
