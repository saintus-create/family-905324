# FIN § 50128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 2. Licensing: Residential Mortgage Lender

**Citation:** FIN § 50128

---

## Text

As used in this act, the term “principal officer” means an officer with direct responsibility for the conduct of the residential mortgage lender or servicer licensee’s or license applicant’s lending or servicing activities in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 160, Sec. 57. (SB 36) Effective October 11, 2009.)
