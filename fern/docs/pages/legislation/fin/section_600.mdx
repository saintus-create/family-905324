# FIN § 600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 7. Liquidation and Conservation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 600

---

## Text

In this chapter, “Federal Insurance Agency” means the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation or the National Credit Union Administration, as appropriate, or their respective successors-in-interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 29. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
