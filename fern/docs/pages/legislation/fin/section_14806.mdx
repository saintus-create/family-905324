# FIN § 14806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 5. Members

**Citation:** FIN § 14806

---

## Text

In credit unions formed on or after September 15, 1945, no member shall have more than one vote irrespective of the number of shares held by the member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 539, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 1999.)
