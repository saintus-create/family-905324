# FIN § 4944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 2. Conversion of Federal Depository Corporation Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4944

---

## Text

A converting depository corporation shall file the following with the commissioner:

(a) The plan of conversion.

(b) An officer’s certificate certifying that the plan of conversion has been approved as required by federal law.

(c) An application for approval of the conversion.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 536. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
