# FIN § 18521.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 3. Purpose and Scope of Guaranty Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 18521.6

---

## Text

No industrial loan company which fails to comply with the requirements of subdivision (b) of Section 18521.5 by June 30, 1990, may continue to sell and issue investment certificates beyond that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 787, Sec. 6.)
