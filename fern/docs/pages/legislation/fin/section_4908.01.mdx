# FIN § 4908.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 4. Merger of California State Independent Trust Company into Uninsured Foreign (Other State) Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4908.01

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “merger” means any of the mergers described in Section 4908.02.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 46. Effective September 25, 1996.)
