# FIN § 4995.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.10. HIGHER-PRICED MORTGAGE LOANS

**Citation:** FIN § 4995.4

---

## Text

The provisions of this division shall apply to higher-priced mortgage loans originated on or after July 1, 2010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 629, Sec. 4. (AB 260) Effective January 1, 2010.)
