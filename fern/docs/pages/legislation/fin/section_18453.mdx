# FIN § 18453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Practices and Penalties

**Citation:** FIN § 18453

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly sells investment certificates in violation of any provision of this division or any order or regulation of the commissioner, is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
