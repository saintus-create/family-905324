# FIN § 12212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 12212

---

## Text

No action may be brought on the bond by any person after the expiration of two years from the time when the act or default complained of occurred.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 364.)
