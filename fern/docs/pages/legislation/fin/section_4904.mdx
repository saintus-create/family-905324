# FIN § 4904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 3. Merger Into Federal Depository Corporation, California Federally Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank, or Insured Foreign (Other State) State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4904

---

## Text

Promptly after a merger becomes effective, the surviving depository corporation shall:

(1) Surrender to the commissioner for cancellation the certificates of authority or licenses issued by the commissioner to the disappearing depository corporation; and

(2) File with the commissioner such report of the merger as the commissioner may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 521. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
