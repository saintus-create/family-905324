# FIN § 461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 4. Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 461

---

## Text

Any debt due a licensee on which interest is past due and unpaid for the period of one year shall be charged off, unless the debt is well secured or is in process of collection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
