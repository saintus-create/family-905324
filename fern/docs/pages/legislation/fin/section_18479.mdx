# FIN § 18479

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18479

---

## Text

“Capital contribution” means the amount each member is required to maintain as an investment in the corporation pursuant to Section 18535.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 142, Sec. 10. Effective July 1, 1985.)
