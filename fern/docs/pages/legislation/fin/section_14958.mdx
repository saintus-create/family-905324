# FIN § 14958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 7. Loans > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14958

---

## Text

A credit union may participate in guaranteed loan programs of the federal or state governments, subject to the limitations on loans set forth in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 52, Sec. 10.)
