# FIN § 1365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 10. Restrictions and Prohibited Practices > Article: 2. Loans to Insiders

**Citation:** FIN § 1365

---

## Text

No provision of this article or of Article 3 (commencing with Section 1480) of Chapter 14 shall apply to an advance of money made by a bank pursuant to Section 317 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
