# FIN § 18356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18356

---

## Text

The commissioner may order any industrial loan company to desist from any conduct which the commissioner finds in violation of this division or any rule or order of the commissioner made pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 593. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
