# FIN § 4820.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Other General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 4820.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, depository corporations are divided into the following classes:

(a) Banks.

(b) Savings associations.

(c) Industrial loan companies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 99. Effective October 2, 1995.)
