# FIN § 4883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 1. Merger Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4883

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 1300) of Division 1 of Title 1 of the Corporations Code shall not apply to the shareholders of the surviving depository corporation in a merger.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1411, Sec. 3.)
