# FIN § 4805.08

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.08

---

## Text

“Federal savings association” means a savings association or savings bank organized pursuant to Section 5 of the Home Owners’ Loan Act (12 U.S.C. Sec. 1464).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 97. Effective October 2, 1995.)
