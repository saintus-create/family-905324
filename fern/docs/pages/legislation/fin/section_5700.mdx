# FIN § 5700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 6. Conversions

**Citation:** FIN § 5700

---

## Text

A state association may convert itself into a federal association by following the procedure outlined in Sections 5701 through 5708.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
