# FIN § 17004.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 17004.5

---

## Text

“Internet escrow agent” means any person engaged in the business of receiving escrows for deposit or delivery over the Internet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 441, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
