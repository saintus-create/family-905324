# FIN § 21200.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21200.10

---

## Text

No licensed pawnbroker shall promise any seller of tangible personal property that the seller may repurchase property sold to the pawnbroker.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21200.9 by Stats. 2024, Ch. 185, Sec. 2. (SB 1198) Effective January 1, 2025.)
