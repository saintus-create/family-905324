# FIN § 18565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18565

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “company” means a premium finance agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
