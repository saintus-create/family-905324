# FIN § 5810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 8. Holding Companies and Stock Acquisitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5810

---

## Text

The commissioner shall have the enforcement powers with respect to savings and loan holding companies and their subsidiaries that are provided with respect to associations in Section 8200.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1162, Sec. 9. Effective September 26, 1987.)
