# FIN § 5862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 10. Mutual Holding Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 5862

---

## Text

A reorganization of a mutual association pursuant to this article shall be approved by the board of directors and by the members of the mutual association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 718, Sec. 10.)
