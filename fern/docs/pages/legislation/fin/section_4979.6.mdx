# FIN § 4979.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. COVERED LOANS > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 4979.6

---

## Text

A person who originates a covered loan shall not make a covered loan that finances points and fees in excess of one thousand dollars ($1,000) or 6 percent of the original principal balance, exclusive of points and fees, whichever is greater.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 732, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002. Applicable, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 732, to consumer loans applied for on or after July 1, 2002.)
