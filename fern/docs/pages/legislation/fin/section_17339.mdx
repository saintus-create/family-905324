# FIN § 17339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 17339

---

## Text

Prior to incurring any extraordinary or nonrecurring expense, Fidelity Corporation shall first obtain the written consent of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1458, Sec. 26. Effective September 28, 1988.)
