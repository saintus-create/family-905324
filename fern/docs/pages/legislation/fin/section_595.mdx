# FIN § 595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 6. Enforcement > Article: 5. Suspension or Possession of a License

**Citation:** FIN § 595

---

## Text

The commissioner is deemed to take possession of the entire property and business of a licensee when the commissioner takes possession of the business and property of the head office of the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
