# FIN § 6662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 2. Savings Accounts

**Citation:** FIN § 6662

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit an association or federal association from issuing tenancy in common, community property, and other types of savings accounts authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 958, Sec. 3.)
