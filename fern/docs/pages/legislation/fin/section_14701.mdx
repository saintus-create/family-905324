# FIN § 14701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 7. Reserves

**Citation:** FIN § 14701

---

## Text

Losses incurred by a credit union may be charged to its regular reserve as permitted by rule of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 765, Sec. 3.)
