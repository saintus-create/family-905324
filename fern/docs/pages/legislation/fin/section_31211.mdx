# FIN § 31211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Corporate Matters > Article: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** FIN § 31211

---

## Text

The board of directors of each licensee shall hold a meeting not less frequently than once each calendar quarter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
