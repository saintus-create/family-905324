# FIN § 153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 153

---

## Text

“Certificate of determination” has the meaning set forth in Section 156 of the Corporations Code, subject, however, to the provisions of Section 1104.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
