# FIN § 22019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22019

---

## Text

“Efficiency improvement” means one or more permanent improvements fixed to real property financed through a PACE assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 17. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017.)
