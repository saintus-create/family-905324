# FIN § 32217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 32217

---

## Text

“Life cycle cost analysis” means an analysis of a proposed system in which all costs and benefits are evaluated over the expected life of the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
