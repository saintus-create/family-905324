# FIN § 129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 129

---

## Text

Unless the provision or the context otherwise requires, the definitions set forth in this chapter govern the construction of the Financial Institutions Law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 8. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
