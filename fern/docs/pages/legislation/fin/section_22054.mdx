# FIN § 22054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** FIN § 22054

---

## Text

This division does not apply to bona fide conditional contracts of sale involving the disposition of personal property when these forms of sales agreements are not used for the purpose of evading this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
