# FIN § 1170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 5. Corporate Requirements > Article: 5. Directors

**Citation:** FIN § 1170

---

## Text

Any reference in this article to the time a notice is given or sent shall be construed in accordance with Section 118 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
