# FIN § 209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 209

---

## Text

“Surviving,” when used with respect to a corporation, means a corporation in which one or more other corporations are merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
