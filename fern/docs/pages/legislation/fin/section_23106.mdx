# FIN § 23106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FIN § 23106

---

## Text

This division creates and authorizes an exempt class of persons pursuant to Section 1 of Article XV of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Provisions of this section are operative (no later than Dec. 31, 2004) as prescribed in Section 23104.)
