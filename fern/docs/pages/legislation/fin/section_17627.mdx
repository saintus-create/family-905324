# FIN § 17627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 6. Liquidation and Conservatorship > Article: 1. Possession by Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 17627

---

## Text

The commissioner may issue subpoenas and require the attendance of parties for examination under this article as provided for in this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 772, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
