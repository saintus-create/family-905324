# FIN § 18415.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Possession, Conservatorship, and Liquidation by the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 18415.2

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken from the judgment of the court by the commissioner or by the industrial loan company in the manner provided by law for appeals from the judgment of a superior court to the court of appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 149. Effective September 28, 1998.)
