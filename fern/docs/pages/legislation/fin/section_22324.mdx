# FIN § 22324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 3. Loan Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 22324

---

## Text

Any person who contracts for or negotiates in this state a loan to be made outside the state for the purpose of evading or avoiding the provisions of this division is subject to the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
