# FIN § 18411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 3. Reports and Financial Statements

**Citation:** FIN § 18411

---

## Text

The commissioner may by rule or regulation specify the form and contents of the financial statements, reports, certificates and opinions required to be filed pursuant to this article, and may require that such reports and financial statements be verified in such manner as he may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
