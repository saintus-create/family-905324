# FIN § 17403.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 3. Escrow Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 17403.5

---

## Text

(a) All records required by this chapter may be retained by an Internet escrow agent and provided to the commissioner in electronic format.

(b) All transfers by an Internet escrow agent between trust accounts and interest-bearing accounts, and between escrow accounts, may be made electronically.

(c) A statement of account may be delivered by an Internet escrow agent to a customer by electronic mail or via the Internet, unless otherwise requested by the customer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 437, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
