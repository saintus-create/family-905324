# FIN § 4979.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. COVERED LOANS > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 4979.8

---

## Text

The provisions of this division shall not impose liability on an assignee that is a holder in due course. The provisions of this division shall not apply to persons chartered by Congress to engage in secondary mortgage market transactions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 732, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002. Applicable, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 732, to consumer loans applied for on or after July 1, 2002.)
