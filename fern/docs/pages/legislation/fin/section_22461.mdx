# FIN § 22461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 5. Open-End Loan Programs

**Citation:** FIN § 22461

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 22151, Sections 22154 and 22325, and subdivision (b) of Section 22337 shall not apply to a licensee with respect to advances made through an open-end credit program.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
