# FIN § 31404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Transaction of Business

**Citation:** FIN § 31404

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in subdivision (b) of Section 31403, no licensee shall provide financing assistance or management assistance for use outside this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 867, Sec. 23.)
