# FIN § 5623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 4. Issuance of Stock and Certificates

**Citation:** FIN § 5623

---

## Text

A mutual association may issue mutual capital certificates in accordance with regulations of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
