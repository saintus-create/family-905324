# FIN § 1131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 5. Corporate Requirements > Article: 3. Distributions to Shareholders

**Citation:** FIN § 1131

---

## Text

Section 500 of the Corporations Code does not apply to the making by a bank or by any majority-owned subsidiary of a bank of any distribution to the shareholders of such bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
