# FIN § 12200.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 12200.2

---

## Text

A license as a business agent or special prorater may be issued to an individual.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 367.)
