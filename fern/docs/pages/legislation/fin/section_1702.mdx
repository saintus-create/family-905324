# FIN § 1702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 19. Foreign (Other State) Banks > Article: 3. Facilities of Insured Foreign (Other State) Banks

**Citation:** FIN § 1702

---

## Text

Not less than 30 days before an insured foreign (other state) bank establishes a facility, the bank shall file with the commissioner a report and the appointment required pursuant to Section 1703.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 41. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
