# FIN § 32320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Corporate Matters > Article: 3. Board of Directors

**Citation:** FIN § 32320

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 32325 and 32352.5, the board of directors of the corporation shall consist of six members, two official and four public directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 25, Sec. 4. Effective March 17, 2004.)
