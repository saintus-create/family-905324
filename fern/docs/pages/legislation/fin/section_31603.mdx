# FIN § 31603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger and Purchase or Sale of Business

**Citation:** FIN § 31603

---

## Text

No licensee shall sell all or substantially all of its business to any other person unless such other person is a licensee and such sale shall have first been approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 675. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
