# FIN § 21207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21207

---

## Text

No pawnbroker shall receive anything in pledge from any person who is a minor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1418, Sec. 4.)
