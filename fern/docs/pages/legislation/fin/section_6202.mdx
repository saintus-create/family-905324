# FIN § 6202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 5. Indemnity Bonds

**Citation:** FIN § 6202

---

## Text

No indemnity bond coverage is required of any agent that is a financial institution insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1118, Sec. 30.)
