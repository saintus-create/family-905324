# FIN § 4879.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 5. Other Sales of Partial Business Units

**Citation:** FIN § 4879.03

---

## Text

A seller and purchaser shall make an agreement of sale, providing:

(a) That the seller shall sell to the purchaser, and the purchaser shall purchase from the seller, a partial business unit, describing it.

(b) Other provisions as may be appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 155. Effective October 2, 1995.)
