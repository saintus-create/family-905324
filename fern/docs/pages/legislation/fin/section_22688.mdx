# FIN § 22688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3.5. Program Administrators

**Citation:** FIN § 22688

---

## Text

A program administrator shall be subject to all provisions of the California Financial Information Privacy Act (Division 1.4 (commencing with Section 4050)) that are applicable to financial institutions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 798, Sec. 8. (SB 1087) Effective January 1, 2019.)
