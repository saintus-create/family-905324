# FIN § 1578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 16. Trust Companies > Article: 3. Deposits with State Treasurer

**Citation:** FIN § 1578

---

## Text

The commissioner may, pursuant to Section 329, levy a civil penalty against any trust company that fails to comply with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
