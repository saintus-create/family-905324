# FIN § 18408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 3. Reports and Financial Statements

**Citation:** FIN § 18408

---

## Text

An industrial loan company shall make other special reports to the commissioner as the commissioner may from time to time require.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
