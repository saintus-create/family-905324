# FIN § 31151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 31151

---

## Text

No person other than a California corporation may apply for or be issued a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
