# FIN § 1760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 20. Foreign (Other Nation) Banks > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1760

---

## Text

(a) No foreign (other nation) bank which is licensed to maintain an agency or branch office shall concurrently maintain a federal agency or federal branch in this state.

(b) No foreign (other nation) bank which maintains a federal agency or federal branch in this state shall concurrently be licensed to maintain an agency or branch office in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
