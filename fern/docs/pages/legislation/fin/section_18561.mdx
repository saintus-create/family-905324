# FIN § 18561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18561

---

## Text

Such terms relating to insurance as are used in this chapter shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Insurance Code and in the practices of the insurance business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
