# FIN § 1286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 8. Bank Holding Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 1286

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the commissioner to require reports from a national bank or to examine a national bank contrary to federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
