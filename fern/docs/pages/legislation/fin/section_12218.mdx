# FIN § 12218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 12218

---

## Text

The license shall be kept conspicuously posted in the principal place of business of the licensee.
