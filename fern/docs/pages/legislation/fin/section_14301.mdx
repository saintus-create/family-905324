# FIN § 14301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 14301

---

## Text

Any requirement in this chapter for notice or hearing before the commissioner issues an order may be waived by the person to whom the order is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 734, Sec. 20. Effective September 20, 2002.)
