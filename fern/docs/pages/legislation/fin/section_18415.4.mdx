# FIN § 18415.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Possession, Conservatorship, and Liquidation by the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 18415.4

---

## Text

The commissioner may also, upon the request of the board of directors of a company, take possession of the property and business of a company and as conservator, take such action as the commissioner deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 140, Sec. 1. Effective July 1, 1985.)
