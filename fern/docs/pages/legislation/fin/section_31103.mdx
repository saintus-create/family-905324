# FIN § 31103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31103

---

## Text

Every final order, decision, license, or other official act of the commissioner under this division is subject to judicial review in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 631. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
