# FIN § 1130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 5. Corporate Requirements > Article: 3. Distributions to Shareholders

**Citation:** FIN § 1130

---

## Text

This article does not apply to any distribution made to the shareholders of a bank in any proceeding to wind up and dissolve or to liquidate such bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
