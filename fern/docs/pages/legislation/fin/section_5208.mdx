# FIN § 5208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 5208

---

## Text

This division, being a comprehensive coverage of its subject matter, shall not be deemed to be impliedly repealed, in whole or in part, by subsequent legislation not specifically repealing it, if that construction can be avoided.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
