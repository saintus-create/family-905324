# FIN § 18520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 3. Purpose and Scope of Guaranty Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 18520

---

## Text

It shall be the purpose of Guaranty Corporation to guarantee full payment of guaranteed accounts of members, exclusive of special members, up to fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) for each account, subject to the express limitations provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 142, Sec. 27. Effective July 1, 1985.)
