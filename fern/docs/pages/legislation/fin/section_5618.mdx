# FIN § 5618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 3. Postincorporation Organization of Associations

**Citation:** FIN § 5618

---

## Text

The commissioner may amend, alter, or revoke any permit issued by the commissioner, or temporarily suspend the rights of the association under such permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 287, Sec. 10. Effective July 6, 1984.)
