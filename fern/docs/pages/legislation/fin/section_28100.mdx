# FIN § 28100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. STUDENT LOAN SERVICING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** FIN § 28100

## Text

This division may be known and cited as the “Student Loan Servicing Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 824, Sec. 2. (AB 2251) Effective January 1, 2017. Operative July 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 28182.)
