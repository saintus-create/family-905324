# FIN § 5111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5111

---

## Text

“Mutual capital certificate” means a certificate evidencing an investment in a mutual association and issued pursuant to provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
