# FIN § 17304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 17304

---

## Text

“Loss,” within the meaning of this chapter, means the loss of trust obligations held by a member within the State of California as a result of the fraudulent or dishonest abstraction, misappropriation, or embezzlement of trust obligations by an officer, director, trustee, stockholder, manager, or employee of a member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 662, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
