# FIN § 18056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Advertising

**Citation:** FIN § 18056

---

## Text

An industrial loan company shall not use any advertising which refers to the supervision of such company by the state or any department or official thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
