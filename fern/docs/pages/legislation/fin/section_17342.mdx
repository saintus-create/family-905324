# FIN § 17342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 17342

---

## Text

Memberships issued by Fidelity Corporation shall be nontransferable and shall be exempt from the provisions of the Corporate Securities Law of 1968.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1560, Sec. 30. Effective October 2, 1985.)
