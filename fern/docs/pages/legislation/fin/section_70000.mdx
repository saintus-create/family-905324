# FIN § 70000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA FINANCIAL LITERACY FUND

**Citation:** FIN § 70000

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, “fund” means the California Financial Literacy Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 612, Sec. 1. (AB 597) Effective January 1, 2012.)
