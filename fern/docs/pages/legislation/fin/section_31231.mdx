# FIN § 31231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Corporate Matters > Article: 4. Distributions to Shareholders

**Citation:** FIN § 31231

---

## Text

No licensee shall, except with the prior approval of the commissioner, make, or obligate itself to make, any distribution to its shareholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 652. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
