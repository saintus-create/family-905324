# FIN § 31301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Offices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 31301

---

## Text

No licensee shall maintain an office at any place outside this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
