# FIN § 18682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 10. Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies: Agency Activities > Article: 3. California Branch Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 18682

---

## Text

No foreign (other state) industrial loan company may establish or maintain a California branch office unless it is qualified to transact intrastate business in this state under Chapter 21 (commencing with Section 2100) of Division 1 of Title 1 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 479, Sec. 7. Effective October 2, 1995.)
