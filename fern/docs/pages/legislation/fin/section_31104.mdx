# FIN § 31104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31104

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this division:

(a) The burden of proving that an application should be approved is upon the applicant.

(b) The burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming such exemption or such exception from a definition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
