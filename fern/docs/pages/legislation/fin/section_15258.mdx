# FIN § 15258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger, Dissolution and Conversion > Article: 2. Dissolution

**Citation:** FIN § 15258

---

## Text

The certificate of dissolution shall be filed in the office of the Secretary of State and copies, certified by him, shall be filed in the office of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
