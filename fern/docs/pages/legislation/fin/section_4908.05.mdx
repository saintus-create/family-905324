# FIN § 4908.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 4. Merger of California State Independent Trust Company into Uninsured Foreign (Other State) Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4908.05

---

## Text

A merger shall not become effective unless it has been approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 521.4. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
