# FIN § 14852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14852

---

## Text

Every credit union may charge a reasonable fee for the transfer of its shares.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
