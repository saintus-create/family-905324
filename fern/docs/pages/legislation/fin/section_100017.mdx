# FIN § 100017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. DEBT COLLECTION LICENSING ACT > Chapter: 2. Licensing > Article: 3. Nationwide Multistate Licensing System and Registry

**Citation:** FIN § 100017

---

## Text

The commissioner shall report regularly violations of this division, as well as enforcement actions and other relevant information, to the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System & Registry, to the extent that the information is a public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. (SB 908) Effective January 1, 2021. Operative January 1, 2022, pursuant to Sec. 100000.5.)
