# FIN § 18014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18014

---

## Text

“Obligation” as used in Sections 18265, 18271, 18272, and 18343 includes lease obligations as authorized by Section 18310.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 911.)
