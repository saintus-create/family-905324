# FIN § 18590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 2. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18590

---

## Text

Upon payment of a loan in full the company upon request shall return the premium finance agreement marked “Paid” to the insured.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
