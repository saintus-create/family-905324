# FIN § 6510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers of Associations > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** FIN § 6510

---

## Text

An association may sell any loan, including a participating interest in a loan, at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
