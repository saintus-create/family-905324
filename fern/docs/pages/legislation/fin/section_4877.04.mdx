# FIN § 4877.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 4. Certain Sales of Partial Business Units

**Citation:** FIN § 4877.04

---

## Text

A sale is subject to the provisions of Sections 4879.03 to 4879.06, inclusive, and Section 4879.08 as if the sale were a sale of the type defined in Section 4879.01.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 34. Effective September 25, 1996.)
