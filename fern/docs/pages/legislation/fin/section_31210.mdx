# FIN § 31210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Corporate Matters > Article: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** FIN § 31210

---

## Text

The board of directors of each licensee shall consist of not less than five directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 867, Sec. 14.)
