# FIN § 1203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 6. Securities

**Citation:** FIN § 1203

---

## Text

An application for a permit shall be in such form and contain such information as the commissioner may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
