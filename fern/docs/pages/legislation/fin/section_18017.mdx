# FIN § 18017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18017

---

## Text

“Assets,” as used in this division, means all assets excluding intangibles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
