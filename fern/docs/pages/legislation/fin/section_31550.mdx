# FIN § 31550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 8. Acquisition of Control

**Citation:** FIN § 31550

---

## Text

No person shall, except with the prior approval of the commissioner, acquire control of a licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 669. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
