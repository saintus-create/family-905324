# FIN § 18318.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 4. Investment Certificates

**Citation:** FIN § 18318.5

---

## Text

An investment or thrift certificate that is a multiple-party account as defined in Section 5132 of the Probate Code is governed by Part 2 (commencing with Section 5100) of Division 5 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1991, pursuant to Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 46.)
