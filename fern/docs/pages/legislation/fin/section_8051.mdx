# FIN § 8051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 7. Supervision > Article: 3. General Supervisory Powers and Duties of Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 8051

---

## Text

Each decision, order, or instruction shall be in writing signed by the commissioner or a deputy commissioner and shall be sent by registered or first-class mail, addressed to the home office of the appropriate association or person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 361, Sec. 10.)
