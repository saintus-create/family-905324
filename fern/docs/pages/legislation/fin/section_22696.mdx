# FIN § 22696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3.5. Program Administrators

**Citation:** FIN § 22696

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 22684, 22685, 22686, 22687, 22688, and 22695, this chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2019.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 71. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Note: Operative date provision applies, with specified exceptions, to Chapter 3.5, comprising Sections 22680 to 22697.)
