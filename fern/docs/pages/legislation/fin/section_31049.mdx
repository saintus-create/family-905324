# FIN § 31049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31049

---

## Text

“Principal shareholder”, when used with respect to a corporation, means any person who owns, directly or indirectly, of record or beneficially, securities representing 10 percent or more of the voting power of such corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31047 by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1570, Sec. 5.)
