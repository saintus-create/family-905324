# FIN § 31106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31106

---

## Text

No provision of this division shall be construed to require by implication that the commissioner make written findings on any matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 633. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
