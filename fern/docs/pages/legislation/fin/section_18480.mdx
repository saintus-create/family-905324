# FIN § 18480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18480

---

## Text

“Member account” means the total of all amounts credited to a member for paid-in capital contributions, and other credits, net of any charges to that participating member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 142, Sec. 11. Effective July 1, 1985.)
