# FIN § 18427.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Securities Transactions

**Citation:** FIN § 18427.2

---

## Text

An application for a permit shall be in such form and contain such information as the commissioner may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 602. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
