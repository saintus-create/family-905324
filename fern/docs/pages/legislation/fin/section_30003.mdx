# FIN § 30003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 30003

---

## Text

“Person” means, in addition to the singular, persons, group of persons, cooperative, association, company, firm, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other legal entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 118. Effective January 1, 1995.)
