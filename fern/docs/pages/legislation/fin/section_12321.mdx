# FIN § 12321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 4. Licensee Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 12321

---

## Text

Unless paid by check or money order a prorater shall deliver a receipt to a debtor for each payment within five (5) days after receipt of a payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 498.)
