# FIN § 1540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 15. Industrial Banks > Article: 2. Industrial Bank Business

**Citation:** FIN § 1540

---

## Text

Each industrial bank shall be an insured bank at all times while it is engaged in the industrial banking business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
