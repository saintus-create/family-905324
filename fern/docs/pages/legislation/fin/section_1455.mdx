# FIN § 1455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 13. Withdrawals and Collections > Article: 2. Certified Checks

**Citation:** FIN § 1455

---

## Text

Whenever a bank certifies a check, the amount thereof shall be immediately charged against the account of the drawer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
