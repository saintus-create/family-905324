# FIN § 10006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 10. Foreign (National) Savings Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 10006

---

## Text

Except as provided by regulation, this division applies to a foreign savings association or its holding company as if business conducted in this state were that of a California savings association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1162, Sec. 24. Effective September 26, 1987. Section operative January 1, 1991, pursuant to Section 10009.)
