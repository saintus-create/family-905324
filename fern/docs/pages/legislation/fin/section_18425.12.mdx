# FIN § 18425.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Possession, Conservatorship, and Liquidation by the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 18425.12

---

## Text

If it appears improbable that anything can be realized from any property of the company and that the cost of maintaining, preserving, or protecting the property would probably be lost, the court may direct the liquidator to abandon the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 140, Sec. 1. Effective July 1, 1985.)
