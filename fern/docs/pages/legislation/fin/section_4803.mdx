# FIN § 4803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title, Construction, and Severability

**Citation:** FIN § 4803

---

## Text

If and to the extent that any provision of this division is preempted by federal law, the provision does not apply and shall not be enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 95.1. Effective October 2, 1995.)
