# FIN § 31109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31109

---

## Text

The commissioner may honor applications from interested persons for interpretive opinions regarding any provision of this division or of any regulation or order issued under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 636. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
