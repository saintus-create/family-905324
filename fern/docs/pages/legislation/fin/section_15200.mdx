# FIN § 15200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger, Dissolution and Conversion > Article: 1. Merger

**Citation:** FIN § 15200

---

## Text

Any credit union may, with the approval of the commissioner, merge with another credit union or with a central credit union.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 452, Sec. 5.)
