# FIN § 1522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FIN § 1522

---

## Text

For the purposes of any law authorizing or providing for the deposit of funds with a savings bank, a commercial bank accepting savings deposits shall be deemed a savings bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
