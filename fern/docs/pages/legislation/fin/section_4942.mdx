# FIN § 4942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 2. Conversion of Federal Depository Corporation Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4942

---

## Text

A converting depository corporation shall adopt a plan of conversion pursuant to the provisions of federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1411, Sec. 3.)
