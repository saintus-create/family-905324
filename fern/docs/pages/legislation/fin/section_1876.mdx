# FIN § 1876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 21. International and Foreign Banking and Financing > Article: 1. International and Foreign Banking and Financing Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 1876

---

## Text

Every corporation shall hold a meeting of its stockholders annually upon a date fixed in its bylaws at its main office, or if its main office is to be located outside of this state, at its branch or other office in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
