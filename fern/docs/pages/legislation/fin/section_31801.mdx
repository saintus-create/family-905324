# FIN § 31801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 12. Crimes and Criminal Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 31801

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person having custody of any of the books, accounts, or other records of a licensee willfully to refuse to allow the commissioner, upon request, to inspect or make copies of any of such books, accounts, or other records.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 695. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
