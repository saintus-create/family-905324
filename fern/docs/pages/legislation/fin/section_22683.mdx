# FIN § 22683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3.5. Program Administrators

**Citation:** FIN § 22683

---

## Text

A program administrator shall develop and implement policies and procedures for responding to questions and addressing complaints as soon as reasonably practicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 71. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2019, pursuant to Section 22696.)
