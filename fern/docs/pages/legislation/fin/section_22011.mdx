# FIN § 22011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22011

---

## Text

A “regulatory ceiling provision” is a statement in a section or subdivision that specifies an original bona fide principal loan amount at or above which that section or subdivision does not apply to a loan.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
