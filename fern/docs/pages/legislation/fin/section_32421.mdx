# FIN § 32421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 3. Corporate Finances > Article: 3. Government Guaranteed Securities Trust Fund

**Citation:** FIN § 32421

---

## Text

The finance officer shall place into the trust fund all securities held by the corporation which carry a guarantee of repayment issued by the Small Business Administration or guarantee of repayment issued by any other governmental authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
