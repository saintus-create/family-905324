# FIN § 23064.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 3. Civil Damages

**Citation:** FIN § 23064.5

---

## Text

The rights, remedies, and penalties established by this division are cumulative to the rights, remedies, or penalties established under other laws. It is not necessary to exhaust administrative remedies in order to pursue the civil remedies provided for in this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
