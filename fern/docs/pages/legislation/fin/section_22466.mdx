# FIN § 22466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 5. Open-End Loan Programs

**Citation:** FIN § 22466

---

## Text

An open-end loan is in compliance with Section 22330 if it is an open-end loan of a bona fide principal amount of five thousand dollars ($5,000) or more as determined in accordance with Section 22467.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
