# FIN § 801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 10. Legal Investments for Nonbank Licensees

**Citation:** FIN § 801

---

## Text

Any securities or other assets that are described in Sections 803 to 819, inclusive, are legal investments for savings banks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
