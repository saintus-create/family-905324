# FIN § 18011.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18011.1

---

## Text

“Affiliate” means an affiliated company or a person who, directly or indirectly, controls over 10 percent of the voting stock of an industrial loan company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 467, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
