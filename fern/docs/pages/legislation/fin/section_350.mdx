# FIN § 350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 3. Deputies and Employees

**Citation:** FIN § 350

---

## Text

The chief deputy shall be appointed by the Governor and hold office at the pleasure of the Governor. The annual salary of the chief deputy shall be fixed by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 353, Sec. 50. (SB 820) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 129 of Ch. 353.)
