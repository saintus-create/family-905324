# FIN § 6456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 6. Records

**Citation:** FIN § 6456

---

## Text

Each mutual association shall maintain membership records which shall show the name and address of the member and the date of membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 983, Sec. 4. Effective September 26, 1985.)
