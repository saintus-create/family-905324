# FIN § 193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 193

---

## Text

“Resulting,” when used with respect to a corporation, means:

(a) In the case of a consolidation, the corporation into which the constituent corporations are consolidated.

(b) In the case of a conversion, the corporation into which the converting corporation is converted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
