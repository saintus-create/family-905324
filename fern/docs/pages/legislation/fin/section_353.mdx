# FIN § 353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 3. Deputies and Employees

**Citation:** FIN § 353

---

## Text

Before entering upon the duties of his or her office each deputy and examiner shall take and subscribe to the constitutional oath of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 353, Sec. 53. (SB 820) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 129 of Ch. 353.)
