# FIN § 22463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 5. Open-End Loan Programs

**Citation:** FIN § 22463

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the authority of the commissioner to disapprove advertising with respect to open-end loans pursuant to Section 22165.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 708, Sec. 13. (AB 539) Effective January 1, 2020.)
