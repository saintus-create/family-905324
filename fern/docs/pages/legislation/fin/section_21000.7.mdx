# FIN § 21000.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 21000.7

---

## Text

As used in this division “receiving goods as security for a loan” does not include a good faith purchase of goods.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1037.)
