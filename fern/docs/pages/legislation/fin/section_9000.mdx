# FIN § 9000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 9. Fees

**Citation:** FIN § 9000

---

## Text

Associations shall pay all fees required by this division to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 566. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
