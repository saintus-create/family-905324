# FIN § 31881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 12. Crimes and Criminal Penalties > Article: 3. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FIN § 31881

---

## Text

Nothing in this division limits the power of the state to punish any person for any act which constitutes a crime under any statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
