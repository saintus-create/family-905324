# FIN § 51013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.5. EXCHANGE FACILITATORS

**Citation:** FIN § 51013

---

## Text

A person who violates this division is subject to civil suit in a court of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 708, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
