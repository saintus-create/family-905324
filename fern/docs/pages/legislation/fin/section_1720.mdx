# FIN § 1720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 19. Foreign (Other State) Banks > Article: 4. Facilities of Uninsured Foreign (Other State) Banks

**Citation:** FIN § 1720

---

## Text

Each uninsured foreign (other state) bank that is licensed to maintain a facility shall post its license in a conspicuous place at the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
