# FIN § 31034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31034

---

## Text

“Business firm” means any person which transacts business on a regular and continual basis, with respect to the transacting of such business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
