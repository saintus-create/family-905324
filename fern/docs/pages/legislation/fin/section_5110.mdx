# FIN § 5110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5110

---

## Text

“Member” means a person holding a savings account of a mutual association. A joint and survivorship or other multiple owner constitutes a single membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
