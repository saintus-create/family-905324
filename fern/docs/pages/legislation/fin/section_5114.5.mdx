# FIN § 5114.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5114.5

---

## Text

“Residential real estate” means any improved real property that is used or intended to be used as a residence and contains not more than four dwelling units.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 983, Sec. 1.5. Effective September 26, 1985.)
