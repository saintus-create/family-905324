# FIN § 5206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 5206

---

## Text

Whenever the terms “board of directors,” “directors,” or “director” are used in any provision of law, such terms shall mean, with respect to savings banks organized under this division, “board of trustees,” “trustees,” or “trustee.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 868, Sec. 3.5.)
