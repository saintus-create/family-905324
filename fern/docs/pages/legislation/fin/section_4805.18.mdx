# FIN § 4805.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.18

---

## Text

“Uninsured,” when used with respect to a deposit, means a deposit or any part of a deposit that is not insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation under the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. Sec. 1811 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 97. Effective October 2, 1995.)
