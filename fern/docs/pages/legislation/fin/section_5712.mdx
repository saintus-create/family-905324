# FIN § 5712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 6. Conversions

**Citation:** FIN § 5712

---

## Text

The verified copies of the minutes of the meeting, when filed as required by Section 5711, are presumptive evidence of the holding and action of the meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
