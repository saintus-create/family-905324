# FIN § 14384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Credit Union Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FIN § 14384

---

## Text

The commissioner may by order or regulation prescribe rules governing the Credit Union Advisory Committee and its members, including such matters as meetings, quorum, and actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 577. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
