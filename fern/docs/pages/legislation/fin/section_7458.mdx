# FIN § 7458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 6. Investment Operations > Article: 5. Investment in Loans

**Citation:** FIN § 7458

---

## Text

An association may make overdraft loans specifically related to transaction accounts, subject to regulations issued by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 868, Sec. 11.3.)
