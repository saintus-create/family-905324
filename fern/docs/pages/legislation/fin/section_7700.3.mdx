# FIN § 7700.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 6. Investment Operations > Article: 10. Finance Leasing

**Citation:** FIN § 7700.3

---

## Text

An association may engage in leasing activities that are the functional equivalent of lending, subject to the limitations of Sections 7701 to 7704, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 983, Sec. 13. Effective September 26, 1985.)
