# FIN § 17321.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 3. Membership Fee and Assessments

**Citation:** FIN § 17321.2

---

## Text

Fidelity Corporation shall be entitled to collect late fees as specified in Fidelity Corporation’s bylaws for any late payment of assessments under Section 17320, 17321, or 17321.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1458, Sec. 16. Effective September 28, 1988.)
