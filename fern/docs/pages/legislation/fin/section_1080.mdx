# FIN § 1080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 4. Bank Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 1080

---

## Text

If a bank violates any provision of this chapter or fails to comply with any order, the commissioner may levy a penalty against the bank pursuant to Section 329.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 34. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
