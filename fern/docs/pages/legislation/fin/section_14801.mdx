# FIN § 14801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 5. Members

**Citation:** FIN § 14801

---

## Text

Every credit union may expel members as provided in Section 14456 of this division and Section 7341 of the Corporations Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 209, Sec. 4.)
