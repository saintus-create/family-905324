# FIN § 31602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger and Purchase or Sale of Business

**Citation:** FIN § 31602

---

## Text

No licensee shall purchase all or substantially all of the business of any other person unless such purchase shall have first been approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 674. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
