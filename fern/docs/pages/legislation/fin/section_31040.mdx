# FIN § 31040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31040

---

## Text

“Corporate name” means the name of a corporation as set forth in the articles of incorporation of such corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
