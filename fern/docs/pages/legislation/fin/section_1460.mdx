# FIN § 1460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1460

---

## Text

A commercial bank shall not rediscount, borrow money, or hypothecate its assets as security for money borrowed except to the extent and upon the conditions set forth in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
