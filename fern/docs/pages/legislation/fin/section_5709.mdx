# FIN § 5709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 6. Conversions

**Citation:** FIN § 5709

---

## Text

Any federal association may convert itself into an association by following the procedure set forth in Sections 5710 through 5718.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 287, Sec. 12.5. Effective July 6, 1984.)
