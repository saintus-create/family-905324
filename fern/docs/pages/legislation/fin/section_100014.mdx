# FIN § 100014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. DEBT COLLECTION LICENSING ACT > Chapter: 2. Licensing > Article: 2. Application for Licensure

**Citation:** FIN § 100014

---

## Text

A license shall remain effective until the license is either suspended or revoked by the commissioner or surrendered by the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. (SB 908) Effective January 1, 2021. Operative January 1, 2022, pursuant to Sec. 100000.5.)
