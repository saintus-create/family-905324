# FIN § 12300.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 4. Licensee Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 12300.1

---

## Text

Licensees shall observe reasonable precautions against theft or alteration of checks, drafts or money orders, and against burglary or holdup.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1817.)
