# FIN § 4845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 2. Sale of Whole Business Unit to California State Depository Corporation or California State-Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank

**Citation:** FIN § 4845

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “sale” means any of the sales described in Section 4846.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 487. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
