# FIN § 31154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 31154

---

## Text

Whenever any application for a license has been approved and all conditions precedent to the issuance of such license have been fulfilled, the commissioner shall issue a license to the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 648. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
