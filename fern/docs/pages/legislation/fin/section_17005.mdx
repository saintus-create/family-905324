# FIN § 17005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 17005

---

## Text

“Licensee” means any person holding a valid, unrevoked license as an escrow agent.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 364.)
