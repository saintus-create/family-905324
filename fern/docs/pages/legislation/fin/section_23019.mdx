# FIN § 23019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Administration and Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 23019

---

## Text

Every licensee shall post a complete, detailed, and unambiguous schedule of fees. The information required by this section shall be clear, legible, and in letters not less than one-half inch in height. The information shall be posted in a conspicuous location in the unobstructed view of the public within the licensee’s location.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
