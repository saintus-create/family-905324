# FIN § 32204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 32204

---

## Text

“State Controller” means the State Controller or any person to whom the State Controller delegates the authority to act for him or her in the particular matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
