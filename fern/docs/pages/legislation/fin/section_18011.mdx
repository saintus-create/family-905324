# FIN § 18011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18011

---

## Text

“Affiliated company”, as used in this division, is a company under substantially the same management or control, directly or indirectly, as the industrial loan company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
