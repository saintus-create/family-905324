# FIN § 22015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22015

---

## Text

“PACE assessment” means a voluntary contractual assessment, voluntary special tax, or special tax, as described in subdivisions (a), (b), and (c) of Section 26054 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 12. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017.)
