# FIN § 17006.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 17006.5

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 778.)
