# FIN § 18694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 10. Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies: Agency Activities > Article: 4. California Industrial Loan Company as Principal

**Citation:** FIN § 18694

---

## Text

No California industrial loan company may have an insured depository institution conduct as its agent any activity that the California industrial loan company is prohibited from conducting itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 63. Effective September 25, 1996.)
