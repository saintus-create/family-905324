# FIN § 31102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31102

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner issues an order or license under this division, he or she may impose such conditions as are in his or her opinion necessary to carry out the provisions and purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 630. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
