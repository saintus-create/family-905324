# FIN § 18680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 10. Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies: Agency Activities > Article: 3. California Branch Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 18680

---

## Text

No foreign (other state) industrial loan company may transact core business in this state except at a branch office established in accordance with federal law and the law of the domicile of the industrial loan company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 479, Sec. 7. Effective October 2, 1995.)
