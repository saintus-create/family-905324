# FIN § 18010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18010

---

## Text

“Principal balance”, and “face amount” as used in this division, means the unpaid balance of a loan or other obligation.
