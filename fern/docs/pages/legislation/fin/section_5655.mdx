# FIN § 5655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 5. Association Name, Office

**Citation:** FIN § 5655

---

## Text

No association shall advertise or hold itself out to the public as a bank. This subdivision shall not be construed to prohibit an association which is a savings bank from advertising or holding itself out to the public as a “savings bank.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 287, Sec. 12. Effective July 6, 1984.)
