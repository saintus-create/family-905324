# FIN § 4058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.4. CALIFORNIA FINANCIAL INFORMATION PRIVACY ACT

**Citation:** FIN § 4058

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed as altering or annulling the authority of any department or agency of the state to regulate any financial institution subject to its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 241, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004. Section operative July 1, 2004, pursuant to Section 4060.)
