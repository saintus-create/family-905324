# FIN § 14601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 5. Credit Committee and Loan Officers

**Citation:** FIN § 14601

---

## Text

No member of the credit committee or the credit manager or any loan officer shall serve as a member of the supervisory committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 539, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1999.)
