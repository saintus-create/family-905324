# FIN § 18652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 9. Interstate Acquisitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18652

---

## Text

The fee for filing with the commissioner an application for an approval under this chapter is four hundred dollars ($400).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 479, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1995.)
