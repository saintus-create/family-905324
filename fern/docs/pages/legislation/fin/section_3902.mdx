# FIN § 3902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.25. DIGITAL FINANCIAL ASSETS BUSINESSES > Chapter: 9. Digital Financial Asset Transaction Kiosks

**Citation:** FIN § 3902

---

## Text

An operator shall not accept or dispense more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) in a day from or to a customer via a digital financial asset transaction kiosk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 871, Sec. 1. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2024.)
