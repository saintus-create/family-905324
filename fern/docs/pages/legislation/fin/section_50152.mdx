# FIN § 50152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 3.6. Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry

**Citation:** FIN § 50152

---

## Text

The commissioner shall report regularly violations of this division, as well as enforcement actions and other relevant information, to the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry, to the extent that information is public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 160, Sec. 61. (SB 36) Effective October 11, 2009.)
