# FIN § 16503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 12. Foreign (Other Nation) Credit Unions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 16503

---

## Text

No foreign (other nation) credit union may establish a branch office unless its deposit or share accounts are insured by the National Credit Union Administration or other insurer that is not unsatisfactory to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 612, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
