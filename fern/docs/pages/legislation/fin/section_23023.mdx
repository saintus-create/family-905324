# FIN § 23023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Administration and Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 23023

---

## Text

No licensee shall transact the business licensed or make any transaction provided for by this division under any other name or at any other place of business than that named in the license except pursuant to a currently effective written order of the commissioner authorizing the other name or other place of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
