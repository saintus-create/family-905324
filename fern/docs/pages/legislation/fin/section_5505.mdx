# FIN § 5505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 2. Petition for Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** FIN § 5505

---

## Text

The articles of incorporation of a proposed association shall not be filed in the office of the Secretary of State unless a certificate of approval of the articles of incorporation issued by the commissioner is attached thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
