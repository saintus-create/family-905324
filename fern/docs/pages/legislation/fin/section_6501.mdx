# FIN § 6501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers of Associations > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** FIN § 6501

---

## Text

An association may have perpetual existence, adopt and use a corporate seal which may be affixed by imprint, facsimile, or otherwise, and adopt and amend bylaws as provided in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
