# FIN § 50305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 50305

---

## Text

Upon written request from the commissioner, a licensee shall forward authorization to release workpapers of the licensee’s independent auditor to the commissioner within two business days of receiving the request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 994, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995. Section operative January 1, 1996, pursuant to Section 50601 (later repealed).)
