# FIN § 31055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31055

---

## Text

“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation, or other person to whom the commissioner delegates the authority to act for the commissioner in the particular matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 157. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
