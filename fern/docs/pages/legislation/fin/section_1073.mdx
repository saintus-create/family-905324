# FIN § 1073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 4. Bank Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 1073

---

## Text

A bank, with the approval of its board, may establish and maintain one or more offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
