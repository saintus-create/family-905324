# FIN § 3213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.25. DIGITAL FINANCIAL ASSETS BUSINESSES > Chapter: 2. Licensure

**Citation:** FIN § 3213

---

## Text

A license under this division is not transferable or assignable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 792, Sec. 1. (AB 39) Effective January 1, 2024.)
