# FIN § 15300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger, Dissolution and Conversion > Article: 3. Conversion Into Federal Credit Unions

**Citation:** FIN § 15300

---

## Text

A credit union may convert itself into a federal credit union by following the procedure contained in this article.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
