# FIN § 32400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 3. Corporate Finances > Article: 1. Corporate Accounts

**Citation:** FIN § 32400

---

## Text

The financial accounts and records of the corporation shall be kept by the finance officer according to policies and rules which the board shall adopt.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
