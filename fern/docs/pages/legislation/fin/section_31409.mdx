# FIN § 31409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Transaction of Business

**Citation:** FIN § 31409

---

## Text

No licensee shall, except with the prior approval of the commissioner, provide a lien or security interest in any of its property for the purpose of securing an obligation of, or an obligation incurred for the benefit of, any other person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 660. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
