# FIN § 323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 2. Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation

**Citation:** FIN § 323

---

## Text

The commissioner shall be a citizen of the United States and a resident of the state for at least three years prior to his or her appointment. The commissioner shall be chosen solely for his or her qualifications and fitness to perform the duties of his or her office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
