# FIN § 31509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 7. Records, Reports, and Examinations

**Citation:** FIN § 31509

---

## Text

The commissioner may publish any report filed with him or her under this division or under any regulation or order issued under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 668. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
