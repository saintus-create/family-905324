# FIN § 31500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 7. Records, Reports, and Examinations

**Citation:** FIN § 31500

---

## Text

Each licensee shall adopt as its fiscal year the period from July 1st to and including the following June 30th.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
