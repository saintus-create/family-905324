# FIN § 31051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31051

---

## Text

To “provide management assistance” to a person includes:

(a) To provide management or technical advice to such person; and

(b) To provide management or technical services to such person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31049 by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1570, Sec. 7.)
