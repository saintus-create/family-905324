# FIN § 6450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 6. Records

**Citation:** FIN § 6450

---

## Text

Each association shall keep correct and complete books and records of accounts, as specified in Section 1500 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
