# FIN § 1402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 12. Deposits > Article: 1. Depositors

**Citation:** FIN § 1402

---

## Text

A bank account that is a multiple-party account as defined in Section 5132 of the Probate Code is governed by Part 2 (commencing with Section 5100) of Division 5 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
