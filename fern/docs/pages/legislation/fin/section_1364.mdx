# FIN § 1364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 10. Restrictions and Prohibited Practices > Article: 2. Loans to Insiders

**Citation:** FIN § 1364

---

## Text

In making any extension of credit that is subject to this article, a bank shall comply with all other applicable provisions of this division relating to extensions of credit by banks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
