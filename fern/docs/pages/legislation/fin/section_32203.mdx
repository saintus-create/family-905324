# FIN § 32203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 32203

---

## Text

“Small business” means a business defined as an eligible small business as set forth in Section 121.3-10 of Part 121, Chapter 1, Title 13 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
