# FIN § 18502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 2. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18502

---

## Text

Memberships issued by Guaranty Corporation shall be nontransferable and shall be exempt from the provisions of the Corporate Securities Law of 1968.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
