# FIN § 17209.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2. License and Bond

**Citation:** FIN § 17209.4

---

## Text

The license shall state whether the licensee is licensed as an escrow agent or joint control agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 663, Sec. 8.)
