# FIN § 17403.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 3. Escrow Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 17403.1

---

## Text

No person subject to this division shall describe as an escrow, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, any transaction that is not defined as such in Section 17003.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 441, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2000.)
