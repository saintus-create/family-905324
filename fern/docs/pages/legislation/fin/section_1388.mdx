# FIN § 1388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 11. Agency Activities > Article: 2. California State Bank as Principal

**Citation:** FIN § 1388

---

## Text

No California state bank may have an insured depository institution conduct as its agent any activity that the California state bank is prohibited from conducting itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
