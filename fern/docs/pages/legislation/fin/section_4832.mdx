# FIN § 4832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 4832

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner issues an order or license under this division, he or she may impose conditions as may in his or her opinion be necessary to carry out the provisions and purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 480. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
