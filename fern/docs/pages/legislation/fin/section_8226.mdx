# FIN § 8226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 7. Supervision > Article: 6. Conservatorship

**Citation:** FIN § 8226

---

## Text

Any conservator shall have all the rights, powers, and privileges possessed by the officers, directors, members and stockholders of the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
