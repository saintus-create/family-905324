# FIN § 456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 4. Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 456

---

## Text

Every licensee shall keep its corporate records, financial records, and books of account in words and figures of the English language and in form satisfactory to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
