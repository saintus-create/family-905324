# FIN § 5612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 3. Postincorporation Organization of Associations

**Citation:** FIN § 5612

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “security” means any stock, subordinated debenture, warrant, or right or option to purchase any stock issued by an association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 718, Sec. 4.)
