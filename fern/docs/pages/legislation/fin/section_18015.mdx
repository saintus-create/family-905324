# FIN § 18015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18015

---

## Text

“Lease obligation” as used in this division, means a lease contract entered into by an industrial loan company as lessor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 911.)
