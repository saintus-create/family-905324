# FIN § 31230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Corporate Matters > Article: 4. Distributions to Shareholders

**Citation:** FIN § 31230

---

## Text

In this article, “distribution to its shareholders” has the meaning set forth in Corporations Code Section 166.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
