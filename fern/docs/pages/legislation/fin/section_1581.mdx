# FIN § 1581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 16. Trust Companies > Article: 4. Investments

**Citation:** FIN § 1581

---

## Text

Trust funds received by any trust company in connection with its trust business, if invested, shall be invested as provided in Part 4 (commencing with Section 16000) of Division 9 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
