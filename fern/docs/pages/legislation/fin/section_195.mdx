# FIN § 195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 195

---

## Text

“ROCA supervisory rating” shall have the meaning set forth in Section 327.8(k) of Title 12 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
