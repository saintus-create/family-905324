# FIN § 4876.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 3.5. Sale of Whole Business Unit of California State Independent Trust Company to Uninsured Foreign (Other State) State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4876.02

---

## Text

With the approval of the commissioner, a California state independent trust company may sell its whole business unit to an uninsured foreign (other state) state depository corporation pursuant to this article and the law of the purchaser’s domicile.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 497.2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
