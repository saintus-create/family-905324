# FIN § 125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 125

---

## Text

“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation and “department” means the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 97. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
