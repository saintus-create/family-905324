# FIN § 17314.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 2. Purpose: Scope of Guarantee

**Citation:** FIN § 17314.2

---

## Text

Claims filed prior to the effective date of this chapter shall be governed by the provisions in effect when the loss occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1458, Sec. 9. Effective September 28, 1988.)
