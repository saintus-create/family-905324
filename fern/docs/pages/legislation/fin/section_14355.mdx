# FIN § 14355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Costs of Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 14355

---

## Text

All money received or collected by the commissioner under this division or any other law relating to credit unions or the credit union business shall be paid at least once each week, accompanied by a detailed statement thereof, into the State Treasury to the credit of the Credit Union Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 14354 (as amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 427) by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 575. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
