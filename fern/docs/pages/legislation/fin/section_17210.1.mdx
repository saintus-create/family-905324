# FIN § 17210.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2. License and Bond

**Citation:** FIN § 17210.1

---

## Text

The license shall be kept conspicuously posted in all places of business of the licensee.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1076.)
