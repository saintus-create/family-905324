# FIN § 7275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 6. Investment Operations > Article: 2.5. Other Authorized Investments in Bonds and Securities

**Citation:** FIN § 7275

---

## Text

As used in this article, “funded debt” means all interest-bearing indebtedness of a corporation not maturing within one year of the date it was incurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 718, Sec. 14.)
