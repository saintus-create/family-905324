# FIN § 131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 131

---

## Text

References in this division to the voting of shares shall be construed in accordance with Section 111 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
