# FIN § 22780.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 4. Revocation and Penalties > Article: 3. Commercial Loan Penalties

**Citation:** FIN § 22780.1

---

## Text

A violation of Division 9.5 (commencing with Section 22800) by a licensee shall constitute a violation of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 1011, Sec. 1. (SB 1235) Effective January 1, 2019.)
