# FIN § 31052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31052

---

## Text

“Regulation” means any published regulation, rule, or standard of general application issued by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 626. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
