# FIN § 18427.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Securities Transactions

**Citation:** FIN § 18427.6

---

## Text

Every permit issued pursuant to Section 18427.4 shall recite that it is permissive only and does not constitute a recommendation or endorsement of the securities permitted to be sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 608. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
