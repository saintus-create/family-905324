# FIN § 31110.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 31110.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may provide information relating to a licensee to the Small Business Administration or to any governmental agency which licenses or regulates the licensee or any parent or subsidiary of the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 638. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
