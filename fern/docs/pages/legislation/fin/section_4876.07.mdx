# FIN § 4876.07

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 3.5. Sale of Whole Business Unit of California State Independent Trust Company to Uninsured Foreign (Other State) State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4876.07

---

## Text

A sale shall have the same effect as provided in Sections 4859 and 4860 in the case of a sale of the type defined in Section 4845.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 32. Effective September 25, 1996.)
