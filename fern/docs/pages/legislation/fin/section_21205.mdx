# FIN § 21205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21205

---

## Text

Representatives of the pawnbroker industry shall poll their members annually to gather data relating to the current financial condition of the California pawn industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 245, Sec. 7. (SB 285) Effective January 1, 2016.)
