# FIN § 1386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 11. Agency Activities > Article: 2. California State Bank as Principal

**Citation:** FIN § 1386

---

## Text

An application by a California state bank for approval to have an insured depository institution engage in authorized agency activities as its agent shall be accompanied by a filing fee of two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
