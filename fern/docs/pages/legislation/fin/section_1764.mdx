# FIN § 1764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 20. Foreign (Other Nation) Banks > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1764

---

## Text

Each foreign (other nation) bank that is licensed to maintain an office shall post its license in a conspicuous place at the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
