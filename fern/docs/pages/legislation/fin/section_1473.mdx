# FIN § 1473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 2. Loan and Investment Limitations

**Citation:** FIN § 1473

---

## Text

Sections 1481 and 1510 shall not apply to investments held by a bank prior to January 1, 2009. All authorizations regarding investments by a bank issued by the commissioner prior to January 1, 2009, are terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 37. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
