# FIN § 30704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES > Chapter: 4. Hearings

**Citation:** FIN § 30704

---

## Text

Every order, decision, license or other official act of the commissioner is subject to review, in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1057.)
