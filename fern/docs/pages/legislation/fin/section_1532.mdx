# FIN § 1532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 15. Industrial Banks > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1532

---

## Text

The Industrial Loan Account of the Financial Institutions Fund is renamed to be the Industrial Bank Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
