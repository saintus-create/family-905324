# FIN § 186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 186

---

## Text

“Majority-owned subsidiary” has the meaning set forth for “subsidiary” in subdivision (a) of Section 189 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 13. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
