# FIN § 28153.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. STUDENT LOAN SERVICING ACT > Chapter: 4. Administration and Power of the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 28153.5

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this division, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 379, Sec. 20. (AB 38) Effective January 1, 2019.)
