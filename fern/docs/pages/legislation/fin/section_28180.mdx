# FIN § 28180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. STUDENT LOAN SERVICING ACT > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FIN § 28180

---

## Text

The rights and remedies provided in this division are in addition to any other rights and remedies provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 824, Sec. 2. (AB 2251) Effective January 1, 2017. Operative July 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 28182.)
