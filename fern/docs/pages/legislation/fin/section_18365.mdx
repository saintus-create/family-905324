# FIN § 18365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18365

---

## Text

The authority granted to the commissioner by Section 18363, may be exercised by him in conjunction with all other powers granted by this chapter, or independently from them.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
