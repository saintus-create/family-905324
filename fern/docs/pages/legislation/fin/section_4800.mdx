# FIN § 4800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title, Construction, and Severability

**Citation:** FIN § 4800

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the “Depository Corporation Sale, Merger, and Conversion Law.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1411, Sec. 3.)
