# FIN § 1833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 20. Foreign (Other Nation) Banks > Article: 5. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 1833

---

## Text

Any foreign (other nation) bank whose license to maintain an office is suspended or revoked shall immediately surrender the license to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
