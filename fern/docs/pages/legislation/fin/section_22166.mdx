# FIN § 22166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 22166

---

## Text

The commissioner may require licensees to maintain a file of all advertising copy for a period of two years from the date of its use. The file shall be available to the commissioner upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 640, Sec. 2. (SB 1146) Effective January 1, 2011.)
