# FIN § 32811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 7. Borrowing From the State > Article: 2. State Enterprise Loan Fund

**Citation:** FIN § 32811

---

## Text

The name of this account shall be the “State Enterprise Loan Fund,” which fund shall be continuously appropriated for the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1040, Sec. 20.)
