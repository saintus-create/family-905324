# FIN § 14653.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 6. Investments

**Citation:** FIN § 14653.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a credit union may make any investment authorized by regulation or in writing by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 951, Sec. 10.)
