# FIN § 1753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 20. Foreign (Other Nation) Banks > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1753

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, offices of foreign (other nation) banks are divided into classes and ranked in ascending order, as follows:

(a) Representative office.

(b) Nondepositary agency.

(c) Depositary agency.

(d) Limited branch office.

(e) Wholesale branch office.

(f) Retail branch office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
