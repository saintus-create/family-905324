# FIN § 32331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Corporate Matters > Article: 4. Management of the Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 32331

---

## Text

The operations of the corporation shall be directed by the president of the corporation, who shall be selected and appointed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
