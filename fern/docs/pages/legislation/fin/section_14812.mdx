# FIN § 14812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 5. Members

**Citation:** FIN § 14812

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in the bylaws, a quorum for a meeting of members shall be 10 percent of the members or 50 members, whichever is less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 321, Sec. 11.)
