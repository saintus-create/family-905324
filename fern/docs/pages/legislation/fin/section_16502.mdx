# FIN § 16502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 12. Foreign (Other Nation) Credit Unions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 16502

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall apply to a foreign (other state) state credit union or be deemed to authorize a foreign (other state) state credit union to transact business in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 612, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
