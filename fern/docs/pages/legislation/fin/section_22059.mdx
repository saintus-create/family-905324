# FIN § 22059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** FIN § 22059

---

## Text

A license to act as a broker under this division does not authorize the licensee to negotiate or perform any act as a broker in connection with loans made or to be made by a lender not licensed as a finance lender under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
