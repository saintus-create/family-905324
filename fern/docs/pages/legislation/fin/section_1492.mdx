# FIN § 1492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 3. Loan Limits

**Citation:** FIN § 1492

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter restricts a commercial bank in taking any lien on or pledge of any property as additional security for a loan already made in good faith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
