# FIN § 647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 7. Liquidation and Conservation > Article: 3. Conservatorship of an Uninsured Licensee

**Citation:** FIN § 647

---

## Text

The commissioner may assess and collect from all licensees for whom a conservator is appointed their ratable share of the costs incurred in the administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
