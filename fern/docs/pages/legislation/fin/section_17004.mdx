# FIN § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 17004

---

## Text

“Escrow agent” means any person engaged in the business of receiving escrows for deposit or delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 773, Sec. 1.)
