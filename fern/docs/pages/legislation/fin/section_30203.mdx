# FIN § 30203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES > Chapter: 2. License

**Citation:** FIN § 30203

---

## Text

All money received by the commissioner shall be paid by him or her into the State Treasury to the credit of the State Corporations Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 427, Sec. 41. Effective January 1, 1993.)
