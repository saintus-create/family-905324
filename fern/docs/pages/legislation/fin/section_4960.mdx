# FIN § 4960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 3. Conversion of California State Depository Corporation Into Federal Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4960

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “conversion” means any of the conversions described in Section 4961.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 192. Effective October 2, 1995.)
