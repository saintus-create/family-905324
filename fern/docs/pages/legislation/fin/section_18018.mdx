# FIN § 18018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18018

---

## Text

“Capital,” as used in this division, consists of all of the following:

(a) Capital stock.

(b) Primary capital to the extent not included in capital stock.

(c) Secondary capital.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 663, Sec. 1. Operative January 1, 1991, by Sec. 20 of Ch. 663.)
