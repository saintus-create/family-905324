# FIN § 161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 161

---

## Text

“Constituent corporation,” when used with respect to a corporation:

(a) In the case of a merger, has the meaning set forth in Section 161 of the Corporations Code.

(b) In the case of a consolidation, means a corporation that is consolidated with one or more other corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
