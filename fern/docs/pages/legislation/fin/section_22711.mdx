# FIN § 22711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 4. Revocation and Penalties > Article: 1. Revocation and Suspension of License

**Citation:** FIN § 22711

---

## Text

Any licensee may surrender any license by delivering to the commissioner written notice that the licensee surrenders that license. Surrender of the license does not affect the licensee’s civil or criminal liability for acts committed prior to surrender of the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
