# FIN § 18103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 2. Formation and Organization > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18103

---

## Text

An industrial loan company’s certificate to engage in the industrial loan business is not transferable or assignable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
