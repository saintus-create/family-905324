# FIN § 22706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 4. Revocation and Penalties > Article: 1. Revocation and Suspension of License

**Citation:** FIN § 22706

---

## Text

The commissioner may require the attendance of witnesses and examine under oath all persons whose testimony relates to activities and businesses regulated by this division or to the subject matter of any examination, investigation, or hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 160, Sec. 1. (AB 2559) Effective January 1, 2021.)
