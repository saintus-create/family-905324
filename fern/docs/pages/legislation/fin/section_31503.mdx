# FIN § 31503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 7. Records, Reports, and Examinations

**Citation:** FIN § 31503

---

## Text

The commissioner may by order require a licensee to write down any asset on its books and records to a valuation which represents its then value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 663. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
