# FIN § 14605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 5. Credit Committee and Loan Officers

**Citation:** FIN § 14605

---

## Text

A credit manager shall not disburse or have the authority to authorize any person to disburse the proceeds of any loan. A credit manager shall not authorize or make loans to other credit unions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 691, Sec. 12.)
