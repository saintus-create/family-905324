# FIN § 30600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES > Chapter: 3. Revocation of License

**Citation:** FIN § 30600

---

## Text

A security depository license remains in effect until surrendered, revoked or suspended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1057.)
