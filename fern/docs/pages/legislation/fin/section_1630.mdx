# FIN § 1630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 17. Safe Deposit > Article: 2. Remedies for Nonpayment of Rent

**Citation:** FIN § 1630

---

## Text

Every bank conducting a safe-deposit business shall be entitled to the special remedies set forth in this article in enforcing the liabilities of safe-deposit box renters and of safekeeping and storage depositors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
