# FIN § 31829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 12. Crimes and Criminal Penalties > Article: 2. Conflicts of Interest

**Citation:** FIN § 31829

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any licensee, directly or indirectly, to sell or otherwise transfer any of its assets to any of its associates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
