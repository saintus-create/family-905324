# FIN § 14201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14201

---

## Text

The commissioner may establish or waive such rules and regulations as may be reasonable or necessary to carry out the purposes and provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 263, Sec. 1.)
