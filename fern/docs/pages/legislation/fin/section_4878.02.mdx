# FIN § 4878.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 4.5. Sale of Partial Trust Business Unit by California State Bank or Savings Association to Uninsured Foreign (Other State) State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4878.02

---

## Text

With the approval of the commissioner, a California state bank or savings association may sell a partial trust business unit to an uninsured foreign (other state) state depository corporation pursuant to (a) this article and (b) the law of the purchaser’s domicile.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 499.3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
