# FIN § 3411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.25. DIGITAL FINANCIAL ASSETS BUSINESSES > Chapter: 4. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 3411

---

## Text

The department may enter into a consent order with a person regarding an enforcement measure. The order may provide that it does not constitute an admission of fact by a party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 792, Sec. 1. (AB 39) Effective January 1, 2024.)
