# FIN § 6600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 1. Savings Liability

**Citation:** FIN § 6600

---

## Text

The savings liability of an association shall consist only of the aggregate amount of its savings accounts, plus interest earned or credited to the accounts, less redemption and withdrawal payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
