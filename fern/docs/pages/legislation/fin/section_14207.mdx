# FIN § 14207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14207

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
