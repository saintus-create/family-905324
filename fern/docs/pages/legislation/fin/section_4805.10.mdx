# FIN § 4805.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.10

---

## Text

In this division, “industrial loan company” means an industrial bank as defined in Section 111.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 49. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
