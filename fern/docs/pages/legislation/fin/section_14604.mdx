# FIN § 14604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 5. Credit Committee and Loan Officers

**Citation:** FIN § 14604

---

## Text

A credit union member whose written application to enter into any obligation is denied, may appeal from the denial of the credit committee, the credit manager, or loan officer to the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 789, Sec. 5.)
