# FIN § 4884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 1. Merger Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4884

---

## Text

A surviving depository corporation shall file with the commissioner an application for approval of the merger.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 512. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
