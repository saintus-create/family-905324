# FIN § 31156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 31156

---

## Text

Each licensee shall post its license in a conspicuous place at its head office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
