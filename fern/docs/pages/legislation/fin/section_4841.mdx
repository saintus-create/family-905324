# FIN § 4841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 4841

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 1001 of the Corporations Code shall not apply to any transaction which is subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1411, Sec. 3.)
