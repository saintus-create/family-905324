# FIN § 18000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the “Industrial Loan Law,” the “Industrial Banking Law,” or the “Thrift and Loan Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 827, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
