# FIN § 18139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 2. Formation and Organization > Article: 3. Capitalization

**Citation:** FIN § 18139

---

## Text

A sale, merger, or conversion involving an industrial loan company and another industrial loan company, a bank, or a savings association is subject to Division 1.6 (commencing with Section 4800).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 277, Sec. 10. (AB 2907) Effective January 1, 2017.)
