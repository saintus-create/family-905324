# FIN § 32205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 32205

---

## Text

“Rules Committee” means the rules committee of the State Senate or any person to whom the rules committee of the State Senate delegates to act for it in the particular matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
