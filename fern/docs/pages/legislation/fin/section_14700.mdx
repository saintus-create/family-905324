# FIN § 14700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 7. Reserves

**Citation:** FIN § 14700

---

## Text

Every credit union shall create and maintain a regular reserve as specified by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 539, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
