# FIN § 1882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 21. International and Foreign Banking and Financing > Article: 1. International and Foreign Banking and Financing Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 1882

---

## Text

No person shall act in this state as the representative of any foreign corporation in transacting the business described in this article as the business of a corporation unless such corporation shall have complied with the provisions of this article relating to such corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
