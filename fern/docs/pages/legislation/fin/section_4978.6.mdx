# FIN § 4978.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. COVERED LOANS > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 4978.6

---

## Text

A person who originates covered loans shall inform any employee, who originates covered loans on behalf of the person, of the administrative or civil penalties for a violation of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 733, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2002. Applicable, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 733, only to covered loans applied for on or after July 1, 2002.)
