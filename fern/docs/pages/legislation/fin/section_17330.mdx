# FIN § 17330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 17330

---

## Text

Any member or successor in interest who suffers a loss may file a claim with Fidelity Corporation for the amount of the loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1458, Sec. 21. Effective September 28, 1988.)
