# FIN § 22100.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** FIN § 22100.5

---

## Text

(a) A person shall not engage in the business of a program administrator without obtaining a license from the commissioner.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2019.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 20. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2019, by its own provisions.)
