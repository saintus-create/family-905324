# FIN § 1072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 4. Bank Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 1072

---

## Text

Every bank shall establish and maintain a head office which shall be located in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
