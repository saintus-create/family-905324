# FIN § 18420.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Possession, Conservatorship, and Liquidation by the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 18420.6

---

## Text

The commissioner shall have the authority to terminate the appointment of a conservator or liquidator whenever the commissioner deems it in the best interest of the company, its creditors, and investors. The commissioner shall file a copy of the notice of termination with the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 140, Sec. 1. Effective July 1, 1985.)
