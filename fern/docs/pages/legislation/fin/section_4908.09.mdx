# FIN § 4908.09

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 4. Merger of California State Independent Trust Company into Uninsured Foreign (Other State) Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4908.09

---

## Text

Promptly after a merger becomes effective, the surviving depository corporation shall:

(a) Surrender to the commissioner for cancellation the certificates of authority or licenses issued by the commissioner to the disappearing depository corporation.

(b) File with the commissioner any report regarding the merger that the commissioner may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 521.8. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
