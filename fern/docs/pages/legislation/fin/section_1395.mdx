# FIN § 1395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 11. Agency Activities > Article: 3. California State Bank as Agent

**Citation:** FIN § 1395

---

## Text

No California state bank may conduct any activity as an agent for an insured depository institution that the California state bank would be prohibited from conducting as a principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
