# FIN § 14501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 3. Officers

**Citation:** FIN § 14501

---

## Text

If the bylaws so provide, the directors may elect the same person as secretary and treasurer or chief financial officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 951, Sec. 7.)
