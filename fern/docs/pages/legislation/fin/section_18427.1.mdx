# FIN § 18427.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Securities Transactions

**Citation:** FIN § 18427.1

---

## Text

No industrial loan company organized under the laws of this state shall offer or sell any security issued by it unless the commissioner has issued a permit authorizing such sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 600. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
