# FIN § 1766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 20. Foreign (Other Nation) Banks > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1766

---

## Text

Whenever a foreign (other nation) bank is licensed to maintain two or more agencies or branch offices, it shall designate one of such offices as its primary office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
