# FIN § 4963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 3. Conversion of California State Depository Corporation Into Federal Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4963

---

## Text

A conversion shall have the same effect as provided in Section 4950 in the case of a conversion of the type defined in Section 4940.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 197. Effective October 2, 1995.)
