# FIN § 7261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 6. Investment Operations > Article: 2.5. Other Authorized Investments in Bonds and Securities

**Citation:** FIN § 7261

---

## Text

Bonds of the State of California and those for which the faith and credit of the State of California are pledged for the payment of principal and interest, and registered warrants of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 718, Sec. 14.)
