# FIN § 14152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Certificate to Act as a Credit Union

**Citation:** FIN § 14152

---

## Text

At the time of filing an application to operate as a credit union, the applicant shall pay to the commissioner a filing fee of five dollars ($5).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
