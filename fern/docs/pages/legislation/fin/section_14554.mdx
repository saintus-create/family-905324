# FIN § 14554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 4. Supervisory Committee

**Citation:** FIN § 14554

---

## Text

No member of the supervisory committee shall serve as a member of the credit committee, as the credit manager, as a member of the board of directors, or as an employee of the credit union.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 691, Sec. 6.)
