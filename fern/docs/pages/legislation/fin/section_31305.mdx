# FIN § 31305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Offices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 31305

---

## Text

Each licensee shall maintain at each of its offices personnel who are competent to conduct the business of such office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
