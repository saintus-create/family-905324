# FIN § 18018.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18018.5

---

## Text

“Limited life preferred,” as used in this division, means preferred stock which has a maturity or which may be redeemed at the option of the holder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 663, Sec. 1.5. Operative January 1, 1991, by Sec. 20 of Ch. 663.)
