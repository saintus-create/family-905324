# FIN § 17343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 17343

---

## Text

No provision of the Insurance Code shall apply to Fidelity Corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1560, Sec. 31. Effective October 2, 1985.)
