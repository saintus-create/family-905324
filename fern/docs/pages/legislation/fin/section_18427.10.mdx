# FIN § 18427.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Securities Transactions

**Citation:** FIN § 18427.10

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this article shall affect the Corporate Securities Law of 1968, Division 1 (commencing with Section 25000) of Title 4 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 612. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
