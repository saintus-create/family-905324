# FIN § 1552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 16. Trust Companies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1552

---

## Text

No California state bank may engage in trust business at a place unless the place is its head office, an authorized branch office, or an authorized place of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
