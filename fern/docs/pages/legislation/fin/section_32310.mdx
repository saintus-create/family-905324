# FIN § 32310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Corporate Matters > Article: 2. Corporate Name

## Text
