# FIN § 22501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3. Commercial Loans > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22501

---

## Text

“Charges” do not include commissions received as a licensed insurance agent or broker.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
