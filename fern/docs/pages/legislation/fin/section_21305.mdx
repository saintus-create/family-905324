# FIN § 21305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 3. Licensure

**Citation:** FIN § 21305

---

## Text

A license issued pursuant to this chapter shall not be transferred or assigned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 782, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
