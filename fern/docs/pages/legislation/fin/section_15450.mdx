# FIN § 15450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 10. Central Credit Union > Article: 2. General

**Citation:** FIN § 15450

---

## Text

Central credit unions may be organized and operated under this division. Each such credit union shall use the word “central” in its official name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
