# FIN § 4895.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 2. Merger Into California State-Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank

**Citation:** FIN § 4895.03

---

## Text

In case the disappearing depository corporation is a California state depository corporation, a merger is subject to the provisions of Section 1108 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 164. Effective October 2, 1995.)
