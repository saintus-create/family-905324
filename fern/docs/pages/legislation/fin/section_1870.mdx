# FIN § 1870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 21. International and Foreign Banking and Financing > Article: 1. International and Foreign Banking and Financing Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 1870

---

## Text

No corporation shall by any system of accounting or any device of bookkeeping, directly or indirectly enter any of its assets upon its books in the name of any other person, or under any title or designation that is not truly descriptive thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
