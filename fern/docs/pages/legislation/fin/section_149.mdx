# FIN § 149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 149

---

## Text

“CAMELS composite rating” shall have the meaning set forth in Section 327.8(j) of Title 12 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
