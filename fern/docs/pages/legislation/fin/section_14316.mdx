# FIN § 14316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 14316

---

## Text

The commissioner shall supervise the acts of the liquidating agent or the liquidating committee appointed under this article and may remove the liquidating agent or any member of the liquidating committee in his or her discretion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 734, Sec. 38. Effective September 20, 2002.)
