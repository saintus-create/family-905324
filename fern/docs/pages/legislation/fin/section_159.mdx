# FIN § 159

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 159

---

## Text

(a) “Confidential information” means any information regarding a licensee contained in, or related to, any of the following:

(1) Applications filed with the commissioner.

(2) Examination, operating, condition, or any other reports prepared by, on behalf of, or for the use of, the commissioner.

(3) Information received in confidence by the commissioner.

(b) Confidential information is the property of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
