# FIN § 18670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 10. Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies: Agency Activities > Article: 2. Facilities of Insured Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 18670

---

## Text

No provision of this article applies to an insured foreign (other state) industrial loan company that maintains a California branch office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 479, Sec. 7. Effective October 2, 1995.)
