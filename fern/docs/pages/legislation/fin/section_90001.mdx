# FIN § 90001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER FINANCIAL PROTECTION LAW > Chapter: 2. Short Title

**Citation:** FIN § 90001

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the “California Consumer Financial Protection Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 157, Sec. 7. (AB 1864) Effective January 1, 2021.)
