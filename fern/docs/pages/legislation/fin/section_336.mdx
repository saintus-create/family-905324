# FIN § 336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 2. Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation

**Citation:** FIN § 336

---

## Text

The commissioner may, at any time, require a licensee to write down any asset held by the licensee to a valuation that will represent the asset’s then fair market value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
