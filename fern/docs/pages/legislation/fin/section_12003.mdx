# FIN § 12003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 12003

---

## Text

“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation, or any deputy, investigator, auditor, or any other person employed by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 110. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
