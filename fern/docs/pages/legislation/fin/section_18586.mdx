# FIN § 18586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 2. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18586

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 18023, 18024, 18120, 18205, 18268, 18269, 18271, 18272, 18274, and 18455 shall not apply to a premium finance agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 159, Sec. 95. Effective January 1, 2002.)
