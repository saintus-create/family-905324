# FIN § 10009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 10. Foreign (National) Savings Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 10009

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10003 by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1162, Sec. 21. Effective September 26, 1987. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 10 (added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1057), commencing with Section 10000.)
