# FIN § 4880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 1. Merger Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4880

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) “Agreement of merger” includes a certificate of ownership executed pursuant to Section 1110 of the Corporations Code.

(b) “Merger” means any of the mergers described in Section 4881.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 509.1. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
