# FIN § 4820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Other General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 4820

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, a national banking association or federal savings association is deemed to be a corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 98. Effective October 2, 1995.)
