# FIN § 32403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 3. Corporate Finances > Article: 1. Corporate Accounts

**Citation:** FIN § 32403

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided for in this division, all funds received by the corporation shall be deposited in the general operating account of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
