# FIN § 18695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 10. Foreign (Other State) Industrial Loan Companies: Agency Activities > Article: 4. California Industrial Loan Company as Principal

**Citation:** FIN § 18695

---

## Text

No office of an insured depository institution that is performing authorized agency activities as agent for a California industrial loan company in accordance with this article shall, on that account, be deemed to be an office of the California industrial loan company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 64. Effective September 25, 1996.)
