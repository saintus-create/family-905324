# FIN § 100022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. DEBT COLLECTION LICENSING ACT > Chapter: 3. Licensee Duties

**Citation:** FIN § 100022

---

## Text

A licensee that ceases to engage in debt collection shall inform the commissioner in writing and surrender the license and all other indicia of license to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. (SB 908) Effective January 1, 2021. Operative January 1, 2022, pursuant to Sec. 100000.5.)
