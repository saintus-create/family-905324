# FIN § 5624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 4. Issuance of Stock and Certificates

**Citation:** FIN § 5624

---

## Text

An association may issue net worth certificates in accordance with applicable regulations of the Office of Thrift Supervision or the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1118, Sec. 23.)
