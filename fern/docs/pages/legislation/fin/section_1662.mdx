# FIN § 1662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 18. Interstate Acquisitions

**Citation:** FIN § 1662

---

## Text

The fee for filing with the commissioner an application for an approval under this chapter is two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
