# FIN § 21200.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21200.7

---

## Text

The maximum charge of compensation charged by a pawnbroker pursuant to the authority of Section 21200 shall be posted in a place clearly visible to the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21200.5 by Stats. 1969, Ch. 25.)
