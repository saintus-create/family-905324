# FIN § 22694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3.5. Program Administrators

**Citation:** FIN § 22694

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a finance lender, mortgage loan originator, or broker licensee, unless they engage in the business of a program administrator, PACE solicitor, or PACE solicitor agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 798, Sec. 13. (SB 1087) Effective January 1, 2019.)
