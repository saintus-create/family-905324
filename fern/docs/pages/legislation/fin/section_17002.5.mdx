# FIN § 17002.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 1. Application of This Division

**Citation:** FIN § 17002.5

---

## Text

“Person” means, in addition to the singular, persons, group of persons, co-operative, association, company, firm, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other legal entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 1995.)
