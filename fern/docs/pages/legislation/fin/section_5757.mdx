# FIN § 5757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 7. Power to Reorganize, Merge, Consolidate, or Transfer Assets

**Citation:** FIN § 5757

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the approval of transactions under this article shall not be required by any borrowers or by holders of savings accounts in associations that issue stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
