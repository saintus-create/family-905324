# FIN § 12325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 4. Licensee Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 12325

---

## Text

A prorater shall not solicit or require a debtor to purchase or agree to purchase any policy of insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 498.)
