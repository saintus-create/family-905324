# FIN § 4805.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.15

---

## Text

“Share” means:

(a) When used with respect to a California state savings association that is authorized to issue guarantee stock, a share of guarantee stock.

(b) When used with respect to a federal savings association that is authorized to issue capital stock, a share of capital stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 97. Effective October 2, 1995.)
