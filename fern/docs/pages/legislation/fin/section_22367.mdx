# FIN § 22367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 3.6. Pilot Program for Increased Access to Responsible Small Dollar Loans

**Citation:** FIN § 22367

---

## Text

Every entity approved by the commissioner to participate in the program shall file with the commissioner on or before March 15 an annual report consistent with Section 22159, separate from any other annual report the licensee may be required to file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 467, Sec. 2. (SB 318) Effective January 1, 2014. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 22381.)
