# FIN § 28118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. STUDENT LOAN SERVICING ACT > Chapter: 2. Licensing > Article: 2. Application for License

**Citation:** FIN § 28118

---

## Text

The commissioner shall, when the application is complete, including having received the information from the Department of Justice, either grant a license pursuant to this division or provide a written explanation for the denial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 824, Sec. 2. (AB 2251) Effective January 1, 2017. Operative July 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 28182.)
