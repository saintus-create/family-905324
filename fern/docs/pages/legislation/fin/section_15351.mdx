# FIN § 15351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger, Dissolution and Conversion > Article: 4. Conversion of Federal Credit Union

**Citation:** FIN § 15351

---

## Text

The officers and directors of the federal credit union shall be the officers and directors of the credit union after conversion takes effect, to hold office until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
