# FIN § 4921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 1. Conversion of California State Depository Corporation Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4921

---

## Text

With the approval of the commissioner, a California state depository corporation of any class may convert into a California state depository corporation of another class pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 523. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
