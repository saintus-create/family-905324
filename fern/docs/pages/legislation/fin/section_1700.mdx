# FIN § 1700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 19. Foreign (Other State) Banks > Article: 3. Facilities of Insured Foreign (Other State) Banks

**Citation:** FIN § 1700

---

## Text

No provision of this article applies to an insured foreign (other state) bank that maintains a California branch office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
