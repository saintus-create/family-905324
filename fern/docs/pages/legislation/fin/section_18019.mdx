# FIN § 18019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18019

---

## Text

“Capital Stock,” as used in this division, means one-class voting common stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 663, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1991, by Sec. 20 of Ch. 663.)
