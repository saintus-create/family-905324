# FIN § 21208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21208

---

## Text

A pawnbroker shall comply with the reporting requirements imposed on secondhand dealers under Article 4 (commencing with Section 21625) of Chapter 9 of Division 8 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 172, Sec. 8. (AB 391) Effective August 17, 2012.)
