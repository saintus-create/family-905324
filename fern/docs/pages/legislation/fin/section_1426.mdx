# FIN § 1426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 12. Deposits > Article: 2. Disclosure of Delayed Availability Policy

**Citation:** FIN § 1426

---

## Text

The commissioner is authorized to gather from depository institutions such information as may be necessary for the formulation and promulgation of the regulations required by Section 1424.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
