# FIN § 4878.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 4.5. Sale of Partial Trust Business Unit by California State Bank or Savings Association to Uninsured Foreign (Other State) State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4878.03

---

## Text

A sale is subject to the provisions of Sections 4879.03 to 4879.09, inclusive, as if the sale were a sale of the type defined in Section 4879.01.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 35. Effective September 25, 1996.)
