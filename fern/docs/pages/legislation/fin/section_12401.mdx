# FIN § 12401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 5. Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** FIN § 12401

---

## Text

The commissioner may upon three days’ notice and a hearing, suspend any license for a period not exceeding 30 days, pending investigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 364.)
