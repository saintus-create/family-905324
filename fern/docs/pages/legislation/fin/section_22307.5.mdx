# FIN § 22307.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 3. Loan Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 22307.5

---

## Text

A licensee shall not charge, impose, or receive any penalty for the prepayment of a loan. This section does not apply to a loan secured by real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 708, Sec. 7. (AB 539) Effective January 1, 2020.)
