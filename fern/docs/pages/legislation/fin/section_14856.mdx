# FIN § 14856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14856

---

## Text

Every credit union may impress a lien upon the shares and dividends of any member to the extent of any obligations entered into with that member and for any dues or charges payable by that member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 789, Sec. 9.)
