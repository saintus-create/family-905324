# FIN § 18643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 5. Charges on Scheduled Balances

**Citation:** FIN § 18643

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law not within this article, with respect to precomputed loans, premium finance agencies derive authority only from this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 270.)
