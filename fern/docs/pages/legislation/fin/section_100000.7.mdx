# FIN § 100000.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. DEBT COLLECTION LICENSING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** FIN § 100000.7

---

## Text

No county, city, or other political subdivision within this state shall require a debt collector to be licensed or to register as a debt collector.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. (SB 908) Effective January 1, 2021. Operative January 1, 2022, pursuant to Sec. 100000.5.)
