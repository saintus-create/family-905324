# FIN § 21200.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 2. Pawnbroker Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 21200.8

---

## Text

In addition to other allowed charges, a pawnbroker may collect a processing charge of twenty dollars ($20) for each firearm pawned.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 189, Sec. 3. (AB 1186) Effective January 1, 2020.)
