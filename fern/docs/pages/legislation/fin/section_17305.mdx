# FIN § 17305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 17305

---

## Text

“Monthly average escrow liability,” as used in this chapter, means the average escrow liability for the 12-month period as reported in the most recent report made by the member to the commissioner pursuant to Section 17348.
