# FIN § 14381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Credit Union Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FIN § 14381

---

## Text

The Credit Union Advisory Committee shall advise the commissioner and the Deputy Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation for the Office of Credit Unions on matters relating to credit unions or the credit union business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 117. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
