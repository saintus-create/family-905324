# FIN § 18427.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 5. Securities Transactions

**Citation:** FIN § 18427.7

---

## Text

The commissioner may amend, alter, suspend, or revoke any permit issued pursuant to Section 18427.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 609. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
