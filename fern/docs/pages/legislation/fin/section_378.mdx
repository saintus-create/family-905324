# FIN § 378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. Department of Financial Protection and Innovation > Article: 4. Administration of the Department

**Citation:** FIN § 378

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner is notified of or discovers a violation of the state law punishable by criminal penalties, he or she shall promptly advise the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
