# FIN § 4805.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.12

---

## Text

“Law of the domicile” means:

(a) When used with respect to a federal depository corporation, the law of the United States.

(b) When used with respect to a state depository corporation, the law of the state of the United States under which the depository corporation is organized.

(c) When used with respect to a foreign (other nation) bank, the law of the foreign nation under which the bank is organized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 97. Effective October 2, 1995.)
