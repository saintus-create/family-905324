# FIN § 12403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 5. Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** FIN § 12403

---

## Text

Every order, decision, license, or other official act of the commissioner is subject to review in accordance with law.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 364.)
