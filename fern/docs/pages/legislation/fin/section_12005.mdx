# FIN § 12005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 12005

---

## Text

“Mobile unit” means a vehicle or other movable means from which the business of selling checks, drafts, or money orders is conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 660, Sec. 4.)
