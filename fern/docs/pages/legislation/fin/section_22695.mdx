# FIN § 22695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 3.5. Program Administrators

**Citation:** FIN § 22695

---

## Text

(a) A violation of this chapter by any person is not subject to the criminal penalties established pursuant to Sections 22753 and 22780.

(b) Notwithstanding Section 22696, this section shall become operative on the effective date of this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 71. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Section operative October 4, 2017, by its own provisions.)
