# FIN § 4834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** FIN § 4834

---

## Text

The commissioner may honor applications from interested persons for interpretive opinions regarding any provision of this division or of any regulation or order issued under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 481. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
