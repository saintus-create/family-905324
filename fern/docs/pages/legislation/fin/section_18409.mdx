# FIN § 18409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 3. Reports and Financial Statements

**Citation:** FIN § 18409

---

## Text

The commissioner shall make and file annually with the department as a public record a composite of reports filed by industrial loan companies, and any comments thereon that he or she deems in the public interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 596. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
