# FIN § 8055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 7. Supervision > Article: 3. General Supervisory Powers and Duties of Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 8055

---

## Text

Every final decision of the commissioner is subject to judicial review in accordance with law. An action or proceeding for judicial review pursuant to this section shall be commenced within 60 days after issuance of the final decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 361, Sec. 12.)
