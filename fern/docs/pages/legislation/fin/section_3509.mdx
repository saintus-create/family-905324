# FIN § 3509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.25. DIGITAL FINANCIAL ASSETS BUSINESSES > Chapter: 5. Disclosures and Protections

**Citation:** FIN § 3509

---

## Text

The requirements imposed on a covered person or a covered exchange under this chapter shall be operative on July 1, 2026.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 945, Sec. 3. (AB 1934) Effective January 1, 2025.)
