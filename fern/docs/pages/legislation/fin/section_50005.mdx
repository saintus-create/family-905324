# FIN § 50005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 50005

---

## Text

This division creates and authorizes a class of exempt persons pursuant to Section 1 of Article XV of the California Constitution.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 564, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
