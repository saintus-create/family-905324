# FIN § 183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 183

---

## Text

“Law of the domicile” means:

(a) When used with respect to a national bank, the law of the United States.

(b) When used with respect to a state bank, the law of the state of the United States under which the bank is organized.

(c) When used with respect to a foreign (other nation) bank, the law of the foreign nation under which the bank is organized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
