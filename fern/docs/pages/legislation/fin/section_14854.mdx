# FIN § 14854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14854

---

## Text

Subject to Section 14860, a credit union share account that is a multiple-party account, as defined in Section 5132 of the Probate Code, is governed by Part 2 (commencing with Section 5100) of Division 5 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79, Sec. 9. Operative July 1, 1991, pursuant to Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 46.)
