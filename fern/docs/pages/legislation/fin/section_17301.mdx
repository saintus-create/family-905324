# FIN § 17301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 17301

---

## Text

“Member” means any person licensed under this division who is required by Section 17312 to be a member of Fidelity Corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1560, Sec. 7. Effective October 2, 1985.)
