# FIN § 14502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 4. Management and Operations > Article: 3. Officers

**Citation:** FIN § 14502

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the officers of any credit union required pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 14500 are as prescribed in the bylaws.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 951, Sec. 8.)
