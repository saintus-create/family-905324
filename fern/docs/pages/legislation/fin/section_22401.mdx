# FIN § 22401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 4. Charges On Scheduled Balances

**Citation:** FIN § 22401

---

## Text

With respect to precomputed loans, licensees shall be subject only to, comply only with, and derive authority only from Sections 22400 and 22402, notwithstanding any other provision of law that is not within this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
