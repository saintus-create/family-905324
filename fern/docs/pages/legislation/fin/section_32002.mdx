# FIN § 32002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title, Construction and Severability

**Citation:** FIN § 32002

---

## Text

If any provision of this division or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such invalidity, illegality, or enforceability shall not affect other provisions or applications of this division which can be given effect without the invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision or application, and to this end, the provisions of this division are declared to be severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
