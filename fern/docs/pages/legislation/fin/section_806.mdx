# FIN § 806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 10. Legal Investments for Nonbank Licensees

**Citation:** FIN § 806

---

## Text

Bonds of the State of California and those for which the faith and credit of the State of California are pledged for the payment of principal and interest and in registered warrants of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
