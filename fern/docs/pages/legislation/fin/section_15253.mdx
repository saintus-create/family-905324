# FIN § 15253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 9. Merger, Dissolution and Conversion > Article: 2. Dissolution

**Citation:** FIN § 15253

---

## Text

After a vote to dissolve a credit union no business may be carried on by the credit union except in the proper course of liquidation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
