# FIN § 5100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5100

---

## Text

When used in this division, the words and phrases set forth in this article shall have the meanings given in this article unless the context requires another meaning.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
