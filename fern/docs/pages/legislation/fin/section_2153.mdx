# FIN § 2153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.2. MONEY TRANSMISSION ACT > Chapter: 8. Additional Enforcement Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 2153

---

## Text

The enforcement provisions of this division are in addition to any other enforcement powers that the commissioner may have under law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 356, Sec. 29. (SB 979) Effective January 1, 2013.)
