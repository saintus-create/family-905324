# FIN § 18004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18004

---

## Text

“Borrower” means the person receiving the proceeds or benefits of a loan.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
