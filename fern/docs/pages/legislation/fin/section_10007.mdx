# FIN § 10007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 10. Foreign (National) Savings Companies

**Citation:** FIN § 10007

---

## Text

If a foreign savings association is controlled by a foreign holding company, the requirements under this chapter applicable to both a foreign savings association and foreign holding company are required to be met.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1162, Sec. 25. Effective September 26, 1987. Section operative January 1, 1991, pursuant to Section 10009.)
