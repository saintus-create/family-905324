# FIN § 18595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 2. General

**Citation:** FIN § 18595

---

## Text

A premium finance agency shall not incorporate the words “industrial loan company” in its corporate name, on its loan forms, or in its advertising.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
