# FIN § 1326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 10. Restrictions and Prohibited Practices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1326

---

## Text

No bank shall publish a statement of its resources or liabilities in connection with those of any other bank, unless such statement shall show the resources and liabilities of each bank separately.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
