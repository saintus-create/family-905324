# FIN § 4805.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 4805.06

---

## Text

“Depository corporation” means a state or federal depository corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 97. Effective October 2, 1995.)
