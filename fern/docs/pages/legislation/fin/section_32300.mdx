# FIN § 32300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Corporate Matters > Article: 1. Creation of the Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 32300

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby authorizes the creation of a nonprofit corporation pursuant to the provisions of the Nonprofit Corporation Law (Part 1 (commencing with Section 9000), Division 2, Title 1 of the Corporations Code) on January 1, 1981.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
