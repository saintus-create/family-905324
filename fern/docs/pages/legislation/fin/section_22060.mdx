# FIN § 22060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** FIN § 22060

---

## Text

This division does not apply to a loan made or arranged by a licensed residential mortgage lender or servicer when acting under the authority of that license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 564, Sec. 6.5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
