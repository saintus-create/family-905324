# FIN § 14001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 14001.5

---

## Text

If and to the extent that any provision of this division is preempted by federal law, the provision shall not apply and shall not be enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 734, Sec. 4. Effective September 20, 2002.)
