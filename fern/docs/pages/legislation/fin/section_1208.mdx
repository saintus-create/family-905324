# FIN § 1208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 6. Securities

**Citation:** FIN § 1208

---

## Text

The commissioner may amend, alter, suspend, or revoke any permit issued pursuant to Section 1205.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
