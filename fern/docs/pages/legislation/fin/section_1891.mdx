# FIN § 1891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 21. International and Foreign Banking and Financing > Article: 1. International and Foreign Banking and Financing Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 1891

---

## Text

Nothing in this division restricts the right of a state international or foreign banking or financing corporation to convert into an international or foreign banking or financing corporation organized under the laws of the United States upon compliance with such laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
