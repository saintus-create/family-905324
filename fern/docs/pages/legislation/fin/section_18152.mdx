# FIN § 18152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 2. Formation and Organization > Article: 4. Branch Offices

**Citation:** FIN § 18152

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 18146, 18147, 18148, 18149, 18150, and 18151 shall not affect branch offices which are opened and operating prior to September 18, 1959.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
