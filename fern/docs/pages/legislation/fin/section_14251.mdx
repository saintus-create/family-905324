# FIN § 14251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 2. Examinations and Reports

**Citation:** FIN § 14251

---

## Text

The commissioner upon the written consent of any credit union officer or director may make any examination of the credit union in any regularly established office of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
