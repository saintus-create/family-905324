# FIN § 1461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1461

---

## Text

Assets hypothecated by a commercial bank as security for moneys borrowed shall not exceed in value the amount borrowed by more than 50 percent except with the prior written consent of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
