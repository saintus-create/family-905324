# FIN § 1464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 14. Loans and Investments > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1464

---

## Text

A commercial bank may borrow for the purpose of buying from the United States, United States bonds, United States Treasury certificates, or notes or other obligations of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
