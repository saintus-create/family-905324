# FIN § 80001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. BANK ON CALIFORNIA PROGRAM

**Citation:** FIN § 80001

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation.

(b) “Program” means the Bank on California Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (SB 577) Effective April 28, 2022.)
