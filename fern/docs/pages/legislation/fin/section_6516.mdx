# FIN § 6516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers of Associations > Article: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** FIN § 6516

---

## Text

(a) Subject to regulations issued by the commissioner, an association may own and use or participate in the use or ownership and use of remote service units.

(b) A remote service unit is not a branch or agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
