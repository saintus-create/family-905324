# FIN § 4995.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.10. HIGHER-PRICED MORTGAGE LOANS

**Citation:** FIN § 4995.6

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to affect any other rights or remedies otherwise available under the law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 629, Sec. 4. (AB 260) Effective January 1, 2010.)
