# FIN § 16002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 11. Foreign (Other State) Credit Unions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 16002

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to authorize a foreign (other nation) credit union to transact business in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 612, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
