# FIN § 28001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COLLEGE LOANS

**Citation:** FIN § 28001

---

## Text

This division shall not apply to community colleges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 176, Sec. 1.)
