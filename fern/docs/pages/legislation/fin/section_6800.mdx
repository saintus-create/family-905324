# FIN § 6800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 6. Multiple-Party Accounts

**Citation:** FIN § 6800

---

## Text

An account in an association or federal association that is a multiple-party account as defined in Section 5132 of the Probate Code is governed by Part 2 (commencing with Section 5100) of Division 5 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79, Sec. 6. Operative July 1, 1991, pursuant to Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 46.)
