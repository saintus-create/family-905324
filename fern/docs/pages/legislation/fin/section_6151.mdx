# FIN § 6151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 4. Directors

**Citation:** FIN § 6151

---

## Text

No person shall be eligible for election or shall serve as a director or officer of an association who has been convicted of a criminal offense involving dishonesty or a breach of trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
