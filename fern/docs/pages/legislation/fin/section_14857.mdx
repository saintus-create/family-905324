# FIN § 14857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14857

---

## Text

Every credit union may cancel the shares of any member who withdraws or is expelled, and apply the value of the shares to the liquidation of the member’s indebtedness to the credit union.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 112.)
