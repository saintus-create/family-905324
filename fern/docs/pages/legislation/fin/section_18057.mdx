# FIN § 18057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Advertising

**Citation:** FIN § 18057

---

## Text

An industrial loan company shall not use any advertising nor make any representations which indicate, imply or might lead a person to believe that the company is a savings association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 585. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
