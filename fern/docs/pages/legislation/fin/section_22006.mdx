# FIN § 22006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22006

---

## Text

As used in this division, the terms “security interest,” “accounts,” “chattel paper,” “documents,” “general intangibles,” “goods,” and “instruments” are as defined in the Uniform Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
