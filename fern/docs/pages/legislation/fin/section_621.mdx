# FIN § 621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 7. Liquidation and Conservation > Article: 2. Conservation or Liquidation of an Insured Licensee

**Citation:** FIN § 621

---

## Text

The Federal Insurance Agency may be, and act as, a conservator, liquidator, or receiver without bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
