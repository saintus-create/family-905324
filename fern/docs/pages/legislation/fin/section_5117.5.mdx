# FIN § 5117.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 1. Short Title, General Definitions, and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5117.5

---

## Text

“Savings institution” means a financial institution as defined in Section 5102.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 868, Sec. 2.5.)
