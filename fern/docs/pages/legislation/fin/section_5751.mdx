# FIN § 5751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 7. Power to Reorganize, Merge, Consolidate, or Transfer Assets

**Citation:** FIN § 5751

---

## Text

At the sole discretion of the commissioner a public hearing may be held on applications filed under this article, in accordance with procedures established by the commissioner by regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
