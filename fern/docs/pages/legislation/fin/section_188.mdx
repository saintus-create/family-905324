# FIN § 188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 188

---

## Text

“Money transmitter” means a person authorized pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 2030) of Division 1.2 to engage in the business of money transmission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 16. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
