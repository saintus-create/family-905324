# FIN § 18481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 7. Guarantee of Thrift Accounts > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18481

---

## Text

“Guaranteed investment certificate” means a thrift obligation guaranteed by Guaranty Corporation pursuant to Section 18523.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 142, Sec. 12. Effective July 1, 1985.)
