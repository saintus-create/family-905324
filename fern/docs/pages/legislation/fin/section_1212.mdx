# FIN § 1212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 6. Securities

**Citation:** FIN § 1212

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this chapter shall affect the Corporate Securities Law of 1968, Division 1 (commencing with Section 25000) of Title 4 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
