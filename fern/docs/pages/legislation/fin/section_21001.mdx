# FIN § 21001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 21001

---

## Text

“Compensation” includes expenses, interest, disbursements, storage charges, and all other charges of any nature in connection with a loan or forbearance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 364.)
