# FIN § 22325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 3. Loan Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 22325

---

## Text

Every licensee shall display prominently in each licensed place of business a full and accurate schedule of the charges to be made and the method of computing the charges. The schedule is subject to the approval of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
