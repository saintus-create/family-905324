# FIN § 454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 4. Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 454

---

## Text

The commissioner shall call for the report specified in Section 453 from all licensees at least four times each year upon dates selected by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
