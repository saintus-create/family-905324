# FIN § 18003.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18003.7

---

## Text

“Demand deposit” means investment or thrift certificates in account, passbook, or certificate form which are redeemable and payable upon demand to the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 345, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
