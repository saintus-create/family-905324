# FIN § 18425.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 5. Administration and Powers of the Commissioner > Article: 4. Possession, Conservatorship, and Liquidation by the Commissioner

**Citation:** FIN § 18425.11

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner determines to liquidate a company, the commissioner shall cause an inventory of all the assets of the company to be made in duplicate, file the original with the court, and file the duplicate in the commissioner’s office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 140, Sec. 1. Effective July 1, 1985.)
