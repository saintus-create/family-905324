# FIN § 32814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 7. Borrowing From the State > Article: 2. State Enterprise Loan Fund

**Citation:** FIN § 32814

---

## Text

The Controller shall deposit in the fund all moneys received from the corporation in repayment of the principal and interest of loans made to the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
