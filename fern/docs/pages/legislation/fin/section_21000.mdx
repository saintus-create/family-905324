# FIN § 21000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 21000

---

## Text

Every person engaged in the business of receiving goods, including motor vehicles, in pledge as security for a loan is a pawnbroker within the meaning of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 923, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
