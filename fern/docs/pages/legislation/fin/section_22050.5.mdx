# FIN § 22050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** FIN § 22050.5

---

## Text

This division does not apply to any person who makes no more than one loan in a 12-month period if that loan is a commercial loan as defined in Section 22502.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 16, Sec. 4. (SB 577) Effective April 28, 2022.)
