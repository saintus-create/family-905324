# FIN § 31400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Transaction of Business

**Citation:** FIN § 31400

---

## Text

(a) Each licensee shall transact its business in a safe and sound manner and shall maintain itself in a safe and sound condition.

(b) No licensee shall commit any unsafe or unsound act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
