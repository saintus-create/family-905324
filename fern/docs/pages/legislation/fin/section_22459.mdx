# FIN § 22459

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 2. Consumer Loans > Article: 5. Open-End Loan Programs

**Citation:** FIN § 22459

---

## Text

Subdivision (e) of Section 22337 shall not apply to an open-end loan that has no balance outstanding if the open-end loan agreement continues in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1115, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1115.)
