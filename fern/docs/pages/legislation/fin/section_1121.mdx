# FIN § 1121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 5. Corporate Requirements > Article: 2. Shares

**Citation:** FIN § 1121

---

## Text

No bank or trust company after October 1, 1949, shall issue any shares before they are fully paid for.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
