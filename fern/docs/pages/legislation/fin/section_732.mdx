# FIN § 732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 9. Approval of Names of Nonbank Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 732

---

## Text

The fee for filing with the commissioner an application for a certificate of approval of the subject name of a nonbank corporation shall be twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
