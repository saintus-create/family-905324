# FIN § 18213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 3. Loans and Purchased Obligations > Article: 2. Terms and Maximum Charges

**Citation:** FIN § 18213

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law not within this division, industrial loan companies making loans pursuant to this division may not precompute interest on such loans.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 270.)
