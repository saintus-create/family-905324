# FIN § 101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 101

---

## Text

If and to the extent that any provision of the Financial Institutions Law is preempted by federal law, the provision does not apply and shall not be enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 6. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
