# FIN § 14900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 2. Dividends

**Citation:** FIN § 14900

---

## Text

Dividends need not be paid on a share account having less than the minimum balance prescribed in the bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 267, Sec. 7. (AB 2862) Effective January 1, 2019.)
