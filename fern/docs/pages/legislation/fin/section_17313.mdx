# FIN § 17313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 2. Purpose: Scope of Guarantee

**Citation:** FIN § 17313

---

## Text

The commissioner shall review and approve the articles of incorporation and bylaws of Fidelity Corporation before they are filed with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1560, Sec. 11. Effective October 2, 1985.)
