# FIN § 6457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 6. Records

**Citation:** FIN § 6457

---

## Text

Each capital stock association shall maintain a register of stockholders.
