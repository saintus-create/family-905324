# FIN § 16510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 12. Foreign (Other Nation) Credit Unions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 16510

---

## Text

Whenever a foreign (other nation) credit union is licensed to establish more than one office, it shall designate one of its offices as its primary office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 612, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
