# FIN § 18625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 8. Insurance Premium Financing > Article: 4. Limitation on Finance Charges

**Citation:** FIN § 18625

---

## Text

A premium finance agency shall not, except as otherwise provided by law, impose, take, receive, reserve or charge a finance charge which in the aggregate is greater than that which is permitted by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 964.)
