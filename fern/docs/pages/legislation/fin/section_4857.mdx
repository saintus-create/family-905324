# FIN § 4857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 2. Sale of Whole Business Unit to California State Depository Corporation or California State-Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank

**Citation:** FIN § 4857

---

## Text

After an application for approval of a sale has been approved and all conditions precedent to the sale have been fulfilled, the commissioner shall approve the agreement of sale and endorse the approval on the original or a copy of the agreement of sale, and at that time the sale shall become effective for all purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 493.1. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
