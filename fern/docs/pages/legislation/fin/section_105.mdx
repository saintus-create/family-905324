# FIN § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 105

---

## Text

Banks are divided into the following classes:

(a) Commercial banks.

(b) Industrial banks.

(c) Trust companies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
