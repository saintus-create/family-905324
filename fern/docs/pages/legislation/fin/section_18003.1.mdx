# FIN § 18003.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. INDUSTRIAL LOAN COMPANIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 18003.1

---

## Text

“Investment and loan” means an industrial loan company.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 623, Sec. 2.)
