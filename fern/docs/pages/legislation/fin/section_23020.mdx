# FIN § 23020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Administration and Operations

**Citation:** FIN § 23020

---

## Text

A licensee shall maintain only one place of business under an original or amended license issued pursuant to Section 23008. The commissioner may issue more than one license to the same licensee upon compliance with all the provisions of this division governing an original issuance of a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
