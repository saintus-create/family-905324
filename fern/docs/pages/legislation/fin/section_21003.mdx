# FIN § 21003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PAWNBROKERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 21003

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, the term “month” means a period of time consisting of 30 consecutive calendar days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 318, Sec. 1. (AB 424) Effective January 1, 2012.)
