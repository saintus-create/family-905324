# FIN § 32402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 3. Corporate Finances > Article: 1. Corporate Accounts

**Citation:** FIN § 32402

---

## Text

In addition to other accounts the board may establish, the financial accounts of the corporation shall include:

(a) A general operating account.

(b) A general reserve account.

(c) An enterprise loan fund.

(d) A government guaranteed securities trust fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1040, Sec. 15.)
