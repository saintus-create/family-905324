# FIN § 50004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 50004

---

## Text

“Fraud,” “deceit,” and “defraud” are not limited to common law fraud or deceit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 994, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995. Section operative January 1, 1996, pursuant to Section 50601 (later repealed).)
