# FIN § 6951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 9. Accounts of Deceased Nonresidents

**Citation:** FIN § 6951

---

## Text

No action at law or in equity shall be maintained against an association or federal association for payment made in accordance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 958, Sec. 12.)
