# FIN § 7457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 6. Investment Operations > Article: 5. Investment in Loans

**Citation:** FIN § 7457

---

## Text

An association may make loans on the security of its savings accounts, whether or not the borrower is the owner of the account, subject to the limitations of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 868, Sec. 11.2.)
