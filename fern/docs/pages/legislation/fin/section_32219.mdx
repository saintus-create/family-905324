# FIN § 32219

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. STATE ASSISTANCE FUND FOR ENTERPRISE, BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 32219

---

## Text

“Special projects” means any financing assistance or other program undertaken by the corporation pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 32720) of Chapter 6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 819, Sec. 1.)
