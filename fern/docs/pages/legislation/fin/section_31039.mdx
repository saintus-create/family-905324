# FIN § 31039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 31039

---

## Text

“Controlling person”, when used with respect to a specified person, means any person who controls such specified person, directly or indirectly through one or more intermediaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
