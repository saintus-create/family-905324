# FIN § 1366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 10. Restrictions and Prohibited Practices > Article: 2. Loans to Insiders

**Citation:** FIN § 1366

---

## Text

A bank may make a loan, otherwise complying with the provisions of this division, for the benefit of a trust, notwithstanding that the bank or any one or more executive officers or directors of the bank are trustees of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
