# FIN § 5811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 2. Corporate Organization and Corporate Changes > Article: 8. Holding Companies and Stock Acquisitions

**Citation:** FIN § 5811

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to an acquisition of stock made pursuant to a plan or agreement of reorganization, merger, consolidation, or transfer of assets under Section 5750.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
