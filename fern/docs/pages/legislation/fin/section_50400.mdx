# FIN § 50400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING ACT > Chapter: 6. Assessments

**Citation:** FIN § 50400

---

## Text

The commissioner has the authority to levy assessments for the support of this part as provided for in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 994, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995. Section operative January 1, 1996, pursuant to Section 50601 (later repealed).)
