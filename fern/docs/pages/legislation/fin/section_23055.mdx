# FIN § 23055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CALIFORNIA DEFERRED DEPOSIT TRANSACTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 1. Administrative Actions

**Citation:** FIN § 23055

---

## Text

The proceedings under this article shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, and in all cases the commissioner has all the powers granted therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
