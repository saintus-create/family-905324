# FIN § 6453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 3. Corporate Administration > Article: 6. Records

**Citation:** FIN § 6453

---

## Text

Each association shall close its books at the close of business at least annually, or more often if authorized or required in writing by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 361, Sec. 8.)
