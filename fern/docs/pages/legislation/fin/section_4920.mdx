# FIN § 4920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 1. Conversion of California State Depository Corporation Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4920

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “conversion” means any of the conversions described in Section 4921.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 522. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
