# FIN § 4895.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 2. Merger Into California State-Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank

**Citation:** FIN § 4895.05

---

## Text

(a) A merger shall not become effective unless it has been approved by the commissioner.

(b) After an application for approval of a merger has been approved and all conditions precedent to the merger have been fulfilled, the commissioner shall approve the merger.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 520.1. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
