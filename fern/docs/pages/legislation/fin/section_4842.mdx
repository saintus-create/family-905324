# FIN § 4842

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 4842

---

## Text

If a trust is transferred under this chapter, the transfer shall be good cause for removal of the trustee under the Trust Law (Division 9 (commencing with Section 15000) of the Probate Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 126. Effective October 2, 1995.)
