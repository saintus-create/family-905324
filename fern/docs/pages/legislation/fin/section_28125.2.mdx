# FIN § 28125.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. STUDENT LOAN SERVICING ACT > Chapter: 2. Licensing > Article: 3. Nationwide Multistate Licensing System and Registry

**Citation:** FIN § 28125.2

---

## Text

The commissioner shall report regularly violations of this division, as well as enforcement actions and other relevant information, to the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System & Registry, to the extent that the information is a public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 379, Sec. 12. (AB 38) Effective January 1, 2019.)
