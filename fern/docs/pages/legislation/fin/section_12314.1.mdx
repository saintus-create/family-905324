# FIN § 12314.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CHECK SELLERS, BILL PAYERS, AND PRORATERS > Chapter: 4. Licensee Regulations

**Citation:** FIN § 12314.1

---

## Text

A cancellation fee or termination penalty may not be charged to a debtor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 999.)
