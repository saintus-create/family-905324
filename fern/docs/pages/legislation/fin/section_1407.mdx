# FIN § 1407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 12. Deposits > Article: 1. Depositors

**Citation:** FIN § 1407

---

## Text

An overdraft of more than 90 days standing shall not be allowed as an asset of any bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
