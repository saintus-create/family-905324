# FIN § 6601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 1. Savings Liability

**Citation:** FIN § 6601

---

## Text

Except as limited by the association from time to time or by state or federal law or regulations, account holders may make additions to their savings accounts in amounts and at times they elect.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
