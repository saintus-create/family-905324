# FIN § 17313.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Escrow Agents’ Fidelity Corporation > Article: 2. Purpose: Scope of Guarantee

**Citation:** FIN § 17313.1

---

## Text

The fiscal year of Fidelity Corporation shall commence on July 1 of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1458, Sec. 5. Effective September 28, 1988.)
