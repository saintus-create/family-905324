# FIN § 17609.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ESCROW AGENTS > Chapter: 4. Revocation of License

**Citation:** FIN § 17609.1

---

## Text

The power of investigation and examination by the commissioner is not terminated by the surrender, suspension, or revocation of any license issued by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 705.)
