# FIN § 1850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 21. International and Foreign Banking and Financing > Article: 1. International and Foreign Banking and Financing Corporations

**Citation:** FIN § 1850

---

## Text

As used in this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “corporation” means a corporation organized under the laws of this state for the purpose of transacting business pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
