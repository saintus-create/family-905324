# FIN § 14861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 6. Share Accounts and Certificates for Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FIN § 14861

---

## Text

No credit union shall issue shares to anyone not qualified for membership under its bylaws, except shares issued in coownership as provided in Section 14851.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 52, Sec. 9.)
