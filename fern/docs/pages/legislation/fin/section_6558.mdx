# FIN § 6558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers of Associations > Article: 2. Branching and Agency Powers

**Citation:** FIN § 6558

---

## Text

The commissioner may adopt rules and regulations relating to any action pertaining to branch offices and agencies regulated by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
