# FIN § 31304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Offices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 31304

---

## Text

Each licensee shall maintain telephone service at each of its offices and shall maintain a telephone listing under its corporate name for each of its offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
