# FIN § 4877.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 4. Certain Sales of Partial Business Units

**Citation:** FIN § 4877.05

---

## Text

A sale shall have the same effect as provided in Sections 4879.14 and 4879.15 in the case of a sale of the type defined in Section 4879.01.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 480, Sec. 154. Effective October 2, 1995.)
