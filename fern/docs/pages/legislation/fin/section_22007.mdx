# FIN § 22007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALIFORNIA FINANCING LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FIN § 22007

---

## Text

(a) “Licensee” means any finance lender, broker, or program administrator who receives a license in accordance with this division.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2019.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 8) and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 9. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2019, by its own provisions.)
