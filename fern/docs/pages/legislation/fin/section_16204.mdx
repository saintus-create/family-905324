# FIN § 16204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CREDIT UNIONS > Chapter: 11. Foreign (Other State) Credit Unions > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** FIN § 16204

---

## Text

Any foreign (other state) credit union whose license to maintain an office is suspended or revoked shall immediately surrender the license to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 612, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
