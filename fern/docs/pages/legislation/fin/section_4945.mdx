# FIN § 4945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 5. Conversion > Article: 2. Conversion of Federal Depository Corporation Into California State Depository Corporation

**Citation:** FIN § 4945

---

## Text

If the commissioner finds all of the factors set forth in Section 4925 with respect to an application for approval of a conversion, the commissioner shall approve the application. If the commissioner finds otherwise, the commissioner shall deny the application for approval of the conversion.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 537. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
