# FIN § 4879.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 5. Other Sales of Partial Business Units

**Citation:** FIN § 4879.01

---

## Text

In this article, unless the context otherwise requires, “sale” means any of the sales described in Section 4879.02.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1064, Sec. 500. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative July 1, 1997.)
