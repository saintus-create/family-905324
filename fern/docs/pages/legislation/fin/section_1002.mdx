# FIN § 1002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.1. BANKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FIN § 1002

---

## Text

No bank shall be a close corporation (as defined in Section 158 of the Corporations Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 243, Sec. 3. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2012.)
