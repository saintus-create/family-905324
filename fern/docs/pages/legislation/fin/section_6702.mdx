# FIN § 6702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SAVINGS ASSOCIATION LAW > Chapter: 5. Savings Operations > Article: 3. Contracts for Savings Programs

**Citation:** FIN § 6702

---

## Text

An association may pay commissions or fees in cash or merchandise for soliciting deposits to any employee of the association or to any broker or agent as determined by the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1091, Sec. 2.)
