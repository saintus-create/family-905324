# FIN § 4877.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 3. Sale > Article: 4. Certain Sales of Partial Business Units

**Citation:** FIN § 4877.03

---

## Text

No provision of Division 1.1 (commencing with Section 1000), except the provisions of Chapter 19 (commencing with Section 1670) of Division 1.1, prohibits or restricts a sale in a case where the seller is a California state bank or a California industrial loan company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 334, Sec. 59. (SB 537) Effective January 1, 2014.)
