# FIN § 4895.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.6. DEPOSITORY CORPORATIONS—SALE, MERGER, AND CONVERSION > Chapter: 4. Merger > Article: 2. Merger Into California State-Licensed Foreign (Other Nation) Bank

**Citation:** FIN § 4895.06

---

## Text

A merger is subject to the provisions of Sections 4884 to 4885, inclusive, and 4888 to 4891, inclusive, as if the merger were a merger of the type defined in Section 4880.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 43. Effective September 25, 1996.)
