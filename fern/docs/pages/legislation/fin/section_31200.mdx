# FIN § 31200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Corporate Matters > Article: 1. Name

**Citation:** FIN § 31200

---

## Text

The corporate name of each licensee shall include the phrase “California business and industrial development corporation”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1164.)
