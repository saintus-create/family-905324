# SHC § 8774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 12. REDEMPTION FUND DEFICIENCIES > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8774

---

## Text

The legislative body may hold supplemental hearings on like notice and such other and supplemental orders may be made from time to time as may be equitable and proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
