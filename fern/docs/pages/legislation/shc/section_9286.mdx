# SHC § 9286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 8. Payment and Collection of Reassessments > Article: 5. Payment in Installments

**Citation:** SHC § 9286

---

## Text

For each year the interest shall be computed and collected up to the next second day of July succeeding, no deduction being made by reason of any installment of such reassessment being due or paid prior thereto in such year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
