# SHC § 9452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 12. Advance Retirement of Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9452

---

## Text

The proposals for the sale of bonds shall be opened in public at the time and place specified in the notice. The legislative body in its discretion may reject any or all of the proposals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
