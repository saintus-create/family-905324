# SHC § 31789

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 8. Board of Parking Place Commissioners

**Citation:** SHC § 31789

---

## Text

An operator shall not conduct any business other than that of the operation of public parking facilities on any parking place of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
