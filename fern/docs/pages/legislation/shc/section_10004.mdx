# SHC § 10004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 10004

---

## Text

“Legislative body” includes any body which by law is the legislative department of government of the city, or municipality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
