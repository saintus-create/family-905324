# SHC § 416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 416

---

## Text

Route 116 is from:

(a) Route 1 near Jenner to Route 101 near Cotati.

(b) Route 101 near Petaluma to Route 121 near Schellville.
