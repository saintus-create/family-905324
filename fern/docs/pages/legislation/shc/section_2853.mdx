# SHC § 2853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 3. HEARING UPON THE REPORT

**Citation:** SHC § 2853

---

## Text

The notice of the hearing must be mailed at least 30 days prior to the date of the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1194, Sec. 6. Effective October 11, 1993.)
