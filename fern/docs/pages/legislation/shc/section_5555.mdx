# SHC § 5555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 20. Reassessments to Relieve Owners

**Citation:** SHC § 5555

---

## Text

The notice shall contain a reference to the original assessment and diagram and reference shall also be made to the reassessment and diagram of the street superintendent for full particulars, as to the extent of the district affected by these proceedings and for all other particulars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
