# SHC § 26203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Levy and Collection of Taxes

**Citation:** SHC § 26203

---

## Text

All money raised by taxation which is authorized by this part shall belong to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 285.)
