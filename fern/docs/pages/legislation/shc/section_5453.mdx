# SHC § 5453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 18.1. Collecting the Assessment on the Tax Roll

**Citation:** SHC § 5453

---

## Text

Said officer shall extend upon such roll the total amounts of such assessments and interest.

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 65.)
