# SHC § 5330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 14.5. Assessments of Indian-Owned Property

**Citation:** SHC § 5330

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, the provisions of this chapter shall apply to, and govern the assessment of, Indian-owned property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 970.)
