# SHC § 27532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 18. Golden Gate Bridge, Highway and Transportation District > Article: 2. Planning

**Citation:** SHC § 27532

---

## Text

In developing the transportation facilities plan, the district shall hold public hearings so that interested persons and organizations are provided an opportunity to express their viewpoints.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 678.)
