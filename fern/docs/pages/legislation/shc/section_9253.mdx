# SHC § 9253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 8. Payment and Collection of Reassessments > Article: 1. Reassessment Records

**Citation:** SHC § 9253

---

## Text

The percentages when collected shall cover the expenses and compensation of the treasurer incurred in the collection of such reassessments, and of the interest and penalties thereon. No other percentage shall be claimed for any such collections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
