# SHC § 9227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 7. Recordation of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9227

---

## Text

Assessments originally levied, and all penalties and interest accrued thereon, shall be deemed superseded and supplanted by the reassessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
