# SHC § 100.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 100.22

---

## Text

The city council or board of supervisors shall, prior to entering into the agreement contemplated by Section 100.2, conduct a public hearing on the subject.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 515.)
