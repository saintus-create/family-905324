# SHC § 36509.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36509.5

---

## Text

“Clerk” means clerk of the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 37. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
