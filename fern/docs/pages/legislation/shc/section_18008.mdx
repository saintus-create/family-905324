# SHC § 18008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18008

---

## Text

“Assessment district” means the territory to be benefited by the proposed improvement and to be assessed to pay the costs and expenses thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
