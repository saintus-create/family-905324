# SHC § 36638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 36638

---

## Text

(a) A business assessment pursuant to this part is a fee imposed by a government on the transaction for purposes of paragraph (29) of subdivision (a) of Section 1770 of the Civil Code.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 400, Sec. 9. (SB 478) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative July 1, 2024, by its own provisions.)
