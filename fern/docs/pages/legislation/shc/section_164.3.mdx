# SHC § 164.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 4.3. Transportation Funding Plan

**Citation:** SHC § 164.3

---

## Text

The interregional road system shall include, and shall be limited to, those routes that are specified in Sections 164.10 to 164.20, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 622, Sec. 44. Effective January 1, 1998.)
