# SHC § 31111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 2. Organization of the Authority

**Citation:** SHC § 31111

---

## Text

All of the members of the authority shall serve thereon without compensation, but they shall receive their necessary actual traveling expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
