# SHC § 862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Highway Proceedings Affecting Private Property > Article: 2. Establishment of Boundaries

**Citation:** SHC § 862

---

## Text

The department may proceed as provided in this article to establish the boundaries of any State highway right of way where such boundaries are unknown or uncertain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 689.)
