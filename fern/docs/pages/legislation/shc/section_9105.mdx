# SHC § 9105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 1. Computing the Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9105

---

## Text

Tax-deeded land shall be subject to reassessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
