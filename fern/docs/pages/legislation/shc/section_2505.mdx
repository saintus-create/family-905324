# SHC § 2505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 11. Federal Aid for Rural Highway Public Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 2505

---

## Text

The department shall establish operating procedures and take such other appropriate actions to comply with the provisions of this chapter and with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 781.)
