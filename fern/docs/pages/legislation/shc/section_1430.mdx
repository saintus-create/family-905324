# SHC § 1430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 5. County Bridges and Subways > Article: 4. Subways

**Citation:** SHC § 1430

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “subway” includes tube or tunnel.
