# SHC § 8309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 8309

---

## Text

“Vacation” means the complete or partial abandonment or termination of the public right to use a street, highway, or public service easement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
