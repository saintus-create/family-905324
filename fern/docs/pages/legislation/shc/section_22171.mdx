# SHC § 22171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 7. Sale and Redemption of Delinquent Property

**Citation:** SHC § 22171

---

## Text

The deed shall recite substantially the matters contained in the certificate of sale, any change of interest therein, and the fact that no person has redeemed the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
