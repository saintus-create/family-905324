# SHC § 189.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 5. Funds for Highway and Public Mass Transit Guideway Purposes

**Citation:** SHC § 189.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, State Transportation District 12, consisting of the County of Orange, is hereby created. The district shall have a separate district organization, staff, and facilities in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1050, Sec. 1. Effective September 24, 1987.)
