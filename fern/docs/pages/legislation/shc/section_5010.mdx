# SHC § 5010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 5010

---

## Text

“Council chambers” refers to the place where the regular meetings of the legislative body of the county or city are held.
