# SHC § 2220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 5.1. Combined Road Plan

**Citation:** SHC § 2220

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Federal-Aid Combined Road Plan Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 647, Sec. 2. Effective September 11, 1990. Inoperative upon determination described in Section 2227. Repealed on January 1 after inoperative date, as provided in Section 2227.)
