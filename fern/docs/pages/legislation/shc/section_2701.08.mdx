# SHC § 2701.08

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 17. Passenger Rail and Clean Air Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 2. Transportation Improvement Program

**Citation:** SHC § 2701.08

---

## Text

At least 15 percent of the money in the fund shall be used for intercity rail purposes and shall be equitably expended on intercity rail corridors based on the relative population served by each corridor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 108, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 108 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
