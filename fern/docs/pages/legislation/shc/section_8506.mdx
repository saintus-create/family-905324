# SHC § 8506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 8506

---

## Text

“Treasurer” means:

(a) When used with reference to a county, the county treasurer.

(b) When used with reference to a city, the city treasurer.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
