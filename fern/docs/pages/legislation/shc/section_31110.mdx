# SHC § 31110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 2. Organization of the Authority

**Citation:** SHC § 31110

---

## Text

The El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority is hereby created. The authority shall be governed by a board consisting of the members of the Board of Supervisors of El Dorado County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
