# SHC § 8303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 8303

---

## Text

“Clerk” includes a person or officer who is the clerk of a legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
