# SHC § 35507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 3. Bonds for Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35507

---

## Text

Upon the award of the bonds to a purchaser, the treasurer shall issue bonds representing the liens of the unpaid assessments as shown on the list and bearing interest at the rate fixed by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
