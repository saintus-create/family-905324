# SHC § 2203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 5. Federal Aid for Secondary Highways

**Citation:** SHC § 2203

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “county highway” means a county highway, as defined by Section 25, which is on the system of secondary and feeder roads as defined by Section 103(c) of Title 23 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 580. Inoperative on date prescribed in Section 2202. Repealed on January 1 after inoperative date, pursuant to Section 2202.)
