# SHC § 30780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 5. San Francisco-Oakland Rapid Transit Tube

**Citation:** SHC § 30780

---

## Text

The rapid transit tube shall be repaired, maintained and operated, and insured against such risks and in such amount as the district deems proper by or at the expense of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1755.)
