# SHC § 1401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 5. County Bridges and Subways > Article: 3. Joint Bridges

**Citation:** SHC § 1401

---

## Text

Any work done pursuant to such an agreement is exempt from any provisions of law regarding the letting of contracts by counties for the performance of any work upon bridges.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
