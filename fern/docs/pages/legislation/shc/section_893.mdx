# SHC § 893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 4. Electric Bicycle Safety and Training

**Citation:** SHC § 893

---

## Text

As used in this article, “electric bicycle” has the meaning provided in Section 312.5 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 147, Sec. 1. (AB 1946) Effective January 1, 2023.)
