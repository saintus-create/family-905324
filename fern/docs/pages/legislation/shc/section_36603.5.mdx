# SHC § 36603.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Declarations

**Citation:** SHC § 36603.5

---

## Text

Any provision of this part that conflicts with any other provision of law shall prevail over the other provision of law, as to districts created under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 3. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
