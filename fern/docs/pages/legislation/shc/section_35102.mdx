# SHC § 35102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 35102

---

## Text

“Legislative body” means the legislative body of the city in which the district is located.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
