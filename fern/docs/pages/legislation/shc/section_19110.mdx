# SHC § 19110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 6. Establishing the District

**Citation:** SHC § 19110

---

## Text

If a majority of the votes cast at the election is in favor of a lighting district, the board of supervisors shall by resolution establish the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
