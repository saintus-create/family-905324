# SHC § 35548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35548

---

## Text

The reassessment shall be collected, paid, and enforced in the same manner as an original assessment, and shall have the same weight in evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
