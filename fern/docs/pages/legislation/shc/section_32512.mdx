# SHC § 32512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 32512

---

## Text

“State public body” means the State, or any city, city and county, county, district, authority, or any other subdivision or public body of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
