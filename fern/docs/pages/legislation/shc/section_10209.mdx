# SHC § 10209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 3. Resolution of Intention and Report

**Citation:** SHC § 10209

---

## Text

In the assessment of land, credit may be given for dedications and for improvements constructed at private expense.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 739.)
