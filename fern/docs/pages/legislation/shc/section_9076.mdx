# SHC § 9076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 2. Preliminary Proceedings > Article: 3. Nonconsenting Bondholders

**Citation:** SHC § 9076

---

## Text

The reassessment shall not be recorded until adequate provision has been made in accordance with the terms of this article for the payment or retirement of bonds of nonconsenting holders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
