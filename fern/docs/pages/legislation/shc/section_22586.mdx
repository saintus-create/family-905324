# SHC § 22586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 2. Organization of the Assessment District; Levy of First Assessment > Article: 1. Formation of an Assessment District

**Citation:** SHC § 22586

---

## Text

Upon completion, the engineer shall file the report with the clerk for submission to the legislative body. The legislative body may approve the report, as filed, or it may modify the report in any particular and approve it as modified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
