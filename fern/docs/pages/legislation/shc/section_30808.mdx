# SHC § 30808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 1. Franchises

**Citation:** SHC § 30808

---

## Text

All money received from any franchise granted by the department pursuant to this article shall be deposited in the State Highway Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 777, Sec. 245.)
