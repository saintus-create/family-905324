# SHC § 308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 308

---

## Text

Route 8 is from:

(a) Sunset Cliffs Boulevard to Route 5 in San Diego.

(b) Route 5 in San Diego to Yuma via El Centro.
