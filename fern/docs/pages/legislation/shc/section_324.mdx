# SHC § 324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 324

---

## Text

Route 24 is from:

(a) Route 580 in Oakland to Route 680 in Walnut Creek.

(b) Route 680 in Walnut Creek to Route 4 near Pittsburg.
