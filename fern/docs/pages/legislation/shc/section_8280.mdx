# SHC § 8280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 14. Annexation of Territory

**Citation:** SHC § 8280

---

## Text

Any territory which will not be benefited by inclusion in the district shall not be included in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1230.)
