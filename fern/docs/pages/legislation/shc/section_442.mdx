# SHC § 442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 442

---

## Text

Route 142 is from:

(a) Route 90 near Brea to Route 71 near Chino.

(b) Route 71 near Chino to Route 210 near Upland.
