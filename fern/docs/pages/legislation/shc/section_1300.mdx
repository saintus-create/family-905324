# SHC § 1300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 5. County Bridges and Subways > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 1300

---

## Text

No tolls shall ever be charged for crossing any bridge constructed under the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
