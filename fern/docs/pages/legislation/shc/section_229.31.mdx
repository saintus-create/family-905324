# SHC § 229.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1.5. Tourist Oriented Directional Signs > Article: 3. Eligibility

**Citation:** SHC § 229.31

---

## Text

No business or attraction shall be eligible for a generic tourist oriented directional sign if it is adjacent to, and visible from, the highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
