# SHC § 30685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 3. San Pedro-Terminal Island Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30685

---

## Text

The commission shall transfer fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000) to the Toll Bridge Seismic Retrofit Account in the State Transportation Fund from funds in the Vincent Thomas Toll Bridge Revenue Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 327, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1998.)
