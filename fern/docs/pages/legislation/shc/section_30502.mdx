# SHC § 30502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 10. Local Cooperation

**Citation:** SHC § 30502

---

## Text

Money or property advanced or contributed pursuant to this article may be immediately transferred or delivered to the department or to the commission to be used for the purpose for which the advance or contribution was made.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 777, Sec. 201.)
