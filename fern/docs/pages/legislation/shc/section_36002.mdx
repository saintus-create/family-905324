# SHC § 36002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 36002

---

## Text

“Business” as used in this part means all types of business, including professions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 241.)
