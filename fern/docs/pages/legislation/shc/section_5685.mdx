# SHC § 5685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 25. Duties of Officers > Article: 1. The Superintendent of Streets

**Citation:** SHC § 5685

---

## Text

The superintendent of streets shall keep a record of the service of all notices which are served by him or with his permission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
