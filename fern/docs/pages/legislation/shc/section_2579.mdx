# SHC § 2579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 15.5. Vanpool Financing

**Citation:** SHC § 2579

---

## Text

A vanpool operator is not eligible for any California income tax deduction or credit applicable to vanpooling with respect to the amount of any grant under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 799, Sec. 2. Effective September 26, 1989.)
