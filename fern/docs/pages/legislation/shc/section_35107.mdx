# SHC § 35107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 35107

---

## Text

Any portion of a city may be formed into a parking district for the purposes set forth under this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
