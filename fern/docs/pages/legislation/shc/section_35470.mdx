# SHC § 35470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 2. Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35470

---

## Text

After the assessment roll is recorded, all persons are deemed to have notice of its contents.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
