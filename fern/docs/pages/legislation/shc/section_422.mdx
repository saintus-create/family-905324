# SHC § 422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 422

---

## Text

Route 122 is from:

(a) Route 14 south of Palmdale to Route 138.

(b) Route 138 to Route 48.

(c) Route 48 northeasterly to Route 58.
