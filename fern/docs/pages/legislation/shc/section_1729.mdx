# SHC § 1729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 9. Cooperation by Counties > Article: 5. County Highways Within Cities

**Citation:** SHC § 1729

---

## Text

The police power of a city with reference to a county highway within the city shall not be limited except as to those matters specifically provided for in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 876.)
