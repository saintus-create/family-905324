# SHC § 332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 332

---

## Text

Route 32 is from:

(a) Route 5 near Orland to Route 99 near Chico.

(b) Route 99 near Chico to Route 36.
