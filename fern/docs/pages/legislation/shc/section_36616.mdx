# SHC § 36616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36616

---

## Text

“Tenant” means an occupant pursuant to a lease of commercial space or a dwelling unit, other than an owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
