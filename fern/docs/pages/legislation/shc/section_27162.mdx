# SHC § 27162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. District Powers

**Citation:** SHC § 27162

---

## Text

The district may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
