# SHC § 114.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 114.5

---

## Text

The department shall not charge any self-help counties with countywide sales tax measures dedicated to transportation improvements more than 10 percent for administration indirect cost recovery. The department shall charge those self-help counties for functional overhead.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 54, Sec. 11. (SB 125) Effective July 10, 2023.)
