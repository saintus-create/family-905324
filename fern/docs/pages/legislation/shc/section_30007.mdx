# SHC § 30007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 30007

---

## Text

“Bond” means any written evidence of indebtedness which the authority may issue pursuant to this chapter in order to secure funds with which to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
