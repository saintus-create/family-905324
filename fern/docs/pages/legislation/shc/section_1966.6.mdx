# SHC § 1966.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. CITY STREETS > Chapter: 8. Neighborhood Electric Vehicle Transportation Plans > Article: 2. County of San Diego Neighborhood Electric Vehicle Transportation Plans

**Citation:** SHC § 1966.6

---

## Text

If the County of San Diego or any city in the county adopts an NEV transportation plan, it may acquire, by dedication, purchase, or condemnation, real property, including easements or rights-of-way, to establish NEV routes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 564, Sec. 4. (SB 1151) Effective January 1, 2019. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 1966.8.)
