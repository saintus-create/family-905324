# SHC § 541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 541

---

## Text

Route 241 is from Route 5 south of San Clemente to Route 91 in the City of Anaheim.
