# SHC § 30840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 2. Regulations

**Citation:** SHC § 30840

---

## Text

No person shall construct or take tolls on a bridge or ferry unless authorized by the governmental agency which has or had power to issue a franchise therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
