# SHC § 6490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 5.5. Alternative Procedure for Division of Land and Bond

**Citation:** SHC § 6490

---

## Text

This chapter provides an alternative to the procedure for division of land and bond set out in Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 6480).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 874, Sec. 1. Effective September 17, 1986.)
