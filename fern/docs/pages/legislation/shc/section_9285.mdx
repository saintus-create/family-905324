# SHC § 9285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 8. Payment and Collection of Reassessments > Article: 5. Payment in Installments

**Citation:** SHC § 9285

---

## Text

Reassessments shall bear interest from the date of recording of the reassessment at the rate or rates stated in the refunding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
