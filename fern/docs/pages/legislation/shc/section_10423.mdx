# SHC § 10423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 5. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 10423

---

## Text

The deed of the tax collector is prima facie evidence of the truth of all the matters which it recites, and of the regularity of all proceedings prior to the execution of the deed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
