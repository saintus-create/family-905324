# SHC § 9406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9406

---

## Text

The last maturity of any refunding bonds shall not exceed 19 years from the second day of July next succeeding 10 months after the date of said bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
