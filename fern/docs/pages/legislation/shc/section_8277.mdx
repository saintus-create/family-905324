# SHC § 8277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 14. Annexation of Territory

**Citation:** SHC § 8277

---

## Text

The notice shall contain a description of the territory proposed to be annexed and the time and place at which objections to the annexation will be heard.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1230.)
