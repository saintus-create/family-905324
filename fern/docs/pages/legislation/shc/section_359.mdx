# SHC § 359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 359

---

## Text

Route 59 is from:

(a) Route 152 northerly to Route 99 near Merced.

(b) Route 99 near Merced to Snelling.
