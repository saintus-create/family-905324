# SHC § 357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 357

---

## Text

Route 57 is from:

(a) Route 1 near Huntington Beach to Route 22 near Santa Ana.

(b) Route 5 near Santa Ana to Route 210 near San Dimas.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 221, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
