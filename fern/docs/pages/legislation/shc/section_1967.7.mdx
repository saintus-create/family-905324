# SHC § 1967.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. CITY STREETS > Chapter: 9. Treasure Island Transportation Management

**Citation:** SHC § 1967.7

---

## Text

Meetings of the transportation management agency shall be held pursuant to the Ralph M. Brown Act (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 317, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
