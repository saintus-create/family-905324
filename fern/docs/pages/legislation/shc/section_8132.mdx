# SHC § 8132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 3. The Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 8132

---

## Text

Each commissioner shall serve until the appointment and qualification of his successor. Vacancies shall be filled for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1147.)
