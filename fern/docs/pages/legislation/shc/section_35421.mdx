# SHC § 35421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Bonds, Acquisitions and Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 35421

---

## Text

If satisfactory bids are received, the bonds offered for sale shall be awarded to the highest responsible bidder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
