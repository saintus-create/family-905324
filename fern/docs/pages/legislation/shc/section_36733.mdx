# SHC § 36733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 36733

---

## Text

The city council may execute baseline service contracts that would establish levels of city services that would continue after a district has been formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 22, Sec. 191. Effective January 1, 2006.)
