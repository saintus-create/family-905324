# SHC § 2206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 5. Federal Aid for Secondary Highways

**Citation:** SHC § 2206

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “match” means to provide for the payment of the cost of any project to the extent that such cost is not to be paid from federal funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192. Inoperative on date prescribed in Section 2202. Repealed on January 1 after inoperative date, pursuant to Section 2202.)
