# SHC § 22161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 7. Sale and Redemption of Delinquent Property

**Citation:** SHC § 22161

---

## Text

The date fixed for the sale shall be not less than five days, nor more than 10 days after either the last publication or the completion of posting of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
