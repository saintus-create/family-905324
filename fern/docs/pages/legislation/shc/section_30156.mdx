# SHC § 30156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties of the Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 30156

---

## Text

The department may designate and adopt as a state highway any county road or portion thereof connecting a state highway with a publicly owned bridge or publicly owned toll bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
