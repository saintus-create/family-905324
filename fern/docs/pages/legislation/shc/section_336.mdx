# SHC § 336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 336

---

## Text

Route 36 is from Route 101 near Alton to Route 395 near Johnsonville passing near Forest Glen via Red Bluff and Mineral, via the vicinity of Morgan Summit, and via Susanville.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 877, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1999.)
