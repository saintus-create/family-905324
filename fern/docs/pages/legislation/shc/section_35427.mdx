# SHC § 35427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Bonds, Acquisitions and Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 35427

---

## Text

All contracts for the construction of any improvements shall be let and entered into as other contracts are let and entered into by the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
