# SHC § 30103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties of the Commission

## Text

Toll charges so fixed may be changed from time to time as conditions warrant.
