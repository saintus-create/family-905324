# SHC § 32513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 32513

---

## Text

“Project” means any acquisition, improvement, construction, or undertaking of any kind authorized by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
