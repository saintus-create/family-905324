# SHC § 8621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 5. ISSUANCE OF BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8621

---

## Text

The treasurer shall determine the assessments which are unpaid and the aggregate amount thereof.
