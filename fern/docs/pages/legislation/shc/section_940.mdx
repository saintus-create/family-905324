# SHC § 940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Boards of Supervisors

**Citation:** SHC § 940

---

## Text

Boards of supervisors shall have general supervision, management, and control of the county highways.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
