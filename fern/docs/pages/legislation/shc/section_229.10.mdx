# SHC § 229.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1.5. Tourist Oriented Directional Signs > Article: 1. Scope

**Citation:** SHC § 229.10

---

## Text

This chapter governs the administration, standards, eligibility, and fees concerning the tourist oriented directional sign program.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
