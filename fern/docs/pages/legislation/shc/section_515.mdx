# SHC § 515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 515

---

## Text

Route 215 is from Route 15 near Temecula to Route 15 near Devore via Riverside and San Bernardino.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1220, Sec. 45. Effective September 30, 1994.)
