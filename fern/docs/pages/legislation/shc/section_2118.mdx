# SHC § 2118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 3. Highway Users Tax Account

**Citation:** SHC § 2118

---

## Text

When the State Controller determines it to be necessary, he may require a county or city to deposit money received from the Highway Users Tax Fund in a separate bank account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1060.)
