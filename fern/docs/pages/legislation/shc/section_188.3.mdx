# SHC § 188.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 5. Funds for Highway and Public Mass Transit Guideway Purposes

**Citation:** SHC § 188.3

---

## Text

The cost of maintenance of all toll bridges under the jurisdiction of the commission shall be paid out of money in the State Highway Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
