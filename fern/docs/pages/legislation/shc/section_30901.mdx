# SHC § 30901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 4. Toll Ferries in Cities

**Citation:** SHC § 30901

---

## Text

In granting any such franchise, privilege, or license, the legislative body may require the grantee thereof to pay an annual fee either in a flat amount or a percentage of the gross receipts derived from the operation of such toll ferry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 658.)
