# SHC § 5683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 25. Duties of Officers > Article: 1. The Superintendent of Streets

**Citation:** SHC § 5683

---

## Text

The superintendent of streets shall superintend and direct the cleaning of all sewers, and the expense of the same shall be paid out of the street or sewer fund of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
