# SHC § 22094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 4. Undertaking the Improvement

**Citation:** SHC § 22094

---

## Text

After the hearing, the city forester, under the direction and supervision of the board, shall abandon the improvement or proceed with it, according to the decision of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
