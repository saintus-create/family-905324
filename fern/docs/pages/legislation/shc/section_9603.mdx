# SHC § 9603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9603

---

## Text

The refunding bonds shall represent and be secured by the reassessments and any later reassessments which may be levied or issued upon the same property in lieu of the reassessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
