# SHC § 5394

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 17. Demanding Payment of the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 5394

---

## Text

If any warrant is lost, upon proof of such loss a duplicate can be issued upon which collections may be made with the same effect as on the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
