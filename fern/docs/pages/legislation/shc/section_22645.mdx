# SHC § 22645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 4. Completion of Proceedings; Collection of Assessments > Article: 2. Collection of Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 22645

---

## Text

After the filing of the diagram and assessment, the county auditor shall enter on the county assessment roll opposite each lot or parcel of land the amount assessed thereupon, as shown in the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
