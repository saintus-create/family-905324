# SHC § 30845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 2. Regulations

**Citation:** SHC § 30845

---

## Text

Any person who enters into an undertaking to keep and attend a ferry and violates the conditions of such undertaking is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
