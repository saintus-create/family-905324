# SHC § 5452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 18.1. Collecting the Assessment on the Tax Roll

**Citation:** SHC § 5452

---

## Text

A certified copy of said resolution shall be delivered immediately to the officer designated by law to extend city taxes upon the tax roll on which they are collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 65.)
