# SHC § 22505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 22505

---

## Text

An assessment district may consist of contiguous or noncontinguous areas. The improvements in one area need not be of benefit to other areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
