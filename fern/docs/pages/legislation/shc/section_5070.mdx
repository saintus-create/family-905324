# SHC § 5070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 4. Notice by Mail

**Citation:** SHC § 5070

---

## Text

Whenever in this division notice is required to be given by mail, notice shall be given in accordance with the provisions of Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 194, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
