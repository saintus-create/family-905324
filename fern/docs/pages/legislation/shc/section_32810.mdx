# SHC § 32810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Powers

**Citation:** SHC § 32810

---

## Text

The authority may exercise all or any part or combination of the powers granted by this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
