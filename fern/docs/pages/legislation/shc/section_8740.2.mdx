# SHC § 8740.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 10.5. ALTERNATIVE PROCEDURE FOR DIVISION OF LAND AND BOND > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8740.2

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the application and fee the street superintendent shall apportion to each separate part of the original lot or parcel of land the proportionate part of the amount remaining unpaid on the assessment that would have been levied thereon had the lot or parcel of land been so divided at the time the original assessment was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1527.)
