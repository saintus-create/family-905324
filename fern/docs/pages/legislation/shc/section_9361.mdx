# SHC § 9361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 10. Foreclosure of Reassessment Lien

**Citation:** SHC § 9361

---

## Text

A city shall have the right to advance and pay county or other taxes wherever necessary to protect its interest in property against which there is a delinquent reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
