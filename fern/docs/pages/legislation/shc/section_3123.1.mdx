# SHC § 3123.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. NOTICE OF SPECIAL ASSESSMENT, SPECIAL TAX, AND FORECLOSURE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. RECORDATION OF NOTICE OF PENDENCY OF SALE OR FORECLOSURE

**Citation:** SHC § 3123.1

---

## Text

The lienholder is entitled to recover the cost of recordation of any notice of pendency in any sale or foreclosure resulting from the delinquency and provision therefor shall be made in any notice, order, or judgment authorizing or providing for the sale or foreclosure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1102, Sec. 59. Effective September 24, 1986.)
