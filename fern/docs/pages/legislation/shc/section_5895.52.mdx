# SHC § 5895.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 27. Construction of Sidewalks and Curbs > Article: 4. Construction Outside of Jurisdiction

**Citation:** SHC § 5895.52

---

## Text

The city or county proceeding under the provisions of this article may pay for such work out of the road fund or any other fund available for such purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 276.)
