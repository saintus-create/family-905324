# SHC § 8509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 8509

---

## Text

“Auditor” means:

(a) When used with reference to a county, the county auditor.

(b) When used with reference to a city, any person who is charged with the duty of extending taxes upon the assessment rolls and lists.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
