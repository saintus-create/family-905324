# SHC § 36609.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36609.4

---

## Text

“Clerk” means the clerk of the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 5. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
