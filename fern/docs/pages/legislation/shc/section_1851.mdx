# SHC § 1851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. CITY STREETS > Chapter: 2. City Streets as Part of the State and County Highway Systems

**Citation:** SHC § 1851

---

## Text

The legislative body of any city may establish the grades of those portions of any county highway lying within the city boundaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
