# SHC § 25441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 18. Funding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 25441

---

## Text

Funding bonds shall be dated as of the date of the resolution or order of the board of directors authorizing their issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
