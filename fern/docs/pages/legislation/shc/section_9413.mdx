# SHC § 9413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9413

---

## Text

The refunding bonds shall be delivered to the holders of the outstanding bonds in accordance with the contract with such bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
