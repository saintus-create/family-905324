# SHC § 30680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 3. San Pedro-Terminal Island Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30680

---

## Text

As used in this article, “toll bridge” means a toll bridge, with at least four lanes, across Los Angeles Harbor from San Pedro at or near Boschke Slough to Terminal Island, and the approaches thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 74.)
