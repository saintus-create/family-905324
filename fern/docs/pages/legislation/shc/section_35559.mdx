# SHC § 35559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 4. Parking Place Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 35559

---

## Text

The commission shall operate, manage, and control the parking places and make and enforce all necessary rules for their use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
