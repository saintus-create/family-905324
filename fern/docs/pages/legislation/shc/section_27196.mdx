# SHC § 27196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 10.6. Annual Audit of the District

**Citation:** SHC § 27196

---

## Text

The accountant who is under contract to perform the audit provided for in Section 27195 may at any reasonable time and place examine the books and records of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1472.)
