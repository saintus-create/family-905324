# SHC § 619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 619

---

## Text

Route 605 is from:

(a) Route 1 near Seal Beach to Route 405.

(b) Route 405 to Route 210 near Duarte.

Route 605 shall be known and designated as the “San Gabriel River Freeway.”
