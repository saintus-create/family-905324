# SHC § 9358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 10. Foreclosure of Reassessment Lien

**Citation:** SHC § 9358

---

## Text

The foreclosure action shall be governed and regulated by the provisions of this chapter, and also where not in conflict herewith by the codes of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
