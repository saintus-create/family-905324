# SHC § 8003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 8003

---

## Text

“City council” and “council” include any body which by law is the legislative department of the government of the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
