# SHC § 30953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 4.3. Metropolitan Transportation Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 30953

---

## Text

Toll revenues and all other income derived from bridges pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 30910) shall be deposited in the Bay Area Toll Account, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 71, Sec. 11. Effective July 18, 2005.)
