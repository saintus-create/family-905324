# SHC § 5558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 20. Reassessments to Relieve Owners

**Citation:** SHC § 5558

---

## Text

The reassessment shall be collectible by and payable to the treasurer or such other officer as the legislative body may designate so far as practicable in the same manner as an original assessment and shall be enforceable by action in the manner provided in this division for enforcing an original assessment and shall have the same weight in evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
