# SHC § 32661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 2. Parking Authorities

**Citation:** SHC § 32661

---

## Text

A member of an authority may be removed by the mayor with the consent of the legislative body.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
