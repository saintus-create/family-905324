# SHC § 9225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 7. Recordation of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9225

---

## Text

When the reassessment, as made or as reviewed and corrected, has been confirmed by the legislative body, it shall be recorded in the office of the superintendent of streets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
