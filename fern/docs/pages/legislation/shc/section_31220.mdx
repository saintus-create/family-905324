# SHC § 31220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 7. Insurance of Property

**Citation:** SHC § 31220

---

## Text

When the toll tunnel is being built by the authority, it may carry or cause to be carried such an amount of insurance or indemnity bond as protection against loss or damage as it deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
