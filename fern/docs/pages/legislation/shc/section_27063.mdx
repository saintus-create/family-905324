# SHC § 27063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Proceeding by Election

**Citation:** SHC § 27063

---

## Text

The county elections official shall certify the results of the election to the Secretary of State together with a copy of the action of the board of supervisors calling the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 142. Effective January 1, 2003.)
