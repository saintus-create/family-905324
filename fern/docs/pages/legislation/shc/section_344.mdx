# SHC § 344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 344

---

## Text

Route 44 is from Route 299 at Redding to Route 36 west of Susanville, via the vicinity of Lassen Volcanic National Park.
