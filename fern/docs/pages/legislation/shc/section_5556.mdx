# SHC § 5556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 20. Reassessments to Relieve Owners

**Citation:** SHC § 5556

---

## Text

At the time fixed for the hearing or at such time or times to which the hearing may be continued the legislative body shall consider all objections to the reassessment and may correct inequalities or mistakes therein. When the legislative body is satisfied with the correctness of the reassessment it shall confirm the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
