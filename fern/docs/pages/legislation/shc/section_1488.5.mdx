# SHC § 1488.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Obstructions and Injuries to County Highways

**Citation:** SHC § 1488.5

---

## Text

The notice referred to in Sections 1487 and 1488 shall be given in the same manner as provided in Section 1482, except that the action it requires shall be taken immediately.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 182, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2004.)
