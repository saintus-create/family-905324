# SHC § 32514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 32514

---

## Text

“Parking facilities” includes public transportation terminal facilities and other facilities related thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 965.)
