# SHC § 27325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 17. Annexation of Territory

**Citation:** SHC § 27325

---

## Text

Upon the issuance of the certificate of annexation by the Secretary of State the territory named therein shall become a part of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
