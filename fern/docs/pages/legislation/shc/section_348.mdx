# SHC § 348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 348

---

## Text

Route 48 is from Route 14 near Lancaster to Route 122 near the San Bernardino county line.
