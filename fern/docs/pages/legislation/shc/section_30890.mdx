# SHC § 30890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 3.5. Toll Bridge Revenues

**Citation:** SHC § 30890

---

## Text

The authority shall transfer the net transit revenues, as defined in Section 30884, to the commission on a regularly scheduled basis as set forth in the authority’s annual budget resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 54. Effective January 1, 2004.)
