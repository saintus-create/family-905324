# SHC § 263.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 2.5. State Scenic Highways

**Citation:** SHC § 263.1

---

## Text

The state scenic highway system shall include all of the following state routes:

Routes 28, 35, 38, 52, 53, 62, 74, 75, 76, 89, 96, 97, 127, 128, 150, 151, 154, 156, 158, 161, 173, 197, 199, 203, 209, 221, 236, 239, 243, 247, 254, and 330 in their entirety.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 104, Sec. 1. (AB 998) Effective January 1, 2020.)
