# SHC § 36611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36611

---

## Text

“Management district plan” or “plan” means a proposal as defined in Section 36622.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36614 by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 10. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
