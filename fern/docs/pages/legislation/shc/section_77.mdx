# SHC § 77

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 2. California Transportation Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 77

---

## Text

The commission may accept, by appropriate resolution, a grant or modification of a grant of right-of-way for any of the roads mentioned in Section 76 across property of the United States of America.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1310.)
