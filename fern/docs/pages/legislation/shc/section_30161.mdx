# SHC § 30161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties of the Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 30161

---

## Text

When necessary to facilitate movement of traffic, the department may limit the use of any toll bridge or other toll highway crossing on a credit basis to particular classes of vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
