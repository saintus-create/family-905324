# SHC § 10362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 4.7. Alternative Procedure for Authorizing Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 10362

---

## Text

All provisions of this division shall apply to proceedings pursuant to this chapter, except as expressly provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1298, Sec. 26.)
