# SHC § 9606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9606

---

## Text

In no event shall the first maturity of any refunding bonds be earlier than the second day of September next succeeding 12 months after the date of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 65, Sec. 32. Effective June 6, 1985.)
