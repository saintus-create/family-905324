# SHC § 10005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 10005

---

## Text

“City treasurer” includes any person or officer who has charge and makes payments of the city or municipal funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
