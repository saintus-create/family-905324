# SHC § 562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 562

---

## Text

Route 262 is from Route 880 to Route 680 near Warm Springs.

Route 262 shall cease to be a state highway when Route 237 is constructed between Route 880 and Route 680.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 409, Sec. 63.)
