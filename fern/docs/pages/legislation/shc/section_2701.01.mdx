# SHC § 2701.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 17. Passenger Rail and Clean Air Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 2701.01

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Committee” means the Passenger Rail Finance Committee created pursuant to Section 2701.12.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Transportation.

(c) “Fund” means the Passenger Rail Bond Fund created pursuant to Section 2701.05.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 108, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 108 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
