# SHC § 6640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 13. Refunding of Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 6640

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this part may be refunded pursuant to the procedures in Division 11.5 (commencing with Section 9500).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1332, Sec. 6. Effective October 1, 1985.)
