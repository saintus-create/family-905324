# SHC § 8223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 9. Levy and Collection of Taxes

**Citation:** SHC § 8223

---

## Text

All money raised by taxation which is authorized by this chapter shall belong to the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1147.)
