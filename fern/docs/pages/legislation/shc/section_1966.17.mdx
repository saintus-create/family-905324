# SHC § 1966.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. CITY STREETS > Chapter: 8. Neighborhood Electric Vehicle Transportation Plans > Article: 3. County of Los Angeles Neighborhood Electric Vehicle Transportation Plans

**Citation:** SHC § 1966.17

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2040, and as of that date, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 158, Sec. 1. (AB 2432) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2040, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 3, commencing with Section 1966.10. Exception: Section 1966.16 is repealed on January 1, 2033.)
