# SHC § 9501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 9501

---

## Text

The procedures of this division are an alternative to Division 11 (commencing with Section 9000) which relates to the refunding of bonds under the Improvement Bond Act of 1915.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
