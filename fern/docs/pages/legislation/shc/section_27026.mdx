# SHC § 27026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Ordinance of Intention

**Citation:** SHC § 27026

---

## Text

If the ordinance is adopted by the board of supervisors, it shall be subject to the referendum provisions applicable to ordinances generally in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
