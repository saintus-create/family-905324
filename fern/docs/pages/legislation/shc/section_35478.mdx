# SHC § 35478

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 2. Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35478

---

## Text

The notice shall be published twice in a daily or weekly newspaper of general circulation printed and published in the city in which the assessed land lies.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
