# SHC § 31130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** SHC § 31130

---

## Text

When necessary to facilitate movement of traffic, the authority may limit the use of the toll tunnel on a credit basis to particular classes of vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
