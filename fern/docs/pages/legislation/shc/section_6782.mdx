# SHC § 6782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 3. Repairs and Improvements to Street Standards

**Citation:** SHC § 6782

---

## Text

The legislative body may also require, by resolution, any person to pave alongside of, and contiguous to, its rails with special type of brick or paving blocks or other material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 157.)
