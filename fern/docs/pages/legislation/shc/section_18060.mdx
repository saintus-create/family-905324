# SHC § 18060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 4. The Resolution of Intention

**Citation:** SHC § 18060

---

## Text

After the report is considered by it, the city council may pass a resolution of intention to order the improvement.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
