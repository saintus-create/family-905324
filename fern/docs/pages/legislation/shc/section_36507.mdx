# SHC § 36507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36507

---

## Text

“Business” means all types of businesses and includes financial institutions and professions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 591, Sec. 2.)
