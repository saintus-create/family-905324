# SHC § 8664.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 6.5. VARIABLE INTEREST RATE BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8664.5

---

## Text

The legislative body may, by resolution, specify terms and conditions under which the bonds may be converted to a fixed interest rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1332, Sec. 7. Effective October 1, 1985.)
