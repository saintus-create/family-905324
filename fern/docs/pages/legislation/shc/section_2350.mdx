# SHC § 2350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 7. Federal Aid for Urban Systems > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 2350

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Federal Aid for Urban Systems Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 201. Inoperative upon determination described in Section 2352. Repealed on January 1 after inoperative date, as provided in Section 2352.)
