# SHC § 2157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 4. Accounting, Reports and Audits

**Citation:** SHC § 2157

---

## Text

The Departmental Transportation Advisory Committee is hereby abolished and the department shall succeed to all of its duties and responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 77, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 2006.)
