# SHC § 9356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 10. Foreclosure of Reassessment Lien

**Citation:** SHC § 9356

---

## Text

The court may adjudge and decree a lien against the lot or parcel of land covered by the reassessment for the amount of the judgment and may order the premises to be sold on execution as in other cases of the sale of real estate by the process of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 174. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
