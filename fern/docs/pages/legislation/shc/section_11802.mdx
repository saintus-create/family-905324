# SHC § 11802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. PEDESTRIAN MALLS > Chapter: 9. Special Ad Valorem Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 11802

---

## Text

If a district has not been established as contemplated by either Section 11800 or Section 11801, this chapter shall not be applicable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 459.)
