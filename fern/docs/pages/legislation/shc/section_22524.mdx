# SHC § 22524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 22524

---

## Text

“Fiscal year” means a 12-month period commencing on July 1 and ending on the following June 30.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
