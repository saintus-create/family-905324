# SHC § 35420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Bonds, Acquisitions and Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 35420

---

## Text

Before selling all or any part of the bonds, the legislative body shall give notice inviting sealed bids in such mannner as it prescribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
