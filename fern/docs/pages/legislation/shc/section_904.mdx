# SHC § 904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 904

---

## Text

No route of travel used by one or more persons over another’s land shall become a county highway by use.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
