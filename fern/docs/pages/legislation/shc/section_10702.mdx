# SHC § 10702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 8. Deferral of Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 10702

---

## Text

No deferral arrangement may restrict, reduce, or eliminate any remedy of a bondholder provided by this division in the event of a default.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1298, Sec. 29.)
