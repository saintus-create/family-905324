# SHC § 27161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. District Powers

**Citation:** SHC § 27161

---

## Text

The district may sue and be sued in its corporate name in all actions and proceedings in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
