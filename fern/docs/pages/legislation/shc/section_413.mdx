# SHC § 413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 413

---

## Text

Route 113 is from:

(a) Route 12 to Route 80 near Dixon.

(b) Route 80 near Davis to Route 99 passing near Woodland.
