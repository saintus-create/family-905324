# SHC § 6468.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 4.5. Issuance of Bonds to Represent Unpaid Assessments Against Publicly Owned Property

**Citation:** SHC § 6468.3

---

## Text

The final maturity of the bonds shall not exceed 24 years from that second day of June next succeeding this date.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 629.)
