# SHC § 35539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35539

---

## Text

The total of the reassessment, exclusive of interest, shall not exceed the total amount necessary for the redemption of the bonds which are outstanding.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
