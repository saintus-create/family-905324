# SHC § 18009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18009

---

## Text

“Clerk” and “city clerk” mean any person or officer who is or acts as clerk of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
