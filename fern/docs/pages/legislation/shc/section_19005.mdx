# SHC § 19005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 19005

---

## Text

“Clerk” or “county clerk” means any person or officer who is or acts as clerk of the board of supervisors of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
