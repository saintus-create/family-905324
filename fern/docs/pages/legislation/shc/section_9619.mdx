# SHC § 9619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9619

---

## Text

The treasurer shall keep a register in his or her office which shall show the series, number, date, amount, rate of interest, and registered owner of each bond. The treasurer shall cancel and file each bond paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
