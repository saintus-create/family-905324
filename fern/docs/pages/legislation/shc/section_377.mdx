# SHC § 377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 377

---

## Text

Route 77 is from:

(a) Route 880 near 42nd Avenue to a connection with Route 580 near High Street in Oakland.

(b) Route 580 in Oakland to Route 24 near Lafayette.
