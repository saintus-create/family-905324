# SHC § 5305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 13. General Provisions Relating to Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 5305

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for deferral of payment of assessments in accordance with procedures prescribed by Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 10700) of Division 12.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 449, Sec. 1. Effective July 22, 1986.)
