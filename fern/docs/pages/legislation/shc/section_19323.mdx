# SHC § 19323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 18. Consolidation

**Citation:** SHC § 19323

---

## Text

The notice shall state that it is proposed to consolidate into one district all of the territory within the named districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 759.)
