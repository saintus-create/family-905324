# SHC § 1179.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 4. Methods of Performing Highway Work > Article: 3. By Permanent Road Divisions

**Citation:** SHC § 1179.5

---

## Text

In lieu of, or in addition to, any special tax levied pursuant to Section 1178, the board may fix and collect parcel charges for any permanent road division pursuant to the assessment ballot procedures in Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 158, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2009.)
