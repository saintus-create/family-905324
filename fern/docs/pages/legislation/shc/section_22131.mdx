# SHC § 22131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 6. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 22131

---

## Text

Upon the levying of the assessment, the clerk of the council shall transmit to the city tax collector the diagram and assessment upon which the levy is based.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
