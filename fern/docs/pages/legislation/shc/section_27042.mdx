# SHC § 27042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Proceeding by Petition

**Citation:** SHC § 27042

---

## Text

Attached to each copy of the petition shall be a copy of the ordinance providing for its circulation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
