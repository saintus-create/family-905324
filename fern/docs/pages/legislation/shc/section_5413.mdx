# SHC § 5413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 18. Collecting the Assessment by Action

**Citation:** SHC § 5413

---

## Text

If the court finds an unnecessary number of actions have been brought, where the parties are identical, it may allow the costs of one action only.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
