# SHC § 27245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Issuance of District Bonds and Disposition of Surplus Proceeds

**Citation:** SHC § 27245

---

## Text

The district bonds may be made registerable as to principal alone or as to both principal and interest under such terms and conditions as may be fixed by the board prior to the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
