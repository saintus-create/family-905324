# SHC § 10208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 3. Resolution of Intention and Report

**Citation:** SHC § 10208

---

## Text

The exterior boundaries of the assessment district may be specified and described as provided in the Improvement Act of 1911.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
