# SHC § 311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 311

---

## Text

Route 11 is from the northerly border of the new Federal Port of Entry and east of the Otay Mesa Port of Entry to near the junction of Route 125 and Route 905.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1220, Sec. 32. Effective September 30, 1994.)
