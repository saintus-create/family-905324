# SHC § 226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 7. Safety Roadside Rests

**Citation:** SHC § 226

---

## Text

Areas comprising such safety roadside rests shall be deemed a part of the state highway right-of-way for all purposes, including those set forth in Section 731.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 992.)
