# SHC § 9009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

The curative clauses of this division are cumulative and each of them is to be given full force and effect.
