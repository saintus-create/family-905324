# SHC § 36614.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36614.7

---

## Text

“Property-based district” means any district in which a city levies a property-based assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 13. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
