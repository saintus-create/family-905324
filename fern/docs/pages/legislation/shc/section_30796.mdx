# SHC § 30796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 7. San Diego-Coronado Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30796

---

## Text

The San Diego-Coronado Bridge is the bridge, together with necessary approaches, across San Diego Bay connecting the Cities of San Diego and Coronado.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1774.)
