# SHC § 27062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Proceeding by Election

**Citation:** SHC § 27062

---

## Text

The election shall be called and held in accordance with the Uniform District Election Law (Part 4 (commencing with Section 10500) of Division 10 of the Elections Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 141. Effective January 1, 2003.)
