# SHC § 35545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35545

---

## Text

The clerk shall certify at the end of the reassessment, that it is the reassessment approved by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
