# SHC § 36620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 2. Establishment

**Citation:** SHC § 36620

---

## Text

A property and business improvement district may be established as provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
