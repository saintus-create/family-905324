# SHC § 8004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 8004

---

## Text

“Clerk” and “city clerk” include any person or officer who is clerk of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
