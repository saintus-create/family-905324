# SHC § 22550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 3. Notice

**Citation:** SHC § 22550

---

## Text

“Notice” means any resolution, order, notice, or other instrument authorized or required by this part to be published, posted, or mailed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
