# SHC § 432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 432

---

## Text

Route 132 is from:

(a) Route 580 west of Vernalis to Route 99 at Modesto.

(b) Route 99 to Route 49.
