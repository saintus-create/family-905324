# SHC § 35544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35544

---

## Text

When the reassessment is revised, corrected, or modified to comply with its judgment the legislative body shall pass a resolution confirming the reassessment.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
