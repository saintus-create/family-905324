# SHC § 27224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 12. Authority for Issuance and Sale of District Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 27224

---

## Text

All the expenses of holding the election shall be borne by the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
