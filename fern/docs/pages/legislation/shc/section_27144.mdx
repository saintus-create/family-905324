# SHC § 27144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Organization and Powers of the Board and the Executive Officers

**Citation:** SHC § 27144

---

## Text

The board shall adopt rules to govern its proceedings, including the use of motions in connection with such proceedings.
