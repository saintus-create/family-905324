# SHC § 27021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Ordinance of Intention

**Citation:** SHC § 27021

---

## Text

A district may consist of part or all of one or more contiguous counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
