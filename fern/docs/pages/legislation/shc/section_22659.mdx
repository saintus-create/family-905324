# SHC § 22659

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 22659

---

## Text

All contributions authorized prior to confirmation of an assessment shall be deducted from the total improvement costs to be assessed within the assessment district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
