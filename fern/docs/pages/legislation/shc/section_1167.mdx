# SHC § 1167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 4. Methods of Performing Highway Work > Article: 3. By Permanent Road Divisions

**Citation:** SHC § 1167

---

## Text

The boundaries established by the board shall be the boundaries of the division until such boundaries are changed in the manner provided in this article.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
