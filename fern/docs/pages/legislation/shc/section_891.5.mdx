# SHC § 891.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 3. California Bicycle Transportation Act

**Citation:** SHC § 891.5

---

## Text

The Sacramento Area Council of Governments, pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 2551, may purchase, operate, and maintain callboxes on class 1 bikeways.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 262, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
