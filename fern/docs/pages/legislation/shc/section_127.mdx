# SHC § 127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 127

---

## Text

The California Highway Patrol shall cooperate with the department in the enforcement of the closing, or restriction of use, of any State highway.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
