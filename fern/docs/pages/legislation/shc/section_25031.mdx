# SHC § 25031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Organization and Reorganization of Districts

**Citation:** SHC § 25031

---

## Text

The adoption of the original resolution by the board of supervisors of the county initiating the proceedings shall constitute a sufficient approval in behalf of that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
