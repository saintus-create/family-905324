# SHC § 345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 345

---

## Text

Route 45 is from:

(a) Route 113 near Knights Landing to Route 20 near Sycamore.

(b) Route 20 near Colusa to Route 32 near Hamilton City.
