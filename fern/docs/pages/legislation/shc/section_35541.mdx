# SHC § 35541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35541

---

## Text

The reassessment shall be presented to the legislative body, which shall fix a time for hearing. The hearing shall be at least 20 days after the presentation of the reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
