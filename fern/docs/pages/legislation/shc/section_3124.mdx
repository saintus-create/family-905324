# SHC § 3124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. NOTICE OF SPECIAL ASSESSMENT, SPECIAL TAX, AND FORECLOSURE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. RECORDATION OF NOTICE OF PENDENCY OF SALE OR FORECLOSURE

**Citation:** SHC § 3124

---

## Text

This part does not apply in any case where payments of special taxes or principal and interest due upon a special assessment or bond are enforced for delinquency in the same manner and at the same times as general taxes of the city on real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1102, Sec. 60. Effective September 24, 1986.)
