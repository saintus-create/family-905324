# SHC § 11009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. PEDESTRIAN MALLS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 11009

---

## Text

“Assessment roll” means the assessment roll or rolls used by the city for purposes of city ad valorem taxes on real property of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1960, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 33.)
