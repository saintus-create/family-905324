# SHC § 30760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 4.5. Antioch Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30760

---

## Text

As used in this article, “Antioch Bridge” means the high-level fixed-span bridge across the San Joaquin River near Antioch together with the approaches thereto and the toll collection facilities for the bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2004.)
