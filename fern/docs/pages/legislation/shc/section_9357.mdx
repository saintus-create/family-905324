# SHC § 9357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 10. Foreclosure of Reassessment Lien

**Citation:** SHC § 9357

---

## Text

On appeal, the appellate courts shall have the same power to adjudge and decree a lien and order such premises to be sold on execution as is provided in this chapter for the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
