# SHC § 35504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 3. Bonds for Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35504

---

## Text

The legislative body shall sell the bonds for cash, and if bids are made, for cash to the best responsible bidder.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
