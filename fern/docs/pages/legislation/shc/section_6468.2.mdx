# SHC § 6468.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 4.5. Issuance of Bonds to Represent Unpaid Assessments Against Publicly Owned Property

**Citation:** SHC § 6468.2

---

## Text

The legislative body may by resolution establish the denomination of the bonds, the amount to mature each fiscal year and provide for the issuance and sale of the bonds.
