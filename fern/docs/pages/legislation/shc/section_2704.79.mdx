# SHC § 2704.79

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 20.5. Implementation of the Safe, Reliable High-Speed Passenger Train Bond Act for the 21st Century

**Citation:** SHC § 2704.79

---

## Text

Pursuant to the authorization set forth in subdivision (h) of Section 2704.08, not more than 5 percent of the proceeds of bonds described in paragraph (1) of subdivision (b) of Section 2704.04 shall be used for administrative purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 53, Sec. 14. (AB 173) Effective July 2, 2024.)
