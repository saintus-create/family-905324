# SHC § 8835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 14. FORECLOSURE BY ACTION > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8835

---

## Text

The amount of penalties, costs and interest due shall be calculated up to the date of the judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
