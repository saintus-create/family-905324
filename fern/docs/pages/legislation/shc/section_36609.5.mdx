# SHC § 36609.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36609.5

---

## Text

“General benefit” means, for purposes of a property-based district, any benefit that is not a “special benefit” as defined in Section 36615.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 6. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
