# SHC § 27172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. District Powers

**Citation:** SHC § 27172

---

## Text

The district may do all things necessary for the complete exercise of the powers described in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
