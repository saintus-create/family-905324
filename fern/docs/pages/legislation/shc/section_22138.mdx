# SHC § 22138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 6. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 22138

---

## Text

All assessments unpaid shall be delinquent at the expiration of a period of 30 days from the first publication or posting of the notice that the assessment is due and payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
