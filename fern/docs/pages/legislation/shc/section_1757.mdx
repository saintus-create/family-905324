# SHC § 1757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 10. County Ferries

**Citation:** SHC § 1757

---

## Text

Any ferry constructed or acquired under this chapter by a county or counties may be operated as either a free ferry or a toll ferry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 28.)
