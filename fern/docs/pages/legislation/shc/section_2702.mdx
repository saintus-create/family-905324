# SHC § 2702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 18. Federal Aid for Tribes

**Citation:** SHC § 2702

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by federal and state law, a federally recognized Native American tribe shall be eligible for federal funding for a transportation project and may be the lead agency for a transportation project that receives federal funding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 102, Sec. 1. (AB 2261) Effective July 15, 2024.)
