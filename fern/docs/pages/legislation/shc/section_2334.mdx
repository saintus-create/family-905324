# SHC § 2334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 6.5. Federal Aid for Highway Safety Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 2334

---

## Text

Expenditure of such funds on local streets and roads shall be exempt from the provisions of Sections 188 and 188.8.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 777, Sec. 94.)
