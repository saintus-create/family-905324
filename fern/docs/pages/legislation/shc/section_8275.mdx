# SHC § 8275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 14. Annexation of Territory

**Citation:** SHC § 8275

---

## Text

At any time after the formation of a district the board may determine that contiguous territory should be annexed to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1230.)
