# SHC § 22503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 22503

---

## Text

An assessment district shall consist of all territory which, as determined by the legislative body, will be benefited by the improvements and is to be assessed to pay the costs thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
