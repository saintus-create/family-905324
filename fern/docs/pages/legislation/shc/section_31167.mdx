# SHC § 31167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 31167

---

## Text

All bonds issued pursuant to this chapter and the income therefrom are at all times exempt from taxation, except for transfer, inheritance, and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
