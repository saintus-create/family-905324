# SHC § 2532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 12. Federal Aid for Safer Off-System Roads > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** SHC § 2532

---

## Text

Within 60 days after the apportionment is made pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 2530, each county shall notify the department as to the amount of the apportionment the county and its cities wish to claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1270.)
