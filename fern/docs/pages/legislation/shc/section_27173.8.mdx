# SHC § 27173.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. District Powers

**Citation:** SHC § 27173.8

---

## Text

Every purchase or contract made in violation of the competitive bidding requirements of this part shall be void.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1472.)
