# SHC § 30602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 1. San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30602

---

## Text

The department shall permanently maintain and operate the toll bridge as a primary state highway in such a manner that the physical condition and operating efficiency thereof shall be of the highest character.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
