# SHC § 26000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 26000

---

## Text

“District,” means a boulevard district unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 285.)
