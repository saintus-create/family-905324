# SHC § 229.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1.5. Tourist Oriented Directional Signs > Article: 3. Eligibility

**Citation:** SHC § 229.25

---

## Text

The department shall determine that the facilities and attractions for which generic tourist oriented directional signs are provided meet the minimum criteria set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
