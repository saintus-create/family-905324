# SHC § 164.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 4.3. Transportation Funding Plan

**Citation:** SHC § 164.20

---

## Text

For purposes of subdivision (e) of Section 164.3, the eligible interregional and intercounty routes include all of the following:

Route 330, between the north urban limits of San Bernardino-Riverside and Route 18.

Route 371.

Route 395.

Route 505.

Route 580.

Route 680.

Route 905, except within the urban limits of San Diego.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 105, Sec. 57. Effective July 10, 1989.)
