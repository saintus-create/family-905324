# SHC § 5455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 18.1. Collecting the Assessment on the Tax Roll

**Citation:** SHC § 5455

---

## Text

Said assessments and the interest so entered shall become due and payable to the contractor or his assigns at the office of the city treasurer on the second day of January next succeeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 65.)
