# SHC § 857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Highway Proceedings Affecting Private Property > Article: 1. Change of Grade

**Citation:** SHC § 857

---

## Text

After the expiration of such sixty day period, the commission may by resolution officially change and reestablish the official grade of said highway in accordance with such resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 689.)
