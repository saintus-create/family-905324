# SHC § 5890.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 27. Construction of Sidewalks and Curbs > Article: 3. Collection of Cost of Construction

**Citation:** SHC § 5890.5

---

## Text

In assessing land, credit may be given for dedications and for improvements constructed at private expense.
