# SHC § 9207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 6. Protest and Hearing

**Citation:** SHC § 9207

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing the reassessment as originally made or as reviewed and corrected shall be confirmed by resolution entered upon the minutes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
