# SHC § 5550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 20. Reassessments to Relieve Owners

**Citation:** SHC § 5550

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, the legislative body shall have power at any time before the assessments levied under this division are fully paid and discharged to order a reassessment under the terms and conditions set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
