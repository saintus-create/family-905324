# SHC § 22168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 7. Sale and Redemption of Delinquent Property

**Citation:** SHC § 22168

---

## Text

Upon redemption of any lot or parcel of land, the tax collector shall enter the fact and date of redemption upon the duplicate certificate of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
