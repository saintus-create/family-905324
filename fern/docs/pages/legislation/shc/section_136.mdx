# SHC § 136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 136

---

## Text

The department may enter into contracts for the leasing or renting of tools or equipment for State highway purposes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1935, Ch. 514.)
