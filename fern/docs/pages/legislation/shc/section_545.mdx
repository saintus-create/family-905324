# SHC § 545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

## Text

Route 245 is from Route 198 to Route 180 near the General Grant Grove section of Kings Canyon National Park.
