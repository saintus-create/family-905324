# SHC § 11307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. PEDESTRIAN MALLS > Chapter: 4. Notice and Hearing

**Citation:** SHC § 11307

---

## Text

If assessments are to be levied as contemplated by Section 11202, then the notice, protest, and hearing procedures shall comply with Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 262, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 2001.)
