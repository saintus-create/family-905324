# SHC § 9706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 4. Curation and Validation

**Citation:** SHC § 9706

---

## Text

If refunding bonds are invalidated or held to be illegal, all of the provisions of the act shall apply to the original bonds and to any bonds issued under Section 9705 to replace the original bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
