# SHC § 2453.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 10. Grade Separation Projects

**Citation:** SHC § 2453.5

---

## Text

The department may submit its comments and recommendations to the commission on any project for which an allocation is to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1178, Sec. 1.)
