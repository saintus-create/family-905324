# SHC § 2506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 11. Federal Aid for Rural Highway Public Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 2506

---

## Text

All applications for federal funds for rural public mass transportation projects under Section 147 of the Federal Aid Highway Act of 1973 (Public Law 93-87) shall be made through the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 781.)
