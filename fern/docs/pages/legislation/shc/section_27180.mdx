# SHC § 27180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 10. Duties of District Officers

**Citation:** SHC § 27180

---

## Text

The president shall sign all contracts on behalf of the district, and perform such other duties as may be imposed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
