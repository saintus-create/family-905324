# SHC § 22137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 6. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 22137

---

## Text

When any assessment is paid, the tax collector shall mark opposite the assessment the word “paid,” with the date of the payment and shall give a receipt therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
