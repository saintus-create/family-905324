# SHC § 179.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 4.8. Local Bridge Seismic Safety Retrofit

**Citation:** SHC § 179.1

---

## Text

The department may administer projects for local bridge seismic safety retrofits consistent with the requirements of Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 2400) of Division 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 805, Sec. 4. Effective September 23, 2002.)
