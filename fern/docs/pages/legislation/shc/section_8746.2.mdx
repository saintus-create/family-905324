# SHC § 8746.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 10.7. BOND ANTICIPATION NOTES > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8746.2

---

## Text

The bond anticipation notes and the resolution authorizing them may contain any provisions, conditions, or limitations which a resolution of the legislative body authorizing the issuance of bonds may contain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1298, Sec. 15.)
