# SHC § 5013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 5013

---

## Text

“Engineer” when applied to a county means the county surveyor, and when applied to a city means the city engineer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
