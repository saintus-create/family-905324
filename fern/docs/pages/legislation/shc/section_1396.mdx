# SHC § 1396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 5. County Bridges and Subways > Article: 3. Joint Bridges

**Citation:** SHC § 1396

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors in their respective counties may erect free county bridges on county highways across navigable bodies of water in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
