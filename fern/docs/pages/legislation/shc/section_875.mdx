# SHC § 875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Highway Proceedings Affecting Private Property > Article: 3. Procedure

**Citation:** SHC § 875

---

## Text

The procedure provided in this chapter is not exclusive and shall not prohibit the department from proceeding in any other manner authorized by law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 689.)
