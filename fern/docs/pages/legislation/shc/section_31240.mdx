# SHC § 31240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 9. State Cooperation

**Citation:** SHC § 31240

---

## Text

The California Highway Commission and the Department of Public Works shall, upon the request of the authority, aid in the construction of any necessary connections with U.S. Highway 50.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
