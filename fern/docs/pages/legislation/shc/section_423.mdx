# SHC § 423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 423

---

## Text

Route 123 is from Route 580 at San Pablo Avenue in Oakland to Route 80 in Richmond at Cutting Boulevard.
