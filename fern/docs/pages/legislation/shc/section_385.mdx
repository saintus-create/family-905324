# SHC § 385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 385

---

## Text

Route 85 is from Route 101 near Bernal Road in San Jose to Route 101 near Moffett Boulevard in Mountain View.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 106, Sec. 22. Effective May 13, 1988. Operative January 1, 1989, by Sec. 31 of Ch. 106.)
