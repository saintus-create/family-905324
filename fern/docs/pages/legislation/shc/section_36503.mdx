# SHC § 36503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Declarations

**Citation:** SHC § 36503

---

## Text

Any provision of this part which conflicts with any other provision of law shall prevail over the other provision of law.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 591, Sec. 2.)
