# SHC § 18307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18307

---

## Text

“Improvement,” and any of its variants, includes the maintenance or servicing, or both, of all or part of any one or more street lighting systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
