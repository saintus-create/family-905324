# SHC § 31780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 8. Board of Parking Place Commissioners

**Citation:** SHC § 31780

---

## Text

The board shall operate, manage, and control the parking places and make and enforce all necessary regulations for their use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
