# SHC § 22551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 3. Notice

**Citation:** SHC § 22551

---

## Text

The clerk shall give notice or cause the same to be given in accordance with this article, unless the legislative body delegates the duty of giving the notice to some other officer or board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
