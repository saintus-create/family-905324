# SHC § 22553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 3. Notice

**Citation:** SHC § 22553

---

## Text

Publication of notice of hearing shall be completed at least 10 days prior to the date of hearing specified therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
