# SHC § 27024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Ordinance of Intention

**Citation:** SHC § 27024

---

## Text

The ordinance of intention may also contain a distinctive corporate name for the proposed district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
