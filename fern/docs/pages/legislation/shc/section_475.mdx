# SHC § 475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 475

---

## Text

Route 175 is from:

(a) Route 101 at Hopland to Route 29 near Lakeport.

(b) Route 29 near Kelseyville to Route 29 at Middletown.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1220, Sec. 43. Effective September 30, 1994.)
