# SHC § 2828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 2. INVESTIGATION AND REPORT ON THE PROJECT

**Citation:** SHC § 2828

---

## Text

The person preparing the report shall appear at the hearing on the report and give all information concerning it to all interested property owners and to the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
