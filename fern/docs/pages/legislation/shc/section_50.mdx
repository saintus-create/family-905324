# SHC § 50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 50

---

## Text

Any reference in any law or regulation to the State Highway Engineer shall be deemed to refer to the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1253.)
