# SHC § 22014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 22014

---

## Text

No publication or notice other than that provided for in this part shall be necessary to give validity to any proceedings had hereunder.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
