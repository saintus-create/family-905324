# SHC § 1684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 9. Cooperation by Counties > Article: 2. Cooperation with Cities

**Citation:** SHC § 1684

---

## Text

The city shall use all aid which it receives in the activities described in the resolution and shall return to the county any portion of such aid which is not thus used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
