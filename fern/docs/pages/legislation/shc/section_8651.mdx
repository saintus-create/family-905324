# SHC § 8651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 6. FORM AND CONTENT OF BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8651

---

## Text

The final series of installment of the bonds shall mature and be payable on a date which shall not exceed 39 years from the second day of September next succeeding 12 months from their date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 65, Sec. 4. Effective June 6, 1985.)
