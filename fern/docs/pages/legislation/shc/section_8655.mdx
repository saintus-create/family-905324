# SHC § 8655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 6. FORM AND CONTENT OF BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8655

---

## Text

The bonds, by their issuance, shall be conclusive evidence of the regularity of all proceedings had prior thereto under this division and under the law pursuant to which the assessment was levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 194, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1994.)
