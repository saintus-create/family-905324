# SHC § 36614.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36614.6

---

## Text

“Property-based assessment” means any assessment made pursuant to this part upon real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 12. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
