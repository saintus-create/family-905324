# SHC § 421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 421

---

## Text

Route 121 is from:

(a) Route 37 near Sears Point to Route 29 near Napa.

(b) Route 29 in Napa to Route 128.
