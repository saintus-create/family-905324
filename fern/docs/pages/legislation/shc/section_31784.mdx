# SHC § 31784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 8. Board of Parking Place Commissioners

**Citation:** SHC § 31784

---

## Text

Rentals, fees, and charges shall be fixed after public hearing following such notice as the board of commissioners prescribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
