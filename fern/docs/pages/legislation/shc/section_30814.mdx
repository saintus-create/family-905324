# SHC § 30814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 1. Franchises

**Citation:** SHC § 30814

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), no toll shall be imposed on the passage of a pedestrian, a bicycle, or a personal micromobility device, as defined in Section 1940.41 of the Civil Code, over any bridge that is part of the state highway system, on which the travel of pedestrians, bicycles, or personal micromobility devices is otherwise authorized, and on which tolls are imposed on the passage of motor vehicles, including any bridge constructed pursuant to a franchise granted under this article.

(b) Subdivision (a) shall not apply if the bridge was under construction on or after January 1, 2025, and if the tolls are used to fund the cost of constructing the bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 731, Sec. 3. (AB 2669) Effective January 1, 2025.)
