# SHC § 36614.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36614.5

---

## Text

“Property and business improvement district,” or “district,” means a property and business improvement district established pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36611 by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 7. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
