# SHC § 10555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 6.1. Loan Repayment Assessment Districts

**Citation:** SHC § 10555

---

## Text

The legislative body shall provide in the resolutions required under Sections 10200 and 10312 that the assessment will be collected in annual installments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 253, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
