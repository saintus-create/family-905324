# SHC § 8802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 13. SALE FOR DELINQUENCY > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8802

---

## Text

The city may pay and transfer into the redemption fund the amount of the delinquent assessment and of the delinquent interest for which the property has become tax-defaulted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 194, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1994.)
