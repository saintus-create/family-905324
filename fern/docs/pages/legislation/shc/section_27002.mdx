# SHC § 27002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 27002

---

## Text

As used in this part “board” means the board of directors of the district unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.
