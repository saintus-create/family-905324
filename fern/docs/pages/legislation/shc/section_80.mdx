# SHC § 80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 2. California Transportation Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 80

---

## Text

The commission shall act by resolution or by vote or order entered in its minutes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 436.)
