# SHC § 27001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 27001

---

## Text

As used in this part “district” means a bridge and highway district unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.
