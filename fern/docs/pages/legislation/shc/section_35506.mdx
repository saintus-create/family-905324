# SHC § 35506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 3. Bonds for Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35506

---

## Text

The bonds shall extend over a period not to exceed 24 years from January 2nd next succeeding the next September 1st following their date.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
