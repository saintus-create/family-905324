# SHC § 710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 3. The Care and Protection of State Highways > Article: 2.5. Special Provisions re Utilities in Freeways

**Citation:** SHC § 710

---

## Text

The department, in acting upon applications for utility permits, shall consider both the interests of the traveling public upon the freeway and the needs of consumers for public utility services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 11.)
