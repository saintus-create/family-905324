# SHC § 8350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 6. Effect of Vacation > Article: 1. Effect on Property Rights

**Citation:** SHC § 8350

---

## Text

Except as provided in Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 8340), the vacation of a street, highway, or public service easement extinguishes all public easements therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
