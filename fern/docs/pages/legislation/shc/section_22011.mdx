# SHC § 22011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 22011

---

## Text

“Lot line” means the boundary line separating that portion of a lot or parcel of land set apart for individual use and occupancy from the street.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
