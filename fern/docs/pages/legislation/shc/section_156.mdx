# SHC § 156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3.5. Barriers to Fish Passage

**Citation:** SHC § 156

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Fish passage” means the ability of an anadromous fish to access appropriate habitat at all points in its life cycle, including spawning and rearing.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 589, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
