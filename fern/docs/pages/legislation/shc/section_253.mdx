# SHC § 253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 2. The California Freeway and Expressway System

**Citation:** SHC § 253

---

## Text

The California Freeway and Expressway System is hereby established and shall be composed of the highways specified in this article.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 282.)
