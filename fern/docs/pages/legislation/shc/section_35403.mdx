# SHC § 35403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Bonds, Acquisitions and Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 35403

---

## Text

The legislative body shall describe the form of bonds and of the interest coupons to be attached thereto.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
