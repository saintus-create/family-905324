# SHC § 6763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 2. New Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 6763

---

## Text

The resolution of intention and notice of proposed improvement shall be notice to the person of the intention to order the improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
