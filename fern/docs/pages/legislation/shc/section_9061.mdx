# SHC § 9061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 2. Preliminary Proceedings > Article: 2. Consent of Bondholders

**Citation:** SHC § 9061

---

## Text

If the owners of 75 percent or more of the principal amount of the outstanding bonds of any issue join in the contract or contracts, the city may proceed under this division to refund all of the bonds of that issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
