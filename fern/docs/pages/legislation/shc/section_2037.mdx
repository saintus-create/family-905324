# SHC § 2037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 2. Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Program

**Citation:** SHC § 2037

---

## Text

A city or county may spend its apportionment of funds under the program on transportation priorities other than those allowable pursuant to this chapter if the city’s or county’s average Pavement Condition Index meets or exceeds 80.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 5, Sec. 36. (SB 1) Effective April 28, 2017.)
