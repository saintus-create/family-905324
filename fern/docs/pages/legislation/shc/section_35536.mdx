# SHC § 35536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35536

---

## Text

If a reassessment is directed, by a decree of court or by an order of the legislative body, the street superintendent shall proceed to make a reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
