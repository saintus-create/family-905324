# SHC § 27145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Organization and Powers of the Board and the Executive Officers

**Citation:** SHC § 27145

---

## Text

The board shall select an office and place of business within the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
