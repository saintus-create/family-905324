# SHC § 35103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 35103

---

## Text

“Assessment roll” means the assessment roll used by the city for purposes of city taxation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
