# SHC § 573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 573

---

## Text

Route 273 is from:

(a) Route 5 near Anderson to Route 299 in Redding.

(b) From Route 299 in Redding to Route 5 northeast of Redding.
