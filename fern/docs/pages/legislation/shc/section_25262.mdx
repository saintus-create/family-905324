# SHC § 25262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 10. Final Surveys, Plans, Specifications and Drawings

**Citation:** SHC § 25262

---

## Text

All laws relating to the use of state highways are applicable to any highways under the control of any district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 173.)
