# SHC § 9609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9609

---

## Text

The interest on refunding bonds shall be payable on March 2, and September 2, respectively, of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 65, Sec. 34. Effective June 6, 1985.)
