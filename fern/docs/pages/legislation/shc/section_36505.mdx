# SHC § 36505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36505

---

## Text

“Advisory board” means the advisory board or commission appointed by the city council pursuant to Section 36530.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 591, Sec. 2.)
