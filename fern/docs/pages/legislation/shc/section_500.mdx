# SHC § 500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 500

## Text

Route 200 is from Route 101 to Route 299 staying north of the Mad River.
