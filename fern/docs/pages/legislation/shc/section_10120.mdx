# SHC § 10120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 2.5. Elections

**Citation:** SHC § 10120

---

## Text

Approval of the owners of land for any improvements specified in Section 10112 may, at the discretion of the legislative body, be secured through an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 630, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1981.)
