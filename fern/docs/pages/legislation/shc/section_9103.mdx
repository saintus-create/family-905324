# SHC § 9103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 1. Computing the Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9103

---

## Text

When the reassessment has been spread under the alternative method, it shall be filed with the auditor and the auditor shall include it in his statement in lieu of the reassessment computed by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
