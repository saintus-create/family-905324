# SHC § 31232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 8. Local Cooperation

**Citation:** SHC § 31232

---

## Text

Money or property advanced or contributed pursuant to this article may be immediately transferred or delivered to the authority to be used for the purpose for which such advance or contribution was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
