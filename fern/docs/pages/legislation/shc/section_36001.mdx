# SHC § 36001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 36001

---

## Text

“Parking and business improvement area” or “area” as used in this part means an area designated as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 241.)
