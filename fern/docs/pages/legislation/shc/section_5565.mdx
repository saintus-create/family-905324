# SHC § 5565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 20. Reassessments to Relieve Owners

**Citation:** SHC § 5565

---

## Text

The making of any reassessment pursuant to this chapter in any proceedings shall not constitute a bar or limit the right to make further reassessments as the legislative body may determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
