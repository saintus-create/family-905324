# SHC § 8740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 10.5. ALTERNATIVE PROCEDURE FOR DIVISION OF LAND AND BOND > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8740

---

## Text

The procedures of this part are alternative to Part 10 of this division, relating to division of land and bond.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1527.)
