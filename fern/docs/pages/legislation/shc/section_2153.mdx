# SHC § 2153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 4. Accounting, Reports and Audits

**Citation:** SHC § 2153

---

## Text

The State Controller shall take such steps as he may deem necessary to insure that such reports are adequate and accurate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 11.)
