# SHC § 941.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Boards of Supervisors

**Citation:** SHC § 941.3

---

## Text

Before taking any action authorized by Section 941.2 affecting any state highway, such action must be approved by the Department of Public Works.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
