# SHC § 418

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 418

---

## Text

Route 118 is from:

(a) Route 126 near Saticoy to Route 210 near San Fernando.

(b) Route 210 near Sunland to Route 249 north of La Canada.
