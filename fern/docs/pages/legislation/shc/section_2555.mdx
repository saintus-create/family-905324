# SHC § 2555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 14. Service Authority for Freeway Emergencies

**Citation:** SHC § 2555

---

## Text

An authority may impose a fee of one dollar ($1) per year, on vehicles registered in the county pursuant to Section 9250.10 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 766, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
