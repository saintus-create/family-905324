# SHC § 30610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 1. San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30610

---

## Text

Expenditures for maintenance and operation of the Terminal Building in San Francisco shall be proper charges against the revenues of the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1592.)
