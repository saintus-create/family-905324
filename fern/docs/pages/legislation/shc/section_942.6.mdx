# SHC § 942.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Boards of Supervisors

**Citation:** SHC § 942.6

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may delegate the powers contained in Section 942.5 to the county road commissioner.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 488.)
