# SHC § 25104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. District Officers and Employees

**Citation:** SHC § 25104

---

## Text

All engineering work performed in the district shall be done under the direction and control of the district engineer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
