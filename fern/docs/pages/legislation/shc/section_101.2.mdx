# SHC § 101.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 101.2

---

## Text

The department may, when it deems it necessary, replace or cause to be replaced any city limit road sign.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1273.)
