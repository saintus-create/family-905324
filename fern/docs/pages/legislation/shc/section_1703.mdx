# SHC § 1703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 9. Cooperation by Counties > Article: 3. Extension of County Highways Through Cities

**Citation:** SHC § 1703

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this article shall limit in any manner the police power of any city with reference to any such street or portion of street improved under the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
