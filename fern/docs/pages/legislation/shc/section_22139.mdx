# SHC § 22139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 6. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 22139

---

## Text

The tax collector shall, when any assessment is delinquent, mark opposite the assessment the word “delinquent,” and shall add 10 per cent to the amount of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
