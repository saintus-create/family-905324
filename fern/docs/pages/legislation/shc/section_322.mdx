# SHC § 322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 322

---

## Text

Route 22 is from:

(a) Route 1 near Long Beach to Route 405.

(b) Route 405 to Route 55 near Orange.
