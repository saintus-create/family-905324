# SHC § 36511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36511

---

## Text

“Parking and business improvement area,” or “area,” means an area designated as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 591, Sec. 2.)
