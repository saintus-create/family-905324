# SHC § 9402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9402

---

## Text

The refunding bonds shall represent and be secured by said reassessments and any later reassessments which may be levied or issued upon the same property in lieu of said assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
