# SHC § 9544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 2. Reassessment Proceedings

**Citation:** SHC § 9544

---

## Text

The auditor shall keep a record in his office showing the several installments of principal and interest on the reassessments which are to be collected in each year during the term of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
