# SHC § 30796.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 7. San Diego-Coronado Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30796.4

---

## Text

The net revenues received by the department from the operation of the ferry system shall be deposited in the San Diego-Coronado Toll Bridge Revenue Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1774.)
