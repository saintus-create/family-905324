# SHC § 886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 886

---

## Text

There is in the department a bicycle facilities coordinator who is responsible for the administration of bicycle-related activities of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 517, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
