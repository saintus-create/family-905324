# SHC § 31784.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 8. Board of Parking Place Commissioners

**Citation:** SHC § 31784.1

---

## Text

The board may, if it so desires, operate the parking places, or any thereof, as free public parking places without fee or charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 202.)
