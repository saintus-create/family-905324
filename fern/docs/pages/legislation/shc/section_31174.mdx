# SHC § 31174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 31174

---

## Text

Any money or funds which may by law be invested in bonds of the state, cities, counties, or school districts in the state may be invested in the bonds issued by the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
