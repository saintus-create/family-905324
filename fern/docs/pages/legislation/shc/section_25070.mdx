# SHC § 25070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. District Boards of Directors

**Citation:** SHC § 25070

---

## Text

The district shall be managed by, and the powers conferred upon the district shall be exercised by a board of directors appointed as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
