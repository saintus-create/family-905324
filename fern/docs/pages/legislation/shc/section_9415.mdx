# SHC § 9415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9415

---

## Text

If the city has appropriated money to assist in the refunding, such money shall be paid to the holders of outstanding bonds in accordance with the contract with the bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
