# SHC § 27143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Organization and Powers of the Board and the Executive Officers

**Citation:** SHC § 27143

---

## Text

All meetings of the board, whether regular or special, shall be open to the public. A majority of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
