# SHC § 2102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 3. Highway Users Tax Account

**Citation:** SHC § 2102

---

## Text

Net revenue derived from a tax means the amount of revenue derived from a tax that is deposited into the Highway Users Tax Account in the Transportation Tax Fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1153.)
