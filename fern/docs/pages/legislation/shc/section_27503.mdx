# SHC § 27503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 18. Golden Gate Bridge, Highway and Transportation District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 27503

---

## Text

“Transit services” means the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage by the district by means other than sightseeing ferryboats.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 678.)
