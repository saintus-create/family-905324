# SHC § 9230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 7. Recordation of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9230

---

## Text

Unmatured installments, interest, and penalties on unpaid reassessments shall not be deemed to be within the terms of any general warranty of title as to any parcel against which a reassessment is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
