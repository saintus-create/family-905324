# SHC § 2031.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 2. Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Program

**Citation:** SHC § 2031.5

---

## Text

For each fiscal year, the annual Budget Act shall contain an appropriation from the Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Account for the costs of administering this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 5, Sec. 36. (SB 1) Effective April 28, 2017.)
