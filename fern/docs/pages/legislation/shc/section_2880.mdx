# SHC § 2880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 4. ACTING UPON THE REPORT

**Citation:** SHC § 2880

---

## Text

If the hearing on the report is held before the legislative body, it may consider the report immediately after the hearing or at such time within 30 days as it may designate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
