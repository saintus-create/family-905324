# SHC § 5895.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 27. Construction of Sidewalks and Curbs > Article: 4. Construction Outside of Jurisdiction

**Citation:** SHC § 5895.54

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this article shall limit in any manner the police power of any city or county with reference to any sidewalk or curb contructed under the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 276.)
