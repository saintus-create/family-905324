# SHC § 30413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 8. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** SHC § 30413

---

## Text

Any money received for any such property shall be received into the funds for the bridge, other highway crossing, or transportation facilities for which the property was originally acquired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
