# SHC § 6468.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 4.5. Issuance of Bonds to Represent Unpaid Assessments Against Publicly Owned Property

**Citation:** SHC § 6468.5

---

## Text

Sections 5302.6, 8653, 8654, 8655, 8670, 8672, 8673 and 8851 of this code are applicable to bonds issued under the provisions of this chapter.
