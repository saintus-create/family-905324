# SHC § 11010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. PEDESTRIAN MALLS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 11010

---

## Text

If “the city” is a charter city, “similar special assessment law” shall include a procedural assessment ordinance of such city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 459.)
