# SHC § 27107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Incorporation of the District

**Citation:** SHC § 27107

---

## Text

The judgment of the superior court shall not be appealable, and all questions of fact therein contained shall be final and conclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
