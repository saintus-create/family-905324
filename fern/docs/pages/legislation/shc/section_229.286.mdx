# SHC § 229.286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1.5. Tourist Oriented Directional Signs > Article: 3. Eligibility

**Citation:** SHC § 229.286

---

## Text

Signs identifying the location or proximity of gambling activities are not eligible for placement under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
