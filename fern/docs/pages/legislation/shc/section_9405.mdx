# SHC § 9405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9405

---

## Text

In no event shall the first maturity of any refunding bonds be earlier than the second day of July next succeeding 10 months after the date of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
