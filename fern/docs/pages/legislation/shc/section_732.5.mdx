# SHC § 732.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 3. The Care and Protection of State Highways > Article: 3. Nuisance and Penal Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 732.5

---

## Text

Survey monuments shall be preserved, referenced, or replaced pursuant to Section 8771 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 458, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1995.)
