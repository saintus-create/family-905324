# SHC § 887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 2. Nonmotorized Transportation Facilities

**Citation:** SHC § 887

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “nonmotorized transportation facility” means a facility designed primarily for the use of pedestrians, bicyclists, or equestrians. It may be designed primarily for one or more of those uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 517, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
