# SHC § 22110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 5. Performing the Work

**Citation:** SHC § 22110

---

## Text

The city forester, under the direction of the board, shall do, or cause to be done, all of the work ordered to be done by the city council under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 170.)
