# SHC § 8282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 14. Annexation of Territory

**Citation:** SHC § 8282

---

## Text

The procedure prescribed in this part shall be the exclusive method of formation of such district and of annexation of territory to a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1230.)
