# SHC § 31775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 8. Board of Parking Place Commissioners

**Citation:** SHC § 31775

---

## Text

A commissioner may be removed by a four-fifths vote of the legislative body at any time.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
