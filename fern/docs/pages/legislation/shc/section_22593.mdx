# SHC § 22593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 2. Organization of the Assessment District; Levy of First Assessment > Article: 1. Formation of an Assessment District

**Citation:** SHC § 22593

---

## Text

Proceedings for the formation of the assessment district shall be abandoned if there is a majority protest, as defined in Section 53753 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 262, Sec. 57. Effective January 1, 2001.)
