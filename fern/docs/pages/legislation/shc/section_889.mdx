# SHC § 889

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 2.5. California Bicycle Routes of National, State, or Regional Significance

**Citation:** SHC § 889

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the California Bicycle Routes of National, State, or Regional Significance Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 396, Sec. 2. (AB 1464) Effective January 1, 2010.)
