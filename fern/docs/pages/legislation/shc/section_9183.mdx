# SHC § 9183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 5. Consent of Landowners to Refunding and Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9183

---

## Text

As to any land deeded to a city for delinquent taxes or for delinquent taxes and assessments, the legislative body of the city is authorized to consent to the refunding and reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
