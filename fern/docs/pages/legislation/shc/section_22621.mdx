# SHC § 22621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 3. Levy of Annual Assessments After Formation of an Assessment District

**Citation:** SHC § 22621

---

## Text

Proceedings shall be taken pursuant to this chapter for any fiscal year during which an assessment is to be levied and collected within an existing assessment district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
