# SHC § 18010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18010

---

## Text

“Treasurer” and “city treasurer” include any person or officer who has charge of and makes payment of city funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
