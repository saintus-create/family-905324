# SHC § 18313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18313

---

## Text

“City council” includes any body which by law is the legislative department of the government of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
