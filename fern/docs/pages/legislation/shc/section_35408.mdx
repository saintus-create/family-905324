# SHC § 35408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3. Bonds, Acquisitions and Improvements

**Citation:** SHC § 35408

---

## Text

The interest coupons on the bonds shall be numbered consecutively and signed by the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
