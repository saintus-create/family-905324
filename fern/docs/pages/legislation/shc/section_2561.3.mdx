# SHC § 2561.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 15. Freeway Service Patrols

**Citation:** SHC § 2561.3

---

## Text

The freeway service patrol in any particular area shall be operated pursuant to an agreement between the Department of the California Highway Patrol, the department, and the appropriate regional or local entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 578, Sec. 2. Effective September 16, 2002.)
