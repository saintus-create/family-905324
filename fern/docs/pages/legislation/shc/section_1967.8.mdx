# SHC § 1967.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. CITY STREETS > Chapter: 9. Treasure Island Transportation Management

**Citation:** SHC § 1967.8

---

## Text

Section 9400.8 of the Vehicle Code shall not apply to any fee imposed by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 317, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
