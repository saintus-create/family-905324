# SHC § 2704.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 20. Safe, Reliable High-Speed Passenger Train Bond Act for the 21st Century > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 2704.19

---

## Text

The bonds may be refunded in accordance with Article 6 (commencing with Section 16780) of the State General Obligation Bond Law. Approval by the electors of the state for the issuance of bonds shall include approval of the issuance of any bonds issued to refund any bonds originally issued or any previously issued refunding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 267, Sec. 9. Approved in Proposition 1A at the November 4, 2008, election.)
