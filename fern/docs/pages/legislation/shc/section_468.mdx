# SHC § 468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 468

---

## Text

Route 168 is from:

(a) Fresno to Huntington Lake.

(b) Camp Sabrina to Route 395.

(c) Route 395 at Big Pine to Route 266 at Oasis.
