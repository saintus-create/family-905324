# SHC § 22536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 22536

---

## Text

“Public utility” means any public utility subject to the jurisdiction of and regulated by the Public Utilities Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
