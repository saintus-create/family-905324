# SHC § 35106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 35106

---

## Text

“Parking places” includes parking lots, garages subsurface structures, and buildings for the parking of motor vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
