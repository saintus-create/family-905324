# SHC § 571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 571

---

## Text

Route 271 is from Route 101 near Cummings to Route 101 near the Humboldt-Mendocino county line.
