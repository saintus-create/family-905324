# SHC § 9316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 9. Sale for Delinquency > Article: 2. Procedure When Funds Not Available for Payment

**Citation:** SHC § 9316

---

## Text

The delay in execution of a certificate of sale to a city or to the State referred to in the next preceding section shall not extend the period of redemption from the tax sale; nor shall the rights or privileges of the property owner be thereby affected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
