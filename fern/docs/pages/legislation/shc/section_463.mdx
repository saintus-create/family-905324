# SHC § 463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 463

---

## Text

Route 163 is from Route 5 near Balboa Park in San Diego to Route 15 near Miramar Naval Air Station.
