# SHC § 35505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 3. Bonds for Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 35505

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold at any rate of interest not exceeding that stated in the resolution of intention. The maximum interest rate shall not exceed 7 percent a year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 234.)
