# SHC § 9250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 8. Payment and Collection of Reassessments > Article: 1. Reassessment Records

**Citation:** SHC § 9250

---

## Text

A copy of the resolution of the legislative body confirming the reassessments upon the security of which the refunding bonds are issued, shall be filed in the office of the auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
