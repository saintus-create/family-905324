# SHC § 6623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 11. Foreclosure of Bond Lien

**Citation:** SHC § 6623

---

## Text

The clerk of the court shall enter the judgment or decree of foreclosure in the action upon the delivery of the certificate of cancellation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 590. Effective January 1, 2003.)
