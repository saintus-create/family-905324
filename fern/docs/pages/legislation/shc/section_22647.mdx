# SHC § 22647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 4. Completion of Proceedings; Collection of Assessments > Article: 2. Collection of Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 22647

---

## Text

After collection by the county, the net amount of the assessments, after deduction of any compensation due the county for collection, shall be paid to the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
