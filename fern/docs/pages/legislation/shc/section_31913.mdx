# SHC § 31913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 11. Substitution of Other Lands, Property or Rights of Way for Those Previously Acquired

**Citation:** SHC § 31913

---

## Text

The legislative body may initiate a substitution proceeding by adopting a resolution proposing to order the making of a substitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1168.)
