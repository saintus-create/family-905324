# SHC § 36510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36510

---

## Text

“Improvement” means the acquisition, construction, installation, or maintenance of any tangible property with an estimated useful life of five years or more including, but not limited to, the following:

(a) Parking facilities.

(b) Benches.

(c) Trash receptacles.

(d) Street lighting.

(e) Decorations.

(f) Parks.

(g) Fountains.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 591, Sec. 2.)
