# SHC § 581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 581

---

## Text

Route 281 is from Route 29 south of Lakeport to Route 29 southerly of Konocti Bay and via the vicinity of Soda Bay.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1473.)
