# SHC § 1193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 4. Methods of Performing Highway Work > Article: 3. By Permanent Road Divisions

**Citation:** SHC § 1193

---

## Text

Any money in the fund of the division, after completion of, and final payment for, the work contracted for, shall remain in such fund and be expended solely in maintaining the highways of that division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
