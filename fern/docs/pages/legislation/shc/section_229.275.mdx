# SHC § 229.275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1.5. Tourist Oriented Directional Signs > Article: 3. Eligibility

**Citation:** SHC § 229.275

---

## Text

All sign contracts are for a two-year period and are subject to reevaluation by the department at the end of that period.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
