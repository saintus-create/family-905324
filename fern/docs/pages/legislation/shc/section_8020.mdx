# SHC § 8020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 2. The Ordinance of Intention

**Citation:** SHC § 8020

---

## Text

The city council of any city may change or modify the grade of streets therein, in the manner provided in this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
