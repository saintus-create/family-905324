# SHC § 30885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 3.5. Toll Bridge Revenues

**Citation:** SHC § 30885

---

## Text

“Toll bridge” means any state-owned bridge, including the approaches to the toll bridge from the nearest highway that is not for the exclusive use of toll bridge traffic, located within the region under the jurisdiction of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 2004.)
