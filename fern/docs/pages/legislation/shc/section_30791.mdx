# SHC § 30791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 6. San Mateo-Hayward and Dumbarton Bridges

**Citation:** SHC § 30791

---

## Text

The department is authorized to modify, improve, reconstruct, and remodel the San Mateo-Hayward Bridge and the Dumbarton Bridge as is necessary to adequately handle anticipated traffic and permit the collection of tolls.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2004.)
