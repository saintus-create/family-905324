# SHC § 9144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 3. Approval of Reassessment

## Text

If the assessment was spread as provided in Section 9102 the resolution shall state that fact.
