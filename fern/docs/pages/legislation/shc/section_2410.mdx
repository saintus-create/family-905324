# SHC § 2410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 9. Federal Aid for Bridge Reconstruction and Replacement > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** SHC § 2410

---

## Text

The department, cities, and counties are authorized to cooperate with the federal government in any inventory or classification of bridges requested by the federal government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 587.)
