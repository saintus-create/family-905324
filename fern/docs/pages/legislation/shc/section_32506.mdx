# SHC § 32506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 32506

---

## Text

“Authority” or “parking authority” means any of the public corporations created by Chapter 2 of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
