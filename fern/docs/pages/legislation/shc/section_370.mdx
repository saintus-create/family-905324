# SHC § 370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 370

---

## Text

Route 70 is from:

(a) Route 99 near Catlett Road to Route 20 in Marysville.

(b) Route 20 in Marysville to Route 395 near Hallelujah Junction via Quincy and Beckwourth Pass.
