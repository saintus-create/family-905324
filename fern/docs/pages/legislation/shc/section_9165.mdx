# SHC § 9165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 4. Election of Landowners to Pay Original Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9165

---

## Text

If any property owner does not elect to continue to pay assessments as provided in this chapter, then the legislative body shall make a reassessment against his property in the refunding proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
