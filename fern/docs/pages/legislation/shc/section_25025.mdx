# SHC § 25025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Organization and Reorganization of Districts

**Citation:** SHC § 25025

---

## Text

Districts, to be composed of two or more counties, may be created in accordance with this part for the purpose of improving public highways.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
