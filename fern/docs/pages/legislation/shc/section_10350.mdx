# SHC § 10350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 4.5. Change of Work, Boundaries of Assessment District, Amounts of Assessments or Proceedings

**Citation:** SHC § 10350

---

## Text

“Changes” as used in this chapter shall include corrections, alterations, modifications, additions, omissions, increases or decreases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 349.)
