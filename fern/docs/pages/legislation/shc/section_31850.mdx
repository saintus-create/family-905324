# SHC § 31850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 10. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** SHC § 31850

---

## Text

The legislative body may sell or lease any property acquired for parking places which is not needed for that public use.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 426.)
