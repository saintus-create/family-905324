# SHC § 30807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 1. Franchises

**Citation:** SHC § 30807

---

## Text

The department may make orders and prescribe rules and regulations with respect to toll roads, and toll bridges, or toll ferries in conformity with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
