# SHC § 2801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Text
