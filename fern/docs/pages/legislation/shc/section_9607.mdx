# SHC § 9607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9607

---

## Text

The last maturity of any refunding bonds shall not exceed 39 years from the second day of September next succeeding 12 months after the date of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1388, Sec. 25.)
