# SHC § 33100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 33100

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “bonds” means revenue bonds issued pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
