# SHC § 19093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 5. Calling and Conducting the Election

**Citation:** SHC § 19093

---

## Text

Every registered voter in the jurisdiction shall be entitled to vote at this election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 2003.)
