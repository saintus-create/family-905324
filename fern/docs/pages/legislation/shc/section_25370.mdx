# SHC § 25370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 16. General Bond Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 25370

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all bonds issued under the authority of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
