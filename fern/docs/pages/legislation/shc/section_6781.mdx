# SHC § 6781

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 3. Repairs and Improvements to Street Standards

**Citation:** SHC § 6781

---

## Text

The legislative body may require and order any or all of the work designated in the resolution to be done in the manner designated therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 156.)
