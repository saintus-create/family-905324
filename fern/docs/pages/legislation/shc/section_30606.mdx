# SHC § 30606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 1. San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30606

---

## Text

The cost of operating and maintaining the architectural lights on the toll bridge shall be paid by the department from toll bridge revenue funds available for the operation of the toll bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2004.)
