# SHC § 9205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 6. Protest and Hearing

**Citation:** SHC § 9205

---

## Text

The hearing may be continued from time to time by order entered in the minutes, but must be concluded within 30 days from the date orginally fixed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
