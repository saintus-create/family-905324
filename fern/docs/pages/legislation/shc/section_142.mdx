# SHC § 142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 142

---

## Text

All expense incurred in carrying out the objects of section 141 is part of the administrative expense of the department.
