# SHC § 9455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 12. Advance Retirement of Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9455

---

## Text

All refunding bonds so purchased or redeemed pursuant to call shall be canceled by the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
