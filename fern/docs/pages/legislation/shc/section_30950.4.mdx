# SHC § 30950.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 4.3. Metropolitan Transportation Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 30950.4

---

## Text

All authority of the California Transportation Commission as to the bay area bridges is transferred to the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 80. Effective January 1, 2004.)
