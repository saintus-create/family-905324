# SHC § 8550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8550

---

## Text

Whenever in this division a notice, resolution, order or other matter is required to be posted and the time or method for such posting is not specified, the clerk shall conspicuously post the notice, resolution, order or other matter on the door of the legislative body’s regular meeting place for not less than five days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
