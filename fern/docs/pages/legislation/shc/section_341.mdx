# SHC § 341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 341

---

## Text

Route 41 is from:

(a) Route 1 in Morro Bay to Route 46.

(b) Route 46 to Route 99 in Fresno.

(c) Route 99 in Fresno to Yosemite National Park.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1243, Sec. 31. Effective September 30, 1992.)
