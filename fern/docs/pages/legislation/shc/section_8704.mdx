# SHC § 8704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 9. ASSESSMENT LIENS AND REASSESSMENTS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8704

---

## Text

Unmatured installments, interest and penalties secured by any such lien or liens shall not be deemed to be within the terms of any general warranty of title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1465.)
