# SHC § 10006.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 10006.5

---

## Text

Subject to the limitation of Section 10204.1, “incidental expense” has the same meaning as specified in Section 5024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 104, Sec. 27.)
