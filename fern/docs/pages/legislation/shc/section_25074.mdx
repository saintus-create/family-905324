# SHC § 25074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. District Boards of Directors

**Citation:** SHC § 25074

---

## Text

If a position on the board of directors becomes vacant, the vacancy shall be filled by the appointing authority whose appointee last held the position.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
