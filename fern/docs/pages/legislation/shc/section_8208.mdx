# SHC § 8208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 8. The Bond Issue

**Citation:** SHC § 8208

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold by the commission in such manner and such quantities as it may determine, but no bond shall be sold for less than its face value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1147.)
