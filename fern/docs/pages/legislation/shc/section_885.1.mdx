# SHC § 885.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 8. Nonmotorized Transportation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 885.1

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Protected Bikeways Act of 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 495, Sec. 2. (AB 1193) Effective January 1, 2015.)
