# SHC § 31853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 10. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** SHC § 31853

---

## Text

All contracts for the lease or purchase, or both, of real or personal property shall be made by the legislative body.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 396.)
