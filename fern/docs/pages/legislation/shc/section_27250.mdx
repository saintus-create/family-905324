# SHC § 27250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Issuance of District Bonds and Disposition of Surplus Proceeds

**Citation:** SHC § 27250

---

## Text

A district formed under this part may issue either general obligation bonds or revenue bonds secured solely by tolls.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1592.)
