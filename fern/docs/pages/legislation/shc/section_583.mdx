# SHC § 583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 583

---

## Text

Route 283 is from Route 101 south of Rio Dell to the north end of the Eel River Bridge and Overhead in Rio Dell.
