# SHC § 621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 621

---

## Text

Route 805 is from Route 5 near San Ysidro to Route 5 north of La Jolla and easterly of existing Route 5.
