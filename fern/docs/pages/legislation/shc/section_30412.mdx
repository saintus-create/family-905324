# SHC § 30412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 8. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** SHC § 30412

---

## Text

Any agreement, lease, or conveyance authorized by this chapter shall be executed or accepted on behalf of the State by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
