# SHC § 27007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 27007

---

## Text

No irregularity or informality in conducting any district election which has been otherwise fairly conducted shall invalidate or affect the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
