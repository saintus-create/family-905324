# SHC § 8505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 8505

---

## Text

“Clerk” means:

(a) When used with reference to a county, the person or officer who is or acts as the clerk of the legislative body of the county.

(b) When used with reference to a city, the person who is or acts as clerk of the legislative body of the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
