# SHC § 434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 434

---

## Text

Route 134 is from Route 101 near Riverside Drive easterly to Route 210 via the vicinity of Glendale.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 385.)
