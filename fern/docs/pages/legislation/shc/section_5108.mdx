# SHC § 5108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 1.5. Elections

**Citation:** SHC § 5108

---

## Text

Approval of the owners of land for any improvements specified in Section 5101.4 shall be secured in accordance with the provisions of Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 194, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2004.)
