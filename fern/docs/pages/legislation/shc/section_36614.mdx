# SHC § 36614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 36614

---

## Text

“Property” means real property situated within a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36612 by Stats. 2014, Ch. 240, Sec. 8. (AB 2618) Effective January 1, 2015.)
