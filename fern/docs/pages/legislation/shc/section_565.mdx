# SHC § 565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 565

## Text

Route 265 is from Route 97 in Weed northwesterly to Route 5 at North Weed Interchange.
