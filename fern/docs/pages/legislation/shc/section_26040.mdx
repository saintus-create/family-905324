# SHC § 26040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Establishing District Boundaries

**Citation:** SHC § 26040

---

## Text

In fixing the final boundaries of the district the board of supervisors may exclude any lands which it finds have been improperly included.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 285.)
