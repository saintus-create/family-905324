# SHC § 9608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9608

---

## Text

The rate of interest on refunding bonds shall not exceed the rate set forth in the resolution of intention adopted pursuant to Section 9520.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
