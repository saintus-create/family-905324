# SHC § 490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 490

---

## Text

Route 190 is from Route 99 near Tipton to Route 127 near Death Valley Junction via the vicinity of Porterville, Camp Nelson, Olancha, and Death Valley.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 385.)
