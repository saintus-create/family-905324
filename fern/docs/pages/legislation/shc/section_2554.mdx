# SHC § 2554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. APPORTIONMENT AND EXPENDITURE OF HIGHWAY FUNDS > Chapter: 14. Service Authority for Freeway Emergencies

**Citation:** SHC § 2554

---

## Text

An authority may contract and may undertake any act convenient or necessary to carry out this chapter and any other law relating to the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2552 by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1132, Sec. 6. Effective September 22, 1988.)
