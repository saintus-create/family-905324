# SHC § 5835.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 26. Maintenance Districts

**Citation:** SHC § 5835.1

---

## Text

“Sewers” as used in this chapter includes lateral and collecting sewers, septic tanks, and all other means of handling, gathering, and disposing of sewage in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 144.)
