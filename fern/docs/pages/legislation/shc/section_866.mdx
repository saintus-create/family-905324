# SHC § 866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Highway Proceedings Affecting Private Property > Article: 2. Establishment of Boundaries

**Citation:** SHC § 866

---

## Text

The department shall restore any such signs which have been destroyed or become illegible in the third month after the first posting.

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 689.)
