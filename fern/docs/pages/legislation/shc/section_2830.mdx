# SHC § 2830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 2. INVESTIGATION AND REPORT ON THE PROJECT

**Citation:** SHC § 2830

---

## Text

When the report has been approved by the legislative body, the fact and date of such approval shall be indorsed thereon, and the report shall be filed with the clerk of the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
