# SHC § 9537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 2. Reassessment Proceedings

**Citation:** SHC § 9537

---

## Text

When recorded with the superintendent of streets and upon compliance with the provisions of Division 4.5 (commencing with Section 3100), the reassessment shall become a lien upon the various parcels of land assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
