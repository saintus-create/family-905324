# SHC § 31113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 2. Organization of the Authority

**Citation:** SHC § 31113

---

## Text

The authority may employ a secretary, a treasurer, an attorney, a chief engineer, and such other persons as are necessary to enable it properly to perform the duties imposed upon it by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
