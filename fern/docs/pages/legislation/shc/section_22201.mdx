# SHC § 22201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 8. Improvement Funds

**Citation:** SHC § 22201

---

## Text

Payments out of the special fund shall be made by the city treasurer to the board as funds are required for the prosecution and completion of the improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
