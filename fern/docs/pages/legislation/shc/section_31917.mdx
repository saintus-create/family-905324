# SHC § 31917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 11. Substitution of Other Lands, Property or Rights of Way for Those Previously Acquired

**Citation:** SHC § 31917

---

## Text

At the hearing all objections and protests shall be heard and considered. The hearing may be continued from time to time by order entered on the minutes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1168.)
