# SHC § 18321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 2. Authority to Maintain and Service

**Citation:** SHC § 18321

---

## Text

The improvement shall be for a period to commence at a time to be fixed by the city council but not exceeding five years from the date of commencement thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
