# SHC § 31210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 6. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** SHC § 31210

---

## Text

Any money received for any such property shall be received into the funds for the toll tunnel for which the property was originally acquired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
