# SHC § 9514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 9514

---

## Text

This division and all of its provisions shall be liberally construed in order that its purposes may be made effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
