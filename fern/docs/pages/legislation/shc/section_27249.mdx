# SHC § 27249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Issuance of District Bonds and Disposition of Surplus Proceeds

**Citation:** SHC § 27249

---

## Text

If the surplus from the proceeds of the bonds exceeds one year’s interest on the bonds, the balance shall be placed in the fund for the payment of the principal of such bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
