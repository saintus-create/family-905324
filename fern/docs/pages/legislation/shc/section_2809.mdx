# SHC § 2809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. THE SPECIAL ASSESSMENT INVESTIGATION, LIMITATION AND MAJORITY PROTEST ACT OF 1931 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 2809

---

## Text

As used in this division “legislative body” shall include the governing body of any district or other public corporation authorized by law to construct such public improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1070.)
