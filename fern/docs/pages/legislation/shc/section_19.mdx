# SHC § 19

**Citation:** SHC § 19

---

## Text

“Person” means any person, firm, partnership, association, corporation, organization, limited liability company, or business trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 224. Effective January 1, 1995.)
