# SHC § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6.5. Limited Maintenance Highway

**Citation:** SHC § 1500

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may adopt standards for limited maintenance of county highways which have a low traffic volume.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 629, Sec. 1.)
