# SHC § 8305.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** SHC § 8305.5

---

## Text

“Public entity” means a local agency or the California Transportation Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
