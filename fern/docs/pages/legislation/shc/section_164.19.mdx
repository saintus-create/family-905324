# SHC § 164.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 4.3. Transportation Funding Plan

**Citation:** SHC § 164.19

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 164.3, the eligible interregional and intercounty routes include the following:

Route 203.

Route 205.

Route 207.

Route 215.

Route 239.

Route 243.

Route 267.

Route 299, between Route 101 and Route 89, and between Route 139 and Route 395.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 598, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
