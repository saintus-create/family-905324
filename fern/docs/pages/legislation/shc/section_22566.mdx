# SHC § 22566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 4. Reports

**Citation:** SHC § 22566

---

## Text

A report shall be prepared for each fiscal year for which assessments are to be levied and collected to pay the costs of the improvements described in the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 630.)
