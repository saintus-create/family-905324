# SHC § 5360.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 16. Making and Confirming the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 5360.3

---

## Text

In assessing land, credit may be given for dedications and for improvements constructed at private expense.
