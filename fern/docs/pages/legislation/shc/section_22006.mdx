# SHC § 22006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 22006

---

## Text

“Improvement” includes the planting, maintenance, or removal of trees, and any and all acts necessarily incident thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 169.)
