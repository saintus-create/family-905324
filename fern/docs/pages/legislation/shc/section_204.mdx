# SHC § 204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 5. Funds for Highway and Public Mass Transit Guideway Purposes

**Citation:** SHC § 204

---

## Text

The department shall exercise the same powers and duties with respect to State highways within cities as with respect to other State highways.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 642.)
