# SHC § 27140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Organization and Powers of the Board and the Executive Officers

**Citation:** SHC § 27140

---

## Text

The board shall be the governing body of the district and shall outline rules of policy, approve plans, and vote or withhold appropriations to carry the same into effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 286.)
