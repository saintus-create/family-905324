# SHC § 9602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. REFUNDING ACT OF 1984 FOR 1915 IMPROVEMENT ACT BONDS > Chapter: 3. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9602

---

## Text

The refunding bonds shall be issued in a principal amount equal to the total principal amount of the reassessment as approved and confirmed by the legislative body pursuant to either Section 9525 or Section 9535.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1984.)
