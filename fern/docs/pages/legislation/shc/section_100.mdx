# SHC § 100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 100

---

## Text

Using existing resources, the department shall monitor the cumulative impact of fragmented gaps in the state highway system to identify safety and long-term maintenance issues.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 205. Effective October 12, 2001.)
