# SHC § 8345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 5. Reservation and Preservation of Easements > Article: 2. Preservation of Public Easements

**Citation:** SHC § 8345

---

## Text

As used in this article, “public body” means a city or special district as defined in Section 54775 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
