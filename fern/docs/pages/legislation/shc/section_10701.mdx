# SHC § 10701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 8. Deferral of Assessments

**Citation:** SHC § 10701

---

## Text

The legislative body may determine criteria that property owners must meet to qualify for deferral, and may determine procedures to ensure that the criteria are satisfied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1298, Sec. 29.)
