# SHC § 18305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18305

---

## Text

“Service,” and any of its variants, means the electrical current or energy or other illuminating agent used to light a street lighting system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
