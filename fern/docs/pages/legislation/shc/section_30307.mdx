# SHC § 30307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 1. The California Toll Bridge Authority Act > Article: 6. Custody and Use of Money

**Citation:** SHC § 30307

---

## Text

Warrants for payments to be made on account of the bonds shall be duly drawn by the State Controller upon request of the State Treasurer whenever the drawing of the warrants is required in order to make such payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
