# SHC § 5060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 2. Notice by Publication

**Citation:** SHC § 5060

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 6000) of Division 7 of Title 1 of the Government Code shall not have any application to any publication provided for in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 194, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
