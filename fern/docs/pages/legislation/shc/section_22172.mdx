# SHC § 22172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 7. Sale and Redemption of Delinquent Property

**Citation:** SHC § 22172

---

## Text

The applicant for a deed shall pay the tax collector one dollar ($1) for making the deed. If the city is the purchaser no charge shall be made for making the deed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
