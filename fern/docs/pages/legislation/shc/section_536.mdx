# SHC § 536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 536

---

## Text

Route 236 is from Route 9 in Boulder Creek to Route 9 near Waterman Gap via Governor’s Camp in Big Basin Redwoods State Park.
