# SHC § 10610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 7. Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 10610

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this chapter shall be conclusive evidence of the regularity of all proceedings under this division leading up to such issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 310.)
