# SHC § 351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 351

---

## Text

Route 51 is from Route 50 in Sacramento to Route 80 east of Sacramento.

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 292, Sec. 25.)
