# SHC § 30871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 3. Toll Bridges Under Franchises Granted Prior to 1929 and Toll Ferries Under Franchises Granted Prior to 1933

**Citation:** SHC § 30871

---

## Text

The proceeds of the license tax on ferries and toll-bridges shall be paid into the county treasury for the use of roads and highways, or may be used by the board of supervisors at any time in the purchase of toll-roads and toll-bridges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 176.)
