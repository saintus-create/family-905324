# SHC § 18011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18011

---

## Text

“City” includes all corporations organized and existing for municipal purposes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
