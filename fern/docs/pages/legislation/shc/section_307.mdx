# SHC § 307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 307

---

## Text

Route 7 is from the northerly boundary of the Federal Port of Entry near Calexico to Route 8 near El Centro.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1220, Sec. 31. Effective September 30, 1994.)
