# SHC § 1492.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 6. Obstructions and Injuries to County Highways

**Citation:** SHC § 1492.5

---

## Text

Survey monuments shall be preserved, referenced, or replaced pursuant to Section 8771 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 458, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1995.)
