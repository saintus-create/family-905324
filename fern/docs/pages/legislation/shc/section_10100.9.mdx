# SHC § 10100.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 2. Improvements Authorized

**Citation:** SHC § 10100.9

---

## Text

Prior to any hearing pursuant to Section 10100.8, the clerk shall cause notice of the hearing to be published pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1298, Sec. 21.5.)
