# SHC § 351.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 351.1

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 640, Route 51 shall be signed Interstate Business Loop 80.
