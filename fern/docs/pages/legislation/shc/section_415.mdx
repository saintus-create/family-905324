# SHC § 415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 415

---

## Text

Route 115 is from:

(a) Route 8 southeasterly of Holtville to Route 78.

(b) Route 78 east of Brawley to Route 111 at Calipatria.
