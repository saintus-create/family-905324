# SHC § 8316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CHANGE OF GRADE AND VACATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 8316

---

## Text

One or more streets, highways, and public service easements, whether or not contiguous, may be included and vacated in the same proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1050, Sec. 29.)
