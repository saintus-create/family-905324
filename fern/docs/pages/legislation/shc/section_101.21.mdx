# SHC § 101.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. The Department of Transportation

**Citation:** SHC § 101.21

---

## Text

The department shall, in the next revision of the California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices, allow supplemental destination signs for a state special school that is located within five miles of the highway, regardless of whether the state special school is located in a major metropolitan area, urbanized area, or rural area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 152, Sec. 1. (AB 2367) Effective January 1, 2025.)
