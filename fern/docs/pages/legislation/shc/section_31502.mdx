# SHC § 31502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** SHC § 31502

---

## Text

As used in this part, “parking places” includes parking lots, garages, buildings, and other improvements for the parking of motor vehicles.
