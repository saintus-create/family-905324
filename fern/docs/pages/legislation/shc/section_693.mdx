# SHC § 693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 3. The Care and Protection of State Highways > Article: 2. Permit Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 693

## Text

All sums payable by the grantee under any such franchise shall be paid to the grantor city or county.
