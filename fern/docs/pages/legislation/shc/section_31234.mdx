# SHC § 31234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 8. Local Cooperation

**Citation:** SHC § 31234

---

## Text

The collection of tolls shall be continued on the toll tunnel until all bonds issued pursuant to this chapter for its construction are fully redeemed and paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
