# SHC § 31129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** SHC § 31129

---

## Text

The authority may also require the holder of any such permit to pay a fee of not to exceed five dollars ($5) a month to cover printing and other overhead expense, the proceeds thereof to be deposited in the fund from which such expenses are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
