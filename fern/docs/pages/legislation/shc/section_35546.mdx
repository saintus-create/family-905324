# SHC § 35546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 3.5. Assessments and Bonds for Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 35546

---

## Text

The street superintendent shall record the reassessment with the clerk’s certificate.
