# SHC § 1179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 4. Methods of Performing Highway Work > Article: 3. By Permanent Road Divisions

**Citation:** SHC § 1179

---

## Text

The tax so levied shall be collected in the same manner as county taxes and, when collected, shall be paid into the county treasury for the use of the division in which the tax is collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 97, Sec. 3.)
