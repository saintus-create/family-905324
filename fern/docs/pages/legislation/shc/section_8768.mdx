# SHC § 8768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 11.1. ALTERNATIVE PROCEDURE FOR COLLECTING ASSESSMENTS AND ADVANCE RETIREMENT OF BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8768

---

## Text

The treasurer shall select bonds for retirement in such a way that the ratio of outstanding bonds to issued bonds shall be approximately the same in each annual series insofar as possible. Within each annual series, bonds shall be selected for retirement by lot.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1388, Sec. 21.)
