# SHC § 31071.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 4.6. State-Owned Toll Bridge Seismic Retrofit Financing Act of 2001

**Citation:** SHC § 31071.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, during the construction period, all revenues generated from the toll surcharge shall be available to the department only for the construction and financing purposes of the toll bridge seismic retrofit program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 907, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
