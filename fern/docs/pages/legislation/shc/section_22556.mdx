# SHC § 22556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions > Article: 3. Notice

**Citation:** SHC § 22556

---

## Text

Prior to levying a new assessment pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 22585), the legislative body shall cause notice of the public hearing to be given pursuant to Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 262, Sec. 53. Effective January 1, 2001.)
