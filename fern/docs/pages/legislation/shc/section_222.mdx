# SHC § 222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 7. Safety Roadside Rests

**Citation:** SHC § 222

---

## Text

The department may accept grants on behalf of the State and may accept financial or other assistance for, or in aid of, safety roadside rests.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 992.)
