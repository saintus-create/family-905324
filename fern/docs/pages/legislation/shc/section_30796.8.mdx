# SHC § 30796.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 7. San Diego-Coronado Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30796.8

---

## Text

The department may permit any class of vehicles to use the bridge on a credit basis without posting the security required by Section 30158.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1151, Sec. 2.)
