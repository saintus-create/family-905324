# SHC § 30761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 2. Particular Highway Crossings > Article: 4.5. Antioch Bridge

**Citation:** SHC § 30761

---

## Text

The department is authorized to modify, improve, reconstruct, and remodel the Antioch Bridge as is necessary to adequately handle anticipated traffic and permit the collection of tolls.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 715, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2004.)
