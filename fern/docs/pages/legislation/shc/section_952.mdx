# SHC § 952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Boards of Supervisors

**Citation:** SHC § 952

---

## Text

Such board shall cause properly inscribed guideposts to be erected and maintained at all county highway crossings and forks lying outside of any city.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 29.)
