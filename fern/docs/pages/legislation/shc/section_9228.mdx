# SHC § 9228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 7. Recordation of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9228

---

## Text

The lien of the reassessment shall be given superiority and priority as of the date that the original assessment became a lien upon the property reassessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
