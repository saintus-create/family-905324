# SHC § 3123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. NOTICE OF SPECIAL ASSESSMENT, SPECIAL TAX, AND FORECLOSURE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. RECORDATION OF NOTICE OF PENDENCY OF SALE OR FORECLOSURE

**Citation:** SHC § 3123

---

## Text

Such notice of pendency shall be in addition to any notice required to be given by the principal act or bond act pursuant to which such sale or foreclosure is being taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1210.)
