# SHC § 9141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 3. Approval of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9141

---

## Text

If the legislative body finds the statement of the auditor to be correct upon examination, it shall approve the statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
