# SHC § 8753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 11. ADVANCE RETIREMENT OF BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8753

---

## Text

The cost of serving or publishing the notice of advanced maturity shall be paid from the redemption fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
