# SHC § 25002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 25002

---

## Text

“District,” means a joint highway district, unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 284.)
