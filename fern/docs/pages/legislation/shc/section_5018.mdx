# SHC § 5018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** SHC § 5018

---

## Text

“Place” includes any public park or pleasure ground and common which has been dedicated and accepted according to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
