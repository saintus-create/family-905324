# SHC § 10006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 10006

---

## Text

“Install” includes construct, reconstruct, extend, repair, and maintain.
