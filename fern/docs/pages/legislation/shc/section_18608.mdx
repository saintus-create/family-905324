# SHC § 18608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 18608

---

## Text

“Clerk” or “city clerk” means any person or officer who is or acts as clerk of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
