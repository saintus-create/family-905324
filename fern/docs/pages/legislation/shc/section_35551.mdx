# SHC § 35551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 4. Parking Place Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 35551

---

## Text

The commission shall consist of three members, each of whom shall be a resident and qualified elector of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
