# SHC § 10422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. THE MUNICIPAL IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1913 > Chapter: 5. Levying and Collecting the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 10422

---

## Text

The deed of the tax collector conveys the title in fee to the property and entitles the grantee, upon the receipt thereof, to immediate possession of the property described in the deed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
