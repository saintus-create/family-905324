# SHC § 9401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 11. Issuance of Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 9401

---

## Text

In no event shall refunding bonds be issued for more than the par value of the outstanding bonds and the due and unpaid interest coupons to be refunded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
