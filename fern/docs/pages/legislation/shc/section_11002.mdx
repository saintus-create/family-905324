# SHC § 11002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. PEDESTRIAN MALLS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 11002

---

## Text

“City” includes every county, city, and city and county within this State. “The city” means the particular county, city, or city and county, acting pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1960, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 33.)
