# SHC § 30891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 3.5. Toll Bridge Revenues

**Citation:** SHC § 30891

---

## Text

The commission may retain, for its cost in administering this article, an amount not to exceed one-quarter of 1 percent of the revenues allocated by it pursuant to Section 30892 and of the revenues allocated by it pursuant to Sections 30913, 30914, and 30914.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 650, Sec. 5. (SB 595) Effective January 1, 2018.)
