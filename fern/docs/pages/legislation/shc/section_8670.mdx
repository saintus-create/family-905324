# SHC § 8670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 7. BOND RECORDS AND PAYMENTS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8670

---

## Text

The bonds and interest shall be paid at the office of the treasurer or of another paying agent designated by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1332, Sec. 8. Effective October 1, 1985.)
