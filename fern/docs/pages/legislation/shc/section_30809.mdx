# SHC § 30809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 3. Toll Bridges, Toll Roads, and Toll Ferries Generally > Article: 1. Franchises

**Citation:** SHC § 30809

---

## Text

The enforcement and administration of this article is declared to be a part of the highway program of the state, and all necessary expenditures of the department therefor are payable out of the State Highway Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 777, Sec. 246.)
