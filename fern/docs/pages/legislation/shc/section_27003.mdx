# SHC § 27003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. HIGHWAY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 27003

---

## Text

As used in this part, “contiguous counties” include those counties separated by any of the waters of this State.
