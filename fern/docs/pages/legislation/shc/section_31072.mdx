# SHC § 31072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 4.6. State-Owned Toll Bridge Seismic Retrofit Financing Act of 2001

**Citation:** SHC § 31072

---

## Text

Any federal funds received by the department as a direct loan or line of credit under TIFIA are hereby appropriated to the department for transfer to the account for the purposes of that account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 907, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
