# SHC § 9128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 2. Contributions and Appropriations

**Citation:** SHC § 9128

---

## Text

Such appropriation or contribution may be of money to be received from the collection of such delinquent taxes or may be made by authorizing the cancellation of the taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
