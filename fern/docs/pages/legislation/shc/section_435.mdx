# SHC § 435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 435

---

## Text

Route 135 is from:

(a) Route 101 near Los Alamos to Route 1 south of Orcutt.

(b) Route 1 near Orcutt to Route 101 in Santa Maria.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1243, Sec. 40. Effective September 30, 1992.)
