# SHC § 5901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. THE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1911 > Chapter: 29.1. Clean Energy Assessment Contracts

**Citation:** SHC § 5901

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to any public agency that does not use a program administrator to administer a PACE program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 484, Sec. 1. (SB 242) Effective January 1, 2018.)
