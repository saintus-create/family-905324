# SHC § 1685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. COUNTY HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 9. Cooperation by Counties > Article: 2. Cooperation with Cities

**Citation:** SHC § 1685

---

## Text

Boards of supervisors may enter into contracts or agreements with the legislative body of any city for the purposes specified in Section 1803.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
