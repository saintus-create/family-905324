# SHC § 9226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 7. Recordation of Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9226

---

## Text

When recorded with the superintendent of streets the reassessment shall become a lien upon the various parcels of land assessed and shall be in lieu of the assessments orginally levied thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
