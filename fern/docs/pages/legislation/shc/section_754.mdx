# SHC § 754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 3. The Care and Protection of State Highways > Article: 5. Control of Junkyards, Scrap Metal Processing Facilities, and Automobile Dismantling Facilities

**Citation:** SHC § 754

---

## Text

Any junkyard which is established or maintained in violation of the provisions of this article or the regulations prescribed thereunder is a public nuisance and may be removed or otherwise disposed of by any public employee or entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 919.)
