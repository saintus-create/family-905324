# SHC § 456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 456

---

## Text

Route 156 is from:

(a) Route 1 near Castroville to Route 101 near Prunedale.

(b) Route 101 to Route 152 passing near San Juan Bautista and Hollister.
