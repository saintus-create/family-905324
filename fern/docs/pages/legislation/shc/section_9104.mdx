# SHC § 9104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. REFUNDING OF BONDS ISSUED UNDER THE “IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915” > Chapter: 3. Reassessment > Article: 1. Computing the Reassessment

**Citation:** SHC § 9104

---

## Text

The amount of the reassessment upon any parcel shall not exceed the unpaid amount of the original assessment upon that parcel and the unpaid annual interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 463.)
