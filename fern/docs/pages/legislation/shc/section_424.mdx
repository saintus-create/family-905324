# SHC § 424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 424

---

## Text

Route 124 is from:

(a) Route 88 south of Ione to Route 104.

(b) Route 104 to Route 16 near Waits Station.
