# SHC § 22013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. TREE PLANTING, LANDSCAPING, AND LIGHTING > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** SHC § 22013

---

## Text

“Tax collector” includes any body, board, bureau or officer charged with the duty of collecting assessments for a city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 79.)
