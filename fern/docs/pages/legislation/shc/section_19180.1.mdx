# SHC § 19180.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. STREET LIGHTING > Chapter: 10. Estimating and Levying the Assessment

**Citation:** SHC § 19180.1

---

## Text

Whenever the board of supervisors levies an assessment authorized by Section 19180, the assessment shall be levied pursuant to Chapter 6.1 (commencing with Section 54701) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 487, Sec. 21. Effective July 10, 1982.)
