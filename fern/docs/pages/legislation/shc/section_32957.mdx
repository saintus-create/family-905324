# SHC § 32957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 4. Property

**Citation:** SHC § 32957

---

## Text

Sections 32952 to 32956, inclusive, shall not apply to the lease of a project by the authority to the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 965.)
