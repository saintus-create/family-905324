# SHC § 35555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PARKING > Chapter: 4. Parking Place Commission

**Citation:** SHC § 35555

---

## Text

A commissioner may be removed by a four-fifths vote of the legislative body at any time.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 192.)
