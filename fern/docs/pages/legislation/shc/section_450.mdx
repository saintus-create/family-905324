# SHC § 450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 450

---

## Text

Route 150 is from Route 101 near the Ventura-Santa Barbara county line to Route 126 near Santa Paula.
