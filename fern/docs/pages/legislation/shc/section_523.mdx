# SHC § 523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

**Citation:** SHC § 523

## Text

Route 223 is from:

(a) Route 5 to Route 99 south of Greenfield.

(b) Route 99 south of Greenfield to Route 58.
