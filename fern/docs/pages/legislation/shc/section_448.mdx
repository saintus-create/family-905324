# SHC § 448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 2. The State Highway System > Article: 3. The State Highway Routes

## Text

Route 148 is from:

(a) Route 5 near Sacramento to Route 143 south of Route 16.

(b) Route 143 south of Route 16 to Route 65.
