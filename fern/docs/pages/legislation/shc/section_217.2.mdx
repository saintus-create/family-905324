# SHC § 217.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE HIGHWAYS > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 6.5. Job Order Contracting

**Citation:** SHC § 217.2

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until December 31, 2033, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 58, Sec. 4. (SB 146) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of December 31, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6.5, commencing with Section 217.)
