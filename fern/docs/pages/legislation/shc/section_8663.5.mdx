# SHC § 8663.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. THE IMPROVEMENT BOND ACT OF 1915 > Part: 6.5. VARIABLE INTEREST RATE BONDS > Chapter: 2. Notice by Posting

**Citation:** SHC § 8663.5

---

## Text

Interest on all unpaid assessments shall run from the date of the bonds, and shall be computed for each interest pay period at the date determined pursuant to the terms of the bond for that interest pay period.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1332, Sec. 7. Effective October 1, 1985.)
