# SHC § 31150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. TOLL FACILITIES AND RELATED MATTERS > Chapter: 5. El Dorado County Toll Tunnel Authority Act > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** SHC § 31150

---

## Text

The purchase price of all bonds issued pursuant to this chapter shall be paid to the treasurer for the account of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 923.)
