# CIV § 2961

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 3. Disclosures on Purchase Money Liens on Residential Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2961

---

## Text

Every disclosure required by this article and every act which is to be performed in making that disclosure shall be made in good faith. For the purposes of this article, “good faith” means honesty in fact in the conduct of the transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 968, Sec. 1. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 968.)
