# CIV § 2139

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 3. Carriage of Property > Article: 4. Freightage

**Citation:** CIV § 2139

---

## Text

No freightage can be charged upon the natural increase of freight.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
