# CIV § 1917.332

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 7. Shared Appreciation Loans for Seniors > Article: 2. Terms and Conditions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.332

---

## Text

Any provision in any loan made pursuant to this chapter for the discontinuation or termination of a monthly annuity other than upon the occurrence of a maturity event is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1701, Sec. 1.)
