# CIV § 1798.99.23

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.46. Online Violence Prevention Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.99.23

---

## Text

This title does not apply to a social media platform with fewer than 1,000,000 discrete monthly users.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 881, Sec. 1. (SB 1056) Effective January 1, 2023.)
