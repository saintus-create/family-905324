# CIV § 2989.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2d. Vehicle Leasing Act

**Citation:** CIV § 2989.8

---

## Text

Any person who shall knowingly and willfully violate any provision of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1284.)
