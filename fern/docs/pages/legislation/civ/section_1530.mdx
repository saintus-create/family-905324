# CIV § 1530

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 5. Novation [1530. - [1533.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1530

## Text

Novation is the substitution of a new obligation for an existing one.
