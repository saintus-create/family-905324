# CIV § 55.61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2.55. Small Business Gender Discrimination In Services Compliance Act > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 55.61

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Small Business Gender Discrimination in Services Compliance Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 156, Sec. 1. (AB 1615) Effective January 1, 2018.)
