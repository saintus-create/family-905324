# CIV § 1785.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6. CONSUMER CREDIT REPORTING AGENCIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1785.6

---

## Text

The notices and disclosures to consumers provided for in this title shall be required to be made only to those consumers who have a mailing address in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 285, Sec. 2. Effective August 2, 1993.)
