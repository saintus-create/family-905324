# CIV § 2846

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 7. Position of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2846

---

## Text

A surety may compel his principal to perform the obligation when due.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
