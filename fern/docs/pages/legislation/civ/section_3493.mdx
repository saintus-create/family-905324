# CIV § 3493

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PUBLIC NUISANCES

**Citation:** CIV § 3493

---

## Text

A private person may maintain an action for a public nuisance, if it is specially injurious to himself, but not otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
