# CIV § 2308

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2308

---

## Text

A consideration is not necessary to make an authority, whether precedent or subsequent, binding upon the principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
