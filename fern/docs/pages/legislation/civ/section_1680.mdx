# CIV § 1680

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES > Chapter: 2. Default on Real Property Purchase Contract

**Citation:** CIV § 1680

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects any right a party to a contract for the purchase and sale of real property may have to obtain specific performance.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 198.)
