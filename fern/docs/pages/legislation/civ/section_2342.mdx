# CIV § 2342

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 4. Obligations of Agents to Third Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2342

---

## Text

One who assumes to act as an agent thereby warrants, to all who deal with him in that capacity, that he has the authority which he assumes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
