# CIV § 1951.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 2. Hiring of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1951.8

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 1951.2 or 1951.4 affects the right of the lessor under a lease of real property to equitable relief where such relief is appropriate.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 89.)
