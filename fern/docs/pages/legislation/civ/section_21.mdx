# CIV § 21

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 21

---

## Text

This Act, whenever cited, enumerated, referred to, or amended, may be designated simply as “The Civil Code,” adding, when necessary, the number of the section.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
