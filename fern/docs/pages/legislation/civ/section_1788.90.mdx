# CIV § 1788.90

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.7. Educational Debt Collection Practices

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.90

---

## Text

This title shall be known, and may be cited, as the Educational Debt Collection Practices Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 518, Sec. 1. (AB 1313) Effective January 1, 2020.)
