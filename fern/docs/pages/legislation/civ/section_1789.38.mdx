# CIV § 1789.38

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6F. CHECK CASHERS

**Citation:** CIV § 1789.38

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
