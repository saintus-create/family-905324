# CIV § 1421

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. WATER RIGHTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1421

---

## Text

The Recorder of each county must keep a book, in which he must record the notices provided for in this Title.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
