# CIV § 1486

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1486

---

## Text

An offer of partial performance is of no effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
