# CIV § 1568.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Consent

**Citation:** CIV § 1568.5

---

## Text

A representation by a minor that the minor’s parent or legal guardian has consented shall not be considered to be consent for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 28, Sec. 1. (AB 891) Effective January 1, 2022.)
