# CIV § 815.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Conservation Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 815.10

---

## Text

A conservation easement granted pursuant to this chapter constitutes an enforceable restriction, for purposes of Section 402.1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 777, Sec. 1.)
