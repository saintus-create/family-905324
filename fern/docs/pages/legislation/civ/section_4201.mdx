# CIV § 4201

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 2. Application of Act

**Citation:** CIV § 4201

---

## Text

Nothing in this act may be construed to apply to a real property development that does not contain common area. This section is declaratory of existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
