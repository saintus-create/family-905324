# CIV § 1437

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 3. Conditional Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 1437

---

## Text

Conditions concurrent are those which are mutually dependent, and are to be performed at the same time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
