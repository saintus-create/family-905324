# CIV § 1798.196

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.196

---

## Text

This title is intended to supplement federal and state law, if permissible, but shall not apply if such application is preempted by, or in conflict with, federal law or the United States or California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 55, Sec. 3) by Stats. 2018, Ch. 735, Sec. 15. (SB 1121) Effective September 23, 2018. Section operative January 1, 2020, pursuant to Section 1798.198.)
