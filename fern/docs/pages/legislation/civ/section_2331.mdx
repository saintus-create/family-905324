# CIV § 2331

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 3. Mutual Obligations of Principals and Third Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2331

---

## Text

A principal is bound by an incomplete execution of an authority, when it is consistent with the whole purpose and scope thereof, but not otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
