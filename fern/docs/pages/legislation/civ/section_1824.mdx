# CIV § 1824

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 1. Deposit in General > Article: 2. Obligations of the Depositary

**Citation:** CIV § 1824

---

## Text

A depositary must deliver the thing deposited at his residence or place of business, as may be most convenient for him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
