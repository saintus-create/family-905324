# CIV § 1614

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 5. Consideration

**Citation:** CIV § 1614

---

## Text

A written instrument is presumptive evidence of a consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
