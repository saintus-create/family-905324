# CIV § 1917.044

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 4. Shared Appreciation Loans of E.R.I.S.A. Pension Funds > Article: 4. Annual Appraisals

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.044

---

## Text

The qualifications of the appraisers may be specified by the terms of the shared appreciation loan for purposes of this article and Article 5 (commencing with Section 1917.050).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11. Inoperative January 1, 1990, by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11.5.)
