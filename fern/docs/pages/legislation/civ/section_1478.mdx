# CIV § 1478

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 1. Performance [1473. - [1479.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1478

---

## Text

Performance of an obligation for the delivery of money only, is called payment.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
