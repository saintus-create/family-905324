# CIV § 3283

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 1. Damages in General > Article: 1. General Principles

**Citation:** CIV § 3283

---

## Text

Damages may be awarded, in a judicial proceeding, for detriment resulting after the commencement thereof, or certain to result in the future.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
