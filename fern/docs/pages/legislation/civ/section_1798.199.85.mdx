# CIV § 1798.199.85

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.199.85

---

## Text

Any decision of the agency with respect to a complaint or administrative fine shall be subject to judicial review in an action brought by an interested party to the complaint or administrative fine and shall be subject to an abuse of discretion standard.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 2020, by initiative Proposition 24, Sec. 24.16. Effective December 16, 2020. Operative December 16, 2020, pursuant to Sec. 31 of Proposition 24.)
