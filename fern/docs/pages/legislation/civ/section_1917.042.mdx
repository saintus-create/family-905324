# CIV § 1917.042

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 4. Shared Appreciation Loans of E.R.I.S.A. Pension Funds > Article: 4. Annual Appraisals

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.042

---

## Text

If the appraisal by the appraiser selected by the borrower is lower in amount than the appraisal by the appraiser selected by the lender, the amount to be used to calculate the annual adjustment shall be one-half of the sum of the two appraisals.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11. Inoperative January 1, 1990, by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11.5.)
