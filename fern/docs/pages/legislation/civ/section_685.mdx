# CIV § 685

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 685

---

## Text

An interest in common is one owned by several persons, not in joint ownership or partnership.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
