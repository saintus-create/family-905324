# CIV § 3448

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. VOID AND VOIDABLE TRANSFERS AND UNDERTAKINGS > Chapter: 3. Undertaking in Voidable Transfer Action

**Citation:** CIV § 3448

---

## Text

The undertaking shall be in the lesser of the following amounts:

(a) Double the value of the property or obligation.

(b) Double the amount of the creditor’s claim in the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 517, Sec. 93.)
