# CIV § 1852

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 2. Deposit for Keeping > Article: 3. Storage

**Citation:** CIV § 1852

---

## Text

A depositary for hire must use at least ordinary care for the preservation of the thing deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
