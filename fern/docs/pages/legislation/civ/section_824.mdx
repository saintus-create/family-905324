# CIV § 824

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 1. Incidents of Ownership

**Citation:** CIV § 824

---

## Text

Rent due upon a lease for life may be recovered in the same manner as upon a lease for years.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
