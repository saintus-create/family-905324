# CIV § 3425.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. UNIFORM SINGLE PUBLICATION ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 3425.5

---

## Text

This title shall not be retroactive as to causes of action existing on its effective date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 867.)
