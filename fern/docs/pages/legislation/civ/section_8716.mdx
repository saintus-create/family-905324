# CIV § 8716

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 7. Security for Large Project > Article: 2. Security Requirement

**Citation:** CIV § 8716

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect any statute providing for mechanics liens, stop payment notices, bond remedies, or prompt payment rights of a subcontractor, including the direct contractor’s payment responsibilities under Section 7108.5 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
