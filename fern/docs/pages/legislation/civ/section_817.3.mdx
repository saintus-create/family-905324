# CIV § 817.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Housing Cooperatives and Housing Cooperative Trusts

**Citation:** CIV § 817.3

---

## Text

Each entity named as a sponsor organization of a workforce housing cooperative trust formed pursuant to Section 817 shall have the legal standing of a member unless it revokes, in writing, its sponsorship.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 520, Sec. 2. (AB 1246) Effective January 1, 2010.)
