# CIV § 799.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 9. Subdivisions, Cooperatives, and Condominiums

**Citation:** CIV § 799.6

---

## Text

No agreement shall contain any provision by which the purchaser waives his or her rights under the provisions of this article. Any such waiver shall be deemed contrary to public policy and void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 519, Sec. 16.)
