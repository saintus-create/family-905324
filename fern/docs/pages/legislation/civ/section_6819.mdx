# CIV § 6819

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 7. Assessments and Assessment Collection > Article: 2. Assessment Payment and Delinquency

**Citation:** CIV § 6819

---

## Text

An association that fails to comply with the procedures set forth in this section shall, prior to recording a lien, recommence the required notice process. Any costs associated with recommencing the notice process shall be borne by the association and not by the owner of a separate interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
