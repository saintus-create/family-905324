# CIV § 1523

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 4. Accord and Satisfaction [[1521.] - 1526.]

**Citation:** CIV § 1523

---

## Text

Acceptance, by the creditor, of the consideration of an accord extinguishes the obligation, and is called satisfaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
