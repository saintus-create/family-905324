# CIV § 2022

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 2. Particular Employments > Article: 2. Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2022

---

## Text

A mere agent of an agent is not responsible as such to the principal of the latter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
