# CIV § 3425.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. UNIFORM SINGLE PUBLICATION ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 3425.2

---

## Text

This act shall be so interpreted as to effectuate its purpose to make uniform the law of those states or jurisdictions which enact it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 867.)
