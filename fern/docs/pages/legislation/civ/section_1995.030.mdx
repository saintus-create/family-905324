# CIV § 1995.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 6. Assignment and Sublease > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1995.030

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 1995.270, this chapter applies to a lease executed before, on, or after January 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 982, Sec. 2.)
