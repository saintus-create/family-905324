# CIV § 699

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 699

---

## Text

Future interests pass by succession, will, and transfer, in the same manner as present interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
