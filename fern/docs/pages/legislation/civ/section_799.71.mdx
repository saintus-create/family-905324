# CIV § 799.71

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 5. Defaulting Residents

**Citation:** CIV § 799.71

---

## Text

Evictions pursuant to this article shall be subject to the requirements set forth in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 1159) of Title 3 of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
