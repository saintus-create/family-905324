# CIV § 5385

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 6. Association Governance > Article: 9. Managing Agent

**Citation:** CIV § 5385

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “managing agent” does not include a full-time employee of the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
