# CIV § 800.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 2. Rental Agreement

**Citation:** CIV § 800.22

---

## Text

The rental agreement may include other provisions permitted by law, but need not include specific language contained in state or local laws not a part of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
