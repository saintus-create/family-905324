# CIV § 1052

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 3. Mode of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1052

---

## Text

A transfer may be made without writing, in every case in which a writing is not expressly required by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
