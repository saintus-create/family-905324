# CIV § 1812.609

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.95. AUCTIONEER AND AUCTION COMPANIES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.609

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2003.)
