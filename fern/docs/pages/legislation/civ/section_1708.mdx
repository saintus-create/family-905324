# CIV § 1708

**Hierarchy:** Part: 3. OBLIGATIONS IMPOSED BY LAW > Title: 5. EXTINCTION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1708

---

## Text

Every person is bound, without contract, to abstain from injuring the person or property of another, or infringing upon any of his or her rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 38.5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
