# CIV § 3501

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PRIVATE NUISANCES

**Citation:** CIV § 3501

---

## Text

The remedies against a private nuisance are:

1. A civil action; or,

2. Abatement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
