# CIV § 799.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 9. Subdivisions, Cooperatives, and Condominiums

**Citation:** CIV § 799.3

---

## Text

The ownership or management shall not require the removal of a mobilehome from a subdivision, cooperative, or condominium for mobilehomes, or resident-owned mobilehome park in the event of its sale to a third party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 72, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
