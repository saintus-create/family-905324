# CIV § 1499

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1499

---

## Text

A debtor has a right to require from his creditor a written receipt for any property delivered in performance of his obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
