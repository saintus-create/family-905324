# CIV § 2921

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2921

---

## Text

A mortgage may be created upon property held adversely to the mortgagor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
