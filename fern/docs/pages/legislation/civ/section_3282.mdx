# CIV § 3282

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 1. Damages in General > Article: 1. General Principles

**Citation:** CIV § 3282

---

## Text

Detriment is a loss or harm suffered in person or property.
