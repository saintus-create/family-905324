# CIV § 2324

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2324

---

## Text

An authority to sell and convey real property includes authority to give the usual convenants of warranty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
