# CIV § 1788.307

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.17. Fair Debt Settlement Practices > Chapter: 3. Enforcement of the Fair Debt Settlement Practices Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.307

---

## Text

The provisions of this title are severable. If any provision of this title or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 454, Sec. 1. (AB 1405) Effective January 1, 2022.)
