# CIV § 1804.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 4. Restrictions on Retail Installment Contracts

**Citation:** CIV § 1804.4

---

## Text

Any provision in a contract which is prohibited by this chapter shall be void but shall not otherwise affect the validity of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
