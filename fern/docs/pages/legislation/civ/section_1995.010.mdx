# CIV § 1995.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 6. Assignment and Sublease > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1995.010

---

## Text

This chapter applies to transfer of a tenant’s interest in a lease of real property for other than residential purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 982, Sec. 2.)
