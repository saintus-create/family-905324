# CIV § 2888

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 3. Effect of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2888

---

## Text

Notwithstanding an agreement to the contrary, a lien, or a contract for a lien, transfers no title to the property subject to the lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
