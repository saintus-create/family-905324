# CIV § 1812.646

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.96. CALIFORNIA RENTAL-PURCHASE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.646

---

## Text

Any waiver or modification of the provisions of this title by the consumer or lessor shall be void and unenforceable as contrary to public policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1026, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
