# CIV § 1997.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 7. Use Restrictions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1997.030

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter authorizes a restriction on use that is otherwise prohibited by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 67, Sec. 3.)
