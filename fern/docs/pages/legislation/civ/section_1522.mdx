# CIV § 1522

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 4. Accord and Satisfaction [[1521.] - 1526.]

**Citation:** CIV § 1522

---

## Text

Though the parties to an accord are bound to execute it, yet it does not extinguish the obligation until it is fully executed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
