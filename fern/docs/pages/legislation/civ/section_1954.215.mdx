# CIV § 1954.215

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 2.5. Water Service

**Citation:** CIV § 1954.215

---

## Text

The rights or obligations established under this chapter shall not be waived. Any purported waiver is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. (SB 7) Effective January 1, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2018, pursuant to Sec. 1954.218.)
