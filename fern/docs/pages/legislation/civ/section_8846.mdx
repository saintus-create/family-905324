# CIV § 8846

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 8. Prompt Payment > Article: 3. Stop Work Notice

**Citation:** CIV § 8846

---

## Text

It is against public policy to waive the provisions of this article by contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
