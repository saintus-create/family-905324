# CIV § 3360

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 3360

---

## Text

When a breach of duty has caused no appreciable detriment to the party affected, he may yet recover nominal damages.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
