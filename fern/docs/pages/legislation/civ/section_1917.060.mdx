# CIV § 1917.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 4. Shared Appreciation Loans of E.R.I.S.A. Pension Funds > Article: 6. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.060

---

## Text

The relationship of the borrower and the lender, as to a shared appreciation loan, is that of debtor and creditor and shall not be, or be construed to be, a joint venture, equity venture, partnership, or other relationship.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11. Inoperative January 1, 1990, by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 11.5.)
