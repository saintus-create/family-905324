# CIV § 1798.60

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 11. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.60

---

## Text

An individual’s name and address may not be distributed for commercial purposes, sold, or rented by an agency unless such action is specifically authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
