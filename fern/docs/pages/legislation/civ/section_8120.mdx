# CIV § 8120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Waiver and Release

**Citation:** CIV § 8120

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply to a work of improvement governed by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
