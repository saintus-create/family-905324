# CIV § 3357

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 3357

---

## Text

The damages prescribed by this Chapter are exclusive of exemplary damages and interest, except where those are expressly mentioned.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
