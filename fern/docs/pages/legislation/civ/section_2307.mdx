# CIV § 2307

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2307

---

## Text

An agency may be created, and an authority may be conferred, by a precedent authorization or a subsequent ratification.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
