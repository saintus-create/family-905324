# CIV § 22.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 22.1

---

## Text

The will of the supreme power is expressed:

(a) By the Constitution.

(b) By statutes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
