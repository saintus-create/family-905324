# CIV § 2825

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 6. Exoneration of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2825

---

## Text

A surety is not exonerated by the discharge of his principal by operation of law, without the intervention or omission of the creditor.
