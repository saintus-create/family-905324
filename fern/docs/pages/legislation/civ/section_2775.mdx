# CIV § 2775

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. INDEMNITY

**Citation:** CIV § 2775

---

## Text

An agreement to indemnify against the acts of a certain person, applies not only to his acts and their consequences, but also to those of his agents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
