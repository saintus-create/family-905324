# CIV § 3516

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3516

---

## Text

Acquiescence in error takes away the right of objecting to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
