# CIV § 1657.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. INTERPRETATION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1657.1

---

## Text

Any time specified in a contract of adhesion for the performance of an act required to be performed shall be reasonable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 222, Sec. 1. (SB 762) Effective January 1, 2022.)
