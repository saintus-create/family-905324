# CIV § 7103

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.5. AUTOMATIC CHECKOUT SYSTEM > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 7103

---

## Text

Improper pricing on the shelf or on the item due to unintentional error shall not constitute a violation of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 224, Sec. 2.)
