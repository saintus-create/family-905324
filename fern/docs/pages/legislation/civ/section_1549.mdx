# CIV § 1549

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 1. Definition

**Citation:** CIV § 1549

---

## Text

A contract is an agreement to do or not to do a certain thing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
