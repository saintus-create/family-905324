# CIV § 3104

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. Internet Neutrality

**Citation:** CIV § 3104

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 3268 or any other law, any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy and shall be unenforceable and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 976, Sec. 2. (SB 822) Effective January 1, 2019.)
