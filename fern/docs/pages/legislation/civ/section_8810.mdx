# CIV § 8810

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 8. Prompt Payment > Article: 2. Retention Payment

**Citation:** CIV § 8810

---

## Text

This article governs a retention payment withheld by an owner from a direct contractor or by a direct contractor from a subcontractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
