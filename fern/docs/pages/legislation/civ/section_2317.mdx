# CIV § 2317

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2317

---

## Text

Ostensible authority is such as a principal, intentionally or by want of ordinary care, causes or allows a third person to believe the agent to possess.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
