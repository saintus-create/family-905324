# CIV § 1567

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Consent

**Citation:** CIV § 1567

---

## Text

An apparent consent is not real or free when obtained through:

1. Duress;

2. Menace;

3. Fraud;

4. Undue influence; or,

5. Mistake.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
