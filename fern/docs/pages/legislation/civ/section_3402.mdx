# CIV § 3402

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Specific Relief > Article: 4. Revision of Contracts

**Citation:** CIV § 3402

---

## Text

A contract may be first revised and then specifically enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
