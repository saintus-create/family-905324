# CIV § 1785.28.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6. CONSUMER CREDIT REPORTING AGENCIES ACT > Chapter: 3.6. Escrow Agent Rating Service

**Citation:** CIV § 1785.28.6

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 105, Sec. 1. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.6, commencing with Section 1785.28.)
