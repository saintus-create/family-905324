# CIV § 8160

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Application of Title

**Citation:** CIV § 8160

---

## Text

This title applies to a work of improvement that is not governed by Title 3 (commencing with Section 9000) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
