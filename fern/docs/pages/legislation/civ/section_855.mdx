# CIV § 855

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 3. Environmental Responsibility Acceptance Act

**Citation:** CIV § 855

---

## Text

The notification requirements of Section 851 shall not become effective until 180 days after the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 873, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
