# CIV § 798.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** CIV § 798.4

---

## Text

“Mobilehome park” is an area of land where two or more mobilehome sites are rented, or held out for rent, to accommodate mobilehomes used for human habitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1031.)
