# CIV § 1047

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 2. What May Be Transferred

**Citation:** CIV § 1047

---

## Text

Any person claiming title to real property in the adverse possession of another may transfer it with the same effect as if in actual possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
