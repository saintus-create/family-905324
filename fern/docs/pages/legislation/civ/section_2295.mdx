# CIV § 2295

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 1. Definition of Agency

**Citation:** CIV § 2295

---

## Text

An agent is one who represents another, called the principal, in dealings with third persons. Such representation is called agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
