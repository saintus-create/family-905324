# CIV § 3368

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 1. General Principles

**Citation:** CIV § 3368

---

## Text

Preventive relief is given by prohibiting a party from doing that which ought not to be done.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
