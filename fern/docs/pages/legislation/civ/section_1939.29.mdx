# CIV § 1939.29

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 1.5. Rental Passenger Vehicle Transactions

**Citation:** CIV § 1939.29

---

## Text

A waiver of any of the provisions of this chapter, except for Sections 1939.21, 1939.35, and 1939.37, shall be void and unenforceable as contrary to public policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 183, Sec. 5. (AB 2051) Effective January 1, 2017.)
