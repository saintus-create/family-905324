# CIV § 1926

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 1. Hiring in General

**Citation:** CIV § 1926

---

## Text

The products of a thing hired, during the hiring, belong to the hirer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
