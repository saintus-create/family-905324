# CIV § 1102.16

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.5. Disclosures Upon Transfer of Residential Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1102.16

---

## Text

The disclosure of the existence of any window security bars and any safety release mechanism on those window security bars shall be made pursuant to Section 1102.6 or 1102.6a of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2005.)
