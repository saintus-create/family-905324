# CIV § 703

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 703

---

## Text

No future interest in property is recognized by the law, except such as is defined in this Division of the Code.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
