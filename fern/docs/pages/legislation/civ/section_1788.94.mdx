# CIV § 1788.94

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.7. Educational Debt Collection Practices

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.94

---

## Text

Any waiver of a provision of this title is contrary to public policy and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 853, Sec. 13. (AB 3281) Effective January 1, 2025.)
