# CIV § 800.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 800.6

---

## Text

“Homeowner” means a person who owns or resides in a floating home which is in a floating home marina pursuant to a rental agreement with management.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
