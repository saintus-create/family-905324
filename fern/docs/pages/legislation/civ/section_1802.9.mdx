# CIV § 1802.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 1802.9

---

## Text

“Total sale price” means the total of the cash price of the goods or services, the amounts, if any, included for insurance, official fees, and the finance charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1075, Sec. 3. Operative October 1, 1982, or sooner, by Sec. 25 of Ch. 1075, as amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 129, Sec. 12.)
