# CIV § 3524

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3524

---

## Text

Between those who are equally in the right, or equally in the wrong, the law does not interpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
