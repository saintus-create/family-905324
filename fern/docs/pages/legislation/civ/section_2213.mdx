# CIV § 2213

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 6. Carrier of Passengers Act of 2024 > Article: 1. Title

**Citation:** CIV § 2213

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Carrier of Passengers Act of 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 742, Sec. 1. (AB 2780) Effective January 1, 2025.)
