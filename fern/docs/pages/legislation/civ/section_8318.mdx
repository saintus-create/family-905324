# CIV § 8318

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 3. Design Professionals Lien

**Citation:** CIV § 8318

---

## Text

A design professional may not obtain a lien under this chapter for services provided for a work of improvement relating to a single-family, owner-occupied residence for which the expected construction cost is less than one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
