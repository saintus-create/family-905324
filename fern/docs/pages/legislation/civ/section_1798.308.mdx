# CIV § 1798.308

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.7. Reproductive and Gender-Affirming Health Care Services

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.308

---

## Text

This title shall not be construed to provide jurisdiction over a California resident in an out-of-state forum when the California resident has not availed themselves of that forum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 260, Sec. 6. (SB 345) Effective January 1, 2024.)
