# CIV § 1660

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. INTERPRETATION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1660

---

## Text

A promise, made in the singular number, but executed by several persons, is presumed to be joint and several.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
