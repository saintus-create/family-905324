# CIV § 4202

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 2. Application of Act

**Citation:** CIV § 4202

---

## Text

This part does not apply to a commercial or industrial common interest development, as defined in Section 6531.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 19. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
