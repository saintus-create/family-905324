# CIV § 1748.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.3A. CREDIT CARD DISCLOSURE

**Citation:** CIV § 1748.10

---

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the “Areias Credit Card Full Disclosure Act of 1986.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 159, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2002.)
