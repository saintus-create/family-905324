# CIV § 1812.531

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.91. EMPLOYMENT AGENCY, EMPLOYMENT COUNSELING, AND JOB LISTING SERVICES ACT > Chapter: 7. Nurses’ Registries

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.531

---

## Text

No nurses’ registry shall divide fees with any physician and surgeon, nurse, hospital, patient, or any agent or employee of any of these.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 704, Sec. 2.)
