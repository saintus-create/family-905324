# CIV § 3494

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PUBLIC NUISANCES

**Citation:** CIV § 3494

---

## Text

A public nuisance may be abated by any public body or officer authorized thereto by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
