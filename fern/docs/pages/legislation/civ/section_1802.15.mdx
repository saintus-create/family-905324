# CIV § 1802.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 1802.15

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, association or other group, however organized.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 1995.)
