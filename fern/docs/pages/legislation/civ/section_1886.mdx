# CIV § 1886

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 1. Loan for Use

**Citation:** CIV § 1886

---

## Text

A borrower for use must use great care for the preservation in safety and in good condition of the thing lent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
