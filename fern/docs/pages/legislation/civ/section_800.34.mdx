# CIV § 800.34

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 3. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** CIV § 800.34

---

## Text

The management of a floating home marina shall disclose, in writing, the name and address of the floating home marina owner upon the request of a homeowner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
