# CIV § 1055

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 3. Mode of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1055

---

## Text

A grant duly executed is presumed to have been delivered at its date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
