# CIV § 2079.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 3. Service Without Employment > Article: 2. Duty to Prospective Purchaser of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2079.22

---

## Text

Nothing in this article precludes a seller’s agent from also being a buyer’s agent. If a seller or buyer in a transaction chooses to not be represented by an agent, that does not, of itself, make that agent a dual agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 907, Sec. 45. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
