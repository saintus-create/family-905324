# CIV § 1749.60

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.4B. SUPERMARKET CLUB CARDS

**Citation:** CIV § 1749.60

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the “Supermarket Club Card Disclosure Act of 1999.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 586, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, pursuant to Sec. 2 of Ch. 586.)
