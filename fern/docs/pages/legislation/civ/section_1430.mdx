# CIV § 1430

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Joint or Several Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 1430

---

## Text

An obligation imposed upon several persons, or a right created in favor of several persons, may be:

1. Joint;

2. Several; or,

3. Joint and several.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
