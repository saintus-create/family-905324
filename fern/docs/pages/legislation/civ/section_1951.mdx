# CIV § 1951

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 2. Hiring of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1951

---

## Text

As used in Sections 1951.2 to 1952.6, inclusive:

(a) “Rent” includes charges equivalent to rent.

(b) “Lease” includes a sublease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 89.)
