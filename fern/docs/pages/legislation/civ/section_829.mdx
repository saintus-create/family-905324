# CIV § 829

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 2. Boundaries

**Citation:** CIV § 829

---

## Text

The owner of land in fee has the right to the surface and to everything permanently situated beneath or above it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
