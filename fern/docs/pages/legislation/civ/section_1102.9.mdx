# CIV § 1102.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.5. Disclosures Upon Transfer of Residential Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1102.9

---

## Text

Any disclosure made pursuant to this article may be amended in writing by the seller or his or her agent, but the amendment shall be subject to Section 1102.3 or 1102.3a.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 907, Sec. 16. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
