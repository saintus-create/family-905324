# CIV § 1799.304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.90. Commercial Financing Transactions with Small Businesses

**Citation:** CIV § 1799.304

---

## Text

A waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 881, Sec. 1. (SB 666) Effective January 1, 2024.)
