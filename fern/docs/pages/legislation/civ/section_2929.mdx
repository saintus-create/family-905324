# CIV § 2929

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2929

---

## Text

No person whose interest is subject to the lien of a mortgage may do any act which will substantially impair the mortgagee’s security.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
