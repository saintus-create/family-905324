# CIV § 815.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Conservation Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 815.4

---

## Text

All interests not transferred and conveyed by the instrument creating the easement shall remain in the grantor of the easement, including the right to engage in all uses of the land not affected by the easement nor prohibited by the easement or by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 179.)
