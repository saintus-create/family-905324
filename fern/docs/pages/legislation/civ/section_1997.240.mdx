# CIV § 1997.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 7. Use Restrictions > Article: 2. Use Restrictions

**Citation:** CIV § 1997.240

---

## Text

A restriction on use of leased property by a tenant may provide that a change in use is subject to any express standard or condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 67, Sec. 3.)
