# CIV § 1917.334

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 7. Shared Appreciation Loans for Seniors > Article: 2. Terms and Conditions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.334

---

## Text

Except as provided in this article, the terms and conditions of the shared appreciation loan for seniors shall be subject to all laws applicable to loans in effect on the date the loan is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1701, Sec. 1.)
