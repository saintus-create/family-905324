# CIV § 1622

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. MANNER OF CREATING CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1622

---

## Text

All contracts may be oral, except such as are specially required by statute to be in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
