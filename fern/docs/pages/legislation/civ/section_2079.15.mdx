# CIV § 2079.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 3. Service Without Employment > Article: 2. Duty to Prospective Purchaser of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2079.15

---

## Text

In any circumstance in which the seller or buyer refuses to sign an acknowledgment of receipt pursuant to Section 2079.14, the agent shall set forth, sign, and date a written declaration of the facts of the refusal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 907, Sec. 40. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
