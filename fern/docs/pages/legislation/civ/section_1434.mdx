# CIV § 1434

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 3. Conditional Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 1434

---

## Text

An obligation is conditional, when the rights or duties of any party thereto depend upon the occurrence of an uncertain event.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
