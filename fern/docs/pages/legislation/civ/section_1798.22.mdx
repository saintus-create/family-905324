# CIV § 1798.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 5. Agency Requirements

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.22

---

## Text

Each agency shall designate an agency employee to be responsible for ensuring that the agency complies with all of the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
