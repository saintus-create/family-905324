# CIV § 1818

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 1. Deposit in General > Article: 1. Nature and Creation of Deposit

**Citation:** CIV § 1818

---

## Text

A deposit for exchange is one in which the depositary is only bound to return a thing corresponding in kind to that which is deposited.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
