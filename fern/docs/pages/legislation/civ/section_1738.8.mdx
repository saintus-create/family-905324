# CIV § 1738.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. CONSIGNMENT OF FINE ART > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1738.8

---

## Text

Any provision of a contract or agreement whereby the consignor waives any provision of this title is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 953.)
