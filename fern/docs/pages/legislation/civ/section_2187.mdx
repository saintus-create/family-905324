# CIV § 2187

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 2. Common Carriers of Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2187

---

## Text

A common carrier may demand the fare of passengers, either at starting or at any subsequent time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
