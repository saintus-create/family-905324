# CIV § 2171

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 1. Common Carriers in General [[2168.] - 2178.]

**Citation:** CIV § 2171

---

## Text

A common carrier must always give a preference in time, and may give a preference in price, to the United States and to this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
