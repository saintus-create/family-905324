# CIV § 2315

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2315

---

## Text

An agent has such authority as the principal, actually or ostensibly, confers upon him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
