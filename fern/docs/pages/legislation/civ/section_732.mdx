# CIV § 732

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 3. Rights of Owners

**Citation:** CIV § 732

---

## Text

The owner of a thing owns also all its products and accessions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
