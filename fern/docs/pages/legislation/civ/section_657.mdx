# CIV § 657

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF PROPERTY

**Citation:** CIV § 657

---

## Text

Property is either:

l. Real or immovable; or,

2. Personal or movable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
