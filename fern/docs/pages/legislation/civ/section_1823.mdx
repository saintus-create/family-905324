# CIV § 1823

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 1. Deposit in General > Article: 2. Obligations of the Depositary

**Citation:** CIV § 1823

---

## Text

A depositary is not bound to deliver a thing deposited without demand, even where the deposit is made for a specified time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
