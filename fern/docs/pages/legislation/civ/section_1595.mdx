# CIV § 1595

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. Object of a Contract

**Citation:** CIV § 1595

---

## Text

The object of a contract is the thing which it is agreed, on the part of the party receiving the consideration, to do or not to do.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
