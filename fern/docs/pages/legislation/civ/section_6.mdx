# CIV § 6

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 6

---

## Text

No action or proceeding commenced before this Code takes effect, and no right accrued, is affected by its provisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
