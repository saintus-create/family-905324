# CIV § 883.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. MARKETABLE RECORD TITLE > Chapter: 3. Mineral Rights > Article: 2. Termination of Dormant Mineral Right

**Citation:** CIV § 883.210

---

## Text

The owner of real property subject to a mineral right may bring an action to terminate the mineral right pursuant to this article if the mineral right is dormant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 240, Sec. 2.)
