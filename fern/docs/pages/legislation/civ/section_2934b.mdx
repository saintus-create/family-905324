# CIV § 2934b

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2934b

---

## Text

Sections 15643 and 18102 of the Probate Code apply to trustees under deeds of trust given to secure obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 820, Sec. 12. Operative July 1, 1987, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 820.)
