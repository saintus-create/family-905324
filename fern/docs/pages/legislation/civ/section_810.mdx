# CIV § 810

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Servitudes

**Citation:** CIV § 810

---

## Text

The owner in fee of a servient tenement may maintain an action for the possession of the land, against any one unlawfully possessed thereof, though a servitude exists thereon in favor of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
