# CIV § 8464

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 4. Mechanics Lien > Article: 6. Enforcement of Lien

**Citation:** CIV § 8464

---

## Text

In addition to any other costs allowed by law, the court in an action to enforce a lien shall allow as costs to each claimant whose lien is established the amount paid to verify and record the claim of lien, whether the claimant is a plaintiff or defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
