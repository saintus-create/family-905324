# CIV § 1997.220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 7. Use Restrictions > Article: 2. Use Restrictions

**Citation:** CIV § 1997.220

---

## Text

An ambiguity in a restriction on use of leased property by a tenant shall be construed in favor of unrestricted use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 67, Sec. 3.)
