# CIV § 3522

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3522

---

## Text

One who grants a thing is presumed to grant also whatever is essential to its use.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
