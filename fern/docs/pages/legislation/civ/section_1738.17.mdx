# CIV § 1738.17

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1A. INDEPENDENT WHOLESALE SALES REPRESENTATIVES

**Citation:** CIV § 1738.17

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any person licensed pursuant to Division 9 (commencing with Section 23000) of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 964, Sec. 1.)
