# CIV § 8208

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 2. Preliminary Notice

**Citation:** CIV § 8208

---

## Text

A direct contractor shall make available to any person seeking to give preliminary notice the following information:

(a) The name and address of the owner.

(b) The name and address of the construction lender, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
