# CIV § 3525

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3525

---

## Text

Between rights otherwise equal, the earliest is preferred.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
