# CIV § 8720

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 7. Security for Large Project > Article: 3. Form of Security

**Citation:** CIV § 8720

---

## Text

An owner shall provide security by any of the following means:

(a) A bond that satisfies Section 8722.

(b) An irrevocable letter of credit that satisfies Section 8724.

(c) An escrow account that satisfies Section 8726.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
