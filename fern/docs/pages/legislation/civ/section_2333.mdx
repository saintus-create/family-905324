# CIV § 2333

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 3. Mutual Obligations of Principals and Third Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2333

---

## Text

When an agent exceeds his authority, his principal is bound by his authorized acts so far only as they can be plainly separated from those which are unauthorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
