# CIV § 6800

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 7. Assessments and Assessment Collection > Article: 1. Establishment and Imposition of Assessments

**Citation:** CIV § 6800

---

## Text

The association shall levy regular and special assessments sufficient to perform its obligations under the governing documents and this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
