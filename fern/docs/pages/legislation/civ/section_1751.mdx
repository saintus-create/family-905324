# CIV § 1751

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.5. CONSUMERS LEGAL REMEDIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1751

---

## Text

Any waiver by a consumer of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy and shall be unenforceable and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1550.)
