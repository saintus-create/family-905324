# CIV § 3273.26

**Hierarchy:** Title: 19.1. Mortgage Forbearance Act

**Citation:** CIV § 3273.26

---

## Text

Failure to comply with this title shall not affect the validity of a trustee’s sale or a sale to a bona fide purchaser for value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 128, Sec. 1. (AB 238) Effective September 22, 2025.)
