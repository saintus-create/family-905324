# CIV § 1788.200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.15. Private Student Loan Collections Reform Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.200

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Private Student Loan Collections Reform Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 559, Sec. 1. (AB 424) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative July 1, 2022, pursuant to Section 1788.211.)
