# CIV § 800.303

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 10. Cooperatives and Condominiums

**Citation:** CIV § 800.303

---

## Text

The ownership or management shall not require the removal of a floating home from a cooperative or condominium in the event of its sale to a third party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
