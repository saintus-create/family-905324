# CIV § 9454

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PUBLIC WORK OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 4. Stop Payment Notice > Article: 3. Distribution of Funds Withheld

**Citation:** CIV § 9454

---

## Text

A person that willfully gives a false stop payment notice to the public entity or that willfully includes in the notice work not provided for the public works contract for which the stop payment notice is given forfeits all right to participate in the distribution under Section 9450.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
