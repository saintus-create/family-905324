# CIV § 1812.212

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.7. CONTRACTS FOR SELLER ASSISTED MARKETING PLANS

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.212

---

## Text

No seller shall make or authorize the making of any reference to its compliance with this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 876.)
