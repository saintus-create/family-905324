# CIV § 1789.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6D. ELECTRONIC COMMERCE > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1789.6

---

## Text

Nothing in this title shall be construed to limit the liability of a provider of service to a consumer for errors or omissions arising from the operation of an electronic commercial service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 638, Sec. 1.)
