# CIV § 2838

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 7. Position of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2838

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the recovery of judgment by a creditor against a surety, the latter still occupies the relation of surety.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
