# CIV § 1917.611

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 7. Shared Appreciation Loans for Seniors > Article: 5. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.611

---

## Text

Any waiver of any right of a borrower under the provisions of this chapter shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1701, Sec. 1.)
