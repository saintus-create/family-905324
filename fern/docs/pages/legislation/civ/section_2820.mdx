# CIV § 2820

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 6. Exoneration of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2820

---

## Text

That a promise by a creditor is for any cause void, or voidable by him at his option, shall not prevent it from altering the obligation or suspending or impairing the remedy within the meaning of the last section.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 453.)
