# CIV § 1749.51

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.4A. GIFT CERTIFICATES

**Citation:** CIV § 1749.51

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
