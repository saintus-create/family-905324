# CIV § 1795.93

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.7. CONSUMER WARRANTIES > Chapter: 1.5. Motor Vehicle Warranty Adjustment Programs

**Citation:** CIV § 1795.93

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to exclude, modify, or otherwise limit any other remedy provided by law to a consumer or lessee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 814, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
