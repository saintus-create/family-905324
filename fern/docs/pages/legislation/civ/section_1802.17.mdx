# CIV § 1802.17

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 1802.17

---

## Text

“Billing cycle” means the time interval between regular monthly billing statement dates.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 546.)
