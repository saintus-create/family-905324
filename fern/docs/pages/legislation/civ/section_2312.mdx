# CIV § 2312

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2312

---

## Text

A ratification is not valid unless, at the time of ratifying the act done, the principal has power to confer authority for such an act.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
