# CIV § 687

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 687

---

## Text

Community property is property that is community property under Part 2 (commencing with Section 760) of Division 4 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 163, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 161 of Ch. 163.)
