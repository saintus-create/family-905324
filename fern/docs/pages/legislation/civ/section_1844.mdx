# CIV § 1844

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 2. Deposit for Keeping > Article: 2. Gratuitous Deposit

**Citation:** CIV § 1844

---

## Text

Gratuitous deposit is a deposit for which the depositary receives no consideration beyond the mere possession of the thing deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
