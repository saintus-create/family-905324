# CIV § 887.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. MARKETABLE RECORD TITLE > Chapter: 7. Abandoned Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 887.020

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to an easement that is part of a unified or reciprocal system for the mutual benefit of multiple parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 157, Sec. 2.)
