# CIV § 1797

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.7. CONSUMER WARRANTIES > Chapter: 3. Mobilehome Warranties

**Citation:** CIV § 1797

---

## Text

All new mobilehomes and manufactured homes sold to a buyer shall be covered by the warranty set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 730, Sec. 1.)
