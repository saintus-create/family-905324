# CIV § 831

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 2. Boundaries

**Citation:** CIV § 831

---

## Text

An owner of land bounded by a road or street is presumed to own to the center of the way, but the contrary may be shown.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
