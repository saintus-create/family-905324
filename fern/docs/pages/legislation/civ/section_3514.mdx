# CIV § 3514

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3514

---

## Text

One must so use his own rights as not to infringe upon the rights of another.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
