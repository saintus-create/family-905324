# CIV § 800.74

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 6. Termination of Tenancy

**Citation:** CIV § 800.74

---

## Text

A homeowner shall give written notice to the management of not less than 60 days before vacating his or her tenancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
