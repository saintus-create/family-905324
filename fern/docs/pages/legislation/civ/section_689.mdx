# CIV § 689

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 689

---

## Text

A present interest entitles the owner to the immediate possession of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
