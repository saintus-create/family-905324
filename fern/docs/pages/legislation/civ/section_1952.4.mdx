# CIV § 1952.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 2. Hiring of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1952.4

---

## Text

An agreement for the exploration for or the removal of natural resources is not a lease of real property within the meaning of Sections 1951 to 1952.2, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 89.)
