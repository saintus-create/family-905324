# CIV § 1798.74

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 12. Construction With Other Laws

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.74

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 67110) of Part 40 of the Education Code shall, with regard to student records, prevail over the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
