# CIV § 1812.93

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.5. CONTRACTS FOR HEALTH STUDIO SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.93

---

## Text

Any waiver of the buyer of the provisions of this title shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1675.)
