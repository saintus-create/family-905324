# CIV § 1889

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 1. Loan for Use

**Citation:** CIV § 1889

---

## Text

A borrower for use must repair all deteriorations or injuries to the thing lent, which are occasioned by his negligence, however slight.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
