# CIV § 3509

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3509

---

## Text

The maxims of jurisprudence hereinafter set forth are intended not to qualify any of the foregoing provisions of this code, but to aid in their just application.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 20. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
