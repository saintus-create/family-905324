# CIV § 2085

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. Carriage in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2085

---

## Text

The contract of carriage is a contract for the conveyance of property, persons, or messages, from one place to another.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
