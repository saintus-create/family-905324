# CIV § 2019

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 2. Particular Employments > Article: 2. Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2019

---

## Text

An agent must not exceed the limits of his actual authority, as defined by the Title on Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
