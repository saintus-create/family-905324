# CIV § 2941.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2941.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, if no payoff demand statement is issued pursuant to Section 2943, nothing in Section 2941 shall be construed to prohibit the charging of a reconveyance fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 438, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 7 of Ch. 438.)
