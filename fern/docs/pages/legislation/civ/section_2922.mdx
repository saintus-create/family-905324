# CIV § 2922

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2922

---

## Text

A mortgage can be created, renewed, or extended, only by writing, executed with the formalities required in the case of a grant of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
