# CIV § 2190

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 2. Common Carriers of Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2190

---

## Text

After having ejected a passenger, a carrier has no right to require the payment of any part of his fare.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
