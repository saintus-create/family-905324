# CIV § 3120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15.5. Digital Equity Bill of Rights

**Citation:** CIV § 3120

---

## Text

This title shall be known, and may be cited, as the Digital Equity Bill of Rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 436, Sec. 1. (AB 414) Effective January 1, 2024.)
