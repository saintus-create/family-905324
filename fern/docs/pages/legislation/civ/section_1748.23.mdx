# CIV § 1748.23

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.3B. CHARGE CARD DISCLOSURES

**Citation:** CIV § 1748.23

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
