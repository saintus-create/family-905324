# CIV § 1786.57

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6A. INVESTIGATIVE CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCIES > Article: 4. Remedies

**Citation:** CIV § 1786.57

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
