# CIV § 1812.702

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.97. CONSUMER COLLECTION NOTICE

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.702

---

## Text

Any violation of this act shall be considered a violation of the Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (Title 1.6C (commencing with Section 1788)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 259, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004. Operative July 1, 2004, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 259.)
