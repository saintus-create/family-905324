# CIV § 799.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.22

---

## Text

“Defaulting occupant” means an occupant who fails to pay for his or her occupancy in a park or who fails to comply with reasonable written rules and regulations of the park given to the occupant upon registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
