# CIV § 1679

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES > Chapter: 2. Default on Real Property Purchase Contract

**Citation:** CIV § 1679

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to a provision for liquidated damages to the seller if the buyer fails to complete the purchase of real property. The validity of any other provision for liquidated damages in a contract to purchase and sell real property shall be determined under Section 1671.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 198.)
