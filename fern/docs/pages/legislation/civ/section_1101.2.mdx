# CIV § 1101.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.4. Installation of Water Use Efficiency Improvements

**Citation:** CIV § 1101.2

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 1101.7, this article shall apply to residential and commercial real property built and available for use on or before January 1, 1994.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 587, Sec. 1. (SB 407) Effective January 1, 2010.)
