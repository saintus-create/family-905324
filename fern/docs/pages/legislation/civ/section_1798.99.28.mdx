# CIV § 1798.99.28

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.47. The California Age-Appropriate Design Code Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.99.28

---

## Text

This title shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Age-Appropriate Design Code Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 320, Sec. 2. (AB 2273) Effective January 1, 2023.)
