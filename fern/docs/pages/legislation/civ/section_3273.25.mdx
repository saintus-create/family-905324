# CIV § 3273.25

**Hierarchy:** Title: 19.1. Mortgage Forbearance Act

**Citation:** CIV § 3273.25

---

## Text

During the time of forbearance granted pursuant to this title, a mortgage servicer shall not initiate any judicial or nonjudicial foreclosure process, move for a foreclosure judgment or order of sale, or execute a foreclosure-related eviction or foreclosure sale if the borrower is performing pursuant to the terms of the forbearance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 128, Sec. 1. (AB 238) Effective September 22, 2025.)
