# CIV § 1817

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 1. Deposit in General > Article: 1. Nature and Creation of Deposit

**Citation:** CIV § 1817

---

## Text

A deposit for keeping is one in which the depositary is bound to return the identical thing deposited.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
