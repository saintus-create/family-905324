# CIV § 8004

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 8004

---

## Text

“Claimant” means a person that has a right under this part to record a claim of lien, give a stop payment notice, or assert a claim against a payment bond, or do any combination of the foregoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
