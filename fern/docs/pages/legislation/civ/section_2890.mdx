# CIV § 2890

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 3. Effect of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2890

---

## Text

The creation of a lien does not of itself imply that any person is bound to perform the act for which the lien is a security.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
