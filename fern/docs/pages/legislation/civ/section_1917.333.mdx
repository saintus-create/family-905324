# CIV § 1917.333

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 7. Shared Appreciation Loans for Seniors > Article: 2. Terms and Conditions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.333

---

## Text

(a) The borrower shall have the right to prepay, at any time, in full or in part, the total loan obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1701, Sec. 1.)
