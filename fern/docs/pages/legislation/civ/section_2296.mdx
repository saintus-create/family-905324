# CIV § 2296

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 1. Definition of Agency

**Citation:** CIV § 2296

---

## Text

Any person having capacity to contract may appoint an agent, and any person may be an agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
