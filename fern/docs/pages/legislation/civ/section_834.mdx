# CIV § 834

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 2. Boundaries

**Citation:** CIV § 834

---

## Text

Trees whose trunks stand partly on the land of two or more coterminous owners, belong to them in common.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
