# CIV § 1101.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.4. Installation of Water Use Efficiency Improvements

**Citation:** CIV § 1101.7

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to any of the following:

(a) Registered historical sites.

(b) Real property for which a licensed plumber certifies that, due to the age or configuration of the property or its plumbing, installation of water-conserving plumbing fixtures is not technically feasible.

(c) A building for which water service is permanently disconnected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 587, Sec. 1. (SB 407) Effective January 1, 2010.)
