# CIV § 1633.13

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.5. ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS

**Citation:** CIV § 1633.13

---

## Text

In a proceeding, evidence of a record or signature may not be excluded solely because it is in electronic form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
