# CIV § 86

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2.9. CALIFORNIA FAIR DEALERSHIP LAW > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 86

---

## Text

The prevailing party in any action based on a violation of the provisions of this part shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney’s fees and taxable court costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 914, Sec. 1.)
