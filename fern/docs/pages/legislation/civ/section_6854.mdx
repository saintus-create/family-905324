# CIV § 6854

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 9. Dispute Resolution and Enforcement > Article: 1. Disciplinary Action

**Citation:** CIV § 6854

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 6850 shall be construed to create, expand, or reduce the authority of the board to impose monetary penalties on a member for a violation of the governing documents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
