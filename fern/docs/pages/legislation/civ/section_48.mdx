# CIV § 48

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2. PERSONAL RIGHTS > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 48

---

## Text

In the case provided for in subdivision (c) of Section 47, malice is not inferred from the communication.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2004.)
