# CIV § 761

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. Estates in General

**Citation:** CIV § 761

---

## Text

Estates in real property, in respect to the duration of their enjoyment are either:

l. Estates of inheritance or perpetual estates;

2. Estates for life;

3. Estates for years; or,

4. Estates at will.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
