# CIV § 8058

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 8058

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “day” means a calendar day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
