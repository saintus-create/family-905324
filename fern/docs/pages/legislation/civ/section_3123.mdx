# CIV § 3123

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15.5. Digital Equity Bill of Rights

**Citation:** CIV § 3123

---

## Text

(a) This title does not create a private right of action against the state to enforce this title.

(b) This title does not create an obligation of the state to enforce the principle or policy established by this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 436, Sec. 1. (AB 414) Effective January 1, 2024.)
