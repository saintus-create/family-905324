# CIV § 1812.408

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.9. CREDITOR REMEDIES: DISABILITY INSURANCE

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.408

---

## Text

Any waiver by the debtor of the provisions of this title shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 973, Sec. 1.)
