# CIV § 2881

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 2. Creation of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2881

---

## Text

A lien is created:

1. By contract of the parties; or,

2. By operation of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
