# CIV § 804

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Servitudes

**Citation:** CIV § 804

---

## Text

A servitude can be created only by one who has a vested estate in the servient tenement.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
