# CIV § 5615

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 8. Assessments and Assessment Collection > Article: 1. Establishment and Imposition of Assessments

**Citation:** CIV § 5615

---

## Text

The association shall provide individual notice pursuant to Section 4040 to the members of any increase in the regular or special assessments of the association, not less than 30 nor more than 60 days prior to the increased assessment becoming due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
