# CIV § 2311

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2311

---

## Text

Ratification of part of an indivisible transaction is a ratification of the whole.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
