# CIV § 1044

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 2. What May Be Transferred

**Citation:** CIV § 1044

---

## Text

Property of any kind may be transferred, except as otherwise provided by this Article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
