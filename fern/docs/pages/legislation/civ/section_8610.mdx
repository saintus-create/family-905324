# CIV § 8610

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 6. Payment Bond

**Citation:** CIV § 8610

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 8609, if a payment bond under this title is recorded before completion of a work of improvement, an action to enforce the liability on the bond may not be commenced later than six months after completion of the work of improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
