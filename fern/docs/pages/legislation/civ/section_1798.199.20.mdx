# CIV § 1798.199.20

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.199.20

---

## Text

Members of the agency board, including the chairperson, shall serve at the pleasure of their appointing authority but shall serve for no longer than eight consecutive years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 2020, by initiative Proposition 24, Sec. 24.3. Effective December 16, 2020. Operative December 16, 2020, pursuant to Sec. 31 of Proposition 24.)
