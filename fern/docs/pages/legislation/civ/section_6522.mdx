# CIV § 6522

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 6522

---

## Text

If a provision of this act requires that an action be approved by a majority of all members, the action shall be approved or ratified by an affirmative vote of a majority of the votes entitled to be cast.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
