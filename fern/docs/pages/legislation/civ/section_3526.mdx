# CIV § 3526

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3526

---

## Text

No person is responsible for that which no person can control.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 8. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
