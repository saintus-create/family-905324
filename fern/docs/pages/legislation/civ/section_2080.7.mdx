# CIV § 2080.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 4. Lost and Unclaimed Property > Article: 1. Lost Money and Goods

**Citation:** CIV § 2080.7

---

## Text

The provisions of this article have no application to things which have been intentionally abandoned by their owner.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1512.)
