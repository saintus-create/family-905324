# CIV § 816

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Conservation Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 816

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be liberally construed in order to effectuate the policy and purpose of Section 815.
