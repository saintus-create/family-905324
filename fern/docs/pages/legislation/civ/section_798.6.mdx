# CIV § 798.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** CIV § 798.6

---

## Text

“Park” is a manufactured housing community as defined in Section 18210.7 of the Health and Safety Code, or a mobilehome park.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 596, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
