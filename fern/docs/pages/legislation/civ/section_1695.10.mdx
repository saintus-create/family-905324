# CIV § 1695.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. EXTINCTION OF CONTRACTS > Chapter: 2.5. Home Equity Sales Contracts

**Citation:** CIV § 1695.10

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this chapter shall be void and unenforceable as contrary to the public policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1029.)
