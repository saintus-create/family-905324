# CIV § 2877

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 1. Definition of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2877

---

## Text

Contracts of mortgage, pledge, bottomry, or respondentia are subject to all of the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 13. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
