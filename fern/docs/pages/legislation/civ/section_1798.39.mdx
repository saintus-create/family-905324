# CIV § 1798.39

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 8. Access to Records and Administrative Remedies

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.39

---

## Text

Sections 1798.35, 1798.36, and 1798.37 shall not apply to any record evidencing property rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
