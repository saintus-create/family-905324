# CIV § 818

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 1. Incidents of Ownership

**Citation:** CIV § 818

---

## Text

The owner of a life estate may use the land in the same manner as the owner of a fee simple, except that he must do no act to the injury of the inheritance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
