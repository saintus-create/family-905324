# CIV § 43.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2. PERSONAL RIGHTS > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 43.56

---

## Text

No cause of action arises against a foster parent for alienation of affection of a foster child.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 43.55 (as amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 195) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 5.)
