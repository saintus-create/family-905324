# CIV § 1067

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 4. Interpretation of Grants

**Citation:** CIV § 1067

---

## Text

A clear and distinct limitation in a grant is not controlled by other words less clear and distinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
