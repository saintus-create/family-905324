# CIV § 1812.647

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.96. CALIFORNIA RENTAL-PURCHASE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.647

---

## Text

Any person who willfully violates any provision of this title is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1026, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
