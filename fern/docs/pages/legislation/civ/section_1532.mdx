# CIV § 1532

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 5. Novation [1530. - [1533.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1532

---

## Text

Novation is made by contract, and is subject to all the rules concerning contracts in general.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
