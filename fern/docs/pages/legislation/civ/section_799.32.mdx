# CIV § 799.32

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.32

---

## Text

“Tenant” means the owner or operator of a recreational vehicle who has occupied a lot in a park for more than 30 consecutive days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
