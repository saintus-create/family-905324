# CIV § 799.30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.30

---

## Text

“Recreational vehicle park” or “park” has the same meaning as defined in Section 18862.39 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 530, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
