# CIV § 808

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Servitudes

**Citation:** CIV § 808

---

## Text

The owner of a future estate in a dominant tenement may use easements attached thereto for the purpose of viewing waste, demanding rent, or removing an obstruction to the enjoyment of such easements, although such tenement is occupied by a tenant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
