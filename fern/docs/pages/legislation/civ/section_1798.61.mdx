# CIV § 1798.61

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 11. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.61

---

## Text

(a) Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit the release of only names and addresses of persons possessing licenses to engage in professional occupations.

(b) Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit the release of only names and addresses of persons applying for licenses to engage in professional occupations for the sole purpose of providing those persons with informational materials relating to available professional educational materials or courses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 962, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
