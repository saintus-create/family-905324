# CIV § 3045.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 4. Hospital Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 3045.2

---

## Text

The lien shall apply whether the damages are recovered, or are to be recovered, by judgment, settlement, or compromise.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2080.)
