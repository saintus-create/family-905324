# CIV § 1000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. MODES IN WHICH PROPERTY MAY BE ACQUIRED

**Citation:** CIV § 1000

---

## Text

Property is acquired by:

1. Occupancy;

2. Accession;

3. Transfer;

4. Will; or,

5. Succession.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
