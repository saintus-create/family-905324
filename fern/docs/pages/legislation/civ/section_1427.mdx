# CIV § 1427

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. DEFINITION OF OBLIGATIONS [1427. - [1428.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1427

---

## Text

An obligation is a legal duty, by which a person is bound to do or not to do a certain thing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
