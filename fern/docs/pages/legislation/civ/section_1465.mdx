# CIV § 1465

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. TRANSFER OF OBLIGATIONS

**Citation:** CIV § 1465

---

## Text

A covenant running with the land binds those only who acquire the whole estate of the covenantor in some part of the property.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
