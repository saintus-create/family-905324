# CIV § 2882

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 2. Creation of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2882

---

## Text

No lien arises by mere operation of law until the time at which the act to be secured thereby ought to be performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
