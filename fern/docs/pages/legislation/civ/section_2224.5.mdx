# CIV § 2224.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. INVOLUNTARY TRUSTS

**Citation:** CIV § 2224.5

---

## Text

An action brought by the Attorney General pursuant to Section 2223 or 2224 may be brought at any time within 10 years after the cause of action accrued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 299, Sec. 2. (AB 556) Effective January 1, 2016.)
