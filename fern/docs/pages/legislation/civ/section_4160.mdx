# CIV § 4160

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 4160

---

## Text

“Member” means an owner of a separate interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
