# CIV § 8210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 2. Preliminary Notice

**Citation:** CIV § 8210

---

## Text

If one or more construction loans are obtained after commencement of a work of improvement, the owner shall give notice of the name and address of the construction lender or lenders to each person that has given the owner preliminary notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
