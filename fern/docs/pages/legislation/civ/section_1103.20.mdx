# CIV § 1103.20

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.8. Buyer’s Choice Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1103.20

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Buyer’s Choice Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 264, Sec. 1. (AB 957) Effective October 11, 2009.)
