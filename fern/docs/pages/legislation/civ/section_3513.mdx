# CIV § 3513

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3513

---

## Text

Any one may waive the advantage of a law intended solely for their benefit. But a law established for a public reason cannot be contravened by a private agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 2. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
