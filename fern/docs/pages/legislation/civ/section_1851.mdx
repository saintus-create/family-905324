# CIV § 1851

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 2. Deposit for Keeping > Article: 3. Storage

**Citation:** CIV § 1851

---

## Text

A deposit not gratuitous is called storage. The depositary in such case is called a depositary for hire.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
