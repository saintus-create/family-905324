# CIV § 1812.108

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.108

---

## Text

All contracts for discount buying services, which may be in effect between a seller or related sellers and a buyer, the terms of which overlap for any period of time, shall be considered as one contract for the purpose of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
