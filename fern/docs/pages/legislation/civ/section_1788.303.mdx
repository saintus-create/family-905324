# CIV § 1788.303

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.17. Fair Debt Settlement Practices > Chapter: 2. Application of the Fair Debt Settlement Practices Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.303

---

## Text

This title applies to persons providing payment processing services, debt settlement services, and persons purporting to engage in debt settlement services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 454, Sec. 1. (AB 1405) Effective January 1, 2022.)
