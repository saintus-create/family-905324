# CIV § 800.26

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 2. Rental Agreement

**Citation:** CIV § 800.26

---

## Text

On or before March 12, 1991, the management shall notify all floating homeowners, in writing, that a copy of the Floating Home Residency Law is available to them, upon request, from the management.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
