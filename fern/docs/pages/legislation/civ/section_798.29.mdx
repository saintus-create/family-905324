# CIV § 798.29

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 3. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** CIV § 798.29

---

## Text

The management shall post a Mobilehome Assistance Center sign provided by the Department of Housing and Community Development, as required by Section 18253.5 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 957, Sec. 1. (SB 1078) Effective January 1, 2019.)
