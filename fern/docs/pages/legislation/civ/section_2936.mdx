# CIV § 2936

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2936

---

## Text

The assignment of a debt secured by mortgage carries with it the security.
