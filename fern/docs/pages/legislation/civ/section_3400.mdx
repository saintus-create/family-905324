# CIV § 3400

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Specific Relief > Article: 4. Revision of Contracts

**Citation:** CIV § 3400

---

## Text

For the purpose of revising a contract, it must be presumed that all the parties thereto intended to make an equitable and conscientious agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
