# CIV § 1458

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. TRANSFER OF OBLIGATIONS

**Citation:** CIV § 1458

---

## Text

A right arising out of an obligation is the property of the person to whom it is due, and may be transferred as such.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
