# CIV § 2096

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 2. Carriage of Persons > Article: 1. Gratuitous Carriage of Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2096

---

## Text

A carrier of persons without reward must use ordinary care and diligence for their safe carriage.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
