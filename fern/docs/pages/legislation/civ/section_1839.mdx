# CIV § 1839

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 2. Deposit for Keeping > Article: 1. General Provisions [1833. - [1840.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1839

---

## Text

So far as any service is rendered by a depositary, or required from him, his duties and liabilities are prescribed by the Title on Employment and Service.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
