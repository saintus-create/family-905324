# CIV § 2772

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. INDEMNITY

**Citation:** CIV § 2772

---

## Text

Indemnity is a contract by which one engages to save another from a legal consequence of the conduct of one of the parties, or of some other person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
