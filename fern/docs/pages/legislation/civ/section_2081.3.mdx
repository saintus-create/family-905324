# CIV § 2081.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 4. Lost and Unclaimed Property > Article: 2. Unclaimed Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2081.3

---

## Text

If the owner or his agent fails to demand the surplus within 60 days after the sale, it shall be paid into the county treasury, subject to the order of the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 656.)
