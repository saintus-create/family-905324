# CIV § 1997.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 7. Use Restrictions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1997.050

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 1997.270, this chapter applies to a lease executed before, on, or after January 1, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 67, Sec. 3.)
