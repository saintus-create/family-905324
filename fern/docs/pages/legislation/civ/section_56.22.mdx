# CIV § 56.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2.6. CONFIDENTIALITY OF MEDICAL INFORMATION > Title: OF. THE ACT > Chapter: 3. Use and Disclosure of Medical Information by Employers

**Citation:** CIV § 56.22

---

## Text

Upon demand by the patient or the person who signed an authorization, an employer possessing the authorization shall furnish a true copy thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 782, Sec. 2.)
