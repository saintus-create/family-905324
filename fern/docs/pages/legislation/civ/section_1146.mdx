# CIV § 1146

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 3. Transfer of Personal Property > Article: 3. Gifts

**Citation:** CIV § 1146

---

## Text

A gift is a transfer of personal property, made voluntarily, and without consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
