# CIV § 2325

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2325

---

## Text

A general agent to sell, who is intrusted by the principal with the possession of the thing sold, has authority to receive the price.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
