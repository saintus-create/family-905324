# CIV § 1565

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Consent

**Citation:** CIV § 1565

---

## Text

The consent of the parties to a contract must be:

1. Free;

2. Mutual; and,

3. Communicated by each to the other.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
