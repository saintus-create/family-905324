# CIV § 1045

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 2. What May Be Transferred

**Citation:** CIV § 1045

---

## Text

A mere possibility, not coupled with an interest, cannot be transferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
