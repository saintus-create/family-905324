# CIV § 1169

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 4. Recording Transfers > Article: 2. Mode of Recording

**Citation:** CIV § 1169

---

## Text

Instruments entitled to be recorded must be recorded by the County Recorder of the county in which the real property affected thereby is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
