# CIV § 1086

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 6. Agency Listings for the Transfer of Certain Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1086

---

## Text

(a) For purposes of this article, the definitions contained in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 10000) of Part 1 of Division 4 of the Business and Professions Code apply.

(b) An “agent” is one authorized by law to act in that capacity for that type of property and is licensed as a real estate broker under Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 10130) of Part 1 of Division 4 of the Business and Professions Code, or is a licensee, as defined in Section 18006 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 907, Sec. 1. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
