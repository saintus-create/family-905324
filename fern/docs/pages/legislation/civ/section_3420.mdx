# CIV § 3420

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 3. Preventive Relief

**Citation:** CIV § 3420

---

## Text

Preventive relief is granted by injunction, provisional or final.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
