# CIV § 2086

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. Carriage in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2086

---

## Text

Carriage is either:

1. Inland; or,

2. Marine.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
