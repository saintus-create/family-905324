# CIV § 2337

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 3. Mutual Obligations of Principals and Third Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2337

---

## Text

An instrument within the scope of his authority by which an agent intends to bind his principal, does bind him if such intent is plainly inferable from the instrument itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
