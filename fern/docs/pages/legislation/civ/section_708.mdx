# CIV § 708

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 2. Conditions of Ownership

**Citation:** CIV § 708

---

## Text

Conditions are precedent or subsequent. The former fix the beginning, the latter the ending, of the right.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
