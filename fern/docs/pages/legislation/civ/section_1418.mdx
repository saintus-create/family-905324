# CIV § 1418

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. WATER RIGHTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1418

---

## Text

By a compliance with the above rules the claimant’s right to the use of the water relates back to the time the notice was posted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
