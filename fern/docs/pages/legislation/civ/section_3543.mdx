# CIV § 3543

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3543

---

## Text

Where one of two innocent persons must suffer by the act of a third, the person, by whose negligence it happened, must be the sufferer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 10. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
