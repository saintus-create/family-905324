# CIV § 2947

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 2. Mortgage of Real Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2947

---

## Text

Any interest in real property which is capable of being transferred may be mortgaged.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
