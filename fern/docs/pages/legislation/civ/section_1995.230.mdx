# CIV § 1995.230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 6. Assignment and Sublease > Article: 2. Restrictions on Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1995.230

---

## Text

A restriction on transfer of a tenant’s interest in a lease may absolutely prohibit transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 982, Sec. 2.)
