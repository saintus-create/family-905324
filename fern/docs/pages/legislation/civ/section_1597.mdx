# CIV § 1597

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. Object of a Contract

**Citation:** CIV § 1597

---

## Text

Everything is deemed possible except that which is impossible in the nature of things.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
