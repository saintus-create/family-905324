# CIV § 901

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. REQUIREMENTS FOR ACTIONS FOR CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS > Chapter: 3. Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 901

---

## Text

A builder may, but is not required to, offer greater protection or protection for longer time periods in its express contract with the homeowner than that set forth in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 896). A builder may not limit the application of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 896) or lower its protection through the express contract with the homeowner. This type of express contract constitutes an “enhanced protection agreement.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 722, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
