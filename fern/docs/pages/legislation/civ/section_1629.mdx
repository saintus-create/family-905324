# CIV § 1629

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. MANNER OF CREATING CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1629

---

## Text

All distinctions between sealed and unsealed instruments are abolished.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
