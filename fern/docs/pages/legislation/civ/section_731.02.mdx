# CIV § 731.02

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2.6. Legal Estates Principal and Income Law

**Citation:** CIV § 731.02

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply to all transactions by which a principal was established without the interposition of a trust on or after September 13, 1941, or is hereafter so established. Transactions by which a principal is held in trust are governed by Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 16300) of Part 4 of Division 9 of the Probate Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 820, Sec. 3. Operative July 1, 1987, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 820.)
