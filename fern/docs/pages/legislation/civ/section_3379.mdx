# CIV § 3379

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Specific Relief > Article: 2. Possession of Personal Property [3379. - [3380.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 3379

---

## Text

A person entitled to the immediate possession of specific personal property may recover the same in the manner provided by the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
