# CIV § 2983.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2b. Automobile Sales Finance Act

**Citation:** CIV § 2983.6

---

## Text

Any person who shall willfully violate any provision of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1338.)
