# CIV § 5865

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 10. Dispute Resolution and Enforcement > Article: 1. Discipline and Cost Reimbursement

**Citation:** CIV § 5865

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 5850 or 5855 shall be construed to create, expand, or reduce the authority of the board to impose monetary penalties on a member for a violation of the governing documents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
