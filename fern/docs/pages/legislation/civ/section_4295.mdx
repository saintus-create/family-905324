# CIV § 4295

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 3. Governing Documents > Article: 4. Condominium Plan

**Citation:** CIV § 4295

---

## Text

A condominium plan may be amended or revoked by a recorded instrument that is acknowledged and signed by all the persons who, at the time of amendment or revocation, are persons whose signatures are required under Section 4290.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
