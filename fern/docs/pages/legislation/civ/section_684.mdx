# CIV § 684

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 684

---

## Text

A partnership interest is one owned by several persons, in partnership, for partnership purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
