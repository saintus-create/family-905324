# CIV § 1810.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 10. Retail Installment Accounts

**Citation:** CIV § 1810.8

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 1806.1 and 1806.4 shall be applicable to retail installment accounts.
