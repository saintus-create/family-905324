# CIV § 800.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 800.2

---

## Text

“Management” means the owner of a floating home marina or an agent or representative authorized to act on his or her behalf in connection with matters relating to a tenancy in the floating home marina.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
