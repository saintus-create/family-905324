# CIV § 9000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PUBLIC WORK OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Application of Title

**Citation:** CIV § 9000

---

## Text

This title applies to a work of improvement contracted for by a public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
