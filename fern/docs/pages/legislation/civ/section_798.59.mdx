# CIV § 798.59

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 6. Termination of Tenancy

**Citation:** CIV § 798.59

---

## Text

A homeowner shall give written notice to the management of not less than 60 days before vacating his or her tenancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1397, Sec. 27.)
