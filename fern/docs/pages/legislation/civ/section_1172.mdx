# CIV § 1172

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 4. Recording Transfers > Article: 2. Mode of Recording

**Citation:** CIV § 1172

---

## Text

The duties of county recorders, in respect to recording instruments, are prescribed by the Government Code.
