# CIV § 2962

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 3. Disclosures on Purchase Money Liens on Residential Property

**Citation:** CIV § 2962

---

## Text

Any disclosure made pursuant to this article may be amended in writing by the person making the disclosure, provided that any amendment shall be subject to the provisions of Section 2959.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 968, Sec. 1. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 968.)
