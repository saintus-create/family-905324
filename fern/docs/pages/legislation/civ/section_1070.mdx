# CIV § 1070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 4. Interpretation of Grants

**Citation:** CIV § 1070

---

## Text

If several parts of a grant are absolutely irreconcilable, the former part prevails.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
