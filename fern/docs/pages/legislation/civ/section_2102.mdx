# CIV § 2102

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 2. Carriage of Persons > Article: 2. Carriage for Reward

**Citation:** CIV § 2102

---

## Text

A carrier of persons for reward must not overcrowd or overload his vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
