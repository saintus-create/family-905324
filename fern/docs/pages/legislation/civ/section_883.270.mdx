# CIV § 883.270

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. MARKETABLE RECORD TITLE > Chapter: 3. Mineral Rights > Article: 2. Termination of Dormant Mineral Right

**Citation:** CIV § 883.270

---

## Text

Subject to Section 880.370 (grace period for recording notice), this article applies to all mineral rights, whether executed or recorded before, on, or after January 1, 1985.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 240, Sec. 2.)
