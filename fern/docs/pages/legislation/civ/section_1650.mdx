# CIV § 1650

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. INTERPRETATION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1650

---

## Text

Particular clauses of a contract are subordinate to its general intent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
