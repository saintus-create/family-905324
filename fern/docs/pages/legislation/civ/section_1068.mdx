# CIV § 1068

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 4. Interpretation of Grants

**Citation:** CIV § 1068

---

## Text

If the operative words of a grant are doubtful, recourse may be had to its recitals to assist the construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
