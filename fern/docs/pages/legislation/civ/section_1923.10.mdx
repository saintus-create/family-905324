# CIV § 1923.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 8. Reverse Mortgages

**Citation:** CIV § 1923.10

---

## Text

This chapter shall only apply to those reverse mortgage loans executed on or after January 1, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 797, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
