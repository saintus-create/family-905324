# CIV § 6750

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 6. Association Governance > Article: 1. Association Existence and Powers

**Citation:** CIV § 6750

---

## Text

A common interest development shall be managed by an association that may be incorporated or unincorporated. The association may be referred to as an owners’ association or a community association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
