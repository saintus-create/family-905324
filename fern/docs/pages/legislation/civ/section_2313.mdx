# CIV § 2313

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2313

---

## Text

No unauthorized act can be made valid, retroactively, to the prejudice of third persons, without their consent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
