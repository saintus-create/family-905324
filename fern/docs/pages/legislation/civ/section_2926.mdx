# CIV § 2926

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2926

---

## Text

A mortgage is a lien upon everything that would pass by a grant of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
