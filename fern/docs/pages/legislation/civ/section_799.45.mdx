# CIV § 799.45

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.45

---

## Text

The management may offer a rental agreement to an occupant of the park who intends to remain in the park for a period in excess of 30 consecutive days.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
