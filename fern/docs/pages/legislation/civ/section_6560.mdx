# CIV § 6560

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 6560

---

## Text

“Person” means a natural person, corporation, government or governmental subdivision or agency, business trust, estate, trust, partnership, limited liability company, association, or other entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 21. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
