# CIV § 1798.99.88

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.48. Data Broker Registration

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.99.88

---

## Text

Nothing in this title shall be construed to supersede or interfere with the operation of the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (Title 1.81.5 (commencing with Section 1798.100)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 753, Sec. 2. (AB 1202) Effective January 1, 2020.)
