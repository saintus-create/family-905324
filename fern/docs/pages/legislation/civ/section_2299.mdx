# CIV § 2299

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 1. Definition of Agency

**Citation:** CIV § 2299

---

## Text

An agency is actual when the agent is really employed by the principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
