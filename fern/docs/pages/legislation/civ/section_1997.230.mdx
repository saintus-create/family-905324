# CIV § 1997.230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 7. Use Restrictions > Article: 2. Use Restrictions

**Citation:** CIV § 1997.230

---

## Text

A restriction on use of leased property by a tenant may absolutely prohibit a change in use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 67, Sec. 3.)
