# CIV § 9504

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PUBLIC WORK OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 4. Stop Payment Notice > Article: 4. Enforcement of Payment of Claim Stated in Stop Payment Notice

**Citation:** CIV § 9504

---

## Text

Within five days after commencement of an action to enforce payment of the claim stated in a stop payment notice, the claimant shall give notice of commencement of the action to the public entity in the same manner that a stop payment notice is given.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
