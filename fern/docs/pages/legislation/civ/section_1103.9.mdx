# CIV § 1103.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.7. Disclosure of Natural and Environmental Hazards, Right-to-Farm, and Other Disclosures Upon Transfer of Residential Property

**Citation:** CIV § 1103.9

---

## Text

Any disclosure made pursuant to this article may be amended in writing by the seller or the seller’s agent, but the amendment shall be subject to Section 1103.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 907, Sec. 27. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
