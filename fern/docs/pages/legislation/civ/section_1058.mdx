# CIV § 1058

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 3. Mode of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1058

---

## Text

Redelivering a grant of real property to the grantor, or canceling it, does not operate to retransfer the title.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
