# CIV § 1072

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 4. Interpretation of Grants

**Citation:** CIV § 1072

---

## Text

Words of inheritance or succession are not requisite to transfer a fee in real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
