# CIV § 3546

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3546

---

## Text

Things happen according to the ordinary course of nature and the ordinary habits of life.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
