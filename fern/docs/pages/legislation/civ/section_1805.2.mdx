# CIV § 1805.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 5. Finance Charge Limitation

**Citation:** CIV § 1805.2

---

## Text

Contracts may be payable in successive monthly, semimonthly or weekly installments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
