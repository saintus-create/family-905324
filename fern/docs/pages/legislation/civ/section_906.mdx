# CIV § 906

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. REQUIREMENTS FOR ACTIONS FOR CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS > Chapter: 3. Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 906

---

## Text

A builder’s election to use an enhanced protection agreement addresses only the issues set forth in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 896) and does not constitute an election to use or not use the provisions of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 910). The decision to use or not use Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 910) is governed by the provisions of that chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 722, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
