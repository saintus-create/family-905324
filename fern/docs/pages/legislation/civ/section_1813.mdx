# CIV § 1813

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. DEPOSIT > Chapter: 1. Deposit in General > Article: 1. Nature and Creation of Deposit

**Citation:** CIV § 1813

---

## Text

A deposit may be voluntary or involuntary; and for safe keeping or for exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
