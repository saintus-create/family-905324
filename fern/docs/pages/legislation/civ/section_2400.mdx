# CIV § 2400

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Particular Agencies > Article: 3. Powers of Attorney Under Probate Code

**Citation:** CIV § 2400

---

## Text

Powers of attorney are governed by the Power of Attorney Law (Division 4.5 (commencing with Section 4000) of the Probate Code) to the extent provided in that law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
