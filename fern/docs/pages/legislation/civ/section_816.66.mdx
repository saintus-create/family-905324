# CIV § 816.66

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4.5. Greenway Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 816.66

---

## Text

A greenway easement granted pursuant to this chapter constitutes an enforceable restriction, for purposes of Section 402.1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 639, Sec. 3. (AB 1251) Effective January 1, 2016.)
