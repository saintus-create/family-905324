# CIV § 1798.180

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.180

---

## Text

Preemption

This title is a matter of statewide concern and supersedes and preempts all rules, regulations, codes, ordinances, and other laws adopted by a city, county, city and county, municipality, or local agency regarding the collection and sale of consumers’ personal information by a business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended November 3, 2020, by initiative Proposition 24, Sec. 20. Effective December 16, 2020. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 31 of Proposition 24.)
