# CIV § 1607

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 5. Consideration

**Citation:** CIV § 1607

---

## Text

The consideration of a contract must be lawful within the meaning of Section 1667.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
