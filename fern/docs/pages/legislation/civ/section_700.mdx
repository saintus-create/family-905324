# CIV § 700

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 700

---

## Text

A mere possibility, such as the expectancy of an heir apparent, is not to be deemed an interest of any kind.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
