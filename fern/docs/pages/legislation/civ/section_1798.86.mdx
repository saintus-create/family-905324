# CIV § 1798.86

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.1. CONFIDENTIALITY OF SOCIAL SECURITY NUMBERS

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.86

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 815, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2003.)
