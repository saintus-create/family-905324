# CIV § 2082

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 4. Lost and Unclaimed Property > Article: 3. Fees of Officers

**Citation:** CIV § 2082

---

## Text

The fees of officers under this chapter are the same allowed by law for similar services, and shall be paid by the taker up or finder and recovered from the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 656.)
