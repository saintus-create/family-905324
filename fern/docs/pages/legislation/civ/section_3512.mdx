# CIV § 3512

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3512

---

## Text

One shall not change their purpose to the injury of another.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 1. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
