# CIV § 1812.109

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.109

---

## Text

No contract for discount buying services shall require payments or financing by the buyer over a period in excess of two years from the date the contract was entered into.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
