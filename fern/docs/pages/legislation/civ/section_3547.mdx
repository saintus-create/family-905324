# CIV § 3547

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3547

---

## Text

A thing continues to exist as long as is usual with things of that nature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
