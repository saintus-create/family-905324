# CIV § 83

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2.9. CALIFORNIA FAIR DEALERSHIP LAW > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 83

---

## Text

On or after January 1, 1981, no grantor, directly or indirectly, shall refuse to grant a dealership to any person because of any characteristic listed or defined in subdivision (b) or (e) of Section 51.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 568, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2008.)
