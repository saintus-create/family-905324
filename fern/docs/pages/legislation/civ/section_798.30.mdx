# CIV § 798.30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 3.5. Fees and Charges

**Citation:** CIV § 798.30

---

## Text

The management shall give a homeowner written notice of any increase in his or her rent at least 90 days before the date of the increase.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 448, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
