# CIV § 3361

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 3361

---

## Text

Estimations, measures, or calculations of past, present, or future damages for lost earnings or impaired earning capacity resulting from personal injury or wrongful death shall not be reduced based on race, ethnicity, or gender.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 136, Sec. 2. (SB 41) Effective January 1, 2020.)
