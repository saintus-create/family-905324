# CIV § 1798.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 5. Agency Requirements

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.15

---

## Text

Each agency shall collect personal information to the greatest extent practicable directly from the individual who is the subject of the information rather than from another source.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 595, Sec. 6.)
