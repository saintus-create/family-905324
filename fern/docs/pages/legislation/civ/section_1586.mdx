# CIV § 1586

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Consent

**Citation:** CIV § 1586

---

## Text

A proposal may be revoked at any time before its acceptance is communicated to the proposer, but not afterwards.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
