# CIV § 8714

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 7. Security for Large Project > Article: 2. Security Requirement

**Citation:** CIV § 8714

---

## Text

It is against public policy to waive the provisions of this chapter by contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
