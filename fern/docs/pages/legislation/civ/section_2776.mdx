# CIV § 2776

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. INDEMNITY

**Citation:** CIV § 2776

---

## Text

An agreement to indemnify several persons applies to each, unless a contrary intention appears.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
