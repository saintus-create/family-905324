# CIV § 8062

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 8062

---

## Text

No act of an owner in good faith and in compliance with a provision of this part shall be construed to prevent a direct contractor’s performance of the contract, or exonerate a surety on a performance or payment bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
