# CIV § 2020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. SERVICE > Chapter: 2. Particular Employments > Article: 2. Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2020

---

## Text

An agent must use ordinary diligence to keep his principal informed of his acts in the course of the agency.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
