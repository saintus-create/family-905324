# CIV § 3490

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PUBLIC NUISANCES

**Citation:** CIV § 3490

---

## Text

No lapse of time can legalize a public nuisance, amounting to an actual obstruction of public right.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
