# CIV § 3273.16

**Hierarchy:** Title: 19. COVID-19 Small Landlord and Homeowner Relief Act > Chapter: 2. Mortgages

**Citation:** CIV § 3273.16

---

## Text

Any waiver by a borrower of the provisions of this article is contrary to public policy and shall be void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 37, Sec. 13. (AB 3088) Effective August 31, 2020.)
