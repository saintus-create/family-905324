# CIV § 1785.20.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6. CONSUMER CREDIT REPORTING AGENCIES ACT > Chapter: 3. Requirements on Users of Consumer Credit Reports

**Citation:** CIV § 1785.20.6

---

## Text

A person who uses a consumer credit report in connection with a credit transaction shall not use a medical debt listed on the report as a negative factor when making a credit decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 520, Sec. 3. (SB 1061) Effective January 1, 2025.)
