# CIV § 938

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. REQUIREMENTS FOR ACTIONS FOR CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS > Chapter: 4. Prelitigation Procedure

**Citation:** CIV § 938

---

## Text

This title applies only to new residential units where the purchase agreement with the buyer was signed by the seller on or after January 1, 2003.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 762, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2004.)
