# CIV § 3301

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 1. Damages for Breach of Contract [[3300.] - 3322.]

**Citation:** CIV § 3301

---

## Text

No damages can be recovered for a breach of contract which are not clearly ascertainable in both their nature and origin.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
