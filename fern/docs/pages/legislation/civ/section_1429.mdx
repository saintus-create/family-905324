# CIV § 1429

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Rules of Interpretation

**Citation:** CIV § 1429

---

## Text

The rules which govern the interpretation of contracts are prescribed by Part II of this Division. Other obligations are interpreted by the same rules by which statutes of a similar nature are interpreted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
