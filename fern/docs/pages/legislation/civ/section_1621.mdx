# CIV § 1621

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. MANNER OF CREATING CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1621

---

## Text

An implied contract is one, the existence and terms of which are manifested by conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
