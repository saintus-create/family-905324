# CIV § 1588

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Consent

**Citation:** CIV § 1588

---

## Text

A contract which is voidable solely for want of due consent, may be ratified by a subsequent consent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
