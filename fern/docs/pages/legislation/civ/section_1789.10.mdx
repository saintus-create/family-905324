# CIV § 1789.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6E. CREDIT SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1789.10

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the “Credit Services Act of 1984.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1177, Sec. 1.)
