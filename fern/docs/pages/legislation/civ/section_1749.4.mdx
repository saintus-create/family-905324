# CIV § 1749.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.4. LAYAWAY PRACTICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1749.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this title shall be construed to limit or reduce any legal obligations imposed under Title 2 (commencing with Section 1801) of Part 4 of Division 3 or under any other applicable law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 825.)
