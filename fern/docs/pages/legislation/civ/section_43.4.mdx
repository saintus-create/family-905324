# CIV § 43.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2. PERSONAL RIGHTS > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 43.4

---

## Text

A fraudulent promise to marry or to cohabit after marriage does not give rise to a cause of action for damages.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 381.)
