# CIV § 2191

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 2. Common Carriers of Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2191

---

## Text

A common carrier has a lien upon the luggage of a passenger for the payment of such fare as he is entitled to from him. This lien is regulated by the Title on Liens.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
