# CIV § 1954.16

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 2.5. Transitional Housing Participant Misconduct > Article: 2. Temporary Restraining Order and Injunction

**Citation:** CIV § 1954.16

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall promulgate forms and related instructions to implement the procedures required by this chapter. The petition and response forms shall be simple and concise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 714, Sec. 5. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2017.)
