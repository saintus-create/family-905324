# CIV § 1812.126

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.126

---

## Text

The prohibitions of this title are not exclusive and do not relieve the parties or the contracts subject thereto from compliance with any other applicable provision of law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
