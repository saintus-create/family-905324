# CIV § 1798.199.25

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.199.25

---

## Text

For each day on which they engage in official duties, members of the agency board shall be compensated at the rate of one hundred dollars ($100), adjusted pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 1798.199.95, and shall be reimbursed for expenses incurred in performance of their official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 121, Sec. 10. (AB 3286) Effective January 1, 2025.)
