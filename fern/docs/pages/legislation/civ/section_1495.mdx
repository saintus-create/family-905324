# CIV § 1495

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1495

---

## Text

An offer of performance is of no effect if the person making it is not able and willing to perform according to the offer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
