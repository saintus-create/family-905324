# CIV § 1812.620

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.96. CALIFORNIA RENTAL-PURCHASE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.620

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Karnette Rental-Purchase Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1026, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
