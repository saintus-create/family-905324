# CIV § 8530

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 5. Stop Payment Notice > Article: 3. Stop Payment Notice to Construction Lender

**Citation:** CIV § 8530

---

## Text

A person that has a lien right under Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 8400) may give a construction lender a stop payment notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
