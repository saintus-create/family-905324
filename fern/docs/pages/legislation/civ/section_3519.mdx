# CIV § 3519

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3519

---

## Text

A person who can and does not forbid that which is done on their behalf, is deemed to have bidden it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 6. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
