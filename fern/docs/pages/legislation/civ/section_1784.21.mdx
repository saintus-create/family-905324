# CIV § 1784.21

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.5B. California Combating Auto Retail Scams (CARS) Act > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1784.21

---

## Text

Any waiver by a consumer of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy and is unenforceable and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 354, Sec. 1. (SB 766) Effective January 1, 2026. Operative October 1, 2026, pursuant to Section 1784.28.)
