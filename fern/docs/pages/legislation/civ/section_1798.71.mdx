# CIV § 1798.71

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 12. Construction With Other Laws

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.71

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be deemed to abridge or limit the rights of litigants, including parties to administrative proceedings, under the laws, or case law, of discovery of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
