# CIV § 1556

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties

**Citation:** CIV § 1556

---

## Text

All persons are capable of contracting, except minors, persons of unsound mind, and persons deprived of civil rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
