# CIV § 682

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 682

---

## Text

The ownership of property by several persons is either:

(a) Of joint interest.

(b) Of partnership interests.

(c) Of interests in common.

(d) Of community interest of spouses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 7. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
