# CIV § 1105

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 2. Effect of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1105

---

## Text

A fee simple title is presumed to be intended to pass by a grant of real property, unless it appears from the grant that a lesser estate was intended.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
