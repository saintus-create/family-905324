# CIV § 4900

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 6. Association Governance > Article: 2. Board Meeting

**Citation:** CIV § 4900

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Common Interest Development Open Meeting Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
