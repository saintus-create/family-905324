# CIV § 1798.199

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.81.5. California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.199

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 1798.198, Section 1798.180 shall be operative on the effective date of the act adding this section.

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 735, Sec. 17. (SB 1121) Effective September 23, 2018. Operative September 23, 2018.)
