# CIV § 1799.200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.86. CONSUMER CONTRACT AWARENESS ACT OF 1990

**Citation:** CIV § 1799.200

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Consumer Contract Awareness Act of 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1079, Sec. 1. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1079.)
