# CIV § 1647

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. INTERPRETATION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1647

---

## Text

A contract may be explained by reference to the circumstances under which it was made, and the matter to which it relates.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
