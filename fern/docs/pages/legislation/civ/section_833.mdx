# CIV § 833

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 2. Boundaries

**Citation:** CIV § 833

---

## Text

Trees whose trunks stand wholly upon the land of one owner belong exclusively to him, although their roots grow into the land of another.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
