# CIV § 1217

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 4. Recording Transfers > Article: 4. Effect of Recording, or the Want Thereof

**Citation:** CIV § 1217

---

## Text

An unrecorded instrument is valid as between the parties thereto and those who have notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
