# CIV § 1788.306

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.17. Fair Debt Settlement Practices > Chapter: 3. Enforcement of the Fair Debt Settlement Practices Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.306

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 454, Sec. 1. (AB 1405) Effective January 1, 2022.)
