# CIV § 3081

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 7. Stoppage in Transit

**Citation:** CIV § 3081

---

## Text

Any corporation engaged in the business of renting to the public safe deposit boxes may dispose of the unclaimed contents of the safe deposit boxes in the manner set forth in Sections 1660 to 1679, inclusive, of the Financial Code.
