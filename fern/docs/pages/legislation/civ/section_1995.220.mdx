# CIV § 1995.220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 6. Assignment and Sublease > Article: 2. Restrictions on Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1995.220

---

## Text

An ambiguity in a restriction on transfer of a tenant’s interest in a lease shall be construed in favor of transferability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 982, Sec. 2.)
