# CIV § 1802.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 1802.4

---

## Text

“Retail buyer” or “buyer” means a person who buys goods or obtains services from a retail seller in a retail installment sale and not principally for the purpose of resale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
