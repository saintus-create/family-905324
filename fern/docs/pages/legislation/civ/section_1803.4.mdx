# CIV § 1803.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 3. Provisions of Retail Installment Contracts

**Citation:** CIV § 1803.4

---

## Text

The seller shall not obtain the signature of the buyer to a contract when it contains blank spaces to be filled in after it has been signed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
