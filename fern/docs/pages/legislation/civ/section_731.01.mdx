# CIV § 731.01

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2.6. Legal Estates Principal and Income Law

**Citation:** CIV § 731.01

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall affect the provisions of the Personal Income Tax Law and the Bank and Corporation Tax Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 193.)
