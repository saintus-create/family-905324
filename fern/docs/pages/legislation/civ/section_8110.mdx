# CIV § 8110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Notice

**Citation:** CIV § 8110

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by this part, notice by mail under this part shall be given by registered or certified mail, express mail, or overnight delivery by an express service carrier.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
