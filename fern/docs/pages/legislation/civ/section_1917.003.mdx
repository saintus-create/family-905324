# CIV § 1917.003

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 3.5. Shared Appreciation Loans

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.003

---

## Text

The lien or liens of a deed or deeds of trust securing a shared appreciation loan shall include and secure the principal amount of the shared appreciation loan, and all interest, whether accrued or to be accrued, including all amounts of contingent deferred interest.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1416, Sec. 5.)
