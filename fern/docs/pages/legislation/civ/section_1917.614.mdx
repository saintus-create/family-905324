# CIV § 1917.614

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 7. Shared Appreciation Loans for Seniors > Article: 5. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.614

---

## Text

The lien of a deed of trust securing a shared appreciation loan for seniors shall include and secure the total loan obligation of the shared appreciation loan for seniors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1701, Sec. 1.)
