# CIV § 3414

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Specific Relief > Article: 6. Cancellation of Instruments

**Citation:** CIV § 3414

---

## Text

Where an instrument is evidence of different rights or obligations, it may be canceled in part, and allowed to stand for the residue.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
