# CIV § 800.305

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 10. Cooperatives and Condominiums

**Citation:** CIV § 800.305

---

## Text

No agreement shall contain any provision by which the purchaser waives his or her rights under this article. Any waiver thereof shall be deemed contrary to public policy and void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
