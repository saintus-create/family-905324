# CIV § 1493

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1493

---

## Text

An offer of performance must be made in good faith, and in such manner as is most likely, under the circumstances, to benefit the creditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
