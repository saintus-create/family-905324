# CIV § 799.26

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.26

---

## Text

“Management” means the owner of a recreational vehicle park or an agent or representative authorized to act on his or her behalf in connection with matters relating to the park.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
