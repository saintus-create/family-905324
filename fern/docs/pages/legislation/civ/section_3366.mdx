# CIV § 3366

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. SPECIFIC AND PREVENTIVE RELIEF > Chapter: 1. General Principles

**Citation:** CIV § 3366

---

## Text

Specific or preventive relief may be given as provided by the laws of this state.
