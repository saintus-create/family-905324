# CIV § 1812.127

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.127

---

## Text

Any waiver by the buyer of the provisions of this title shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
