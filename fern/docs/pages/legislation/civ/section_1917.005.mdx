# CIV § 1917.005

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 3.5. Shared Appreciation Loans

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.005

---

## Text

Lenders shall be exempt from the usury provisions of Article XV of the California Constitution with respect to shared appreciation loan transactions. This section is declaratory of existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 652, Sec. 1.)
