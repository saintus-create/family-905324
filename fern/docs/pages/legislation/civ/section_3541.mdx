# CIV § 3541

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3541

---

## Text

An interpretation which gives effect is preferred to one which makes void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
