# CIV § 3340

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 2. Damages for Wrongs

**Citation:** CIV § 3340

---

## Text

For wrongful injuries to animals being subjects of property, committed willfully or by gross negligence, in disregard of humanity, exemplary damages may be given.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
