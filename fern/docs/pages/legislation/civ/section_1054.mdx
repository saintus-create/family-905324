# CIV § 1054

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 3. Mode of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1054

---

## Text

A grant takes effect, so as to vest the interest intended to be transferred, only upon its delivery by the grantor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
