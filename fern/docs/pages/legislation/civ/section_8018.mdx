# CIV § 8018

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 8018

---

## Text

“Direct contractor” means a contractor that has a direct contractual relationship with an owner. A reference in another statute to a “prime contractor” in connection with the provisions in this part means a “direct contractor.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
