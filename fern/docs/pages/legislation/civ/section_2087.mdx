# CIV § 2087

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. Carriage in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2087

---

## Text

Carriers upon the ocean and upon arms of the sea are marine carriers. All others are inland carriers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
