# CIV § 2821

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 6. Exoneration of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2821

---

## Text

The rescission of an agreement altering the original obligation of a debtor, or impairing the remedy of a creditor, does not restore the liability of a surety who has been exonerated by such agreement.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 453.)
