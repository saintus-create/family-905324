# CIV § 18

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 18

---

## Text

Notice is:

1. Actual—which consists in express information of a fact; or,

2. Constructive—which is imputed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
