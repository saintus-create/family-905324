# CIV § 8851

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 8. Prompt Payment > Article: 4. Claims and Disputes

**Citation:** CIV § 8851

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 583, Sec. 2. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4, commencing with Section 8850.)
