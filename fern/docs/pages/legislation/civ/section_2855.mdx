# CIV § 2855

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 7. Position of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2855

---

## Text

An arbitration award rendered against a principal alone shall not be, be deemed to be, or be utilized as, an award against his surety.

The intent of this legislation is to apply existing law to arbitration awards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 346.)
