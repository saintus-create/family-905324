# CIV § 8312

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 3. Design Professionals Lien

**Citation:** CIV § 8312

---

## Text

A design professional shall record a claim of lien under this chapter no later than 90 days after the design professional knows or has reason to know that the work of improvement will not be commenced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
