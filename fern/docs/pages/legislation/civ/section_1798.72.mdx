# CIV § 1798.72

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 12. Construction With Other Laws

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.72

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the disclosure of any record containing personal information, other than to the subject of such records, in violation of any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
