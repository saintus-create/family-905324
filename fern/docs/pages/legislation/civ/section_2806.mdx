# CIV § 2806

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 4. Liability of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2806

---

## Text

A suretyship obligation is to be deemed unconditional unless its terms import some condition precedent to the liability of the surety.
