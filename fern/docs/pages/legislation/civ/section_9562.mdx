# CIV § 9562

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PUBLIC WORK OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 5. Payment Bond

**Citation:** CIV § 9562

---

## Text

Notice to the principal and surety under Section 9560 shall comply with the requirements of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 8100) of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
