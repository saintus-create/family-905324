# CIV § 3439.13

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. VOID AND VOIDABLE TRANSFERS AND UNDERTAKINGS > Chapter: 1. Uniform Voidable Transactions Act

**Citation:** CIV § 3439.13

---

## Text

This chapter shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to the subject of this chapter among states enacting it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3439.11 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 44, Sec. 14. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2016.)
