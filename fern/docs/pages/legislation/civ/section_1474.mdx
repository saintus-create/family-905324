# CIV § 1474

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 1. Performance [1473. - [1479.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 1474

---

## Text

Performance of an obligation, by one of several persons who are jointly liable under it, extinguishes the liability of all.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
