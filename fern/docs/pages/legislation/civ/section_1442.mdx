# CIV § 1442

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 3. Conditional Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 1442

---

## Text

A condition involving a forfeiture must be strictly interpreted against the party for whose benefit it is created.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
