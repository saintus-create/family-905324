# CIV § 2182

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 2. Common Carriers of Persons

**Citation:** CIV § 2182

---

## Text

The liability of a carrier for luggage received by him with a passenger is the same as that of a common carrier of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
