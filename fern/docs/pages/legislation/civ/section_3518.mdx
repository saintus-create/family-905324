# CIV § 3518

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3518

---

## Text

A person who has fraudulently dispossessed themselves of a thing may be treated as if they still had possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 5. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
