# CIV § 1494

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1494

---

## Text

An offer of performance must be free from any conditions which the creditor is not bound, on his part, to perform.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
