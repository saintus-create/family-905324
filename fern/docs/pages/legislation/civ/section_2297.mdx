# CIV § 2297

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 1. Definition of Agency

**Citation:** CIV § 2297

---

## Text

An agent for a particular act or transaction is called a special agent. All others are general agents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
