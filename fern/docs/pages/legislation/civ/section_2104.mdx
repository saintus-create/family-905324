# CIV § 2104

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 2. Carriage of Persons > Article: 2. Carriage for Reward

**Citation:** CIV § 2104

---

## Text

A carrier of persons for reward must travel at a reasonable rate of speed, and without any unreasonable delay, or deviation from his proper route.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
