# CIV § 1066

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 4. Interpretation of Grants

**Citation:** CIV § 1066

---

## Text

Grants are to be interpreted in like manner with contracts in general, except so far as is otherwise provided in this Article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
