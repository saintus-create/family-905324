# CIV § 1939.17

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 1.5. Rental Passenger Vehicle Transactions

**Citation:** CIV § 1939.17

---

## Text

A customer facility charge or alternative customer facility charge may be collected by a rental company pursuant to Section 50474.3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 183, Sec. 5. (AB 2051) Effective January 1, 2017.)
