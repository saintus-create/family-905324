# CIV § 1798.63

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 11. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.63

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be liberally construed so as to protect the rights of privacy arising under this chapter or under the Federal or State Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
