# CIV § 1148

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 3. Transfer of Personal Property > Article: 3. Gifts

**Citation:** CIV § 1148

---

## Text

A gift, other than a gift in view of impending death, cannot be revoked by the giver.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 2.)
