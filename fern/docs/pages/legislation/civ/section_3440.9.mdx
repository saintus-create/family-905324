# CIV § 3440.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. VOID AND VOIDABLE TRANSFERS AND UNDERTAKINGS > Chapter: 2. Conveyance of Personal Property Without Delivery

**Citation:** CIV § 3440.9

---

## Text

Subdivision (2) of Section 2402 and subdivision (a) of Section 10308 of the Commercial Code are not restricted by the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 668, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
