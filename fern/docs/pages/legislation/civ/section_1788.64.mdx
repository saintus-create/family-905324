# CIV § 1788.64

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.5. Fair Debt Buying Practices

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.64

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this title is contrary to public policy, and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 64, Sec. 2. (SB 233) Effective January 1, 2014.)
