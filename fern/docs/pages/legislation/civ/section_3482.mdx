# CIV § 3482

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. GENERAL PRINCIPLES

**Citation:** CIV § 3482

---

## Text

Nothing which is done or maintained under the express authority of a statute can be deemed a nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
