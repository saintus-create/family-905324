# CIV § 44

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 2. PERSONAL RIGHTS > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 44

---

## Text

Defamation is effected by either of the following:

(a) Libel.

(b) Slander.
