# CIV § 1487

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1487

---

## Text

An offer of performance must be made by the debtor, or by some person on his behalf and with his assent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
