# CIV § 923

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. REQUIREMENTS FOR ACTIONS FOR CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS > Chapter: 4. Prelitigation Procedure

**Citation:** CIV § 923

---

## Text

The builder shall provide the homeowner or his or her legal representative, upon request, with copies of all correspondence, photographs, and other materials pertaining or relating in any manner to the repairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 722, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
