# CIV § 1812.61

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.4. CONTRACTS FOR DANCE STUDIO LESSONS AND OTHER SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.61

---

## Text

Any waiver of the buyer of the provisions of this title shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1571.)
