# CIV § 2823

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. SURETYSHIP > Article: 6. Exoneration of Sureties

**Citation:** CIV § 2823

---

## Text

Mere delay on the part of a creditor to proceed against the principal, or to enforce any other remedy, does not exonerate a surety.
