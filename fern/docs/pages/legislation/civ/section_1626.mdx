# CIV § 1626

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. MANNER OF CREATING CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1626

---

## Text

A contract in writing takes effect upon its delivery to the party in whose favor it is made, or to his agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
