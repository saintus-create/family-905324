# CIV § 798.11

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** CIV § 798.11

---

## Text

“Resident” is a homeowner or other person who lawfully occupies a mobilehome.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1397, Sec. 3.)
