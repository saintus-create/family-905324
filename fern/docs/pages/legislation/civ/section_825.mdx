# CIV § 825

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF OWNERS > Chapter: 1. Rights of Owners > Article: 1. Incidents of Ownership

**Citation:** CIV § 825

---

## Text

Rent dependent on the life of a person may be recovered after as well as before his death.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
