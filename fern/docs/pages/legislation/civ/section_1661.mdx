# CIV § 1661

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. INTERPRETATION OF CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1661

---

## Text

An executed contract is one, the object of which is fully performed. All others are executory.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
