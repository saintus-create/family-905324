# CIV § 1812.110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.110

---

## Text

Every contract for discount buying services shall provide that the buying services shall become available to the buyer within seven days from the date the contract was entered into.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
