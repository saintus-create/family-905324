# CIV § 38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PERSONS > Part: 1. PERSONS WITH UNSOUND MIND > Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** CIV § 38

---

## Text

A person entirely without understanding has no power to make a contract of any kind, but the person is liable for the reasonable value of things furnished to the person necessary for the support of the person or the person’s family.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 161 of Ch. 163.)
