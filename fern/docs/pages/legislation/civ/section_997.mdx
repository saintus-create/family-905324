# CIV § 997

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTICULAR KINDS OF PERSONAL PROPERTY [[953.] - 998.] > Chapter: 4. Other Kinds of Personal Property

**Citation:** CIV § 997

---

## Text

In this state, for any purpose, porcelain painting and stained glass artistry shall be considered a fine art and not a craft.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1609, Sec. 3.)
