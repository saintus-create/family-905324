# CIV § 800.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 800.8

---

## Text

“Resident” means a homeowner or other person who lawfully occupies a floating home.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
