# CIV § 1917.168

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 5. Shared Appreciation Loans > Article: 6. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1917.168

---

## Text

The qualification requirements of Sections 25110, 25120, and 25130 of the Corporations Code do not apply to a shared appreciation loan to the extent the exemption afforded by subdivision (p) of Section 25100 of that code is applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 12. Inoperative January 1, 1987, by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 12.5.)
