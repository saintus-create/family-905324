# CIV § 688

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 688

---

## Text

In respect to the time of enjoyment, an interest in property is either:

1. Present or future; and,

2. Perpetual or limited.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
