# CIV § 681

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 681

---

## Text

The ownership of property by a single person is designated as a sole or several ownership.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
