# CIV § 3059

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 6. Other Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 3059

---

## Text

The liens of mechanics, for materials and services upon real property, are regulated by Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 8400) of Title 2 of Part 6 of Division 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 13. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697.)
