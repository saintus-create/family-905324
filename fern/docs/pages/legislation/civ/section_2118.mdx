# CIV § 2118

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 3. Carriage of Property > Article: 2. Obligations of the Carrier [2114. - [2121.]]

**Citation:** CIV § 2118

---

## Text

Subject to Section 7303 of the Uniform Commercial Code, a carrier of property must deliver it to the consignee, at the place to which it is addressed, in the manner usual at that place.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 819.)
