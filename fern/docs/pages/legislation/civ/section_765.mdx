# CIV § 765

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. Estates in General

**Citation:** CIV § 765

---

## Text

Estates of inheritance and for life are called estates of freehold; estates for years are chattels real; and estates at will are chattel interests, but are not subject to enforcement of a money judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
