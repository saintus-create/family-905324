# CIV § 1928

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 1. Hiring in General

**Citation:** CIV § 1928

---

## Text

The hirer of a thing must use ordinary care for its preservation in safety and in good condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
