# CIV § 1101.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 2. Transfer of Real Property > Article: 1.4. Installation of Water Use Efficiency Improvements

**Citation:** CIV § 1101.9

---

## Text

Any city, county, or city and county that has adopted an ordinance requiring retrofit of noncompliant plumbing fixtures prior to July 1, 2009, shall be exempt from the requirements of this article so long as the ordinance remains in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 587, Sec. 1. (SB 407) Effective January 1, 2010.)
