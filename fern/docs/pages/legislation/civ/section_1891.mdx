# CIV § 1891

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. LOAN > Chapter: 1. Loan for Use

**Citation:** CIV § 1891

---

## Text

The borrower of a thing for use must not part with it to a third person, without the consent of the lender.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
