# CIV § 1812.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 12.2. Penalties

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.6

---

## Text

Any person who shall willfully violate any provision of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
