# CIV § 815.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Conservation Easements

**Citation:** CIV § 815.5

---

## Text

Instruments creating, assigning, or otherwise transferring conservation easements shall be recorded in the office of the county recorder of the county where the land is situated, in whole or in part, and such instruments shall be subject in all respects to the recording laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 179.)
