# CIV § 1496

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 2. Offer of Performance

**Citation:** CIV § 1496

---

## Text

The thing to be delivered, if any, need not in any case be actually produced, upon an offer of performance, unless the offer is accepted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
