# CIV § 1812.114

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CONTRACTS FOR DISCOUNT BUYING SERVICES

**Citation:** CIV § 1812.114

---

## Text

No right of action or defense arising out of a contract for discount buying services which the buyer has against the discount buying organization shall be cut off by assignment or transfer of the contract to any third party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 237.)
