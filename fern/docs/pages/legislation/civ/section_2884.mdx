# CIV § 2884

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 2. Creation of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2884

---

## Text

A lien may be created by contract, to take immediate effect, as security for the performance of obligations not then in existence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
