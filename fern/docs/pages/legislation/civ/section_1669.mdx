# CIV § 1669

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. UNLAWFUL CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1669

---

## Text

Every contract in restraint of the marriage of any person, other than a minor, is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 198.)
