# CIV § 1785.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6. CONSUMER CREDIT REPORTING AGENCIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1785.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this title shall apply to any person licensed pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 7500) of Division 3 of the Business and Professions Code, or to any employee of such person, unless such person is employed directly by a consumer credit reporting agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1271.)
