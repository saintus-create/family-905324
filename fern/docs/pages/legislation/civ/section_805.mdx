# CIV § 805

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Servitudes

**Citation:** CIV § 805

---

## Text

A servitude thereon cannot be held by the owner of the servient tenement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
