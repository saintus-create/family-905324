# CIV § 799.41

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.6. Recreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 799.41

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall apply to a mobilehome as defined in Section 18008 of the Health and Safety Code or to a manufactured home as defined in Section 18007 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
