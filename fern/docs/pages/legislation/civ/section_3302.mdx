# CIV § 3302

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361.] > Article: 1. Damages for Breach of Contract [[3300.] - 3322.]

**Citation:** CIV § 3302

---

## Text

The detriment caused by the breach of an obligation to pay money only, is deemed to be the amount due by the terms of the obligation, with interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
