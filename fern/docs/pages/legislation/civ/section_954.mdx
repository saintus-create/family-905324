# CIV § 954

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTICULAR KINDS OF PERSONAL PROPERTY [[953.] - 998.] > Chapter: 1. Things in Action [[953.] - 955.1.] > Article: 1. Transfer of Things in Action [[953.] - 955.1.]

**Citation:** CIV § 954

---

## Text

A thing in action, arising out of the violation of a right of property, or out of an obligation, may be transferred by the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 79, as amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 46.)
