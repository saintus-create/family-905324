# CIV § 1633.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.5. ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS

**Citation:** CIV § 1633.4

---

## Text

This title applies to any electronic record or electronic signature created, generated, sent, communicated, received, or stored on or after January 1, 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
