# CIV § 3290

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF > Chapter: 1. Damages in General > Article: 2. Interest as Damages

**Citation:** CIV § 3290

---

## Text

Accepting payment of the whole principal, as such, waives all claim to interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
