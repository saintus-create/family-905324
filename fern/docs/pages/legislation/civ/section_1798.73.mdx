# CIV § 1798.73

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 12. Construction With Other Laws

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.73

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to deny or limit any right of privacy arising under Section 1 of Article I of the California Constitution.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 709.)
