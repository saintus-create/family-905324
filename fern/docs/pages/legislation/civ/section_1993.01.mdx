# CIV § 1993.01

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. HIRING > Chapter: 5.5. Disposition of Property Remaining on Premises at Termination of Commercial Tenancy

**Citation:** CIV § 1993.01

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 1980.5, the requirements of Sections 1982, 1987, and 1990 shall apply to property that is subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 161, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
