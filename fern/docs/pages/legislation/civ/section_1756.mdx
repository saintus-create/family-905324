# CIV § 1756

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.5. CONSUMERS LEGAL REMEDIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 1756

---

## Text

The substantive and procedural provisions of this title shall only apply to actions filed on or after January 1, 1971.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1550.)
