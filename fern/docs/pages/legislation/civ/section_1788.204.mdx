# CIV § 1788.204

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.6C.15. Private Student Loan Collections Reform Act

**Citation:** CIV § 1788.204

---

## Text

A private education lender or private education loan collector shall not bring suit or initiate an arbitration or other legal proceeding to collect a private education loan if the applicable statute of limitations for the private education lender’s or private education loan collector’s claim has expired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 559, Sec. 1. (AB 424) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative July 1, 2022, pursuant to Section 1788.211.)
