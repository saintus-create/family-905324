# CIV § 2942

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2942

---

## Text

Contracts of bottomry or respondentia, although in the nature of mortgages, are not affected by any of the provisions of this Chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
