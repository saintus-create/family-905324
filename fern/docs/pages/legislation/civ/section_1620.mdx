# CIV § 1620

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. MANNER OF CREATING CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1620

---

## Text

An express contract is one, the terms of which are stated in words.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
