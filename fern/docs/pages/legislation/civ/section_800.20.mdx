# CIV § 800.20

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 2. Rental Agreement

**Citation:** CIV § 800.20

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided, the management shall make available to floating homeowners, upon request, copies of all notices required by this article and Article 3 (commencing with Section 800.30).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
