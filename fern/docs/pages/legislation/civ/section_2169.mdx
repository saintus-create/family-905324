# CIV § 2169

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. CARRIAGE > Chapter: 5. Common Carriers [[2168.] - 2212.] > Article: 1. Common Carriers in General [[2168.] - 2178.]

**Citation:** CIV § 2169

---

## Text

A common carrier must, if able to do so, accept and carry whatever is offered to him, at a reasonable time and place, of a kind that he undertakes or is accustomed to carry.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
