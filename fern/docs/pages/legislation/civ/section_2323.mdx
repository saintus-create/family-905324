# CIV § 2323

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2323

---

## Text

An authority to sell personal property includes authority to warrant the title of the principal, and the quality and quantity of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
