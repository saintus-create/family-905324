# CIV § 800.73

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 6. Termination of Tenancy

**Citation:** CIV § 800.73

---

## Text

No tenancy shall be terminated for the purpose of making a homeowner’s berth available for a person who purchases a floating home from the owner of the floating home marina or his or her agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
