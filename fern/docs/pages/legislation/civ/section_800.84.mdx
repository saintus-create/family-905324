# CIV § 800.84

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 7. Transfer of a Floating Home

**Citation:** CIV § 800.84

---

## Text

The management shall not require the removal of a floating home from the floating home marina in the event of its sale to a third party during the term of the homeowner’s rental agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
