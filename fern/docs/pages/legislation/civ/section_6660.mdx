# CIV § 6660

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5.3. Commercial and Industrial Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 4. Ownership and Transfer of Interests > Article: 2. Restrictions on Transfers

**Citation:** CIV § 6660

---

## Text

If the association is served with a claim of lien pursuant to Part 6 (commencing with Section 8000) for a work of improvement on a common area, the association shall, within 60 days of service, give individual notice to the members, pursuant to Section 6514.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 44, Sec. 4. (AB 534) Effective January 1, 2018.)
