# CIV § 697

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 697

---

## Text

A future interest is not void merely because of the improbability of the contingency on which it is limited to take effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
