# CIV § 1688

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. EXTINCTION OF CONTRACTS > Chapter: 2. Rescission

**Citation:** CIV § 1688

---

## Text

A contract is extinguished by its rescission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
