# CIV § 1682

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. EXTINCTION OF CONTRACTS > Chapter: 1. Contracts, How Extinguished

**Citation:** CIV § 1682

---

## Text

A contract may be extinguished in like manner with any other obligation, and also in the manner prescribed by this Title.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
