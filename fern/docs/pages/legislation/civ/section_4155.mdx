# CIV § 4155

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 4155

---

## Text

“Item of business” means any action within the authority of the board, except those actions that the board has validly delegated to any other person or persons, managing agent, officer of the association, or committee of the board comprising less than a quorum of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
