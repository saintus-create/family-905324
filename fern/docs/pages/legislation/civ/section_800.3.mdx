# CIV § 800.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 800.3

---

## Text

“Floating home” has the same meaning as defined in subdivision (d) of Section 18075.55 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
