# CIV § 798.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** CIV § 798.9

---

## Text

“Homeowner” is a person who has a tenancy in a mobilehome park under a rental agreement.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1397, Sec. 2.)
