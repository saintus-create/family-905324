# CIV § 1039

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. TRANSFER > Chapter: 1. Transfers in General > Article: 1. Definition of Transfer

**Citation:** CIV § 1039

---

## Text

Transfer is an act of the parties, or of the law, by which the title to property is conveyed from one living person to another.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
