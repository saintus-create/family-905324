# CIV § 4065

**Hierarchy:** Part: 5. Common Interest Developments > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CIV § 4065

---

## Text

If a provision of this act requires that an action be approved by a majority of all members, the action shall be approved or ratified by an affirmative vote of a majority of the votes entitled to be cast.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 180, Sec. 2. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 180.)
