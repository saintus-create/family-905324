# CIV § 1670.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. UNLAWFUL CONTRACTS

**Citation:** CIV § 1670.7

---

## Text

Any provision of a contract that purports to allow a deduction from a person’s wages for the cost of emigrating and transporting that person to the United States is void as against public policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 35, Sec. 2. (SB 174) Effective January 1, 2010.)
