# CIV § 690

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OWNERSHIP > Chapter: 2. Modifications of Ownership > Article: 1. Interests in Property

**Citation:** CIV § 690

---

## Text

A future interest entitles the owner to the possession of the property only at a future period.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
