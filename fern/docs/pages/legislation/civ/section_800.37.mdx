# CIV § 800.37

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 3. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** CIV § 800.37

---

## Text

A floating home which is owned by a floating home marina shall be deemed abandoned according to the procedures and requirements of Section 1951.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 942, Sec. 4.)
