# CIV § 2304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Agency in General > Article: 2. Authority of Agents

**Citation:** CIV § 2304

---

## Text

An agent may be authorized to do any acts which his principal might do, except those to which the latter is bound to give his personal attention.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
