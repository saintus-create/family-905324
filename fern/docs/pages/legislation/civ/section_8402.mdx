# CIV § 8402

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 4. Mechanics Lien > Article: 1. Who is Entitled to Lien

**Citation:** CIV § 8402

---

## Text

A person that provides work authorized for a site improvement has a lien right under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
