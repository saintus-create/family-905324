# CIV § 1802.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. CREDIT SALES > Chapter: 1. Retail Installment Sales > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CIV § 1802.3

---

## Text

“Retail seller” or “seller” means a person engaged in the business of selling goods or furnishing services to retail buyers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 201.)
