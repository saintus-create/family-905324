# CIV § 2914

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 1. Liens in General > Article: 6. Extinction of Liens

**Citation:** CIV § 2914

---

## Text

None of the provisions of this chapter apply to any transaction or security interest governed by the Uniform Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 819.)
