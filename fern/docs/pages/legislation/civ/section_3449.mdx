# CIV § 3449

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. VOID AND VOIDABLE TRANSFERS AND UNDERTAKINGS > Chapter: 3. Undertaking in Voidable Transfer Action

**Citation:** CIV § 3449

---

## Text

The undertaking becomes effective 10 days after service of the undertaking on the creditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 517, Sec. 93.)
