# CIV § 1798.75

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.8. PERSONAL DATA > Chapter: 1. Information Practices Act of 1977 > Article: 12. Construction With Other Laws

**Citation:** CIV § 1798.75

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be deemed to supersede Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code, except as to the provisions of Sections 1798.60, 1798.69, and 1798.70.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 47. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
