# CIV § 8300

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PRIVATE WORKS OF IMPROVEMENT > Chapter: 3. Design Professionals Lien

**Citation:** CIV § 8300

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, a “design professional” is a person described in Section 8014 who provides services pursuant to a written contract with a landowner for the design, engineering, or planning of a work of improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
