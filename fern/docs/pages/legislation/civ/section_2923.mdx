# CIV § 2923

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. LIEN > Chapter: 2. Mortgage > Article: 1. Mortgages in General

**Citation:** CIV § 2923

---

## Text

The lien of a mortgage is special, unless otherwise expressly agreed, and is independent of possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
