# CIV § 1794.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.7. CONSUMER WARRANTIES > Chapter: 1. Consumer Warranty Protection > Article: 3. Sale Warranties

**Citation:** CIV § 1794.3

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to any defect or nonconformity in consumer goods caused by the unauthorized or unreasonable use of the goods following sale.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1523.)
