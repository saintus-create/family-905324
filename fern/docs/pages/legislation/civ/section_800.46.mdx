# CIV § 800.46

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.7. Floating Home Residency Law > Article: 4. Fees and Charges

**Citation:** CIV § 800.46

---

## Text

A homeowner shall not be charged a fee for the enforcement of any of the rules and regulations of the floating home marina.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1505, Sec. 1.)
