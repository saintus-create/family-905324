# CIV § 1436

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. INTERPRETATION OF OBLIGATIONS > Chapter: 3. Conditional Obligations

**Citation:** CIV § 1436

---

## Text

A condition precedent is one which is to be performed before some right dependent thereon accrues, or some act dependent thereon is performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
