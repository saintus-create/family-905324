# CIV § 798.28

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. ESTATES IN REAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2.5. Mobilehome Residency Law > Article: 3. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** CIV § 798.28

---

## Text

The management of a mobilehome park shall disclose, in writing, within 10 business days, the name, business address, and business telephone number of the mobilehome park owner upon the receipt of a written request of a homeowner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 31, Sec. 1. (AB 294) Effective January 1, 2018.)
