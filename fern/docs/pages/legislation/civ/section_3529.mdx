# CIV § 3529

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE > Title: 4. MOTION PICTURES

**Citation:** CIV § 3529

---

## Text

That which ought to have been done is to be regarded as done, in favor of a person to whom, and against a person from whom, performance is due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 9. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
