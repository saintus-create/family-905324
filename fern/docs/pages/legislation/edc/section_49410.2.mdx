# EDC § 49410.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 1. General Powers—School Boards

**Citation:** EDC § 49410.2

---

## Text

School districts and county offices of education may apply to the State Allocation Board pursuant to Section 39619.6 for funds for the purposes of containment or removal of asbestos materials posing a hazard to health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1751, Sec. 8. Effective September 30, 1984.)
