# EDC § 81430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 7. Sale or Lease Between Agencies

**Citation:** EDC § 81430

---

## Text

The governing boards of any community college district may sell, exchange, grant or quitclaim all or any of its interest in, or may lease for a term not exceeding 99 years, to the federal government or its agencies, to the state, or to any county, city and county, city or special district, or to any other school district, any real property belonging to the district, and which is not or will not at the time of delivery of title or possession be needed for school classroom buildings by the district owning it, as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
