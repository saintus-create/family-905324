# EDC § 56322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 2. Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 56322

---

## Text

The assessment shall be conducted by persons competent to perform the assessment, as determined by the local educational agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 2008.)
