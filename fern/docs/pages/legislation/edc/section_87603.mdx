# EDC § 87603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 2. Employment of Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 87603

## Text

This article does not apply to the employment of administrators employed by appointment or contract pursuant to Section 72411.
