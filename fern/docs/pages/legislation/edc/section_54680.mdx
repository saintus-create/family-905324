# EDC § 54680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 29. PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous Programs > Article: 4. Dream Resource Center Grant Program

**Citation:** EDC § 54680

---

## Text

(a) The Dream Resource Center Grant Program is hereby established with the goal of creating Dream Resource Centers at schoolsites that serve pupils in grades 9 to 12, inclusive.

(b) The purpose of a Dream Resource Center is to provide pupils, including undocumented pupils, in grades 9 to 12, inclusive, with resources that include, but are not limited to, all of the following:

(1) Financial aid support.

(2) Social services support.

(3) State-funded immigration legal services.

(4) Academic opportunities.

(5) Parent and family workshops.

(c) For purposes of this article, “local educational agency” means a school district, county office of education, or charter school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 424, Sec. 1. (AB 278) Effective January 1, 2024.)
