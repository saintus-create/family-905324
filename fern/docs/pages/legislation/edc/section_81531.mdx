# EDC § 81531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 15. Lease and Lease-Purchase of Sites, Buildings, and Facilities

**Citation:** EDC § 81531

---

## Text

Any lease or lease-purchase contract or agreement executed prior to the effective date of this article shall remain in full force and effect. The renewal of such contracts or agreements, however, shall be governed by the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
