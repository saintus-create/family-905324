# EDC § 44452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Teacher Education Internship Act of 1967

**Citation:** EDC § 44452

---

## Text

Any school district may, in cooperation with an approved college or university, establish a teacher education internship program as provided in Section 44321, and meeting the provisions of the statutes and of the regulations of the Commission on Teacher Credentialing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 342, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2002.)
