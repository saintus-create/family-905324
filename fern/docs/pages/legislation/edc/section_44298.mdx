# EDC § 44298

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 5. Subject Matter Examinations and Assessments

**Citation:** EDC § 44298

---

## Text

Fees charged for the examinations shall be sufficient to furnish the full cost of the examination system, except as these funds may be augmented from designated appropriations by the Legislature from the Teacher Credentials Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
