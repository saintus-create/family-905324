# EDC § 45353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 7.5. Teacher Assistants

**Citation:** EDC § 45353

---

## Text

Teacher assistants, as described in this article, shall not be deemed to be teachers for any purpose under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1345, Sec. 2.)
