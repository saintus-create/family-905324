**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Education Data and Information Act of 2008

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Education Data and Information Act of 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 561, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
