# EDC § 22154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22154

---

## Text

“Pay period” means a payroll period of not less than four weeks or more than one calendar month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
