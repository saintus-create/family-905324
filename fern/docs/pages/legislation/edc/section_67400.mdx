# EDC § 67400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 16. Applicability to University of California

**Citation:** EDC § 67400

---

## Text

No provision of this part shall apply to the University of California except to the extent that the Regents of the University of California, by appropriate resolution, make that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1198, Sec. 20.)
