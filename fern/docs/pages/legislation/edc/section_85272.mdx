# EDC § 85272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Expenditures and Appropriations > Article: 6. Void Warrants

**Citation:** EDC § 85272

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools shall keep a record of all orders, or warrants which have become void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
