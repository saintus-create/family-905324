# EDC § 1626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. County School Service Fund > Article: 2. County Office of Education Budget Approval

**Citation:** EDC § 1626

---

## Text

Until the time the county office of education receives approval of its budget under this article, the county office of education shall continue to operate on the basis of the last budget adopted or revised for the county office of education for the fiscal year immediately preceding the budget year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1213, Sec. 3.)
