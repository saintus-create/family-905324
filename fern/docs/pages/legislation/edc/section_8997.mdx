# EDC § 8997

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. School Nutrition > Article: 1. Nutrition Education

**Citation:** EDC § 8997

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on July 1, 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1163, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 2004) for Chapter 10, commencing with Section 8990.)
