# EDC § 10902.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Community Recreation Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 10902.5

---

## Text

Any individual who willfully, and without just cause, interferes with or disrupts the recreational activities of individuals, groups, or entities granted the use of a recreation center by a governing body is guilty of an infraction, and shall be punished by a fine of not more than two hundred dollars ($200).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 422, Sec. 1.)
