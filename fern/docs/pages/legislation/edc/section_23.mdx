# EDC § 23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Continuation of Rights and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 23

---

## Text

All persons who, at the time this code goes into effect, are entitled to a certificate or credential under any act repealed by this code, are thereby entitled to a certificate or credential under the provisions of this code so far as the provisions of this code are applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
