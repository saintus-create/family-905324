# EDC § 90190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. California Maritime Academy > Article: 9. Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 90190

---

## Text

Employees of the academy shall retain and carry over to their status as employees of the trustees any seniority credit, permanent status, and accumulated leave as earned and available immediately prior to the employee’s transfer to employment with the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
