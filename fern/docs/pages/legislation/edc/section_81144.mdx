# EDC § 81144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Sites > Article: 7. Approvals

**Citation:** EDC § 81144

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly violates any of the provisions of this article or fraudulently makes any false statement in any verified report or affidavit required pursuant to this article, is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 470, Sec. 219.)
