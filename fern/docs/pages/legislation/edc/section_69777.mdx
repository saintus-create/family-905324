# EDC § 69777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69777

---

## Text

The commission shall encourage private lenders to increase participation in the guaranteed student loan program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1201. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
