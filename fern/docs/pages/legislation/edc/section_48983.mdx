# EDC § 48983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 4. Notification of Parent or Guardian

**Citation:** EDC § 48983

---

## Text

If any activity covered by the sections set forth in Section 48980 will be undertaken by the school during the forthcoming school term, the notice shall state that fact and shall also state the approximate date upon which any of such activities will occur.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
