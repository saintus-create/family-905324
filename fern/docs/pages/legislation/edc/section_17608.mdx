# EDC § 17608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Property Maintenance and Control > Article: 4. Healthy Schools Act of 2000

**Citation:** EDC § 17608

---

## Text

This article and Article 17 (commencing with Section 13180) of Chapter 2 of Division 7 of the Food and Agricultural Code shall be known and cited as the Healthy Schools Act of 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 730, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2008.)
