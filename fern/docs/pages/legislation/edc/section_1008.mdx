# EDC § 1008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 1. Election, Jurisdiction, Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** EDC § 1008

---

## Text

When any vacancy exists on the county board of education of any county on or after December 1, 1978, the vacancy shall be filled in the manner prescribed in Article 3 (commencing with Section 5090) of Chapter 1 of Part 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 9.)
