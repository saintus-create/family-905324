# EDC § 89

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 89

---

## Text

The word “department” whenever used in this code, unless the context otherwise requires, means the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1059, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1980.)
