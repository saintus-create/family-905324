# EDC § 89332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Students > Article: 4. Program for California Residents 60 Years of Age or Older

**Citation:** EDC § 89332

---

## Text

Fee waivers authorized pursuant to this article shall not be in place of fee waivers for disadvantaged students.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 246.)
