# EDC § 92495.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 3. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 92495.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 13332.11 and 13332.19 of the Government Code or any other law, the University of California may proceed with capital expenditures and capital outlay projects pursuant to Sections 92495 and 92495.5 without any further limitations or approvals, except those delineated in Sections 92495 and 92495.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 34, Sec. 30. (SB 860) Effective June 20, 2014.)
