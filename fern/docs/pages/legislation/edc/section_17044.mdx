# EDC § 17044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. State School Building Lease-Purchase Law of 1976 > Article: 3. Allowances

**Citation:** EDC § 17044

---

## Text

There shall be allowed to each district a maximum area of 75 square feet for each attendance unit of the district in grades 7 and 8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
