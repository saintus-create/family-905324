# EDC § 74501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46.3. The Common Cloud Data Platform > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION

**Citation:** EDC § 74501

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, both of the following definitions apply:

(a) “Chancellor’s office” means the office of the Chancellor of the California Community Colleges.

(b) “Real time” means real-time data or near real-time data.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 79, Sec. 13. (SB 135) Effective July 13, 2026.)
