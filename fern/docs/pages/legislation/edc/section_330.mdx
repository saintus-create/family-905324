# EDC § 330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. English Language Education for Immigrant Children > Article: 7. Operative Date

**Citation:** EDC § 330

---

## Text

This initiative shall become operative for all school terms which begin more than sixty days following the date on which it becomes effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 2, 1998, by initiative Proposition 227. Note: In the text, "this initiative" refers to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 300), which was added by Prop. 227.)
