# EDC § 41003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Financial Management and Control > Article: 1. Moneys Received by School Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 41003

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may, by resolution, specify the particular fund or funds maintained for the district into which shall be deposited moneys received for the rental or lease of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
