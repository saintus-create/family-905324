# EDC § 35140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 3. Meetings

**Citation:** EDC § 35140

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this article the governing board of any school district shall by rule and regulation fix the time and place for its regular meetings. Such action shall be proper notice to all members of the board of the regular meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
