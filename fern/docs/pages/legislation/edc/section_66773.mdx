# EDC § 66773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9.7. Cross-Enrollment in Online Education between Campuses of the California Community Colleges

**Citation:** EDC § 66773

---

## Text

A course provided entirely online pursuant to this chapter shall be accepted for credit at the student’s home college on the same basis as it would be for a student matriculated at the teaching college.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 743, Sec. 1. (AB 637) Effective January 1, 2018.)
