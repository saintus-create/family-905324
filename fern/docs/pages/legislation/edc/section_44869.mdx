# EDC § 44869

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 2. Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 44869

---

## Text

A teacher librarian, when employed full time as a teacher librarian or serving full time, partly as a teacher librarian and partly as a teacher, shall rank as a teacher.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 730, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 2008.)
