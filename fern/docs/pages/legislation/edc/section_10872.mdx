# EDC § 10872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.5. California Cradle-to-Career Data System Act > Article: 2. California Cradle-to-Career Data System

**Citation:** EDC § 10872

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, records or source data contained in the data system shall not be subject to disclosure under the California Public Records Act (Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 28, Sec. 31. (SB 1380) Effective January 1, 2023.)
