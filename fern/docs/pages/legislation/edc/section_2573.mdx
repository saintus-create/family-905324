# EDC § 2573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. County Tax Rates, County Superintendents > Article: 4. Allocation of Property Tax Revenues

**Citation:** EDC § 2573

---

## Text

The remainder computed pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 2571 is the amount of property tax revenues to be allocated for programs funded pursuant to Section 2558.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 797, Sec. 4. Effective July 28, 1980.)
