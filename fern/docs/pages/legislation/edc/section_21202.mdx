# EDC § 21202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 12. FOUNDATIONS AND STATE COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Foundations, Trusts, and Institutions of Arts and Sciences > Article: 3. Legal Validation Proceedings

**Citation:** EDC § 21202

---

## Text

A certified copy of the judgment of the court in the special proceeding shall be recorded in the office of the recorder of the county in which the action is brought and in the office of the recorder of every county in which any of the real property affected is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
