# EDC § 51763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 7. Work Experience Education

**Citation:** EDC § 51763

---

## Text

All laws or rules applicable to minors in employment relationships are applicable to students enrolled in work experience education courses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
