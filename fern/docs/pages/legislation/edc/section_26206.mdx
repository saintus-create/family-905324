# EDC § 26206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26206

---

## Text

All administrative costs of the board and system for the plan with respect to the Cash Balance Benefit Program shall be paid from the Teachers’ Retirement Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1999.)
