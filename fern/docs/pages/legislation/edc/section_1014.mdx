# EDC § 1014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 1. Election, Jurisdiction, Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** EDC § 1014

---

## Text

No teacher’s certificate shall be issued or renewed, nor shall any books or apparatus be adopted except by an affirmative vote of at least a majority of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
