# EDC § 48980.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 4. Notification of Parent or Guardian

**Citation:** EDC § 48980.3

---

## Text

The notification required pursuant to Section 48980 shall include information regarding pesticide products as specified in subdivision (a) of Section 17612.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 718, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
