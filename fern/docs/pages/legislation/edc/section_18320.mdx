# EDC § 18320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 3. Establishment

**Citation:** EDC § 18320

---

## Text

Any union high school district may establish, equip, and maintain a public library for the dissemination of knowledge of the arts, sciences, and general literature, in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
