# EDC § 48914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 1. Suspension or Expulsion

**Citation:** EDC § 48914

---

## Text

Each school district is authorized to establish a policy that permits school officials to conduct a meeting with the parent or guardian of a suspended pupil to discuss the causes, the duration, the school policy involved, and other matters pertinent to the suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 134, Sec. 6. Effective July 7, 1987.)
