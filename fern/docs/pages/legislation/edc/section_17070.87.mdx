# EDC § 17070.87

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 17070.87

---

## Text

New construction and modernization applications submitted before October 31, 2024, shall be processed and apportioned in accordance with this chapter, as it read on January 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 81, Sec. 7. (AB 247) Effective July 3, 2024. Operative November 6, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 30 of Ch. 81.)
