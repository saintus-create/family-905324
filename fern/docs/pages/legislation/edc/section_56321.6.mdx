# EDC § 56321.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 2. Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 56321.6

---

## Text

The copy of the notice of parent rights shall include information regarding the state special schools for pupils who are deaf, hard of hearing, blind, visually impaired, or deaf-blind.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 245, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
