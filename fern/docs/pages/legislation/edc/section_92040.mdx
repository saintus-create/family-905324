# EDC § 92040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Condemnation of Property by the Regents

**Citation:** EDC § 92040

---

## Text

The Regents of the University of California may acquire by eminent domain any property necessary to carry out any of the powers or functions of the University of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
