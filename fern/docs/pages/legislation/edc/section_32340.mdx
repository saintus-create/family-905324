# EDC § 32340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Miscellaneous > Article: 5. Unlawful Issuance of a Credential

**Citation:** EDC § 32340

---

## Text

Any state, county, or city and county superintendent, or any state, county, or city and county board of education, who issues a credential, except as provided by law, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
