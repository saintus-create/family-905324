# EDC § 35315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 12. Scholarship and Loan Funds

**Citation:** EDC § 35315

---

## Text

All money in the fund shall be available for making interest-free loans for educational advancement, for scholarship, or for grants-in-aid to eligible persons. An eligible person for such loans shall be any of the following:

(a) A bona fide organization of a school under the jurisdiction of the governing board of the school district.

(b) An enrollee at a school under the jurisdiction of the governing board of the school district.

(c) A graduate of a school under the jurisdiction of the governing board of the school district.
