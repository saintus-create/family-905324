# EDC § 8325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 14. Contract Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 8325

---

## Text

The department shall develop an annual calendar identifying target dates for contract application deadlines, contract award announcements, contract approvals, and contract evaluations. Each calendar shall be available to the public and shall be updated at least annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8440 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 220. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
