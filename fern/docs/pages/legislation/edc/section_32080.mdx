# EDC § 32080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. School Safety—Public and Private Institutions > Article: 7. Carbon Monoxide Devices

**Citation:** EDC § 32080

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “fossil fuel” has the same meaning as defined in Section 13262 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 475, Sec. 2. (AB 56) Effective January 1, 2014.)
