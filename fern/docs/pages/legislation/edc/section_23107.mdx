# EDC § 23107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Refund of Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 23107

---

## Text

Any member of the Defined Benefit Program without terminating membership in the program and upon making application on forms provided by the system shall be paid a refund of the accumulated annuity deposit contributions under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 129. Effective January 1, 1999.)
