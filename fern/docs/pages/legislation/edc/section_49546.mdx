# EDC § 49546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 10.5. Child Care Food Program

**Citation:** EDC § 49546

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that funding for costs for the implementation of this article shall come from either federal funds or transfer of funds appropriated for the support of the Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 959, Sec. 1.)
