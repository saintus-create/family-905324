# EDC § 47603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 47603

---

## Text

(a) This part shall not be construed to prohibit any private person or organization from providing funding or other assistance to the establishment or operation of a charter school.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 54) and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 38, Sec. 55. (SB 1016) Effective June 27, 2012. Section operative July 1, 2017, by its own provisions.)
