# EDC § 74152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Initiation of Petition and Local Review Procedures > Article: 3. Formation

**Citation:** EDC § 74152

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 74102 and 74103 shall apply to a petition to form a new district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
