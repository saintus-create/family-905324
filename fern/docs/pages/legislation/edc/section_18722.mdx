# EDC § 18722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 18722

---

## Text

The concurrence of seven members of the state board shall be necessary to the validity of any of its acts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1255.)
