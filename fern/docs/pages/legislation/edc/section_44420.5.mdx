# EDC § 44420.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 1. Revocation and Suspension of Certification Documents

**Citation:** EDC § 44420.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Institution of higher education” has the same meaning as used in Section 44203.

(b) “Regionally accredited” has the same meaning as used in Section 44203.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 663, Sec. 27. (AB 320) Effective January 1, 2022.)
