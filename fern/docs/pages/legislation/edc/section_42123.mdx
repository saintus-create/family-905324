# EDC § 42123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Statements of School Districts > Article: 2. Budget Requirements

**Citation:** EDC § 42123

---

## Text

Each budget shall be itemized to set forth the necessary revenues and expenditures in each fund to operate the public schools of the district as authorized by law and on forms prescribed by the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1150, Sec. 11.)
