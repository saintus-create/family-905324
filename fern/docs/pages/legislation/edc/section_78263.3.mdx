# EDC § 78263.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Courses of Study > Article: 3.54. Allied Health Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 78263.3

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 362, Sec. 1. (AB 1891) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Repeal affects Article 3.54, commencing with Section 78263.)
