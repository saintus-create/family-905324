# EDC § 56441.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.45. Special Education Programs for Individuals With Exceptional Needs Between the Ages of Three and Five Years, Inclusive

**Citation:** EDC § 56441.14

---

## Text

Criteria and options for meeting the special education transportation needs of individuals with exceptional needs between the ages of three and five, inclusive, shall be included in the local transportation policy required pursuant to paragraph (5) of subdivision (b) of Section 56195.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 854, Sec. 59. Effective January 1, 1998. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 56449.)
