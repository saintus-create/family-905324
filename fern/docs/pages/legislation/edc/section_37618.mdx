# EDC § 37618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Continuous School Programs > Article: 2. Establishment and Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 37618

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district operating pursuant to the provisions of this chapter shall establish a school calendar whereby the teaching sessions and vacation period during the school year are on a rotating basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
