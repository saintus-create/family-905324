# EDC § 69775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69775

---

## Text

The commission shall develop and distribute in cooperation with postsecondary educational institutions and private lenders, simple, common consumer information for prospective student borrowers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 847, Sec. 8. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
