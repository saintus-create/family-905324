# EDC § 58502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 31. EXPERIMENTAL SCHOOL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Alternative Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 58502

---

## Text

The parent or guardian of any pupil may request the governing board of a school district to establish an alternative school program or programs in the district pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
