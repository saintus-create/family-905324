# EDC § 90040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90040

---

## Text

Bonds shall bear interest at a fixed or variable rate of not to exceed 12 percent per annum, payable in the time, form, and manner set forth in the indenture for the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 352, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2008.)
