# EDC § 33593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 6. Advisory Commission on Special Education

**Citation:** EDC § 33593

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction or the superintendent’s designee shall serve as executive secretary to the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 530, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
