# EDC § 1945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 17. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** EDC § 1945

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools may, with the approval of the county board of education, provide advisory services in school business administration activities, in maintenance of school building and grounds, and in the processing of special problems concerning credentials as designated by the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
