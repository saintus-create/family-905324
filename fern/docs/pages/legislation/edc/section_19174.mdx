# EDC § 19174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 3. Government

**Citation:** EDC § 19174

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may receive, on behalf of the county, any gift, bequest, or devise for the county free library, or for any branch or subdivision of the library.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
