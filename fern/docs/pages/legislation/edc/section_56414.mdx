# EDC § 56414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.3. Family Empowerment Centers on Disability

**Citation:** EDC § 56414

---

## Text

The State Department of Education is required to implement this chapter only if an appropriation is made for this purpose in the Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 690, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
