# EDC § 100442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade > Article: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade School Facilities Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 100442

---

## Text

All money deposited in the 1998 State School Facilities Fund, that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 16. Approved in Proposition 1A at the November 3, 1998, election.)
