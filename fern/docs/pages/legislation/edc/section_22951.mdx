# EDC § 22951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 16. Employer and State Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 22951

---

## Text

In addition to any other contributions required by this part, employers shall, on account of liability for benefits pursuant to Section 22717, contribute monthly to the Teachers’ Retirement Fund 0.25 percent of the creditable compensation upon which members’ contributions under this part are based.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1025, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2001.)
