# EDC § 22302.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22302.5

---

## Text

The board may contract with a qualified third-party administrator for custodial, record keeping, or other administrative services necessary to carry into effect the provisions of Chapter 38 (commencing with Section 25000) of this part or Part 14.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 22302 (as added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74) by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1021, Sec. 13.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
