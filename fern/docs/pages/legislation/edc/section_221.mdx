# EDC § 221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 3. Prohibition of Discrimination

**Citation:** EDC § 221

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to an educational institution that is controlled by a religious organization if the application would not be consistent with the religious tenets of that organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 220.5 by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2008.)
