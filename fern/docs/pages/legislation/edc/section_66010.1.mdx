# EDC § 66010.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 2. Comprehensive Mission Statement

**Citation:** EDC § 66010.1

---

## Text

The purpose of this article is to identify common educational missions shared by educational institutions in California and to differentiate more specific missions and functions between the various educational segments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1198, Sec. 4.1.)
