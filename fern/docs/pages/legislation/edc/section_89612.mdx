# EDC § 89612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Optional Retirement Program > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 89612

---

## Text

“Compensation” and “salary” have the same meaning as set forth in subdivisions (a) and (b) of Section 20022 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1178, Sec. 1.)
