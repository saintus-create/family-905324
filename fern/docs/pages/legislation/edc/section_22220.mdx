# EDC § 22220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** EDC § 22220

---

## Text

In addition to the authority granted pursuant to Section 11181 of the Government Code, the board may subpoena witnesses and compel their attendance to testify before it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 864, Sec. 3. (AB 2663) Effective January 1, 2013.)
