# EDC § 17484

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 4. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** EDC § 17484

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district, constituting the governing body of an elementary district, a high school district, or any two of those districts, may sell any building, structure, or other fixture, belonging to one of its respective districts to another district governed by it, for an amount to be fixed by the governing body, without advertisement for or receipt of bids or compliance with any other provisions of this code.

Whenever any property is sold under this section it shall be removed from the premises of the district selling it within 60 days from the date of the sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
