# EDC § 8246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 4. Reimbursement Rates

**Citation:** EDC § 8246

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction and the State Controller shall establish the necessary plans to advance preschool funds to contracting agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8268 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 94. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
