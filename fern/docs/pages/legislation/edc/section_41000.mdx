# EDC § 41000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Financial Management and Control > Article: 1. Moneys Received by School Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 41000

---

## Text

No assessor, tax collector, city, city and county, or county treasurer shall charge or receive any fees or compensation for assessing, collecting, receiving, keeping, or disbursing any school moneys, but the whole moneys collected shall be paid to the city, city and county, or county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
