# EDC § 335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. English Language Education for Immigrant Children > Article: 8. Amendment

**Citation:** EDC § 335

---

## Text

The provisions of this act may be amended by a statute that becomes effective upon approval by the electorate or by a statute passed by a majority vote of each house of the Legislature and signed by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 753, Sec. 8. (SB 1174) Approved in Proposition 58 at the November 8, 2016, election. Operative July 1, 2017, by Sec. 9 of Proposition 58. Note: In the text, "this act" refers to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 300), which was added June 2, 1998, by Prop. 227 and amended Nov. 8, 2016, by Prop. 58.)
