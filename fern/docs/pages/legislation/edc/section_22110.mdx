# EDC § 22110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22110

---

## Text

“California service” means service performed in California for which credit may be given.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 933, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1994.)
