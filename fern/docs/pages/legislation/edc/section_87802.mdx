# EDC § 87802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87802

---

## Text

The governing board of each community college district shall adopt and cause to be printed and made available to each academic employee a schedule of salaries to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 158. Effective September 25, 1990.)
