# EDC § 17528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 9. Joint Use

**Citation:** EDC § 17528

---

## Text

As used in this article, “building” includes onsite and offsite facilities, utilities, and improvements which, as agreed upon by the parties, are appropriate for the proper operation or function of the building to be jointly occupied and used. It also includes the permanent improvement of school grounds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
