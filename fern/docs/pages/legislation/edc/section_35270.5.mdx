# EDC § 35270.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 9. Property

**Citation:** EDC § 35270.5

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may acquire by eminent domain any property necessary to carry out any of the powers or functions of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
