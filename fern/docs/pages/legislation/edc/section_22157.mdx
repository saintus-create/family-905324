# EDC § 22157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22157

---

## Text

“Projected final compensation” means the final compensation used in computing the disability or family allowance increased by 2 percent, compounded annually to the earlier of normal retirement age or the date the disability allowance is terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 933, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1994.)
