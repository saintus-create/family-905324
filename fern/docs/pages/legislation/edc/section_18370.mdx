# EDC § 18370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 5. Dissolution of Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 18370

---

## Text

The district may at any time be dissolved if two-thirds of the votes cast at an election called by the library trustees upon the question of dissolution are in favor of the dissolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
