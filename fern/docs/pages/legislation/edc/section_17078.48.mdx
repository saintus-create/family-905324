# EDC § 17078.48

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 11.7. State Augmentation of Federal Supporting America’s School Infrastructure Grant Program for Priority School Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 17078.48

## Text

The State Department of Education shall adopt regulations setting forth the requirements and procedures for the allocation and use of bond funds pursuant to this article, including, but not limited to, all of the following:

(a) The process for determining recipients of direct technical assistance.

(b) The framework for state and county collaboration efforts.

(c) The development, maintenance, and accessibility of centralized online resources.

(d) Reporting and accountability measures to ensure the effective use of the allocated funds and the achievement of its intended outcomes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 81, Sec. 24. (AB 247) Effective July 3, 2024. Operative November 6, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 30 of Ch. 81.)
