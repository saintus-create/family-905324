# EDC § 89611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Optional Retirement Program > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 89611

---

## Text

“Benefit” means any monthly or lump-sum payment due a retirant, or other beneficiary, and includes lump-sum payments due on account of death.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 385, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
