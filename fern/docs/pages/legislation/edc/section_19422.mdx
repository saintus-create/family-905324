# EDC § 19422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19422

---

## Text

The trustee shall hold office for the term of four years beginning on the last Friday in November next succeeding their appointment or election.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1376.)
