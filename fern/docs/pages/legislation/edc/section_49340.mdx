# EDC § 49340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Pupil Safety > Article: 4. Hazardous Materials Education

**Citation:** EDC § 49340

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the California Hazardous Materials Education Act of 1982.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 785, Sec. 1.)
