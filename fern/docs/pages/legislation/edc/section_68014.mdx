# EDC § 68014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 68014

---

## Text

“Parent” means the parent with whom the minor resides; or, if both parents are deceased, his or her legal guardian.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
