# EDC § 69763.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69763.3

---

## Text

At any time before wages are garnished or a lien is placed on property, a borrower may pay the Student Aid Commission the amount of the recorded judgment, plus costs. If that payment is made, wage garnishment or the attachment of property shall not commence. The payment shall not constitute a waiver of the right to a hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 659, Sec. 3. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
