# EDC § 92851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Governor’s Teacher Scholars Program

**Citation:** EDC § 92851

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the University of California unless and until the Regents of the University of California act, by resolution, to make it applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 2, Sec. 4. Effective March 29, 1999.)
