# EDC § 46011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Absences

**Citation:** EDC § 46011

---

## Text

Absences due to illness or quarantine shall be verified by the district or the county superintendent of schools in such manner as the Superintendent of Public Instruction may provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
