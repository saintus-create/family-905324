# EDC § 18743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 5. Library System Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18743

---

## Text

Each member library of a system shall provide equal access to all residents of the area served by the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 7. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
