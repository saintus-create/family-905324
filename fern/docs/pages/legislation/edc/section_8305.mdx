# EDC § 8305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 12. Child Development Program Personnel Qualifications

**Citation:** EDC § 8305

---

## Text

The Commission on Teacher Credentialing shall establish standards for the issuance of the permits herein provided for. The standards may be changed from time to time, but changes therein shall not affect then valid permits issued to persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8370 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 201. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
