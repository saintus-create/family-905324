# EDC § 66940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11.3. The California Distance Learning Policy

**Citation:** EDC § 66940

---

## Text

There is hereby established the California Distance Learning Policy, which sets forth the guiding goal and principles for the utilization of technology in California postsecondary education.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 467, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
