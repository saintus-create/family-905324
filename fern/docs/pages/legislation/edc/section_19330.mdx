# EDC § 19330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 3. Books

**Citation:** EDC § 19330

---

## Text

Books may be taken from the California State Library by the Members of the Legislature and by other state officers during regular office hours.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 20. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
