# EDC § 78084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 7. Hire UP Program

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on March 15, 2029, and as of January 1, 2030, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 9, Sec. 10. (AB 123) Effective June 27, 2025. Repealed January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 7 commencing with Section 78080.)
