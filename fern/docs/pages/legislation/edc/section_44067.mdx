# EDC § 44067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 3. Interchange Between Certificated and Classified Positions

**Citation:** EDC § 44067

---

## Text

Any person who, on September 15, 1961, is serving in a position for which a credential was issued or certification qualifications required pursuant to Section 44286 prior to the repeal of such sections, shall be deemed to be an employee in a position requiring such credential or certification qualifications for as long as he holds such position after September 15, 1961.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
