# EDC § 19511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 5. Biennial Election of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19511

---

## Text

To be qualified to vote at any library district election a person shall be registered to vote in the library district, at least 29 days before the election.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1376.)
