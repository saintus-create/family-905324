# EDC § 56165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 5. Licensed Children’s Institutions and Foster Family Homes

**Citation:** EDC § 56165

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to individuals with exceptional needs placed in a licensed children’s institution pursuant to Section 56365.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1044, Sec. 6.5. Operative July 1, 1982, pursuant to Section 56166.5.)
