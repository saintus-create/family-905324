# EDC § 66076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 7.5. Designation of California Black-Serving Institutions

**Citation:** EDC § 66076

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Designation of California Black-Serving Institutions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 627, Sec. 2. (SB 1348) Effective January 1, 2025.)
