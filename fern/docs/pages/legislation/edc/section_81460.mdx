# EDC § 81460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 9. Sale of Personal Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81460

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the governing board of any community college district owning land upon which agricultural products are grown may enter into agreements with an agricultural cooperative or association for the purpose of maintaining, harvesting or selling such products.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
