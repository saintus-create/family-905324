# EDC § 19178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 3. Government

**Citation:** EDC § 19178

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may accept on behalf of the county free library, all books and other property of school libraries and of the teachers’ library, and may manage and maintain them as a part of the county free library.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
