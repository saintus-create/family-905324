# EDC § 87743.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 6.5. Reduction in Services

**Citation:** EDC § 87743.1

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “faculty service area” means a service or instructional subject area or group of related services or instructional subject areas performed by faculty and established by a community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 973, Sec. 52. Operative July 1, 1990, pursuant to Sec. 70(d) of Ch. 973.)
