# EDC § 69776.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69776.5

---

## Text

The commission may enter into contracts with a public agency or a private entity to improve the processing and distribution of guaranteed student loan applications through the use of electronic networks and unified data bases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 501, Sec. 3. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
