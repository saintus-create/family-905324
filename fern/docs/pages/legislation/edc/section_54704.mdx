# EDC § 54704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 29. PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous Programs > Article: 6. University and College Opportunities Program

**Citation:** EDC § 54704

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall adopt rules and regulations necessary to implement the provisions of this article on or before March 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1298, Sec. 16.)
