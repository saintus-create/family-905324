# EDC § 32223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Safety—Public Institutions > Article: 3. Insurance for Athletic Teams

**Citation:** EDC § 32223

---

## Text

The coverage provided under this article shall not exclude additional or broader coverage by an educational institution or its student body organization.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1011.)
