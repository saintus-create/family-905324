# EDC § 94

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 94

---

## Text

“Frontier school district” means a school district that meets either of the following conditions:

(a) The total number of pupils in average daily attendance at all of the schools served by the school district is fewer than 600.

(b) Each county in which a school operated by the school district is located has a total population density fewer than 10 persons per square mile.
