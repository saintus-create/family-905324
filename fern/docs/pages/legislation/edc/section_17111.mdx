# EDC § 17111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. School District Revenue Bonds > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 17111

---

## Text

The governing board may contract with any person, firm, partnership, joint venture, or other private entity for the purpose of issuing revenue bonds pursuant to Section 17810 and for the purpose of renting or leasing the facilities constructed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
