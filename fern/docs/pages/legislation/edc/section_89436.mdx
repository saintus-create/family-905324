# EDC § 89436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.6. Center for African-American Education Excellence and Achievement

**Citation:** EDC § 89436

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Center for African-American Educational Excellence and Achievement be funded by a variety of sources, including grants, contributions, and appropriations from federal, state, local, or private sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1277, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
