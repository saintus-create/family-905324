# EDC § 17072.32

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 5. New Construction Funding Process

**Citation:** EDC § 17072.32

---

## Text

(a) For any project that has received an apportionment pursuant to Section 17072.30, funding shall be released in amounts equal to the amount of the local match upon certification by the school district that the school district has entered into a binding contract for completion of the approved project.

(b) This section is operative January 1, 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 8) and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 894, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2005. Section operative January 1, 2008, by its own provisions.)
