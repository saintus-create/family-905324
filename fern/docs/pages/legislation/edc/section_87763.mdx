# EDC § 87763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 7. Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 87763

---

## Text

Governing boards of community college districts may grant leaves of absence to persons employed in academic positions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 136. Effective September 25, 1990.)
