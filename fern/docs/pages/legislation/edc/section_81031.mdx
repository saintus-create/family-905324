# EDC § 81031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Sites > Article: 2. School Sites

## Text

The board of governors shall establish standards for community college sites.
