# EDC § 89912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 89912

---

## Text

All bonds, notes, borrowings, or other evidences of indebtedness of an auxiliary organization and interest thereon or income therefrom are exempt from all taxation in the state other than estate and generation skipping transfer taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 779, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
