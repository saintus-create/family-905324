# EDC § 53302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 18. Race to the Top > Article: 3. Parent Empowerment

**Citation:** EDC § 53302

---

## Text

(a) No more than 75 schools shall be subject to a petition authorized by this article.

(b) A petition shall be counted toward this limit upon the Superintendent and state board receiving notice from the local educational agency of its final disposition of the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 497, Sec. 73. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2020.)
