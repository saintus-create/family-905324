# EDC § 52412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 6. Summer Environmental Internship Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52412

---

## Text

Approval of environmental internships shall be required by the district superintendent or the county superintendent of schools.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
