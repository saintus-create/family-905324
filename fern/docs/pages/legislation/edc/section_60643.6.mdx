# EDC § 60643.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. California Assessment of Academic Achievement > Article: 4. California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress

**Citation:** EDC § 60643.6

---

## Text

A local educational agency shall be reimbursed by the contractor selected pursuant to this article for any unexpected expenses incurred due to scheduling changes that resulted from the late delivery of testing materials in connection with the California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 327, Sec. 24. (AB 1599) Effective January 1, 2015.)
