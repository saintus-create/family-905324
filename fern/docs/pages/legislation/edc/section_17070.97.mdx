# EDC § 17070.97

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 17070.97

---

## Text

The board shall require the school district to insure against public liability or property damage in connection with any facility constructed or modernized with an apportionment under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 4. Effective August 27, 1998.)
