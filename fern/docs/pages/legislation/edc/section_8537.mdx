# EDC § 8537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Adult Education > Article: 3. Functions of Continuing Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8537

---

## Text

If a mutual agreement cannot be reached by the district governing boards, the points of disagreement shall be resolved by the State Board of Education and the Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
