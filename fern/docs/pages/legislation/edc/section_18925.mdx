# EDC § 18925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Municipal Libraries > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18925

---

## Text

The board of library trustees may borrow books from, lend books to, and exchange books with other libraries, and may allow nonresidents to borrow books upon such conditions as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
