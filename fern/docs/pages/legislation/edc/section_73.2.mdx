# EDC § 73.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 73.2

---

## Text

“Spouse” includes “registered domestic partner,” as required by Section 297.5 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 27. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
