# EDC § 38001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23. SUPPLEMENTAL SERVICES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Security Departments

**Citation:** EDC § 38001

---

## Text

Persons employed and compensated as members of a police department of a school district, when appointed and duly sworn, are peace officers, for the purposes of carrying out their duties of employment pursuant to Section 830.32 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
