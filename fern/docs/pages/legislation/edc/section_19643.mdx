# EDC § 19643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Library Districts and Museums in Unincorporated Towns and Villages > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 19643

---

## Text

At its first meeting held after the general district election the board shall organize by electing one of its number president, and another one of its number secretary. They shall serve as such for one year or until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
