# EDC § 17187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. California School Finance Authority

**Citation:** EDC § 17187

---

## Text

All moneys received under this chapter, whether received as proceeds from the sale of revenue bonds or as revenues, are trust funds to be held and applied solely as provided in this chapter. Any officer, bank, or trust company with whom those moneys have been deposited, shall act as trustee of those moneys and shall hold and apply them for those purposes, subject to the requirements of this chapter and the resolution authorizing the bonds of any issue, or the trust agreement or indenture securing those bonds, may provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
