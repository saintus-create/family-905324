# EDC § 94141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Part: 59. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California Educational Facilities Authority Act > Article: 4. Powers and Duties; Notes and Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 94141

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of this chapter shall be payable from funds provided the authority therefor, and no liability or obligation shall be incurred by the authority hereunder beyond the extent to which moneys shall have been provided therefor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
