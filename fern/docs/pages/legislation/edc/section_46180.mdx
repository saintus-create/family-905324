# EDC § 46180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 6. Opportunity Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 46180

---

## Text

The minimum day in an opportunity school or opportunity class is 180 minutes. A pupil may be enrolled in an opportunity program for not less than a class period of the school, nor more than 179 minutes per day. Attendance for a pupil enrolled in an opportunity program shall be credited to the regular class attendance.

A pupil who is enrolled in both an opportunity program and a regular school, shall attend school for a period of time not less than the minimum schoolday required for the grade level in which he is enrolled.
