# EDC § 33005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** EDC § 33005

---

## Text

The board shall appoint an acting secretary, who shall also act as executive officer of the board in the absence of the Superintendent of Public Instruction from the state, or in case of his incapacity for duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
