# EDC § 1941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 17. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** EDC § 1941

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools may, with the approval of the county board of education, provide services to school districts or community college districts in screening and directing teachers to the schools under his jurisdiction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
