# EDC § 26135.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26135.5

---

## Text

“Public employer” means a public employer as defined by subdivision (i) of Section 7522.04 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 559, Sec. 34. (AB 1381) Effective January 1, 2014. Deemed operative January 1, 2013, by Sec. 1 of Ch. 559.)
