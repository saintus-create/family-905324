# EDC § 90400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Specific Provisions Relating to Particular Campuses > Article: 1. California State Polytechnic University and California Polytechnic State University

**Citation:** EDC § 90400

---

## Text

(a) The California Polytechnic State University included within the California State University is comprised of a campus or complex of buildings, facilities, and land situated within the County of San Luis Obispo.

(b) The California State Polytechnic University included within the California State University is comprised of a campus or complex of buildings, facilities, and land situated within the County of Los Angeles and within or near the City of Pomona and the City of San Dimas.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 143, Sec. 168.)
