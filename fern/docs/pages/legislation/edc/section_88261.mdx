# EDC § 88261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 9. Assumption by County Superintendent of Schools of the Operation of a Community College District’s Data Processing Center

**Citation:** EDC § 88261

---

## Text

A community college district classified employee assigned to a data processing center which is transferred to a county superintendent of schools shall, upon the election of the employee to do so, cease to be an employee of the community college district upon the effective date of the agreement transferring the data processing center to the county superintendent of schools and shall thereafter be an employee of the county superintendent of schools and be paid from the county school service fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 207. Effective September 25, 1990.)
