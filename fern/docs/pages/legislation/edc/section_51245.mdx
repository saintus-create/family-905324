# EDC § 51245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 4. Exemptions from Requirements

**Citation:** EDC § 51245

---

## Text

For purposes of the credit that may be given pursuant to Sections 51243 and 51244 for world language courses undertaken in private schools, it shall not be required that instructors in the private schools be regularly credentialed teachers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 865, Sec. 21. (AB 2319) Effective January 1, 2019.)
