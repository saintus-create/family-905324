# EDC § 53201.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 18. Race to the Top > Article: 2. Intervening in the Persistently Lowest-Achieving Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 53201.5

---

## Text

The Superintendent shall notify the governing board of a school district, county superintendent of schools, or the governing body of a charter school or its equivalent, that one or more of the schools in its jurisdiction have been identified as a persistently lowest-achieving school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, 5th Ex. Sess., Ch. 2, Sec. 8. (SB 1 5x) Effective April 12, 2010.)
