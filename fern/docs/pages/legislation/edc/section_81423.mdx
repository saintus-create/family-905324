# EDC § 81423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 6. Joint Use, School Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81423

---

## Text

No such lease or agreement shall exceed a term of five years, but may be renewed on the same or different conditions at the end of such term.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
