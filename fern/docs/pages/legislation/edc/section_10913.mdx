# EDC § 10913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Community Recreation Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 10913

---

## Text

The governing body of a school district may require persons or organizations desiring to use schoolbuses belonging to a school to pay fees for the use as the governing body may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 94.)
