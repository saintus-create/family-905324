# EDC § 90142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. California Maritime Academy > Article: 4. Tuition, Fees, and Financial Aid

**Citation:** EDC § 90142

---

## Text

The trustees may provide for the payment of tuition and fees charged students by installments payable at intervals determined by the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
