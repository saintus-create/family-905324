# EDC § 18381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 6. Meetings of Boards of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18381

---

## Text

Special meetings may be called at any time by two trustees, by written notices served upon each member at least 12 hours before the time specified for the meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
