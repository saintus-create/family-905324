# EDC § 8668

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.8. California State Summer School for Mathematics and Science

**Citation:** EDC § 8668

---

## Text

The site for the California State Summer School for Mathematics and Science shall be chosen by the Regents of the University of California. The Regents of the University of California are requested to collaborate with other institutions of higher education regarding the planning and administration of the summer school.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 78, Sec. 13. Effective July 7, 1999.)
