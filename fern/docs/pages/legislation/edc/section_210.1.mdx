# EDC § 210.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 210.1

---

## Text

“Disability” includes mental and physical disability as defined in Section 12926 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2008.)
