# EDC § 35700.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 1. Reorganization of School Districts by the Electorate

**Citation:** EDC § 35700.3

---

## Text

A petition filed under Section 35700 shall be required to reasonably identify the territory to be reorganized, which identification may include references to streets or prominent geographic features. The inclusion of legal descriptions or plat maps, or both, however, shall not be a prerequisite for the filing of a valid petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 906, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
