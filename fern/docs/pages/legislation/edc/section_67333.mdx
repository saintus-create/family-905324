# EDC § 67333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.3. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act Program

**Citation:** EDC § 67333

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the 1988 Higher Education Capital Outlay Bond Fund, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 44, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 78 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
