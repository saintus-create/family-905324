# EDC § 74103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Initiation of Petition and Local Review Procedures > Article: 1. Transfer

**Citation:** EDC § 74103

---

## Text

The persons securing the signatures to a petition of electors to reorganize districts shall attach thereto an affidavit that all persons who signed the petition did so in the presence of the affiant and that each signature is a genuine signature of the person whose name it purports to be.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
