# EDC § 18923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Municipal Libraries > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18923

---

## Text

The board of library trustees may purchase real property, and erect or rent and equip, such buildings or rooms, as may be necessary, when in its judgment a suitable building, or portion thereof, has not been provided by the legislative body of the municipality for the library.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
