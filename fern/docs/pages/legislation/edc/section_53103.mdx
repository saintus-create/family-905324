# EDC § 53103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 18. Race to the Top > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 53103

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this act is declaratory of the requirements and definitions specified in the federal guidelines for the federal Race to the Top Fund. It is the intent of the Legislature that, to the extent that the federal guidelines are revised, the state plan or plans also be revised accordingly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, 5th Ex. Sess., Ch. 2, Sec. 8. (SB 1 5x) Effective April 12, 2010.)
