# EDC § 94813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Part: 59. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Private Postsecondary Institutions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 94813

---

## Text

“Accredited” means an institution is accredited by an accrediting agency recognized by the United States Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 840, Sec. 4. (SB 1247) Effective January 1, 2015. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
