# EDC § 44293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 5. Subject Matter Examinations and Assessments

**Citation:** EDC § 44293

---

## Text

In selecting subject matter examinations, the commission shall consider the importance of timely and expeditious processing and the desirability and convenience of machine scoring.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
