# EDC § 5440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 9. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 5440

---

## Text

The provisions of Division 16 (commencing with Section 16000) of the Elections Code, relating to election contests shall apply to school district elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1995.)
