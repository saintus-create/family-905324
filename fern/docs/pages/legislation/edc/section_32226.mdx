# EDC § 32226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Safety—Public Institutions > Article: 3.5. Communication Devices in Classrooms

**Citation:** EDC § 32226

---

## Text

As used in this article, “two-way communication devices” include, but are not necessarily limited to, telephones, intercoms, walkie-talkies, or portable radios.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 972, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
