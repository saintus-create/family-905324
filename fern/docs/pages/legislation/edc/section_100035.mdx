# EDC § 100035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 66. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1996 > Article: 2. School Facilities Program Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 100035

---

## Text

Of the proceeds from the sale of bonds pursuant to this chapter, not more than twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) may be used for projects that include joint use of facilities pursuant to Section 17750 or 17751.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 203 at the March 26, 1996, election.)
