# EDC § 56503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Procedural Safeguards

**Citation:** EDC § 56503

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall preclude the parties to a hearing from agreeing to use a mediation conference or resolving their dispute in an informal, nonadversarial manner, even though a request for a state level hearing has been filed or even if the hearing has commenced.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1360, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1993.)
