# EDC § 44004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 44004

---

## Text

The word “certificate” used as a noun refers to the document issued by a county board of education to license the holder to perform the service specified in the certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
