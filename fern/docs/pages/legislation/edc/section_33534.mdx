# EDC § 33534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 3. Instructional Quality Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33534

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction or his representative shall serve as executive secretary to the commission.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
