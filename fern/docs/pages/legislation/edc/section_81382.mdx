# EDC § 81382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 4. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81382

---

## Text

The failure to comply with the provisions of this article shall not invalidate the transfer or conveyance of real property to a purchaser or encumbrancer for value.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
