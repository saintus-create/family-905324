# EDC § 69764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69764

---

## Text

In the event that the amount of loans applied for under this article exceeds the amount of the loans that may be guaranteed pursuant to this article, the commission may establish a system of priorities for the approval of loans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1113, Sec. 33.1. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
