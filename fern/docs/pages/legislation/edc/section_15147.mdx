# EDC § 15147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Bonds of School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 3. Issuance and Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 15147

---

## Text

Before selling the bonds, or any part of them, the board of supervisors or community college district, as appropriate, shall give notice as required by Section 53692 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 7, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 22, 2004.)
