# EDC § 32210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Safety—Public Institutions > Article: 2. Willful Disturbance, Public Schools or Meetings

**Citation:** EDC § 32210

---

## Text

Any person who willfully disturbs any public school or any public school meeting is guilty of a misdemeanor, and shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars ($500).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 83. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
