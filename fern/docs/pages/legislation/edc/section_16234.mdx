# EDC § 16234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 5. School Housing Aid for Districts Impacted by Seasonal Agricultural Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 16234

## Text

Sections 16000 to 16006, inclusive, Sections 16009, 16018, and 16021, and Sections 16091 to 16100, inclusive, shall be applicable to the administration of this article, unless the context of this article, as determined by the board, requires otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
