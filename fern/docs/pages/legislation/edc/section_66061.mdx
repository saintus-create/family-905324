# EDC § 66061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 6. Campus Child Care and Development Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 66061

---

## Text

A child development program established by the California Community Colleges, the California State University, or the University of California pursuant to Section 66060 is encouraged to give priority to children of students who are single parents and who meet income criteria as established by the institution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 265, Sec. 1. (AB 809) Effective January 1, 2020.)
