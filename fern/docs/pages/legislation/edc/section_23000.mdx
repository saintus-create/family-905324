# EDC § 23000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23000

---

## Text

Each employer shall deduct from the creditable compensation of members employed by the employer the member contributions required by this part and shall remit to the system those contributions plus the employer contributions required by this part and Section 44987.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 482, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1998.)
