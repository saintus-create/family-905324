# EDC § 24925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 35. Joint District Salary Retirement Plan > Article: 2. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24925

---

## Text

The governing boards, after the adoption of the plan, shall at regular intervals, each not exceeding a period of five years, secure a general survey and actuarial report of the plan, and the boards shall from time to time amend the plan in any manner found to be advisable to meet changed conditions, or, in the light of experience, considered necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
