# EDC § 17660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Heating, Ventilation, And Air Conditioning Systems

**Citation:** EDC § 17660

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that it is the policy of the state that school facilities provide healthy indoor air quality, including adequate ventilation, to students, teachers, and other occupants in order to protect occupant health, reduce sick days, and improve student productivity and performance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 777, Sec. 2. (AB 2232) Effective January 1, 2023.)
