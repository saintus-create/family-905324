# EDC § 74012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Legislative Intent and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 74012

---

## Text

(a) “Component school district” means a high school or unified school district which is included within a community college district.

(b) “Nondistrict territory” means a high school or unified school district which is not included within any community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
