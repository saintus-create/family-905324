# EDC § 58802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 31. EXPERIMENTAL SCHOOL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Specialized Secondary Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 58802

---

## Text

Faculty members providing instruction in specialized secondary programs shall develop model curricula which the Superintendent of Public Instruction shall make available to other school districts in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 922, Sec. 166. Effective January 1, 1995.)
