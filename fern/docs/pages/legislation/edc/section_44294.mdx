# EDC § 44294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 5. Subject Matter Examinations and Assessments

**Citation:** EDC § 44294

---

## Text

The commission may approve more than one examination with passing scores appropriate to each examination for any of the categories to be examined as required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
