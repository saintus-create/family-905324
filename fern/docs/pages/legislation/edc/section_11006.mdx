# EDC § 11006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. California Academic Partnership Program

**Citation:** EDC § 11006

---

## Text

The Trustees of the California State University shall adopt rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 8. Effective July 28, 1983. Section operative July 1, 1984, pursuant to Section 11007.)
