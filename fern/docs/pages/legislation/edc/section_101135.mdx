# EDC § 101135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 70. KINDERGARTEN THROUGH COMMUNITY COLLEGE PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2016 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade > Article: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade School Facilities Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 101135

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13340 of the Government Code, there is hereby appropriated from the General Fund in the State Treasury, for the purposes of this chapter, an amount that will equal the total of the following:

(a) The sum annually necessary to pay the principal of, and interest on, bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter, as the principal and interest become due and payable.

(b) The sum necessary to carry out Section 101138, appropriated without regard to fiscal years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 51, Sec. 3.)
