# EDC § 88186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 5. Differential Compensation

**Citation:** EDC § 88186

---

## Text

This article shall apply to community college districts that have adopted the merit system in the same manner and effect as if it were a part of Article 3 (commencing with Section 88060) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
