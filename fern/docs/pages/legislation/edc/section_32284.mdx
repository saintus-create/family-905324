# EDC § 32284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Interagency School Safety Demonstration Act of 1985 > Article: 5. School Safety Plans

**Citation:** EDC § 32284

---

## Text

The comprehensive school safety plan may also include, at local discretion of the governing board of the school district and using local funds, procedures for responding to the release of a pesticide or other toxic substance from properties located within one-quarter mile of a school. No funds received from the state may be used for this purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 35294.4 by Stats. 2003, Ch. 828, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2004.)
