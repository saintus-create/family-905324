# EDC § 8288.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 10. Childcare Subsidy Plan for the City and County of San Francisco

**Citation:** EDC § 8288.5

---

## Text

Commencing on the effective date of the act that added this section, the components of the plan relating to preschool shall be overseen by the department and the components of the plan addressing all other childcare programs shall be overseen by the State Department of Social Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 138. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
