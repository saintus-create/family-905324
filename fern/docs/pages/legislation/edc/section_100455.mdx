# EDC § 100455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Higher Education Facilities > Article: 1. Program Provision

**Citation:** EDC § 100455

---

## Text

(a) Two billion five hundred million dollars ($2,500,000,000) of the proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this part shall be deposited in the 1998 Higher Education Capital Outlay Bond Fund which is hereby established in the State Treasury. These funds shall be available for expenditure when appropriated.

(b) One billion two hundred fifty million dollars ($1,250,000,000) of the bonds described in subdivision (a), shall only be issued and sold pursuant to this chapter on or after July 1, 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 16. Approved in Proposition 1A at the November 3, 1998, election.)
