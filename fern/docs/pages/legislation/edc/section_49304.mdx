# EDC § 49304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Pupil Safety > Article: 1. School Safety Patrols

**Citation:** EDC § 49304

---

## Text

The members of a school safety patrol established hereunder shall be authorized and required only to give traffic signals and directions in order to assist school pupils in safely crossing streets and highways.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
