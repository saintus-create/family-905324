# EDC § 33009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** EDC § 33009

---

## Text

Notice of each meeting shall be given by the secretary by registered mail to each member of the board at least 10 days prior to the time of the meeting. Notice of the meeting may be waived in writing by all members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
