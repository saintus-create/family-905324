# EDC § 92532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92532

---

## Text

Moneys in any construction fund may be invested as the regents in its sole discretion shall determine, subject only to such limitations as may be provided in an indenture providing for the issuance of revenue bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
