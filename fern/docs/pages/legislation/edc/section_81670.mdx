# EDC § 81670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Management and Control of Property > Article: 4. Community College Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81670

---

## Text

The governing board of any community college district may construct and maintain dormitories in connection with any community college within the district for use and occupancy by students in attendance at the community college, and shall fix the rates to be charged the students for quarters in the dormitories.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 251, Sec. 17. Effective June 11, 1982.)
