# EDC § 11505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 16. Programs to Encourage Parental Involvement

**Citation:** EDC § 11505

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by federal law, a school district may contract with nonprofit organizations and agencies experienced in administering parent involvement programs to design or implement, or design and implement, a school’s parent involvement program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1400, Sec. 1.)
