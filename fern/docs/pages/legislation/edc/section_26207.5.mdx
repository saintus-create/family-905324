# EDC § 26207.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26207.5

---

## Text

In no event shall the funding of the Cash Balance Benefit Program be a liability of the state or the General Fund, nor shall the General Fund be used to offset or fund any liabilities attributed to the operation of the Cash Balance Benefit Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 1999.)
