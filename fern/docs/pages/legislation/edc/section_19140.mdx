# EDC § 19140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 2. County Librarian

**Citation:** EDC § 19140

---

## Text

Upon the establishment of a county free library, the board of supervisors shall appoint a county librarian.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
