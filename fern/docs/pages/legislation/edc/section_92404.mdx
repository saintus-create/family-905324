# EDC § 92404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 92404

---

## Text

“Indenture” means an agreement entered into by the regents pursuant to which revenue bonds are issued regardless of whether such agreement is expressed in the form of a resolution of the regents or by other instrument.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
