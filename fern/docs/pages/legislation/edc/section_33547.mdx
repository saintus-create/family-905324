# EDC § 33547

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 3. Instructional Quality Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33547

---

## Text

At the next adoption or followup adoption of instructional materials for use in kindergarten and grades 1 to 8, inclusive, in English Language Arts and English Language Development, the commission shall consider including resources for teachers to help them meet the unique academic and English language development needs of newcomer pupils.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 185, Sec. 1. (AB 1255) Effective October 1, 2025.)
