# EDC § 51929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Comprehensive Health Education > Article: 6. Mandatory Mental Health Education

**Citation:** EDC § 51929

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2024, the department shall develop a plan to expand mental health instruction in California public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 675, Sec. 2. (SB 224) Effective January 1, 2022.)
