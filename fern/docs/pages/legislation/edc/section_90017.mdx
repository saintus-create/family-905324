# EDC § 90017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90017

---

## Text

The board shall determine the time, form, and manner of the issuance of revenue bonds and revenue bond anticipation notes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 740, Sec. 8.)
