# EDC § 232.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 4.5. Single Gender Schools and Classes

**Citation:** EDC § 232.6

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on July 1, 2031, and as of January 1, 2032, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 373, Sec. 3. (AB 2046) Effective January 1, 2025. Inoperative July 1, 2031, by its own provisions. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4.5, commencing with Section 232.)
