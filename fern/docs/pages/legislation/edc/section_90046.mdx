# EDC § 90046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90046

---

## Text

Immediately after the adoption of a resolution by the board directing the preparation of any bonds or notes authorized under this article, the Treasurer shall prepare the requisite number of suitable bonds or notes of the denominations, and in accordance with the specifications contained in such resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 740, Sec. 19.)
