# EDC § 79200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 5. CalWORKs Recipients Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 79200

---

## Text

(a) There is in the California Community Colleges the CalWORKs Recipients Education Program.

(b) As used in this article, “CalWORKs recipient” means a recipient of aid under Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11200) of Part 3 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code or any successor program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 34, Sec. 8. (SB 860) Effective June 20, 2014.)
