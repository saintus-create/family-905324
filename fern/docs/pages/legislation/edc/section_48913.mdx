# EDC § 48913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 1. Suspension or Expulsion

**Citation:** EDC § 48913

---

## Text

The teacher of any class from which a pupil is suspended may require the suspended pupil to complete any assignments and tests missed during the suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 91. Effective July 28, 1983.)
