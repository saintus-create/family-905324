# EDC § 45258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 45258

---

## Text

Positions established for the employment of community representatives in advisory or consulting capacities shall be considered part of the classified service.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 297, Sec. 2. (AB 2261) Effective January 1, 2019.)
