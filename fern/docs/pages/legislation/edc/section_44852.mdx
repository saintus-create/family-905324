# EDC § 44852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 2. Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 44852

---

## Text

Nothing in this code shall be construed as prohibiting the employment of persons in positions requiring certification qualifications for less than a full school year in temporary schools or classes.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
