# EDC § 100010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 66. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1996 > Article: 2. School Facilities Program Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 100010

---

## Text

Two billion twelve million thirty-five thousand dollars ($2,012,035,000) of the proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the State School Building Lease-Purchase Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 2. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1, and approved in Prop. 203 on March 26, 1996.)
