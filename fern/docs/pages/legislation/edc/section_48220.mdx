# EDC § 48220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 3. Pupils Exempt

**Citation:** EDC § 48220

---

## Text

The classes of children described in this article, shall be exempted by the proper school authorities from the requirements of attendance upon a public full-time day school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
