# EDC § 81660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Management and Control of Property > Article: 3.5. Energy Management Systems

**Citation:** EDC § 81660

---

## Text

Any community college district may enter into an energy management agreement for energy management systems with any person, firm, corporation, or public agency pursuant to this article. As used in this article, “energy management systems” means solar, energy, or solar and energy management systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 286, Sec. 2.)
