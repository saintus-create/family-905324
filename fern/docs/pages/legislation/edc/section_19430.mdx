# EDC § 19430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19430

---

## Text

(a) For three-member boards, two members constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

(b) For five-member boards, three members constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 354, Sec. 5.)
