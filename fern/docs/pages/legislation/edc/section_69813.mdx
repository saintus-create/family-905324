# EDC § 69813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 15. Forfeiture of State Aid to Students

**Citation:** EDC § 69813

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “state financial aid” means any assistance given or guaranteed by the state that is predicated on attendance at an institution of higher education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1113, Sec. 40.5.)
