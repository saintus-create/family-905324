# EDC § 78050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 4. Zero-Textbook-Cost Degree Grant Program

**Citation:** EDC § 78050

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the Zero-Textbook-Cost Degree Grant Program.

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 16. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
