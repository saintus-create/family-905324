# EDC § 22138.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22138.6

---

## Text

“Full-time equivalent” means the days or hours of creditable service that a person who is employed on a part-time basis would be required to perform in a school term if he or she were employed full time in that part-time position.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 416, Sec. 6. (SB 1165) Effective January 1, 2019.)
