# EDC § 72029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Organization of District Boards

**Citation:** EDC § 72029

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district may by resolution limit campaign expenditures or contributions in elections to district offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 8, Sec. 27. Effective April 15, 1993.)
