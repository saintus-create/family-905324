# EDC § 51474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 7. State Seal of Civic Engagement

**Citation:** EDC § 51474

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged to a pupil to receive a State Seal of Civic Engagement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 604, Sec. 1. (AB 24) Effective January 1, 2018.)
