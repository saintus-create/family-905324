# EDC § 18032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Public Library Finance > Article: 4. Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18032

---

## Text

(a) Every public library that receives state funds pursuant to this chapter and that provides public access to video recordings shall, by a majority vote of the governing board, adopt a policy regarding access by minors to video recordings by January 1, 2000.

(b) Every public library that is required to adopt a policy pursuant to subdivision (a) shall make that policy available to members of the public at every library branch.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 88, Sec. 21. (AB 176) Effective January 1, 2010.)
