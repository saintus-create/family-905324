# EDC § 35231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 7. Corrupt Practices

**Citation:** EDC § 35231

---

## Text

Any contract or appointment obtained from the governing board of any school district by corrupt means is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
