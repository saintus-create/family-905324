# EDC § 35731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 3. Contents of Plans and Recommendations

**Citation:** EDC § 35731

---

## Text

In any proposal for unification, plans and recommendations may include a provision for a governing board of seven members. In the absence of such a provision, any proposed new district shall have a governing board of five members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
