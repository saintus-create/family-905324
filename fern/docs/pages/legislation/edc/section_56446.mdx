# EDC § 56446

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.45. Special Education Programs for Individuals With Exceptional Needs Between the Ages of Three and Five Years, Inclusive

**Citation:** EDC § 56446

---

## Text

Public special education funding shall not be used to purchase regular preschool services or to purchase any instructional service other than special education and services permitted by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 89, Sec. 42. Effective June 30, 1998. Operative July 1, 1998, by Sec. 62 of Ch. 89.)
