# EDC § 35267

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 8.5. Cybersecurity

**Citation:** EDC § 35267

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 498, Sec. 1. (AB 2355) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 8.5, commencing with Section 35265.)
