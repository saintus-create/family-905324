# EDC § 42833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Revolving Funds, School Districts > Article: 4. Revolving Warehouse Stock Funds

**Citation:** EDC § 42833

---

## Text

The revolving fund shall be kept by the county treasurer as a separate fund and shall be subject to such uses only as are provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
