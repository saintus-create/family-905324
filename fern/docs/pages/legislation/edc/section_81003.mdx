# EDC § 81003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Sites > Article: 1. Location

**Citation:** EDC § 81003

---

## Text

Subject to Section 66904, a community college may build campus facilities on school, local government, or other public property not owned by the community college, if the community college obtains permission from the owner of the public property to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1059, Sec. 1.)
