# EDC § 89570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 4. Investigation of Reported Improper Governmental Activities

**Citation:** EDC § 89570

## Text

This article shall be known and may be referred to as the California State University Investigation of Reported Improper Governmental Activities Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 310, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
