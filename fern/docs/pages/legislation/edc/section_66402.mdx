# EDC § 66402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Academic Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 66402

---

## Text

Any court of competent jurisdiction is hereby authorized to grant such relief as is necessary to enforce the provisions of this chapter, including the issuance of an injunction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
