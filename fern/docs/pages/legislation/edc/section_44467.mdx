# EDC § 44467

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Teacher Education Internship Act of 1967

**Citation:** EDC § 44467

---

## Text

Colleges and universities may continue the development and maintenance of internship credential programs under their own auspices seeking the cooperation of school districts in their full implementation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
