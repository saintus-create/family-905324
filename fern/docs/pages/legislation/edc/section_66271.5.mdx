# EDC § 66271.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education

**Citation:** EDC § 66271.5

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are supplemental to any provision in the Constitution or laws of the United States or laws of the State of California, relating to discrimination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1999.)
