# EDC § 56028.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 56028.3

---

## Text

“Prone restraint” means the application of a behavioral restraint on a pupil in a facedown position for any period of time and includes the procedure known as prone containment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 857, Sec. 4. (SB 483) Effective January 1, 2025.)
