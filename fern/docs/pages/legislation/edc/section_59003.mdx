# EDC § 59003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. California Schools for the Deaf > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 59003

---

## Text

The State Department of Education in relation to the California Schools for the Deaf shall:

(a) Prescribe rules for the government of the schools.

(b) Appoint the superintendents and other officers and employees.

(c) Remove for cause any officer, teacher, or employee.

(d) Fix the compensation of officers, teachers, and employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 51.5. Effective September 21, 1992.)
