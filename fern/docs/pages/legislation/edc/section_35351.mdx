# EDC § 35351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 15. Authority to Transport Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 35351

---

## Text

No public school pupil shall be assigned to or be required to attend a particular school because of the characteristics listed in Section 220.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2008.)
