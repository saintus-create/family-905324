# EDC § 8306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 13. Administrative Review

**Citation:** EDC § 8306

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to authorize an appeal process for the resolution of disputes between the department and local agencies which contract with the department pursuant to Section 8233 to provide preschool services or to furnish property, facilities, personnel, supplies, equipment and administrative services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8401 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 205. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
