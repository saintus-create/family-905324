# EDC § 22822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.2. Out-of-State Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22822

---

## Text

A request pursuant to Section 22820 to purchase out-of-state service credit may be made by a member any time prior to the effective date of a family, survivor, disability, or retirement allowance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 304, Sec. 12. (SB 634) Effective January 1, 2010.)
