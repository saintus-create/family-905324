# EDC § 87828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87828

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this code, no warrant shall be drawn in favor of any academic employee, unless the officer whose duty it is to draw the warrant is satisfied that the employee has faithfully performed all the duties prescribed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 172. Effective September 25, 1990.)
