# EDC § 26142.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26142.5

---

## Text

“System’s headquarters office” means the office building established as the permanent headquarters facility for the system, pursuant to Section 22375.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 123, Sec. 31. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2016.)
