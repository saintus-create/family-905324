# EDC § 26120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26120

---

## Text

“Employee contribution rate” means the percentage of the participant’s salary withheld by the employer as an employee contribution under the Cash Balance Benefit Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 235. Effective January 1, 1999.)
