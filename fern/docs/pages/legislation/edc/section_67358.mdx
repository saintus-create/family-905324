# EDC § 67358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.6. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of June 1992 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 67358

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of June 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 13, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 153 at the June 2, 1992, election.)
