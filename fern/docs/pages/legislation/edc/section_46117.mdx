# EDC § 46117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 2. Kindergarten and Elementary Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 46117

---

## Text

The minimum schoolday for pupils in kindergartens is 180 minutes inclusive of recesses, and no units of average daily attendance shall be credited for attendance in kindergarten classes if the minimum schoolday of such classes is less than 180 minutes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
