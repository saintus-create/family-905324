# EDC § 88100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 88100

---

## Text

The commission may by rule provide for the competition of academic employees of the governing board in promotional examinations for positions in the classified service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 191. Effective September 25, 1990.)
