# EDC § 48242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 4. Supervisors of Attendance

**Citation:** EDC § 48242

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may appoint a school district supervisor of attendance to act under the direction of the county supervisor of attendance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 829, Sec. 2. (AB 2815) Effective January 1, 2017.)
