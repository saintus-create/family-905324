# EDC § 68011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 68011

---

## Text

“Institution” means the University of California, the California State University, or a college of the California Community Colleges.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 1996.)
