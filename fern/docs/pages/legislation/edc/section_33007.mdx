# EDC § 33007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** EDC § 33007

---

## Text

The board shall meet at least six times a year at such times as it may by resolution determine; provided, that it shall meet at least once every three months.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
