# EDC § 18170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 5. Use of Library Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 18170

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district shall expend the library fund, together with the money that is added thereto by donation, in the purchase of school apparatus and books for a school library, including books for supplementary work.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 169.)
