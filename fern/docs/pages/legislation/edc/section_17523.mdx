# EDC § 17523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 8. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** EDC § 17523

---

## Text

At the time and place fixed in the resolution for the meeting of the governing board, the board shall meet and consider all plans and proposals submitted for the joint occupancy of the building to be constructed on the proposed schoolsite.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
