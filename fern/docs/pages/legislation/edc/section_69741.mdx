# EDC § 69741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 12. Public Interest Attorney Loan Repayment Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69741

---

## Text

The Public Interest Attorney Loan Repayment Program is established for licensed attorneys who practice or agree to practice in public interest areas of the law in this state. The program shall be administered by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 881, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
