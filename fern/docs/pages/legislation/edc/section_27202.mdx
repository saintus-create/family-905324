# EDC § 27202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Termination Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27202

---

## Text

A participant shall apply for a termination benefit under this part on a form prescribed by the system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 96, Sec. 24. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2020.)
