# EDC § 8532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Adult Education > Article: 3. Functions of Continuing Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8532

---

## Text

Vocational and occupational training and retraining programs for adults may be made available in high school, unified, and community college districts by mutual agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
