# EDC § 56169.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 5.5. Public Hospitals, Proprietary Hospitals and Other Residential Medical Facilities

**Citation:** EDC § 56169.7

---

## Text

If any provision of this article, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid by an appellate court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of the article, and the application of the provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1296, Sec. 5.)
