# EDC § 45108.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 1. Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 45108.7

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may apply to the State Board of Education to waive the provisions of Section 45108.5 for purposes of expanding the number and type of senior management positions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 73. Effective July 28, 1983.)
