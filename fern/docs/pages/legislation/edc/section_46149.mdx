# EDC § 46149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 3. Junior High School and High School

**Citation:** EDC § 46149

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, a school district, county office of education, or charter school that offers grades 10 and 12 shall also offer and enroll pupils in grade 11.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 65, Sec. 88. (AB 126) Effective July 9, 2026.)
