# EDC § 19421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19421

---

## Text

The governing board of the district shall be called “the Board of Library Trustees of ____ Library District” (inserting the name of the particular district).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
