# EDC § 18917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Municipal Libraries > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18917

---

## Text

The board shall appoint one of its number president, who shall serve for one year and until his successor is appointed, and in his absence shall select a president pro tem.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
