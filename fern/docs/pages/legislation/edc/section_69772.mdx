# EDC § 69772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69772

---

## Text

The commission shall annually report on the numbers of students participating in the program, total loan volume, and the postsecondary educational institutions in which participating students are enrolled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 485, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
