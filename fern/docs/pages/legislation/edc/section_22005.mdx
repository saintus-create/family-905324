# EDC § 22005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 22005

---

## Text

The right of a person to a pension, retirement allowance, return of contributions, any optional benefit, or any other right accrued or accruing to any person under this part is exempt from taxation, including any inheritance tax, whether state, county, municipal, or district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
