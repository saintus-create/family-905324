# EDC § 8312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 13. Administrative Review

**Citation:** EDC § 8312

---

## Text

The determination of the hearing examiner shall be the final administrative determination to be afforded the local contracting agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8405 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 210. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
