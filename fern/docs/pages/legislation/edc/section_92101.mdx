# EDC § 92101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Treasury > Article: 1. Withdrawal From the State Treasury

**Citation:** EDC § 92101

---

## Text

The Regents of the University of California shall submit reports to the State Public Works Board of any allocations made by the Regents of the University of California pursuant to the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
