# EDC § 68017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 68017

---

## Text

A “resident” is a student who has residence, pursuant to Article 5 (commencing with Section 68060) of this chapter in the state for more than one year immediately preceding the residence determination date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
