# EDC § 49418

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 1.5. Office of School-Based Health Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 49418

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “office” means the Office of School-Based Health Programs established within the department that is dedicated to expanding access to school-based health programs pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 44, Sec. 61. (AB 130) Effective July 9, 2021.)
