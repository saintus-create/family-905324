# EDC § 5422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 8. Cost of Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5422

---

## Text

The cost of elections, including consolidated elections, held in territory common to two or more districts shall be borne by the districts concerned in equal shares and paid from district funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
