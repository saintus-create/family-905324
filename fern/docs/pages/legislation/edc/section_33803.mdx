# EDC § 33803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Office of Civil Rights

**Citation:** EDC § 33803

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to enact future legislation to do both of the following:

(a) Establish coordinators to be employed within the office to prevent and address discrimination and bias.

(b) Enact the duties and responsibilities of the coordinators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 428, Sec. 5. (AB 715) Effective January 1, 2026.)
