# EDC § 44946

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Resignations, Dismissals, and Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 44946

---

## Text

If the employee has been suspended pending the hearing, he shall be reinstated within five days after the governing board’s decision in his favor, and shall be paid full salary by the governing board for the period of his suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
