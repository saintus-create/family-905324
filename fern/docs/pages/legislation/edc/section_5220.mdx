# EDC § 5220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Boards of Education > Article: 2. Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5220

---

## Text

Boards of education are elected in cities under the provisions of the laws governing the respective cities, except as otherwise provided in this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
