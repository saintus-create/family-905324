# EDC § 59200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diagnostic Centers > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 59200

---

## Text

There are three diagnostic centers, to be known and designated as Diagnostic Center, Northern California, Diagnostic Center, Central California, and Diagnostic Center, Southern California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 60. Effective September 21, 1992.)
