# EDC § 92202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Special Colleges > Article: 1 A. Law College Affiliated with the University of California

**Citation:** EDC § 92202

---

## Text

The college shall afford facilities for the acquisition of legal learning in all branches of the law. To this end it shall establish a curriculum of studies and shall matriculate students who reside at the University of California or elsewhere in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
