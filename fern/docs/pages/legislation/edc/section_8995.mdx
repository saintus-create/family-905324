# EDC § 8995

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. School Nutrition > Article: 1. Nutrition Education

**Citation:** EDC § 8995

---

## Text

The Legislature encourages school administrations to do the following:

(a) Offer wellness programs that include personalized instruction about healthy eating and physical activity.

(b) Ensure that the nutrition services, health services, and social services children need in order to learn are provided at the schoolsite or in cooperation with other community agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1163, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative July 1, 2004, pursuant to Section 8997.)
