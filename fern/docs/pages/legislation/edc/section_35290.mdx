# EDC § 35290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 10. School Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 35290

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district shall maintain schools and classes as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
