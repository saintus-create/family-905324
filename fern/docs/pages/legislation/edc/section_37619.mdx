# EDC § 37619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Continuous School Programs > Article: 2. Establishment and Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 37619

---

## Text

Each selected school shall be closed for all students and employees on regular school holidays specified in Article 3 (commencing with Section 37220) of Chapter 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 159, Sec. 65. Effective January 1, 2002.)
