# EDC § 32255.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Pupils’ Rights to Refrain From the Harmful or Destructive Use of Animals

**Citation:** EDC § 32255.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, this chapter applies to all levels of instruction in all public schools operating programs from kindergarten through grades 1 to 12, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 65, Sec. 2.)
