# EDC § 87457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 87457

---

## Text

Whenever a person employed in an administrative position is assigned to a faculty position, the governing board of the community college district shall give the employee, when requested by him or her, a written statement of the reasons for the transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 973, Sec. 32. Operative July 1, 1990, pursuant to Sec. 70(d) of Ch. 973.)
