# EDC § 51879.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 16. Aquatic Safety

**Citation:** EDC § 51879.9

---

## Text

Once developed, the Department of Boating and Waterways shall notify the schools and school districts of the availability of the aquatic safety program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 51882 (as added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1033) by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 33.)
