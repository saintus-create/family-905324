# EDC § 26602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. Employee and Employer Accounts

**Citation:** EDC § 26602

---

## Text

All employer contributions on behalf of the participant and interest credited under the minimum interest rate and additional earnings credits in respect of those contributions shall be treated as credits to the participant’s employer account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 592, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1996.)
