# EDC § 56865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Special Education Programs for Individuals With Exceptional Needs Residing in State Hospitals

**Citation:** EDC § 56865

---

## Text

Funds appropriated by Section 11 of Chapter 1191 of the Statutes of 1980 may be used for remodeling classrooms located in a community school, in addition to the purposes of Chapter 25 (commencing with Section 17785) of Part 10, in order to serve state hospital pupils whose individualized education programs require a community school program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1201, Sec. 66. Effective September 22, 1982.)
