# EDC § 27407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Community Property

**Citation:** EDC § 27407

---

## Text

No judgment or court order issued pursuant to this chapter is binding on the plan with respect to the Cash Balance Benefit Program until the plan has been joined as a party to the action and has been served with a certified copy of the judgment or court order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 311. Effective January 1, 1999.)
