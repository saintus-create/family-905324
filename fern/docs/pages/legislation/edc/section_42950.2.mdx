# EDC § 42950.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11.5. Financial Support for Educating Foreign Children

**Citation:** EDC § 42950.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 42238 or any other provision of law, the revenue limit increases authorized by this chapter shall not be used in the computation of any maximum tax rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 247.)
