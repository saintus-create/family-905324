# EDC § 60062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 4. Requirements, Publishers and Manufacturers

**Citation:** EDC § 60062

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 60061 shall apply to the purchase of instructional materials under Sections 18132 and 18171.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
