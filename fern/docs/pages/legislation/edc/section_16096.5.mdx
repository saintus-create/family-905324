# EDC § 16096.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 16096.5

---

## Text

From any moneys in the State School Building Aid Fund available for the purposes of this chapter, the board shall make available to the Director of General Services any amounts that it determines necessary to provide the assistance, pursuant to this chapter, required by Section 15504 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
