# EDC § 44272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 4. Credential Types

**Citation:** EDC § 44272

---

## Text

The commission may issue limited services credentials authorizing administrative service in schools which are not part of the public school system but which are administered by other state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
