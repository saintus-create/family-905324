# EDC § 44929.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 2.7. Permanent Status

**Citation:** EDC § 44929.29

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as affecting the classification of any employee as it existed on September 13, 1941.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 380.)
