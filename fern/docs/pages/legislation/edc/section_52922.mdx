# EDC § 52922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 12.5. International Baccalaureate Diploma Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52922

---

## Text

A school district that offers an International Baccalaureate Diploma Program may help pay the test fees for pupils in need of financial assistance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 538, Sec. 42. (SB 416) Effective January 1, 2016.)
