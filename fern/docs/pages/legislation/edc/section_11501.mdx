# EDC § 11501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 16. Programs to Encourage Parental Involvement

**Citation:** EDC § 11501

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this chapter to ensure that parent and family engagement programs are properly designed and implemented and to provide a focus and structure for these programs based on prior experience and research while maintaining sufficient local flexibility to design a program that best meets the needs of the local community.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 669, Sec. 2. (AB 1661) Effective January 1, 2019.)
