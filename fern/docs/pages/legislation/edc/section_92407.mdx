# EDC § 92407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 92407

---

## Text

“Regents” means the existing corporation known as “the Regents of the University of California,” as such corporation is constituted by the provisions of Section 9 of Article IX of the Constitution.
