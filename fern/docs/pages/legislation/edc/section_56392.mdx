# EDC § 56392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.1. Recognition for Educational Achievement or Completion of Program

**Citation:** EDC § 56392

---

## Text

It is not the intent of the Legislature by enacting this chapter to eliminate the opportunity for an individual with exceptional needs to earn a standard diploma issued by a local or state educational agency when the pupil has completed the prescribed course of study and has passed the proficiency requirements with or without differential standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 54. Effective September 30, 2002.)
