# EDC § 44454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Teacher Education Internship Act of 1967

**Citation:** EDC § 44454

---

## Text

An internship credential authorizes the same service at the same levels as the regular credential authorizes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 342, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2002.)
