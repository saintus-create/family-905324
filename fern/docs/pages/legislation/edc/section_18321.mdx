# EDC § 18321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 3. Establishment

**Citation:** EDC § 18321

---

## Text

The title to all property acquired for the purposes of the library, when not inconsistent with the terms of its acquisition, or not otherwise designated, vests in the district in which the library is, or is to be, situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
