# EDC § 81474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 10. Exchange of Real Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81474

---

## Text

At the time and place fixed in the published notice, the board shall meet and consider the report of the appraisers. It then may order the exchange pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in the resolution and the report of the appraisers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
