# EDC § 33310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 33310

---

## Text

The State Department of Education may sell any educational materials and directories related to its scope and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 825, Sec. 9. Effective October 9, 1997.)
