# EDC § 48305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 7. Pupil Attendance Alternatives

**Citation:** EDC § 48305

---

## Text

School districts of choice may employ existing entrance criteria for specialized schools or programs if the criteria are uniformly applied to all applicants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 21, Sec. 1. Effective March 5, 2004.)
