# EDC § 33032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 33032

---

## Text

The board shall study the educational conditions and needs of the state. It shall make plans for the improvement of the administration and efficiency of the public schools of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
