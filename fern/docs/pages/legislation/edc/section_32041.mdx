# EDC § 32041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. School Safety—Public and Private Institutions > Article: 4. First Aid Equipment

**Citation:** EDC § 32041

---

## Text

The teacher, instructor agent, or employee shall have the first aid kit in his possession, or immediately available, while conducting the field trip.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
