# EDC § 22166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22166

---

## Text

“Retirement allowance” means the amount payable to a retired member or an option beneficiary on a monthly basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 1997.)
