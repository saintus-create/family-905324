# EDC § 41543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.2. Categorical Education Block Grant Funding > Article: 6. Targeted Instructional Improvement Block Grant

**Citation:** EDC § 41543

---

## Text

In expending funds received pursuant to this article, a school district shall give first priority to funding the costs of a court-ordered desegregation program if the order exists and is still in force.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 871, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
