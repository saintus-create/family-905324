# EDC § 25115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 39. School Employer Retirement Investment Product Vendor Registration Process and Information Bank

**Citation:** EDC § 25115

---

## Text

For purposes of restricting the use of 403(b) investment products provided to eligible employees of employers by those vendors and investment products registered with the board pursuant to this chapter, the provisions of Section 770.3 of the Insurance Code do not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 230, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2009.)
