# EDC § 26121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26121

---

## Text

“Employee contribution” means the amount withheld from the participant’s salary by the employer as a contribution by the employee under the Cash Balance Benefit Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 236. Effective January 1, 1999.)
