# EDC § 12031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 4. Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 and National Defense Education Act of 1958

**Citation:** EDC § 12031

---

## Text

In accepting the benefits of these acts of Congress, the people of the state agree to comply with all of their provisions and to observe all of their requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
