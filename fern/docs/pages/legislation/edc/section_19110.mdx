# EDC § 19110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** EDC § 19110

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may contract with the board of supervisors of any other county or two or more other counties to provide for the services of a single qualified librarian to serve simultaneously as the county librarian of each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 52, Sec. 1.)
