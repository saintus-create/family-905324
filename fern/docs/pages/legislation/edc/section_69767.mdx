# EDC § 69767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69767

---

## Text

The Treasurer shall invest, pursuant to statute, any surplus money in the Federal Student Loan Reserve Fund and the Student Loan Operating Fund. The interest or other accretions as a result of the investment of this money shall be deposited in the originating fund, and may be expended for those purposes authorized in this article or the Higher Education Act of 1965.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 636, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2000. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
