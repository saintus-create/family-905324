# EDC § 60200.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Elementary School Materials > Article: 1. Selection and Adoption

**Citation:** EDC § 60200.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 60200 and 60200.1, the state board shall not adopt instructional materials or follow the procedures adopted pursuant to Sections 60200 and 60200.1 until the 2015–16 school year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 7, Sec. 32. (SB 70) Effective March 24, 2011.)
