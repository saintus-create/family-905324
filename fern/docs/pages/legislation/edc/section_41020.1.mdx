# EDC § 41020.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Financial Management and Control > Article: 2. Accounting Regulations, Budget Controls and Audits

**Citation:** EDC § 41020.1

---

## Text

Reports of audits previously filed with the county clerk pursuant to Section 41020 shall be disposed of by the county clerk or returned to the respective superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1239, Sec. 3.)
