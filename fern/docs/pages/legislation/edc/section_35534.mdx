# EDC § 35534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 4. Completion and Effective Dates for Action

**Citation:** EDC § 35534

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 35536 and 35786 and subject to compliance with Section 54900 of the Government Code, any action to reorganize a school district shall be effective for all purposes on July 1 of the calendar year following the calendar year in which the action is completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 89, Sec. 7. (AB 2081) Effective January 1, 2011.)
