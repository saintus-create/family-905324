# EDC § 8483.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 22.5. After School Education and Safety Program

**Citation:** EDC § 8483.25

---

## Text

The State Department of Education shall provide notice to all schools eligible for grants under this article of the availability of such grants as well as the process for making application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2002, by initiative Proposition 49, Sec. 9.)
