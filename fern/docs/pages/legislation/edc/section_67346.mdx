# EDC § 67346

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.4. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of June 1990 > Article: 2. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act Program

**Citation:** EDC § 67346

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the June 1990 Higher Education Capital Outlay Bond Fund, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 121 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
