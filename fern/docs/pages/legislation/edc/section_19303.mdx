# EDC § 19303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19303

---

## Text

The State Librarian shall be appointed by and hold office at the pleasure of the Governor, subject to confirmation by the Senate.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
