# EDC § 26903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Disability Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 26903

---

## Text

All creditable service subject to coverage by the Cash Balance Benefit Program and Defined Benefit Program shall be terminated prior to the disability date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 285. Effective January 1, 1999.)
