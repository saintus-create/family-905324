# EDC § 26805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Retirement Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 26805

---

## Text

The retirement benefit under this part is a benefit payable in the event of retirement that is an amount equal to the sum of the employee account and the employer account as of the retirement date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 276. Effective January 1, 1999.)
