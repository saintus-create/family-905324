# EDC § 18131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 4. Affiliation With County and City Libraries

**Citation:** EDC § 18131

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other section of this article to the contrary, school districts may purchase textbooks, reference books, periodicals, and other publications approved by any board authorized to adopt these materials in addition to those furnished by the county library.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 163.)
