# EDC § 92487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 3. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 92487

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this chapter and the interest or income from such bonds, are exempt from all taxation in this state other than gift, inheritance, and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
