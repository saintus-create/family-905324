# EDC § 41340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 3. Disbursements by Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 41340

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall furnish an abstract of each apportionment of the State School Fund to the State Controller, the Department of Finance and to the county and city and county auditors, county and city and county treasurers, and to the county superintendents of schools of the several counties of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
