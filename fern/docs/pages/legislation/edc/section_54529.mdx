# EDC § 54529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 29. PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Compensatory Education Programs for Disadvantaged Children > Article: 6. Preschool Followthrough Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 54529

---

## Text

Federal funds which may be provided for purposes of this article shall bear all the costs of the administration of the provisions of this article by the State Board of Education and the Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
