# EDC § 18901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Municipal Libraries > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** EDC § 18901

---

## Text

The request may be by a single petition, or by several petitions. The several petitions shall be substantially in the same form. The single petition, or several petitions in the aggregate, shall have, the signatures of the requisite number of electors.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
