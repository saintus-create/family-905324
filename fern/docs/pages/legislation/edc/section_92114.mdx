# EDC § 92114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Treasury > Article: 2. Transfer of Land Acquired by Tax Sales to the University

**Citation:** EDC § 92114

---

## Text

The expense of the publication and posting shall be paid by the Regents of the University of California, unless the expense is paid to the tax collector prior to the day mentioned in the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
