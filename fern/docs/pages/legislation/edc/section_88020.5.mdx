# EDC § 88020.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 1. Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 88020.5

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district shall employ persons for food service positions as a part of the classified service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 705.)
