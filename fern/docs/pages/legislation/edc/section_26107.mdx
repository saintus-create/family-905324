# EDC § 26107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26107

---

## Text

“Basis of employment” means the standard of time over which the employer expects service to be performed by an employee in the position during the school term.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 416, Sec. 26. (SB 1165) Effective January 1, 2019.)
