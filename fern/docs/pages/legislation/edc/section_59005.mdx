# EDC § 59005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. California Schools for the Deaf > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 59005

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the superintendents of the schools are such as are assigned by the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 51.7. Effective September 21, 1992.)
