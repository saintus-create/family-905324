# EDC § 87160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Provisions Applying to All Employees > Article: 6. Reporting by Community College Employees of Improper Governmental Activities

**Citation:** EDC § 87160

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be referred to as the Reporting by Community College Employees of Improper Governmental Activities Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 531, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
