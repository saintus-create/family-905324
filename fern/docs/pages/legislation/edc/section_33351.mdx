# EDC § 33351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 3. Educational Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 33351

---

## Text

The Department of Education may employ the necessary expert and clerical assistants in order to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
