# EDC § 35573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 8. Bonded Indebtedness of School Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 35573

---

## Text

When any school district is in any manner merged with one or more school districts so as to form a single district by any procedure, the district so formed is liable for all of the outstanding bonded indebtedness of the districts united or merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 2.)
