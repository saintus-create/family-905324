# EDC § 17250.69

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.6. Alternative Design-Build Contracts

**Citation:** EDC § 17250.69

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 571, Sec. 14. (AB 185) Effective September 27, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 2.6 commencing with Section 17250.60.)
