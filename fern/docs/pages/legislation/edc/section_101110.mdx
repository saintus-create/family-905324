# EDC § 101110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 70. KINDERGARTEN THROUGH COMMUNITY COLLEGE PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2016 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** EDC § 101110

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Kindergarten Through Community College Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 51, Sec. 3.)
