# EDC § 8265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 8. Local Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8265

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district or a county superintendent of schools with the approval of the county board of education is authorized to establish and maintain preschool programs upon the approval of, and subject to the regulations of the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8320 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 145. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
