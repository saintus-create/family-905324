# EDC § 89281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Education Programs > Article: 9. Doctor of Nursing Practice Degree Program

**Citation:** EDC § 89281

---

## Text

The California State University shall permit students enrolled in the Doctor of Nursing Practice Degree Pilot Program established pursuant to this article, as it read on December 31, 2017, to complete their coursework on and after January 1, 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 702, Sec. 2. (AB 422) Effective January 1, 2018.)
