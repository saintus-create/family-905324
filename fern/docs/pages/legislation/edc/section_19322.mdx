# EDC § 19322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 19322

---

## Text

The State Librarian may do both of the following:

(a) Contract directly with counties, cities, or districts within this state, agencies of the state, and agencies of the United States government for the purpose of providing library services.

(b) Establish and operate library service centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 15. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
