# EDC § 22320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22320

---

## Text

The death benefits provided pursuant to Chapter 22 (commencing with Section 23800), Chapter 23 (commencing with Section 23850), and Chapter 24 (commencing with Section 23880) shall be paid to the beneficiary or estate within 45 days of receipt by the system of all necessary information.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
