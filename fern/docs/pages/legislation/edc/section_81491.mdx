# EDC § 81491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 12. Temporary Transfer of Water Stock

**Citation:** EDC § 81491

---

## Text

The rental, lease, or assignment of the shares of stock shall be made upon such consideration, and such terms and conditions, as may be fixed by action of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
