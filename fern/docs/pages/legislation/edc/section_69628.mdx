# EDC § 69628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 7. Child Development Teacher and Supervisor Grant Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69628

---

## Text

This article shall not be implemented unless and until federal funds are made available for the purposes of implementing this article in accordance with subdivision (a) of Section 69625.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 721, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
