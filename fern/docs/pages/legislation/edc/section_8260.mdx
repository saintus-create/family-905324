# EDC § 8260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 6. Allocations and Expenditures

**Citation:** EDC § 8260

---

## Text

Each county shall, as a minimum, maintain the level of expenditure for child development services provided by the county during the 1970–71 fiscal year. These funds shall be used exclusively for child development programs and shall be considered unrestricted funds unless restricted by the county granting the funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8279 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 123. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
