# EDC § 24706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 32. San Francisco Local System

**Citation:** EDC § 24706

---

## Text

The system shall pay from the fund to the San Francisco Unified School District and the San Francisco Community College District the amounts due for subventions required prior to July 1, 1972, on account of persons who retired or died prior to that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 281. Effective January 1, 1997.)
