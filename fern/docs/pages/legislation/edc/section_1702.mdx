# EDC § 1702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 1702

---

## Text

The county school service fund shall be used to pay for those services provided by the county superintendent of schools that are authorized by this chapter, and for such other purposes as are specifically authorized in Section 1500.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
