# EDC § 66010.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 2.5. State Goals For California’s Postsecondary Education System

**Citation:** EDC § 66010.95

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “segments of postsecondary education” means the California Community Colleges, the California State University, the University of California, independent institutions of higher education, as defined in Section 66010, and private postsecondary educational institutions, as defined in Section 94858.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 367, Sec. 1. (SB 195) Effective January 1, 2014.)
