# EDC § 81374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 4. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81374

---

## Text

The final acceptance by the governing body may be made either at the same session or at any adjourned session of the same meeting held within the 10 days next following.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
