# EDC § 12501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Interstate Agreements > Article: 1. Interstate Agreement on Qualification of Educational Personnel

**Citation:** EDC § 12501

---

## Text

The “designated state official” for this state shall be the Superintendent. The Superintendent shall enter into contracts pursuant to Article III of the agreement only with the approval of the specific text of the contract by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 3. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
