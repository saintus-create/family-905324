# EDC § 17554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 14. Sale of Personal Property

**Citation:** EDC § 17554

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the governing board of any school district owning land upon which agricultural products are grown may enter into agreements with an agricultural cooperative or association for the purpose of maintaining, harvesting or selling the products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
