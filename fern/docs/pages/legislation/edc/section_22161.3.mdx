# EDC § 22161.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22161.3

---

## Text

Qualified military service is military service as defined in Section 414(u) of Title 26 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 703, Sec. 1. (SB 349) Effective January 1, 2012.)
