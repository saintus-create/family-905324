# EDC § 19980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. California Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19980

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this chapter are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 96. Effective January 1, 2007. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 49, and approved in Prop. 85 on Nov. 8, 1988.)
