# EDC § 51015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 51015

---

## Text

“Course” means an instructional unit of an area or field of organized knowledge, usually provided on a semester, year, or prescribed length-of-time basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
