# EDC § 27205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Termination Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27205

---

## Text

A participant may cancel the application for a termination benefit under this part at any time prior to distribution of the benefit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 302. Effective January 1, 1999.)
