# EDC § 66080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 8. English Proficiency in Higher Education

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the “English Proficiency in Higher Education Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 200, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
