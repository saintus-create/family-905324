# EDC § 51040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 3. Role of Local Agency

**Citation:** EDC § 51040

---

## Text

The governing board of every school district shall prepare and shall keep on file for public inspection the courses of study prescribed for the schools under its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
