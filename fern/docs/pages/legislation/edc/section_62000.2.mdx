# EDC § 62000.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 34. EVALUATION AND SUNSETTING OF PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 62000.2

---

## Text

The following programs shall sunset on June 30, 1987:

(a) Miller-Unruh Basic Reading Act of 1965.

(b) School improvement program.

(c) Bilingual education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 79, Sec. 20. Effective July 19, 2006.)
