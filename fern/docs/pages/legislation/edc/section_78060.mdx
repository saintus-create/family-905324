# EDC § 78060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 5. California Community College Teacher Credentialing Partnership Pilot Act

## Text

This section shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Community College Teacher Credentialing Partnership Pilot Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 603, Sec. 3. (SB 577) Effective January 1, 2019.)
