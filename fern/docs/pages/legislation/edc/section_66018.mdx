# EDC § 66018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 66018

---

## Text

Each institution of public higher education shall require that all applications for any type of financial aid for students shall disclose all taxable income and all nontaxable income.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
