# EDC § 35732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 3. Contents of Plans and Recommendations

**Citation:** EDC § 35732

---

## Text

Plans and recommendations may include a provision specifying the territory in which the election to reorganize the school districts will be held. In the absence of such a provision, the election shall be held only in the territory proposed for reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
