# EDC § 18410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 7. Powers of Boards of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18410

---

## Text

All public libraries established under this chapter shall be open for the use of the public at all reasonable times.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
