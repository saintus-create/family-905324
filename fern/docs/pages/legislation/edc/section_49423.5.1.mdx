# EDC § 49423.5.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 2. Employment of Medical Personnel

**Citation:** EDC § 49423.5.1

---

## Text

On or before June 15, 2001, the State Department of Education shall review and make recommendations to the State Board of Education regarding any needed updates to the regulations adopted pursuant to subdivision (e) of Section 49423.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 281, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
