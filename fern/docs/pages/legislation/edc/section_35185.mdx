# EDC § 35185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 4.7. Miscellaneous Administrative Authority

**Citation:** EDC § 35185

---

## Text

A school district may require proof of registration pursuant to Article 8.2 (commencing with Section 12620) of Chapter 6 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, as a condition to agreement to enroll that student.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 825, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
