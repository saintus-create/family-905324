# EDC § 56864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Special Education Programs for Individuals With Exceptional Needs Residing in State Hospitals

**Citation:** EDC § 56864

---

## Text

Individuals with exceptional needs residing in state hospitals shall not be included within the funding calculation made pursuant to Chapter 7.2 (commencing with Section 56836).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 89, Sec. 59. Effective June 30, 1998. Operative July 1, 1998, by Sec. 62 of Ch. 89.)
