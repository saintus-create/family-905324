# EDC § 33311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 33311

---

## Text

When the Department of Education fixes the price of any publication, it shall specify the class of persons or institutions that may receive copies of the publication free of charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
