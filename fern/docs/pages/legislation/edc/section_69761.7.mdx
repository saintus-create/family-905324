# EDC § 69761.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69761.7

---

## Text

To promote responsible and timely repayment of guaranteed student loans, the commission shall establish a process to ensure regular, annual credit reporting on the repayment status of borrowers whose loans are guaranteed by the commission and whose paper has matured.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 485, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
