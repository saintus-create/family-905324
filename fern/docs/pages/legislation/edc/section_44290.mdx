# EDC § 44290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 5. Subject Matter Examinations and Assessments

**Citation:** EDC § 44290

---

## Text

The commission, before the adoption of any examination, shall provide an opportunity to the public and teaching profession to present their viewpoints with respect thereto in open hearings.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
