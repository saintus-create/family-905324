# EDC § 19334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 3. Books

**Citation:** EDC § 19334

---

## Text

Every person who injures or fails to return any book taken is liable in three times its value.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
