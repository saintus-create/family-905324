# EDC § 15101.75

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Bonds of School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 1. Purposes for Authorizing Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 15101.75

---

## Text

(a) This chapter shall apply to bond elections for and the issuance of bonds for school facilities improvement districts created pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 15300) to the extent that this chapter does not conflict with Chapter 2. In the event of a conflict, the provisions of Chapter 2 shall supersede the provisions of this chapter, but only to the extent of the conflict.

(b) A bond adopted by the voters pursuant to this part prior to January 1, 2008, shall be governed by this part as it read on December 31, 2007.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 670, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
