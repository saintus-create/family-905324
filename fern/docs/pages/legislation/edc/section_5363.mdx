# EDC § 5363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 4. Election Notice

**Citation:** EDC § 5363

---

## Text

Except where the procedure prescribed by Section 5362 is utilized, the elections official responsible for the conduct of a governing board member election shall cause formal notice of the election to be published pursuant to Section 12112 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1081, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
