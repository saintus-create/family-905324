# EDC § 33331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 2. Finances

**Citation:** EDC § 33331

---

## Text

The Department of Education may expend the moneys in any appropriation heretofore or hereafter made for the support of the State Board of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
