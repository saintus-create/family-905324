# EDC § 53005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 15. Comprehensive Reading Leadership Program

**Citation:** EDC § 53005

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall designate the school districts and county offices of education that will be served by each of the reading leadership training program providers selected pursuant to Section 53003.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 196, Sec. 2. Effective July 22, 1996.)
