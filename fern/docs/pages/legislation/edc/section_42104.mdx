# EDC § 42104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Statements of School Districts > Article: 1. Statements

**Citation:** EDC § 42104

---

## Text

Any violation of this article or a failure to comply with its provisions by the county superintendent of schools or by the governing board of any school district is punishable under Section 1222 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
