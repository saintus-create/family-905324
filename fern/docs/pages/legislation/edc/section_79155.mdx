# EDC § 79155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 4. Cooperative Agencies Resources for Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 79155

---

## Text

For the 1983–84 fiscal year and each fiscal year thereafter, this article shall be operative only if funds are specifically appropriated for the purposes of the cooperative agencies resources for education programs pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 532.)
