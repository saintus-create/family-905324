# EDC § 33353.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 3. Educational Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 33353.5

---

## Text

The California Interscholastic Federation shall not transact insurance as defined in Section 35 of the Insurance Code, nor shall the federation endorse any insurance programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 617, Sec. 3.)
