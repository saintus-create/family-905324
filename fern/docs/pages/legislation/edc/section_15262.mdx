# EDC § 15262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Bonds of School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 10. Tax for Payment of Bonds of School District or Community College District Located in Two or More Counties

**Citation:** EDC § 15262

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to bonds of a school district which were approved by the electors prior to July 1, 1978, and to bonded indebtedness for the acquisition or improvement of real property approved by two-thirds of the voters on or after June 4, 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
