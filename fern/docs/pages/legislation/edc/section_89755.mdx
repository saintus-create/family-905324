# EDC § 89755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Finance > Article: 4. Expenditures and Control

**Citation:** EDC § 89755

---

## Text

All actions of the trustees taken pursuant to Section 89753 are limited to the fiscal year in which the action is taken and subject to audit by the Department of Finance, as well as legislative review, prior to extension into the subsequent fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
