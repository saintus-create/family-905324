# EDC § 17360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Construction of School Buildings > Article: 5. Factory-Built School Buildings

**Citation:** EDC § 17360

---

## Text

Sections 17297, 17302, 17307, 17309, and 17311 shall not apply with respect to the design and construction of onsite work except where required by Section 17358.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 2005.)
