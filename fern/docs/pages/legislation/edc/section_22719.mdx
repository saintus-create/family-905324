# EDC § 22719

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22719

---

## Text

If the allowance of a retired member is terminated, the employer shall not restore sick leave days for which service credit was granted at retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1006, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
