# EDC § 66100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 66100

---

## Text

A fund in the State Treasury is hereby created, to be known as the Capital Outlay Fund for Public Higher Education. All money in the Capital Outlay Fund for Public Higher Education, including any money deposited in said fund from any source whatsoever after the effective date of this chapter, shall be available, when appropriated by the Legislature, for expenditure for capital outlay purposes relating to public higher education including, but not limited to, acquisition of sites and construction of new institutions of public higher education thereon.
