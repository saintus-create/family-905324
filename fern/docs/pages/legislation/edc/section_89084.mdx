# EDC § 89084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Academic Year Operations

**Citation:** EDC § 89084

---

## Text

Any campus of the California State University which operated on a year-round basis on November 23, 1970, shall continue to operate on that basis.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 143, Sec. 112.)
