# EDC § 60605.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. California Assessment of Academic Achievement > Article: 2. Program Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 60605.10

---

## Text

The Superintendent may recommend and the state board may adopt the college and career readiness anchor standards developed by the Common Core State Standards Initiative consortium. The state board may also take action to resolve any technical issues in the English language arts standards adopted pursuant to Section 60605.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 654, Sec. 1. (SB 1200) Effective January 1, 2013.)
