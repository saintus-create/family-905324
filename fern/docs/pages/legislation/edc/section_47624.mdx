# EDC § 47624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. University Charter Schools > Article: 1. University of California at Los Angeles Elementary Charter School

**Citation:** EDC § 47624

---

## Text

If a charter is granted under this chapter, the University of California shall continue to own and be liable for the resulting charter school to the same extent as before the granting of the charter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 118, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
