# EDC § 90071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90071

---

## Text

The board may also include in an indenture a covenant that no project acquired, constructed or completed from the proceeds of revenue bonds issued under the provisions of this article shall be used without charge therefor or any facilities thereof be furnished free of charge to any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 740, Sec. 30.)
