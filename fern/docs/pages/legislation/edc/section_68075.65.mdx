# EDC § 68075.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 6. Exceptions to Residence Determination

**Citation:** EDC § 68075.65

---

## Text

Each community college shall post on its Internet Web site a notice that sets forth which persons are exempt from paying nonresident tuition pursuant to Section 68075.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 582, Sec. 1. (AB 2210) Effective January 1, 2019.)
