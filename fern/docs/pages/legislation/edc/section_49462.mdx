# EDC § 49462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 4.5. Pupil Health Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 49462

---

## Text

The health assessment shall be conducted over a four-year period and shall be completed on or before December 31, 2002.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 430, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
