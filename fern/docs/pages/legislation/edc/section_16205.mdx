# EDC § 16205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 3. School Housing Aid for Exceptional Children

**Citation:** EDC § 16205

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors fails to make a district tax levy required under Section 16204, the auditor shall make the levy.

In the event the governing board of a school district has elected to provide from the district general fund the amounts required to be paid on behalf of the district from the county school building aid fund under Section 16207, the county superintendent of schools shall order the payment to be made in the amount required from the general fund of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
