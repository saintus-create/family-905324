# EDC § 27412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Community Property

**Citation:** EDC § 27412

---

## Text

The system shall include the contribution and earnings credits awarded to a nonparticipant spouse in the judgment or court order to determine the eligibility of a participant for an annuity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 608, Sec. 55. Effective September 19, 1996. Applicable from July 1, 1996, pursuant to Sec. 58 of Ch. 608.)
