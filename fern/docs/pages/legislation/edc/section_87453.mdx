# EDC § 87453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 87453

---

## Text

No regular employee of a community college district shall be dismissed without his or her consent or deprived of his or her classification as a regular employee of the district when the district does not have sufficient funds to pay his salary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 147. Effective January 1, 1996.)
