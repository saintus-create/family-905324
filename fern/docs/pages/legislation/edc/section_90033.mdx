# EDC § 90033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90033

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause designating the rights, limitations, powers and duties arising upon breach by the board of any of the covenants, conditions, or obligations contained in any indenture.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
