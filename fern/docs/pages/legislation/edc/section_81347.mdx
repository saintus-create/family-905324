# EDC § 81347

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 3. Leasing School Buildings

**Citation:** EDC § 81347

---

## Text

All such bonds, notes, warrants or other evidences of indebtedness referred to in Section 81346 and the interest thereon are exempt from all taxation in the state other than inheritance, gift and franchise taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
