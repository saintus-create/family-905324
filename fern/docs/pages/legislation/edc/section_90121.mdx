# EDC § 90121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. California Maritime Academy > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 90121

---

## Text

The president of the academy is also the commander of the academy and has, subject to the regulations of the trustees, the direct control, supervision, and management of the academy, and any other powers and duties as provided by regulations of the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
