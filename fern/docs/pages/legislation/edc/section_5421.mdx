# EDC § 5421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 8. Cost of Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5421

---

## Text

The cost of any election held within a single district shall be borne by the entire district, and shall be paid out of its funds. Election costs shall be determined by the county elections official and approved by the county board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
