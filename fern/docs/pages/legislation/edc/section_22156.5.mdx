# EDC § 22156.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22156.5

---

## Text

“Prior year’s compensation earnable” means the compensation earnable for the most recent school year in which the member earned service credit that precedes the last school year in which the member earned service credit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 939, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2000.)
