# EDC § 14225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 9. FINANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Local Taxation by School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 2. Taxes on School Districts Located in Two or More Counties

**Citation:** EDC § 14225

---

## Text

The money received shall be deposited in the county treasury of the county whose superintendent of schools has jurisdiction over the school district or community college district, and such county treasury shall be the legal depository of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
