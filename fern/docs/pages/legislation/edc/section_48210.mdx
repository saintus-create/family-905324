# EDC § 48210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 2. Persons Excluded

**Citation:** EDC § 48210

---

## Text

Children under six years of age are excluded from the public schools, except as provided in this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
