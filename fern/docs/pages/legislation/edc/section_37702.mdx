# EDC § 37702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Four-Day School Week

**Citation:** EDC § 37702

---

## Text

Prior to operating a school on a four-day school week, the school district shall secure the approval of the governing board of the district and of any collective bargaining agents representing employees of the school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 465, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
