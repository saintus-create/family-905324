# EDC § 22720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22720

---

## Text

The service credited pursuant to Section 22717 may not be used in the determination of final compensation, except as provided in Section 22134.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 911, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
