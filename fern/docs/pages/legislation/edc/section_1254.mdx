# EDC § 1254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 2. Duties, Responsibilities, and General Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 1254

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools of the county in which the greatest part of the assessed valuation of any unified school district lies has jurisdiction over the unified school district for the purposes specified in this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
