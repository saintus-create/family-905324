# EDC § 56474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Career and Vocational Education Programs, Transition Services, and Project Workability > Article: 3. Project Workability

## Text
