# EDC § 25003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 38. Defined Benefit Supplement Program > Article: 2. Program Accounts

**Citation:** EDC § 25003

---

## Text

The board may transfer amounts between the Gain and Loss Reserve and the Annuitant Reserve upon the recommendation of the actuary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 2001.)
