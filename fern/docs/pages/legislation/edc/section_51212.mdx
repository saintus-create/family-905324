# EDC § 51212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 2. Course of Study, Grades 1 to 6

**Citation:** EDC § 51212

---

## Text

It is the intent and purpose of the Legislature to encourage the establishment of programs of instruction in world language, with instruction beginning as early as feasible for each school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 865, Sec. 15. (AB 2319) Effective January 1, 2019.)
