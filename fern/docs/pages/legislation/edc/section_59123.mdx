# EDC § 59123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California School for the Blind > Article: 3. Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 59123

---

## Text

All pupils in the school shall be maintained at the expense of the state, except as provided in Sections 59121, 59124 to 59128, inclusive, and 59131.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
