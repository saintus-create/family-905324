# EDC § 26114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26114

---

## Text

“Death benefit” means the benefit payable under this part upon the death of the participant.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1999.)
