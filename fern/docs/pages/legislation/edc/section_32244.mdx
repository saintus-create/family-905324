# EDC § 32244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Safety—Public Institutions > Article: 4. Lead-Safe Schools Protection Act

**Citation:** EDC § 32244

---

## Text

Lead-based paint, lead plumbing and solders, or other potential sources of lead contamination shall not be utilized in the construction of any new school facility or the modernization or renovation of any existing school facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 589, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1994.)
