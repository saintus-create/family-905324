# EDC § 240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 7. Instructional Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 240

---

## Text

Governing boards of school districts shall adopt instructional materials in accordance with the provisions of Section 60040.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1117, Sec. 1.)
