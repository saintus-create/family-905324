# EDC § 79510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 10. California Community Colleges Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander Student Achievement Program

**Citation:** EDC § 79510

---

## Text

As used in this article, unless the context requires otherwise, the following definitions apply:

(a) “AANHPI” means Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander.

(b) “Community college” means a college of the California Community College system.

(c) “Program” means the AANHPI Student Achievement Program established pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 54, Sec. 34. (AB 183) Effective June 30, 2022.)
