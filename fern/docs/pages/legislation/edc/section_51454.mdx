# EDC § 51454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 5. Golden State Seal Merit Diploma

**Citation:** EDC § 51454

---

## Text

Each school district that confers high school diplomas shall maintain appropriate records in order to identify students who have earned a Golden State Seal Merit Diploma, and shall affix the appropriate insignia to the diploma and transcript of each student that earns a Golden State Seal Merit Diploma.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 204, Sec. 13. Effective July 22, 1996.)
