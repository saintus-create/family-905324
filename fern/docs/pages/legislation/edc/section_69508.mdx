# EDC § 69508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 1.5. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 69508

---

## Text

When federal regulations defining the financial independence of students are altered, the Student Aid Commission shall as soon as possible report to the Legislature on what changes are necessary to conform this article to federal standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1214.)
