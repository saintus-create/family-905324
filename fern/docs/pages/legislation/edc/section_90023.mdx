# EDC § 90023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90023

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause relating to the bonds issued thereunder requiring the board to make all necessary repairs, renewals and replacements to any project, and to keep the project at all times in good repair, working order and condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
