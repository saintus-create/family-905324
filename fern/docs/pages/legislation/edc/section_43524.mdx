# EDC § 43524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24.6. Instruction Support Grants > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. In-Person Instruction and Expanded Learning Opportunities Grants

**Citation:** EDC § 43524

---

## Text

The requirements of this chapter shall not be waived by the state board pursuant to Section 33050 or any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 10, Sec. 2. (AB 86) Effective March 5, 2021. Inoperative June 30, 2026, pursuant to Section 43525. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 43525.)
