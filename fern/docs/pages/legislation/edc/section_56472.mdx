# EDC § 56472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Career and Vocational Education Programs, Transition Services, and Project Workability > Article: 3. Project Workability

**Citation:** EDC § 56472

---

## Text

The population served by workability projects may include secondary students with disabilities, adults with disabilities and other individuals who experience barriers to successful completion of school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1484, Sec. 9.)
