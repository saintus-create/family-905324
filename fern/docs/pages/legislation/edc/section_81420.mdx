# EDC § 81420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 6. Joint Use, School Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81420

---

## Text

A community college district may enter into a lease or agreement with a city, county, or city and county for the joint occupancy, or a private education institution for its sole occupancy, of the real property and buildings of the district, in accordance with the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 293, Sec. 2. Effective July 29, 1985.)
