# EDC § 49405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 1. General Powers—School Boards

**Citation:** EDC § 49405

---

## Text

The control of smallpox is under the direction of the State Department of Health Services, and no rule or regulation on the subject of vaccination shall be adopted by school or local health authorities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 93.)
