# EDC § 221.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education Act

**Citation:** EDC § 221.6

---

## Text

On or before July 1, 2006, the department shall post on its Internet Web site, in both English and Spanish and at a reading level that may be comprehended by pupils in high school, the information set forth in the federal regulations implementing Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (20 U.S.C. Sec. 1681 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 52. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
