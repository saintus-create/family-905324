# EDC § 48984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 4. Notification of Parent or Guardian

**Citation:** EDC § 48984

---

## Text

No school district shall undertake any activity covered by the sections set forth in Section 48980 with respect to any particular pupil unless the parent or guardian has been informed of such action pursuant to this article or has received separate special notification.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
