# EDC § 89557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 2.5. Separation of Employees From Service for Lack of Funds or Lack of Work

**Citation:** EDC § 89557

---

## Text

The trustees shall adopt appropriate rules and guidelines relating to the implementation of the Reduced Worktime Act in Article 1.6 (commencing with Section 19996.20) of Chapter 7 of Part 2.6 of Division 5 of Title 2 of the Government Code, for employees of the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1040, Sec. 11.)
