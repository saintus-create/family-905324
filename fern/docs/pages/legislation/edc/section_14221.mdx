# EDC § 14221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 9. FINANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Local Taxation by School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 2. Taxes on School Districts Located in Two or More Counties

**Citation:** EDC § 14221

---

## Text

The certificate of the assessor shall be made in the same manner and at the same time as is required for school districts or community college districts located wholly within the boundaries of one county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
