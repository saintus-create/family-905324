# EDC § 79152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 4. Cooperative Agencies Resources for Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 79152

---

## Text

Participants in cooperative agencies resources for education programs shall be at least 18 years of age, be a single head-of-household, be receiving Aid to Families with Dependent Children, and be desirous of completing their high school education or pursuing a job-relevant curriculum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1029, Sec. 2.)
