# EDC § 78080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 7. Hire UP Program

**Citation:** EDC § 78080

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Hire UP Pilot Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 54, Sec. 27. (AB 183) Effective June 30, 2022. Inoperative March 15, 2029, pursuant to Section 78084. Repealed January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 78084.)
