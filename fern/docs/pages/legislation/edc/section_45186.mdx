# EDC § 45186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Differential Compensation

**Citation:** EDC § 45186

---

## Text

This article shall apply to school districts that have adopted the merit system in the same manner and effect as if it were a part of Article 6 (commencing with Section 45240) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
