# EDC § 81401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 5. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** EDC § 81401

---

## Text

Any building constructed for the use of a community college district pursuant to this article is subject to Sections 81130 to 81144, inclusive, and all other provisions of this code relating to the physical structure of school buildings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 117. Effective January 1, 1996.)
