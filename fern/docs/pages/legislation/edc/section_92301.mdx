# EDC § 92301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 92301

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to permit the regents to expend state funds appropriated for support of the University of California for the direct operating costs of campus child development centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
