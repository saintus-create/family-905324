# EDC § 52509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 52509

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district maintaining an adult school shall prescribe the requirements for the granting of diplomas.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
