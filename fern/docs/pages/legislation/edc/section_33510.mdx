# EDC § 33510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 2. Educational Innovation and Planning Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33510

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the commission shall have the powers, duties, and responsibilities of a state advisory council prescribed in Title IV.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1354, Sec. 13. Effective September 30, 1980.)
