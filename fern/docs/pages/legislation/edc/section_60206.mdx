# EDC § 60206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Elementary School Materials > Article: 1. Selection and Adoption

**Citation:** EDC § 60206

---

## Text

The state board may adopt appropriate regulations to implement this chapter. These regulations may include a procedure to review district invoices for instructional materials purchases made pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 60242.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1503, Sec. 1.5.)
