# EDC § 69512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 2. The Student Aid Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 69512

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall annually select a chairperson from the members of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1379, Sec. 1. Effective October 1, 1985.)
