# EDC § 45039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 5. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 45039

---

## Text

If the governing board of a school district or charter school arranges to pay persons employed by it in 12 equal payments for the year, it may pay each monthly installment at the end of each calendar month, whether or not the persons are actually engaged in teaching during the month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 524, Sec. 2. Effective October 12, 2007.)
