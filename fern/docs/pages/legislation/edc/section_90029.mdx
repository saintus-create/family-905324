# EDC § 90029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90029

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause defining the power of the board in applying the proceeds of the sale of any issue of bonds for the acquiring, constructing, or completing of any project or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
