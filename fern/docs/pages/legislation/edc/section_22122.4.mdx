# EDC § 22122.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22122.4

---

## Text

“Death payment” means the amount payable upon the death of a member pursuant to Section 23801, 23851, or 23880.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
