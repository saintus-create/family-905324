# EDC § 33362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 4. Workshops

**Citation:** EDC § 33362

---

## Text

Any member or member-elect of a school district governing board who attends and successfully completes a workshop as authorized by this article may be reimbursed for expenses as provided for in Section 35173.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
