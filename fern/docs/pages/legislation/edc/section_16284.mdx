# EDC § 16284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 7.5. Regional Occupational Center of Kern

## Text

On or before the first day of January of each fiscal year the Controller shall determine the annual repayment due.
