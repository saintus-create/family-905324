# EDC § 24252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 27.5. Replacement Benefits Program

**Citation:** EDC § 24252

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to a member subject to the California Public Employees’ Pension Reform Act of 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 559, Sec. 31. (AB 1381) Effective January 1, 2014. Deemed operative January 1, 2013, by Sec. 1 of Ch. 559.)
