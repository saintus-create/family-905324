# EDC § 59210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diagnostic Centers > Article: 2. Teaching Force

**Citation:** EDC § 59210

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the directors of the diagnostic centers are such as are assigned by the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 64. Effective September 30, 2002.)
