# EDC § 37200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. School Calendar > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** EDC § 37200

---

## Text

The school year begins on the first day of July and ends on the last day of June.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
