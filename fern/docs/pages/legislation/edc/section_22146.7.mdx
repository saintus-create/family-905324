# EDC § 22146.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22146.7

---

## Text

“Minimum interest rate” means the annual interest rate determined by the board by plan amendment at which interest shall be credited to Defined Benefit Supplement accounts for a plan year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2001. See identical section added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1021.)
