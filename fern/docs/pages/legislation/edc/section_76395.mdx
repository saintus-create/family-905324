# EDC § 76395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 47. STUDENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fees > Article: 2. Authorized Fees

**Citation:** EDC § 76395

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district may impose a fee on a participating student for the additional expenses incurred when physical education courses are required to use nondistrict facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 8, Sec. 34. Effective April 15, 1993.)
