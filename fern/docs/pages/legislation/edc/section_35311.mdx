# EDC § 35311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 12. Scholarship and Loan Funds

**Citation:** EDC § 35311

---

## Text

The superintendent of school district shall be the chairman of the committee and shall be the chief executive officer of the fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
