# EDC § 78218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Courses of Study > Article: 1. Student Matriculation

**Citation:** EDC § 78218

---

## Text

In the 2012–13 fiscal year and each fiscal year thereafter, this article shall be operative only if funds are specifically appropriated for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 624, Sec. 12. (SB 1456) Effective January 1, 2013. Note: Operational conditions apply to Article 1, commencing with Section 78210.)
