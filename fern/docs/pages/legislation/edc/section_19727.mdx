# EDC § 19727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Library Districts and Museums in Unincorporated Towns and Villages > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 19727

---

## Text

The total amount of bonds issued shall not exceed 5 percent of the taxable property of the district, as shown by the last equalized assessment book of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
