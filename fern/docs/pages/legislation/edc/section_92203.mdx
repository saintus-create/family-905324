# EDC § 92203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Special Colleges > Article: 1 A. Law College Affiliated with the University of California

**Citation:** EDC § 92203

---

## Text

The faculty of the University of California shall grant, and the president shall sign and issue, diplomas to the students of the college.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
