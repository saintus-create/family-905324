# EDC § 8484.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 22.5. After School Education and Safety Program

**Citation:** EDC § 8484.65

---

## Text

If a program is operated at a schoolsite located in an area that has a population density of less than 11 persons per square mile, additional funding may be provided for transportation pursuant to Section 8483.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 370, Sec. 20. (SB 1221) Effective January 1, 2015.)
