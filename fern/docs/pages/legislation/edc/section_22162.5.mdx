# EDC § 22162.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22162.5

---

## Text

“Regular meeting” means a meeting of the board held in accordance with a schedule of meetings that states the dates and places of the meetings and that is adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 1997.)
