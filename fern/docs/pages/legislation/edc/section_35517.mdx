# EDC § 35517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 35517

---

## Text

“Uninhabited territory” means territory in which fewer than 12 persons are registered to vote at least 54 days before the time of filing of a petition or adoption of a resolution for a school district boundary change.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 344, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
