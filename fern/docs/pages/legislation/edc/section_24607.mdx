# EDC § 24607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 31. Procedures Concerning Payment

**Citation:** EDC § 24607

---

## Text

Any warrant in an amount less than two thousand dollars ($2,000) paid by the system under this part, for the month in which a retired member or disabled member dies, shall not be invalidated by the system, except upon the request of the beneficiary of the retired member or disabled member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 209. Effective January 1, 1999.)
