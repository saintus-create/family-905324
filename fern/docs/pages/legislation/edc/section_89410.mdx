# EDC § 89410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers > Article: 2. San Francisco State University

**Citation:** EDC § 89410

---

## Text

The San Francisco State University may maintain a child care center as an integral part of the state university. The center shall be used for observation, demonstration, study, student experience, and student teaching.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 106, Sec. 24.)
