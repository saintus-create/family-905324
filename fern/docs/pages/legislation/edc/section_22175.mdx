# EDC § 22175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22175

---

## Text

“System’s headquarters office” means the office building established as the permanent headquarters facility for the system, pursuant to Section 22375.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 558, Sec. 5. (AB 1379) Effective January 1, 2014.)
