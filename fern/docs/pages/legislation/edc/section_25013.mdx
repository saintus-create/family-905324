# EDC § 25013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 38. Defined Benefit Supplement Program > Article: 4. Annuities

**Citation:** EDC § 25013

---

## Text

Upon election by the member to receive a benefit payable under the Defined Benefit Supplement Program in the form of an annuity, the balance of credits in the member’s Defined Benefit Supplement account shall be transferred to the Annuitant Reserve.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 2001.)
