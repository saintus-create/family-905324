# EDC § 90031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90031

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause requiring, specifying or limiting the kind, amount and character of insurance to be maintained by the board on any project, or any part thereof, and the use and disposition of the proceeds of any such insurance thereafter collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
