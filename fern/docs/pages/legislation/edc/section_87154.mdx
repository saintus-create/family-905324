# EDC § 87154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Provisions Applying to All Employees > Article: 5. Community College Faculty and Staff Development Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 87154

---

## Text

This article shall be operative during any fiscal year only if funds are provided therefor in the annual Budget Act for that fiscal year or other legislation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 973, Sec. 26.)
