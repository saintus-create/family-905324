# EDC § 23010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23010

---

## Text

A person or entity that reports directly to the system that is assessed penalties or interest pursuant to Section 23003, 23006, or 23008 may appeal the assessed penalties or interest subject to the appeals process established pursuant to Section 22219.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 249, Sec. 8. (AB 654) Effective January 1, 2010.)
