# EDC § 35291.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 10. School Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 35291.7

---

## Text

Additional employees may not be hired and substitute teachers may not be utilized in order to comply with the requirements of Sections 35291 and 35291.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 87, Sec. 2.5.)
