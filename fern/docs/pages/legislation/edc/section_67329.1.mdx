# EDC § 67329.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.28. California Student Housing Revolving Loan Fund Act of 2022 > Article: 1. Title

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Student Housing Revolving Loan Fund Act of 2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. (AB 190) Effective September 27, 2022.)
