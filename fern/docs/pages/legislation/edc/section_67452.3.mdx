# EDC § 67452.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40.3. STUDENT ATHLETE BILL OF RIGHTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 67452.3

---

## Text

An institution of higher education may establish a degree completion fund, in accordance with applicable rules and bylaws of the governing body of the institution and applicable rules and bylaws of any athletic association of which the institution is a member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 382, Sec. 2. (AB 1573) Effective January 1, 2020.)
