# EDC § 41935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 14. Allowances for Project Connected Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 41935

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, pupils in grades 7 and 8 attending a junior high school maintained by a high school district shall be deemed to be in attendance in the schools of the elementary district of residence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
