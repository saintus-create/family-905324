# EDC § 89404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 89404

---

## Text

Each child development center maintained pursuant to Section 89400 shall have an advisory council, composed of representatives of the parent-users and persons from fields related to the well-being of children.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
