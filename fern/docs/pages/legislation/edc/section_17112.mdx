# EDC § 17112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. School District Revenue Bonds > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 17112

---

## Text

No revenue bonds may be issued for sale by the governing board unless the facilities are to be constructed on district-owned property, except as to facilities to be acquired by purchase.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
