# EDC § 79227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 7. Support Services for Foster Youth Attending Community College

**Citation:** EDC § 79227

---

## Text

This article shall be operative in a fiscal year only if funds have been appropriated for purposes of this article for that fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 771, Sec. 2. (SB 1023) Effective January 1, 2015.)
