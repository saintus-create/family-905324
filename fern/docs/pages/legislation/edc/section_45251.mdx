# EDC § 45251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 45251

---

## Text

In a unified school district with an average daily attendance in excess of 250,000, the governing board may authorize payment to members of the commission an amount not to exceed one hundred dollars ($100) per meeting, and not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500) per month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 920, Sec. 10. (SB 913) Effective January 1, 2023.)
