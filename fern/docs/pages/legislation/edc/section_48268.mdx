# EDC § 48268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 5. Truants

**Citation:** EDC § 48268

---

## Text

The court may render judgment that the parent, guardian, or person having the control or charge of the pupil shall deliver the pupil at the beginning of each schoolday, for the remainder of the school term, at the school from which the pupil is a truant or to a school designated by school authorities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 323, Sec. 4. (AB 901) Effective January 1, 2021.)
