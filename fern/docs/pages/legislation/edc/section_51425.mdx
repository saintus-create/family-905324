# EDC § 51425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 3. High School Equivalency Certificates

**Citation:** EDC § 51425

---

## Text

A California high school equivalency certificate shall be deemed to be a high school diploma for the purpose of meeting the requirements of employment by all state and local public agencies in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
