# EDC § 54524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 29. PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Compensatory Education Programs for Disadvantaged Children > Article: 6. Preschool Followthrough Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 54524

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall provide for the establishment of pilot preschool followthrough programs and projects in schools maintaining grades kindergarten, 1, 2, and 3 at the elementary level. The programs and projects shall be subject to the provisions of Section 54461.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
