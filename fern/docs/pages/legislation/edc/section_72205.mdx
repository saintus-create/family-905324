# EDC § 72205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 72205

---

## Text

The approval of any state agency shall not be a prerequisite to acceptance by the governing board of any community college district of a gift, donation, bequest, or devise. No real or personal property, including money, accepted by a governing board pursuant to this section shall be considered in determining the eligibility of the district for an apportionment from the State School Fund nor in determining the amount thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 8, Sec. 28. Effective April 15, 1993.)
