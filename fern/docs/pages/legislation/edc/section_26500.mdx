# EDC § 26500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 26500

---

## Text

Acceptance of employment subject to coverage by the Cash Balance Benefit Program constitutes consent to have contributions deducted from the employee’s salary as required by Section 26501.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 264. Effective January 1, 1999.)
