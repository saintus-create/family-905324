# EDC § 1203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 1. Appointment, Qualifications, Salary and Expenses

**Citation:** EDC § 1203

---

## Text

The expense of providing housing for all the services of the county superintendent of schools shall be allowed by the board of supervisors of the county and paid out of the general fund of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
