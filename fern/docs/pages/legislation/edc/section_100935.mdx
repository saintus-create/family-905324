# EDC § 100935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68.2. KINDERGARTEN–UNIVERSITY PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2004 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Higher Education Facilities > Article: 5. Higher Education Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 100935

---

## Text

The board, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 100910, may request the Pooled Money Investment Board to make a loan from the Pooled Money Investment Account or any other approved form of interim financing, in accordance with Section 16312 of the Government Code, for the purpose of carrying out this chapter. The amount of the request shall not exceed the amount of the unsold bonds that the committee, by resolution, has authorized to be sold for the purpose of carrying out this chapter. The board, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 100910, shall execute any documents required by the Pooled Money Investment Board to obtain and repay the loan. Any amounts loaned shall be deposited in the fund to be allocated by the board in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 33, Sec. 31. Approved in Proposition 55 at the March 2, 2004, election.)
