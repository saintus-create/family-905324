# EDC § 68061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 5. Determination of Student Residence

**Citation:** EDC § 68061

---

## Text

Every person who is married or 18 years of age, or older, and under no legal disability to do so, may establish residence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
