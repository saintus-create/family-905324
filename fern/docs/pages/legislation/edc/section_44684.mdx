# EDC § 44684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.1. School Personnel Staff Development and Resource Centers > Article: 3. Administrator Training and Evaluation

**Citation:** EDC § 44684

---

## Text

Administrator training and evaluation programs shall set aside specific times throughout the school year to conduct programs. The times shall include, but not be limited to, periods when participating administrators are released from their regular duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 37. Effective July 28, 1983.)
