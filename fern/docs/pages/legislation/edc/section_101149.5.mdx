# EDC § 101149.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 70. KINDERGARTEN THROUGH COMMUNITY COLLEGE PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2016 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. California Community College Facilities > Article: 3. California Community College Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 101149.5

---

## Text

The people hereby find and declare that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this chapter are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 51, Sec. 3.)
