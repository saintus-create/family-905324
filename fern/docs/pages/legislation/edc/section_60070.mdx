# EDC § 60070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 5. Prohibited Acts

**Citation:** EDC § 60070

---

## Text

No school official shall require any pupil, except pupils in classes for adults to purchase any instructional material for the pupils’ use in the school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
