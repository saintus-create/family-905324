# EDC § 35720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 2. County Committee on School District Organization Plans and Recommendations for District Reorganization

**Citation:** EDC § 35720

---

## Text

Each county committee on school district organization shall, under the direction of the State Board of Education, formulate plans and recommendations for the organization of the districts in the county or any portion thereof including, if appropriate, a portion of one or more adjacent counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
