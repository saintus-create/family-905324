# EDC § 90089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 3. Affordable Student Housing

**Citation:** EDC § 90089

---

## Text

The trustees shall develop a five-year priority list of the housing needs at each of the campuses, taking into account the impact of the students attending that campus on the local pool of affordable housing available for senior citizens and other limited-income people with critical housing needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1125, Sec. 1.)
