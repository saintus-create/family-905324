# EDC § 89529.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 1.1. Industrial Disability Leave

**Citation:** EDC § 89529.04

---

## Text

An employee who is receiving industrial disability leave benefits shall continue to receive all employee benefits which he or she would have received had he or she not incurred disability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 670, Sec. 2.)
