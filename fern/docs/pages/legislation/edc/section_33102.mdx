# EDC § 33102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction > Article: 1. Appointment and Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 33102

---

## Text

The actual and necessary traveling expenses of the Superintendent of Public Instruction, his deputy and assistants, shall be ordered and paid out of the appropriation made for the Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
