# EDC § 78263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Courses of Study > Article: 3.54. Allied Health Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 78263

---

## Text

As used in this article, “allied health program” means a community college program that offers certificates or degrees related to allied health professionals, as defined in Section 295p of Title 42 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 362, Sec. 1. (AB 1891) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Sec. 78263.3.)
