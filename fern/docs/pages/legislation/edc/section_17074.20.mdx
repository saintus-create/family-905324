# EDC § 17074.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 7. Modernization Apportionment

**Citation:** EDC § 17074.20

---

## Text

As a condition for the receipt of funds under this article, a school district shall ensure that all buildings modernized comply with Sections 17212, 17212.5, and 17213.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 4. Effective August 27, 1998.)
