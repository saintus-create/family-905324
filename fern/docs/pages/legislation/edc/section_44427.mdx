# EDC § 44427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 1. Revocation and Suspension of Certification Documents

**Citation:** EDC § 44427

---

## Text

County boards of education may revoke or suspend, for immoral or unprofessional conduct, evident unfitness for teaching, or persistent defiance of, and refusal to obey the laws regulating the duties of, teachers, the certificates granted by them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
