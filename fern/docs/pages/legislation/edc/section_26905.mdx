# EDC § 26905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Disability Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 26905

---

## Text

The normal form of disability benefit under this part is a lump-sum payment. Upon distribution of the lump-sum payment to the participant, no further benefits shall be payable from the Cash Balance Benefit Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 286. Effective January 1, 1999.)
