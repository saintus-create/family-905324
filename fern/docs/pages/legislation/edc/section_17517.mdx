# EDC § 17517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 8. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** EDC § 17517

---

## Text

The term of a lease or agreement entered into by a school district pursuant to this article shall not exceed 99 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 204, Sec. 2. (AB 1406) Effective January 1, 2019.)
