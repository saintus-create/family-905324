# EDC § 44968.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Resignations, Dismissals, and Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 44968.5

---

## Text

An employee granted a leave of absence pursuant to Section 44966 or 44967 may agree in writing with the governing board of the school district not to receive compensation during the period of the leave.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
