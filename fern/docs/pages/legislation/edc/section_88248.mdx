# EDC § 88248

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 8. Instructional Aides

**Citation:** EDC § 88248

---

## Text

The community college district shall pay to each person employed as an instructional aide compensation at a rate not less than the minimum hourly rate prescribed by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
