# EDC § 87602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 2. Employment of Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 87602

---

## Text

For the purposes of other provisions of law:

(a) A contract employee is a probationary employee.

(b) A regular or tenured employee is a permanent employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 973, Sec. 36. Operative July 1, 1991, pursuant to Sec. 70(e) of Ch. 973.)
