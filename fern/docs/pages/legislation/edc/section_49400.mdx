# EDC § 49400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 1. General Powers—School Boards

**Citation:** EDC § 49400

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district shall give diligent care to the health and physical development of pupils, and may employ properly certified persons for the work.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
