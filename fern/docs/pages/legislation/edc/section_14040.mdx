# EDC § 14040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 9. FINANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State School Fund > Article: 2. Maintenance, Amount and Manner of Drawing Warrants

**Citation:** EDC § 14040

---

## Text

The Controller shall keep a separate account of the State School Fund, and of the interest and income thereof, together with such money as is raised by special tax or otherwise for school purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
