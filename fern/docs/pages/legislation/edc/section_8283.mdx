# EDC § 8283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 10. Childcare Subsidy Plan for the City and County of San Francisco

**Citation:** EDC § 8283

---

## Text

The City and County of San Francisco may develop and implement an individualized county childcare subsidy plan. The plan shall ensure that childcare subsidies received by the city and county are used to address local needs, conditions, and priorities of working families in the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8335 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 169. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
