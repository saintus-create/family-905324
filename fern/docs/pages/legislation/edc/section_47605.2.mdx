# EDC § 47605.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Establishment of Charter Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 47605.2

---

## Text

The Delta Charter High School, located in the County of Santa Cruz, is exempt from the geographic and site limitations contained in subdivision (a) of Section 47605.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 112, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
