# EDC § 56193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 7. Community Advisory Committee

**Citation:** EDC § 56193

---

## Text

At least the majority of such committee shall be composed of parents of pupils enrolled in schools participating in the local plan, and at least a majority of such parents shall be parents of individuals with exceptional needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 797, Sec. 9. Effective July 28, 1980.)
