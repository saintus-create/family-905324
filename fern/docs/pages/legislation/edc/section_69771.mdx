# EDC § 69771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69771

---

## Text

This article shall be applicable to the extent that its provisions do not conflict with Title IV of the Higher Education Act of 1965, or any extensions thereof, or any similar act of Congress, and the rules and regulations adopted thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1113, Sec. 38.3. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
