# EDC § 46203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 8. Incentives for Longer Instructional Day and Year

**Citation:** EDC § 46203

---

## Text

Amounts apportioned to school districts pursuant to this article shall supplement, and not supplant, apportionments received pursuant to any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 80. Effective July 28, 1983.)
