# EDC § 10503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Data-Processing Centers > Article: 1. Regional Data-Processing Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 10503

---

## Text

The functions of regional educational data-processing centers shall include the processing and reporting of information relating, among other things, to programs of instruction, school business administration, and pupil personnel data.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
