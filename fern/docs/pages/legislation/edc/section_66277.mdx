# EDC § 66277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education

**Citation:** EDC § 66277

---

## Text

In regard to admissions to educational institutions, this article shall apply only to institutions of vocational, professional, or postgraduate education, and to public postsecondary education institutions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 227 by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1999.)
