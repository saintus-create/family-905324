# EDC § 1732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 4. Supervision of Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 1732

---

## Text

The services described in Sections 1730 and 1731 shall be performed by persons who hold a valid credential issued by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing authorizing that service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 471, Sec. 1. (SB 1329) Effective January 1, 2025.)
