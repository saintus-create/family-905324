# EDC § 18531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 13. Issuance and Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18531

---

## Text

The total amount of bonds issued, shall not exceed 5 percent of the taxable property of the district, as shown by the last equalized assessment book of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
