# EDC § 51204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 51204

---

## Text

Any course of study adopted pursuant to this division shall be designed to fit the needs of the pupils for which the course of study is prescribed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
