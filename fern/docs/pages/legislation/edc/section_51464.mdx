# EDC § 51464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 6. State Seal of Biliteracy

**Citation:** EDC § 51464

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that no fee be charged to a pupil pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 618, Sec. 2. (AB 815) Effective January 1, 2012.)
