# EDC § 46100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** EDC § 46100

---

## Text

The governing board of each school district shall, subject to the provisions of this chapter, fix the length of the schoolday for the several grades and classes of the schools maintained by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
