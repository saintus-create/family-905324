# EDC § 35580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 9. Formation of the Wiseburn Unified School District, Bonded Indebtedness, and Revenue Limit

**Citation:** EDC § 35580

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Article 8 (commencing with Section 35570), this article shall apply to the reorganization of the Wiseburn School District and the Centinela Valley Union High School District by the formation of the Wiseburn Unified School District and the termination of the Wiseburn School District. This article determines the liability for taxation, the bonding capacity, the permitted use of existing bond proceeds, and the allocation of authorized but unsold bonds of the Wiseburn Unified School District and the Centinela Valley Union High School District, and applies to the activities of the Local Public Schools Funding Authority, a joint powers authority created by the Wiseburn School District and the Centinela Valley Union High School District or the Wiseburn Unified School District and the Centinela Valley Union High School District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 730, Sec. 1. (SB 477) Effective January 1, 2013.)
