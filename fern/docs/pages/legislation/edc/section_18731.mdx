# EDC § 18731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 4. Local Public Library Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18731

---

## Text

A California public library may participate in universal borrowing. A public library participating in universal borrowing shall not exclude the residents of any jurisdiction maintaining a public library.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 5. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
