# EDC § 48296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** EDC § 48296

---

## Text

No fees shall be charged or received by any court or officer in any proceeding under this chapter.
