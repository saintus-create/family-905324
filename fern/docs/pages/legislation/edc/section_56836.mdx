# EDC § 56836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7.2. Special Education Funding > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 56836

---

## Text

Commencing with the 1998–99 fiscal year and for each fiscal year thereafter, apportionments to special education local plan areas for special education programs operated by, and services provided by, districts, county offices, and special education local plan areas shall be computed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 854, Sec. 65. Effective January 1, 1998.)
