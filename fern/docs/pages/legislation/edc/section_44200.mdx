# EDC § 44200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 44200

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Teacher Credentialing Law of 1988” or “The Bergeson Act.”
