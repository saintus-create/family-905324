# EDC § 66602.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66602.5

---

## Text

All meetings of the trustees shall, except as otherwise provided in Section 66602.7, be subject to Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 523, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
