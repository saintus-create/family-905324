# EDC § 17074.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 7. Modernization Apportionment

**Citation:** EDC § 17074.27

---

## Text

In addition to the uses specified in Section 17074.25, a modernization apportionment may also be used for the control, management, or abatement of lead.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1075, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
