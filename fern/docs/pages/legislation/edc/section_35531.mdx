# EDC § 35531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 4. Completion and Effective Dates for Action

**Citation:** EDC § 35531

---

## Text

An action to form a unified school district pursuant to Section 35542 is complete upon the date of completion of the action by which the boundaries of the school districts comprising the unified school district become coterminous.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 186, Sec. 5. (AB 2659) Effective January 1, 2017.)
