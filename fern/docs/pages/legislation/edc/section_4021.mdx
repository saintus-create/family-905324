# EDC § 4021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 2. Transfer of Duties of County Committee to County Board of Education

**Citation:** EDC § 4021

---

## Text

Upon the order of the State Board of Education issued pursuant to Section 4020, the county board of education of a county succeeds to and is vested with all duties, powers, purposes, responsibilities, and jurisdiction formerly vested in the county committee on school district organization of that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 3. Effective September 22, 1982.)
