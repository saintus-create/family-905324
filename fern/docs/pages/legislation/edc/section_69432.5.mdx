# EDC § 69432.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.7. Ortiz-Pacheco-Poochigian-Vasconcellos Cal Grant Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 69432.5

---

## Text

The Budget required by the California Constitution to be submitted by the Governor at each Regular Session of the Legislature shall take into consideration the amount of federal grant funds for student financial aid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 403, Sec. 4. Effective September 12, 2000. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed in Section 69475 (if it becomes operative). Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 69475 (if it becomes operative).)
