# EDC § 37060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Maintenance of Schools > Article: 5. Junior High Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 37060

---

## Text

The governing board of a county, a high school district, a union high school district, or a joint union high school district may establish a junior high school or a system of junior high schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 227.5.)
