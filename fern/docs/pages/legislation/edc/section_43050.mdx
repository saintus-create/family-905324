# EDC § 43050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 15. Voter-Approved Special Taxes for New School Facilities

**Citation:** EDC § 43050

---

## Text

When the proceeds of special taxes are used for construction of new school facilities, the State Allocation Board shall approve such construction, in accordance with building area and cost standards adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 489, Sec. 1. Effective July 13, 1980.)
