# EDC § 81396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 5. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** EDC § 81396

---

## Text

Before entering into a lease or agreement pursuant to this article, the governing board of a community college district shall comply with the provisions of Section 81397.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
