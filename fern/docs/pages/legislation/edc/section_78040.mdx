# EDC § 78040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 3. Baccalaureate Degree Program

**Citation:** EDC § 78040

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “district” means any community college district identified by the Chancellor of the California Community Colleges as participating in the statewide baccalaureate degree program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 565, Sec. 2. (AB 927) Effective January 1, 2022.)
