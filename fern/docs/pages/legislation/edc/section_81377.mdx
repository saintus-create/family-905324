# EDC § 81377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 4. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** EDC § 81377

---

## Text

Nothing in Sections 81360 to 81363, inclusive, shall prevent the governing board of a community college district from acquiring, leasing or subleasing property pursuant to Section 1261 of the Military and Veterans Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
