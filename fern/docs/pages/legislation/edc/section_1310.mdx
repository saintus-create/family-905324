# EDC § 1310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 4. Classified County School Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 1310

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall become operative in any county upon adoption of a resolution by the county board of supervisors to make it operative.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
