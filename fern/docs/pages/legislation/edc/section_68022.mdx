# EDC § 68022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 68022

---

## Text

“Resident classification” means classification as a resident, pursuant to Section 68017, at the University of California, the California State University, the California Maritime Academy, or a California community college.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 233.)
