# EDC § 24929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 35. Joint District Salary Retirement Plan > Article: 2. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24929

---

## Text

The plan may provide for the retirement of teachers or other employees on account of disability after 10 years of service immediately prior to the retirement, the proportion of the disability retirement salary to the full retirement salary to be specified in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
