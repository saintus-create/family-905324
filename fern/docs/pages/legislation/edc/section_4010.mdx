# EDC § 4010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 1. County Committees on School District Organization

**Citation:** EDC § 4010

---

## Text

The members of the county committee shall serve without compensation. However, they shall receive reimbursement for any actual and necessary travel expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 47.)
