# EDC § 51402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 1. Elementary School Diploma

**Citation:** EDC § 51402

---

## Text

The diplomas of graduation from elementary schools shall be conferred only upon the pupils who have completed the course of study prescribed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
