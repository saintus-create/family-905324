# EDC § 48306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 7. Pupil Attendance Alternatives

**Citation:** EDC § 48306

---

## Text

(a) A school district of choice shall give first priority for attendance to siblings of children already in attendance in that school district.

(b) A school district of choice shall give second priority for attendance to pupils eligible for free or reduced-price meals, and pupils who are foster youth or homeless children or youth.

(c) A school district of choice shall give third priority for attendance to children of military personnel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 67, Sec. 55. (AB 1170) Effective January 1, 2026.)
