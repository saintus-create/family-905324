# EDC § 5442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 9. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 5442

---

## Text

Recount of votes in any school district or community college district shall be governed by Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 15600) of Division 15 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 588, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
