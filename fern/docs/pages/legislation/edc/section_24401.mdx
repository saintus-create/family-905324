# EDC § 24401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 29. Benefit Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 24401

---

## Text

This chapter does not give any retired member, or a retired member’s successors in interest or beneficiary, any claim against the board, system, or plan for any increase in any allowance paid or payable prior to July 1, 1972.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 246. Effective January 1, 1997.)
