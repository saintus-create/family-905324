# EDC § 52171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Bilingual Education > Article: 3. Bilingual-Bicultural Education Act of 1976

**Citation:** EDC § 52171

---

## Text

Each district shall submit annually to the department an evaluation of pupil progress for every program that has been approved pursuant to this article in a form and manner prescribed by the superintendent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 922, Sec. 125. Effective January 1, 1995.)
