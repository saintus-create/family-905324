# EDC § 32451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Adult Day Care Food Program

**Citation:** EDC § 32451

---

## Text

The State Department of Education is hereby designated as the single state agency to administer the Adult Day Care Food Program established pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 530, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
