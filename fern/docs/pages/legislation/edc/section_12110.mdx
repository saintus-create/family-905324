# EDC § 12110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 7. Surplus Federal Property

**Citation:** EDC § 12110

## Text

The State Department of Education is hereby designated as the California State Agency for Donated Food Distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 196, Sec. 1. Effective June 13, 1984. Operative July 1, 1984, by Sec. 8.5 of Ch. 196.)
