# EDC § 92430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 2. Powers of Regents

**Citation:** EDC § 92430

---

## Text

In addition to, and in amplification of, all other powers conferred upon the regents by the Constitution or by any statute of this state, the regents shall have all of the powers set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
