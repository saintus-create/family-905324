# EDC § 56022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 56022

---

## Text

“County office” means office of the county superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 797, Sec. 9. Effective July 28, 1980.)
