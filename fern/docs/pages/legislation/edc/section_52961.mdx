# EDC § 52961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 13. Science and Technology Education Improvement Programs > Article: 2. California Science Project

**Citation:** EDC § 52961

---

## Text

The Educational Technology Committee and the State Board of Education shall give careful consideration to funding proposals for classroom application utilizing computers, videos, and other educational technology which would enhance the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1486, Sec. 1. Operative July 8, 1988, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 1486.)
