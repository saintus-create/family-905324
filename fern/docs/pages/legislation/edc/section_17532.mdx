# EDC § 17532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 9. Joint Use

**Citation:** EDC § 17532

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may lease vacant classroom space the total area of which exceeds the 30 percent districtwide limit of classroom space available pursuant to this article, if a lease is for any day care center, nursery school, or special education class.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
