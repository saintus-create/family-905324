# EDC § 27207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Termination Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27207

---

## Text

Upon distribution of the lump-sum payment to the participant under this part, no further benefits shall be payable from the plan under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 303. Effective January 1, 1999.)
