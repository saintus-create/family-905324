# EDC § 68042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 3. Classification

**Citation:** EDC § 68042

---

## Text

The governing board or district governing board may appoint persons to administer oaths or affirmations in connection with taking testimony necessary to ascertain a student’s classification.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
