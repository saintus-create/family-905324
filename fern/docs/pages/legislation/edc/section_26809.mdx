# EDC § 26809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Retirement Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 26809

---

## Text

Upon election of an annuity under this part, the credits in the participant’s employee account and employer account shall be transferred to the Annuitant Reserve.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 279. Effective January 1, 1999.)
