# EDC § 89297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Education Programs > Article: 11. Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander Student Achievement Program

**Citation:** EDC § 89297

---

## Text

As used in this article, unless the context requires otherwise, the following definitions apply:

(a) “AANHPI” means Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander.

(b) “Program” means the AANHPI Student Achievement Program established pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 54, Sec. 42. (AB 183) Effective June 30, 2022.)
