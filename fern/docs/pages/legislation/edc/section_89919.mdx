# EDC § 89919

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 1.5. The Richard McKee Transparency Act of 2011

**Citation:** EDC § 89919

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to any records subject to a request made pursuant to the California Public Records Act, as set forth in Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 86. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
