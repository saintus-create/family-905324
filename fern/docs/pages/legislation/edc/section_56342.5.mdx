# EDC § 56342.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 3. Instructional Planning and Individualized Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 56342.5

---

## Text

A local educational agency shall ensure that the parent of each individual with exceptional needs is a member of any group that makes decisions on the educational placement of the individual with exceptional needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 2008.)
