# EDC § 15276

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Strict Accountability in Local School Construction Bonds Act of 2000 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 15276

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a county board of education may not order an election to determine whether bonds may be issued under this article to raise funds for a county office of education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 44, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
