# EDC § 32245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Safety—Public Institutions > Article: 4. Lead-Safe Schools Protection Act

**Citation:** EDC § 32245

---

## Text

Funding to implement this article shall be provided from the Child Health and Safety Fund created under Chapter 4.6 (commencing with Section 18285) of Part 6 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, upon appropriation by the Legislature pursuant to Section 18285 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 589, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 1994.)
