# EDC § 38080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23. SUPPLEMENTAL SERVICES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Cafeterias > Article: 1. Establishment and Use

**Citation:** EDC § 38080

---

## Text

The term “cafeteria” as used in this code is considered synonymous with the term “food service.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
