# EDC § 22314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22314

---

## Text

The system shall inform a member, upon retirement, that future tax liabilities may occur as the result of the pending retirement allowance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
