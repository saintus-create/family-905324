# EDC § 18031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Public Library Finance > Article: 4. Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18031

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall become operative July 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1498, Sec. 1. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 1983) for Chapter 1.5, commencing with Section 18010.)
