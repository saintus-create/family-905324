# EDC § 78070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 6. Support Services for Justice-Involved Students

**Citation:** EDC § 78070

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Justice-involved” means a person who is currently or formerly incarcerated in a California correctional facility, or currently or formerly detained in a juvenile facility.

(b) “Rising Scholars Network” means the program established pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 78071.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 558, Sec. 2. (AB 417) Effective January 1, 2022.)
