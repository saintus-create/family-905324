# EDC § 18883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.6. California Library Literacy and English Acquisition Services Program

**Citation:** EDC § 18883

---

## Text

A local library shall ensure that funds received pursuant to this chapter are exclusively used for expenses resulting from providing English language and literacy services and shall ensure that at least 90 percent of the funds received for the program are expended on direct services and related materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 573, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
