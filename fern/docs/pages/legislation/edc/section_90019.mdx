# EDC § 90019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90019

---

## Text

An indenture pursuant to which bonds are issued may include any and all of the covenants and agreements on the part of the board as the board deems necessary or advisable for the better security of the bonds issued thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 740, Sec. 10.)
