# EDC § 52515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 52515

---

## Text

State funds shall not be apportioned to a school district unless the courses have been approved by the department pursuant to Section 41976.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 59. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
