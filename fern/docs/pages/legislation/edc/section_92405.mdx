# EDC § 92405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 92405

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, firm, corporation, association, copartnership, trust, business trust, or receiver or trustee or conservator for any individual or any such entity. It does not, however, include the state or any public corporation, political subdivision, city, county, district, or any agency of any such entity or of the state.
