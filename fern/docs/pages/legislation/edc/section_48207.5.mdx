# EDC § 48207.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 1. Persons Included

**Citation:** EDC § 48207.5

---

## Text

Individual instruction in a pupil’s home pursuant to Section 48206.3 shall commence no later than five working days after a school district has determined that the pupil shall receive this instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 167, Sec. 5. (AB 2109) Effective January 1, 2019.)
