# EDC § 27006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. Death Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27006

---

## Text

Upon the beneficiary’s election to receive the death benefit under this part in the form of an annuity, the credits in the participant’s employee account and employer account shall be transferred to the Annuitant Reserve.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 292. Effective January 1, 1999.)
