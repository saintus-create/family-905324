# EDC § 18100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 1. Establishment and Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 18100

---

## Text

The governing board of each school district shall provide school library services for the pupils and teachers of the district by establishing and maintaining school libraries or by contractual arrangements with another public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 153.)
