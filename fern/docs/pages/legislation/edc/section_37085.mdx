# EDC § 37085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Maintenance of Schools > Article: 5. Junior High Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 37085

---

## Text

Any school district within a junior high school or system of junior high schools, maintained by the governing board of a county, union, or joint union high school district, may withdraw from the junior high system when a majority of the qualified voters in the district voting thereat vote in favor of withdrawal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
