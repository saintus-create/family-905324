# EDC § 69503.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 1.5. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 69503.6

---

## Text

Each institution of higher education with a physical presence in this state shall separately list the cost of institutionally operated housing and meal plans on all Internet Web sites and documents it provides to students for purposes of advertising or otherwise displaying the student costs associated with institutionally operated housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 314, Sec. 1. (AB 1961) Effective January 1, 2019.)
