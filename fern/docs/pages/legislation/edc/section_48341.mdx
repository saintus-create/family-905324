# EDC § 48341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 9. Improvement of Pupil Attendance

**Citation:** EDC § 48341

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall prepare and disseminate to school districts and county superintendents of schools information regarding effective practices to improve pupil attendance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1329, Sec. 10.)
