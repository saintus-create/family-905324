# EDC § 66761.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9.6. Cross-Enrollment in Online Education at California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66761.5

---

## Text

The host campus may charge participating students a reasonable administration fee and specific course-based fees, not to exceed an amount sufficient for the campus to recover reasonable administrative costs it incurs pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 363, Sec. 2. (AB 386) Effective January 1, 2014.)
