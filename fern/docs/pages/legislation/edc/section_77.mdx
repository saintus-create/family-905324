# EDC § 77

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 77

---

## Text

“State” means the State of California, unless applied to the different parts of the United States. In the latter case, it includes the District of Columbia and the territories.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
