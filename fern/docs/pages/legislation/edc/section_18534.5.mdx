# EDC § 18534.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 13. Issuance and Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18534.5

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may provide for redemption of bonds before maturity at prices determined by it. A bond shall not be subject to call or redemption prior to maturity unless it contains a recital to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
