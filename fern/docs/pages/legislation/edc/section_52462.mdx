# EDC § 52462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 7.5. Agricultural Career Technical Education Incentive Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52462

---

## Text

The State Board of Education may, with the advice of the Superintendent of Public Instruction, adopt rules and regulations governing the distribution of funds provided for purposes of this article and shall adopt criteria for assessing whether school districts have met the requirements of Section 52461.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1461, Sec. 24.)
