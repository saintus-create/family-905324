# EDC § 18111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** EDC § 18111

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may exclude from schools and school libraries all books, publications, or papers of a sectarian, partisan, or denominational character.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 158.)
