# EDC § 12092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 6. Veterans’ Education

**Citation:** EDC § 12092

---

## Text

The Department of Education through the Director of Education is authorized to enter into agreements with governing boards of school districts for the education by such districts of veterans in accordance with the agreement between the Department of Education and the agency of the federal government.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 380.)
