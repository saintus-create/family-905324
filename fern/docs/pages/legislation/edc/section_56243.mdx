# EDC § 56243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Elements of the Local Plan > Article: 3. Staff Development

**Citation:** EDC § 56243

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature, pursuant to this article, that a local educational agency provide regular classroom teachers serving individuals with exceptional needs appropriate training each year relating to the needs of those individuals.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2008.)
