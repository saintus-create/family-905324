# EDC § 8300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 12. Child Development Program Personnel Qualifications

**Citation:** EDC § 8300

---

## Text

The same fee as that prescribed for a credential provided in Section 44235 shall be charged for either the issuance or renewal of each child development permit authorizing service in the supervision and instruction of children in child development programs or authorizing service as a supervisor in a program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8362 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 191. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
