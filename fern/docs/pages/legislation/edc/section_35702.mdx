# EDC § 35702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 1. Reorganization of School Districts by the Electorate

**Citation:** EDC § 35702

---

## Text

The persons securing the signatures to a petition of electors to reorganize school districts shall attach thereto an affidavit that all persons who signed the petition did so in the presence of the affiant and that each signature is a genuine signature of the person whose name it purports to be.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
