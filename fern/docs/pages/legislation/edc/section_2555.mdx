# EDC § 2555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. County Tax Rates, County Superintendents > Article: 3. Computation of Revenue Limits

**Citation:** EDC § 2555

---

## Text

Beginning July 1, 1979, the Superintendent of Public Instruction shall apportion an amount to each county superintendent sufficient for payments required pursuant to any school building aid law previously funded by a tax levied pursuant to Section 2503.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 282.)
