# EDC § 66550.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7.5. Law School Externships

**Citation:** EDC § 66550.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, a requirement on a law school shall, regarding the University of California, be considered a request on the Regents of the University of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 292, Sec. 2. (AB 1155) Effective January 1, 2026.)
