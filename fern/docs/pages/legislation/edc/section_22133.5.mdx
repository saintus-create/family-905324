# EDC § 22133.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22133.5

---

## Text

“Final benefit” means the amount payable to a beneficiary under the Defined Benefit Supplement Program upon the death of the member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2001.)
