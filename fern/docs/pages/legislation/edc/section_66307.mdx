# EDC § 66307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.1. Stop Campus Hazing Act

**Citation:** EDC § 66307

---

## Text

No person shall be subjected to hazing in any program or activity conducted by any educational institution that receives, or benefits from, state financial assistance or enrolls students who receive state student financial aid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 704, Sec. 2. (AB 2193) Effective January 1, 2025.)
