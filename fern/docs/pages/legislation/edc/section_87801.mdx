# EDC § 87801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87801

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district shall fix and order paid the compensation of persons employed by the board as academic employees unless otherwise prescribed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 157. Effective September 25, 1990.)
