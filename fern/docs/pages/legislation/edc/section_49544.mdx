# EDC § 49544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 10.5. Child Care Food Program

**Citation:** EDC § 49544

---

## Text

The Department of Education shall maximize the use of available federal funds for the federal child care food program by all eligible entities that provide child development services or child care programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 959, Sec. 1.)
