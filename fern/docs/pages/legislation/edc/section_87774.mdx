# EDC § 87774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 7. Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 87774

---

## Text

At the expiration of the leave of absence of the employee, the employee, unless he or she otherwise agrees, shall be reinstated in the position held by him or her at the time of the granting of the leave of absence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 178. Effective January 1, 1996.)
