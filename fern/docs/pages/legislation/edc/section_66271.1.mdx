# EDC § 66271.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 3. Prohibition of Discrimination

**Citation:** EDC § 66271.1

---

## Text

The Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges and the Trustees of the California State University shall, and the Regents of the University of California may, adopt regulations as required by law to implement this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1999.)
