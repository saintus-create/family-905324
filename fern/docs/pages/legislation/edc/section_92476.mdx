# EDC § 92476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 3. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 92476

---

## Text

Bonds shall bear interest at a rate of not to exceed 12 percent per annum, payable annually or semiannually, or in part annually and in part semiannually.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 33) by Stats. 1989, Ch. 321, Sec. 11.)
