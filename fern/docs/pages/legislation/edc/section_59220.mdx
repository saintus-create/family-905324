# EDC § 59220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diagnostic Centers > Article: 3. Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 59220

---

## Text

Pupils residing within California shall be accepted by a diagnostic center pursuant to criteria adopted by the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 67. Effective September 30, 2002.)
