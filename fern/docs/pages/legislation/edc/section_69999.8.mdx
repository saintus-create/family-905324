# EDC § 69999.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 20.5. Management and Disbursement of Funds Previously Set Aside for Repealed Governor’s Scholarship Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 69999.8

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Board” means the Scholarshare Investment Board established pursuant to subparagraph (B) of paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 69984.

(b) “Former Article 20” means former Article 20 (commencing with Section 69995) of Chapter 2 of Part 42 of the Education Code, as it read on January 1, 2003.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 227, Sec. 15. Effective August 16, 2004.)
