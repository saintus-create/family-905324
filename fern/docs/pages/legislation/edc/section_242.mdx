# EDC § 242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 7. Instructional Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 242

---

## Text

No later than July 1, 2025, the department shall develop guidance and public educational materials, including an internet website and other communications materials, to ensure that all Californians can access information about educational laws and policies that safeguard the right to an accurate and inclusive curriculum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 229, Sec. 3. (AB 1078) Effective September 25, 2023.)
