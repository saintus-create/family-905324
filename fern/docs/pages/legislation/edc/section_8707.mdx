# EDC § 8707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Environmental Education > Article: 1. Policy

**Citation:** EDC § 8707

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this chapter to encourage development of educational programs for teachers and students commensurate with the importance of protecting scarce resources and safeguarding the quality of our environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
