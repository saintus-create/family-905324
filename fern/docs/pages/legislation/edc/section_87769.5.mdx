# EDC § 87769.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 7. Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 87769.5

---

## Text

An employee granted a leave of absence pursuant to Section 87767 or 87768 may agree in writing with the governing board of the community college district not to receive compensation during the period of the leave.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
