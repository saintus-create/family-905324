# EDC § 24503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 30. Subrogation

**Citation:** EDC § 24503

---

## Text

The board may compromise claims before or after commencement of suit or entry of judgment for an amount as may be approved by a person duly authorized by the board for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 298, Sec. 14. (AB 1325) Effective January 1, 2018.)
