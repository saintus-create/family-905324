# EDC § 89926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 2. Meetings, Elections, and Judicial Determinations

**Citation:** EDC § 89926

---

## Text

Where the constitution or articles of incorporation of an associated students auxiliary organization provides for a judiciary or judicial council with powers separate from the governing board of the auxiliary organization, decisions rendered by the judiciary or judicial council shall be final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1158, Sec. 2.)
