# EDC § 51050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 4. Enforcement of Courses of Study

**Citation:** EDC § 51050

---

## Text

The governing board of every school district shall enforce in its schools the courses of study and the use of textbooks and other instructional materials prescribed and adopted by the proper authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
