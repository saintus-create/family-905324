# EDC § 49101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Employment of Minors > Article: 1. Employment and Attendance

**Citation:** EDC § 49101

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to any minor who has been graduated from a high school maintaining a four-year course above the eighth grade of elementary schools, or who has had an equal amount of education in a private school or by private tuition, or who has been awarded a certificate of proficiency pursuant to Section 48412 of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 796.)
