# EDC § 19701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Library Districts and Museums in Unincorporated Towns and Villages > Article: 4. Biennial Election of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19701

---

## Text

The number of library trustees for any library district established under the provisions of this chapter is five.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
