# EDC § 19902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.5. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. Deposit of Newspapers in Libraries

**Citation:** EDC § 19902

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may authorize the boards of trustees or other authorities in charge of any free public library with which newspaper files have been deposited in accordance with Section 19900 to deposit the newspaper files with the State Library.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
