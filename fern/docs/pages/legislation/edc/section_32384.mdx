# EDC § 32384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Miscellaneous > Article: 9. Prohibited Activities

**Citation:** EDC § 32384

---

## Text

Any court of competent jurisdiction is hereby authorized to grant such relief as is necessary to enforce the provisions of this article, including the issuance of an injunction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 373.)
