# EDC § 56244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Elements of the Local Plan > Article: 3. Staff Development

**Citation:** EDC § 56244

---

## Text

The superintendent shall, to the extent possible using federal and state funds appropriated for this purpose, provide staff development to child care center staff and family day care providers to improve child care services to individuals with exceptional needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1596, Sec. 6.)
