# EDC § 44429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 1. Revocation and Suspension of Certification Documents

**Citation:** EDC § 44429

---

## Text

All charges of immoral or unprofessional conduct, of evident unfitness for teaching, or persistent defiance of, and refusal to obey the laws regulating the duties of teachers, shall be presented to the board in writing and shall be verified under oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
