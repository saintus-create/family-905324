# EDC § 22821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.2. Out-of-State Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22821

---

## Text

A member’s request to purchase out-of-state service credit pursuant to this chapter shall be submitted in writing and shall include information as required by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 304, Sec. 11. (SB 634) Effective January 1, 2010.)
