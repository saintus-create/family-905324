# EDC § 87351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2.5. Qualifications for Community College Personnel > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 87351

---

## Text

The minimum qualifications for service prescribed by this chapter shall become operative on July 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 31. Effective September 25, 1990.)
