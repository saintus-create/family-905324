# EDC § 49493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 7. School Breakfast and Lunch Program

**Citation:** EDC § 49493

---

## Text

No allocation or reimbursement of funds shall be made pursuant to this article to any public or private nonprofit school or agency in behalf of any child who resides on the premises of such school or agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
