# EDC § 56605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Evaluation, Audits, and Information

**Citation:** EDC § 56605

---

## Text

The superintendent shall periodically sponsor or conduct workshops and seminars for the education of local education agency personnel assigned to, and responsible for, the evaluation of local special education programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1508, Sec. 9.)
