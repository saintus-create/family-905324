# EDC § 35175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 35175

---

## Text

The powers and duties of boards of education in cities are as prescribed in the laws governing the respective cities, except as otherwise provided by this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
