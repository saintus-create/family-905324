# EDC § 2603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. School District Boundaries

**Citation:** EDC § 2603

---

## Text

The county superintendent may, if he deems it necessary, order a description of the boundaries of any district under his jurisdiction printed in pamphlet form and pay for the pamphlets out of the county school service fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
