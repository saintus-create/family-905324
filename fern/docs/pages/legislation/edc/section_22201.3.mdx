# EDC § 22201.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** EDC § 22201.3

---

## Text

The chief executive officer of the system shall act as secretary of the board and shall have charge of all board correspondence and shall keep a record of board proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 45. Effective January 1, 1997.)
