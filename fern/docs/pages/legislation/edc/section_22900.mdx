# EDC § 22900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Employee Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 22900

---

## Text

By accepting employment to perform creditable service, a person consents to make contributions pursuant to Sections 22901 and 22901.7 for service and compensation credited under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 123, Sec. 13. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2016.)
