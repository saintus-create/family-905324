# EDC § 92527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92527

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause designating the rights, limitations, powers, and duties arising upon breach by the regents of any of the covenants, conditions, or obligations contained in any indenture.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
