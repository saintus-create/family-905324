# EDC § 8238

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 8238

---

## Text

The Superintendent may waive or modify California state preschool requirements in order to enable preschool programs to serve combinations of eligible children in areas of low population.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8264.5 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 78. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
