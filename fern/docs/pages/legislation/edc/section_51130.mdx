# EDC § 51130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.5. Parental Involvement > Article: 3. Teresa P. Hughes Family-School Partnership Award and Grant Program

**Citation:** EDC § 51130

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Teresa P. Hughes Family-School Partnership Award and Grant Program and shall be administered by the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 734, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2000.)
