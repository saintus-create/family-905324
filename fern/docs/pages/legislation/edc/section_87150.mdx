# EDC § 87150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Provisions Applying to All Employees > Article: 5. Community College Faculty and Staff Development Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 87150

---

## Text

There is hereby established the Community College Professional Development Program, to be administered by the board of governors, the purpose of which is to provide state general funds to community colleges for supporting locally developed and implemented faculty and staff development programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 473, Sec. 1. (AB 2558) Effective January 1, 2015.)
