# EDC § 68023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 68023

---

## Text

“Residence determination date” is a date or day established by the governing boards or district governing boards, as appropriate, for each semester, quarter, or term to determine a student’s residence.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 234.)
