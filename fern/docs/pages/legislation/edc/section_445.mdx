# EDC § 445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. English Learner and Immigrant Pupil Federal Conformity Act

**Citation:** EDC § 445

---

## Text

In accordance with Section 6846 of Title 20 of the United States Code, nothing in this chapter shall be construed to negate or supersede state law or the legal authority under state law of the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1014, Sec. 1. Effective September 27, 2002.)
