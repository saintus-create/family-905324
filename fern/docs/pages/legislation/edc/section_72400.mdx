# EDC § 72400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 72400

---

## Text

The governing board of each community college district shall fix and prescribe the duties to be performed by all persons in community college service in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 470, Sec. 82.)
