# EDC § 89750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Finance > Article: 4. Expenditures and Control

**Citation:** EDC § 89750

---

## Text

The trustees shall control and expend all money appropriated for the support and maintenance of the California State University, and all money received as donations pursuant to Section 89720.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 143, Sec. 150.)
