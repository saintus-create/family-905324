# EDC § 40090.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Specialized Vehicle Driver Training Courses

**Citation:** EDC § 40090.5

---

## Text

Employers shall take all action necessary to make available to every transit busdriver required to be trained pursuant to Section 40083 or 40085.5 the opportunity to be trained without the loss of wages or benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 646, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2000.)
