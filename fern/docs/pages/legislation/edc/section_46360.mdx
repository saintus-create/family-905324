# EDC § 46360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Average Daily Attendance > Article: 6. County School Service Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 46360

---

## Text

The average daily attendance of individuals with exceptional needs given instruction by a county superintendent of schools and whose attendance is credited to the county school service fund, shall be computed by dividing the total days of attendance of such pupils during the fiscal year by 175.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 395.)
