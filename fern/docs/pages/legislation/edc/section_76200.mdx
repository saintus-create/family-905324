# EDC § 76200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 47. STUDENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.5. Student Records > Article: 1. Legislative Intent

**Citation:** EDC § 76200

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to resolve potential conflicts between California law and the provisions of Public Law 93-380 regarding the confidentiality of student records in order to insure the continuance of federal education funds to public community colleges within the state, and to revise generally and update the law relating to such records.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
