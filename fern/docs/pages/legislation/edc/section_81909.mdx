# EDC § 81909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Revenue Bond Act of 1961

**Citation:** EDC § 81909

---

## Text

An indenture may provide that payments of principal and interest of bonds shall be secured by all or by part of revenues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 251, Sec. 28. Effective June 11, 1982. Operative July 1, 1982, by Sec. 47 of Ch. 251.)
