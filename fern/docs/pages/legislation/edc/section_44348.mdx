# EDC § 44348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 8. Certificates and Credentials

**Citation:** EDC § 44348

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically required in this chapter, the commission shall establish regulations pertaining to the expiration dates of initially issued and renewed credentials.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
