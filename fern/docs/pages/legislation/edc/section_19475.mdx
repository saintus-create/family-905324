# EDC § 19475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 19475

---

## Text

All money acquired by gift, devise, bequest, or otherwise, for the purposes of the library, shall be paid into the county treasury to the credit of the library fund of the district, subject only to the order of the library trustees of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
