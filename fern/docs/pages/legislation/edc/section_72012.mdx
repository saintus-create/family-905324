# EDC § 72012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Nondiscrimination

**Citation:** EDC § 72012

---

## Text

Every community college shall comply with Section 66016, the Equity in Higher Education Act as set forth in Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 66250) of Part 40 of Division 5, and other applicable laws relating to discrimination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 51. Effective January 1, 2008.)
