# EDC § 35026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Districts > Article: 3. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 35026

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district employing eight or more teachers may employ a district superintendent for one or more schools and may delegate to the district superintendent any of the duties provided for in Section 35250.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
