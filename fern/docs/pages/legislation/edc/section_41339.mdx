# EDC § 41339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 3. Disbursements by Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 41339

---

## Text

The Superintendent shall certify each apportionment made by him or her under Sections 41332 to 41340, inclusive, whichever are in effect, to the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 186, Sec. 42. (AB 2659) Effective January 1, 2017.)
