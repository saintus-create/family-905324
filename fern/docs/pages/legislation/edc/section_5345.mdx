# EDC § 5345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 3. Consolidation of Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5345

---

## Text

In the event of consolidation of any school district election or community college district election with any other election the provisions of law governing such other election with respect to the manner of marking ballots shall also apply to the district election consolidated therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
