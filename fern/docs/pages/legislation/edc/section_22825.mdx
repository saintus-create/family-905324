# EDC § 22825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.2. Out-of-State Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22825

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 1999.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (January 1, 1999) for Chapter 14.2, commencing with Section 22820.)
