# EDC § 52325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1. Regional Occupational Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 52325

---

## Text

A day of attendance for pupils enrolled in a regional occupational center or program is 180 minutes of attendance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 730, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2008.)
