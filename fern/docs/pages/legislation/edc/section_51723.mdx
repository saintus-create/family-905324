# EDC § 51723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 3. Evening High School for Minors

**Citation:** EDC § 51723

---

## Text

The attendance of a minor at an evening high school program shall be voluntary, but the written permission of the minor’s parent or guardian is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
