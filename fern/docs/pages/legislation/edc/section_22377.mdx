# EDC § 22377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. System Headquarters

**Citation:** EDC § 22377

---

## Text

The board may contract with the Department of General Services or any other state agency for assistance in the acquisition of real property and any construction thereon of buildings or improvements authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
