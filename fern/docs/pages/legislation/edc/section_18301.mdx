# EDC § 18301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 18301

---

## Text

“Library,” “library district,” or “library districts” as used in this chapter mean “union high school library district.”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
