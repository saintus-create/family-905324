# EDC § 89529.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 1.2. Nonindustrial Disability Insurance

**Citation:** EDC § 89529.23

---

## Text

Filing, determination, and payment of disability benefit claims under this article shall be made in accordance with the procedures prescribed by Article 4 (commencing with Section 2701) of Chapter 2 of Part 2 of Division 1 of the Unemployment Insurance Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 670, Sec. 3.)
