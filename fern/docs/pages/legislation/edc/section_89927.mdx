# EDC § 89927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 2. Meetings, Elections, and Judicial Determinations

**Citation:** EDC § 89927

---

## Text

Each member of a governing board pursuant to this article who attends a meeting of the governing board where action is taken in violation of any provision of this article, with knowledge of the fact that the meeting is in violation of this article, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1158, Sec. 2.)
