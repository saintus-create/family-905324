# EDC § 66600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66600

---

## Text

The California State University shall be administered by a board designated as the Trustees of the California State University, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 638, Sec. 1.)
