# EDC § 18570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 16. Payment of Interest and Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18570

---

## Text

The principal and interest on the bonds shall be paid by the county treasurer, upon the warrant of the county auditor, out of the fund provided therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
