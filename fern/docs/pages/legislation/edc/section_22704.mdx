# EDC § 22704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22704

---

## Text

In any school district that is conducting a year-round school operation or a continuous school program, service shall be credited to the school year in which the service began.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
