# EDC § 33052.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 3. Waiver Authority

**Citation:** EDC § 33052.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “school district” shall include county offices of education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1302, Sec. 1.5. Effective September 30, 1983.)
