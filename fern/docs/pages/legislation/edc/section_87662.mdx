# EDC § 87662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 4. Evaluations and Discipline

**Citation:** EDC § 87662

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 72411 and subdivision (i) of Section 87663, the provisions of this article do not apply to administrators employed pursuant to Section 72411.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 113. Effective September 25, 1990.)
