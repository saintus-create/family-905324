# EDC § 37693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Year-Round Scheduling > Article: 2. Concept 6 Class Scheduling

**Citation:** EDC § 37693

---

## Text

(a) A Concept 6 program may not be operated after July 1, 2012, or such earlier date as may be prescribed by the Legislature pursuant to Section 37692.

(b) Although the Concept 6 program is authorized until July 1, 2012, it is the intent of the Legislature that all school districts eliminate the Concept 6 program as soon as practicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 901, Sec. 3. Effective September 29, 2004.)
