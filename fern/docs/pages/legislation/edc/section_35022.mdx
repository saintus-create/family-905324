# EDC § 35022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Districts > Article: 3. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 35022

---

## Text

Every school district governing board consisting of five or more members shall, at its initial meeting and at each annual meeting, elect a president from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
