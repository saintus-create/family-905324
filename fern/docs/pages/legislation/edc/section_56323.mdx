# EDC § 56323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 2. Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 56323

---

## Text

Admission of a pupil to special education instruction shall be made only in accordance with this article, Article 2.5 (commencing with Section 56333) and standards established by the board and upon a recommendation by the individualized education program team.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 797, Sec. 9. Effective July 28, 1980.)
