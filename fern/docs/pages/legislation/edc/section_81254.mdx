# EDC § 81254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 81254

---

## Text

The Chancellor of the California Community Colleges shall annually report to the Governor and Legislature on the number, types, and disposition of waiver requests submitted pursuant to Section 81250 on or before July 1 for the prior year actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 386, Sec. 15. (AB 1182) Effective January 1, 2010.)
