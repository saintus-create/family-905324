# EDC § 17571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Property Maintenance and Control > Article: 1. Duties of Governing Board

**Citation:** EDC § 17571

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may install and maintain a lighting system in any underpass in the vicinity of a schoolhouse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
