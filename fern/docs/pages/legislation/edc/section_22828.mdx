# EDC § 22828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.2. Out-of-State Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22828

---

## Text

A request to purchase out-of-state service credit pursuant to Section 22827 must be received no later than June 30, 2009.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 755, Sec. 22. (SB 1220) Effective January 1, 2015.)
