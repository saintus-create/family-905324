# EDC § 25921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13.5. HEALTH CARE BENEFITS PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 25921

---

## Text

“Employer” means the state or any agency or political subdivision thereof for which creditable service subject to coverage by the plan, as defined in Section 22155.5, is performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 803, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2002.)
