# EDC § 85300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Expenditures and Appropriations > Article: 8. Optional Method of Registering Warrants

**Citation:** EDC § 85300

---

## Text

In lieu of the method provided in Article 7 (commencing with Section 85280) of this chapter, inclusive, for the registration of community college district orders, the provisions of this article may be followed upon resolution adopted by the county board of supervisors of any county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
