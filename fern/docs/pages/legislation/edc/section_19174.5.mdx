# EDC § 19174.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 3. Government

**Citation:** EDC § 19174.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, funds from the county general fund may be used to support the county free library.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 331.)
