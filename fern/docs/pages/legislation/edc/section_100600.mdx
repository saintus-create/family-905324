# EDC § 100600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68.1. KINDERGARTEN–UNIVERSITY PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2002 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** EDC § 100600

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Kindergarten–University Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 2002.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 33, Sec. 30. Approved in Proposition 47 at the November 5, 2002, election.)
