# EDC § 14222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 9. FINANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Local Taxation by School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 2. Taxes on School Districts Located in Two or More Counties

**Citation:** EDC § 14222

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county, after receiving the certificate of the assessor, shall thereupon determine the rate of taxation necessary to be levied upon the property in the school district or community college district situated in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
