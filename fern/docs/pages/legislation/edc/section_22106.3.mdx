# EDC § 22106.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22106.3

---

## Text

“Base hours” means the number of hours of creditable service required to earn one year of service credit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 22106.2 by Stats. 2014, Ch. 755, Sec. 3. (SB 1220) Effective January 1, 2015.)
