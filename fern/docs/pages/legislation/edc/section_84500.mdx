# EDC § 84500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Attendance

**Citation:** EDC § 84500

---

## Text

Attendance shall be recorded and kept according to rules and regulations prescribed by the board of governors, subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 679.)
