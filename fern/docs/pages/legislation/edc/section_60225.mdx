# EDC § 60225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Elementary School Materials > Article: 2. Duties of Publishers and Manufacturers

**Citation:** EDC § 60225

---

## Text

As agreed upon by publishers and school districts, publishers may provide in-service training or professional development in the use of the instructional materials provided by them.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 413, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1996.)
