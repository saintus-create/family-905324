# EDC § 41962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 16. Allowances for Severance Aid

**Citation:** EDC § 41962

---

## Text

School districts may apply for severance aid as provided in this article on forms provided by the Superintendent, and in accordance with regulations that the Superintendent is authorized to adopt.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 29. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
