# EDC § 37620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Continuous School Programs > Article: 2. Establishment and Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 37620

---

## Text

The teaching sessions and vacation periods established pursuant to Section 37618 shall be established without reference to the school year as defined in Section 37200. The schools and classes shall be conducted for a total of no fewer than 175 days during the academic year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
