# EDC § 90065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90065

---

## Text

The board may insure against loss of revenues from any cause whatsoever and the proceeds of any insurance shall be used for the payment of bonds or notes and the interest thereon, or for other purposes as may be provided in an indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 315, Sec. 2. Effective July 19, 1983.)
