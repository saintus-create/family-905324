# EDC § 18745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 5. Library System Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18745

---

## Text

Each system shall annually apply to the state board for funds for intrasystem communications and delivery and resource sharing. Proposals shall be based upon the most cost-effective methods of exchanging print and digital materials and information among the member libraries.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 9. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
