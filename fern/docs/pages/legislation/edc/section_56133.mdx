# EDC § 56133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 56133

---

## Text

The superintendent shall provide for the mediation conference prescribed by Sections 56502 and 56503 and the state hearing prescribed by Section 56505.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1353, Sec. 38. Effective September 30, 1980.)
