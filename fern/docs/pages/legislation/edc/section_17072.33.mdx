# EDC § 17072.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 5. New Construction Funding Process

**Citation:** EDC § 17072.33

---

## Text

In the case of site acquisition, a district may request that the state’s share of site assistance be provided to the district in amounts equal to the amount of the local match when the district enters escrow for a site included within a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 4. Effective August 27, 1998.)
