# EDC § 39831.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Transportation Services > Article: 3. Schoolbuses

**Citation:** EDC § 39831.2

---

## Text

The state board shall adopt regulations to require a passenger in a school pupil activity bus equipped with passenger restraint systems in accordance with Section 27316.5 of the Vehicle Code to use a passenger restraint system so that the passenger is properly restrained by that system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 38047.6 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 76. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
