# EDC § 44218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 2. Commission on Teacher Credentialing

**Citation:** EDC § 44218

---

## Text

The commission by majority vote of all its members shall elect its own chairman from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
