# EDC § 17012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. State School Building Lease-Purchase Law of 1976 > Article: 2. Projects

**Citation:** EDC § 17012

---

## Text

The board has full charge of the acquisition, construction, completion, and control of all projects authorized by them and may proceed with such work forthwith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
