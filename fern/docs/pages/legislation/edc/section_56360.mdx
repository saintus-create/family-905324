# EDC § 56360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 4. Implementation

**Citation:** EDC § 56360

---

## Text

Each special education local plan area shall ensure that a continuum of program options is available to meet the needs of individuals with exceptional needs for special education and related services, as required by the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. Sec. 1400 et seq.) and federal regulations relating thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 854, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1998.)
