# EDC § 79021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. College Terms > Article: 3. Holidays and Observances

**Citation:** EDC § 79021

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, whenever the Governor appoints any other day as a holiday and does not provide for the closing of the community colleges, but the community colleges do close, the closing shall be deemed a closing for a holiday declared by the governing board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 513.7.)
