# EDC § 52336.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1.7. Career Preparatory Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52336.1

---

## Text

Any pupil who has successfully completed his or her education through grade 10, with the written consent of his or her parent or guardian, may choose to follow either a traditional college preparatory curriculum or a career preparatory program established pursuant to Section 52336.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1180, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
