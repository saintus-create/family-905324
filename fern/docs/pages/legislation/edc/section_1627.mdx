# EDC § 1627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. County School Service Fund > Article: 2. County Office of Education Budget Approval

**Citation:** EDC § 1627

---

## Text

The county school service fund shall be audited annually by a public accountant or a certified public accountant selected by the county superintendent of schools. The cost of the audit shall be a legal charge against the county school service fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1213, Sec. 3.)
