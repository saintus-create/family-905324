# EDC § 16201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 3. School Housing Aid for Exceptional Children

**Citation:** EDC § 16201

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 1 (commencing with Section 16000) and Article 2 (commencing with Section 16150) of this chapter, the obligation of any district receiving an apportionment under this article to repay the apportionment shall not extend to more than one-half of the amount of the apportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
