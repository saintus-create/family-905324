# EDC § 41407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 6. Teaching and Nonteaching Certificated Employee Ratios

**Citation:** EDC § 41407

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a school district is subject, with regard to Section 41402, to audits conducted pursuant to Section 41020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 21. Effective September 30, 2002.)
