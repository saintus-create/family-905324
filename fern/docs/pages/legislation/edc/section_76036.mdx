# EDC § 76036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 47. STUDENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Removal, Suspension, or Expulsion

**Citation:** EDC § 76036

---

## Text

Any violation or violations of law, ordinance, regulation, or rule regulating, or pertaining to, the parking of vehicles, shall not be cause for the removal, suspension, or expulsion of a student from a community college.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1032, Sec. 2.)
