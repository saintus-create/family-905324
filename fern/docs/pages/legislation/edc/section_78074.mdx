# EDC § 78074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 6. Support Services for Justice-Involved Students

**Citation:** EDC § 78074

---

## Text

The board of governors may authorize the chancellor, consistent with the requirements the board may impose, to designate up to 5 percent of the funds allocated pursuant to this article for program administration, program development, and program accountability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 558, Sec. 2. (AB 417) Effective January 1, 2022.)
