# EDC § 59111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California School for the Blind > Article: 2. Teaching Force

**Citation:** EDC § 59111

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the superintendent of the school are such as are assigned by the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 56. Effective September 21, 1992.)
