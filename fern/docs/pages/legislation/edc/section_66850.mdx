# EDC § 66850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10.5. Student Civic and Voter Empowerment Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Student Civic and Voter Empowerment Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 559, Sec. 2. (AB 963) Effective January 1, 2020.)
