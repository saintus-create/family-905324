# EDC § 42950.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11.5. Financial Support for Educating Foreign Children

**Citation:** EDC § 42950.4

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that school districts shall not identify pupils according to their immigration status in carrying out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 247.)
