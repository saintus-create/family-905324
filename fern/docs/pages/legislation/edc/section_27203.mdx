# EDC § 27203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Termination Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27203

---

## Text

A participant may not apply for a termination benefit under this part if less than five years have elapsed following the date the most recent termination benefit was distributed to the participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 300. Effective January 1, 1999.)
