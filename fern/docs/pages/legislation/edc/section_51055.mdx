# EDC § 51055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 4. Enforcement of Courses of Study

**Citation:** EDC § 51055

---

## Text

Courses for continuation high schools shall be prepared under the direction of the governing board having control thereof. Insofar as possible courses shall meet the high school requirements set forth in Sections 51220 and 51222.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
