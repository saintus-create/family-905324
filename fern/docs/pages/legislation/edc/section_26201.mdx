# EDC § 26201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26201

---

## Text

Investment earnings shall be collected by the Treasurer, and together with any other moneys received in connection with the Cash Balance Benefit Program, shall be immediately deposited to the credit of the Teachers’ Retirement Fund and reported to the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 1999.)
