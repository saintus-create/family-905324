# EDC § 7057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 5. PUBLIC SCHOOL PERSONNEL > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Personnel General Provisions > Article: 2. Political Activities of School Officers and Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 7057

---

## Text

No person who is in the classified service or who is upon any eligibility list shall be appointed, demoted, or removed, or in any way discriminated against because of his political acts, opinions, or affiliations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
