# EDC § 44105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 4. Affirmative Action Employment

## Text

The State Board of Education shall adopt all necessary rules and regulations to carry out the intent of this article.
