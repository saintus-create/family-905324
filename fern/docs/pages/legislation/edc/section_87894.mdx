# EDC § 87894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 11. Full-time Faculty and Faculty Diversity

**Citation:** EDC § 87894

---

## Text

This article shall only apply to a community college district as a condition of receiving funds for the purpose of hiring new full-time faculty appropriated in Item 6870-101-0001 of Section 2.00 of the annual Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 50, Sec. 13. (SB 117) Effective July 10, 2023.)
