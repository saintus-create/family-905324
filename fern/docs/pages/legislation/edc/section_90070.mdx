# EDC § 90070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90070

---

## Text

The board may include in an indenture those limitations as to competitive projects, both as to location and comparative rentals, as may be deemed necessary or desirable for the security of revenue bonds issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 740, Sec. 29.)
