# EDC § 81563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 17. Los Angeles Community College District Affordable Housing Pilot Program

**Citation:** EDC § 81563

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2033, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 572, Sec. 1. (SB 330) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 17, commencing with Section 81560.)
