# EDC § 212.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 212.3

---

## Text

“Religion” includes all aspects of religious belief, observance, and practice and includes agnosticism and atheism.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2008.)
