# EDC § 66260.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 66260.5

---

## Text

“Disability” includes mental and physical disability as defined in Section 12926 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 2008.)
