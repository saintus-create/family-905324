# EDC § 23005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23005

---

## Text

Monthly reports are due in the office of the system 30 calendar days immediately following the month in which the compensation being reported under this part was earned, and are delinquent 15 calendar days immediately thereafter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 122. Effective January 1, 1999.)
