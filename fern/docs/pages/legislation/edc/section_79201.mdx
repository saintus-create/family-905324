# EDC § 79201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 5. CalWORKs Recipients Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 79201

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this article to assist CalWORKs recipients prepare for employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 17. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
