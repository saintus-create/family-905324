# EDC § 16191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 3. School Housing Aid for Exceptional Children

**Citation:** EDC § 16191

---

## Text

As used in this article, “exceptional children” means physically handicapped pupils, pupils with intellectual disabilities, educationally handicapped pupils, multihandicapped pupils, or pupils enrolled in development centers for the handicapped required or allowed to be educated pursuant to Part 30 (commencing with Section 56000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 457, Sec. 6. (SB 1381) Effective January 1, 2013.)
