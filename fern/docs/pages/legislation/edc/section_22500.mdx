# EDC § 22500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Membership

**Citation:** EDC § 22500

---

## Text

All persons who were members of the California State Teachers’ Retirement System on June 30, 1996, are members of the Defined Benefit Program under the plan, in accordance with Section 401(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1025, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2001.)
