# EDC § 92206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Special Colleges > Article: 1 A. Law College Affiliated with the University of California

**Citation:** EDC § 92206

---

## Text

Vacancies occurring in the board of directors shall be filled by the Governor and approved by the Senate, a majority of the membership concurring.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 478, Sec. 24. (AB 1936) Effective January 1, 2023.)
