# EDC § 7052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 5. PUBLIC SCHOOL PERSONNEL > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Personnel General Provisions > Article: 2. Political Activities of School Officers and Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 7052

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, or as necessary to meet requirements of federal law as it pertains to a particular employee or employees, no restriction shall be placed on the political activities of any officer or employee of a local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
