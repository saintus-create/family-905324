# EDC § 66750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9.5. Cross-Enrollment

**Citation:** EDC § 66750

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Home campus” means the campus at which the student is matriculated.

(b) “Host campus” means the campus to which the student seeks access.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 552, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
