# EDC § 39831.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Transportation Services > Article: 3. Schoolbuses

**Citation:** EDC § 39831.1

---

## Text

The state board shall adopt regulations to require a passenger in a schoolbus equipped with passenger restraint systems in accordance with Section 27316 of the Vehicle Code to use a passenger restraint system so that the passenger is properly restrained by that system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 38047.5 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 75. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
