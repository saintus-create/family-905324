# EDC § 66604.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66604.5

---

## Text

Each appointive trustee shall receive actual and necessary travel expenses and one hundred dollars ($100) for each day he or she is attending to official business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 954, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
