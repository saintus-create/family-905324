# EDC § 60042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 3. Requirements, Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 60042

---

## Text

When adopting instructional materials for use in the schools, governing boards shall require such materials as they deem necessary and proper to encourage thrift, fire prevention and the humane treatment of animals and people.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
