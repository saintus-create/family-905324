# EDC § 22218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** EDC § 22218

## Text

The board shall establish and maintain records and accounts following recognized accounting principles and controls.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 1997.)
