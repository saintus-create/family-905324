# EDC § 35160.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 35160.2

---

## Text

For the purposes of Section 35160, “school district” shall include county superintendents of schools and county boards of education.

This section shall be interpreted to be declaratory of existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1124, Sec. 3.)
