# EDC § 74292.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous > Article: 5. Continuing Services If Compton Community College Loses Accreditation

**Citation:** EDC § 74292.7

---

## Text

Cal Grant awards to students of the Compton Community College District shall not be adversely affected by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 50, Sec. 11. Effective June 30, 2006.)
