# EDC § 45392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 9.5. Professional Development for Classified School Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 45392

---

## Text

Nothing in this article prohibits a local educational agency from providing professional development to teachers and administrators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 723, Sec. 1. (SB 590) Effective January 1, 2014.)
