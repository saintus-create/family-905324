# EDC § 69654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 8. Community College Extended Opportunity Programs and Services

**Citation:** EDC § 69654

---

## Text

The board shall review the need for state funds to carry out the purposes of this chapter and shall include an estimate of such need in its budget for each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
