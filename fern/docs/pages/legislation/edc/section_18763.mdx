# EDC § 18763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 6. Statewide Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18763

---

## Text

The budget of any reference center established pursuant to Section 18760 may include funds for the general operations of such centers, including funds for collection development and use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1255.)
