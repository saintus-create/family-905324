# EDC § 74102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Initiation of Petition and Local Review Procedures > Article: 1. Transfer

**Citation:** EDC § 74102

---

## Text

In any petition to reorganize districts by transfer, there shall be designated no more than three of the petitioners as chief petitioners for the purpose of receiving notice of any public hearings to be held on the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
