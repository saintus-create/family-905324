# EDC § 51200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 51200

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, the provisions contained in this chapter are the requirements for courses of study in grades 1 through 12.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
