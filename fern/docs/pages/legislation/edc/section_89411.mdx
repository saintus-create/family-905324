# EDC § 89411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers > Article: 2. San Francisco State University

**Citation:** EDC § 89411

---

## Text

The trustees shall determine the fee which the state university shall charge the parents or guardian of any child admitted to the center. All fees shall be used to reimburse the state university for the cost of operating and maintaining the center.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
