# EDC § 58504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 31. EXPERIMENTAL SCHOOL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Alternative Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 58504

---

## Text

Previous classroom performance shall not be a criterion limiting any student from the opportunity of attending an alternative school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
