# EDC § 21198

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 12. FOUNDATIONS AND STATE COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Foundations, Trusts, and Institutions of Arts and Sciences > Article: 3. Legal Validation Proceedings

**Citation:** EDC § 21198

---

## Text

Every material statement of the petition not specifically controverted by the answer shall, for the purposes of the special proceeding, be taken as true, and each person failing to answer the petition shall be deemed to admit as true all the material allegations of the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
