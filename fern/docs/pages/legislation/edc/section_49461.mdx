# EDC § 49461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 4.5. Pupil Health Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 49461

---

## Text

The coordinating center at the California State University shall select a sample of schools that is demographically and ethnically representative of the state’s population to participate in the health assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 430, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
