# EDC § 89451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.8. California State University, Stanislaus:  Reuse of Stockton Development Center

**Citation:** EDC § 89451

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Stockton Development Center be a productive asset focused on educationally based programs. Further, it is the intent of the Legislature to supplement state resources in site maintenance through public-public and public-private partnerships.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1020, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
