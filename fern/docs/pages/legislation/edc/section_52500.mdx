# EDC § 52500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 52500

---

## Text

Adult schools and evening high schools shall consist of classes for adults. Minors may be admitted to those classes pursuant to board policy and Section 52500.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1193, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 6 of Ch. 1193.)
