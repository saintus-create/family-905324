# EDC § 35112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 1.5. Common Governing Boards

**Citation:** EDC § 35112

---

## Text

When a governing board has adopted a resolution pursuant to Section 35110, the school districts under its jurisdiction may transfer funds between the districts to the extent permitted by Section 6 of Article XVI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 22, Sec. 1.)
