# EDC § 87824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87824

---

## Text

When the payroll form of warrant is used, the approved and allowed payroll warrant shall be deposited with the county treasurer, who shall make payment to the employee or his order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
