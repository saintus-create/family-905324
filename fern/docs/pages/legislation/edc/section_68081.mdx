# EDC § 68081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 6. Exceptions to Residence Determination

**Citation:** EDC § 68081

---

## Text

A student who is enrolled in a state government legislative, executive, or judicial fellowship program administered by the state or the California State University is entitled to resident classification at the California State University during the period of the fellowship.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 896, Sec. 66. Effective September 29, 2004.)
