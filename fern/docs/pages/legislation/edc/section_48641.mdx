# EDC § 48641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Attendance at Adjustment Schools > Article: 2. Opportunity Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 48641

---

## Text

An opportunity school established by a county board of education in a county which has an average daily attendance of less than 8,000 shall be deemed to be in lieu of any continuation education program, and shall excuse the school districts within the county from complying with otherwise applicable statutes requiring the establishment of continuation education schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010. Note: The Jan. 1, 2006, article repeal date (in former Section 48642) was made inapplicable to this section by Stats. 2005, Ch. 118, Sec. 10.)
