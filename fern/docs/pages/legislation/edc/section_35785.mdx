# EDC § 35785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 5. Lapsation

**Citation:** EDC § 35785

---

## Text

Following the order of the county committee, the county superintendent of schools shall give notice of the board action to the county board of supervisors and to the State Board of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
