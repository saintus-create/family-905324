# EDC § 62004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 34. EVALUATION AND SUNSETTING OF PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 62004

---

## Text

The State Auditor shall audit, on a sampling basis, school districts’ use of the funds specified in Section 62002.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 107, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2004.)
