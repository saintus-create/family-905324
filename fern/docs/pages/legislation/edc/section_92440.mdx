# EDC § 92440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 2. Powers of Regents

**Citation:** EDC § 92440

---

## Text

The regents may adopt such rules and regulations as may be necessary to enable the regents to exercise the powers and to perform the duties conferred or imposed upon the regents by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
