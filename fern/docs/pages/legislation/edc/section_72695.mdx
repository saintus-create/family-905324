# EDC § 72695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 7. The Richard McKee Transparency Act of 2011

**Citation:** EDC § 72695

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall require an auxiliary organization to disclose information that is exempt from disclosure pursuant to an exemption set forth in Section 7922.000 of the Government Code or in any provision listed in Section 7920.505 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 76. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
