# EDC § 56190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 7. Community Advisory Committee

**Citation:** EDC § 56190

---

## Text

Each plan submitted under Section 56195.1 shall establish a community advisory committee. The committee shall serve only in an advisory capacity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 854, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1998.)
