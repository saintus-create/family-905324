# EDC § 85280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Expenditures and Appropriations > Article: 7. Registration of Orders, Issuance of Warrants

**Citation:** EDC § 85280

---

## Text

When any order against the funds of a community college district is presented to the county superintendent of schools, and the order constitutes a valid claim against the funds of the district, and moneys are not available in the funds of the district from which to pay the order, the county superintendent shall endorse on the order the words “Not approved for want of funds,” and shall register the order in the records of his or her office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 654, Sec. 33. Effective October 7, 2005.)
