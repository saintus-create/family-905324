# EDC § 1299

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 3. Staff

**Citation:** EDC § 1299

---

## Text

A supervisor of health employed by the county superintendent of schools shall perform such duties in connection with the supervision of the health of pupils as are prescribed by the county superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
