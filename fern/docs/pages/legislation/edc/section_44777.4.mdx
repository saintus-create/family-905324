# EDC § 44777.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.66. Latino Heritage Resource Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 44777.4

---

## Text

Funds allocated to regional Latino heritage resource centers pursuant to Section 44777.3 shall be used by the centers only for initial development costs of exhibits, collections, and teacher training materials in Latino heritage, encompassing history, art, and culture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 308, Sec. 16. Effective August 3, 1995.)
