# EDC § 15140.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Bonds of School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 3. Issuance and Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 15140.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “bonds” means bonds, notes, warrants, or other evidence of indebtedness payable, both principal and interest, from the proceeds of ad valorem property taxes that may be levied without limitation as to rate or amount upon property subject to taxation by the governing board of the school district or community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 477, Sec. 1. (AB 182) Effective January 1, 2014.)
