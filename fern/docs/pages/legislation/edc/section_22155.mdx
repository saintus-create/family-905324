# EDC § 22155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22155

---

## Text

“Payroll” includes registers, warrants, and any other documents upon which the employer identifies persons to whom compensation is paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 482, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1998.)
