# EDC § 32330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Miscellaneous > Article: 4. Use of County Funds for Agriculture

**Citation:** EDC § 32330

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors of any county may appropriate and use county funds for the support and maintenance of extension work in agriculture and home economics. The extension work shall be designed for the benefit of the respective counties and conducted under the approval of the United States Department of Agriculture and in cooperation with the University of California.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
