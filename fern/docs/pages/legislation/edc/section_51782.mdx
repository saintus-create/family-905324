# EDC § 51782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 7.5. Genetic Diseases and Disorders Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 51782

---

## Text

The instructional program in genetic diseases and disorders shall provide pupils with practical information concerning, but not limited to, the following elements:

(a) Genetics.

(b) Maternal and prenatal health care.

(c) Transmission of genetic diseases and disorders.

(d) Human development.

(e) Nutrition.

(f) Effects of environmental factors on human development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 685, Sec. 1. Effective September 23, 1981.)
