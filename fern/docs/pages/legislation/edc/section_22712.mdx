# EDC § 22712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22712

---

## Text

A member under this part shall receive credit for time served as an exchange teacher in any location.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 83. Effective January 1, 1999.)
