# EDC § 35501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 1. Legislative Intent and Purpose

**Citation:** EDC § 35501

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 1981, this chapter and Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 35700) shall apply to an action to reorganize school districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1186, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
