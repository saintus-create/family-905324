# EDC § 92614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 2.5. Retirement Plan for Employees Excluded from theUniversity of California Retirement System

**Citation:** EDC § 92614

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 92613 shall supersede Sections 3560 to 3599, inclusive, of the Government Code and any memorandum of understanding entered into pursuant to those sections, for any employees hired or rehired on or after the effective date of this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 986, Sec. 1. Effective September 28, 1992.)
