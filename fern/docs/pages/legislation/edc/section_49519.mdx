# EDC § 49519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 9. Nutrition

**Citation:** EDC § 49519

---

## Text

Each meal provided under this article shall include an appropriate serving of milk; provided, that the provisions of this section shall not be mandatory in the case of demonstration projects conducted pursuant to Section 49514.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
