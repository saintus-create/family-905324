# EDC § 1200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 1. Appointment, Qualifications, Salary and Expenses

**Citation:** EDC § 1200

---

## Text

Each county superintendent shall receive his actual and necessary traveling expenses. The expenses shall be allowed by the board of supervisors, and be paid out of the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
