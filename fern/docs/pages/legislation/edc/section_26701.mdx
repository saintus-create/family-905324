# EDC § 26701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Rights to Benefits

**Citation:** EDC § 26701

---

## Text

The right of a participant to a benefit under this part, whether by lump sum or annuity, is not subject to execution or any other process whatsoever, except to the extent permitted by Section 704.110 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and is unassignable except as specifically provided under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 1999.)
