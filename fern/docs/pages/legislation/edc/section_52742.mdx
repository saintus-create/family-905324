# EDC § 52742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Miscellaneous > Article: 3. Educational Films or Video Tapes

**Citation:** EDC § 52742

---

## Text

The films or video recordings produced pursuant to this article shall be submitted to the Curriculum Development and Supplemental Materials Commission for its review, and may be made available to schools, as provided by this article, only upon adoption by the Curriculum Development and Supplemental Materials Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 88, Sec. 26. (AB 176) Effective January 1, 2010.)
