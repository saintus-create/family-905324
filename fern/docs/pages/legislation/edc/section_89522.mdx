# EDC § 89522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 89522

---

## Text

All applications for appointment to academic, administrative and nonacademic positions shall include a place for listing volunteer experience and such experience shall be considered if it is relevant to the position being applied for.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 544.)
