# EDC § 87782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 7. Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 87782

---

## Text

(a) An academic employee of a community college district who has been an employee of that district for a period of one school year or more shall have transferred with the employee to a second district the total amount of leave of absence for illness or injury to which the employee is entitled under Section 87781.

(b) The board of governors shall adopt rules and regulations prescribing the manner in which the first district shall certify to the second district the total amount of leave of absence for illness or injury to be transferred. No governing board shall adopt any policy or rule, written or unwritten, that requires any employee transferring to its district to waive any part or all of the leave of absence that the employee may be entitled to have transferred in accordance with this section.
