# EDC § 4024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 2. Transfer of Duties of County Committee to County Board of Education

**Citation:** EDC § 4024

---

## Text

A county committee on school district organization reestablished pursuant to Section 4022 succeeds to and is vested with all the duties, powers, purposes, responsibilities, and jurisdiction previously transferred to the county board of education pursuant to Section 4021.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1263, Sec. 7.)
