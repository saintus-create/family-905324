# EDC § 60046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 3. Requirements, Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 60046

---

## Text

Any governing board may conduct an investigation of the compliance of any instructional materials which it adopts with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
