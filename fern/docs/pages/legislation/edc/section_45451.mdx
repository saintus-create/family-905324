# EDC § 45451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 12. Particular Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 45451

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district which employs school crossing guards pursuant to this article may be reimbursed from funds including, but not limited to, those collected pursuant to Sections 42200 and 42201 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 295.)
