# EDC § 24400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 29. Benefit Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 24400

---

## Text

The Legislature recognizes that inflation erodes the purchasing power of benefits paid under the plan under this part. It is the intent of the Legislature to understand the degree of erosion of these benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 728, Sec. 31. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2013.)
