# EDC § 8225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 2. Program Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 8225

---

## Text

A contractor providing California state preschool services is subject to the requirements of the Americans with Disabilities Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 12101, et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8250.5 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 53. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
