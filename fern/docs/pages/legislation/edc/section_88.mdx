# EDC § 88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 88

---

## Text

“State Board” or “state board” whenever used in this code means the State Board of Education, unless the context requires otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 118, Sec. 1. Effective July 25, 2005.)
