# EDC § 210.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 210.7

---

## Text

“Gender” means sex, and includes a person’s gender identity and gender expression. “Gender expression” means a person’s gender-related appearance and behavior whether or not stereotypically associated with the person’s assigned sex at birth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 719, Sec. 4. (AB 887) Effective January 1, 2012.)
