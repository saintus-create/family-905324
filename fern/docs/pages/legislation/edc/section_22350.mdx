# EDC § 22350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Investments

**Citation:** EDC § 22350

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that changing economic conditions and increasing complexity in the investment market make it necessary and desirable that the system obtain the best possible investment expertise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
