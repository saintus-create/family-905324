# EDC § 44665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 11. Evaluation and Assessment of Performance of Certificated Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 44665

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “employing authority” means the superintendent of the school district in which the employee is employed, or his designee, or in the case of a district which has no superintendent, a school principal or other person designated by the governing board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
