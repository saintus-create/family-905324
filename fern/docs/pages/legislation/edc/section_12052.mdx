# EDC § 12052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 5. Vocational Education Acts

**Citation:** EDC § 12052

---

## Text

In accepting the benefits of the acts of Congress referred to in Section 12050, the people of the state agree to comply with all of the provisions and to observe all of the requirements of those acts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1996.)
