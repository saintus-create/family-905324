# EDC § 8331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 14. Contract Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 8331

---

## Text

The department shall develop a grievance procedure for resolving disputes arising from the awarding or administering of contracts, in addition to the remedies provided under the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8445 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 225. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
