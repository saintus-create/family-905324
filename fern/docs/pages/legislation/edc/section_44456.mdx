# EDC § 44456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Teacher Education Internship Act of 1967

**Citation:** EDC § 44456

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 44455, an internship credential may be renewed by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing if in its judgment an applicant is unable to complete renewal requirements because of illness or other circumstances judged to be extenuating and not within the control of the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 342, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2002.)
