# EDC § 56130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 56130

---

## Text

The superintendent shall develop in accordance with Section 56602, a biennial performance report of special education programs authorized under this part for submission to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 49. Effective September 30, 2002.)
