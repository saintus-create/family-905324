# EDC § 39837.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Transportation Services > Article: 3. Schoolbuses

**Citation:** EDC § 39837.5

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may provide for the transportation on schoolbuses of employees of the school district, parents of pupils of the school district, and adult volunteers to and from educational activities authorized by the school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 173, Sec. 1. (AB 1453) Effective January 1, 2018.)
