# EDC § 69456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.7. Ortiz-Pacheco-Poochigian-Vasconcellos Cal Grant Program > Article: 8. Cal Grant Alternative Delivery System Pilot Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69456

---

## Text

All costs associated with developing, implementing, maintaining, and improving the pilot alternative delivery system shall be paid by the qualifying institutions that elect to participate in the pilot alternative delivery system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 644, Sec. 4. (AB 187) Effective November 5, 2009. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed in Section 69475 (if it becomes operative). Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 69475 (if it becomes operative).)
