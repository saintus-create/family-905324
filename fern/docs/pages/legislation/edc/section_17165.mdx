# EDC § 17165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. The Archie-Hudson and Cunneen School Technology Revenue Bond Act

**Citation:** EDC § 17165

---

## Text

The State Department of Education shall include in its annual survey of schools on the use of lottery funds, the amount of lottery revenues expended to repay bonds issued pursuant to this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
