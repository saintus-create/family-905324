# EDC § 9004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. School Nutrition > Article: 2. Garden Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 9004

---

## Text

The department shall select grant recipients from the northern, southern, and central regions of the state and from urban, rural, and suburban areas, so that the recipients are broadly representative of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 61. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
