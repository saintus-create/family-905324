# EDC § 74016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Legislative Intent and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 74016

---

## Text

“Affected districts” means any component school district or nondistrict territory within the territory to be transferred, annexed, or formed, or both, or any community college district subject to an action to reorganize districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 964, Sec. 3.)
