# EDC § 88773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 53.7. Rebuilding Nursing Infrastructure Grant Program > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION

**Citation:** EDC § 88773

---

## Text

(a) This part shall become inoperative on July 1, 2030, and, as of January 1, 2031, is repealed.

(b) Any recipient that has not fully expended their grant by July 1, 2030, shall continue adhering to the requirements of this part that were operative on June 30, 2030, until the grant is expended or the encumbrance period described in subdivision (f) ends, whichever occurs first.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 71, Sec. 10. (SB 155) Effective July 2, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions. Repeal affects Part 53.7, commencing with Section 88770.)
