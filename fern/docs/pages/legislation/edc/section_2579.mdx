# EDC § 2579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. County Local Control Funding Formula

**Citation:** EDC § 2579

---

## Text

Commencing on July 1, 2013, all of the following shall apply:

(a) All references to Section 2558 shall instead refer to Section 2575.

(b) Unless the context otherwise requires, all references to the revenue limit of a county office of education or county superintendent of schools shall instead refer to the county local control funding formula.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 47, Sec. 2. (AB 97) Effective July 1, 2013.)
