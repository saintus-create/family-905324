# EDC § 44261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 4. Credential Types

**Citation:** EDC § 44261

---

## Text

The minimum requirements for the multiple or single subject teaching credential with a designated emphasis shall be as specified in Section 44259.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1245, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
