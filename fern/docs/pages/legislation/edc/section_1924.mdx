# EDC § 1924

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 15. Emergency Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 1924

---

## Text

The units of average daily attendance for a fiscal year in an emergency school maintained by the county superintendent of schools shall be computed by dividing the total number of days of pupils’ attendance in the emergency school during the fiscal year by 175.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
