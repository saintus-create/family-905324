# EDC § 68051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 4. Nonresident Tuition

**Citation:** EDC § 68051

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided by law, the governing board or district governing board shall adopt rules and regulations relating to the method of calculation of the amount of nonresident tuition, the method of payment, and the method and amount of refund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 238.)
