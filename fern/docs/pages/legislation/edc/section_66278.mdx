# EDC § 66278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education

**Citation:** EDC § 66278

---

## Text

In regard to admissions to educational institutions, this article shall not apply to any public institution of undergraduate higher education which traditionally and continually from its establishment has had a policy of admitting only students of one sex.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 228 by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1999.)
