# EDC § 45364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 8. Teacher Aides

**Citation:** EDC § 45364

---

## Text

All funds received by school districts under the provisions of this article shall be used for and restricted to the purpose of paying the salaries of teacher aides employed under the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
