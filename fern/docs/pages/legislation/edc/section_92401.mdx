# EDC § 92401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 92401

---

## Text

Wherever used or referred to in this chapter, or in any indenture entered into pursuant to this chapter, the definitions set forth in this article govern the construction of this chapter, unless a different meaning appears from the context.
