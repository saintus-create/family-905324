# EDC § 90014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90014

---

## Text

Notes authorized to be issued under this article shall be sold by the Treasurer, for cash, in the manner that the Treasurer shall be directed by a resolution requesting the sale adopted by the board. Notes in the form of commercial paper notes shall be sold in the manner specified in the indenture for the notes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 352, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
