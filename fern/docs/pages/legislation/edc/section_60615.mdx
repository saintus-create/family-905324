# EDC § 60615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. California Assessment of Academic Achievement > Article: 2. Program Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 60615

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a parent’s or guardian’s written request to school officials to excuse his or her child from any or all parts of the assessments administered pursuant to this chapter shall be granted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 975, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
