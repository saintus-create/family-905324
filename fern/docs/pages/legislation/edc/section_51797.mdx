# EDC § 51797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 8.5. School Instructional Gardens

**Citation:** EDC § 51797

---

## Text

During its annual discretionary grant funding process, the California Integrated Waste Management Board may give preferential consideration to providing an appropriate level of funding to the program established pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 49. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
