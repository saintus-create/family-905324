# EDC § 45020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 4. Increased Cost of Dismissal Procedure

**Citation:** EDC § 45020

---

## Text

The amount determined pursuant to subdivision (g) of Section 42233 shall be adjusted to allow for any increased costs to a school district which may be incurred by it in carrying out the requirements imposed by the amendments made to Section 44949 by the act enacting this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
