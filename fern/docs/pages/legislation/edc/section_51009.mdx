# EDC § 51009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 1. Legislative Intent

**Citation:** EDC § 51009

---

## Text

The month of May is hereby deemed to be Labor History Month throughout the public schools, and school districts are encouraged to commemorate this month with appropriate educational exercises that make pupils aware of the role the labor movement has played in shaping California and the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 584, Sec. 1. (AB 2269) Effective January 1, 2013.)
