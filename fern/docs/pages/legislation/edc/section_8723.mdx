# EDC § 8723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Environmental Education > Article: 2. Conservation Education Service

## Text

The Conservation Education Service shall have such other powers and duties as shall be vested in it by law.
