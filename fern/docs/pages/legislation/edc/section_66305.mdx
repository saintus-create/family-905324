# EDC § 66305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.1. Stop Campus Hazing Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Stop Campus Hazing Act.
