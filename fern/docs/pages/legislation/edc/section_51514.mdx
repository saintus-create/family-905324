# EDC § 51514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Prohibited Instruction > Article: 2. Prohibited Materials

**Citation:** EDC § 51514

---

## Text

If a local educational agency administers a voluntary survey that already includes questions pertaining to sexual orientation and gender identity, the local educational agency shall not remove those questions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 744, Sec. 2. (AB 677) Effective January 1, 2018.)
