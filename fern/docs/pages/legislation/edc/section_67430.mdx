# EDC § 67430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40.2. FINISH IN FOUR AND THROUGH IN TWO > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION

**Citation:** EDC § 67430

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Finish in Four and Through in Two program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 677, Sec. 2. (AB 1205) Effective January 1, 2025.)
