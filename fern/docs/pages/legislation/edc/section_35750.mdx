# EDC § 35750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 4. Approval and Adoption of Plans and Recommendations

**Citation:** EDC § 35750

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall establish minimum standards which it shall apply in approving or disapproving petitions and proposals for the formation or reorganization of school districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
