# EDC § 89070.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3.5. California Mathematics Diagnostic Testing Program

**Citation:** EDC § 89070.15

---

## Text

The California Mathematics Diagnostic Testing Program is hereby established. The Trustees of the California State University shall administer the program and in so doing shall cooperate with the University of California, the California Community Colleges, and the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 872, Sec. 2.)
