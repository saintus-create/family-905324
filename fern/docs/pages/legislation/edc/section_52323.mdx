# EDC § 52323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1. Regional Occupational Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 52323

---

## Text

No instruction shall be given in a regional occupational center except by a qualified teacher holding a certificate issued pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 44200) of Part 25 of Division 3 of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
