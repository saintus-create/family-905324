# EDC § 17512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 7. Leasing for Production of Gas

**Citation:** EDC § 17512

---

## Text

No well for the production of gas shall be drilled on any land owned by the district and leased pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
