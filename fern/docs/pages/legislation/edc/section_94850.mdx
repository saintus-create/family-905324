# EDC § 94850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Part: 59. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Private Postsecondary Institutions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 94850

---

## Text

“Noninstitutional charges” means charges for an educational program paid directly to an entity other than an institution that are specifically required for participation in an educational program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 79. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
