# EDC § 35166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 35166

---

## Text

The governing board of each unified school district shall have the same powers and duties as are by law granted to the governing boards of the elementary school districts, and high school districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
