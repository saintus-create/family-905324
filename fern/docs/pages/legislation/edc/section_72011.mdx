# EDC § 72011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Nondiscrimination

**Citation:** EDC § 72011

---

## Text

Every community college district shall provide access to its services, classes, and programs without regard to the characteristics listed in Section 66270.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2008.)
