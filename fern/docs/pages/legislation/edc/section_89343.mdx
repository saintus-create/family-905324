# EDC § 89343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Students > Article: 5. Higher Education Outreach and Assistance Act for Foster Youth

**Citation:** EDC § 89343

---

## Text

The Trustees of the California State University and Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges shall evaluate the extent to which their current programs are meeting the needs of foster youth and how those outreach and retention services can be improved.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 193, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2005.)
