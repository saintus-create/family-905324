# EDC § 87825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87825

---

## Text

When the payroll form of warrant is used the name of each employee shall be listed.
