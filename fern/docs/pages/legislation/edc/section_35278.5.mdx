# EDC § 35278.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 9.5. Schoolsite Replacement Housing

**Citation:** EDC § 35278.5

---

## Text

This article does not require a local governing agency to acquire real property, develop replacement housing, or perform any other act pursuant to this article. A local governing agency may, at its option, take action pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 574, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
