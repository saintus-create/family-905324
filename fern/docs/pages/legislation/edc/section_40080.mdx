# EDC § 40080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Specialized Vehicle Driver Training Courses

**Citation:** EDC § 40080

---

## Text

(a) This article governs the minimum training required for drivers to obtain or renew a certificate described in Section 12517, 12519, or 12804.6 of the Vehicle Code.

(b) As used in this article, “department” means the State Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 646, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2000.)
