# EDC § 52185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Bilingual Education > Article: 4. Bilingual Teacher Training Assistance Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52185

---

## Text

Agencies funded under Section 52182 to provide training services shall report annually to the Superintendent of Public Instruction the information required under Section 52184.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1461, Sec. 22.)
