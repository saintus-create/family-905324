# EDC § 59113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California School for the Blind > Article: 2. Teaching Force

**Citation:** EDC § 59113

---

## Text

The Department of Education may employ any person, otherwise qualified, who has retired for service under either the Public Employees’ Retirement System or the State Teachers’ Retirement System as a substitute in a position requiring certification qualifications at the California School for the Blind; provided, that the total of such service and any service rendered pursuant to Section 23919 shall not exceed 90 teaching days in any one fiscal year.
