# EDC § 1722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 3. Preparation of Courses of Studies

**Citation:** EDC § 1722

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools, with the approval of the county board of education, and in cooperation with school districts and community college districts and the State Department of Education, or the board of governors, according to jurisdiction may participate in projects for developmental program planning.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
