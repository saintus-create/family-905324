# EDC § 81951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Revenue Bond Act of 1961

**Citation:** EDC § 81951

---

## Text

At all times the operation, maintenance, control, repair, construction, reconstruction, alteration, and improvement of any project are vested in the board subject to authorized leases permitted by any indenture. The board shall comply with all applicable county and city zoning, building, and health regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 251, Sec. 28. Effective June 11, 1982. Operative July 1, 1982, by Sec. 47 of Ch. 251.)
