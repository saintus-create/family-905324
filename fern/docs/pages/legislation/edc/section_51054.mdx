# EDC § 51054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 4. Enforcement of Courses of Study

**Citation:** EDC § 51054

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 51053, the course of study for grades 7 through 12 shall be prepared under the direction of the governing board having control thereof and shall be subject to approval as may be required by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
