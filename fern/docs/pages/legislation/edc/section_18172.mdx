# EDC § 18172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 5. Use of Library Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 18172

---

## Text

All orders of the governing board of any school district for books or apparatus shall in every case be submitted to the superintendent of schools of the county, city, or city and county, respectively, for his or her approval, before the books or apparatus shall be purchased.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 171.)
