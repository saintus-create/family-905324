# EDC § 19345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 5. Local Public Library Partnership Program

**Citation:** EDC § 19345

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit a local public library from partnering with a local educational agency to issue student success cards to pupils in kindergarten or any of grades 1 to 12, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 598, Sec. 2. (SB 321) Effective January 1, 2024.)
