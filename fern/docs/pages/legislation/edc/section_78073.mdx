# EDC § 78073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 6. Support Services for Justice-Involved Students

**Citation:** EDC § 78073

---

## Text

The board of governors shall be responsible for the administration of funds for the Rising Scholars Network. To the extent possible, services provided under this article to justice-involved students shall be coordinated with, and shall not supplant, other services provided by the county and state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 558, Sec. 2. (AB 417) Effective January 1, 2022.)
