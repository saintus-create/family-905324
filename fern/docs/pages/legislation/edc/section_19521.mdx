# EDC § 19521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 19521

---

## Text

The bond election shall be called and conducted and the results thereof canvassed, returned, and declared in the manner provided in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 5300) of Part 4 of this division.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
