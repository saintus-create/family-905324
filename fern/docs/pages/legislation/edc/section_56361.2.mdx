# EDC § 56361.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 4. Implementation

**Citation:** EDC § 56361.2

---

## Text

All special education and related services for any individual with exceptional needs who is younger than three years of age shall be provided pursuant to Chapter 4.4 (commencing with Section 56425).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1296, Sec. 5.5. Operative July 1, 1987, by Sec. 25 of Ch. 1296.)
