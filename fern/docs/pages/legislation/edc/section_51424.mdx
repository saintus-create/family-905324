# EDC § 51424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 3. High School Equivalency Certificates

**Citation:** EDC § 51424

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall keep a permanent record of California high school equivalency certificates issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 163, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
