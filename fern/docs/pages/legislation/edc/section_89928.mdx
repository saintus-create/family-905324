# EDC § 89928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 2. Meetings, Elections, and Judicial Determinations

**Citation:** EDC § 89928

---

## Text

This article is not applicable to either of the following:

(a) Any entity formed or operating pursuant to Section 89300.

(b) The governing board of any statewide student organization that represents the students of the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 330, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
