# EDC § 89561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 3. California State University Police

**Citation:** EDC § 89561

---

## Text

Every member of a California State University police department shall be supplied with, and authorized to wear, a badge bearing the words “California State University Police.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 143, Sec. 136.)
