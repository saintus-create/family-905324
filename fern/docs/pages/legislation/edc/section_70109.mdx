# EDC § 70109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Nursing Assumption Program of Loans for Education (SNAPLE) > Article: 1. Nursing Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 70109

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, in any fiscal year, the commission shall award no more than the number of warrants that are authorized by the Governor and the Legislature in the annual Budget Act for that year for the assumption of loans pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 69616.9 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 837, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2007.)
