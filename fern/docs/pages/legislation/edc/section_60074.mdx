# EDC § 60074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 5. Prohibited Acts

**Citation:** EDC § 60074

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to prevent any publisher, manufacturer, or agent from supplying for purposes of examination necessary sample copies of instructional materials to any school official.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
