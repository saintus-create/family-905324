# EDC § 23301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 20. Beneficiary Designation

**Citation:** EDC § 23301

---

## Text

A corporation, trust, charitable organization, parochial institution, or public entity may be designated as a beneficiary under this part, but they shall not be designated as option beneficiaries, except a trust as defined in Section 22149.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 96, Sec. 16. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2020.)
