# EDC § 24107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 26. Disability Retirement

**Citation:** EDC § 24107

---

## Text

A member retired for disability under this part may elect an option pursuant to Section 24332 to modify the disability retirement allowance payable pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 24106.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 755, Sec. 29. (SB 1220) Effective January 1, 2015.)
