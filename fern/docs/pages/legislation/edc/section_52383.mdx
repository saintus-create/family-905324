# EDC § 52383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 5. Summer Vocational Education

**Citation:** EDC § 52383

---

## Text

Wages earned by pupils participating in a program of summer career technical and technical education, shall be paid weekly, or, if not reasonably possible, biweekly.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1058, Sec. 78. Effective January 1, 2001.)
