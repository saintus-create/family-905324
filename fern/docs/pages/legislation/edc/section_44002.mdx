# EDC § 44002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 44002

---

## Text

A “credential” includes a credential, certificate, life document, life diploma, permit, certificate of clearance, or waiver issued by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 342, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
