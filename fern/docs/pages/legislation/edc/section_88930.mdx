# EDC § 88930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 54.82. Community Colleges Student Success Completion Grant > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION

**Citation:** EDC § 88930

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “academic year” means the total of two consecutive semesters or three quarters, commencing with the opening of the fall term. Each semester or quarter is approximately the same length.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 33, Sec. 50. (AB 1809) Effective June 27, 2018.)
