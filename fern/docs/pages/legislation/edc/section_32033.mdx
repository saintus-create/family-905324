# EDC § 32033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. School Safety—Public and Private Institutions > Article: 3. School Eye Safety

**Citation:** EDC § 32033

---

## Text

The eye protective devices may be sold to the pupils and teachers or instructors at a price that shall not exceed the actual cost of the eye protective devices to the school or governing board.

This section shall become operative January 1, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010) by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1118, Sec. 2. Section operative January 1, 1992, by its own provisions.)
