# EDC § 92655.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 6.3. Health Professions Education and Outreach

**Citation:** EDC § 92655.1

---

## Text

The Legislature requests the Regents of the University of California, to the extent possible, to use existing resources to establish dental, medical, and optometric health professions outreach and exposure programs for elementary, high school, and undergraduate students, including community college students.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 92665.1 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 70. Effective January 1, 2003.)
