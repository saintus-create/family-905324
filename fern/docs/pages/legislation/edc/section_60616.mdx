# EDC § 60616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. California Assessment of Academic Achievement > Article: 2. Program Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 60616

---

## Text

Any achievement test adopted by the state board pursuant to this chapter may be reviewed by any Member of the Legislature or any member of the governing board of a school district, if the member agrees in writing prior to the review to maintain the confidentiality of the test.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 757, Sec. 14. Effective September 30, 2008.)
