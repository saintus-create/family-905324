# EDC § 19726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Library Districts and Museums in Unincorporated Towns and Villages > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 19726

---

## Text

The money shall be raised by taxation upon the taxable property in the district, for the redemption of the bonds and the payment of the interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
