# EDC § 33313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 33313

---

## Text

All moneys received from the sale of materials pursuant to Section 33310 of the Education Code shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the fund against which the cost of printing the publication was charged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1158, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1996.)
