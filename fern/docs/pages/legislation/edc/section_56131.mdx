# EDC § 56131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 56131

---

## Text

The superintendent shall apportion funds in accordance with Chapter 7.2 (commencing with Section 56836) and approved local plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 89, Sec. 18. Effective June 30, 1998. Operative July 1, 1998, by Sec. 62 of Ch. 89.)
