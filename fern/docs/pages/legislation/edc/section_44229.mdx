# EDC § 44229

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 2. Commission on Teacher Credentialing

**Citation:** EDC § 44229

---

## Text

The commission shall invite the public, the teaching profession, and interested professional groups and associations to appear before it and submit proposals for commission consideration and action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
