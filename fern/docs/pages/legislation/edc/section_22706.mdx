# EDC § 22706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22706

---

## Text

A member shall not receive credit for service performed while receiving a retirement or disability allowance under the Defined Benefit Program or while receiving a retirement or disability benefit under the Defined Benefit Supplement Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1021, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2001.)
