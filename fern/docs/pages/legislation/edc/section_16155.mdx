# EDC § 16155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 2. School Housing Aid for Reorganized Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 16155

---

## Text

If an annexation or other inclusion of a portion of an applicant district in another district comprises less than 5 percent of the assessed valuation of the applicant district on the effective date of the change, no annexation or other inclusion shall be deemed to have taken place for the purposes of Sections 16154 and 16156.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
