# EDC § 51720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 3. Evening High School for Minors

**Citation:** EDC § 51720

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district which maintains high schools and a continuation education program may establish and maintain evening high school programs in grades 9 to 12, inclusive, for minors subject to Section 48200.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
