# EDC § 58505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 31. EXPERIMENTAL SCHOOL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Alternative Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 58505

---

## Text

A district may establish alternative schools in each attendance area or on a districtwide basis, with enrollment open to all students districtwide, or any combination thereof.
