# EDC § 89303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Students > Article: 1. Student Body Organizations

**Citation:** EDC § 89303

---

## Text

The trustees may provide for the construction of a building to serve as a student body center to be financed entirely or in part by fees required of students as authorized in Section 89304 pursuant to the provisions of the State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 106, Sec. 23.)
