# EDC § 17336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Construction of School Buildings > Article: 3.5. Earthquake Construction of Private Schools

## Text

Any person who willfully violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
