# EDC § 19467

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 19467

---

## Text

The board of library trustees shall borrow money, give security therefor, purchase on contract, and do and perform any and all other acts and things necessary or proper to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
