# EDC § 56302.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 1. Identification and Referral

**Citation:** EDC § 56302.5

---

## Text

The term “assessment,” as used in this chapter, shall have the same meaning as the term “evaluation” in the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, as provided in Section 1414 of Title 20 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 691, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 1999.)
