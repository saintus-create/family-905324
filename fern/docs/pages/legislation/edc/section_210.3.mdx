# EDC § 210.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 210.3

---

## Text

“Educational institution” means a public or private preschool, elementary, or secondary school or institution; the governing board of a school district; or any combination of school districts or counties recognized as the administrative agency for public elementary or secondary schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 210.1 by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
