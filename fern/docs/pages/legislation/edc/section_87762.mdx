# EDC § 87762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 7. Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 87762

---

## Text

In specifying or defining the rights of employees in Section 87746, “war” means “war” as defined in Section 22804.
