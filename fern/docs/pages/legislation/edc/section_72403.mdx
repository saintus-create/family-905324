# EDC § 72403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 72403

---

## Text

The governing board of each community college district shall annually at its initial meeting select one of its members to serve as its representative for purposes of Article 1 (commencing with Section 4000) of Chapter 1 of Part 3. The representative selected pursuant to this section shall have one vote for each member to be elected to the county committee pursuant to Article 1 (commencing with Section 4000) of Chapter 1 of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1095, Sec. 5. Effective September 27, 1983.)
