# EDC § 78210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Courses of Study > Article: 1. Student Matriculation

**Citation:** EDC § 78210

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Seymour-Campbell Student Success Act of 2012.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 624, Sec. 3. (SB 1456) Effective January 1, 2013. Note: See provision in Section 78218 making operation contingent upon funding.)
