# EDC § 52322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1. Regional Occupational Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 52322

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall make provision in allocating any funds received from the federal government pursuant to Public Law 576 of the 90th Congress to include regional occupational centers and programs that comply with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
