# EDC § 19800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. California Freedom to Read Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Freedom to Read Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 941, Sec. 1. (AB 1825) Effective January 1, 2025.)
