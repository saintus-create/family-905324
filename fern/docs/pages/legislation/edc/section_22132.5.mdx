# EDC § 22132.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22132.5

---

## Text

“Exclusive representative” means an exclusive representative as defined in subdivision (e) of Section 3540.1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 754, Sec. 1. (AB 1667) Effective January 1, 2023.)
