# EDC § 52335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1.5. Apportionment of Funds for Regional Occupational Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 52335

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “ROC/P” means regional occupational center or program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 19, Sec. 109. (SB 78) Effective June 24, 2015.)
