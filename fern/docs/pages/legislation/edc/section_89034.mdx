# EDC § 89034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Powers of the Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 89034

---

## Text

The authority to select and change the name of any institution of higher education in the California State University shall be vested in the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 8, Sec. 41. Effective April 15, 1993.)
