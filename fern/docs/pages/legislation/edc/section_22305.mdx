# EDC § 22305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22305

---

## Text

Any rules and regulations adopted by the board for the purpose of the administration of this part and Part 14 (commencing with Section 26000), and not inconsistent with this part and Part 14 (commencing with Section 26000), have the force and effect of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 1999.)
