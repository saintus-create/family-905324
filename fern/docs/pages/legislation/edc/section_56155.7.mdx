# EDC § 56155.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 5. Licensed Children’s Institutions and Foster Family Homes

**Citation:** EDC § 56155.7

---

## Text

A licensed children’s institution shall not require that a child be identified as an individual with exceptional needs as a condition of admission or residency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 571, Sec. 2. (SB 121) Effective January 1, 2013.)
