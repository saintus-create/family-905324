# EDC § 47607.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Establishment of Charter Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 47607.8

---

## Text

The department, in consultation with the state board, shall collect data to track implementation of Chapter 486 of the Statutes of 2019 (Assembly Bill 1505 of the 2019-20 Regular Session).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 370, Sec. 75. (SB 1371) Effective January 1, 2021.)
