# EDC § 25925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13.5. HEALTH CARE BENEFITS PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Member” means a current or retired employee of an employer, as defined in Section 25921.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 803, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2002.)
