# EDC § 91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 91

---

## Text

“World language” means any language other than English, including American Sign Language, and is synonymous with “foreign language.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 865, Sec. 2. (AB 2319) Effective January 1, 2019.)
