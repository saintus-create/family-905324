# EDC § 45292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 45292

---

## Text

The commission may provide for the continuous examination of applicants for both open and promotional examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 652, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1996.)
