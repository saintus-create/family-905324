# EDC § 42403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Supplemental School Revenues

**Citation:** EDC § 42403

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, a “school district” includes a community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 14, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1993. Note: Condition in Sec. 4 of Ch. 14 was satisfied by enactment of AB 1930 as Ch. 1024, Stats. 1991, but operative date is not earlier than effective date.)
