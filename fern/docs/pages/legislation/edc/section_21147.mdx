# EDC § 21147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 12. FOUNDATIONS AND STATE COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Foundations, Trusts, and Institutions of Arts and Sciences > Article: 2. Institutions of Arts and Sciences

**Citation:** EDC § 21147

---

## Text

If he elects to veto, annul, or modify any act of the trustees, he shall, within 30 days after notice of the performance of the act, file in the office of the trustees, or deliver to their president or principal officer, a notice in writing, of the veto, annulment, or modification.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
