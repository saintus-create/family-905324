# EDC § 37703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Four-Day School Week

**Citation:** EDC § 37703

---

## Text

A school site council in the school district shall be involved in the planning and evaluation of a four-day school week.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 465, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
