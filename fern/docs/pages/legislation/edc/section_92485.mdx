# EDC § 92485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 3. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 92485

---

## Text

Pending the actual issuance or delivery of revenue bonds, the regents may issue temporary or interim bonds, certificates, or receipts of any denominations whatsoever, and with or without coupons, to be exchanged for definitive bonds when ready for delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
