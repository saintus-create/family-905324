# EDC § 4003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 1. County Committees on School District Organization

**Citation:** EDC § 4003

---

## Text

In every county with six or more school districts or community college districts in the territory under the jurisdiction of the county superintendent of schools of the county, the county committee on school district organization shall have 11 members.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 40.)
