# EDC § 49162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Employment of Minors > Article: 6. Duties of Employer

**Citation:** EDC § 49162

---

## Text

The employer of any minor subject to this chapter shall send to the officer authorized to issue the permit to work a written notification of intent to employ a minor. The form of the intent to employ a minor shall be prescribed by the Department of Education and shall be furnished to the employer by the officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
