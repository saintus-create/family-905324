# EDC § 87436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 87436

---

## Text

Any librarian when employed full time as librarian or serving full time, partly as librarian and partly as an instructor, shall rank as an instructor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
