# EDC § 22824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.2. Out-of-State Service Credit

**Citation:** EDC § 22824

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the extent it would require any action to be taken that would create a conflict with Section 415 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1968 (Title 26 of the United States Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998. Section operative January 1, 1999, pursuant to Section 22825.)
