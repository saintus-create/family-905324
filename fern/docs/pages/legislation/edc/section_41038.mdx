# EDC § 41038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Financial Management and Control > Article: 3. Foundation Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 41038

---

## Text

Except as may be otherwise provided in this article, other provisions of this chapter shall be applicable to the money placed in the Foundation Fund pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1168, Sec. 16. Effective September 30, 2002.)
