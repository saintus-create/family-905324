# EDC § 16503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. School Construction Law of 1957 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 16503

---

## Text

The Director of General Services shall administer this chapter and shall provide any assistance to the board that it may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
