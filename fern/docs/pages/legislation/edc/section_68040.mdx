# EDC § 68040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 3. Classification

**Citation:** EDC § 68040

---

## Text

Each student shall be classified as a resident or nonresident at the University of California, the California State University, or the California Maritime Academy or at a California community college.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 235.)
