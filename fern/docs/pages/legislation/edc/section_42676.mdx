# EDC § 42676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. School District Funds—Expenditures and Appropriations > Article: 6. Orders and Warrants When Moneys Are Not Available

**Citation:** EDC § 42676

---

## Text

The special interest requisition shall bear upon its face substantially the following notation: “In full payment of interest due on warrants numbered __ to __, inclusive, of the ____ School District.”

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
