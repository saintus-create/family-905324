# EDC § 42802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Revolving Funds, School Districts > Article: 1. Revolving Cash Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 42802

---

## Text

No bond shall be required when the revolving cash fund does not at any time exceed twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
