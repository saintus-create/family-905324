# EDC § 88201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Resignations and Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 88201

---

## Text

The governing boards of any community college district may accept the resignation of any employee and may fix the time when the resignation shall take effect, which shall not be later than the close of the school year during which the resignation is received by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
