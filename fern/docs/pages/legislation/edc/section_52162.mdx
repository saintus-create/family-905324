# EDC § 52162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Bilingual Education > Article: 3. Bilingual-Bicultural Education Act of 1976

**Citation:** EDC § 52162

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall adopt such rules and regulations as are necessary for the effective administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1339, Sec. 6. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 39 of Ch. 1339.)
