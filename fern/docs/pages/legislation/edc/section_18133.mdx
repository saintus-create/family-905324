# EDC § 18133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 4. Affiliation With County and City Libraries

**Citation:** EDC § 18133

---

## Text

The county librarian may (a) at his discretion dispose of books and other materials no longer fit for service, and (b) with the approval of the county board of education dispose of any books or other materials no longer needed by the course of study.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
