# EDC § 44685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.1. School Personnel Staff Development and Resource Centers > Article: 3. Administrator Training and Evaluation

**Citation:** EDC § 44685

---

## Text

Any school district governing board, upon petition by a majority of the administrators designated to design an administrator training and evaluation program, may request the State Board of Education to grant a waiver of any provision of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 38. Effective July 28, 1983.)
