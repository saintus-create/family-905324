# EDC § 89402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 89402

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, children under two years of age whose parent or parents are students may attend child development centers consistent with the priorities established.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
