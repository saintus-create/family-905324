# EDC § 52701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Miscellaneous > Article: 1. School Farms

**Citation:** EDC § 52701

---

## Text

The cost of purchasing and equipping a school farm and all other costs not met from the receipts of the operation of the farm shall be a charge against the funds of the school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
