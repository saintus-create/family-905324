# EDC § 51221.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 3. Courses of Study, Grades 7 to 12

**Citation:** EDC § 51221.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this code, the phrase “vocational-technical education” shall have the same meaning as “career technical education” as described in subdivision (i) of Section 51220.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 988, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
