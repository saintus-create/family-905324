# EDC § 92117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Treasury > Article: 2. Transfer of Land Acquired by Tax Sales to the University

**Citation:** EDC § 92117

---

## Text

The deed shall recite the facts provided in this article as authorizing its execution and is prima facie evidence of the facts and of all matters therein recited and of the ownership of the lands by the regents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
