# EDC § 70011.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 21.5. The California Memorial Scholarship Program

**Citation:** EDC § 70011.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to authorize or require the admission of a participant into a specific institution of higher education or degree program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 38, Sec. 1. Effective May 13, 2002.)
