# EDC § 68134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 11. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 68134

---

## Text

No provision of this part shall be applicable to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by resolution, make such provision applicable.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
