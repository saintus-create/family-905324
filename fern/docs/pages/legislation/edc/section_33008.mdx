# EDC § 33008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** EDC § 33008

---

## Text

Special meetings may be called by the president. Upon the request of any four members in writing, the secretary shall call a special meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
