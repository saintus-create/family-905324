# EDC § 89613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Optional Retirement Program > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 89613

---

## Text

“Eligible employee” means all faculty and executive, managerial, and supervisory employees of the California State University system whose employment would qualify them for membership in the Public Employees’ Retirement System, and who are initially appointed on or after the operative date of the optional retirement program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1178, Sec. 1.)
