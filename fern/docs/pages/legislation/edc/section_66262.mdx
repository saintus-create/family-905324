# EDC § 66262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Equity in Higher Education Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 66262

---

## Text

“Religion” includes all aspects of religious belief, observance, and practice and includes agnosticism and atheism. “Discrimination on the basis of religion” includes, but is not limited to, anti-Semitism and Islamophobia.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 844, Sec. 3. (AB 2925) Effective January 1, 2025.)
