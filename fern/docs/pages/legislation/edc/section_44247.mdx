# EDC § 44247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 3. Committee of Credentials

**Citation:** EDC § 44247

---

## Text

Any applicant for the renewal certification document who is denied a renewal by the Committee of Credentials may request a reevaluation of his application by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
