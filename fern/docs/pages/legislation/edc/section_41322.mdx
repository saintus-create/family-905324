# EDC § 41322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 2. Emergency Apportionments

**Citation:** EDC § 41322

---

## Text

(a) If a trustee has been appointed over a school district pursuant to Section 41320.1, the trustee may stay or rescind the actions of the personnel commission of the school district if a personnel commission exists.

(b) If an administrator has been appointed over a school district pursuant to Section 41326, the administrator shall suspend the actions of the personnel commission of the school district if a personnel commission exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 924, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
