# EDC § 51812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 10. Community Service Classes

**Citation:** EDC § 51812

---

## Text

Community service classes may be convened at such hours and for such length of time during the day or evening and at such period and for such length of time during the school year as may be determined by the governing board of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
