# EDC § 47630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Funding > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 47630

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that each charter school be provided with operational funding that is equal to the total funding that would be available to a similar school district serving a similar pupil population, except that a charter school may not be funded as a necessary small school or a necessary small high school.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 19, Sec. 96. (SB 78) Effective June 24, 2015.)
