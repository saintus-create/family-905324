# EDC § 18725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 18725

---

## Text

The state board shall serve as the State Advisory Council on Libraries for the purpose of meeting the requirements of the federal Library Services and Technology Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 219, Sec. 2. (SB 1044) Effective January 1, 2013.)
