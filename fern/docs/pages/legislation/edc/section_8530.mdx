# EDC § 8530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Adult Education > Article: 3. Functions of Continuing Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8530

---

## Text

Adult basic education is the responsibility of high school and unified school districts except in those instances where by mutual agreement the responsibility is assigned to a community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
