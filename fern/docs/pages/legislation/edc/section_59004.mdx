# EDC § 59004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. California Schools for the Deaf > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 59004

---

## Text

The superintendent of the school shall have had not less than three years’ experience in the art of teaching the deaf and shall hold a credential issued by the State Board of Education authorizing him to teach in secondary schools of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
