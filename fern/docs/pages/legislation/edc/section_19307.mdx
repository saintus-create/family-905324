# EDC § 19307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19307

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public library” means any public library as defined in this code, or any county law library established pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 6300) of Division 3 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 318, Sec. 2. (SB 711) Effective January 1, 2016.)
