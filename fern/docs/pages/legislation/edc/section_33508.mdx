# EDC § 33508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 2. Educational Innovation and Planning Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33508

---

## Text

Commission members shall serve for four-year terms and shall be eligible to serve more than one full term, except in the case of student representatives who shall serve a one-year term.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1354, Sec. 11. Effective September 30, 1980.)
