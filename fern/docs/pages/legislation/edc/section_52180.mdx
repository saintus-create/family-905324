# EDC § 52180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Bilingual Education > Article: 4. Bilingual Teacher Training Assistance Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52180

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that school districts, county offices of education and institutions of higher education, with the assistance of the State Department of Education, shall continue to provide training for teachers under bilingual teacher waivers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1169, Sec. 1.)
