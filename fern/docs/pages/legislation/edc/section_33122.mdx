# EDC § 33122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 33122

---

## Text

The Director of Education shall employ such persons as are necessary for the coordination and the supervision of services for hard-of-hearing children.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
