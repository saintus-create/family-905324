# EDC § 90000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 1. Housing

**Citation:** EDC § 90000

---

## Text

Student housing facilities may be established and maintained at any state university for the accommodation of students of the university.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 285, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
