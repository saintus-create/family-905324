# EDC § 44253.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 4. Credential Types

**Citation:** EDC § 44253.9

---

## Text

The commission shall promulgate regulations to clarify and make specific the requirements and authorizations of credentials, certificates, and permits established pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1050, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1993.)
