# EDC § 44110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 5. Reporting by School Employees of Improper Governmental Activities

**Citation:** EDC § 44110

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be referred to as the Reporting by School Employees of Improper Governmental Activities Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 531, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
