# EDC § 67329.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.28. California Student Housing Revolving Loan Fund Act of 2022 > Article: 8. Administrative Costs

**Citation:** EDC § 67329.9

---

## Text

Each authority may charge against the fund its administrative costs, which shall not exceed 3 percent of the authority’s respective allocation amount or proportion of the fund, as the fund may be augmented by revenue bonds over time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 572, Sec. 2. (AB 190) Effective September 27, 2022.)
