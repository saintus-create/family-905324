# EDC § 44210.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 2. Commission on Teacher Credentialing

**Citation:** EDC § 44210.5

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that appointees to the commission reflect the ethnic and cultural diversity of the California public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1355, Sec. 4.6.)
