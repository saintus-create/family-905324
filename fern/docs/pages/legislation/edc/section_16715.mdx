# EDC § 16715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Urban School Construction Aid Law of 1968 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 16715

---

## Text

No apportionment shall be made for new construction, the area of which exceeds the area of the unsafe buildings being replaced under Section 16708. The area of school buildings shall be computed in a uniform manner as prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
