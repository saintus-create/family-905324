# EDC § 56208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Elements of the Local Plan > Article: 1.1. State Requirements

**Citation:** EDC § 56208

---

## Text

This article shall apply to special education local plan areas that are submitting a revised local plan for approval pursuant to Section 56836.03 or that have an approved revised local plan pursuant to Section 56836.03.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 854, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1998.)
