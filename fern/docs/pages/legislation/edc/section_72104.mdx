# EDC § 72104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Meetings and Members > Article: 1. Membership

**Citation:** EDC § 72104

---

## Text

No member of the governing board of a community college district shall, during the term for which he or she was elected, be eligible to serve on the governing board of a high school district whose boundaries are coterminous with those of the community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 654, Sec. 3. Effective October 7, 2005.)
