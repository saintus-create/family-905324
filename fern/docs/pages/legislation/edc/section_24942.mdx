# EDC § 24942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 35. Joint District Salary Retirement Plan > Article: 2. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24942

---

## Text

The retirement salaries provided for in this chapter shall be in addition to any other retirement salaries received by any person under Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 22000) to Chapter 31 (commencing with Section 24600), inclusive, or as may be otherwise provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
