# EDC § 60002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 1. Legislative Intent

**Citation:** EDC § 60002

---

## Text

Each district board shall provide for substantial teacher involvement in the selection of instructional materials and shall promote the involvement of parents and other members of the community in the selection of instructional materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 413, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
