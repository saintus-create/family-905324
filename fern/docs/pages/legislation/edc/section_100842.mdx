# EDC § 100842

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68.2. KINDERGARTEN–UNIVERSITY PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2004 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade > Article: 2. Kindergarten Through 12th Grade School Facilities Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 100842

---

## Text

All money deposited in the 2004 State School Facilities Fund, that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 33, Sec. 31. Approved in Proposition 55 at the March 2, 2004, election.)
