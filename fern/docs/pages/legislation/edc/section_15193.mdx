# EDC § 15193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Bonds of School Districts and Community College Districts > Article: 5. Registration of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 15193

---

## Text

After any bond has been registered as provided in this article, the principal and interest of the bond shall be payable to the registered owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
