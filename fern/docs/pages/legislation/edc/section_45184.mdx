# EDC § 45184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Differential Compensation

**Citation:** EDC § 45184

---

## Text

No employee assigned to work a shift entitled to differential compensation shall be demoted in class or grade as a result of such an assignment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
