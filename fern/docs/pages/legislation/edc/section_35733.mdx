# EDC § 35733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 3. Contents of Plans and Recommendations

**Citation:** EDC § 35733

---

## Text

Whenever the recommendation is to divide the entire territory of an existing school district into two or more separate school districts, the recommendation may provide that the plans and recommendations be voted upon as a single proposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1192, Sec. 3.)
