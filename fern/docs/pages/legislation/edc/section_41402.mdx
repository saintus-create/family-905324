# EDC § 41402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 6. Teaching and Nonteaching Certificated Employee Ratios

**Citation:** EDC § 41402

---

## Text

The maximum ratios of administrative employees to each 100 teachers in the various types of school districts shall be as follows:

(a) In elementary school districts—9.

(b) In unified school districts—8.

(c) In high school districts—7.

This section shall not apply to a school district that has one or fewer administrators.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 677, Sec. 13. Effective October 7, 2005.)
