# EDC § 52616.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 6. Finances

**Citation:** EDC § 52616.6

---

## Text

A school district or county superintendent of schools may expend funds allocated to it for the purposes of classes for adults in correctional facilities only for the direct instructional charges of those classes and for related direct and indirect support costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 670, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1994.)
