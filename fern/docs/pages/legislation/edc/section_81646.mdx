# EDC § 81646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Management and Control of Property > Article: 3. Contracts

**Citation:** EDC § 81646

---

## Text

Nothing in this code shall preclude the governing board of a community college district from purchasing materials, equipment, supplies, or services under the same terms and conditions as are specified in a contract lawfully awarded by the University of California or the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 366, Sec. 2. (AB 653) Effective January 1, 2016.)
