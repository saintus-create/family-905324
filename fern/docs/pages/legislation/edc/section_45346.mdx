# EDC § 45346

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 7. Instructional Aides

**Citation:** EDC § 45346

---

## Text

Classroom teachers and other certificated personnel shall not be required to hold a standard supervision credential or a standard administration credential as a prerequisite to the supervision and direction of instructional aides.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
