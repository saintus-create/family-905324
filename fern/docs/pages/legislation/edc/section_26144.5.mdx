# EDC § 26144.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26144.5

---

## Text

“Trustee service” means duties performed by a member of the governing body of an employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1020, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2001.)
