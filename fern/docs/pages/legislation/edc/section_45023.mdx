# EDC § 45023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 5. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 45023

---

## Text

The governing board of each school district shall adopt and cause to be printed and made available to each certificated employee a schedule of salaries to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
