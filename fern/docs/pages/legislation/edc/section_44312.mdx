# EDC § 44312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 6. Subject Matter Preparation

**Citation:** EDC § 44312

---

## Text

A finding by the commission of the inadequacy or inappropriateness of such program as the basis for waiving the subject matter examination shall cause persons completing such programs to be ineligible for the examination waiver.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
