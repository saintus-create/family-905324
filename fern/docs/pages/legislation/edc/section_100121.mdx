# EDC § 100121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 66. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1996 > Article: 3. Higher Education Facilities Program

**Citation:** EDC § 100121

---

## Text

The Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges shall consider the historic significance of community college district buildings that are 50 years of age or older if those buildings are to be renovated, reconstructed, or demolished in connection with the construction of buildings utilizing the funds provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 203 at the March 26, 1996, election.)
