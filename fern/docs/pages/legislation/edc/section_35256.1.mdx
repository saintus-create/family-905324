# EDC § 35256.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 8. Records and Reports

**Citation:** EDC § 35256.1

---

## Text

In addition to the information required under Section 35256, each School Accountability Report Card shall include the information required under Section 41409.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1463, Sec. 1.)
