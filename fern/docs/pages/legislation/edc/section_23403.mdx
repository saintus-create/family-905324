# EDC § 23403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 21. Pre-1972 Benefits

**Citation:** EDC § 23403

---

## Text

Survivor benefits payable on account of deaths that occurred prior to July 1, 1972, shall be continued in the amounts and under the conditions stated in former Sections 14186 and 14189, as they read prior to July 1, 1972.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 933, Sec. 52. Effective September 28, 1994.)
