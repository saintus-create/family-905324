# EDC § 32044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. School Safety—Public and Private Institutions > Article: 4. First Aid Equipment

**Citation:** EDC § 32044

---

## Text

Any member of the governing board of any school district, and any superintendent of schools, principal, teacher, instructor, or agent who willfully violates the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 470, Sec. 31.)
