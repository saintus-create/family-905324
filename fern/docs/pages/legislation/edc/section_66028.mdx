# EDC § 66028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3.7. Working Families Student Fee Transparency and Accountability Act

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Working Families Student Fee Transparency and Accountability Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 620, Sec. 2. (AB 970) Effective January 1, 2013.)
