# EDC § 53303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 18. Race to the Top > Article: 3. Parent Empowerment

**Citation:** EDC § 53303

---

## Text

A local educational agency shall not be required to implement the option requested by the parent petition if the request is for reasons other than improving academic achievement or pupil safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, 5th Ex. Sess., Ch. 3, Sec. 2. (SB 4 5x) Effective April 12, 2010.)
