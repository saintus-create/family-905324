# EDC § 35142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 3. Meetings

**Citation:** EDC § 35142

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of Section 35141, the times at which the regular meetings of the governing board of a high school district are to be held shall be prescribed by the rules and regulations adopted by such board for its own government.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
