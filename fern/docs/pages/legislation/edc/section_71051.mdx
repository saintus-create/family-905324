# EDC § 71051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 44. BOARD OF GOVERNORS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Board of Governors > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 71051

---

## Text

(a) The board of governors shall develop a process for the approval and funding of new collaborative facilities projects that are proposed by community college districts.

(b) The board of governors shall not implement subdivision (a) without statutory authorization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 193, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2005.)
