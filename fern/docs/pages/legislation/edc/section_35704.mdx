# EDC § 35704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 1. Reorganization of School Districts by the Electorate

**Citation:** EDC § 35704

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools, within 30 days after any petition for reorganization is filed, shall examine the petition and, if he or she finds it to be sufficient and signed as required by law, transmit the petition simultaneously to the county committee and to the State Board of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1058, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2001.)
