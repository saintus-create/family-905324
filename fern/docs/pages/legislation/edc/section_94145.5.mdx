# EDC § 94145.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Part: 59. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California Educational Facilities Authority Act > Article: 4. Powers and Duties; Notes and Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 94145.5

---

## Text

A provision that the authority may include in a trust agreement or resolution providing for the issuance of bonds pursuant to this chapter may also be included in a bond and shall have the same effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 141. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
