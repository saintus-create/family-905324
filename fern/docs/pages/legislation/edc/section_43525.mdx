# EDC § 43525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24.6. Instruction Support Grants > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. In-Person Instruction and Expanded Learning Opportunities Grants

**Citation:** EDC § 43525

---

## Text

This part shall become inoperative on June 30, 2026, and, as of January 1, 2027, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 38, Sec. 25. (SB 153) Effective June 29, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Part 24.6, commencing with Section 43520.)
