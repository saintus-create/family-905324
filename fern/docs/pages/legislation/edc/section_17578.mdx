# EDC § 17578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Property Maintenance and Control > Article: 1. Duties of Governing Board

**Citation:** EDC § 17578

---

## Text

The governing board of each district maintaining a high school shall provide for the annual cleaning, sterilizing, and necessary repair of football equipment of their respective schools pursuant to Sections 17579 and 17580.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 646, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2000.)
