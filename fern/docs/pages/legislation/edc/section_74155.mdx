# EDC § 74155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Initiation of Petition and Local Review Procedures > Article: 3. Formation

**Citation:** EDC § 74155

---

## Text

Within 90 days after affording the public an opportunity to comment on the petition, the report, and the recommendations included in the report, a county committee shall forward a copy of the petition and the report to each affected governing board and the board of governors for review.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 392.)
