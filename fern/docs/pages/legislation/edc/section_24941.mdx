# EDC § 24941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 35. Joint District Salary Retirement Plan > Article: 2. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24941

---

## Text

The county auditor of the county in the county treasury of which the “joint district retirement fund” is created shall audit the accounts of the joint district retirement board at least once every 12 months and report upon the financial condition thereof to the governing boards of the districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
