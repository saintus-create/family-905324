# EDC § 56129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 56129

---

## Text

The Superintendent shall maintain the state special schools and diagnostic centers in accordance with Part 32 (commencing with Section 59000) so that the services of those schools and centers are coordinated with the services of the local educational agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2008.)
