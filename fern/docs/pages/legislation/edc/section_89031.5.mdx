# EDC § 89031.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Powers of the Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 89031.5

---

## Text

The trustees shall perform inspections of all motor vehicles owned by the California State University or under their jurisdiction. These inspections shall be provided for in the appropriate administrative manual of the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 843, Sec. 1.)
