# EDC § 47643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Funding > Article: 4. Special Education Funding

**Citation:** EDC § 47643

---

## Text

If the approval of a petition for a charter school requires a change to the allocation plan developed pursuant to subdivision (i) of Section 56195.7 or Section 56836.05, the change shall be adopted pursuant to the policymaking process of the special education local plan area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 78, Sec. 32.8. Effective July 7, 1999.)
