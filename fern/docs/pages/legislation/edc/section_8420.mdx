# EDC § 8420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 19. 21st Century High School After School Safety and Enrichment for Teens Program

**Citation:** EDC § 8420

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the 21st Century High School After School Safety and Enrichment for Teens (High School ASSETs) program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1025, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
