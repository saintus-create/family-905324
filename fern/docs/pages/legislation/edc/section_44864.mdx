# EDC § 44864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 2. Employment

**Citation:** EDC § 44864

---

## Text

Each teacher in a joint elementary district shall hold a valid certificate in the county in which the schoolhouse is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
