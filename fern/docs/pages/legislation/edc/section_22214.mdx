# EDC § 22214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** EDC § 22214

---

## Text

The board may take any action it deems necessary to ensure the continued right of members or beneficiaries to receive monthly payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1997.)
