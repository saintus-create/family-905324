# EDC § 5361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 4. Election Notice

**Citation:** EDC § 5361

---

## Text

The formal notice of election for any school district election or community college district election, except a bond measure election, shall be prepared by the county superintendent of schools and shall contain the following:

(a) The date of the election.

(b) The purpose of the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 667, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
