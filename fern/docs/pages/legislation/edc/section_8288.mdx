# EDC § 8288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 10. Childcare Subsidy Plan for the City and County of San Francisco

**Citation:** EDC § 8288

---

## Text

A participating contractor shall receive any increase or decrease in funding that the contractor would have received if the contractor had not participated in the local subsidy plan established by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8335.6 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 174. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
