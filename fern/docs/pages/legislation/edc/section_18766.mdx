# EDC § 18766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. California Library Services Act > Article: 6. Statewide Services

**Citation:** EDC § 18766

---

## Text

The state board shall establish and maintain a statewide communications and delivery and resource-sharing network between and among systems, state reference centers, independent public libraries, and all other libraries participating in the programs authorized by this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 12. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
