# EDC § 74139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Initiation of Petition and Local Review Procedures > Article: 2. Annexation

**Citation:** EDC § 74139

---

## Text

Whenever a county committee approves a petition, within 10 days it shall transmit a copy of the approved petition, together with any related information or recommendations, to the Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges for review.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 386.)
