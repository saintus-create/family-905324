# EDC § 56145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3.5. Charter Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 56145

---

## Text

Individuals with exceptional needs attending charter schools pursuant to Part 26.8 (commencing with Section 47600) shall be served in the same manner as individuals with exceptional needs are served in other public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 691, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1999.)
