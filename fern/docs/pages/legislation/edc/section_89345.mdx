# EDC § 89345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Students > Article: 5. Higher Education Outreach and Assistance Act for Foster Youth

**Citation:** EDC § 89345

---

## Text

The State University Educational Opportunity Program and California Community College Extended Opportunity Programs and Services shall ensure that identified foster youth are informed of services, including mentoring, provided by these programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 763, Sec. 6. (AB 1567) Effective January 1, 2018.)
