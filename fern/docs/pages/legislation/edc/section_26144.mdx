# EDC § 26144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26144

---

## Text

“Total and permanent disability” means any medically determinable physical or mental incapacity that is expected to prevent the participant from performing creditable service under this part for the employer for a continuous period of at least one year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 249. Effective January 1, 1999.)
