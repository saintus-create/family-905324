# EDC § 38090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23. SUPPLEMENTAL SERVICES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Cafeterias > Article: 2. Cafeterias, Funds and Accounts

**Citation:** EDC § 38090

---

## Text

Money received for the sale of food or for any services performed by the cafeterias may be paid into the county treasury to the credit of the “cafeteria fund” of the particular school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
