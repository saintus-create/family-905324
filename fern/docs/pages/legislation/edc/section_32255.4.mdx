# EDC § 32255.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 19. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Pupils’ Rights to Refrain From the Harmful or Destructive Use of Animals

**Citation:** EDC § 32255.4

---

## Text

Each teacher teaching a course that utilizes live or dead animals or animal parts shall also inform the pupils of their rights pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 65, Sec. 2.)
