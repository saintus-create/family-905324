# EDC § 8214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 2. Program Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 8214

---

## Text

If families have to be disenrolled from subsidized preschool services, families shall be disenrolled in reverse priority order for services, as specified in Sections 8210 and 8211, as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 27. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
