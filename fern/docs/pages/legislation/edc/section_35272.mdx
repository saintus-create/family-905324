# EDC § 35272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 9. Property

**Citation:** EDC § 35272

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may acquire and pay for educational and athletic equipment, supplies and materials, and other personal property necessary to its operation of the schools, as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
