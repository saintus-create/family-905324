# EDC § 87620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 3. Community College Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 87620

---

## Text

The provisions of this article apply to all persons employed as faculty members by a community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 105. Effective September 25, 1990.)
