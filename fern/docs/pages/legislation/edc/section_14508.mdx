# EDC § 14508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 9. FINANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Financial and Compliance Audits

**Citation:** EDC § 14508

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 10231.5 of the Government Code, the Controller shall submit a report, consistent with Section 9795 of the Government Code, to the Legislature on or before August 31 of each year regarding any recommendations made to the Superintendent pursuant to Section 14507.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 65, Sec. 32. (AB 126) Effective July 9, 2026.)
