# EDC § 12401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Participation in Federal Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 12401

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by, and pursuant to, federal law, agreements may be entered into by the governing board of a school district without prior approval of the State Board of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 102.)
