# EDC § 92525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92525

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause requiring, specifying, or limiting the kind, amount, and character of insurance to be maintained by the regents on any project, or any part of any project, and the use and disposition of the proceeds of any such insurance thereafter collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
