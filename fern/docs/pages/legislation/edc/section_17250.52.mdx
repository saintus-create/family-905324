# EDC § 17250.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Design-Build Contracts

**Citation:** EDC § 17250.52

---

## Text

Beginning January 1, 2023, a project using an alternative design-build contract, as defined in Section 17250.60, entered into on or after January 1, 2023, shall be governed by Chapter 2.6 (commencing with Section 17250.60).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 571, Sec. 13. (AB 185) Effective September 27, 2022.)
