# EDC § 69762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 13. Federal Family Education Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69762

---

## Text

Loans made pursuant to this chapter shall be made without regard to race, religion, creed, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 637, Sec. 9. (AB 620) Effective January 1, 2012. Repealed conditionally by Stats. 2007, Ch. 184, Secs. 2 and 4.)
