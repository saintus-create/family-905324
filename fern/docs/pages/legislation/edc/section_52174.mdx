# EDC § 52174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Bilingual Education > Article: 3. Bilingual-Bicultural Education Act of 1976

**Citation:** EDC § 52174

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall preclude the participation by an individual school district in a consortium or a cooperative in order to provide support and contract services to school districts that receive funds for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1339, Sec. 30. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 39 of Ch. 1339.)
