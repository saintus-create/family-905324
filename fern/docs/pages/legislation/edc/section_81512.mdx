# EDC § 81512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 14. Leasing for Production of Gas

**Citation:** EDC § 81512

---

## Text

No well for the production of gas shall be drilled on any land owned by the district and leased pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
