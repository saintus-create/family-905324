# EDC § 21149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 12. FOUNDATIONS AND STATE COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Foundations, Trusts, and Institutions of Arts and Sciences > Article: 2. Institutions of Arts and Sciences

**Citation:** EDC § 21149

---

## Text

The founder may also reserve the right to alter, amend, or modify, at any time during his life, or by his last will and testament, the terms and conditions of the grant, and the trust created in respect to the institution, its buildings, and the property conveyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
