# EDC § 44418

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 16. Teacher Recruitment, Retention, and Support Grant Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 44418

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2036, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 65, Sec. 75. (AB 126) Effective July 9, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2036, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 16, commencing with Section 44415.)
