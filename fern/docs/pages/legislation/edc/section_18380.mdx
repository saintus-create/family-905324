# EDC § 18380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 6. Meetings of Boards of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18380

---

## Text

A board of library trustees shall meet at least quarterly, at the time and place that it may fix by resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 579, Sec. 4. Effective October 4, 1995. Operative January 1, 1996.)
