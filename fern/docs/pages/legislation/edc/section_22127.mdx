# EDC § 22127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22127

---

## Text

“Disability allowance” means the amount payable to a disabled member on a monthly basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1997.)
