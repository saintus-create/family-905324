# EDC § 79120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 1. Child Development Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 79120

---

## Text

The governing board of any community college district may establish and maintain a child development center on or near each community college campus pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 8200) of Part 6 of Division 1 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
