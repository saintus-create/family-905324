# EDC § 22110.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22110.5

---

## Text

“Certificated” means the holding by a person of a credential that is required by the laws of the state to be held as a condition to valid employment in the position in which the person is employed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
