# EDC § 100115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 66. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1996 > Article: 3. Higher Education Facilities Program

**Citation:** EDC § 100115

---

## Text

Nine hundred seventy-five million dollars ($975,000,000) of the proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the 1996 Higher Education Capital Outlay Bond Fund, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 3. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1, and approved in Prop. 203 on March 26, 1996.)
