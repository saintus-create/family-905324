# EDC § 92302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 92302

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, children under two years of age whose parent or parents are students may attend child development centers consistent with the priorities established within this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
