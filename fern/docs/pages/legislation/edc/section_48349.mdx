# EDC § 48349

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 9.5. Local Educational Agency Instruction Collaboration Agreements

**Citation:** EDC § 48349

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on July 1, 2029, and, as of January 1, 2030, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. (SB 941) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 9.5, commencing with Section 48345.)
