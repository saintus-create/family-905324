# EDC § 49466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 4.5. Pupil Health Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 49466

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by resolution, make that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 195, Sec. 5. Effective July 12, 1994.)
