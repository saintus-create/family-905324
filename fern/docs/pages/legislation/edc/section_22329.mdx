# EDC § 22329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22329

---

## Text

In order to provide equitable telephone assistance to all members and beneficiaries, regardless of their location in California, the system shall install a toll-free, “800” prefix, line.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 86. Effective January 1, 1997.)
