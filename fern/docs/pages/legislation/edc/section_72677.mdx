# EDC § 72677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 6. Auxiliary Organizations

**Citation:** EDC § 72677

---

## Text

No member of the board of directors of an auxiliary organization shall be financially interested in any contract or other transaction entered into by the board of which he or she is a member. Any contract or transaction entered into in violation of this section is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 858, Sec. 2. Effective August 31, 1980.)
