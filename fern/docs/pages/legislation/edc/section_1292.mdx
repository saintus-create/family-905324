# EDC § 1292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 3. Staff

**Citation:** EDC § 1292

---

## Text

The compensation of each deputy school superintendent of a city and county shall be not less than the minimum received by any high school principal in the city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
