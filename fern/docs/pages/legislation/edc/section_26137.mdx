# EDC § 26137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26137

---

## Text

“Retirement benefit” means an amount payable under this part in the event of the participant’s retirement for service that is equal to the sum of the participant’s employee account and employer account as of the retirement date and that is payable pursuant to Section 26806, 26807, or 26807.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 655, Sec. 66. Effective January 1, 2007.)
