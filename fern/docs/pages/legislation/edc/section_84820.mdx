# EDC § 84820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Apportionment > Article: 2.5. Adjustments to Lottery Fund Allocations

**Citation:** EDC § 84820

---

## Text

Any lottery revenue allocated to a public education entity pursuant to Section 8880.5 of the Government Code shall not be reduced by more than one-half of 1 percent per year when that reduction is the direct result of any entity changing its method of computing workload units.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 129. Effective January 1, 1996.)
