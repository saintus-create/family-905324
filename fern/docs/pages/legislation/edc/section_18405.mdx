# EDC § 18405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 7. Powers of Boards of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18405

---

## Text

The board of library trustees may request the appropriate state officials to furnish the library with copies of any and all reports, laws, and other publications of the state not otherwise disposed of by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
