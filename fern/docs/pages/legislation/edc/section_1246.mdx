# EDC § 1246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. County Superintendents of Schools > Article: 2. Duties, Responsibilities, and General Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 1246

---

## Text

Whenever the destruction of records of a county superintendent of schools is not otherwise authorized or provided for by law, the county superintendent of schools may destroy the records of his or her office in accordance with regulations of the Superintendent of Public Instruction which he or she is herewith authorized to adopt.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 14. Operative January 1, 1992, by Sec. 709 of Ch. 1372.)
