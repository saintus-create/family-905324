# EDC § 25008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 38. Defined Benefit Supplement Program > Article: 2. Program Accounts

**Citation:** EDC § 25008

---

## Text

A member’s right to an amount equal to the member’s Defined Benefit Supplement account balance shall be vested at the time contributions are initially credited to the member’s account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 74) by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1021, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2001.)
