# EDC § 48618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Attendance at Adjustment Schools > Article: 1. Twenty-four Hour Elementary Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 48618

---

## Text

The enrollment in each 24-hour school shall be limited to the standards established under Section 56615.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
