# EDC § 8730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Environmental Education > Article: 3. Conservation Education Library

**Citation:** EDC § 8730

---

## Text

There is in the Department of Education a Central Library and Repository for conservation education materials. Such materials may be developed by private conservation groups, by industry, and by professional, scientific, and governmental sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
