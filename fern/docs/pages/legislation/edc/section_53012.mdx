# EDC § 53012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 16. California Career Pathways Trust

**Citation:** EDC § 53012

---

## Text

A grant recipient under this chapter may be a school district, county office of education, direct-funded charter school, regional occupational center or program operated by a joint powers authority, or community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 687, Sec. 7. (SB 876) Effective September 27, 2014.)
