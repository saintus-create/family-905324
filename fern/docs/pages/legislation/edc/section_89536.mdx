# EDC § 89536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Personnel > Article: 2. Appointment, Tenure, Layoff and Dismissal of Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 89536

---

## Text

Any permanent or probationary employee who is physically or mentally unfit for the position occupied may be suspended, demoted, or dismissed pursuant to the provisions of Sections 89538, 89539, and 89540.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
