# EDC § 87611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 2. Employment of Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 87611

---

## Text

A final decision reached following a grievance or hearing conducted pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 87610.1 shall be subject to judicial review pursuant to Section 1094.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 973, Sec. 48. Operative July 1, 1991, pursuant to Sec. 70(e) of Ch. 973.)
