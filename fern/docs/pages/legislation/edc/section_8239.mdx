# EDC § 8239

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 8239

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction may provide outreach services and technical assistance to new contracting agencies and to those providing preschool services during nontraditional times, in underserved geographic areas, and for children with special childcare needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8264.6 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 79. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
