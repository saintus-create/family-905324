# EDC § 33546.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 3. Instructional Quality Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33546.4

---

## Text

When the “Health Education Framework for California Public Schools” (health framework) is next revised, on or after January 1, 2025, the commission shall consider including information on evidence-based schoolwide programs to support pupils in developing skills in mindfulness, distress tolerance, interpersonal effectiveness, and emotional regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 176, Sec. 1. (AB 3010) Effective January 1, 2025.)
