# EDC § 54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 5. General Provisions, Public Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 54

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 1020 of the Government Code, any pupil attending a California public secondary school who is under the age of 18 years may serve on any board or commission established pursuant to this title or Title 2 (commencing with Section 33000) that includes members who are pupils or minors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 182, Sec. 1. (AB 1887) Effective August 24, 2018.)
