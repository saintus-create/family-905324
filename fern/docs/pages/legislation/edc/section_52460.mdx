# EDC § 52460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 7.5. Agricultural Career Technical Education Incentive Program

**Citation:** EDC § 52460

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district which operates an agricultural career technical education program may apply to the Superintendent of Public Instruction for an incentive grant, pursuant to this article, for the purpose of purchasing or leasing agricultural career technical education equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1058, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 2001.)
