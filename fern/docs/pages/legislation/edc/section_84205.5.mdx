# EDC § 84205.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property Valuations for Allocations

**Citation:** EDC § 84205.5

---

## Text

For purposes of subdivision (a) of Section 2 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution, the “full cash value” of all property in a community college district shall include the assessed valuation of all real properties in those territories or components which subsequent to the 1975–76 fiscal year became a part of the community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 292.)
