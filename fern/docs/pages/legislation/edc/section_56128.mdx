# EDC § 56128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 56128

---

## Text

The superintendent shall prepare for board approval, as necessary, any state plan required by federal law in order that this state may qualify for any federal funds available for the education of individuals with exceptional needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 797, Sec. 9. Effective July 28, 1980.)
