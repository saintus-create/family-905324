# EDC § 87604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 2. Employment of Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 87604

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district shall employ each academic employee as a contract employee, regular employee, or temporary employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 157. Effective January 1, 1996.)
