# EDC § 66760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9.6. Cross-Enrollment in Online Education at California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66760

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Chancellor” means the Chancellor of the California State University.

(b) “CSU” means the California State University.

(c) “Home campus” means the California State University campus at which the student matriculates.

(d) “Host campus” means the campus to which the student seeks access.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 363, Sec. 2. (AB 386) Effective January 1, 2014.)
