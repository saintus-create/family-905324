# EDC § 69993.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 19. Golden State Scholarshare Trust Act

**Citation:** EDC § 69993.7

---

## Text

No public funds nor any funds available pursuant to this article may be expended to pay for, nor shall the board enter into any agreement that provides for, the appearance of any elected official or declared candidate for public office in any paid advertisement promoting the act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 664, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2000.)
