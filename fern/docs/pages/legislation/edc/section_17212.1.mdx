# EDC § 17212.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Schoolsites > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 17212.1

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that corporations, public utilities, local publicly owned utilities, governmental agencies, and school districts work collaboratively in assessing the safety of a proposed schoolsite or addition to an existing schoolsite.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 578, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
