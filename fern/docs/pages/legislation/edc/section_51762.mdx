# EDC § 51762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 7. Work Experience Education

**Citation:** EDC § 51762

---

## Text

The Department of Education shall adopt any rules and regulations necessary to implement the standards set by the Superintendent of Public Instruction, in order to maintain the educational purpose and character of work experience education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1024, Sec. 4.)
