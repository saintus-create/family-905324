# EDC § 78101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.5. Library Services

**Citation:** EDC § 78101

---

## Text

The board of governors shall adopt standards, rules, and regulations for community college library services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 459.)
