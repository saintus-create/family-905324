# EDC § 42692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. School District Funds—Expenditures and Appropriations > Article: 7. Optional Method—Registering Warrants

**Citation:** EDC § 42692

---

## Text

When the warrant is presented to the county treasurer for payment, he shall endorse, register, advertise, and pay it, with interest at the rate of 5 percent per annum, in the manner prescribed, as nearly as may be, for county warrants in Sections 29821 to 29824, inclusive, and Sections 29826 and 29827 of the Government Code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
