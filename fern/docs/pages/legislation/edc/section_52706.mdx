# EDC § 52706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Miscellaneous > Article: 1. School Farms

**Citation:** EDC § 52706

---

## Text

The governing board may buy feed, livestock, and materials and supplies for a farm and may sell produce, livestock and other products of a farm in accordance with rules and regulations adopted by the board notwithstanding any provision of this code in conflict with such rules and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
