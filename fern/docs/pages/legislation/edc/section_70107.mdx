# EDC § 70107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Nursing Assumption Program of Loans for Education (SNAPLE) > Article: 1. Nursing Faculty

**Citation:** EDC § 70107

## Text

The commission shall work to develop a streamlined application process for participation in the program set forth in this article.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 69616.7 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 837, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2007.)
