# EDC § 51041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 3. Role of Local Agency

**Citation:** EDC § 51041

---

## Text

The governing board of every school district shall evaluate its educational program, and shall make such revisions as it deems necessary. Any revised educational program shall conform to the requirements of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
