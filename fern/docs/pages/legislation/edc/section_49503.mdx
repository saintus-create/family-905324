# EDC § 49503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 8. Meals

**Citation:** EDC § 49503

---

## Text

School district funds may also be used for the purchase of school meals for pupils, as provided in Sections 49500 and 49501.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 600, Sec. 7. (SB 348) Effective January 1, 2024.)
