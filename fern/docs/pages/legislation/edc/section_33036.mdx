# EDC § 33036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 33036

---

## Text

The board shall cause the Department of General Services to do any printing required by it. All orders for printing shall first be approved by the Department of General Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
