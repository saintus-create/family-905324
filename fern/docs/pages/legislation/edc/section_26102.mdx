# EDC § 26102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26102

---

## Text

“Actuary” means a person professionally trained in the technical and mathematical aspects of insurance, pensions, and related fields who has been appointed by the board for the purpose of actuarial services required under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 231. Effective January 1, 1999.)
