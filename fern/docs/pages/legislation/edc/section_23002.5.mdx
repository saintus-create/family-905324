# EDC § 23002.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23002.5

---

## Text

Member and employer contributions from school districts conducting a year-round school operation or a continuous school program shall be reported as part of the school year in which the service began.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
