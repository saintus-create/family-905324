# EDC § 48070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Admission > Article: 7. Promotion and Retention

**Citation:** EDC § 48070

---

## Text

The governing board of each school district and each county superintendent of schools shall adopt policies regarding pupil promotion and retention. A pupil shall be promoted or retained only as provided in the policies adopted pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 81. Effective July 28, 1983.)
