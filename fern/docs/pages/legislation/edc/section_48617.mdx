# EDC § 48617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Attendance at Adjustment Schools > Article: 1. Twenty-four Hour Elementary Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 48617

---

## Text

The county probation officer having management and control of a 24-hour school may enter into a contract with any other county or state agency to provide for the supervision, care and treatment of the minors placed in the 24-hour school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
