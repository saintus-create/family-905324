# EDC § 56836.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7.2. Special Education Funding > Article: 2.2. Special Education Funding Formula

**Citation:** EDC § 56836.14

---

## Text

Commencing with the 2020–21 fiscal year and for each fiscal year thereafter, the Superintendent shall annually calculate the special education funding formula pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 24, Sec. 76. (SB 98) Effective June 29, 2020.)
