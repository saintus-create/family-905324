# EDC § 92511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92511

---

## Text

An indenture pursuant to which bonds are issued may include any and all such convenants and agreements on the part of the regents as the regents deems necessary or advisable for the better security of the bonds issued pursuant to the indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
