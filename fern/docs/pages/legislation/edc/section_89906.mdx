# EDC § 89906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Auxiliary Organizations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 89906

---

## Text

No member of the governing board of an auxiliary organization shall be financially interested in any contract or other transaction entered into by the board of which he is a member, and any contract or transaction entered into in violation of this section is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
