# EDC § 18110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** EDC § 18110

---

## Text

County boards of education may adopt lists of books and other library materials for districts not employing a superintendent of schools or a librarian for full time. The lists may be distributed to all school districts in a county for use in the selection of books and other library materials.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 157.)
