# EDC § 54694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 29. PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous Programs > Article: 5. Partnership Academies

**Citation:** EDC § 54694

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall develop guidelines with respect to the California Partnership Academies. The guidelines shall include, but not be limited to, enrollment provisions, application procedures, and student eligibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 574, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
