# EDC § 10851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 7. JOINT PROGRAMS, SERVICES, AND POWERS (COUNTIES, SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND HIGHER EDUCATION) > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.5. California Cradle-to-Career Data System Act > Article: 1. Cradle-to-Career Data System Workgroup

**Citation:** EDC § 10851

---

## Text

The California Cradle-to-Career Data System Workgroup created pursuant to former Section 10853, as it read on January 1, 2024, is hereby dissolved.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 736, Sec. 2. (AB 2723) Effective September 27, 2024.)
