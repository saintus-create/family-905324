# EDC § 53023.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 16.1. Golden State Pathways Program

**Citation:** EDC § 53023.5

---

## Text

Community college districts may partner with local educational agencies to submit applications to receive funding under this program to support the offering of a Golden State Pathways Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 52, Sec. 88. (AB 181) Effective June 30, 2022.)
