# EDC § 8100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Career Technical Education > Article: 6. Approval of Courses

**Citation:** EDC § 8100

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall approve courses of career technical training for the purposes of loans authorized by Section 7185 of the Financial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1058, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2001.)
