# EDC § 235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 6. Alternative Schools, Charter Schools, and School Choice

**Citation:** EDC § 235

---

## Text

There shall be no discrimination on the basis of the characteristics listed in Section 220 in any aspect of the operation of alternative schools or charter schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 569, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2008.)
