# EDC § 1047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 2. Duties and Responsibilities

**Citation:** EDC § 1047

---

## Text

A county board of education, to which all of the duties and functions of the county board of supervisors have been transferred pursuant to Section 1080 may exercise the power of eminent domain to acquire any property necessary or convenient for carrying out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 143, Sec. 1. Effective May 30, 1980.)
