# EDC § 2572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. County Tax Rates, County Superintendents > Article: 4. Allocation of Property Tax Revenues

**Citation:** EDC § 2572

---

## Text

The product computed pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 2571 is the amount of property tax revenues to be allocated to special education programs. This amount shall be subtracted pursuant to Section 56836.15.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 24, Sec. 2. (SB 98) Effective June 29, 2020.)
