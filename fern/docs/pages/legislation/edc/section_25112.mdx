# EDC § 25112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 39. School Employer Retirement Investment Product Vendor Registration Process and Information Bank

**Citation:** EDC § 25112

---

## Text

Personnel, including elected school officials, acting on behalf of an employer, may not receive consideration from a vendor in exchange for the promotion of a particular vendor or vendor’s products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 230, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2009.)
