# EDC § 5024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Election of School District Board Members > Article: 1. Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5024

---

## Text

The county committee may provide that an election to elect trustees, pursuant to its recommendations under Section 5023, shall be held at the same time as is held the election to effect a change in trustee areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
