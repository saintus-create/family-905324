# EDC § 22323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 22323

---

## Text

The system shall report monthly to the board concerning outstanding death benefits payable that have not been paid within six months of the notification of the death of the member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 82. Effective January 1, 1997.)
