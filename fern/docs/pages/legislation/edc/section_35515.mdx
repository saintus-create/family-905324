# EDC § 35515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 35515

---

## Text

“Component district” means an elementary school district that is included within a high school district or an elementary school district that is excluded from an action to unify a high school district but that continues to feed into the high schools of the new unified school district pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 35542.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 448, Sec. 3. (SB 148) Effective January 1, 2016.)
