# EDC § 92523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92523

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause defining the power of the regents in applying the proceeds of the sale of any issue of bonds for the acquiring, constructing, or completing of any project or any part of any project.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
