# EDC § 92437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 2. Powers of Regents

**Citation:** EDC § 92437

---

## Text

The regents may make contracts, leases, and agreements with any person or public corporation, political subdivision, city, county, district, or any agency of any person or such entity and may generally perform all acts necessary for the full exercise of the powers vested in the regents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
