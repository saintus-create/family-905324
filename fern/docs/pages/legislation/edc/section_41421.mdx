# EDC § 41421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 7. Conditions Disqualifying School Districts from Apportionments

**Citation:** EDC § 41421

---

## Text

A school district is a newly formed district up to the close of the fiscal year in which its formation became effective for all purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
