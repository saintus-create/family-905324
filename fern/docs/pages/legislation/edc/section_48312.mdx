# EDC § 48312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 7. Pupil Attendance Alternatives

**Citation:** EDC § 48312

---

## Text

(a) Each school district may make information regarding its schools, programs, policies, and procedures available to any interested person upon request.

(b) A school district of choice shall make public announcements regarding its schools, programs, policies, and procedures, including transportation options pursuant to Section 48311, if applicable, during the enrollment period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 15, Sec. 39. (AB 99) Effective June 27, 2017.)
