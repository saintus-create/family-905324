# EDC § 47600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26.8. CHARTER SCHOOLS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Charter Schools Act of 1992.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 781, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
