# EDC § 100800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68.2. KINDERGARTEN–UNIVERSITY PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2004 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** EDC § 100800

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Kindergarten-University Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 33, Sec. 31. Approved in Proposition 55 at the March 2, 2004, election.)
