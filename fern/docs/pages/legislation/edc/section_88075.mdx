# EDC § 88075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 88075

---

## Text

The commission may, with respect to the staff of the commission, expend funds for their orientation, training, retraining, and development and for any purpose prescribed by Article 7 (commencing with Section 88220) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
