# EDC § 67345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 14.4. Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of June 1990 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 67345

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Higher Education Facilities Bond Act of June 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 121 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
