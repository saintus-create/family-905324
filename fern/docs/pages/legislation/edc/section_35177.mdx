# EDC § 35177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 35177

---

## Text

The governing board of a district may by resolution limit campaign expenditures or contributions in elections to district offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
