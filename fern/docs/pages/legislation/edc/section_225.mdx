# EDC § 225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education Act

**Citation:** EDC § 225

---

## Text

This article shall not preclude father-son or mother-daughter activities at an educational institution, provided that if such activities are offered for students of one sex, opportunities for reasonably comparable activities are offered for students of the other sex.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1117, Sec. 1.)
