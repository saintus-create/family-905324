# EDC § 1922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 15. Emergency Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 1922

---

## Text

The county superintendent of schools may, with the approval of the county board of education, insure all real and personal property constructed, purchased, or otherwise provided by him for emergency schools established by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
