# EDC § 51853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 13. Driver Training

**Citation:** EDC § 51853

---

## Text

Any reference in this code to automobile driver training shall be deemed to refer to the laboratory phase of driver education described by Section 51852.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
