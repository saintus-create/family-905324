# EDC § 24202.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 27. Service Retirement

**Citation:** EDC § 24202.8

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the system identify and propose all statutory changes necessary to fully effectuate the implementation of the changes established in Sections 24202.6 and 24202.7 in all relevant statutes by June 30, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 296, Sec. 5. (AB 340) Effective January 1, 2013.)
