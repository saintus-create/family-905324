# EDC § 92030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Meetings of the Regents

**Citation:** EDC § 92030

---

## Text

All meetings of the Regents of the University of California shall, except as otherwise provided in this article, be subject to Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1195, Sec. 1.)
