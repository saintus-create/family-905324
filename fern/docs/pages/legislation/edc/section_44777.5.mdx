# EDC § 44777.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.66. Latino Heritage Resource Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 44777.5

---

## Text

Funds allocated to a regional Latino heritage resource center pursuant to this chapter shall supplement and not supplant funding for the efforts of these centers to develop collections and to provide teacher training or educational services to public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 308, Sec. 16. Effective August 3, 1995.)
