# EDC § 44258.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 4. Credential Types

**Citation:** EDC § 44258.15

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 44258.1, the holder of a credential authorizing instruction in a self-contained classroom may provide instruction in a team teaching setting or may regroup pupils across classrooms in subjects authorized by the governing board of the school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 922, Sec. 80. Effective January 1, 1995.)
