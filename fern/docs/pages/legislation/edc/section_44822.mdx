# EDC § 44822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 1. Rights and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 44822

---

## Text

The principal of any high school may act as principal of any elementary school situated in the high school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
