# EDC § 51891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Comprehensive Health Education > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 51891

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “community participation” means the active participation in the planning, implementation, and evaluation of comprehensive health education by parents, professional practicing health care and public safety personnel, and public and private health care and service agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 809.)
