# EDC § 26203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26203

---

## Text

The board may amortize any unfunded actuarial obligation in accordance with standards established by the Actuarial Standards Board and Governmental Accounting Standards Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 592, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1996.)
