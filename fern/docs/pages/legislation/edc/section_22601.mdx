# EDC § 22601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. Exclusions from Membership

**Citation:** EDC § 22601

---

## Text

Persons serving as exchange teachers or sojourn teachers from outside of this state are excluded from membership in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 1997.)
