# EDC § 8317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 13. Administrative Review

**Citation:** EDC § 8317

---

## Text

Except for causes listed in Sections 8315 and 8316, termination of a California state preschool program contract shall not occur without good cause and without notice as described in Section 8313 at least 90 days before the effective date given in the notice of termination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8407 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 215. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
