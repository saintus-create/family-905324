# EDC § 51220.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 3. Courses of Study, Grades 7 to 12

**Citation:** EDC § 51220.1

---

## Text

In addition to the requirements specified in subdivision (j) of Section 51220, automobile driver education shall be designed to develop a knowledge of the dangers involved in consuming alcohol or drugs in connection with the operation of a motor vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1455, Sec. 2.)
