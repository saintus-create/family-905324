# EDC § 12511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Interstate Agreements > Article: 2. Compact for Education

**Citation:** EDC § 12511

---

## Text

Pursuant to subdivision (i) of Article III of the compact, the commission shall file a copy of its bylaws and any amendment thereto with the Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 829, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1999.)
