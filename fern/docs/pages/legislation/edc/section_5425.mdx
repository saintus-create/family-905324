# EDC § 5425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 8. Cost of Elections

**Citation:** EDC § 5425

---

## Text

The cost of the election for the formation of a community college district shall be paid from the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
