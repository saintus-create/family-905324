# EDC § 33803.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Office of Civil Rights

**Citation:** EDC § 33803.5

---

## Text

The Office of Civil Rights shall employ the LGBTQ Discrimination Prevention Coordinator, who shall be appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 429, Sec. 4. (SB 48) Effective January 1, 2026.)
