# EDC § 66013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 66013

---

## Text

Each segment of public higher education shall strive for excellence in its sphere, as assigned in this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
