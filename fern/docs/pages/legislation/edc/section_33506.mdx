# EDC § 33506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 2. Educational Innovation and Planning Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33506

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall serve without compensation, except that they shall receive their actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties and responsibilities, including traveling expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
