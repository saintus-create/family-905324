# EDC § 27303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14. Distribution of Benefits

**Citation:** EDC § 27303

---

## Text

Any overpayment to a participant or beneficiary under this part shall be deducted from any subsequent benefit payment that may be payable under the plan, except as provided in Section 27303.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 207, Sec. 27. (AB 2260) Effective January 1, 2011.)
