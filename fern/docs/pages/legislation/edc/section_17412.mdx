# EDC § 17412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Property:  Sale,Lease, Exchange > Article: 2. Leasing Property

**Citation:** EDC § 17412

---

## Text

An election held pursuant to Section 17409 or Section 17413 shall be held in conjunction with either a statewide primary or general election, or an election date specified in Section 1000 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 588, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2007.)
