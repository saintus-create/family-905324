# EDC § 92120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Treasury > Article: 2. Transfer of Land Acquired by Tax Sales to the University

**Citation:** EDC § 92120

---

## Text

While any mortgage to the regents appears of record and not satisfied of record, no lands which have been sold to the state for taxes and the deed to which has been filed with the Controller, shall be sold or disposed of, except as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
