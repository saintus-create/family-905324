# EDC § 69516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 2. The Student Aid Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 69516

---

## Text

The commission shall work cooperatively with the State Department of Social Services to develop an automated system to verify a student’s status as a foster youth to aid in the processing of applications for federal financial aid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 722, Sec. 1. (SB 12) Effective January 1, 2018.)
