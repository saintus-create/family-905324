# EDC § 1605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. County School Service Fund > Article: 1. County School Service Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 1605

---

## Text

The title to all property purchased by the county superintendent of schools from the county school service fund is in the offices of the county superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
