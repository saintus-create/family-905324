# EDC § 48431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Compulsory Continuation Education > Article: 3. Continuation Classes

**Citation:** EDC § 48431

---

## Text

The governing board of each high school district shall establish and maintain a program of guidance, placement, and followup for all minors within the district subject to compulsory continuation education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
