# EDC § 101000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 69. KINDERGARTEN–UNIVERSITY PUBLIC EDUCATION FACILITIES BOND ACT OF 2006 > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** EDC § 101000

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Kindergarten-University Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 2006.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 35, Sec. 16. Approved in Proposition 1D at the November 7, 2006, election.)
