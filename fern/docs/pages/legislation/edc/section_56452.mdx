# EDC § 56452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Career and Vocational Education Programs, Transition Services, and Project Workability > Article: 1. Career and Vocational Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 56452

---

## Text

The superintendent shall ensure that the state annually secures all federal funds available for career and vocational education of individuals with exceptional needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1484, Sec. 5.)
