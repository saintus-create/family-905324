# EDC § 70031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 23. California DREAM Loan Program

**Citation:** EDC § 70031

---

## Text

The California DREAM Loan Program, which may also be referred to as the DREAM Program, is hereby established.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 754, Sec. 3. (SB 1210) Effective January 1, 2015.)
