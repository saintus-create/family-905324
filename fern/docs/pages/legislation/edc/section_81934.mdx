# EDC § 81934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Revenue Bond Act of 1961

**Citation:** EDC § 81934

---

## Text

Immediately after the adoption of a resolution by the board, directing the preparation of any bonds authorized under this chapter, the county treasurer shall prepare the requisite number of suitable bonds of the denominations and in accordance with the specifications contained in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 251, Sec. 28. Effective June 11, 1982. Operative July 1, 1982, by Sec. 47 of Ch. 251.)
