# EDC § 8633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.6. California Summer Science and Technology Academy

**Citation:** EDC § 8633

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by resolution, make that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1443, Sec. 1.)
