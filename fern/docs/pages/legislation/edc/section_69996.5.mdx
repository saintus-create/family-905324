# EDC § 69996.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 19.5. California Kids Investment and Development Savings Program

**Citation:** EDC § 69996.5

---

## Text

The board may consider marketing the program to California residents. The board may include in its marketing efforts information designed to educate California residents about the benefits of saving for higher education and information to help them decide the combination of savings strategies that may be appropriate for them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 53, Sec. 16. (SB 77) Effective July 1, 2019.)
