# EDC § 49119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Employment of Minors > Article: 2. Permits to Work

**Citation:** EDC § 49119

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall require a minor to obtain a permit to work in order for such minor to participate in horseback riding exhibitions, contests or events specified in paragraph (3) of subdivision (b) of Section 1308 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
