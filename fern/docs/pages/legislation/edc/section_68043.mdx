# EDC § 68043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 41. UNIFORM STUDENT RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Student Residency Requirements > Article: 3. Classification

**Citation:** EDC § 68043

---

## Text

A student classified as a nonresident shall not obtain resident classification as a result of maintaining continuous attendance at an institution without meeting the other requirements of this part for obtaining such classification.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
