# EDC § 18102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. School Libraries > Article: 1. Establishment and Maintenance

**Citation:** EDC § 18102

---

## Text

Libraries may be established and maintained under the control of the governing board of any school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 155.)
