# EDC § 81600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Management and Control of Property > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 81600

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district shall manage and control school property within its district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
