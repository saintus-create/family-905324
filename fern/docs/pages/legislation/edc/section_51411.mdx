# EDC § 51411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Diplomas and Certificates > Article: 2. Diplomas

**Citation:** EDC § 51411

---

## Text

No governing board of any school district maintaining a high school shall require as a condition for graduation from the high schools within the district that a pupil have resided within the district for any minimum length of time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
