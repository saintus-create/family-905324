# EDC § 52507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 52507

---

## Text

Governing boards shall have the authority to provide for granting appropriate credits, certificates, diplomas or other recognition of skill or accomplishment in such classes which such districts are otherwise authorized to grant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
