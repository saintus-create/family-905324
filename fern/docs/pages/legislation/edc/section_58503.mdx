# EDC § 58503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 31. EXPERIMENTAL SCHOOL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Alternative Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 58503

---

## Text

Teachers employed and students enrolled in the alternative school shall be selected entirely from volunteers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
