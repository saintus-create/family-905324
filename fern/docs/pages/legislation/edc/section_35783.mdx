# EDC § 35783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 5. Lapsation

**Citation:** EDC § 35783

---

## Text

After the hearing, and at least 30 days before the end of the school year, the county committee shall order the territory annexed to one or more adjoining districts as seems to the county committee to be in the best interest of the adjoining districts and the residents of the lapsed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 327, Sec. 7. (AB 1599) Effective January 1, 2015.)
