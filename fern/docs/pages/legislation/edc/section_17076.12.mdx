# EDC § 17076.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 9. Program Accountability

**Citation:** EDC § 17076.12

---

## Text

Any school district using funds allocated pursuant to this chapter for the construction or modernization of a school building shall have a participation goal of at least 3 percent, per year, of the overall dollar amount expended each year by the school district, for disabled veteran business enterprises.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 17076.11 by Stats. 2026, Ch. 65, Sec. 34. (AB 126) Effective July 9, 2026.)
