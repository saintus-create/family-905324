# EDC § 26501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 26501

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 26504, the participant shall contribute an amount equivalent to 4 percent of salary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1999.)
