# EDC § 24807.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 34. Administration of the District Retirement Salary Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24807.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 24807, the annuity reserve fund board may adopt rules allowing participants who have reached 70 years of age to withdraw their benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
