# EDC § 16315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. State School Building Aid Law of 1952 > Article: 9. School Housing Aid for Rehabilitation and Replacement of Structurally Inadequate School Facilities

**Citation:** EDC § 16315

---

## Text

The State Allocation Board shall administer this article. The Director of General Services shall provide the assistance to the board as it may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
