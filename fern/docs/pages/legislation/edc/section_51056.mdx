# EDC § 51056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 4. Enforcement of Courses of Study

**Citation:** EDC § 51056

---

## Text

A course of study for each adult school shall be prepared under the direction of the governing board of the district maintaining the adult school and shall be subject to approval of the Department of Education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
