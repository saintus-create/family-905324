# EDC § 56195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2.5. Governance > Article: 1. Local Plans

**Citation:** EDC § 56195

---

## Text

Each special education local plan area, as defined in subdivision (d) of Section 56195.1, shall administer local plans submitted pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 56205) and shall administer the allocation of funds pursuant to Chapter 7.2 (commencing with Section 56836).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 896, Sec. 57. Effective September 29, 2004.)
