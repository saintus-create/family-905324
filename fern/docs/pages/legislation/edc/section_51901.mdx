# EDC § 51901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Comprehensive Health Education > Article: 3. Department of Education

**Citation:** EDC § 51901

---

## Text

The Department of Education shall be responsible for the preparation and distribution of health education materials and for providing assistance for in-service teaching programs carried out with districts that have plans approved pursuant to Section 51911.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 809.)
