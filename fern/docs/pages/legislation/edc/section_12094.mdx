# EDC § 12094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 6. Veterans’ Education

**Citation:** EDC § 12094

---

## Text

The Department of Education shall exercise general supervision over, and shall provide for the coordination of, all services and facilities performed and provided by school districts for the education of veterans to the end that the needs of the veterans shall be met in the most adequate manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
