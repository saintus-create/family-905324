# EDC § 24944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 35. Joint District Salary Retirement Plan > Article: 2. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 24944

---

## Text

Whenever by the provisions of this chapter, the governing boards of two or more school districts are required, or authorized, to perform any act, the act may be done only at joint meetings of the boards, and no action shall be taken by the boards except upon the affirmative vote of a majority of the members of a majority of the boards.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
