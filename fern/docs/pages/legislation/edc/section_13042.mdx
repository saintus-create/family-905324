# EDC § 13042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8.7. CALIFORNIA NATIVE AMERICAN PUBLIC EDUCATION GRANT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS

**Citation:** EDC § 13042

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2003, the State Librarian shall report to the Governor and the appropriate fiscal and policy committees of each house of the Legislature on the use of funds provided for the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 870, Sec. 2. Effective October 14, 2001.)
