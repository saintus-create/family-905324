# EDC § 45050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 5. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 45050

---

## Text

Instead of issuing a single warrant to each employee for salary or wages the district may use a payroll form of warrant making payment to two or more employees on one payroll warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
