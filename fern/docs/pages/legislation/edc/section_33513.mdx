# EDC § 33513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 2. Educational Innovation and Planning Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33513

---

## Text

The State Board of Education shall allocate funds under Title IV, Part C, to provide to the extent feasible a geographical spread of experimental projects in the state. All such grants for a particular project shall be limited to a five-year period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1354, Sec. 15. Effective September 30, 1980.)
