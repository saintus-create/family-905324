# EDC § 49468.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Pupil and Personnel Health > Article: 4.6. The Seizure Safe Schools Act

**Citation:** EDC § 49468.4

---

## Text

Emergency anti-seizure medication or medication prescribed to a pupil to treat the pupil’s seizures, a seizure disorder, or epilepsy symptoms shall be provided to the school with the label affixed by the dispensing pharmacy intact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 906, Sec. 1. (AB 1810) Effective January 1, 2023.)
