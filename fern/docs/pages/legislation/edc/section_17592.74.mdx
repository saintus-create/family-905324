# EDC § 17592.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Property Maintenance and Control > Article: 1.5. School Assessments of Buildings and Emergency Repairs Grant Program

**Citation:** EDC § 17592.74

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the funds provided to school districts from the School Facilities Emergency Repair Account pursuant to this article for the purpose of emergency repair grants shall not be deposited into a school district deferred maintenance fund for purposes described in Section 17582.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 65. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
