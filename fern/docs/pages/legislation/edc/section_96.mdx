# EDC § 96

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 96

---

## Text

“At-promise” has the same meaning as “at-risk,” as that term is generally understood, and consistent with federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 800, Sec. 1. (AB 413) Effective January 1, 2020.)
