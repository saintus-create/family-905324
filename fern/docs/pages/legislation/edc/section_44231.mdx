# EDC § 44231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 2. Commission on Teacher Credentialing

**Citation:** EDC § 44231

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specified, the meetings of the commission shall be open and public and due notice of their time and place shall be posted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
