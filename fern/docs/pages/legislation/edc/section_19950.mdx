# EDC § 19950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. California Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19950

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 49, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 85 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
