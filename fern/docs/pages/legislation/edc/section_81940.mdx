# EDC § 81940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Revenue Bond Act of 1961

**Citation:** EDC § 81940

---

## Text

Pending the actual issuance or delivery of revenue bonds, the board may issue temporary or interim bonds, certificates or receipts of any denomination whatsoever, and with or without coupons, to be exchanged for definitive bonds when ready for delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 251, Sec. 28. Effective June 11, 1982. Operative July 1, 1982, by Sec. 47 of Ch. 251.)
