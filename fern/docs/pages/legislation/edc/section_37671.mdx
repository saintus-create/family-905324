# EDC § 37671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 22. SCHOOL OPERATIONS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Year-Round Scheduling > Article: 1. Multitrack Year-Round Scheduling

**Citation:** EDC § 37671

---

## Text

Schools in programs conducted pursuant to Section 37670 shall be exempted from the requirements of Section 37202.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 756, Sec. 6. Effective October 9, 1991.)
