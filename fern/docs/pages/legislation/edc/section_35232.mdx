# EDC § 35232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Governing Boards > Article: 7. Corrupt Practices

**Citation:** EDC § 35232

---

## Text

Any person may be compelled to testify in any lawful investigation or judicial proceeding against any person who is charged with any offense described in Sections 35230 or 35231.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
