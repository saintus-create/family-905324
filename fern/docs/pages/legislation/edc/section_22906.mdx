# EDC § 22906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Employee Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 22906

---

## Text

A member’s contributions that were made with respect to service that was erroneously credited under the Defined Benefit Program shall be returned to the member if the contributions for that service cannot be credited under the Defined Benefit Supplement Program pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1021, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2001.)
