# EDC § 84900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 50. FINANCE > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Apportionment > Article: 9. Adult Education Program

**Citation:** EDC § 84900

---

## Text

The Adult Education Program is hereby established under the administration of the Chancellor of the California Community Colleges and the Superintendent of Public Instruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 33, Sec. 40. (AB 1809) Effective June 27, 2018.)
