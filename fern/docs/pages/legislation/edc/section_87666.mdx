# EDC § 87666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 4. Evaluations and Discipline

**Citation:** EDC § 87666

---

## Text

During the school year, all contract and regular employees are subject to dismissal and the imposition of penalties on the grounds and pursuant to procedures set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
