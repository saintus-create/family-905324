# EDC § 89412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Child Development Centers > Article: 2. San Francisco State University

**Citation:** EDC § 89412

---

## Text

No child shall be admitted to the center unless he meets the requirements for admittance to a child development center.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
