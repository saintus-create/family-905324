# EDC § 41840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 9. Computation of Allowances for Schools and Classes for Prisoners

**Citation:** EDC § 41840

---

## Text

A school district or county superintendent of schools may only claim average daily attendance for apportionment purposes for schools or classes maintained for adults in correctional facilities if those classes meet the requirements of Section 41976.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 28. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
