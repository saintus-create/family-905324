# EDC § 89344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Students > Article: 5. Higher Education Outreach and Assistance Act for Foster Youth

**Citation:** EDC § 89344

---

## Text

Representation on the appropriate California State University Advisory Councils shall be expanded to include at least one foster youth who is either a current or former student at the university.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 763, Sec. 5. (AB 1567) Effective January 1, 2018.)
