# EDC § 59040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. California Schools for the Deaf > Article: 3. Services

**Citation:** EDC § 59040

---

## Text

The Department of Education, in connection with the California School for the Deaf, may establish and maintain a preschool and kindergarten service for the care and teaching of children under school age. The department shall prescribe the rules and regulations which shall govern the conduct of the preschool and kindergarten service, appoint such teachers as it determines necessary, and fix their salaries.
