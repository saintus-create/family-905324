# EDC § 56026.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 56026.2

---

## Text

“Language mode” means the method of communication used by hard-of-hearing and deaf children that may include the use of sign language to send or receive messages or the use of spoken language, with or without visual signs or cues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1126, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1994. Implementation contingent upon funding, as prescribed by Sec. 6 of Ch. 1126.)
