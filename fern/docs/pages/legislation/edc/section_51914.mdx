# EDC § 51914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Comprehensive Health Education > Article: 4. Comprehensive Health Education Plans

**Citation:** EDC § 51914

---

## Text

No plan shall be approved by the State Board of Education unless it determines that the plan was developed with the active cooperation of parents, community, and teachers, in all stages of planning, approval, and implementation of the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 809.)
