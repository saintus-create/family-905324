# EDC § 19306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19306

---

## Text

The State Librarian shall nominate a Deputy State Librarian to be appointed by, and hold office at the pleasure of, the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 11. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
