# EDC § 33110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 33110

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction may employ one Deputy Superintendent of Public Instruction and necessary clerical and expert assistants, and may fix the compensation of all statutory and other employees as provided by law, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
