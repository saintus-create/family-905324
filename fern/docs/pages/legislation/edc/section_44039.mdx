# EDC § 44039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 2. Rights and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 44039

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may use school district funds for cash deposits, when required to guarantee payment for health plans purchased on credit for school district employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
