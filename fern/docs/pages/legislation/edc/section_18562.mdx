# EDC § 18562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 15. Tax for Interest and Redemption of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18562

---

## Text

All money levied, when collected, shall be paid into the county treasury to the credit of the library district, and be used for the payment of principal and interest on the bonds, and for no other purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
