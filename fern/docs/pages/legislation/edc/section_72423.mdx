# EDC § 72423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 72423

---

## Text

The governing board of each community college district shall provide for the payment of the travel expenses of any representatives of the board when performing services directed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 345.)
