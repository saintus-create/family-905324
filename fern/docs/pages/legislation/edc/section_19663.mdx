# EDC § 19663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Library Districts and Museums in Unincorporated Towns and Villages > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 19663

---

## Text

The title to all property acquired for the purposes of the libraries, when not inconsistent with the terms of its acquisition, or not otherwise designated, vests in the district in which libraries are, or are to be situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
