# EDC § 49091.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6.6. The Education Empowerment Act of 1998 > Article: 1. Parental Review

**Citation:** EDC § 49091.19

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall be construed as restricting teachers in the assignment of homework.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1031, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
