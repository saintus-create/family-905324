# EDC § 45250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 45250

---

## Text

The governing board may authorize payment to members of the commission an amount not to exceed fifty dollars ($50) per meeting and not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars ($250) per month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 723, Sec. 7.)
