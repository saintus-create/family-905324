# EDC § 5304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 5304

---

## Text

The duties of the governing board of any school district or community college district with respect to school district elections shall be:

(a) To order elections as authorized by this code.

(b) To resolve tie votes in governing board member elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
