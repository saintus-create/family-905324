# EDC § 48243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 4. Supervisors of Attendance

**Citation:** EDC § 48243

---

## Text

Two or more districts may unite in appointing a district supervisor of attendance and in paying his compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
