# EDC § 51003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 1. Legislative Intent

**Citation:** EDC § 51003

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that explicit, rigorous statewide academic standards be adopted for all pupils enrolled in public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 274, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
