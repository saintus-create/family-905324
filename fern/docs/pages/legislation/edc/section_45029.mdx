# EDC § 45029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 5. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 45029

---

## Text

Teachers in an opportunity school or opportunity classes shall be paid in the same manner as in other elementary schools of the city, city and county, or the school district in which the opportunity school or opportunity classes are situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
