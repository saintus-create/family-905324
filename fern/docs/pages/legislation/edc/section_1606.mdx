# EDC § 1606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. County School Service Fund > Article: 1. County School Service Fund

**Citation:** EDC § 1606

---

## Text

The money credited to the county school service fund of a county pursuant to Section 16417 of the Government Code may be apportioned by the county superintendent of schools, with the approval of the county board of education, to school districts within which there is land which has been acquired by the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 31. Operative January 1, 1992, by Sec. 709 of Ch. 1372.)
