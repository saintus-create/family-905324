# EDC § 22902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Employee Contributions

**Citation:** EDC § 22902

---

## Text

Members’ accumulated retirement contributions and those other contributions required for credited service under this part shall be in the amounts required based on rates of contribution applicable for the years included in that period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 1999.)
