# EDC § 78043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Programs > Article: 3. Baccalaureate Degree Program

**Citation:** EDC § 78043

---

## Text

Baccalaureate degree programs approved by the Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges pursuant to this article before January 1, 2022, may continue to operate.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 565, Sec. 6. (AB 927) Effective January 1, 2022.)
