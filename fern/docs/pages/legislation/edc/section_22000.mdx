# EDC § 22000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 22000

---

## Text

This part may be known and cited as the E. Richard Barnes Act and together with Part 14 (commencing with Section 26000) shall be known as the Teachers’ Retirement Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 939, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
