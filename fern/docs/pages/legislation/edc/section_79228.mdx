# EDC § 79228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 7. Support Services for Foster Youth Attending Community College

**Citation:** EDC § 79228

---

## Text

The board of governors may authorize the chancellor, consistent with the requirements the board may impose, to designate up to 1 percent of the funds allocated pursuant to this article for program administration, program development, and program accountability.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 54, Sec. 32. (AB 183) Effective June 30, 2022. See ongoing operational condition prescribed in Section 79227.)
