# EDC § 12116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 8. PARTICIPATION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS AND INTERSTATE AGREEMENTS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Administration of Federal Programs—State Board of Education > Article: 7. Surplus Federal Property

**Citation:** EDC § 12116

---

## Text

The California State Agency for Donated Food Distribution may send representatives out of this state for the purpose of inspecting food commodities or conferring with federal and other state officials, provided the traveling and expenses have been approved by the Governor and by the Department of Finance. The out-of-state travel shall not come within the limitation imposed in Section 33335 or 71049.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 196, Sec. 6. Effective June 13, 1984. Operative July 1, 1984, by Sec. 8.5 of Ch. 196.)
