# EDC § 33473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 12. Intergenerational Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 33473

---

## Text

The State Department of Education may consult with the Department of Aging and the Commission on Aging regarding the criteria for selection of programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1592, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
