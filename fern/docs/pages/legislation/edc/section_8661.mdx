# EDC § 8661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.8. California State Summer School for Mathematics and Science

**Citation:** EDC § 8661

---

## Text

The Regents of the University of California are hereby requested to operate the California State Summer School for Mathematics and Science.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 78, Sec. 6. Effective July 7, 1999.)
