# EDC § 79223.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 48. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous > Article: 7. Support Services for Foster Youth Attending Community College

**Citation:** EDC § 79223.5

---

## Text

All current and former foster youth who meet NextUp eligibility criteria specified in Sections 79222 and 79223 are eligible to enroll in the NextUp program and shall not be required to meet additional eligibility requirements as a condition for enrollment in the NextUp program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 572, Sec. 7. (AB 190) Effective September 27, 2022. See ongoing operational condition prescribed in Section 79227.)
