# EDC § 26301.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Employer and Participant Responsibilities

**Citation:** EDC § 26301.5

---

## Text

Each employer shall deduct from the salary of participants employed by the employer the participant contributions required by this part and shall remit to the system those contributions plus the employer contributions required by this part and Section 44987.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 257. Effective January 1, 1999.)
