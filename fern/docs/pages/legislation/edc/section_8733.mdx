# EDC § 8733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Environmental Education > Article: 3. Conservation Education Library

**Citation:** EDC § 8733

---

## Text

The library shall thoroughly evaluate new materials for validity, pertinence, objectivity, and usefulness, and shall advise the state board in the adoption of textbooks in regard to meeting the requirements for conservation education.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
