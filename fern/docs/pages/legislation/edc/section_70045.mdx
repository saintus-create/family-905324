# EDC § 70045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 24. California Ban on Scholarship Displacement Act of 2021

**Citation:** EDC § 70045

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Ban on Scholarship Displacement Act of 2021.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 925, Sec. 1. (AB 288) Effective January 1, 2023.)
