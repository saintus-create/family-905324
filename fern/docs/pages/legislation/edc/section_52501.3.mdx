# EDC § 52501.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 10. Adult Schools > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 52501.3

---

## Text

Each governing board maintaining classes for adults shall meet the requirements set forth in Sections 35145, 41010, 41011, 41015, 41020, 42103, 42600, 51040, 51041, 51050, and 51056.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
