# EDC § 19427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 19427

---

## Text

Each library trustee shall hold office until his successor is elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
