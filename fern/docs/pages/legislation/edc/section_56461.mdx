# EDC § 56461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4.5. Career and Vocational Education Programs, Transition Services, and Project Workability > Article: 2. Transition Services

**Citation:** EDC § 56461

---

## Text

The superintendent shall establish the capacity to provide transition services for a broad range of individuals with exceptional needs such as employment and academic training, strategic planning, interagency coordination, and parent training.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1484, Sec. 8.)
