# EDC § 66310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.2. Campus-Recognized Sorority and Fraternity Transparency Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Campus-Recognized Sorority and Fraternity Transparency Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 268, Sec. 1. (AB 524) Effective January 1, 2023.)
