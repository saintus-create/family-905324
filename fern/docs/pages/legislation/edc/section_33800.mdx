# EDC § 33800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Office of Civil Rights

**Citation:** EDC § 33800

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Local educational agency” means a school district, county office of education, or charter school.

(b) “Office” means the Office of Civil Rights established pursuant to Section 33801.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 428, Sec. 5. (AB 715) Effective January 1, 2026.)
