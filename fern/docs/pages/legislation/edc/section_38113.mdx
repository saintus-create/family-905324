# EDC § 38113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23. SUPPLEMENTAL SERVICES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. Apparatus and Supplies

**Citation:** EDC § 38113

---

## Text

The clerk of each district shall, under the direction of the board of trustees, provide all school supplies authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
