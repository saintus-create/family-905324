# EDC § 52702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Miscellaneous > Article: 1. School Farms

**Citation:** EDC § 52702

---

## Text

The governing board of the district may provide for the general supervision of the farm and the cost of the supervision may be a charge against the funds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
