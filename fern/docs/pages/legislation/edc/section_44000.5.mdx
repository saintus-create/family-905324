# EDC § 44000.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 44000.5

---

## Text

An “adverse action” means the denial of an application for a credential, a private admonition, or public reproval of a credential holder, or the suspension or revocation of a credential.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 342, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
