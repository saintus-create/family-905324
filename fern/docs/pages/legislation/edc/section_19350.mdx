# EDC § 19350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 6. Statewide Imagination Library Program

**Citation:** EDC § 19350

---

## Text

The Statewide Imagination Library Program is hereby established under the administration of the State Librarian for purposes of developing, implementing, promoting, and fostering a comprehensive statewide initiative for encouraging children from birth to five years of age, inclusive, to develop a love of reading and learning.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 992, Sec. 1. (SB 1183) Effective September 30, 2022.)
