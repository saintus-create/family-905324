# EDC § 35721.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Reorganization of School Districts > Article: 2. County Committee on School District Organization Plans and Recommendations for District Reorganization

**Citation:** EDC § 35721.5

---

## Text

Before initiating proceedings to consider any reorganization plan, the county committee on school district organization shall provide written notice of the proposed action to the local agency formation commission for the affected area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
