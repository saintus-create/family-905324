# EDC § 74014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Legislative Intent and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 74014

---

## Text

An “action to reorganize districts” means an action to transfer all or part of a district to another district, to annex nondistrict territory to a district, to form a new district, or to otherwise alter the boundaries of a community college district, or any combination of such actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
