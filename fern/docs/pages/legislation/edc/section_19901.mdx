# EDC § 19901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.5. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. Deposit of Newspapers in Libraries

**Citation:** EDC § 19901

---

## Text

Before making the deposit, the board of supervisors shall obtain from the board of trustees or authorities in charge of the free public library, or the State Librarian, as the case may be, an agreement that it will properly preserve and care for the newspaper files, and make them accessible to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 172.)
