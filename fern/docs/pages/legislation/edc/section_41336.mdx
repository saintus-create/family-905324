# EDC § 41336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State School Fund > Article: 3. Disbursements by Superintendent of Public Instruction

**Citation:** EDC § 41336

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall on or before June 25th of each year apportion to each school district or fund the total of the amounts allowed to the district or fund under Section 41972. This apportionment shall be called the final apportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
