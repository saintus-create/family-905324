# EDC § 22165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22165

---

## Text

“Retirement” means termination of employment subject to coverage by the plan and a change in status from an inactive member, an active member, or a disabled member to a retired member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1025, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
