# EDC § 8534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Adult Education > Article: 3. Functions of Continuing Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8534

---

## Text

Programs for adults involving postsecondary programs that meet the standards prescribed by the Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges for credit and noncredit courses are the responsibility of community college districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 78.)
