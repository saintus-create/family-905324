# EDC § 41934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 14. Allowances for Project Connected Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 41934

---

## Text

A district shall be eligible for an allowance under Section 41932 if the average daily attendance of project-connected pupils is at least 10 in the district during the preceding fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
