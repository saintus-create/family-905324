# EDC § 27000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. Death Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 27000

---

## Text

The death benefit shall become payable to the beneficiary upon receipt of proof of the participant’s death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 592, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1996.)
