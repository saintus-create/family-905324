# EDC § 99163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 65. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Standardized Tests

**Citation:** EDC § 99163

---

## Text

Any test sponsor who intentionally violates any provision of this chapter shall be liable for a civil penalty not to exceed seven hundred fifty dollars ($750) for each violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1505, Sec. 2.)
