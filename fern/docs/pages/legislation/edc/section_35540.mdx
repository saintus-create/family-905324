# EDC § 35540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Reorganization of School Districts—General Provisions > Article: 5. Territory of School Districts

**Citation:** EDC § 35540

---

## Text

The boundaries of each high school district and unified school district shall be coextensive with the boundaries of the component districts, as defined in Section 35515, included within it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 301, Sec. 2. (SB 1057) Effective January 1, 2023.)
