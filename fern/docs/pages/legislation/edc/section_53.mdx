# EDC § 53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 5. General Provisions, Public Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 53

---

## Text

The high schools of the state are designated as four-year high schools, junior high schools, senior high schools, continuation high schools, and evening high schools. Evening high schools may be designated as adult schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
