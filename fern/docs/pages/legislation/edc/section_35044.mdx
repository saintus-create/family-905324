# EDC § 35044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Districts > Article: 3. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 35044

---

## Text

The governing board of each school district shall provide for the payment of the traveling expenses of any representatives of the board when performing services directed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
