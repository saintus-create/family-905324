# EDC § 87806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 8. Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 87806

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district may at any time during any school year increase the salaries of persons employed by the district in academic positions. The increase shall be effective on any date ordered by the governing board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 160. Effective September 25, 1990.)
