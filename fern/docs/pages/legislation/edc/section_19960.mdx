# EDC § 19960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. California Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. California Library Construction and Renovation Program

**Citation:** EDC § 19960

---

## Text

This chapter shall be administered by the State Librarian, who shall adopt rules, regulations, and policies for the implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 49, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 85 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
