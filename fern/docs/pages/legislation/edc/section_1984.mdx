# EDC § 1984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. County Community Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 1984

---

## Text

For the purposes of establishing and maintaining a county community school, a county board of education shall be deemed to be a school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 992.)
