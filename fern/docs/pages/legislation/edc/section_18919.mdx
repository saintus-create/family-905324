# EDC § 18919

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Municipal Libraries > Article: 2. Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18919

---

## Text

The board of library trustees may make and enforce all rules, regulations, and bylaws necessary for the administration, government, and protection of the libraries under its management, and all property belonging thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
