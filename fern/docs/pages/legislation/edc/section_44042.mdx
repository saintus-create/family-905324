# EDC § 44042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Employees > Article: 2. Rights and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 44042

---

## Text

School districts may, but shall not be required to, provide payroll deduction for the collection of insurance premiums except as expressly authorized by Section 44041.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
