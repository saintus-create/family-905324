# EDC § 39832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 23.5. TRANSPORTATION > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Transportation Services > Article: 3. Schoolbuses

**Citation:** EDC § 39832

---

## Text

The name or names of the particular school or schools to which a schoolbus conveys pupils may be painted on the side of the bus, in the manner prescribed by the Department of the California Highway Patrol.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 646, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2000.)
