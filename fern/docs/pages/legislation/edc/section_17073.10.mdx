# EDC § 17073.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10. SCHOOL BONDS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12.5. Leroy F.Greene School Facilities Act of 1998 > Article: 6. Modernization Eligibility Determination

**Citation:** EDC § 17073.10

---

## Text

Each school district that desires to receive an apportionment for modernization under this chapter shall submit an application in a form, and in the number of copies, that the board may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 4. Effective August 27, 1998.)
