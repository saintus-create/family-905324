# EDC § 19302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19302

---

## Text

The California State Library is under the control of an executive who shall be a technically trained librarian and shall be known as the “State Librarian.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 10. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
