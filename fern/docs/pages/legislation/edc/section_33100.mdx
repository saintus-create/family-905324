# EDC § 33100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Superintendent of Public Instruction > Article: 1. Appointment and Salaries

**Citation:** EDC § 33100

---

## Text

A vacancy in the office of Superintendent of Public Instruction shall be filled by appointment by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
