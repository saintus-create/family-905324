# EDC § 60060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 4. Requirements, Publishers and Manufacturers

**Citation:** EDC § 60060

---

## Text

Every publisher or manufacturer of instructional materials offered for adoption or sale in California shall comply with all the requirements and provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
