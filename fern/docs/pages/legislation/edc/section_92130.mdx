# EDC § 92130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Treasury > Article: 3. Insurance

**Citation:** EDC § 92130

---

## Text

All property of the University of California held in fee or otherwise for purposes of income may be insured against damage or loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
