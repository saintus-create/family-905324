# EDC § 22856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 14.5. Military Service

**Citation:** EDC § 22856

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the extent it would require any action to be taken that would cause the plan or its members under this part to incur adverse tax consequences under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (Title 26 of the United States Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 107. Effective January 1, 1999.)
