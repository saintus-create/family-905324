# EDC § 81191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Sites > Article: 9. Disposal of Sites

**Citation:** EDC § 81191

---

## Text

The sale or transfer may be made for cash and other valuable consideration, or for other valuable consideration, as deemed appropriate by the governing board of the community college district. The sale or transfer may be made without first taking a vote of the electors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 36.)
