# EDC § 26208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26208

## Text

The board shall establish and maintain records and accounts following recognized accounting principles and controls with respect to the Cash Balance Benefit Program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 250. Effective January 1, 1999.)
