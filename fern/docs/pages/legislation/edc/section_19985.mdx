# EDC § 19985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. California Reading and Literacy Improvement and Public Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 19985

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Reading and Literacy Improvement and Public Library Construction and Renovation Bond Act of 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 14 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
