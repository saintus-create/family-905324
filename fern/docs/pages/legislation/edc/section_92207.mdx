# EDC § 92207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Special Colleges > Article: 1 A. Law College Affiliated with the University of California

**Citation:** EDC § 92207

---

## Text

The officers of the college are a dean, a registrar, and 11 directors. The dean and registrar shall be appointed by, and may be removed by the board of directors.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1155, Sec. 31.9.)
