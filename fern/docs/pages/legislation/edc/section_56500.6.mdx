# EDC § 56500.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Procedural Safeguards

**Citation:** EDC § 56500.6

---

## Text

Due process and state complaint procedures for children enrolled in private schools by their parents pursuant to Sections 56170 to 56174.5, inclusive, shall be in accordance with Section 300.140 of Title 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 82. Effective January 1, 2008.)
