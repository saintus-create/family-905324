# EDC § 92001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Name of the University

**Citation:** EDC § 92001

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 11425.10 of the Government Code, Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 11400) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code does not apply to a hearing conducted by the University of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
