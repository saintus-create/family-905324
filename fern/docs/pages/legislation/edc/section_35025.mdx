# EDC § 35025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 21. LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Districts > Article: 3. Officers and Agents

**Citation:** EDC § 35025

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district may employ a person not a member of the board to act as secretary and bookkeeper for the board, and may delegate to such secretary the duties prescribed in paragraphs (a) and (c) of Section 35250.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
