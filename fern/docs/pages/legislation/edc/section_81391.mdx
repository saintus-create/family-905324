# EDC § 81391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 5. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** EDC § 81391

---

## Text

Before the governing board of a community college district enters into a lease or agreement pursuant to this article, it shall own a site upon which a building to be used by the district and private person, firm, or corporation may be constructed and shall have complied with the provisions of law relating to the selection and approval of sites.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
