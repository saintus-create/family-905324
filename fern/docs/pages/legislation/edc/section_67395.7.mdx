# EDC § 67395.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 15.9. Uniform Informational Guidance for Survivors of Sexual Assault

**Citation:** EDC § 67395.7

---

## Text

The Trustees of the California State University shall, and the Regents of the University of California are requested to, implement this chapter from existing funds and resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 115, Sec. 1. (AB 1968) Effective January 1, 2023.)
