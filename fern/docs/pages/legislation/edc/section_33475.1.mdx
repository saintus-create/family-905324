# EDC § 33475.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 13. The California Stem Cell and Biotechnology Education and Workforce Development Act of 2009

**Citation:** EDC § 33475.1

---

## Text

The purpose of this article is to establish stem cell and biotechnology education and workforce development as a state priority and to promote stronger links among these industry sectors, the California Institute for Regenerative Medicine, and California public schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 185, Sec. 2. (SB 471) Effective January 1, 2010.)
