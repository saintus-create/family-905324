# EDC § 60090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Instructional Materials > Article: 6. Reports

**Citation:** EDC § 60090

---

## Text

Each district board shall make reports, whenever required, directly to the Superintendent of Public Instruction, concerning the instructional materials used in its schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
