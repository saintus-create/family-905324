# EDC § 33547.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. State Educational Commissions and Committees > Article: 3. Instructional Quality Commission

**Citation:** EDC § 33547.5

---

## Text

The commission shall, when the mathematics curriculum framework is next revised after January 1, 2025, consider including that pupils in grade 8 be offered the opportunity to take an Algebra I or Mathematics I course that is aligned to the content standards adopted by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 476, Sec. 1. (SB 1410) Effective January 1, 2025.)
