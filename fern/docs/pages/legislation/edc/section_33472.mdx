# EDC § 33472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. State Department of Education > Article: 12. Intergenerational Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 33472

---

## Text

The department shall require continuation of contract requirements, such as matching of funds or provision of in-kind services, and any other provisions determined necessary by the department for intergenerational programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 922, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 1995.)
