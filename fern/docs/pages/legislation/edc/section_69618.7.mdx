# EDC § 69618.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 5.5. Graduate Assumption Program of Loans for Education

**Citation:** EDC § 69618.7

---

## Text

The commission shall work in conjunction with lenders participating in the Federal Family Education Loan Program to develop a streamlined application process for participation in the program set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 330, Sec. 32. Effective August 21, 1998.)
