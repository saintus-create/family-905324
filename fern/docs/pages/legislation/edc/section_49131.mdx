# EDC § 49131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Employment of Minors > Article: 3. Permits to Work Full Time

**Citation:** EDC § 49131

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 49130, 49132, or 49134 or subdivision (d) of Section 49133, a permit to work full time may be issued to a minor over the age of 16 and under the age of 18.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
