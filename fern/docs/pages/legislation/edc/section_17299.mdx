# EDC § 17299

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 10.5. SCHOOL FACILITIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Construction of School Buildings > Article: 3. Approvals

**Citation:** EDC § 17299

---

## Text

In each case the application for approval of the plans shall be accompanied by the plans and full, complete, and accurate specifications, and structural design computations, and estimates of cost, which shall comply in every respect with any and all requirements prescribed by the Department of General Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
