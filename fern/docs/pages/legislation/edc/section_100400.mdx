# EDC § 100400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 68. PUBLIC EDUCATION BONDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Class Size Reduction Kindergarten–University Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1998

**Citation:** EDC § 100400

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Class Size Reduction Kindergarten–University Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 16. Approved in Proposition 1A at the November 3, 1998, election.)
