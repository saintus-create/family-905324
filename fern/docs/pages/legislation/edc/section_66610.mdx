# EDC § 66610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. California State University

**Citation:** EDC § 66610

---

## Text

The California State University shall comply with Section 668.18(c)(1)(ii) of Title 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations, as that section read on July 1, 2010. The Trustees of the California State University may adopt regulations to implement this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 145, Sec. 1. (SB 418) Effective January 1, 2016.)
