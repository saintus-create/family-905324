# EDC § 48912.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 1. Suspension or Expulsion

**Citation:** EDC § 48912.5

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district may suspend a pupil enrolled in a continuation school or class for a period not longer than the remainder of the semester if any of the acts enumerated in Section 48900 occurred. The suspension shall meet the requirements of Section 48915.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 91. Effective July 28, 1983.)
