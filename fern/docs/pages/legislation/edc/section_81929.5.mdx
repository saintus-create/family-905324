# EDC § 81929.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Community College Revenue Bond Act of 1961

**Citation:** EDC § 81929.5

---

## Text

Bonds shall bear interest at a rate of not to exceed 12 percent per annum, payable annually or semiannually, or in part annually and in part semiannually.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 33) by Stats. 1989, Ch. 321, Sec. 1.)
