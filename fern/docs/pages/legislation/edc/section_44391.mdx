# EDC § 44391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 12. California School Paraprofessional Teacher Training Program

**Citation:** EDC § 44391

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Classified School Employee Teacher Credentialing Program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 29, Sec. 13. (SB 828) Effective June 27, 2016.)
