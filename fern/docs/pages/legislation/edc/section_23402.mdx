# EDC § 23402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 21. Pre-1972 Benefits

**Citation:** EDC § 23402

---

## Text

Benefits payable on account of deaths that occurred prior to July 1, 1972, and provided under former Section 14193, as it read prior to July 1, 1972, shall be continued. Former Sections 14195 and 14196 as they read prior to that date shall continue to apply to these payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
