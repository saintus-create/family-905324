# EDC § 92601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. University of California Police

**Citation:** EDC § 92601

---

## Text

Every member of the University of California police department shall be supplied with, and authorized to wear, a badge bearing the words “University of California Police.”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
