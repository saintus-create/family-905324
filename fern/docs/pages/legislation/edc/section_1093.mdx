# EDC § 1093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 4. Salaries and Expenses

**Citation:** EDC § 1093

---

## Text

Compensation of the members of the board, shall be payable out of the county general fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
