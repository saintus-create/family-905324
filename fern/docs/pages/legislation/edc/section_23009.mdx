# EDC § 23009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23009

---

## Text

The board, if in the interest of the plan, may, in connection with adjustments to the required contributions referred to in Section 23008, receive or make payments directly from or to the member or beneficiary with interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 1997.)
