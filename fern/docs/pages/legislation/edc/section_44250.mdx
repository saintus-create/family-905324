# EDC § 44250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 4. Credential Types

**Citation:** EDC § 44250

---

## Text

The commission shall issue only the following two types of credentials, with authorizations as hereinafter defined:

(a) A teaching credential.

(b) A services credential.

The commission may issue an internship teaching or services credential.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 859, Sec. 2. Effective October 6, 1993.)
