# EDC § 48437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Compulsory Continuation Education > Article: 3. Continuation Classes

**Citation:** EDC § 48437

---

## Text

The governing board of a school district or school districts, as the case may be, maintaining special continuation education classes may accept and expend grants from the federal government or from other public or private sources for the purpose of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
