# EDC § 46115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 2. Kindergarten and Elementary Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 46115

---

## Text

The minimum schoolday in kindergartens and in grades 1 to 8, inclusive, in elementary schools, is exclusive of noon intermissions and, except in kindergartens, exclusive of recesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
