# EDC § 4001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 1. County Committees on School District Organization

**Citation:** EDC § 4001

---

## Text

If all of the territory of a county under the jurisdiction of the county superintendent of schools of the county is included in one unified school district, the governing board of the unified school district shall constitute the county committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 3. Effective September 22, 1982.)
