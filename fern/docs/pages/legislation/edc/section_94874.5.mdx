# EDC § 94874.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Part: 59. PRIVATE POSTSECONDARY AND HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Private Postsecondary Institutions > Article: 4. Exemptions

**Citation:** EDC § 94874.5

---

## Text

An institution that is otherwise exempt from this chapter shall comply with the requirements of Section 94927.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 593, Sec. 7. (SB 1192) Effective January 1, 2017. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
