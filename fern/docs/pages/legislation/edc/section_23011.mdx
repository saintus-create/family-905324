# EDC § 23011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 17. Employer Collection and Reporting Procedures

**Citation:** EDC § 23011

---

## Text

Penalties and interest due to the system pursuant to Section 23003, 23006, or 23008 are additional required contributions that, when received, shall be deposited to the Teachers’ Retirement Fund and treated in the same manner as other contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 275, Sec. 13. (AB 2101) Effective January 1, 2021.)
