# EDC § 51221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Required Courses of Study > Article: 3. Courses of Study, Grades 7 to 12

**Citation:** EDC § 51221

---

## Text

Instruction required by subdivision (b) of Section 51220 in the area of study of social sciences shall also provide a foundation for understanding the wise use of natural resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
