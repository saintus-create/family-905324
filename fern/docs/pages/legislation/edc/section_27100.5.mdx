# EDC § 27100.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 12. Beneficiary

**Citation:** EDC § 27100.5

---

## Text

A corporation, trust, charitable organization, parochial institution, or public entity may be designated as a beneficiary under this part, but shall not be designated as an annuity beneficiary, except a trust as defined in Section 26106.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 231, Sec. 5. (AB 1824) Effective January 1, 2023.)
