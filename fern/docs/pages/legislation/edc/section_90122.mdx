# EDC § 90122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. California Maritime Academy > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 90122

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the trustees shall administer the academy to comply with any federal statute and regulations governing maritime operations and maritime academies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
