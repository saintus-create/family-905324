# EDC § 81146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. School Sites > Article: 7. Approvals

**Citation:** EDC § 81146

---

## Text

Any public school building which has been approved by the Department of General Services for occupancy shall be deemed to meet the local building requirements for use as a private school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
