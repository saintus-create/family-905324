# EDC § 45255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 6. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 45255

---

## Text

The commission may, with respect to the staff of the commission, expend funds for their orientation, training, retraining, and development and for any purpose prescribed by Article 9 (commencing with Section 45380) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
