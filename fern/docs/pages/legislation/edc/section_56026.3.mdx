# EDC § 56026.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 56026.3

---

## Text

“Local educational agency” means a school district, a county office of education, a nonprofit charter school participating as a member of a special education local plan area, or a special education local plan area.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 5.5. Effective January 1, 2008.)
