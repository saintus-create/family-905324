# EDC § 8294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 11. Individualized County of San Mateo Childcare Subsidy Plan

**Citation:** EDC § 8294

---

## Text

A participating contractor shall receive any increase or decrease in funding that the contractor would have received if the contractor had not participated in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8347.5 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 183. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
