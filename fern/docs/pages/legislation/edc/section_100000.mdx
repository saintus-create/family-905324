# EDC § 100000

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Public Education Facilities Bond Act of 1996.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 203 at the March 26, 1996, election.)
