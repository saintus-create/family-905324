# EDC § 49051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 8. Searches by School Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 49051

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to affect the admissibility of evidence in subsequent juvenile or criminal proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
