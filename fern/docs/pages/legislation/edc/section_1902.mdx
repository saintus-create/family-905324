# EDC § 1902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County School Service Fund Programs and Services > Article: 14.5. Education of Prisoners

**Citation:** EDC § 1902

---

## Text

The county board of education may provide for the maintenance on Saturday of classes for prisoners in any county jail, county honor farm, or any county industrial farm or county or joint county road camp.
