# EDC § 44689

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.1. School Personnel Staff Development and Resource Centers > Article: 3. Administrator Training and Evaluation

**Citation:** EDC § 44689

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the administrator training and evaluation programs be funded annually in each Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 41. Effective July 28, 1983.)
