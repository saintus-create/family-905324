# EDC § 89774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Finance > Article: 5. Use of General Fund Support Appropriations for Capital Expenditures and Capital Outlay Projects

**Citation:** EDC § 89774

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, excluding Part 11 (commencing with Section 15850) of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, the California State University may proceed with capital expenditures and capital outlay projects pursuant to Sections 89772 and 89773 without the need for any further limitations or approvals, except those delineated in Sections 89772 and 89773.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 34, Sec. 24. (SB 860) Effective June 20, 2014.)
