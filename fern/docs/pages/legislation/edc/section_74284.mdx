# EDC § 74284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 46. DISTRICT REORGANIZATION OR FORMATION > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous > Article: 3. Disposition of Records, Funds, Property, and Obligations When Reorganized

**Citation:** EDC § 74284

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provisions of this article, exchanges of property tax revenues between districts as a result of reorganization shall be determined pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 99 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
