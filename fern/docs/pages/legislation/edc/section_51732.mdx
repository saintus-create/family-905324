# EDC § 51732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Authorized Classes and Courses of Instruction > Article: 4. Elementary School Special Day and Evening Classes

**Citation:** EDC § 51732

---

## Text

The evening elementary schools and the special day and evening classes of the elementary schools of any school district shall be open for the admission of such minors and adults as in the judgment of the governing board of the school district maintaining the schools or classes may profit from the instruction offered.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
