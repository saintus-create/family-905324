# EDC § 51021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. Education Program > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 51021

---

## Text

“Objective” means a devised accomplishment that can be verified within a given time and under specifiable conditions which, if attained, advances the system toward a corresponding goal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
