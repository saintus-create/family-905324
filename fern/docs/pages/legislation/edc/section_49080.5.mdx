# EDC § 49080.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6.5. Pupil Records > Article: 6. California School Information Services

**Citation:** EDC § 49080.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “CSIS” means the California School Information Services established pursuant to this article.

(b) “FCMAT” means the County Office Fiscal Crisis and Management Assistance Team established pursuant to Section 42127.8.

(c) “Administrator” means the administrator of the California School Information Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 78, Sec. 36. Effective July 7, 1999.)
