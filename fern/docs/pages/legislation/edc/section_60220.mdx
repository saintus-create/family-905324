# EDC § 60220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Elementary School Materials > Article: 2. Duties of Publishers and Manufacturers

**Citation:** EDC § 60220

---

## Text

All publishers and manufacturers submitting instructional materials for adoption by the state board shall comply with the provisions of Article 1 (commencing with Section 60000) to Article 7 (commencing with Section 60100), inclusive, of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
