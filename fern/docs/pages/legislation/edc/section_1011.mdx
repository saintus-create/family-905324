# EDC § 1011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 1. Election, Jurisdiction, Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** EDC § 1011

---

## Text

Regular meetings of the board shall be held at times it may determine, but not less than once per month and shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 4.)
