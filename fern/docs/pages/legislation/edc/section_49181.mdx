# EDC § 49181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. Employment of Minors > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** EDC § 49181

---

## Text

Failure to produce a permit to work is prima facie evidence of the illegal employment of any minor whose permit to work is not produced.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
