# EDC § 52743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 11. Miscellaneous > Article: 3. Educational Films or Video Tapes

**Citation:** EDC § 52743

---

## Text

The State Department of Education shall make available the films or video recordings produced pursuant to this article to schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 88, Sec. 27. (AB 176) Effective January 1, 2010.)
