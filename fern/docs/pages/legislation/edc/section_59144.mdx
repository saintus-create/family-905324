# EDC § 59144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California School for the Blind > Article: 4. Services and Courses

**Citation:** EDC § 59144

---

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction, in conjunction with the California School for the Blind, shall provide assessment and instructional planning services for individuals who are referred for those services pursuant to Section 56326.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 58. Effective September 21, 1992.)
