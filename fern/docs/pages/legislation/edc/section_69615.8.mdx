# EDC § 69615.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 5. Assumption Program of Loans for Education

**Citation:** EDC § 69615.8

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, in any fiscal year, the commission shall award no more than the number of loan assumption agreements that are authorized by the Governor and the Legislature in the annual Budget Act for that year for the assumption of loans pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 516, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2009.)
