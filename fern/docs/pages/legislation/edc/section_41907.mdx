# EDC § 41907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 12. Allowances for Excess Cost of Driver Training

**Citation:** EDC § 41907

---

## Text

A qualified instructor is one who has passed an approved driver’s instruction examination and holds a designated subjects credential or who holds a valid prior credential authorizing instruction in automobile driver education and driver training.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1042, Sec. 1.)
