# EDC § 81301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 49. COMMUNITY COLLEGES, EDUCATION FACILITIES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Property: Sale, Lease, Use, Gift, and Exchange > Article: 1.5. Conveyances

**Citation:** EDC § 81301

---

## Text

The governing board of a community college district shall have the power to execute and deliver quitclaim deeds, either with or without consideration to the owners of real property adjacent to any real property owned by the district, for the purpose of removing defects in and otherwise clearing up the title to such adjacent real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
