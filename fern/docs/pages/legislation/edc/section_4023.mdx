# EDC § 4023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 3. COUNTY ADMINISTRATION OF DISTRICT ORGANIZATION > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Operational Procedures > Article: 2. Transfer of Duties of County Committee to County Board of Education

**Citation:** EDC § 4023

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (a) of Section 4005, the first annual meeting following the reestablishment of a county committee on school district organization shall occur within 30 days of the State Board of Education’s action to reestablish the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1263, Sec. 6.)
