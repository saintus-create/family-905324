# EDC § 26213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Plan Administration

**Citation:** EDC § 26213

---

## Text

The board shall adopt actuarial assumptions, rates, factors and tables necessary to administer the Cash Balance Benefit Program as an amendment to the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 254. Effective January 1, 1999.)
