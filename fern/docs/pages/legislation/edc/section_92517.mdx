# EDC § 92517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. The University of California Revenue Bond Act of 1947 > Article: 4. Indentures

**Citation:** EDC § 92517

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause relating to the bonds issued pursuant to the indentures requiring the regents to make all necessary repairs, renewals, and replacements to any project, and to keep the project at all times in good repair, working order, and condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
