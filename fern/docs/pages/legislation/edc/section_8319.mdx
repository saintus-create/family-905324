# EDC § 8319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 13. Administrative Review

**Citation:** EDC § 8319

---

## Text

All contracts entered into by the department pursuant to Section 8233 shall contain a complete description of the appeal procedures provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8409 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 217. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
