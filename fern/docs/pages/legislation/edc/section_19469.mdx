# EDC § 19469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Library Districts > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** EDC § 19469

---

## Text

The board of library trustees shall designate the hours during which the library shall be open for the use of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 354, Sec. 8.)
