# EDC § 67001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 40. DONAHOE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 12. Federal Assistance for Higher Education

**Citation:** EDC § 67001

---

## Text

In accepting the benefits of the act of Congress, the people of the state agree to comply with all of the provisions and to observe all of its requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
