# EDC § 8485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Early Education Act > Article: 23. Child Supervision Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8485

---

## Text

The governing board of any school district conducting instruction for pupils in any grade level up to, and including, the completion of junior high school, may establish a program of affordably priced supervision for children before and after school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8480 (as added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1440) by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1120, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1987.)
