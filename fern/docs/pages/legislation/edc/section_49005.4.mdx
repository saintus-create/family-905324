# EDC § 49005.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 5.2. Restraint and Seclusion

**Citation:** EDC § 49005.4

---

## Text

An educational provider may use seclusion or a behavioral restraint only to control behavior that poses a clear and present danger of serious physical harm to the pupil or others that cannot be immediately prevented by a response that is less restrictive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 998, Sec. 1. (AB 2657) Effective January 1, 2019.)
