# EDC § 26700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Rights to Benefits

**Citation:** EDC § 26700

---

## Text

A participant has a vested right to a retirement benefit equal in amount to the total balance of credits in his or her employee account and employer account. The right accrues when a person becomes a participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 592, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1996.)
