# EDC § 92726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Community-Based Health Professions Education Partnership Program

**Citation:** EDC § 92726

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by resolution, make it applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 648, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
