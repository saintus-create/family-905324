# EDC § 2010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. Attendance in Adjoining States

**Citation:** EDC § 2010

---

## Text

All requisitions drawn by the county superintendent of schools under this article shall be approved by the county auditor and paid by the county treasurer.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
