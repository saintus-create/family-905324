# EDC § 69648.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Student Financial Aid Programs > Article: 8. Community College Extended Opportunity Programs and Services

**Citation:** EDC § 69648.5

---

## Text

The board of governors may use up to 1 percent of the funds appropriated for the EOPS program by the annual Budget Act to monitor program activities and to conduct the evaluation of EOPS offered by districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1372, Sec. 256.)
