# EDC § 89044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Powers of the Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 89044

---

## Text

The trustees may budget an amount to be expended for use in carrying out the community relation responsibilities of the president of each campus of the California State University and of the chancellor, as defined by the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 398, Sec. 1.)
