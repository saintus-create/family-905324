# EDC § 18558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 14.5. Sale of Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18558

---

## Text

In the event a petition, as set forth in Section 18557, is filed, the board of supervisors shall not grant the request without first submitting the question to the voters in the same manner and with the same effect as provided for a referendum by the electors of a district pursuant to Section 9340 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1995.)
