# EDC § 92698

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 7. The Center for Cooperatives

**Citation:** EDC § 92698

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by resolution, makes that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1198, Sec. 1.)
