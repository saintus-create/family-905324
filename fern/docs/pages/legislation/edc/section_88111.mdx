# EDC § 88111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 88111

---

## Text

The commission may by rule provide for the continuous examination of eligibles for classes of positions which the commission determines cannot be practicably filled by promotional examination only.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
