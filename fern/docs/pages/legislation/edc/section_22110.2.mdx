# EDC § 22110.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22110.2

---

## Text

“Credential,” “credentials,” and “certificate” mean any life diploma, credential, certificate, or other document provided for, by, and issued pursuant to the laws of the state that authorize service in the public school system of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 6.5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
