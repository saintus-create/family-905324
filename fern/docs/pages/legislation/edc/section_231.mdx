# EDC § 231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Educational Equity > Article: 4. Sex Equity in Education Act

**Citation:** EDC § 231

---

## Text

Nothing herein shall be construed to prohibit any educational institution from maintaining separate toilet facilities, locker rooms, or living facilities for the different sexes, so long as comparable facilities are provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1117, Sec. 1.)
