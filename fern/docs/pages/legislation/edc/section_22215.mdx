# EDC § 22215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** EDC § 22215

---

## Text

The board shall determine the service performed by members to be credited toward qualification for retirement, and shall fix and modify allowances provided under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 634, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1997.)
