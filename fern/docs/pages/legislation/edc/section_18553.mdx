# EDC § 18553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 14. Cancellation of Bonds

**Citation:** EDC § 18553

---

## Text

Thereupon the bonds and the vote by which they were authorized to be issued, shall cease to be of any validity whatever.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
