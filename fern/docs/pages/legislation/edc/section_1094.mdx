# EDC § 1094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 2. COUNTY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. County Boards of Education > Article: 4. Salaries and Expenses

**Citation:** EDC § 1094

---

## Text

Expenses for printing required by the county board of education, and all incidental expenses incurred for stationery or other purposes in the performance of its duties, shall be audited and paid as other claims against the general fund of the county are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
