# EDC § 26143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26143

---

## Text

“Termination benefit” means a benefit that is an amount equal to the sum of the participant’s employee account and employer account payable under this part pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 27200).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 248. Effective January 1, 1999.)
