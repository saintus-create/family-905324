# EDC § 22150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 22150

---

## Text

“Other public systems” means any of the following:

(a) Old age, survivors, disability, and health insurance program, other than the lump-sum death payment, provided by the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C.A. Sec. 300 and following).

(b) The federal civil service retirement program.

(c) Federal military disability.

(d) Railroad retirement.

(e) A workers’ compensation program.

(f) Federal railroad retirement.

(g) Any other public retirement system, including, but not limited to, any disability programs financed from public funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 933, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1994.)
