# EDC § 87421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 87421

---

## Text

Nothing in this code shall be construed as prohibiting the employment of persons in academic positions for less than a full school year in temporary schools or classes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1302, Sec. 50. Effective September 25, 1990.)
