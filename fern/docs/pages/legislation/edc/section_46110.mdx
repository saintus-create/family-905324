# EDC § 46110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 26. ATTENDANCE FOR COMPUTING APPORTIONMENTS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Attendance—Maximum Credit—Minimum Day > Article: 2. Kindergarten and Elementary Schools

**Citation:** EDC § 46110

---

## Text

No pupil in a kindergarten or in any grade of an elementary school shall be credited with more than one day of attendance in any calendar day and nothing in this article shall be construed to the contrary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
