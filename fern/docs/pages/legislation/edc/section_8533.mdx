# EDC § 8533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 6. EDUCATION PROGRAMS—STATE MASTER PLANS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Adult Education > Article: 3. Functions of Continuing Education Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 8533

---

## Text

Adult continuing education, including but not limited to, parent education, consumer education, civic education, education in special fields, and education in the arts and the humanities, may be made available in high school, unified, and community college districts by mutual agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
