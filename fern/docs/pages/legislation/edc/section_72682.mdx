# EDC § 72682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 45. DISTRICTS AND GOVERNING BOARDS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 6. Auxiliary Organizations

**Citation:** EDC § 72682

---

## Text

An auxiliary organization that was in existence on August 31, 1980, shall continue to operate under Article 6 (commencing with Section 72670) of Chapter 6 of Part 45, as it read immediately prior to August 30, 1980, until the time, if any, that the organization is recognized pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 654, Sec. 7. Effective October 7, 2005.)
