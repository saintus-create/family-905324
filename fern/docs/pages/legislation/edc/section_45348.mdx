# EDC § 45348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 7. Instructional Aides

**Citation:** EDC § 45348

---

## Text

The school district shall pay to each person employed as an instructional aide compensation at a rate not less than the minimum hourly rate prescribed by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
