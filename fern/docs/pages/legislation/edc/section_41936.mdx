# EDC § 41936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 24. SCHOOL FINANCE > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Foundation Program > Article: 14. Allowances for Project Connected Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 41936

---

## Text

Amounts allowed pursuant to this article shall be apportioned by the Superintendent of Public Instruction at the same time and as a part of the special purpose apportionment made under the provisions of Section 41334, whichever is in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
