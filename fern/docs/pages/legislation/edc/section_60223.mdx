# EDC § 60223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 33. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS AND TESTING > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Elementary School Materials > Article: 2. Duties of Publishers and Manufacturers

**Citation:** EDC § 60223

---

## Text

Publishers and manufacturers may biennially submit revisions to price schedules submitted pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 60201.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 413, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1996.)
