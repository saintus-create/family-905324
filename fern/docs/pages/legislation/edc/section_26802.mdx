# EDC § 26802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Retirement Benefit

**Citation:** EDC § 26802

---

## Text

Distribution of the retirement benefit under this part shall commence no later than the required beginning date specified in subdivision (c) of Section 26004.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 273. Effective January 1, 1999.)
