# EDC § 26104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26104

---

## Text

“Administrative costs” means the costs of administering the Cash Balance Benefit Program for the plan year as determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1025, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2001.)
