# EDC § 45372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 8.5. Readers for Legally Blind Teachers

## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall establish the procedures and necessary forms for applications for funding pursuant to this section.
