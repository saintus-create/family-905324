# EDC § 90081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Housing and Auxiliary Services > Article: 2. The State University Revenue Bond Act of 1947

**Citation:** EDC § 90081

---

## Text

An action may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure to determine the validity of bonds issued, or any financing contract entered into, by the board for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 880, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
