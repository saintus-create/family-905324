# EDC § 88070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Classified Employees > Article: 3. Merit System

**Citation:** EDC § 88070

---

## Text

The governing board may authorize payment to members of the commission an amount not to exceed fifty dollars ($50) per meeting and not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars ($250) per month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 723, Sec. 15.)
