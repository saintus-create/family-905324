# EDC § 21109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 12. FOUNDATIONS AND STATE COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Foundations, Trusts, and Institutions of Arts and Sciences > Article: 1. Foundations and Trusts

**Citation:** EDC § 21109

---

## Text

The grantor may reserve the right to alter, amend, or modify the terms and conditions of the grant, and the trusts created, in respect to any of the matters mentioned or referred to in Sections 21101 to 21104, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
