# EDC § 87681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 4. Evaluations and Discipline

**Citation:** EDC § 87681

---

## Text

In the case in which the administrative law judge determines that the operation of his or her decision should be postponed, any question of terminating the postponement shall be brought to the administrative law judge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 324, Sec. 10.)
