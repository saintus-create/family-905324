# EDC § 45366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5. Classified Employees > Article: 8. Teacher Aides

**Citation:** EDC § 45366

---

## Text

Apportionments to a school district shall be in the form of grants, no part of which shall be required to be repaid to the state by the school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
