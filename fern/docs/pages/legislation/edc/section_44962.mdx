# EDC § 44962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Employment—Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Resignations, Dismissals, and Leaves of Absence

**Citation:** EDC § 44962

---

## Text

Governing boards of school districts may grant leaves of absence to persons employed in positions requiring certification qualifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
