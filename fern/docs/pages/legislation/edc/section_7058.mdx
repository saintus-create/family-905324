# EDC § 7058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 5. PUBLIC SCHOOL PERSONNEL > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Personnel General Provisions > Article: 2. Political Activities of School Officers and Employees

**Citation:** EDC § 7058

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prohibit the use of a forum under the control of the governing board of a school district or community college district if the forum is made available to all sides on an equitable basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 879, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
