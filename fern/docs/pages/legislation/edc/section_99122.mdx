# EDC § 99122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. MISCELLANEOUS > Part: 65. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2.5. Social Media Privacy

**Citation:** EDC § 99122

---

## Text

A private nonprofit or for-profit postsecondary educational institution shall post its social media privacy policy on the institution’s Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 619, Sec. 2. (SB 1349) Effective January 1, 2013.)
