# EDC § 89257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Education Programs > Article: 6. California State University Educational Opportunity Program

**Citation:** EDC § 89257

---

## Text

Records of the academic progress of each student attending a campus of the California State University under a grant shall be kept by each campus of the California State University having a program and forwarded to the trustees in order that the program created by this article may be evaluated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 263. Effective January 1, 1996.)
