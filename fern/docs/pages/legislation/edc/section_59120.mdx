# EDC § 59120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 32. STATE SPECIAL SCHOOLS AND CENTERS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. California School for the Blind > Article: 3. Pupils

**Citation:** EDC § 59120

---

## Text

Every blind person resident of this state, of suitable age and capacity, is entitled to an education in the California School for the Blind free of charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
