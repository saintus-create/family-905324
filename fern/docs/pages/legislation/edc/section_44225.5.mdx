# EDC § 44225.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 2. Commission on Teacher Credentialing

**Citation:** EDC § 44225.5

---

## Text

The Commission on Teacher Credentialing shall adopt standards for the issuance of teaching credentials to persons who received a score equal to or exceeding the minimum acceptable score set by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 922, Sec. 76. Effective January 1, 1995.)
