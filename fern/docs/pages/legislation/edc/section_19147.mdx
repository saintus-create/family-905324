# EDC § 19147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. County Free Libraries > Article: 2. County Librarian

**Citation:** EDC § 19147

---

## Text

The salary of the county librarians shall be paid by each of the counties in equal monthly installments, at the same time and in the same manner and out of the same fund as the salaries of other county officers are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
