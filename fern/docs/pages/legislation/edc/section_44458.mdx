# EDC § 44458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Certificated Employees > Article: 3. Teacher Education Internship Act of 1967

**Citation:** EDC § 44458

---

## Text

The program shall meet the instructional or service needs of the district with the primary objective being to increase the effectiveness of the intern in the district. Both the district and the intern shall concur in the program planned.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
