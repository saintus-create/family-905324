# EDC § 89255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Education Programs > Article: 6. California State University Educational Opportunity Program

**Citation:** EDC § 89255

---

## Text

The amount of the grant awarded pursuant to Section 89254 may be increased above the maximum amount specified in that section to the extent that the Legislature expressly makes funds available for purposes of this section in the budget for that fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 263. Effective January 1, 1996.)
