# EDC § 44284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Teacher Credentialing > Article: 5. Subject Matter Examinations and Assessments

**Citation:** EDC § 44284

---

## Text

Examinations authorizing advanced or specialist teaching service may be authorized by the commission. Such examinations shall conform to the guidelines established by this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
