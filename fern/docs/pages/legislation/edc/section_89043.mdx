# EDC § 89043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Part: 55. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Powers of the Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 89043

---

## Text

The trustees shall have the powers conferred upon heads of departments of the state by Article 2 (commencing with Section 11180), Chapter 2, Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
