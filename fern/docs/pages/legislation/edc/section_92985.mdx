# EDC § 92985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Part: 57. UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 15. The California Blueprint for Research to Advance Innovations in Neuroscience Act of 2014

**Citation:** EDC § 92985

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Blueprint for Research to Advance Innovations in Neuroscience (Cal-BRAIN) Act of 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 34, Sec. 32. (SB 860) Effective June 20, 2014.)
