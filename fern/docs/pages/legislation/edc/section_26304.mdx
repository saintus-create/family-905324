# EDC § 26304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Employer and Participant Responsibilities

**Citation:** EDC § 26304

---

## Text

An employer shall provide timely notice to the system of the employment, death, or termination of employment of a participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 608, Sec. 17. Effective September 19, 1996. Applicable from July 1, 1996, pursuant to Sec. 58 of Ch. 608.)
