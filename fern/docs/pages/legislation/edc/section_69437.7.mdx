# EDC § 69437.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 42. STUDENT FINANCIAL AID PROGRAM > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1.7. Ortiz-Pacheco-Poochigian-Vasconcellos Cal Grant Program > Article: 5. Competitive Cal Grant A and B Awards

**Citation:** EDC § 69437.7

---

## Text

After two award cycles, the commission shall review the competitive grant program and its priorities to gain a better understanding of early participation patterns and to determine the initial level of program effectiveness. The commission shall report these findings to the Legislature and the Governor by December 31, 2003, and each year thereafter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 403, Sec. 4. Effective September 12, 2000. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed in Section 69475 (if it becomes operative). Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 69475 (if it becomes operative).)
