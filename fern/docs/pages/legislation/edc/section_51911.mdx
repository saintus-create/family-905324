# EDC § 51911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 5.5. Comprehensive Health Education > Article: 4. Comprehensive Health Education Plans

**Citation:** EDC § 51911

---

## Text

Approval of district plans shall be made in accordance with rules and regulations adopted by the State Board of Education.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 809.)
