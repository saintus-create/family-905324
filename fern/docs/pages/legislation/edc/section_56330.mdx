# EDC § 56330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 30. SPECIAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 4. Identification and Referral, Assessment, Instructional Planning, Implementation,and Review > Article: 2. Assessment

**Citation:** EDC § 56330

---

## Text

A local educational agency shall follow the procedures in Section 300.306(c) of Title 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations when interpreting assessment data for the purpose of determining if a child is an individual with exceptional needs under Section 56026.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 42. Effective January 1, 2008.)
