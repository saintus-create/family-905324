# EDC § 33004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 20. STATE EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 1. State Board of Education > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** EDC § 33004

---

## Text

The Superintendent shall be secretary and shall act as executive officer of the state board. The Superintendent shall have charge of all its correspondence and shall keep a record of its proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 21. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
