# EDC § 52327.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 28. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 9. Career Technical Education > Article: 1. Regional Occupational Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 52327.5

---

## Text

Each governing board establishing or maintaining a regional occupational center or program pursuant to Section 52310.5 shall meet the requirements set forth in Sections 35145, 41010, 41011, 41015, 41020, 42103, 51040, 51041, 51050, and 51056.
