# EDC § 87667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COMMUNITY COLLEGES > Part: 51. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Employment > Article: 4. Evaluations and Discipline

**Citation:** EDC § 87667

---

## Text

A contract or regular employee may be dismissed or penalized for one or more of the grounds set forth in Section 87732.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
