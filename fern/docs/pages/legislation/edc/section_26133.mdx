# EDC § 26133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 14. STATE TEACHERS’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM CASH BENEFIT PROGRAM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** EDC § 26133

---

## Text

“Pay period” means a payroll period specified by the employer but not more than 31 calendar days.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 244. Effective January 1, 1999.)
