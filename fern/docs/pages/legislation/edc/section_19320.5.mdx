# EDC § 19320.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. California State Library > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** EDC § 19320.5

---

## Text

The State Librarian may employ consultants to provide technical assistance to public libraries in the development and enhancement of library services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 666, Sec. 13. (AB 486) Effective January 1, 2022.)
