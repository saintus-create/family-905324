# EDC § 44776.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Part: 25. EMPLOYEES > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 3.65. Social Tolerance Resource Centers

**Citation:** EDC § 44776.6

---

## Text

For the 1995–96 fiscal year, the Superintendent of Public Instruction shall designate one regional social tolerance resource center to receive funding pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 308, Sec. 15. Effective August 3, 1995.)
