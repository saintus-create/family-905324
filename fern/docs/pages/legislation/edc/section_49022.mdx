# EDC § 49022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 6. Pupil Rights and Responsibilities > Article: 6. Athletic Programs

**Citation:** EDC § 49022

---

## Text

Insofar as practicable, in apportioning public funds school district governing boards shall apportion amounts available for athletics to ensure that equitable amounts will be allocated for all students, except that allowances may be made for differences in the costs of various athletic programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
