# EDC § 5300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 4. ELECTIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Conduct of Elections > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EDC § 5300

---

## Text

School district elections and community college district elections shall be governed by the Elections Code, except as otherwise provided in this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
