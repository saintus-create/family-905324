# EDC § 24705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 13. STATE TEACHERS' RETIREMENT SYSTEM > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 32. San Francisco Local System

**Citation:** EDC § 24705

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions in Section 24201, a member of the San Francisco system may retire concurrently and receive credit for service performed in other states of the United States, its territories and possessions, and in Canada.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 655, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 2007.)
