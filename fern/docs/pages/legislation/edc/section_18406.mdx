# EDC § 18406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Part: 11. LIBRARIES > Title: 1. GENERAL EDUCATION CODE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Unified School Districts and Union High School Districts Public Libraries > Article: 7. Powers of Boards of Trustees

**Citation:** EDC § 18406

---

## Text

The board of library trustees shall borrow books from, lend books to, and exchange books with other libraries, and shall allow nonresidents to borrow books upon such conditions as it may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
