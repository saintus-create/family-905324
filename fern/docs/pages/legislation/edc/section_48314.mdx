# EDC § 48314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 2. Compulsory Education Law > Article: 7. Pupil Attendance Alternatives

**Citation:** EDC § 48314

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that every parent in this state be informed of his or her opportunity for currently existing school district of choice options under this article regardless of ethnicity, primary language, literacy, or special needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 15, Sec. 41. (AB 99) Effective June 27, 2017.)
