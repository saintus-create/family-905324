# EDC § 49395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. INSTRUCTION AND SERVICES > Part: 27. PUPILS > Title: 2. ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION > Chapter: 8. Pupil Safety > Article: 8. Threats of Homicide at School

**Citation:** EDC § 49395

---

## Text

A local educational agency serving pupils in kindergarten or any of grades 1 to 12, inclusive, and a school of a local educational agency, is immune from civil liability for any damages allegedly caused by, arising out of, or relating to the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 144, Sec. 1. (SB 906) Effective January 1, 2023.)
