> **Research status:** The toolkit is an organized research corpus, not legal advice. Some propositions remain marked for current-law review. Primary authority is intentionally distinguished from secondary and practitioner material.

## What this section does

The Defense Toolkit connects a legal proposition to the authority supporting it, the procedure that implements it, the forms commonly involved, and the evidence that may bear on the disputed issue.

It is designed to be read as a research map rather than a list of guaranteed defenses. A proposition can be supported, limited, distinguished, contradicted, or still require verification.

## Factual defenses

### Denial of statutory abuse or insufficient evidence

The supplied research corpus frames this strategy around contesting whether qualifying abuse has been established by the applicable evidentiary standard.

**Authority:** Family Code §§ 6300, 6320; *S.M. v. E.P.* (2010) 184 Cal.App.4th 1249.

**Procedure:** Review the request and response forms, identify each factual allegation, file the applicable response, and prepare admissible evidence for the hearing.

**Forms:** DV-120, DV-125, and, where financially relevant, FL-150.

**Evidence to research:** communications, timeline or location evidence, witness declarations, and records that directly address the allegations.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

## Procedural defenses

### Lack of a qualifying domestic relationship

The corpus identifies Family Code § 6211 as the starting point for determining whether the relationship falls within the statutory definition covered by the DVPA.

**Authority:** Family Code §§ 6211 and 6209.

**Procedure:** Raise the relationship issue in the response and organize evidence concerning the nature and history of the relationship.

**Form:** DV-120.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

## Renewal and post-judgment defenses

### Contesting renewal

The supplied corpus identifies *Ritchie v. Konrad* and Family Code § 6345 as central authorities for researching contested renewal. The proposition should not be treated as a complete statement of current renewal law without reviewing subsequent authority.

**Form:** DV-720.

**Research path:** § 6345 → renewal cases → subsequent treatment → factual circumstances → evidence → hearing procedure.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

### Modification or termination

The corpus identifies Family Code § 6345 and *Loeffler v. Medina* as authorities for researching modification or termination and identifies DV-300 and DV-400 as the relevant forms in the supplied material.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

## Constitutional and procedural safeguards

### Notice and opportunity to be heard

The corpus frames lack of notice as a due-process issue and identifies the Fourteenth Amendment as the constitutional foundation. The procedural remedy depends on the posture of the case and the specific service or notice defect.

**Research path:** constitutional guarantee → service record → applicable procedural rule → available relief → prejudice.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

### Firearm restriction and constitutional research

The corpus identifies *United States v. Rahimi* and Family Code § 6389 as authorities requiring careful analysis when researching the scope of firearm restrictions. This is an especially important area for current-law verification because constitutional doctrine and statutory implementation can change.

**Status:** Needs current-law review.

## How to read a defense record

Each strategy in the underlying research data carries a source status. `Verified` means the proposition has undergone the project's review process. `Needs current-law review` means the material is retained for research but should not be presented as settled current law. `Unreviewed` identifies a relationship or proposition that has not yet been independently checked.

The research model exposes relationships such as **supports**, **contradicts**, **distinguishes**, **limits**, **amends**, **implements**, and **interprets**, allowing a user to follow a proposition through its authority network rather than stopping at a single citation.
