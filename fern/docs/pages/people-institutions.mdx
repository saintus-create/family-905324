## What an entry should establish

Each eventual directory record should distinguish:

- identity and institutional role
- jurisdiction
- area of work or research
- public source
- date verified
- relationship to cases, legislation, publications, or datasets
- whether the relationship is current, historical, or analytical

## Connected research

The directory becomes useful when it connects to the legal corpus rather than existing as a phone book.

`PERSON → argued → CASE`

`PERSON → authored → PUBLICATION`

`PERSON → sponsored → BILL`

`INSTITUTION → operates → CLINIC`

`INSTITUTION → publishes → RESEARCH`

`ATTORNEY → represented → PARTY`

`SCHOLAR → studies → LEGAL ISSUE`

Those relationships should always be sourced. A name appearing in a case does not, by itself, establish an attorney's current practice area or institutional affiliation.

## Privacy and quality rule

The directory should use professional and institutional information that is appropriate for public research. It should not become a repository for private contact information, personal addresses, or unsupported biographical claims.
