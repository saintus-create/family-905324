<Callout intent="info">
Empirical evidence is kept separate from legal authority. A statistic can inform a legal question without becoming a legal rule.
</Callout>

## Evidence record

A future evidence record should preserve the study or dataset, population, sample, methodology, date, limitations, variables, findings, and original source.

## Research standards

- Identify the original dataset or publication.
- Preserve the date and version used.
- Distinguish descriptive statistics from causal claims.
- Report relevant limitations and missing data.
- Never treat correlation as proof of causation without appropriate design.
- Make analytical transformations reproducible where possible.

## Family-law applications

The corpus can eventually support research on court outcomes, filings, protective orders, custody, support, representation, access to counsel, legislative change, and other empirically measurable questions.

The goal is not to manufacture a single “family court statistic.” It is to make underlying evidence inspectable and comparable.
