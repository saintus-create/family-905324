---
title: Research Pipeline
subtitle: From source material to verified knowledge and analysis
---

<Callout intent="info">
This is the operating model for the research system. New material should move through acquisition, normalization, verification, relationship mapping, and analysis rather than being published as unstructured summaries.
</Callout>

## 1. Build the authoritative corpus

Prioritize primary and official sources: California Family Code, California Rules of Court, statutes and regulations, court decisions, legislative records, official forms, and authoritative government material.

Each imported record receives a provenance entry, jurisdiction, date, source type, and verification state.

## 2. Build the relationship graph

Connect records instead of duplicating information:

`STATUTE ↔ CASE ↔ RULE ↔ BILL ↔ PERSON ↔ INSTITUTION ↔ EVIDENCE`

Relationships are independently classified and sourced. A relationship can be documented, probable, analytical, or unresolved.

## 3. Analyze arguments and evidence

Once sources are structured, the system can represent claims, premises, warrants, objections, rebuttals, empirical premises, rhetorical features, and dialectical status.

The analytical layer must preserve the difference between a source proposition and an interpretation of that proposition.

## 4. Add source-grounded AI

AI tools can summarize, compare, retrieve, classify, map arguments, and identify relationships across the corpus. They must operate against retrieved source material and expose provenance rather than producing unsupported legal assertions.

### Analysis modes

- Case summary and comparison
- Citation and authority analysis
- Legislative-history analysis
- Argument mapping
- Rhetorical analysis
- Computational dialectics
- Empirical analysis
- Cross-jurisdictional comparison

## Verification states

**Verified** means checked against a primary or official source.

**Indexed** means discovered or imported but not yet fully verified.

**Unresolved** means a material fact or relationship remains uncertain.

**Analytical** means the record is an interpretation or derived analytical object rather than primary authority.

## The rule that governs all four phases

**More data is not automatically better research.**

A smaller corpus with reliable provenance and explicit uncertainty is more useful than a huge corpus whose relationships and quotations cannot be verified.
