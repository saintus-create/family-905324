## Attorney index

A future attorney record should contain only publicly appropriate professional information:

- name
- jurisdiction
- bar admission/status where publicly verifiable
- practice areas
- firm or organization
- public office/contact information
- source and verification date
- relevant public publications or court appearances where appropriate

The directory should not become a repository for private contact information, personal addresses, or unverified accusations.

## University and research index

University entries should connect institutions to their substantive resources rather than merely listing names. Possible relationships include:

`university`
→ `law school`
→ `clinic / center`
→ `faculty / researcher`
→ `publication`
→ `dataset`
→ `case / legal issue`

This allows the directory to function as a research map.

## Search and comparison

Eventually entries should be filterable by:

- jurisdiction
- county
- practice area
- organization type
- legal-aid availability
- academic discipline
- research topic
- LGBTQ family law
- domestic violence
- custody
- support
- adoption
- reproductive law
- appellate practice
- administrative law
- constitutional law

No ranking should be implied unless the methodology is explicitly defined and supported by appropriate public data.

## Verification

Directory entries should carry a source, date checked, and status such as **verified**, **needs review**, **inactive**, or **historical**. Professional identity and licensing information should be verified against authoritative public sources where feasible.
