</Callout>

## What can be mapped

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Claims" icon="fa-regular fa-bullseye">Identify propositions asserted by a party, court, legislature, or commentator.</Card>
  <Card title="Reasons" icon="fa-regular fa-link">Connect a claim to the authority, fact, or premise offered in support.</Card>
  <Card title="Objections" icon="fa-regular fa-circle-xmark">Record contradictions, evidentiary challenges, legal objections, and competing interpretations.</Card>
  <Card title="Warrants" icon="fa-regular fa-arrow-right">Make the inferential step between evidence and conclusion explicit.</Card>
  <Card title="Rhetoric" icon="fa-regular fa-comment-dots">Study framing, attribution, presupposition, modality, narrative structure, and agency.</Card>
  <Card title="Dialectics" icon="fa-regular fa-diagram-project">Represent attacks, defenses, concessions, unresolved disputes, and changing commitments.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Provenance first

An analytical record should identify the exact source passage or authority, the operation performed, and whether the result is descriptive, inferential, empirical, or AI-generated.

## Future computational view

`CLAIM` → `REASON` → `WARRANT` → `CONCLUSION`

with opposing positions represented as structured attacks and defenses rather than flattened into a single summary.
